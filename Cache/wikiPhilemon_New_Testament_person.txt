<p><b>Philemon</b> (<span class="rt-commentedText nowrap"><span class="IPA nopopups noexcerpt" lang="en-fonipa"><a href="/wiki/Help:IPA/English" title="Help:IPA/English">/<span style="border-bottom:1px dotted"><span title="&#39;f&#39; in &#39;find&#39;">f</span><span title="/ɪ/: &#39;i&#39; in &#39;kit&#39;">ɪ</span><span title="/ˈ/: primary stress follows">ˈ</span><span title="&#39;l&#39; in &#39;lie&#39;">l</span><span title="/iː/: &#39;ee&#39; in &#39;fleece&#39;">iː</span><span title="&#39;m&#39; in &#39;my&#39;">m</span><span title="/ən/: &#39;on&#39; in &#39;button&#39;">ən</span></span>,<span class="wrap"> </span><span style="border-bottom:1px dotted"><span title="&#39;f&#39; in &#39;find&#39;">f</span><span title="/aɪ/: &#39;i&#39; in &#39;tide&#39;">aɪ</span></span>-/</a></span></span>; <a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span title="Ancient Greek (to 1453)-language text"><span lang="grc"><a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%A6%CE%B9%CE%BB%CE%AE%CE%BC%CF%89%CE%BD#Ancient_Greek" class="extiw" title="wikt:Φιλήμων">Φιλήμων</a></span></span>; <i>Philḗmōn</i>) was an early Christian in <a href="/wiki/Asia_Minor" class="mw-redirect" title="Asia Minor">Asia Minor</a> who was the recipient of a private letter from <a href="/wiki/Paul_of_Tarsus" class="mw-redirect" title="Paul of Tarsus">Paul of Tarsus</a>. This letter is known as <a href="/wiki/Epistle_to_Philemon" title="Epistle to Philemon">Epistle to Philemon</a> in the <a href="/wiki/New_Testament" title="New Testament">New Testament</a>. He is known as a saint by several Christian churches along with his wife Apphia (or <b>Appia</b>). Philemon was a wealthy Christian and a minister (possibly a <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a>)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> of the <a href="/wiki/House_church" title="House church">house church</a> that met in his home.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Menaia" class="mw-redirect" title="Menaia">Menaia</a> of 22 November speak of Philemon as a holy apostle who, in company with Apphia, <a href="/wiki/Archippus" title="Archippus">Archippus</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Onesimus" title="Onesimus">Onesimus</a> had been martyred at <a href="/wiki/Colossae" title="Colossae">Colossae</a> during the first general persecution in the reign of <a href="/wiki/Nero" title="Nero">Nero</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In the list of the <a href="/wiki/Seventy_Apostles" class="mw-redirect" title="Seventy Apostles">Seventy Apostles</a>, attributed to <a href="/wiki/Dorotheus_of_Tyre" title="Dorotheus of Tyre">Dorotheus of Tyre</a>, Philemon is described as bishop of <a href="/wiki/Gaza_City" title="Gaza City">Gaza</a>.
</p>
