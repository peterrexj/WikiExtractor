<p><b>Agofredus</b>, also known as <b>Aifroy</b>, (died 738)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was a French <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. His memorial is June 21.<sup id="cite_ref-newadvent_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-newadvent-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Agofredus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> known throughout <a href="/wiki/Normandy" title="Normandy">Normandy</a> for his holiness, he was the brother of St. <a href="/wiki/Leutfridus" title="Leutfridus">Leutfridus</a> (Leufroy), whom he succeeded as <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> of the Benedictine monastery at La-Croix Saint-Ouen in June 738.<sup id="cite_ref-newadvent_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-newadvent-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
