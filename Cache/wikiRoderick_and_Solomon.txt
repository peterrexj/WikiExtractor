<p><b>Saint Roderick</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Rodericus, Rudericus</i>; <a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>: <i lang="es">San Rodrigo</i>; died 13 March 857) was a <a href="/wiki/Mozarabs" title="Mozarabs">Mozarab</a> <a href="/wiki/Priesthood_in_the_Catholic_Church" title="Priesthood in the Catholic Church">Catholic priest</a>, venerated as one of the <a href="/wiki/Martyrs_of_C%C3%B3rdoba" title="Martyrs of Córdoba">Martyrs of Córdoba</a>. Tradition states that he was a Christian priest of <a href="/wiki/Cabra,_Spain" title="Cabra, Spain">Cabra</a> who had two brothers: one was a <a href="/wiki/Muslim" class="mw-redirect" title="Muslim">Muslim</a>, the other <a href="/wiki/Irreligion" title="Irreligion">irreligious</a>. Once, after his brothers began to fight one another, Roderick attempted to break up the fight. However, they turned on him instead and beat him.
</p><p>When Roderick awoke, he found that his Muslim brother had reported to the authorities that Roderick had converted to <a href="/wiki/Islam" title="Islam">Islam</a>. When Roderick maintained his loyalty to the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic religion</a>, he was accused of <a href="/wiki/Apostasy_in_Islam" title="Apostasy in Islam">apostasy from Islam</a> under <a href="/wiki/Sharia" title="Sharia"><i>Sharia</i> law</a>. He was imprisoned and then <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">beheaded</a> along with Salomon (Solomon) at <a href="/wiki/C%C3%B3rdoba,_Spain" title="Córdoba, Spain">Córdoba</a>.
</p><p>St. Roderick's Convent and Hospital in <a href="/wiki/Cabra,_Spain" title="Cabra, Spain">Cabra</a>, established in the 16th century, bears his name.
</p>
