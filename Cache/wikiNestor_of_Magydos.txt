<p><b>Saint Nestor</b>, also known as <b>Saint Nestor of <a href="/wiki/Perge" class="mw-redirect" title="Perge">Perge</a></b> or <b><a href="/wiki/Hieromartyr" title="Hieromartyr">Hieromartyr</a> Nestor of Magydos</b>, was <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">Bishop</a> of <a href="/wiki/Magydos" class="mw-redirect" title="Magydos">Magydos</a> in <a href="/wiki/Pamphylia" title="Pamphylia">Pamphylia</a>, in what is now modern <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Nestor_of_Magydos&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Little is known about Nestor. His courage and authority were so noteworthy that a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Empire" title="Roman Empire">Roman</a> <a href="/wiki/Magistrate" title="Magistrate">magistrate</a> uttered these words: <i>"Until we have got the better of the bishop, we shall be powerless against the Christians."</i><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> During the persecutions under Emperor <a href="/wiki/Decius" title="Decius">Decius</a>, Bishop Nestor was arrested while praying in his home.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was sentenced to <a href="/wiki/Death_penalty" class="mw-redirect" title="Death penalty">death</a> by the local Roman <a href="/wiki/Governor" title="Governor">governor</a>, Pollio or Epolius of <a href="/wiki/Lycia" title="Lycia">Lycia</a>, after refusing to sacrifice to the pagan gods of the state. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is February 25 in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> and February 28 in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
