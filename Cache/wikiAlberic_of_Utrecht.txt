<p><b>Saint Alberic of Utrecht</b>, <a href="/wiki/Dutch_language" title="Dutch language">Dutch</a>: <i lang="nl"><b>Alberik I van Utrecht</b></i>, (died 21 August 784) was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictines" title="Benedictines">Benedictine monk</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Utrecht" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Utrecht">bishop of Utrecht</a>, in what is today the <a href="/wiki/Netherlands" title="Netherlands">Netherlands</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alberic_of_Utrecht&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Alberic was the nephew of <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Utrecht" title="Gregory of Utrecht">Saint Gregory of Utrecht</a>. Little is known of Alberic before he joined the Order of Saint Benedict.<sup id="cite_ref-cathon_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cathon-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> It is known that he served as <a href="/wiki/Prior" title="Prior">prior</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Cathedral_of_Saint_Martin,_Utrecht" class="mw-redirect" title="Cathedral of Saint Martin, Utrecht">Monastery of Saint Martin</a> in Utrecht. When Gregory died in 775, Alberic succeeded his uncle in administering the diocese of Utrecht.<sup class="reference" id="ref_Anone"><a href="#endnote_Anone">[a]</a></sup> He later traveled to Cologne where he was consecrated Bishop of Utrecht. 
</p><p>Alberic strove to deepen the faith of his faithful who still preserved many pagan traditions.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
His bishopric was noted for the success of its mission among the pagan <a href="/wiki/Teutons" title="Teutons">Teutons</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> as well as the reorganization of the school of Utrecht. In addition, Alberic directed the mission of <a href="/wiki/Ludger" title="Ludger">Ludger</a> in <a href="/wiki/East_Frisia" title="East Frisia">Ostergau</a>.  
</p><p>Alberic was a good friend of <a href="/wiki/Alcuin" title="Alcuin">Alcuin</a>, a teacher and poet from <a href="/wiki/York" title="York">York</a>, England, preeminent among the scholars of that era. This relationship likely speaks to Alberic's own intelligence,<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> as the saint has been noted for his "encyclopedic knowledge of the faith."
</p>
