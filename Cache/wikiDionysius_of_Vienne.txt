<p><b>Dionysius</b> was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Vienne" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Vienne">Bishop of Vienne</a>. He was among the ten missionaries sent by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Sixtus_I" title="Pope Sixtus I">Pope St. Sixtus I</a> with <a href="/wiki/Peregrine_(martyr)" title="Peregrine (martyr)">St. Peregrinus</a> to Gaul. Dionysius later succeeded St. Justus as <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Vienne" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Vienne">Bishop of Vienne</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Dauphin%C3%A9" title="Dauphiné">Dauphiné</a>, France.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
