<p><b>Saint Theofrid</b> (<i>Chaffre, Theofredus, Theofred, Théofroy</i>) <b>of Orange</b> (or <i>of Carmery</i>) (d. 728 or 732 AD) was an abbot of Calmeliac or Carmery-en-Velay (later called Saint-Chaffre, and today <a href="/wiki/Le_Monastier-sur-Gazeille" title="Le Monastier-sur-Gazeille">Le Monastier-sur-Gazeille</a><sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>), which is situated near <a href="/wiki/Le_Puy-en-Velay" title="Le Puy-en-Velay">Le Puy-en-Velay</a> and was founded by <a href="/wiki/Saint_Calminius" title="Saint Calminius">Saint Calminius</a>.
</p><p>A native of <a href="/wiki/Orange,_Vaucluse" title="Orange, Vaucluse">Orange</a>, he is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>, as <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> tradition holds that he was killed by <a href="/wiki/Muslim" class="mw-redirect" title="Muslim">Muslim</a> raiders who had crossed into southern <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated2-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Tradition states that the circumstances of his death are as follows: when the raiders neared Calmeliac, Theofrid ordered the other monks to hide in the forest.<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated2_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated2-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He remained near the monastery and was found in prayer, and was dragged away and mortally wounded in the head with a stone.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:232px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/58/Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG/230px-Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG" decoding="async" width="230" height="173" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/58/Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG/345px-Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/58/Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG/460px-Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG 2x" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="960" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Abbatiale_St_Chaffre.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Abbey Church of Saint-Chaffre, Le Monastier-sur-Gazeille.</div></div></div>
<p>The legend further states that after Theofrid fell to the group, the earth shook and a dark storm cloud unleashed lightning, hail, and winds that dispersed the raiders.<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated2_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated2-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  Theofrid died seven days later.<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated2_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated2-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theofrid&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His cult spread in <a href="/wiki/Southern_France" title="Southern France">Southern France</a> during the 11th century.<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  In <a href="/wiki/Piedmont" title="Piedmont">Piedmont</a>, his cult was confused with that of the legendary member of the <a href="/wiki/Theban_Legion" title="Theban Legion">Theban Legion</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chiaffredo" title="Chiaffredo">Chiaffredo</a> (Theofredus, Teofredo).<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
