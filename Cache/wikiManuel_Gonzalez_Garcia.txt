					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Spanish essayist and poet in Asturian</div>
<p><b>Manuel Asur</b> (<b>Manuel Asur GONZÁLEZ GARCÍA</b>) (<a href="/w/index.php?title=G%C3%BCeria_Carrocera&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Güeria Carrocera (page does not exist)">Güeria Carrocera</a>, <a href="/wiki/San_Mart%C3%ADn_del_Rey_Aurelio" title="San Martín del Rey Aurelio">San Martín del Rey Aurelio</a>, <a href="/wiki/Asturias" title="Asturias">Asturias</a>, 1947) is a Spanish essayist and poet in <a href="/wiki/Asturian_language" title="Asturian language">Asturian</a>. He's considered to be one of the first modern poets in this language. He has a PhD degree in <a href="/wiki/Philosophy" title="Philosophy">Philosophy</a>. His book, <i>Cancios y poemes pa un riscar</i> ('Songs and Poems to a Dawn') meant the beginning in 1977 of the rebirth (Asturian <i>surdimientu</i>) of asturian literature, because he contributed with a poetry more newfangled and risked than anything written before in this language. Most of their works talk about the socio-political facts of the moment. Some of their poems became very popular in Asturias in the 1970s since the group <a href="/wiki/Nuberu" title="Nuberu">Nuberu</a> used them as lyrics for their songs. He wrote also a narrative book in 1987, <i>Hai una llinia trazada</i> ('There's a Drawn Line'), with short stories that he say to consider "almost monologues". He's a current articulist of <i>La Nueva España</i> publications and works in the <i>Consejería de Medio Rural y Pesca del Principado de Asturias</i>.
</p>
