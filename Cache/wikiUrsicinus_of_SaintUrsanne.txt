<p><b>Ursicinus</b> (also <i>Hursannus, Ursitz, Oschanne</i>, fl. 620) was an <a href="/wiki/Hiberno-Scottish_mission" title="Hiberno-Scottish mission">Irish missionary</a> and  <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Jura_region" class="mw-redirect" title="Jura region">Jura region</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Information">Information</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ursicinus_of_Saint-Ursanne&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Information">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A <i>vita</i> of his is preserved in a redaction of the 11th century. According to this account, he was a disciple of <a href="/wiki/Columbanus" title="Columbanus">Columbanus</a> at <a href="/wiki/Luxeuil_Abbey" title="Luxeuil Abbey">Luxeuil</a> who followed his master when he was banished from <a href="/wiki/First_Kingdom_of_Burgundy" class="mw-redirect" title="First Kingdom of Burgundy">Burgundy</a> in 610, but then retired as a hermit in the <a href="/wiki/Doubs_valley" class="mw-redirect" title="Doubs valley">Doubs valley</a>. Veneration of Ursicinus is attested since the 7th century.
In <a href="/wiki/Grandval,_Switzerland" title="Grandval, Switzerland">Grandval</a>, a church was dedicated to Ursicinus in 675. By the 11th century, he was part of the local canon of saints of the <a href="/wiki/Besan%C3%A7on" title="Besançon">Besançon</a> diocese. His feast day is on 20 December. Ursicinus' supposed sarcophagus is preserved in <a href="/wiki/St-Ursanne" class="mw-redirect" title="St-Ursanne">St-Ursanne</a> in what is now the <a href="/wiki/Canton_of_Jura" title="Canton of Jura">canton of Jura</a> in <a href="/wiki/Switzerland" title="Switzerland">Switzerland</a>.
</p>
