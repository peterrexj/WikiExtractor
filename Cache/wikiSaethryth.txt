					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">East Anglian saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the 9th century Mercian queen, see <a href="/wiki/Beorhtwulf_of_Mercia" title="Beorhtwulf of Mercia">Beorhtwulf of Mercia</a>.</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p><b>Saint Sæthryth</b> (<a href="/wiki/Old_English_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Old English language">Old English</a>&#58; <span title="Old English (ca. 450-1100)-language text"><i lang="ang">Sǣþrȳð</i></span>; <a href="/wiki/Floruit" title="Floruit"><i>fl.</i></a> 660s), also called <b>Sedrido</b>, <b>Sethrida</b> or <b>Saethrid</b>, was the stepdaughter of king <a href="/wiki/Anna_of_East_Anglia" title="Anna of East Anglia">Anna of East Anglia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Sæthryth was sent to the <a href="/wiki/Faremoutiers_Abbey" title="Faremoutiers Abbey">Abbey of Faremoutiers</a> in <a href="/wiki/Brie_(region)" title="Brie (region)">Brie</a> to be educated, and became a <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Benedict" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Saint Benedict">Benedictine</a> <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a>,  under its foundress Saint <a href="/wiki/Burgundofara" title="Burgundofara">Burgundofara</a>, whom she succeeded as <a href="/wiki/Abbess" title="Abbess">abbess</a>. Saints <a href="/wiki/Seaxburh_of_Ely" title="Seaxburh of Ely">Seaxburh</a>, <a href="/wiki/%C3%86thelburg_of_Faremoutiers" class="mw-redirect" title="Æthelburg of Faremoutiers">Ethelburga</a>, <a href="/wiki/Etheldreda" class="mw-redirect" title="Etheldreda">Etheldreda</a> and <a href="/wiki/Withburga" class="mw-redirect" title="Withburga">Withburga</a> were half-sisters.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
