<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the third century scholar, priest, theologian and antipope also known as Novatus, see <a href="/wiki/Novatian" title="Novatian">Novatian</a>.</div>

<p>Saint <b>Novatus</b> (died c. 151)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> is an early Christian <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. His feast day is 20 June.
</p><p>Novatus and his brother, the martyr Timotheus, were the sons of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Pudens" title="Saint Pudens">Saint Pudens</a>, and the brothers of Saints <a href="/wiki/Pudentiana" title="Pudentiana">Pudentiana</a> and <a href="/wiki/Praxedes" title="Praxedes">Praxedes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His paternal grandfather was <a href="/wiki/Quintus_Cornelius_Pudens" title="Quintus Cornelius Pudens">Quintus Cornelius Pudens</a>, the Roman senator, who with his wife, Priscilla, was among St. Peter's earliest converts in Rome and in whose house the Apostle dwelt while in that city.  A portion of the structure of the modern church of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Pudenziana" title="Santa Pudenziana">Santa Pudenziana</a> (Via Urbana) is thought to be part of the senatorial palace or of the <a href="/wiki/Bathing" title="Bathing">baths</a> built by Novatus.
</p><p>According to the 5th-century church historian <a href="/wiki/Philostorgius" title="Philostorgius">Philostorgius</a>, Novatus was of <a href="/wiki/Phrygians" title="Phrygians">Phrygian</a> descent.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The city of <a href="/wiki/Novato,_California" title="Novato, California">Novato, California</a>, is named after a local <a href="/wiki/Miwok" title="Miwok">Miwok</a> leader who had probably been given the name of Saint Novatus at his baptism.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
