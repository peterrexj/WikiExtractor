					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Italian Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Patricia of Naples</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">seventh century<br />Constantinople<br />(modern-day <a href="/wiki/%C4%B0stanbul" class="mw-redirect" title="İstanbul">İstanbul</a>, <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>)</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">~665 AD<br />Naples, <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">August 25</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">Patronage</a></th><td class="infobox-data">Naples (co-patron)</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Patricia of Naples</b> (or <b>Patricia of Constantinople</b>) (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">Santa Patrizia</i>) (died ca. 665 AD) is an Italian virgin and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>.  Tradition states that she was noble; she may have been related to the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Roman_Emperor" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Roman Emperor">Roman Emperor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Some sources say that she was a descendant of <a href="/wiki/Constantine_the_Great" title="Constantine the Great">Constantine the Great</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Santa_Patrizia_di_Costantinopoli_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Santa_Patrizia_di_Costantinopoli-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The particulars traditional about her are unreliable and in some instances contradictory.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Wishing to escape a <a href="/wiki/Arranged_marriage" title="Arranged marriage">marriage arranged</a> by <a href="/wiki/Constans_II_(Byzantine_Empire)" class="mw-redirect" title="Constans II (Byzantine Empire)">Constans II</a> and become a <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a>, she went to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.  There she received the veil from <a href="/wiki/Pope_Liberius" title="Pope Liberius">Pope Liberius</a>.  Upon the death of her father, she returned to Constantinople and, renouncing any claim to the imperial crown, distributed her wealth to the poor.  She then planned to go on <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a> to <a href="/wiki/Jerusalem" title="Jerusalem">Jerusalem</a>.
</p><p>However, a terrible storm <a href="/wiki/Shipwreck" title="Shipwreck">shipwrecked</a> her on the shores of <a href="/wiki/Naples" title="Naples">Naples</a>.  Finding refuge on the tiny island of <a href="/w/index.php?title=Megarides&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Megarides (page does not exist)">Megarides</a> (the site of the present-day <a href="/wiki/Castel_dell%27Ovo" title="Castel dell&#39;Ovo">Castel dell'Ovo</a>), the site of a small <a href="/wiki/Hermitage_(religious_retreat)" title="Hermitage (religious retreat)">hermitage</a>, Patricia died shortly after from disease.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Venerations">Venerations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Patricia_of_Naples&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Venerations">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> of Sante Patricia contained her <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a>.  During the turbulent events of 1864, they were translated to the monastery of <a href="/wiki/San_Gregorio_Armeno" title="San Gregorio Armeno">San Gregorio Armeno</a>.  Covered in <a href="/wiki/Wax" title="Wax">wax</a>, her relics were contained in an <a href="/wiki/Urn" title="Urn">urn</a> of gold, gems, and silver, and were placed in the monastery's chapel. The monastery also preserves her blood.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>  Her blood, like that of <a href="/wiki/Januarius" title="Januarius">Januarius</a>, the other, more famous <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of Naples, is also said to liquefy  periodically.<sup id="cite_ref-Santa_Patrizia_di_Costantinopoli_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Santa_Patrizia_di_Costantinopoli-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The associated legend states that after Patricia died, a zealous man pulled out one of her teeth, causing the body to <a href="/wiki/Hemorrhage" class="mw-redirect" title="Hemorrhage">hemorrhage</a>.  Patricia's followers collected the blood and exposed it.  It then liquefied.
</p>
