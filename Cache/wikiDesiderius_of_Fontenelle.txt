					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">French saint</div>

</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Waningus</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Count</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">unknown<br /><a href="/wiki/Rouen" title="Rouen">Rouen</a>, Normandy, France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 683</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Anglican_Communion" title="Anglican Communion">Anglican Communion</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">9 January</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Waningus</b> (also <b>Vaneng</b>) (born in Rouen, died c. 683) was a <a href="/wiki/Merovingian_dynasty" title="Merovingian dynasty">Merovingian</a> count and royal official under <a href="/wiki/Clotaire_III" class="mw-redirect" title="Clotaire III">Clotaire III</a>. He assisted Wandrille in establishing Fontenelle Abbey, and later founded Fécamp Abbey. He is recognized as a <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> saint.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Waningus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born in Rouen, Waningus was governor of the <a href="/wiki/Pays_de_Caux" title="Pays de Caux">Pays de Caux</a> in <a href="/wiki/Neustria" title="Neustria">Neustria</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Waningus was both pious, and fond of hunting. He had a particular devotion to <a href="/wiki/Eulalia_of_Barcelona" title="Eulalia of Barcelona">Saint Aulaire</a>. One night he dreamt that she reminded him of the difficulties the rich had in entering heaven. Around 648, he withdrew from court to assist <a href="/wiki/Wandregisel" title="Wandregisel">Wandrille</a> in founding <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint_Wandrille" title="Abbey of Saint Wandrille">Fontenelle Abbey</a>, helping to endow it. Fontenelle followed the <a href="/wiki/Columbanus#Rule_of_Saint_Columbanus" title="Columbanus">Rule of Saint Columbanus</a>.
</p><p>About ten years later, after recovering from a serious illness, Waningus founded the Church of the Holy Trinity and the adjoining <a href="/wiki/F%C3%A9camp_Abbey" title="Fécamp Abbey">Abbaye de la Trinité de Fécamp</a> for nuns. Around 675, the blinded bishop <a href="/wiki/Leodegar" title="Leodegar">Leodegar</a> was sent to Fecamp, where the nuns tended him with care, until in October 678 he was removed at the instance of the <a href="/wiki/Mayor_of_the_palace" title="Mayor of the palace">Mayor of the palace</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ebroin" title="Ebroin">Ebroin</a>, and murdered.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Waningus's relics were moved from Fecamp to the Augustinian house at <a href="/wiki/Ham,_Somme" title="Ham, Somme">Ham</a> in the ninth century.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Desiderius_of_Fontenelle">Desiderius of Fontenelle</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Waningus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Desiderius of Fontenelle">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Desiderius of Fontenelle</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Monk</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 700<br /><a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint_Wandrille" title="Abbey of Saint Wandrille">Fontenelle Abbey</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">18 December</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Waningus entrusted his son, <a href="/wiki/Desiderius_of_Fontenelle" class="mw-redirect" title="Desiderius of Fontenelle">Desiderius</a> (died c. 700)<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> to the abbot of Fontenelle to be educated. Desiderius later became a monk at the abbey. He also is considered a saint. His feast day is kept on 18 December;<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> his <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> are at <a href="/wiki/Ghent" title="Ghent">Ghent</a>, <a href="/wiki/Belgium" title="Belgium">Belgium</a>.
</p>
