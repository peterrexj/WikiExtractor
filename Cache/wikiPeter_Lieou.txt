<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Peter Lieou<br /> 彼得·列欧</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Martyr</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">1760<br /><a href="/wiki/Guizhou" title="Guizhou">Guizhou</a>, <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">17 May 1834<span style="display:none">(1834-05-17)</span> (aged&#160;73–74)<br /><a href="/wiki/Guiyang" title="Guiyang">Guiyang</a>, <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Latin_Church" title="Latin Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">1900, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter%27s_Square" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Peter&#39;s Square">Saint Peter's Square</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City">Vatican City</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIII" title="Pope Leo XIII">Pope Leo XIII</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">Canonized</a></th><td class="infobox-data">1 October 2000, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter%27s_Square" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Peter&#39;s Square">Saint Peter's Square</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City">Vatican City</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">17 May</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Peter Lieou</b> also known as <b>Liu Wenyuan Petrus</b> / <b>Baiduo</b> / <b>Peter Liu Wenyuan</b><sup id="cite_ref-:4_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was Chinese layman, Convert, missionary and a martyr. He was exiled and was strangled to death in a prison for being a christian and preaching Christianity in China.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Lieou&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Lieou was born in <a href="/wiki/Guizhou" title="Guizhou">Guizhou</a>, <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a> in 1760. Peter was a married man who converted to Christianity and then became a catechist in the church.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Persecution">Persecution</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Lieou&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Persecution">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Peter was warned by authorities about his practice of faith and preaching and finally because of his faith he was exiled to <a href="/wiki/Mongolia" title="Mongolia">Mongolia</a> in 1814.  He was allowed to return from exile in 1827 and as soon as he came back, he started working with Catholic missionaries. Many Christians were made prisoners once again with the fresh persecution of Christianity in China. Peter was able to evade arrest initially but was soon caught ministering to prisoners, his sons were also among the other prisoners in the prison. <sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Martyrdom">Martyrdom</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Lieou&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Martyrdom">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Peter was martyred by strangling to death on May 17, 1834 in <a href="/wiki/Guiyang" title="Guiyang">Guiyang</a>, <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Canonization">Canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Lieou&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On 2 July 1899, Peter was venerated and declared a martyr by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIII" title="Pope Leo XIII">Pope Leo XIII</a>. Pope Leo XIII beatified him in 1900. He was finally canonized and declared a saint on 1 October 2000 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
