<p><b>Saint Tathan</b> (also known as <b>Tatheus</b>) is claimed to be a fifth or sixth century <a href="/wiki/Celtic_Christianity" title="Celtic Christianity">Celtic saint</a>, who travelled from <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a> to <a href="/wiki/Wales" title="Wales">Wales</a> where he founded a Christian church. He is reckoned an early abbot of <a href="/wiki/Caerwent" title="Caerwent">Caerwent</a><sup id="cite_ref-Hando_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Hando-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and has dedications at <a href="/wiki/Llanvaches" title="Llanvaches">Llanvaches</a>, near Caerwent, also known as Llandathan,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> and at <a href="/wiki/St_Athan" title="St Athan">St Athan</a>. He is said to have been a teacher of <a href="/wiki/Cadoc" title="Cadoc">Cadoc</a> and to have brought light to the heathens to undo the work of Satan.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Origins">Origins</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Tathan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Origins">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>William Jenkins Rees, in his book <i>Lives of the Cambro British Saints</i>, describes Tathan as the only son of the Irish King Tathetus. Tathan received a good education and devoted himself to spiritual matters. In preference to succeeding his father as king, Tathan followed the advice of an angel and sailed from Ireland to Britain, taking eight disciples with him. His boat sailed up the <a href="/wiki/River_Severn" title="River Severn">River Severn</a> and landed in the medieval <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Gwent" title="Kingdom of Gwent">Kingdom of Gwent</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Cambro_Saints_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cambro_Saints-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Tathan was feted by <a href="/wiki/Caradoc" title="Caradoc">King Caradoc</a> and founded a <a href="/wiki/Monastry" class="mw-redirect" title="Monastry">monastic school</a> at <a href="/wiki/Venta_Silurum" title="Venta Silurum">Venta Silurum</a> (Caerwent). Scholars came from all parts to be instructed there. King <a href="/wiki/Gwynllyw" title="Gwynllyw">Gwynllyw</a> of Gwynllwg sent his seven-year-old son, <a href="/wiki/Cadoc" title="Cadoc">Cadoc</a> to study under Tathan.<sup id="cite_ref-pickering_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-pickering-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> With a donation from Caradoc's son, Ynyr, Tathan then founded a Christian church.<sup id="cite_ref-Cambro_Saints_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cambro_Saints-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
