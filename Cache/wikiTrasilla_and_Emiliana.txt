					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Italian Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saints</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Tarsila and Emiliana</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">sixth century</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>, 	
<a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">24 December, Trasilla; 5 January (Emiliana)</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Trasilla (Tarsila, Tarsilla, Tharsilla, Thrasilla) and Emiliana</b> were aunts of <a href="/wiki/Gregory_the_Great" class="mw-redirect" title="Gregory the Great">Gregory the Great</a>, and venerated as <a href="/wiki/Virgin" class="mw-redirect" title="Virgin">virgin</a> saints of the sixth century.  They appear in the <i>Roman Martyrology</i>, the former on 24 December, the latter on 5 January.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Trasilla_and_Emiliana&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tarsilla (Tharsilla, Trasilla, Thrasilla) and Emiliana (Aemiliana, Emilie) were sisters and came from an ancient Roman noble family, the gens Anicia. Their brother, Senator Gordian, was a very rich patrician with a magnificent townhouse on the <a href="/wiki/Caelian_Hill" title="Caelian Hill">Caelian Hill</a> and large estates in Sicily.<sup id="cite_ref-oslo_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oslo-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Gregory (<i>Hom. XXXVIII</i>, 15, on the Gospel of St. Matthew, and Lib. Dial., IV, 16) relates that his father, the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Senator" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Senator">senator</a> Gordian, had three sisters: Trasilla, Emiliana, and Gordiana.  All three had devoted themselves to a religious life and led a life of virginity, <a href="/wiki/Fasting" title="Fasting">fasting</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Prayer" title="Prayer">prayer</a>. They practiced their faith in their father's house, located on the <a href="/wiki/Clivus_Scauri" title="Clivus Scauri">Clivus Scauri</a> in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Mershman_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mershman-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Gordiana, at first as devout as her sisters, later abandoned this calling and is thus not venerated as a saint.
</p><p>Tradition states that <a href="/wiki/Felix_III" class="mw-redirect" title="Felix III">Felix III</a>, an ancestor, appeared to Trasilla and bade her to enter <a href="/wiki/Heaven" title="Heaven">Heaven</a>, and on the eve of <a href="/wiki/Christmas" title="Christmas">Christmas</a> Trasilla died, seeing <a href="/wiki/Jesus_Christ" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesus Christ">Jesus Christ</a> beckoning.<sup id="cite_ref-Mershman_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mershman-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The legend also states that Trasilla a few days later appeared to Emiliana, inviting her to celebrate <a href="/wiki/Epiphany_(Christian)" class="mw-redirect" title="Epiphany (Christian)">Epiphany</a> in heaven.
</p><p>Tradition says that their <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> and those of their sister-in-law, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Silvia" title="Saint Silvia">Silvia</a>, are in the Oratory of St. Andrew on the <a href="/wiki/Celian_Hill" class="mw-redirect" title="Celian Hill">Celian Hill</a>.
</p>
