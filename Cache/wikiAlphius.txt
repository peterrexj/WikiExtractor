<p>Saints <b>Alphius, Philadelphus and Cyrinus</b> (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">S.S. Alfio, Filadelfo e Cirino</i>), martyrs in the <a href="/wiki/Byzantine_Empire" title="Byzantine Empire">Byzantine</a> traditions of southern Italy,  were three brothers from <a href="/wiki/Poggiardo" title="Poggiardo">Vaste</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Otranto" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Otranto">diocese of Otranto</a>, who died with their mother, Benedicta, during the <a href="/wiki/Persecution_of_Christians" title="Persecution of Christians">persecution</a> of <a href="/wiki/Decius" title="Decius">Decius</a>, ca 251 AD. The details concerning these martyrdoms are traditional, drawn up at a later date in the <a href="/wiki/Benedictine_order" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine order">Benedictine</a> <i>Acta</i> of Saint Alphius.
</p><p>According to the <i>Acta</i>, Alphius, Philadelphus, Cyrinus, ranging in age from nineteen to twenty-two, and their mother Benedicta were arrested with other Christians during the persecutions under Decius. They were taken to <a href="/wiki/Pozzuoli" title="Pozzuoli">Pozzuoli</a>, near Naples, where one of the Christians, Onesimus, was executed. The brothers were taken on to <a href="/wiki/Sicily" title="Sicily">Sicily</a>, where they were martyred at <a href="/wiki/Lentini" title="Lentini">Lentini</a>; there they are among the patron saints. Alphius had his tongue torn from his mouth. Philadelphus was burned on a stake and Cyrinus was boiled alive in oil.  No details of her execution are given for Benedicta. Their <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 10 May in Trecastagni, Sicily and Labor Day Weekend <a href="/wiki/Lawrence,_Massachusetts" title="Lawrence, Massachusetts">Lawrence, Massachusetts</a>, USA.
</p>
