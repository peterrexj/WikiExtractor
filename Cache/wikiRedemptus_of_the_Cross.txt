Redemptus of the Cross, O.C.D.</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:Denis_of_the_Nativity_and_Redemptus_of_the_Cross_cropped.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Denis of the Nativity and Redemptus of the Cross cropped.jpg" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/16/Denis_of_the_Nativity_and_Redemptus_of_the_Cross_cropped.jpg/220px-Denis_of_the_Nativity_and_Redemptus_of_the_Cross_cropped.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="220" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/16/Denis_of_the_Nativity_and_Redemptus_of_the_Cross_cropped.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="232" data-file-height="232" /></a><div class="infobox-caption">A devotional image of the Blesseds Redemptus and Denis (Redemptus on the right)</div></td></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Discalced Carmelite friar and martyr</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">15 March 1598<br />in <a href="/wiki/Paredes_de_Coura" title="Paredes de Coura">Paredes de Coura</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Portugal" title="Kingdom of Portugal">Portugal</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">27 November 1638<span style="display:none">(1638-11-27)</span> (aged&#160;40)<br /><a href="/wiki/Aceh" title="Aceh">Aceh</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data">Roman Catholic Church (Discalced Carmelite Order)</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">10 June 1900 by Pope Leo XIII</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">29 November</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Redemptus of the Cross</b> (also <b>Redemptorus</b>), (15 March 1598 – 27 November 1638) was a <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_people" title="Portuguese people">Portuguese</a> <a href="/wiki/Lay_brother" title="Lay brother">lay brother</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Discalced_Carmelites" title="Discalced Carmelites">Order of Discalced Carmelites</a>. He was put to death along with other members of a group sent to <a href="/wiki/Aceh" title="Aceh">Aceh</a> by Portuguese authorities.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Redemptus_of_the_Cross&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was born <b>Tomás Rodrigues da Cunha</b> in <a href="/wiki/Paredes_de_Coura" title="Paredes de Coura">Paredes de Coura</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Portugal" title="Kingdom of Portugal">Portugal</a> on 15 March 1598.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He first served as a soldier in the Portuguese army in <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_India" title="Portuguese India">India</a>, where he joined the Carmelites in <a href="/wiki/Goa" title="Goa">Goa</a> as a lay brother in 1615, taking the name Redemptus of the Cross.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Redemptus was sent by the superiors of the Order to accompany Father <a href="/wiki/Denis_of_the_Nativity" title="Denis of the Nativity">Denis of the Nativity</a> as part of an <a href="/wiki/Ambassador" title="Ambassador">ambassadorial</a> mission from the <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_Empire" title="Portuguese Empire">Portuguese Empire</a> to the <a href="/wiki/Aceh_Sultanate" title="Aceh Sultanate">Sultan of Aceh</a>. The mission was led by <a href="/wiki/Dom_(title)" class="mw-redirect" title="Dom (title)">Dom</a> Francisco Sousa de Castro as ambassador.
</p><p>Once in Aceh, all the members of the mission were seized and arrested,<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> at the instigation of the <a href="/wiki/Netherlands" title="Netherlands">Dutch</a> authorities based in <a href="/wiki/Jakarta" title="Jakarta">Jakarta</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> They were then subjected to torture, and those members of the mission who refused to deny their faith were executed one by one. The two friars were led to a desolate spot on the seashore, where Redemptus was shot with arrows, after which his throat was slit.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Father Denis, a crucifix in his hands, was the last to die, his skull shattered by a blow of a <a href="/wiki/Scimitar" title="Scimitar">scimitar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Castro, the ambassador, was the only survivor. He was held in captivity for three years, until his family paid a large ransom for his release.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Redemptus_of_the_Cross&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Redemptus of the Cross was beatified on 10 June 1900 by Pope Leo XIII, together with Denis of the Nativity. Their feast day in the <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Calendar of Saints">Calendar of Saints</a> of the Order is the 29 November.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Prayer">Prayer</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Redemptus_of_the_Cross&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Prayer">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><div class="poem">
<p><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">Deus, qui mirabilis dispositione beatos Dionysium et Redemptum per maris pericula ad palmam martyrii perduxisti: eorum intercessione concede; ut, inter mundanas varietates et sæcularia desideria, fideles usque ad mortem in confessione tui nominis maneamus. Per Christum Dominum Nostrum. Amen.</i></span><br />
<br />
O God, Who in Thy wondrous providence, led the blesseds Denis and Redemptus through the perils of the sea to the palm of martyrdom, grant, through their intercession, that in the midst of earthly <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/vicissitude" class="extiw" title="wiktionary:vicissitude">vicissitudes</a> and worldly desires we may remain steadfast even unto death in the confession of Thy name. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
</p>
