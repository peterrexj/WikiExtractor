					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p>

<b>John Bodey</b> (1549&#160;&#8211;&#32;2 November 1583)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> was an English <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> academic jurist and lay theologian. He was martyred in 1583, and beatified in 1929.
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Blessed</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">John Bodey</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">Martyr</a></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 1549<br /><a href="/wiki/Wells,_Somerset" title="Wells, Somerset">Wells, Somerset</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">2 November 1583 (aged 33 - 34)<br /><a href="/wiki/Winchester" title="Winchester">Winchester</a>, <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Honored&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholicism">Roman Catholicism</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">15 December 1929 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">2 November</td></tr></tbody></table>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Bodey&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>John Bodey was born in <a href="/wiki/Wells,_Somerset" title="Wells, Somerset">Wells</a>, <a href="/wiki/Somerset" title="Somerset">Somerset</a>, in 1549. His father was a wealthy merchant.<sup id="cite_ref-wainewright_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-wainewright-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He studied at <a href="/wiki/Winchester_College" title="Winchester College">Winchester College</a> and <a href="/wiki/New_College,_Oxford" title="New College, Oxford">New College, Oxford</a>, of which he became a Fellow in 1568 and took an M.A. degree in February 1576. In June 1576, he and seven others were deprived of their fellowships by the <a href="/wiki/Visitor" title="Visitor">visitor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Robert_Horne_(bishop)" title="Robert Horne (bishop)">Robert Horne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Winchester" title="Bishop of Winchester">Bishop of Winchester</a>, and expelled.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Camm_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Camm-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The following year he went to <a href="/wiki/Douay_College" class="mw-redirect" title="Douay College">Douay College</a> to study <a href="/wiki/Civil_law_(legal_system)" title="Civil law (legal system)">civil law</a>, but returned to England in February 1578. Upon his return, he became a schoolmaster in Hampshire. Arrested in 1580, he was kept in iron shackles in <a href="/wiki/Winchester" title="Winchester">Winchester</a> gaol, and was condemned in April, 1583, together with John Slade, a schoolmaster, for <a href="/wiki/High_treason" class="mw-redirect" title="High treason">high treason</a> by denying the <a href="/wiki/Royal_Supremacy" class="mw-redirect" title="Royal Supremacy">Royal Supremacy</a>. There was apparently a feeling that this sentence was unjust and illegal, and they were retried—and condemned again—at <a href="/wiki/Andover,_Hampshire" title="Andover, Hampshire">Andover, Hampshire</a>, in August 1583.<sup id="cite_ref-Camm_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Camm-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Bodey had a controversy with <a href="/wiki/Lawrence_Humphrey" title="Lawrence Humphrey">Lawrence Humphrey</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dean_of_Winchester" title="Dean of Winchester">Dean of Winchester</a>, on the <a href="/wiki/First_Council_of_Nicaea" title="First Council of Nicaea">Nicene Council</a>, and his notes from Eusebius still exist. After his second trial, he wrote from prison to Dr. <a href="/wiki/Humphrey_Ely" title="Humphrey Ely">Humphrey Ely</a>, "We consider that iron for this cause borne on earth shall surmount gold and, precious stones in Heaven. That is our mark, that is our desire. In the mean season we are threatened daily, and do look still when the hurdle shall be brought to the door. I beseech you, for God's sake, that we want not the good prayers of you all for our strength, our joy, and our perseverance unto the end. ... From our school of patience the 16th September, 1583."<sup id="cite_ref-Camm_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Camm-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>
Bodey was <a href="/wiki/Hanged,_drawn_and_quartered" title="Hanged, drawn and quartered">hanged, drawn and quartered</a> at Andover on 2 November 1583. At his execution, he said:<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> <style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style></p><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>Indeede, I have been sufficiently censured, for I have been condemned twice; if you may make the hearing of a Blessed Mass treason, or the saying of an Ave Maria treason, you may make what you please treason... I acknowledge her as my Lawful Queen in all temporal causes, and none other... Ye shall understand, good people all, I suffer death not for not granting her Majestie to be supreme head of Christ's Church in England, which I may not and will not grant; I pray God long to preserve her Majestie in tranquility over you, even Queen Elizabeth, your queen and mine; I desire you to obey none other.</p></blockquote>
<p>Bodey's brother Gilbert was arrested with <a href="/wiki/Alexander_Briant" title="Alexander Briant">Alexander Briant</a> on 28 April 1581. He was <a href="/wiki/Scourged" class="mw-redirect" title="Scourged">scourged</a> at <a href="/wiki/Bridewell_Palace" title="Bridewell Palace">Bridewell</a> and afterwards confined at one of the <a href="/wiki/Compter" title="Compter">Counter Prisons</a>. He was released on bond, and when not called to appear, escaped to Rheims.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="John_Slade">John Slade</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Bodey&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: John Slade">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Blessed</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">John Slade</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">Martyr</a></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">30 October 1583<br /><a href="/wiki/Winchester" title="Winchester">Winchester</a>, <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Honored&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholicism">Roman Catholicism</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">15 December 1929 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">30 October</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Like Bodey, Slade attended the New College, Oxford until, being expelled for being Catholic, he traveled to Douai to take the study of law. However, since as a Catholic he was not permitted to practice, he became a tutor in a gentleman's household in Dorset. Slade was eventually arrested and confined with Bodey at Winchester. Slade was hanged, drawn, and quartered at Winchester 30 October 1583.
</p>
