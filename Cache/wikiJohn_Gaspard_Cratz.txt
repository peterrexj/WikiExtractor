<p><b>Emmanuel d'Abreu</b> was a Roman Catholic priest who joined the <a href="/wiki/Society_of_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Society of Jesus">Jesuit order</a> in 1724. He was deputed to <a href="/wiki/Goa" title="Goa">Goa</a> in 1733 and then in <a href="/wiki/Macau" title="Macau">Macau</a> in 1734.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> In March 1736, he was arrested in the kingdom of <a href="/wiki/Tonkin" title="Tonkin">Tonkin</a> (today's <a href="/wiki/Vietnam" title="Vietnam">Vietnam</a>). He was tortured and martyred by beheading for his work in the following year with three of his companions, <b>John Gaspard Cratz</b>, <b>Bartholomew Alvarez</b> and
<b>Vincent da Cunha</b>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Their memorial is on 12 January, the date of their execution.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
