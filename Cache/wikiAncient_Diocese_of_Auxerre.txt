<p>The <b>diocese of Auxerre</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">dioecesis Antissiodorensis</i>) is a former French Roman Catholic diocese. Its historical episcopal see was in the city of <a href="/wiki/Auxerre" title="Auxerre">Auxerre</a> in <a href="/wiki/Burgundy_(region)" class="mw-redirect" title="Burgundy (region)">Burgundy</a>, now part of eastern France. Currently the non-metropolitan Archbishop of Sens, ordinary of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Sens" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Sens">diocese of Sens and Auxerre</a>, resides in Auxerre.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Ecclesiastical_history">Ecclesiastical history</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Auxerre&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Ecclesiastical history">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <i>Gesta pontificum Autissiodorensium</i>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;a&#93;</a></sup> written about 875 by the <a href="/wiki/Canon_(priest)" class="mw-redirect" title="Canon (priest)">canons</a> Rainogala and Alagus, and later continued up to 1278, gives a list of bishops of Auxerre.  <a href="/wiki/Louis_Duchesne" title="Louis Duchesne">Louis Duchesne</a> regards the list as mostly accurate, but very arbitrary in its dates prior to the 7th century. Auxerre is remarkable among French churches for the number of its bishops who have come to be regarded as saints.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CE_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bishops_of_the_original_Gesta">Bishops of the original <i>Gesta</i></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Auxerre&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Bishops of the original Gesta">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><a href="/wiki/Peregrine_of_Auxerre" title="Peregrine of Auxerre">St. Peregrinus</a> (Pélérin 'pilgrim') was the founder of the See of Auxerre; according to the legend, he was sent by Pope <a href="/wiki/Sixtus_II" class="mw-redirect" title="Sixtus II">Sixtus II</a> and was martyred under Emperor <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a> in 303 or 304.<sup id="cite_ref-CE_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After Peregrine, the original 870s <i>Gesta</i> list:<sup id="cite_ref-CE_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>

<p>On November 29, 1801, the diocese of Auxerre was suppressed. On October 7, 1817, it was restored, but in 1821 it was suppressed again. On June 3, 1823, it was united once more to the diocese of Sens. The newly united diocese soon became an archdiocese, but after many years, in 2006, which in turn lost its Metropolitan status in 2006 and became a suffragan see of the Ecclesiastical Province of the <a href="/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Dijon" class="mw-redirect" title="Archdiocese of Dijon">Dijon</a>.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Auxerre_Cathedral" title="Auxerre Cathedral">Cathedral of Auxerre</a>, completed in 1178, contains numerous sculptures in the Byzantine style.<sup id="cite_ref-CE_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
