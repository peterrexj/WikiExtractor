<p><b>Francesco Spoto</b> (8 July 1924 – 27 December 1964) was an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> who served in the missions in the <a href="/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Congo" class="mw-redirect" title="Democratic Republic of Congo">Democratic Republic of Congo</a> and was killed there. He was also a professed member from the Missionary Servants of the Poor.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His beatification was celebrated in <a href="/wiki/Palermo" title="Palermo">Palermo</a> on 21 April 2007 after <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI" title="Pope Benedict XVI">Pope Benedict XVI</a> approved the fact that the priest had been killed "in odium fidei" – in hatred of the faith.<sup id="cite_ref-ZN_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ZN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Salvatore_De_Giorgi" title="Salvatore De Giorgi">Salvatore De Giorgi</a> presided over the celebration on the behalf of the pope.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francesco_Spoto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Francesco Spoto was born in <a href="/wiki/Raffadali" title="Raffadali">Raffadali</a> in mid-1924 and he was <a href="/wiki/Baptized" class="mw-redirect" title="Baptized">baptized</a> on 24 September 1924. His cousin was a <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a>.
</p><p>Spoto commenced his studies for the priesthood in <a href="/wiki/Palermo" title="Palermo">Palermo</a> in 1936 and he later became a professed member of the Missionary Servants of the Poor. He received his <a href="/wiki/Ordination" title="Ordination">ordination</a> to the priesthood from Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Ernesto_Ruffini" title="Ernesto Ruffini">Ernesto Ruffini</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Palermo_Cathedral" title="Palermo Cathedral">Palermo Cathedral</a> on 22 July 1951.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Father Spoto was later elected as the Superior General of his order at its General Chapter in 1959 in a move that surprised him and he later wrote of his shock to a priest cousin.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Sporo – in his role as the Superior General – made the petition to see the introduction of the cause for canonization of the order's founder <a href="/wiki/Giacomo_Cusmano" title="Giacomo Cusmano">Giacomo Cusmano</a>. He also re-vitalized the order and encouraged vocations.
</p><p>On 4 August 1964 he travelled to Biringi to visit the missions in the <a href="/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Congo" class="mw-redirect" title="Democratic Republic of Congo">Democratic Republic of Congo</a>. On 3 December 1964 the Simba rebels captured him along with four other missionaries and on 11 December was beaten and tortured until he managed to escape barefoot though later collapsed due to exhaustion; he forgave his killers and offered his life to <a href="/wiki/God" title="God">God</a>. He died due to his injuries on 27 December 1964 after <a href="/wiki/Christmas" title="Christmas">Christmas</a>; the other four missionaries were saved at some stage.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His remains were later moved to Palermo on 14 October 1984.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francesco_Spoto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The beatification process opened on 5 June 1992 after he was titled as a <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> issued the official "<a href="/wiki/Nihil_obstat" title="Nihil obstat">nihil obstat</a>" to the cause. The diocesan process spanned from 16 December 1992 until 18 March 1996 and received C.C.S. validation on 20 December 1996 in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>. The postulation submitted the <a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">Positio</a> to Roman officials in 1998.
</p><p>The board of theologians approved the cause on 4 October 2005 while the cardinal and bishop members of the C.C.S. likewise approved it on 4 April 2006. <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI" title="Pope Benedict XVI">Pope Benedict XVI</a> approved the beatification on 26 June 2006 and delegated Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Salvatore_De_Giorgi" title="Salvatore De Giorgi">Salvatore De Giorgi</a> to preside over the celebration in the Palermo Cathedral on 21 April 2007. In attendance was the then-Archbishop (now Cardinal) <a href="/wiki/Paolo_Romeo" title="Paolo Romeo">Paolo Romeo</a> and Bishop Marcel Utembi Tapa – of the diocese where Spoto was killed. His beatification was also the first to occur in Palermo.<sup id="cite_ref-ZN_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ZN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> His liturgical feast was affixed to 24 September – the date of his baptism – instead of the date of his death as is the norm.
</p><p>The current <a href="/wiki/Postulator" title="Postulator">postulator</a> for the cause is Bishop Vincenzo Bertolone.
</p>
