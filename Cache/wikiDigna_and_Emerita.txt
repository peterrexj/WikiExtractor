<p>Saints <b>Digna and Emerita</b> (died 259 AD) are venerated as <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.  They were Roman maidens seized and put to the torture as <a href="/wiki/Christians" title="Christians">Christians</a> in the persecution of Valerian (A.D. 254-A.D. 259) at Rome.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Their <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is celebrated on September 22.
</p><p>Their <a href="/wiki/Relic" title="Relic">relics</a> are said to lie at the church of <a href="/wiki/San_Marcello_al_Corso" title="San Marcello al Corso">San Marcello al Corso</a>, in Rome, although it is recorded that on April 5, 838, a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> named Felix appeared at <a href="/wiki/Fulda" title="Fulda">Fulda</a> with the remains of Saints <a href="/wiki/Cornelius_the_Centurion" title="Cornelius the Centurion">Cornelius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Callixtus_I" title="Pope Callixtus I">Callistus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Agapetus_I" title="Pope Agapetus I">Agapitus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Saint_George" title="Saint George">Georgius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vincent_of_Saragossa" title="Vincent of Saragossa">Vincentius</a>, Maximus, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Cecilia" title="Saint Cecilia">Cecilia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eugenia_of_Rome" title="Eugenia of Rome">Eugenia</a>, Digna, Emerita, and Columbana.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
