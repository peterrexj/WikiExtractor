font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Elzéar of Sabran</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">1285<br />Provence, France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">27 September 1323<br />Paris, France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">Canonized</a></th><td class="infobox-data">15 April 1369 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Urban_V" title="Pope Urban V">Pope Urban V</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/27_September" class="mw-redirect" title="27 September">27 September</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Saint_symbolism" title="Saint symbolism">Attributes</a></th><td class="infobox-data">crown, royal attire</td></tr></tbody></table>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Ansouis_5.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/Ansouis_5.jpg/250px-Ansouis_5.jpg" decoding="async" width="250" height="188" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/Ansouis_5.jpg/375px-Ansouis_5.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/Ansouis_5.jpg/500px-Ansouis_5.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1200" /></a><figcaption>Reliquaries of St. Elzéar and Bl. Delphine<br />in the Franciscan church of Ansouis, France</figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Elzéar of Sabran, <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_St._Francis" class="mw-redirect" title="Third Order of St. Francis">TOSF</a></b>, Baron of <a href="/wiki/Ansouis" title="Ansouis">Ansouis</a>, Count of <a href="/wiki/Ariano_Irpino" title="Ariano Irpino">Ariano</a>, was born in the castle of Saint-Jean-de-Robians, near <a href="/wiki/Cabri%C3%A8res-d%27Aigues" title="Cabrières-d&#39;Aigues">Cabrières-d'Aigues</a> in <a href="/wiki/Provence" title="Provence">Provence</a>, southern <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>, in 1285. He died in <a href="/wiki/Paris" title="Paris">Paris</a>, <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>, on September 27, 1323. He was a <a href="/wiki/Third_order" title="Third order">tertiary</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Franciscans" title="Franciscans">Franciscan Order</a> as well as a ruler, <a href="/wiki/Diplomat" title="Diplomat">diplomat</a> and military leader. He was recognised as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the Catholic Church.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Elz%C3%A9ar_of_Sabran&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In his youth, Elzéar was given a thorough training in the Christian faith as well as in the sciences under the supervision of his uncle, William of Sabran, at the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_St._Victor,_Marseille" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of St. Victor, Marseille">Abbey of St. Victor</a> in <a href="/wiki/Marseille" title="Marseille">Marseille</a>, where his uncle ruled as the <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">Abbot</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Carr_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carr-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Acceding to the wish of King <a href="/wiki/Charles_II_of_Naples" title="Charles II of Naples">Charles II of Naples</a>, at the age of ten, Elzéar was betrothed to the twelve-year-old <a href="/wiki/Delphine_of_Gland%C3%A8ves" title="Delphine of Glandèves">Delphine of Glandèves</a>, daughter and heiress of the Count of Puy-Michel. Three years later they were married at the castle of Puy-Michel. Upon their wedding night, Delphine advised her new husband that she had taken a private <a href="/wiki/Vow" title="Vow">vow</a> of <a href="/wiki/Chastity" title="Chastity">chastity</a>. Having grown up together, they regarded each other as brother and sister, rather than husband and wife.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Even though he had the right in <a href="/wiki/Canon_law" title="Canon law">canon law</a> to make her abandon this commitment, Elzéar chose to respect her desire to live in virginity and even copied her example in making the same vow. Together they joined the <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_Saint_Francis" title="Third Order of Saint Francis">Third Order of Saint Francis</a>. 
</p><p>Elzéar and Delphine lived at a castle in <a href="/wiki/Ansouis" title="Ansouis">Ansouis</a> where they vied with one another in the practice of <a href="/wiki/Prayer" title="Prayer">prayer</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mortification_of_the_flesh" title="Mortification of the flesh">mortification of the flesh</a> and in <a href="/wiki/Charity_(practice)" title="Charity (practice)">charity</a> towards the unfortunate. At the age of twenty he moved with his wife from Ansouis to <a href="/wiki/Puimichel" title="Puimichel">Puimichel</a> for greater solitude, and formulated for his servants rules of conduct that made his household a model of Christian virtue.<sup id="cite_ref-Carr_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carr-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Upon the death of his father in 1309, became Count of Ariano in Italy. There he gained by kindness the trust and support of his subjects, who had despised their <a href="/wiki/Normans" title="Normans">Norman</a> conquerors. In 1312 he marched to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> at the head of the army of King <a href="/wiki/Robert_of_Naples" class="mw-redirect" title="Robert of Naples">Robert of Naples</a>, which was mobilized to aid in expelling the <a href="/wiki/Emperor_Henry_VII" class="mw-redirect" title="Emperor Henry VII">Emperor Henry VII</a> from that city.<sup id="cite_ref-Carr_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Carr-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Returning to Provence after the war, he again set up a household in which <a href="/wiki/Piety" title="Piety">piety</a> and faithful practice of the Catholic faith were expected of all the members of his house.
</p><p>In 1317 Elzéar went to <a href="/wiki/Naples" title="Naples">Naples</a> to become the <a href="/wiki/Tutor" class="mw-redirect" title="Tutor">tutor</a> of <a href="/wiki/Charles,_Duke_of_Calabria" title="Charles, Duke of Calabria">Duke Charles</a>, son of King Robert, and later became Charles' <a href="/wiki/Castellan" title="Castellan">castellan</a>, when Charles became <a href="/wiki/Vicar" title="Vicar">Vicar</a> General of the <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Sicily" title="Kingdom of Sicily">Kingdom of Sicily</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was sent as ambassador to the <a href="/wiki/King_of_France" class="mw-redirect" title="King of France">King of France</a> in 1323 to obtain the hand of <a href="/wiki/Marie_of_Valois_(1309-1332)" class="mw-redirect" title="Marie of Valois (1309-1332)">Marie of Valois</a> in marriage for Charles, edifying a worldly court by his <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtues</a>. While serving in that post he died, shortly after accomplishing his mission.
</p><p>His body was returned to his <a href="/wiki/Territory_(country_subdivision)" class="mw-redirect" title="Territory (country subdivision)">domain</a>, and he was buried in the Franciscan <a href="/wiki/Religious_habit" title="Religious habit">habit</a> in the church of the <a href="/wiki/Friars_Minor" class="mw-redirect" title="Friars Minor">Friars Minor</a> at <a href="/wiki/Apt,_Vaucluse" title="Apt, Vaucluse">Apt, Vaucluse</a>. The tomb was destroyed during the French Revolution.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The decree of his <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonization</a> was signed by his <a href="/wiki/Godson" class="mw-redirect" title="Godson">godson</a>, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Urban_V" title="Pope Urban V">Pope Urban V</a>, and was carried out by his successor, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Gregory_XI" title="Pope Gregory XI">Pope Gregory XI</a> about 1371. <a href="/wiki/Countess" class="mw-redirect" title="Countess">Countess</a> Delfine was also <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by Pope Urban at that time. Their <a href="/wiki/Liturgy" title="Liturgy">liturgical</a> <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a>, which they share, is celebrated by the Franciscan Third Order on 26 September.
