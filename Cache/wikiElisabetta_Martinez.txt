<p><b>Elisabetta "Elisa" Martinez</b> <span style="font-size:85%;"><a href="/w/index.php?title=Daughters_of_St._Mary_of_Leuca&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Daughters of St. Mary of Leuca (page does not exist)">DML</a></span> (25 March 1905 – 8 February 1991) was an <a href="/wiki/Italians" title="Italians">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a> and founder of the congregation of the Daughters of Saint Mary of Leuca.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> She was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on June 25, 2023 in <a href="/wiki/Lecce" title="Lecce">Lecce</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Elisabetta_Martinez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born in <a href="/wiki/Galatina" title="Galatina">Galatina</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Italy" title="Kingdom of Italy">Kingdom of Italy</a>, on March 25, 1905, she was the eldest of eight children of Giacoma Martinez and Francesca Rizzelli.<sup id="cite_ref-auto1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>She grew up in a Catholic family thanks to her mother. Her father, however, disagreed about her initiation into the Catholic faith, but she was baptized with her consent on April 16, 1905, in the Parish of the Holy Apostles Peter and Paul, in Galatina. Despite disagreements with his father, in April 1928 she entered religious life, choosing the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_of_Our_Lady_of_Charity_of_the_Good_Shepherd" title="Congregation of Our Lady of Charity of the Good Shepherd">Sisters of Our Lady of Piety of the Good Shepherd</a>. After her admission, she moved to the house of the congregation, located in <a href="/wiki/Chieti" title="Chieti">Chieti</a>, in April 1928. On September 29 of the same year, she was admitted to the religious habit. On September 29 of the following year, she made her first profession of vows, taking the religious name of <i>Lucia</i>. However, due to a severe lung infection, she had to leave the institute.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On March 20, 1938, with the help of the parish priest of <a href="/wiki/Miggiano" title="Miggiano">Miggiano</a>, Father Luigi Cosi, and with the consent of the bishop of <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Ugento-Santa_Maria_di_Leuca" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Ugento-Santa Maria di Leuca">Ugento</a>, Giuseppe Ruotolo, she founded the Pious Union of Sisters of the Immaculate Conception. On August 15, 1941, the union was recognized as an institute. However, she decided to rename it the congregation of the Daughters of St. Mary of Leuca, thus honoring of the Marian shrine in the city of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_di_Leuca" title="Santa Maria di Leuca">Leuca</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In later years, Elisabetta toured throughout <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a> and abroad, thus strengthening her congregation and resisting trials and slander. She worked as Superior General for most of her life, resigning in 1987. She died in the general house of the Daughters of St. Mary of Leuca, located in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, on February 8, 1991. Her remains currently rest in the General House on <a href="/wiki/Via_Tiberina" title="Via Tiberina">Via Tiberina</a>, Rome.<sup id="cite_ref-auto1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Elisabetta_Martinez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The process of his <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> began on July 29, 2016, in the Diocese of Leuca. The Roman phase of the trial began on September 14, 2018. On April 20, 2021, the Peculiar Congress of Theological Consultors obtained a positive result for the cause, followed by the approval of the Plenary Assembly of member Cardinals and Bishops of the <a href="/wiki/Dicastery_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" title="Dicastery for the Causes of Saints">Dicastery for the Causes of Saints</a> on September 28, 2021.<sup id="cite_ref-auto1_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> On October 13, 2021, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> issued the decree recognizing Martinez's <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtues</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> On February 23, 2023, a miracle attributed to her intercession was recognized.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Her <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> took place on June 25, 2023. The ceremony was presided over by <a href="/wiki/Cardinal_(Catholic_Church)" title="Cardinal (Catholic Church)">Cardinal</a> <a href="/wiki/Marcello_Semeraro" title="Marcello Semeraro">Marcello Semeraro</a> in Piazza Giovanni XXIII, in front of the Sanctuary of Santa Maria de Leuca.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
