<p><b>Artaldus</b>, also known as <b>Arthaud</b>, was a 13th-century <a href="/wiki/Carthusian" class="mw-redirect" title="Carthusian">Carthusian</a> <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Belley" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Belley">Bishop of Belley</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Artaldus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born in the castle of Sothonod in <a href="/wiki/Savoy" title="Savoy">Savoy</a>, in 1101. Much of his childhood is not known but at the age of eighteen, Artaldus entered the court of <a href="/wiki/Amadeus_III,_Count_of_Savoy" title="Amadeus III, Count of Savoy">Duke Amadeus III</a>, but after a year or so he left to become a <a href="/wiki/Priesthood_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood (Catholic Church)">priest</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_religious_life">Early religious life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Artaldus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early religious life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Artaldus entered the <a href="/wiki/Carthusian" class="mw-redirect" title="Carthusian">Carthusian</a> house of Portes Charterhouse in modern-day <a href="/wiki/B%C3%A9nonces" title="Bénonces">Bénonces</a>. There he was ordained a <a href="/wiki/Priesthood_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood (Catholic Church)">priest</a>. He spent many years serving as a priest before being sent by the <a href="/wiki/Prior" title="Prior">prior</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Grande_Chartreuse" title="Grande Chartreuse">Grande Chartreuse</a> to found a charterhouse near a valley in the <a href="/wiki/Valromey" class="mw-redirect" title="Valromey">Valromey</a>, a place that was known as "the cemetery". Artaldus decided to take with him six fellow priests from the Portes Charterhouse to establish this new community. The community had to move when the newly built charterhouse buildings were ravaged by fire. Artaldus chose a fresh site on the Arvières River, and the Arvières Charterhouse was founded and dedicated to <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary_(Roman_Catholic)" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary (Roman Catholic)">Our Lady</a>, in 1132.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Appointment_as_Bishop_of_Belley">Appointment as Bishop of Belley</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Artaldus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Appointment as Bishop of Belley">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the years spent at Arvière, Artaldus gained considerable fame and a great reputation, like that of <a href="/wiki/Bruno_of_Cologne" title="Bruno of Cologne">St. Bruno</a>, his master. Similar to <a href="/wiki/Bruno_of_Cologne" title="Bruno of Cologne">St. Bruno</a>, Artaldus was called from his <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a>, to accept the role of serving as a bishop. This was at the <a href="/wiki/See_of_Belley" class="mw-redirect" title="See of Belley">See of Belley</a>. Artaldus, who was over eighty when called to the post, less than two years later he resigned and returned to Arvières.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Later_life_and_death">Later life and death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Artaldus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Later life and death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In his later years Artaldus was visited by <a href="/wiki/Hugh_of_Lincoln" title="Hugh of Lincoln">Hugh of Lincoln</a>, who had convinced King <a href="/wiki/Henry_II_of_England" title="Henry II of England">Henry II of England</a> to become a benefactor of the charterhouse at Arvières. Artaldus who live the remainder of his days at Arvières, living until the age of 105, he died in 1206. His cultus was confirmed in 1834, by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Gregory_XVI" title="Pope Gregory XVI">Pope Gregory XVI</a>.
</p>
