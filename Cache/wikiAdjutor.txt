<p><b>Adjutor</b> (died April 30, 1131) is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>. He is credited to be the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Human_swimming" class="mw-redirect" title="Human swimming">swimmers</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sailor" title="Sailor">boaters</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Drowning" title="Drowning">drowning</a> victims,<sup id="cite_ref-Catholic_Forum_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Catholic_Forum-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and the patron saint of Vernon, France.  Adjutor was born in <a href="/wiki/Vernon,_Eure" title="Vernon, Eure">Vernon</a>, <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>, on July 24, 1073, where he was made a <a href="/wiki/Knight" title="Knight">knight</a> in the <a href="/wiki/First_Crusade" title="First Crusade">First Crusade</a>. The stories given for his patronage of boaters vary, though one common account was that Adjutor was captured by <a href="/wiki/Muslim" class="mw-redirect" title="Muslim">Muslims</a> during the First Crusade, who tried to force him to abandon his faith, and when refusing, he escaped persecution by swimming.<sup id="cite_ref-Catholic_Forum_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Catholic_Forum-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He swam back to France and entered the Abbey of Trion. There he became a recluse until his death on April 30.
</p><p>Additional legends state that it was <a href="/wiki/Angel" title="Angel">angels</a> who freed Adjutor from his captors, and his association with the seas came when he calmed a <a href="/wiki/Whirlpool" title="Whirlpool">whirlpool</a> by throwing <a href="/wiki/Holy_water" title="Holy water">Holy water</a> and the chains of his captivity into it, and <a href="/wiki/Sign_of_the_cross" title="Sign of the cross">signing the cross</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In his later life he became a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>.
</p>
