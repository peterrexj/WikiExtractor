					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">8th century Irish monk and saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the 7th century High King of Ireland, see <a href="/wiki/Blathmac_mac_%C3%81edo_Sl%C3%A1ine" title="Blathmac mac Áedo Sláine">Blathmac mac Áedo Sláine</a>.</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Blathmac</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><span title="circa">c.</span> 750 AD<br /><a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data"><span title="circa">c.</span> 825<br /><a href="/wiki/Iona" title="Iona">Iona</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Blathmac</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Blathmacus, Florentius</i>) was a distinguished Irish monk, born in <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a> about 750 AD. He is known as "Blathmac, son of Flann", to distinguish him from the poet and monk <a href="/wiki/Blathmac_mac_Con_Brettan" title="Blathmac mac Con Brettan">Blathmac mac Con Brettan</a>.
</p><p>He was killed and became a martyr in <a href="/wiki/Iona" title="Iona">Iona</a>, about 825.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  His biography was written by <a href="/wiki/Walafrid_Strabo" title="Walafrid Strabo">Strabo</a>, the Benedictine Abbot of Reichenau (824–849), and thus the story of his martyrdom has been handed down.<sup id="cite_ref-Folklore_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Folklore-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Catholic_dictionary_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Catholic_dictionary-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blathmac&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Blathmac, the <a href="/wiki/Lineal_descendant" title="Lineal descendant">scion</a> of a noble family, early showed a religious turn of mind. Strabo describes him as a warrior prince, and "a future king of his people" who gave up his patrimony to enter religious life.<sup id="cite_ref-Breen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breen-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His name was <a href="/wiki/Latinization_of_names" class="mw-redirect" title="Latinization of names">Latinized</a> <i>Florentius</i> (from the Irish word <i><a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bl%C3%A1th#Old_Irish" class="extiw" title="wikt:bláth">bláth</a></i> meaning "flower").
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career">Career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blathmac&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Career">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He became the <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> of an Irish monastery, and in 824 left to join the community of <a href="/wiki/Columban" class="mw-redirect" title="Columban">Columban</a> monks at <a href="/wiki/Iona" title="Iona">Iona</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was serving as prior or acting abbot in the absence of <a href="/wiki/Diarmait_of_Iona" title="Diarmait of Iona">Diarmait of Iona</a>, who had taken most of the community to <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Kells" title="Abbey of Kells">Kells</a> for safety. Anticipating yet another viking raid, Blathmac had what remained of the relics of Columba hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-Breen_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Breen-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
Not long afterwards the <a href="/wiki/Danes_(Germanic_tribe)" class="mw-redirect" title="Danes (Germanic tribe)">Danes</a> ravaged the island. One morning, as he was celebrating <a href="/wiki/Mass_(liturgy)" title="Mass (liturgy)">mass</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Scandinavia" title="Scandinavia">Scandinavian</a> rovers entered the monastic church. Blathmac refused to point out the shrine of St. Columba, which was the object of plunder. He was hacked to pieces on the altar step.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His body was afterwards reverently interred at the scene. Miracles are claimed to have been wrought through his intercession. The date of his death is given by the "<a href="/wiki/Annals_of_Ulster" title="Annals of Ulster">Annals of Ulster</a>" as 825, although <a href="/wiki/Mabillon" class="mw-redirect" title="Mabillon">Mabillon</a> places it thirty-six years earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
