<p><b>Luigi Maria Palazzolo</b> (10 December 1827 – 15 June 1886) was an Italian <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a>. He established the <a href="/wiki/Sisters_of_the_Poor,_Palazzolo_Institute" title="Sisters of the Poor, Palazzolo Institute">Sisters of the Poor</a> which was also known as the Palazzolo Institute. Other contributions include the construction of an orphanage for children in <a href="/wiki/Traona" title="Traona">Traona</a> and also the Little House of Divine Providence. He also worked for the poor and the outcast until his death.
</p><p>Palazzolo was beatified on 19 March 1963 on the account of his well-known holiness and is considered a patron of the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Bergamo" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Bergamo">Diocese of Bergamo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> approved a miracle attributed to him on 28 November 2019 and canonized Palazzolo as a saint on 15 May 2022.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Luigi_Maria_Palazzolo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Luigi Maria Palazzolo was born in <a href="/wiki/Bergamo" title="Bergamo">Bergamo</a> on 10 December 1827 as the last of eight brothers to Octavius Palazzolo and Theresa Antoine. He had a childhood of lavishness in the sense that his parents could provide for their children to a manageable degree and owned land and houses across Bergamo and in <a href="/wiki/San_Pellegrino_Terme" title="San Pellegrino Terme">San Pellegrino Terme</a> where his father hailed from. During this time Palazzolo's brothers died and his father died on 8 August 1837. Due to his naïve disposition as a child and his wit he was often dubbed "Palazzolino" during his life.
</p><p>Palazzolo commenced his studies for the <a href="/wiki/Priesthood" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood">priesthood</a> in 1844 and on 23 June 1850 he was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> to the priesthood under the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Bergamo" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Bergamo">Bishop of Bergamo</a> Gritti Morlacchi. He became involved in the apostolate of San Alessandro in Colonna and then in the church of San Bernardino of which in 1855 he became rector. Personal loss struck once more on 10 September 1862 with the death of his mother. On 4 October 1872 he established an orphanage in <a href="/wiki/Traona" title="Traona">Traona</a>: this institution became extinct in 1928.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1864 Palazzolo travelled along a lane in a popular suburb and encountered a half-naked child who was an orphan. Palazzolo wrapped his cloak around him and took him with him to clean and feed him. He kept the child to care for him to ensure he did not remain abandoned. He became a member of a Catholic group in 1868 and he offered rooms for meetings. In 1869 he came across a crippled and lame girl – he took her in also and enrolled her into the new women's religious institute that he had founded. He recorded in his journal that he attempted "to do something as much as I can" to ensure others could have an easier life. One of the people he encountered and befriended was Maria Teresa Gabrieli – the future <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> – who became a member of his order.
</p><p>Palazzolo's health started to decline in 1885 with a wheeze for breath that prevented him from proper sleep and compelled him to sleep in a large chair. He later had large sores grow in the lower limbs and he was unable to celebrate Mass. His comfort came from Bishop Gaetano Camillo Guindani who visited him at 4:30pm on 12 May 1885 to bring him the approved rules of the institute. Yet on one particular night he slipped from his bed and remained on the ground for three hours as he was unable to get back up.
</p><p>Palazzolo died in the first hours of 15 June 1886 while murmuring the name of <a href="/wiki/Jesus_Christ" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesus Christ">Jesus Christ</a>. His remains were transferred on 4 January 1904 from the cemetery of San Giorgio to the Mother House of the institute.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Post-mortem">Post-mortem</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Luigi_Maria_Palazzolo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Post-mortem">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bishop <a href="/wiki/Giacomo_Radini-Tedeschi" title="Giacomo Radini-Tedeschi">Giacomo Radini-Tedeschi</a> – on 23 January 1908 – was pleased with the progress and the rise of the institute and he urged for the approval of the constitutions and the rule of the order. The General Mother – Generosa Bruttomesso – wrote them up and final approval was granted on 25 May 1912 under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_X" title="Pope Pius X">Pope Pius X</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sainthood">Sainthood</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Luigi_Maria_Palazzolo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Sainthood">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The formal introduction – which granted Palazzolo the posthumous title Servant of God – came under <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_XXIII" title="Pope John XXIII">Pope John XXIII</a> on 20 November 1958. The pope approved the fact that he had lived a model Christian life of <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a> on 7 July 1962 and proclaimed him to be <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a>.
</p><p>John XXIII also approved two miracles attributed to the intercession of Palazzolo and beatified him on 19 March 1963. <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> confirmed a miracle attributed to him on 28 November 2019. The canonization was postponed from 2020 due to the <a href="/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic" title="COVID-19 pandemic">COVID-19 pandemic</a> until it was announced on 9 November 2021 that Francis would canonize Palazzolo on 15 May 2022.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
