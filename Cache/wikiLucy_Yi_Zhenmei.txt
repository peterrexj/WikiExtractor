<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">In this <a href="/wiki/Chinese_name" title="Chinese name">Chinese name</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Chinese_surname" title="Chinese surname">family name</a> is <i> <a href="/wiki/Yi_(disambiguation)" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Yi (disambiguation)">Yi</a></i>.</div>
<p><b>Lucy Yi Zhenmei</b> (<a href="/wiki/Traditional_Chinese_characters" title="Traditional Chinese characters">traditional Chinese</a>&#58; <span lang="zh-Hant">易貞美</span>; <a href="/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters" title="Simplified Chinese characters">simplified Chinese</a>&#58; <span lang="zh-Hans">易贞美</span>; <a href="/wiki/Pinyin" title="Pinyin">pinyin</a>&#58; <i><span lang="zh-Latn-pinyin">Yì Zhēnměi</span></i>; <a href="/wiki/Wade%E2%80%93Giles" title="Wade–Giles">Wade–Giles</a>&#58; <i><span lang="zh-Latn-wadegile">I Chên-mei</span></i>) (December 9, 1815 – February 19, 1862) was a <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church_in_China" title="Catholic Church in China">Chinese Roman Catholic</a> saint from <a href="/wiki/Mianyang" title="Mianyang">Mianyang</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sichuan" title="Sichuan">Sichuan Province</a>, China. She is the lone woman of the five Guizhou Martyrs, a subset of the much larger <a href="/wiki/Martyr_Saints_of_China" title="Martyr Saints of China">Martyr Saints of China</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life_and_work">Life and work</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Lucy_Yi_Zhenmei&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life and work">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born on December 9, 1815, she was the youngest member of her family. Lucy was a very pious child, to the extent that she made a commitment to <a href="/wiki/Chastity" title="Chastity">chastity</a> at the age of 12.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>As she matured, she developed a love for reading and study. At the age of 20, in the midst of her higher education she grew very ill. After her recovery, Lucy took her spiritual life even more seriously. She devoted herself to the discipline of prayer with great devotion, assuming a way of life much like that of an <a href="/wiki/Asceticism#Christianity" title="Asceticism">ascetic</a> while continuing to assist in the support her family. Her mother taught her how to <a href="/wiki/Hand_spinning" title="Hand spinning">spin</a>, which also became part of her daily life.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After her father died, she lived with her brother and mother, using part of her leisure time to <a href="/wiki/Catechism" title="Catechism">catechise</a> children nearby. The <a href="/wiki/Parish_priest" class="mw-redirect" title="Parish priest">parish priest</a>, who asked her to teach at the school in Mianyang, noticed her devotion and reliable knowledge of her faith. After four years, her brother went to <a href="/wiki/Chongqing" title="Chongqing">Chongqing</a> to practice medicine, and Lucy and her mother joined him. In Chongqing, the local priest also asked her to help teach the women in their parish. When offered money for her work, she refused to take it and offered her work to God.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>A few years later, her brother moved to <a href="/wiki/Guiyang" title="Guiyang">Guiyang</a>, during which time her mother died. Full of enthusiasm for spreading the <a href="/wiki/The_gospel" title="The gospel">Good News</a>, she went on doing missionary work. However, for her own safety she decided to stay in a <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a> of <a href="/wiki/Laity" title="Laity">lay</a> <a href="/wiki/Consecrated_virgin" title="Consecrated virgin">virgins</a>. Shortly after, her failing health forced her to move back home again. In 1861, Bishop Hu asked her to teach once more at the convent. In spite of opposition from relatives, she returned to work there.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Martyrdom">Martyrdom</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Lucy_Yi_Zhenmei&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Martyrdom">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 1862, she went with <a href="/w/index.php?title=Jean-Pierre_N%C3%A9el&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Jean-Pierre Néel (page does not exist)">Jean-Pierre Néel</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Pierre_N%C3%A9el" class="extiw" title="fr:Jean-Pierre Néel">fr</a>&#93;</span>, a missionary priest of <a href="/wiki/Paris_Foreign_Missions_Society" title="Paris Foreign Missions Society">Paris Foreign Missions Society</a>, to start a mission in <a href="/w/index.php?title=Jiashanlong&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Jiashanlong (page does not exist)">Jiashanlong</a>, but just then the administrator of <a href="/wiki/Guizhou_Province" class="mw-redirect" title="Guizhou Province">Guizhou Province</a>, Tian Xingshu, began to stir up <a href="/wiki/Anti-Christian_sentiment" title="Anti-Christian sentiment">hatred against Christians</a>, with the support of the local <a href="/wiki/County_magistrate" title="County magistrate">magistrate</a>. As a result, Zhang Tianshen, Wu Shuisheng, Chen Xianheng and Néel were all imprisoned and sentenced to death without a formal trial. 
</p><p>On February 18, the day of their execution, they encountered Yi Zhenmei on the road. She was also jailed and put on trial that very day and sentenced to death, because she refused to renounce her faith. The following day at noon, February 19, 1862, she was beheaded. Brave believers took the bodies of all five martyrs to Liuchonnguan Seminary grounds for burial.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Lucy_Yi_Zhenmei&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On May 2, 1909, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_X" title="Pope Pius X">Pope Pius X</a> declared Yi and the other four martyrs <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">blessed</a>. <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> canonized Lucy Yi Zhenmei and her companions, the <a href="/wiki/Martyr_Saints_of_China" title="Martyr Saints of China">Martyr Saints of China</a>, on October 1, 2000. Her <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated on February 19 according to the <a href="/wiki/General_Roman_Calendar" title="General Roman Calendar">General Roman Calendar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Lucy Yi Zhenmei is honored (with <a href="/wiki/Agatha_Lin" title="Agatha Lin">Agatha Lin Zhao</a> and <a href="/wiki/Agnes_Tsao_Kou_Ying" title="Agnes Tsao Kou Ying">Agnes Tsao Kou Ying</a>) with a <a href="/wiki/Lesser_Festival_(Anglicanism)" title="Lesser Festival (Anglicanism)">Lesser Feast</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints_(Episcopal_Church)" title="Calendar of saints (Episcopal Church)">liturgical calendar</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_Church_(United_States)" title="Episcopal Church (United States)">Episcopal Church in the United States of America</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> on February 19.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
