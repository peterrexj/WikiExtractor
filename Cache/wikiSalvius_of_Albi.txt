<p><b>Salvius</b>, <b>Salvi</b> or <b>Sauve</b> (died 584) was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Albi" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Albi">bishop of Albi</a> in <a href="/wiki/Francia" title="Francia">Francia</a> between 574 and 584, later venerated as a saint.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Salvius_of_Albi&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He came from a powerful family within the church, which contributed many bishops in the south of France through the end and fall of the Roman Empire. He was a distant relation of <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a> who wrote his life.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was also a relative of Saint <a href="/wiki/Didier_of_Cahors" title="Didier of Cahors">Didier of Cahors</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Salvius_of_Albi&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Salvius was educated in law and humanities, before becoming a lawyer in Albi. Later he became a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> and was made bishop in 574.
</p><p>As bishop he intervened with the powerful <a href="/wiki/Chilperic_I" title="Chilperic I">Chilperic I</a> and stayed in Albi to take care of his flock during a famine and a plague epidemic to which he succumbed in 584.
</p><p>He was buried in his monastery but his remains were later moved to the church of Saint-Salvi in Albi. Their exact location is now lost because of renovation in the 18th century.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> After this he was venerated in the city and was later declared to be a saint.
</p>
