<p><b>Agilberta</b> (d. 680), also known as <b>Aguilberta of Jouarre</b> and <b>Gilberta of Jouarre</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-catholicsaints_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholicsaints-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> is a <a href="/wiki/Benedictines" title="Benedictines">Benedictine</a> French saint, venerated in both the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> and <a href="/wiki/Greek_Orthodox_Church_of_Antioch" class="mw-redirect" title="Greek Orthodox Church of Antioch">Antiochian Orthodox Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-catholicorg_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholicorg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  She was a nun<sup id="cite_ref-catholicsaints_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholicsaints-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and the second <a href="/wiki/Abbess" title="Abbess">abbess</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Jouarre_Abbey" title="Jouarre Abbey">Jouarre Abbey</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Departments_of_France" title="Departments of France">département</a> of <a href="/wiki/Seine-et-Marne" title="Seine-et-Marne">Seine-et-Marne</a>.  Agilberta was a relative of <a href="/wiki/Eberigisil" title="Eberigisil">Ebrigisil</a> and <a href="/w/index.php?title=St._Ado&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="St. Ado (page does not exist)">Ado</a>, who founded Jouarre in 660.  Her brother, <a href="/wiki/Agilbert" title="Agilbert">Agilbert</a>, was bishop of Paris.  Agilberta's sister, <a href="/wiki/Balda_of_Jouarre" title="Balda of Jouarre">Balda</a>, was Jouarre's third abbess.<sup id="cite_ref-catholicorg_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholicorg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Agilberta's feast day is August 10.  She died in 680.<sup id="cite_ref-catholicsaints_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholicsaints-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> She is buried in the crypt at Jouarre in one of three well-preserved sarcophagi.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> It is of particular interest to scholars because of its stonework following the Roman burial tradition.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
