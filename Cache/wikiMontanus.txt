					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Second century Christian prophet</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For other uses, see <a href="/wiki/Montanus_(disambiguation)" class="mw-disambig" title="Montanus (disambiguation)">Montanus (disambiguation)</a>.</div>
<p><b>Montanus</b> was the second century founder of <a href="/wiki/Montanism" title="Montanism">Montanism</a> and a self proclaimed prophet. Montanus emphasized the work of the Holy Spirit, in a manner which set him apart from the <a href="/wiki/Great_Church" title="Great Church">Great church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Edgar_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edgar-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Montanus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Only very little is known about the life of Montanus.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Montanus used to be a pagan priest but later converted into Christianity.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Montanus began his prophesying in a village called Ardabau, located in <a href="/wiki/Phrygia" title="Phrygia">Phrygia</a>. The date of when Montanus started prophesying is somewhere around 157–172, however the Church Fathers gave differing dates on the start of Montanism.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> During his life Montanus was assisted by two women, <a href="/wiki/Priscilla" title="Priscilla">Prisca</a> and <a href="/wiki/Maximilla" title="Maximilla">Maximilla</a>, who also claimed to have prophecies.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Edgar_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Edgar-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Teachings">Teachings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Montanus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Teachings">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The teachings of Montanism include:<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<ol><li>That a new age of prosperity would come, and New Jerusalem will soon be established in <a href="/wiki/Pepuza" title="Pepuza">Pepuza</a>.</li>
<li>One could marry only once.</li>
<li>Abandoning marriage for spiritual reasons was allowed.</li>
<li>Every true Christian had to have recognizable spiritual gifts.</li>
<li>Martyrdom was encouraged and trying to escape was seen as bad</li>
<li>The prophets could forgive sins.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>The church could never permit apostates, murderers and fornicators back.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>Opposition to <a href="/wiki/Formalism_(philosophy)" title="Formalism (philosophy)">formalism</a> in theology.<sup id="cite_ref-Roybal_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roybal-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>A high emphasis on morality.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup></li></ol>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Impact">Impact</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Montanus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Impact">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The influence of Montanus and <a href="/wiki/Marcion_of_Sinope" title="Marcion of Sinope">Marcion</a> helped to spur the early church to begin the process of developing the New Testament canon.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> Montanus was heavily attacked by other early church theologians and seen as a heretic.<sup id="cite_ref-Roybal_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roybal-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> However, <a href="/wiki/Tertullian" title="Tertullian">Tertullian</a> adopted Montanism.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
