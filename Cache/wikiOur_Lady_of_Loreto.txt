<p>A <b>shrine to the Virgin Mary</b> (or <b>Marian shrine</b>) is a <a href="/wiki/Shrine" title="Shrine">shrine</a> marking an <a href="/wiki/Marian_apparitions" class="mw-redirect" title="Marian apparitions">apparition</a> or other <a href="/wiki/Miracle" title="Miracle">miracle</a> ascribed to the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary">Blessed Virgin Mary</a>, or a site on which is centered a historically strong <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary">Marian</a> devotion. Such locales are often the destinations of <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimages</a>.
</p>
