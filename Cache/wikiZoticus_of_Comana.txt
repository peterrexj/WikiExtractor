					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Italian Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Zoticus</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">Martyr</a> and Bishop of <a href="/wiki/Comana_Pontica" title="Comana Pontica">Comana</a></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">204</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> 
<a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">Canonized</a></th><td class="infobox-data">Pre-congregation</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">21 July</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Zoticus</b> was a 3rd-century <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of Comana (also spelled Conana or Comama). Zoticus is known for his opposition to the <a href="/wiki/Montanist" class="mw-redirect" title="Montanist">Montanist</a> <a href="/wiki/Heresy" title="Heresy">heresy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He died in 204 a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> A life of Zoticus, the <i>Vita Zotici</i>, was written during the reign of <a href="/wiki/Michael_IV_the_Paphlagonian" title="Michael IV the Paphlagonian">Michael IV</a> (1034–41). The town of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Zotique,_Quebec" title="Saint-Zotique, Quebec">Saint-Zotique</a>, <a href="/wiki/Quebec" title="Quebec">Quebec</a> is named for him, as is Rue St Zotique in Montreal.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Zoticus_of_Comana&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Zoticus of Comana is sometimes identified with <a href="/wiki/Comana_(Cappadocia)" title="Comana (Cappadocia)">Comana in Cappadocia</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-butler_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> but other sources dispute this identification,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> and suggest a Comana in Italy.<sup id="cite_ref-catholiconline_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholiconline-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  Zoticus is known for his opposition to the <a href="/wiki/Montanist" class="mw-redirect" title="Montanist">Montanist</a> <a href="/wiki/Heresy" title="Heresy">heresy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Sometime in the 3rd century <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">Bishop</a> Zoticus and Bishop Julian of Apamea went to a town called <a href="/wiki/Pepuza" title="Pepuza">Pepuza</a> to <a href="/wiki/Exorcism" title="Exorcism">exorcize</a> <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a> from <a href="/wiki/Maximilla" title="Maximilla">Maximilla</a>. While in Papuza they muzzled the followers of <a href="/wiki/Themiso" title="Themiso">Themiso</a> to stop them from spreading falsehood,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> and Zoticus tried to prove Maximilla why Montanism was wrong.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> Zoticus' exorcism was stopped by followers of Themiso.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Zoticus became a <a href="/wiki/Martyrdom" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrdom">martyr</a> during the persecution of <a href="/wiki/Septimius_Severus" title="Septimius Severus">Septimius Severus</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-catholiconline_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-catholiconline-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> around the year 204.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
