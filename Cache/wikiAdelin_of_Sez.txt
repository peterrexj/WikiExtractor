					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">French Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Adelin of Séez</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Monk and Bishop</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">unknown</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 910</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Anglican_Communion" title="Anglican Communion">Anglican Communion</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">10 November</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Adelin of Séez</b> (also, <b>Hadelin</b>, <b>Adelheim</b>, <b>Adelhelm</b>, or <b>Adalhelmus</b>) (died c. 910) was a <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Benedict" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Saint Benedict">Benedictine monk</a> and <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> at the abbey of Anisole.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_S%C3%A9ez" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Séez">Bishop of Séez</a> for twenty-six years starting around 884.<sup id="cite_ref-patron_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He is noted for authoring a work on the life and miracles of <a href="/wiki/Opportuna_of_Montreuil" title="Opportuna of Montreuil">Opportuna of Montreuil</a> (<i>Vita et miracula Sanctae Opportunae</i>).<sup id="cite_ref-cathen_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cathen-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
