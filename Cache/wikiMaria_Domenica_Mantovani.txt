<p><b>Maria Domenica Mantovani</b> (12 November 1862 - 2 February 1934) was an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">professed religious</a>, and the co-founder of the Little Sisters of the Holy Family; she established them alongside <a href="/wiki/Giuseppe_Nascimbeni" title="Giuseppe Nascimbeni">Giuseppe Nascimbeni</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> As a nun she received the <a href="/wiki/Religious_name" title="Religious name">religious name</a> of <b>Maria of the Immaculate</b>.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>She was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> on 27 April 2003 and <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> on 15 May 2022.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Maria_Domenica_Mantovani&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Maria Domenica Mantovani was born on 12 November 1862 in <a href="/wiki/Verona" title="Verona">Verona</a>, the eldest of four children born to Giovanni Mantovani and Prudenza Zamperini.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Her education did not proceed beyond a period, not very long, in which she studied at a local school. She learnt religion from her parents, which contributed to her sense of having a religious vocation.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In her adolescence – in 1877 – her parish <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> and <a href="/wiki/Spiritual_direction" title="Spiritual direction">spiritual director</a>, <a href="/wiki/Giuseppe_Nascimbeni" title="Giuseppe Nascimbeni">Giuseppe Nascimbeni</a>, encouraged her to visit the ill and to teach <a href="/wiki/Catechism" title="Catechism">catechism</a> in the parish.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> On 8 December 1886, she made a private vow to remain chaste and asked the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary">Blessed Mother</a> to guide her in how best to fulfill her vocation.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> In 1892, she founded a religious congregation alongside Nascimbeni and became its first superior; she led the order for a total of four decades.<sup id="cite_ref-SR_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Mantovani and four of her companions made their solemn profession into the congregation on 4 November 1892. The institute later received the papal approval of <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> on 3 June 1932.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Mantovani died in 1934 due to complications from <a href="/wiki/Influenza" title="Influenza">influenza</a>. Her remains were transferred on 12 November 1987 to be near the remains of her friend and confidante, Nascimbeni.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The countries in which her order now operates include <a href="/wiki/Albania" title="Albania">Albania</a> and <a href="/wiki/Brazil" title="Brazil">Brazil</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Canonization">Canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Maria_Domenica_Mantovani&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mantovani's beatification process commenced after – on 27 January 1987 – the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> issued a <a href="/wiki/Nihil_obstat" title="Nihil obstat">nihil obstat</a> (nothing against) to the cause in a move that accorded Mantovani the posthumous title of <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a>. The conferral of the title acted as the process's first official stage. The diocesan process opened in 1987, and, after a brief period of collecting evidence on her saintliness, closed in 1988. The C.C.S. validated the process on 25 May 1990 in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.
</p><p>The C.C.S. received the <a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">Positio</a> in 1992, allowing them to commence their own investigation into the cause. The consulting theologians approved the contents of the dossier on 24 October 2000, while the C.C.S. approved it on 9 January 2001. On 24 April 2001, Mantovani was proclaimed to be <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> after <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> approved her life of <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a>, both <a href="/wiki/Cardinal_virtues" title="Cardinal virtues">cardinal</a> and <a href="/wiki/Theological_virtues" title="Theological virtues">theological</a>.
</p><p>John Paul II beatified Mantovani on 27 April 2003, along with five others: <a href="/wiki/James_Alberione" title="James Alberione">James Alberione</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Priesthood_in_the_Catholic_Church" title="Priesthood in the Catholic Church">priest</a> and founder of the <a href="/wiki/Pauline_Family" title="Pauline Family">Pauline Family</a>; <a href="/wiki/Marco_d%27Aviano" title="Marco d&#39;Aviano">Marco d'Aviano</a>, a priest and member of the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor_Capuchin" title="Order of Friars Minor Capuchin">Order of Friars Minor Capuchin</a>; <a href="/wiki/Eugenia_Ravasio" title="Eugenia Ravasio">Eugenia Ravasco</a>, <a href="/wiki/Virginity" title="Virginity">virgin</a> and founder of the Congregations of the Daughters of the Sacred Hearts of Jesus and Mary; <a href="/wiki/Maria_Cristina_of_the_Immaculate_Conception_Brando" title="Maria Cristina of the Immaculate Conception Brando">Maria Cristina of the Immaculate Conception Brando</a>, founder of the Congregation of the Sisters, Expiatory Victims of Jesus in the Blessed Sacrament; and <a href="/wiki/Giulia_Salzano" title="Giulia Salzano">Giulia Salzano</a>, virgin and founder of the Congregation of the Catechist Sisters of the Sacred Heart.<sup id="cite_ref-johnpaulII_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-johnpaulII-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> The pope, who called Mantovani a "praiseworthy daughter of the region of Verona" and a "fine example of holiness for every believer" during his homily for the beatification ceremony, said that Mantovani was "inspired by the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Family" title="Holy Family">Holy Family of Nazareth</a> to make herself 'all things to all people', ever attentive to the needs of the 'poor people'."<sup id="cite_ref-johnpaulII_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-johnpaulII-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The second miracle attributed to her – required for her canonization as a saint – was investigated in <a href="/wiki/Bah%C3%ADa_Blanca" title="Bahía Blanca">Bahía Blanca</a> from 16 November 2015 until 10 June 2016; Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Antonio_Ca%C3%B1izares_Llovera" title="Antonio Cañizares Llovera">Antonio Cañizares Llovera</a> presided over the closing of the diocesan process, alongside the vice-postulator and medical experts. On 27 May 2020, Pope Francis authorized a decree during a meeting with the prefect of the C.C.S., Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Giovanni_Angelo_Becciu" title="Giovanni Angelo Becciu">Giovanni Angelo Becciu</a>, which approved a miracle, clearing the way for Mantovani to become a saint.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> It was announced on 9 November 2021 that the canonization for Mantovani and several others would be celebrated on 15 May 2022.
</p><p>The current <a href="/wiki/Postulator" title="Postulator">postulator</a> assigned to the cause is Giovangiuseppe Califano. The current vice-postulator is Luciana Camella. 
</p>
