<p><b>Hildegard</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Female" title="Female">female</a> name derived from the <a href="/wiki/Old_High_German" title="Old High German">Old High German</a> <i>hild</i> ('war' or 'battle') and <i>gard</i> ('enclosure' or 'yard'), and means 'battle enclosure'.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HanksHardcastle2006_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HanksHardcastle2006-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Variant spellings include: <b>Hildegarde</b>; the <a href="/wiki/Polish_language" title="Polish language">Polish</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_language" title="Portuguese language">Portuguese</a>, <a href="/wiki/Slovene_language" title="Slovene language">Slovene</a> and <a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a> <b>Hildegarda</b>; the <a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a> <b>Ildegarda</b>; the <a href="/wiki/Hungarian_language" title="Hungarian language">Hungarian</a> <b>Hildegárd</b>; and the ancient <a href="/wiki/Germanic_peoples" title="Germanic peoples">German</a> <b>Hildegardis</b>.<sup id="cite_ref-Castellón1990_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Castellón1990-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
