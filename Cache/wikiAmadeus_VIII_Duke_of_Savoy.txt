<p><b>Amadeus VIII</b> (4 September 1383 – 7 January 1451), nicknamed <b>the Peaceful</b>, was <a href="/wiki/Count_of_Savoy" class="mw-redirect" title="Count of Savoy">Count of Savoy</a> from 1391 to 1416 and <a href="/wiki/Duke_of_Savoy" class="mw-redirect" title="Duke of Savoy">Duke of Savoy</a> from 1416 to 1440. He was the son of <a href="/wiki/Amadeus_VII,_Count_of_Savoy" title="Amadeus VII, Count of Savoy">Amadeus VII, Count of Savoy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bonne_of_Berry" title="Bonne of Berry">Bonne of Berry</a>. He was a claimant to the <a href="/wiki/Papacy" class="mw-redirect" title="Papacy">papacy</a> from 1439 to 1449 as <b>Felix V<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;a&#93;</a></sup></b> in opposition to Popes <a href="/wiki/Eugene_IV" class="mw-redirect" title="Eugene IV">Eugene IV</a> and <a href="/wiki/Nicholas_V" class="mw-redirect" title="Nicholas V">Nicholas V</a>, and is considered the last historical <a href="/wiki/Antipope" title="Antipope">antipope</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Count_and_duke">Count and duke</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amadeus_VIII,_Duke_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Count and duke">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Amadeus was born in <a href="/wiki/Chamb%C3%A9ry" title="Chambéry">Chambéry</a> on 4 September 1383.<sup id="cite_ref-HDS_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HDS-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He became count of Savoy in 1391 after his father's death, with his mother acting as regent until 1397, during his <a href="/wiki/Minority_reign" title="Minority reign">minority reign</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-HDS_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HDS-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His early rule saw the centralization of power and the territorial expansion of the <a href="/wiki/Savoyard_state" title="Savoyard state">Savoyard state</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-HDS_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HDS-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and in 1416 Amadeus was elevated by <a href="/wiki/Emperor_Sigismund" class="mw-redirect" title="Emperor Sigismund">Emperor Sigismund</a> to duke of Savoy.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEKirsch1909_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKirsch1909-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 1418, his distant cousin <a href="/wiki/Louis_of_Piedmont" title="Louis of Piedmont">Louis of Piedmont</a>, his brother-in-law, the last male of the elder branch of <a href="/wiki/House_of_Savoy" title="House of Savoy">House of Savoy</a>, died, leaving Amadeus as his <a href="/wiki/Heir-general" class="mw-redirect" title="Heir-general">heir-general</a>, thus finally uniting the male-lines of the House of Savoy.
</p><p>Amadeus increased his dominions and encouraged several attempts to negotiate an end to the <a href="/wiki/Hundred_Years%27_War" title="Hundred Years&#39; War">Hundred Years' War</a>. From 1401 to 1422, he campaigned to recover the area around Geneva and Annecy. After the death of his wife in 1428, he founded the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Maurice" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Saint Maurice">Order of Saint Maurice</a> with six other knights in 1434.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEPinder200244_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPinder200244-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> They lived alone in the castle of <a href="/wiki/Ripaille" class="mw-redirect" title="Ripaille">Ripaille</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-HDS_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HDS-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> near Geneva, in a quasi-monastic state according to a rule drawn up by himself. He appointed his son <a href="/wiki/Louis,_Duke_of_Savoy" title="Louis, Duke of Savoy">Louis</a> regent of the duchy.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEKirsch1909_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKirsch1909-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Antipope">Antipope</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amadeus_VIII,_Duke_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Antipope">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_(cropped).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_%28cropped%29.jpg/220px-Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_%28cropped%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="153" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_%28cropped%29.jpg/330px-Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_%28cropped%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_%28cropped%29.jpg/440px-Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_%28cropped%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="461" data-file-height="320" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Vigiles_du_roi_Charles_VII_17_(cropped).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Félix V accepting the authority of <a href="/wiki/Nicholas_V" class="mw-redirect" title="Nicholas V">Nicholas V</a>, <a href="/wiki/Martial_d%27Auvergne" title="Martial d&#39;Auvergne">Martial d'Auvergne</a>, illumination from the <i><a href="/wiki/Vigilles_de_Charles_VII" class="mw-redirect" title="Vigilles de Charles VII">Vigilles de Charles VII</a></i> (15th century)</div></div></div>
<p>Amadeus was sympathetic to <a href="/wiki/Conciliarism" title="Conciliarism">conciliarism</a>, the movement to have the Church managed by <a href="/wiki/Ecumenical_council" title="Ecumenical council">Ecumenical councils</a>, and to prelates like <a href="/wiki/Louis_Aleman" title="Louis Aleman">Cardinal Aleman of Arles</a>, who wanted to set limits upon the doctrine of <a href="/wiki/Papal_supremacy" title="Papal supremacy">Papal supremacy</a>. He had close relations with the Council of Basel (1431-1449), even after most of its members joined the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Florence" title="Council of Florence">Council of Florence</a>, convened by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Eugene_IV" title="Pope Eugene IV">Pope Eugene IV</a> in 1438. The Cardinal of Arles reminded the Council that they needed a rich and powerful pope to defend it from its adversaries.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTECreighton1892211_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTECreighton1892211-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> The rump council at Basel elected Amadeus as Pope Felix V in November 1439.  After long negotiations with a deputation from the council, Amadeus acquiesced in the election on 5 February 1440. He took the inaugural oath formulated by the Basel council; the only pope or antipope to do so. At the same time, he completely renounced  all further participation in the government of his domains: he named his son Louis Duke of Savoy, and his son Philip Count of Geneva.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEKirsch1909_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKirsch1909-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>There is no evidence that he intrigued to obtain the papal office by sending the bishops of Savoy to Basel. Of the twelve bishops present, seven were Savoyards.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTECreighton1892211_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTECreighton1892211-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His reputation is marred by the account of him as a pontiff concerned with money, to avoid disadvantaging his heirs, found in the <i>Commentaries</i> of <a href="/wiki/Pius_II" class="mw-redirect" title="Pius II">Pius II</a>.
</p><p>Amadeus is considered an <a href="/wiki/Antipope" title="Antipope">antipope</a>. He served from November 1439 to April 1449.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEDecaluweIzbickiChristianson2017443_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEDecaluweIzbickiChristianson2017443-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>  After the death of his opponent <a href="/wiki/Pope_Eugene_IV" title="Pope Eugene IV">Pope Eugene IV</a> in 1447, both sides of the church favoured a settlement of the schism, and in 1449 he accepted the authority of <a href="/wiki/Pope_Nicholas_V" title="Pope Nicholas V">Pope Nicholas V</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Later_life">Later life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amadeus_VIII,_Duke_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Later life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After renouncing his claim, Amadeus was appointed <a href="/wiki/Papal_legate" title="Papal legate">papal legate</a> to Savoy.<sup id="cite_ref-HDS_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HDS-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He died in <a href="/wiki/Geneva" title="Geneva">Geneva</a> in 7 January 1451.<sup id="cite_ref-HDS_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HDS-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Marriage_and_issue">Marriage and issue</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amadeus_VIII,_Duke_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Marriage and issue">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He married <a href="/wiki/Mary_of_Burgundy,_Duchess_of_Savoy" title="Mary of Burgundy, Duchess of Savoy">Mary of Burgundy</a> (1386–1422), daughter of <a href="/wiki/Philip_the_Bold" title="Philip the Bold">Philip the Bold</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEVaughan200553_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEVaughan200553-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> They had nine children, only four of whom lived to mature adulthood:
</p>
<ol><li>Margaret of Savoy (13 May 1405 – 1418).</li>
<li>Anthony of Savoy (September 1407 – bef. 12 December 1407).</li>
<li>Anthony of Savoy (1408 – aft. 10 October 1408).</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Marie_of_Savoy_(1411-1469)" class="mw-redirect" title="Marie of Savoy (1411-1469)">Marie</a> (end January 1411 – 22 February 1469), married <a href="/wiki/Filippo_Maria_Visconti" title="Filippo Maria Visconti">Filippo Maria Visconti</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rulers_of_Milan" class="mw-redirect" title="Rulers of Milan">duke of Milan</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEWilkinsWilkins1996107_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEWilkinsWilkins1996107-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>Amadeus of Savoy (26 Mar 1412 – 17 August 1431), Prince of Piedmont, the heir apparent until his premature death.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Louis_of_Savoy" class="mw-redirect" title="Louis of Savoy">Louis</a> (24 February 1413 – 29 January 1465), his successor.</li>
<li>Bonne of Savoy (September 1415 – 25 September 1430).</li>
<li>Philip of Savoy (1417 – 3 March 1444), Count of Genève</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Margaret_of_Savoy,_Countess_Palatine_of_the_Rhine" class="mw-redirect" title="Margaret of Savoy, Countess Palatine of the Rhine">Margaret</a> (7 August 1420 – 30 September 1479), married firstly <a href="/wiki/Louis_III_of_Naples" class="mw-redirect" title="Louis III of Naples">Louis III</a>, titular <a href="/wiki/King_of_Naples" class="mw-redirect" title="King of Naples">king of Naples</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEKekewich200854_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKekewich200854-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> secondly <a href="/wiki/Louis_IV,_Count_Palatine_of_the_Rhine" class="mw-redirect" title="Louis IV, Count Palatine of the Rhine">Louis IV, Count Palatine of the Rhine</a><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEKekewich2008214_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEKekewich2008214-10">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> and thirdly <a href="/wiki/Ulrich_V,_Count_of_W%C3%BCrttemberg" title="Ulrich V, Count of Württemberg">Ulrich V, Count of Württemberg</a>.</li></ol>
