<p><b>Contentius</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> (died 510) was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Bayeux" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Bayeux">bishop of Bayeux</a> from 480 until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He is a <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> and <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Orthodox</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is 19 January.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><br />
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Contentius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to Father Elie,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> he was Bayeusain, and his <a href="/wiki/Hermitage_(religious_retreat)" title="Hermitage (religious retreat)">hermitage</a> was located at Blay, near Bayeux.
</p><p>His great reputation for holiness brought him many visits from <a href="/wiki/Pilgrim" title="Pilgrim">pilgrims</a> who came to him for spiritual comfort.
</p><p>However, the inhabitants managed to have him named <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Bayeux" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Bayeux">bishop of Bayeux</a> to succeed Saint <a href="/w/index.php?title=Manvieu&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Manvieu (page does not exist)">Manvieu</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> In this function he fought against the <a href="/wiki/Idolatry" title="Idolatry">idolatry</a> that still existed in this country, as well as against the <a href="/wiki/Nobility" title="Nobility">nobles</a> who traded it.
</p><p>He has been attributed several <a href="/wiki/Miracle" title="Miracle">miracles</a>, one of which, on the way to <a href="/wiki/S%C3%A9es" title="Sées">Sees</a>, he made water from the ground to Athis-de-l'Orne to quench his companions.
</p><p>He died in 513. First buried in the church of Saint-Exupére, he was transferred in 1162 to the Benedictine abbey of Fécamp. His relics were then transported to Argentan where they escaped the Huguenots in 1562. The translation of his right femur in the church of Saint-Contest was done in 1896 during a great ceremony.
</p><p>A municipality of the Caen conurbation is called <a href="/wiki/Saint-Contest" title="Saint-Contest">Saint-Contest</a> and the town of Mayenne is called <a href="/wiki/Contest,_Mayenne" title="Contest, Mayenne">Contest</a>.
</p>
