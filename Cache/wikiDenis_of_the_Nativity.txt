<p><b>Pierre Berthelot</b>, OCD (known in religion as <b>Denis of the Nativity</b>, or <b>Dionysius</b>; 12 December 1600, in <a href="/wiki/Honfleur" title="Honfleur">Honfleur</a> – 27 November 1638, in <a href="/wiki/Sumatra" title="Sumatra">Sumatra</a>) was a French sailor and cartographer in the service of the <a href="/wiki/King_of_Portugal" class="mw-redirect" title="King of Portugal">king of Portugal</a>, and later <a href="/wiki/Discalced_Carmelites" title="Discalced Carmelites">Discalced Carmelite</a> friar in <a href="/wiki/Goa" title="Goa">Goa</a>, taking the name Denis. He was killed in Sumatra while taking part in a diplomatic mission there on behalf of the <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_Empire" title="Portuguese Empire">Portuguese Empire</a>. He was beatified by Pope <a href="/wiki/Leo_XIII" class="mw-redirect" title="Leo XIII">Leo XIII</a> in 1900.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Denis_of_the_Nativity&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Berthelot was born in <a href="/wiki/Honfleur" title="Honfleur">Honfleur</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Calvados_(department)" title="Calvados (department)">Calvados</a> <a href="/wiki/French_departments" class="mw-redirect" title="French departments">Department</a> of <a href="/wiki/Normandy" title="Normandy">Normandy</a>, the second son of Pierre Berthelot, a ship's captain and master surgeon, and Fleurie (<i>née</i> Morin).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His contemporaries described him as "a handsome, stocky man, blond and fair-skinned, an adventuresome and high-spirited person, with an inquisitive and active mind".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The younger Berthelot's first sea voyage was at the age of twelve, and when he was 19&#160;years old, he embarked on a vessel called <i>L'Espérance</i>, bound for the Far East.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> The ship was captured by a <a href="/wiki/Netherlands" title="Netherlands">Dutch</a> vessel in competition for the spice trade. It was at this time that Berthelot made a religious conversion. He managed to escape and sought refuge with the Portuguese in <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_Malacca" title="Portuguese Malacca">Malacca</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In service with the Portuguese, Berthelot was knighted for bravery and earned an appointment as pilot-in-chief and <a href="/wiki/Cosmography" title="Cosmography">cosmographer</a> to the king of Portugal.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He gained some fame as a cartographer, and his map of the archipelago of <a href="/wiki/Sumatra" title="Sumatra">Sumatra</a> is still preserved in the <a href="/wiki/British_Museum" title="British Museum">British Museum</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Rohrbach_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rohrbach-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Though a career with the Portuguese navy was available to him, he chose to join the Discalced Carmelite Order in <a href="/wiki/Goa" title="Goa">Goa</a> in 1635, taking the name of Denis of the Nativity.<sup id="cite_ref-Guénin,_p._251_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guénin,_p._251-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was <a href="/wiki/Holy_Orders" class="mw-redirect" title="Holy Orders">ordained</a> on 24 August 1638,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> and straightaway was sent by his superiors on a diplomatic mission to the <a href="/wiki/Aceh_Sultanate" title="Aceh Sultanate">Sultan of Aceh</a>, accompanying Francisco de Sousa de Castro, an ambassador of the <a href="/wiki/Viceroy#Portuguese_Empire" title="Viceroy">Viceroy in Goa</a>. Castro had requested his presence, both for spiritual guidance for the group as well as for Father Denis' piloting skills and his knowledge of the region from his service aboard the <i>L'Espérance</i>.
</p><p>Once in Aceh, all the members of the mission were seized and arrested,<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> at the instigation of the Dutch authorities in <a href="/wiki/Jakarta" title="Jakarta">Jakarta</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Guénin,_p._251_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guénin,_p._251-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> The members of the mission were then tortured. Refusing to deny their faith, they were martyred one by one. Father Denis and his companion, Brother <a href="/wiki/Redemptus_of_the_Cross" title="Redemptus of the Cross">Redemptus of the Cross</a>, were led to a desolate spot on the seashore, where Redemptus was shot with arrows, then his throat was slit<sup id="cite_ref-Rohrbach_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rohrbach-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Father Denis, a crucifix in his hands, received a fatal blow to the head from a <a href="/wiki/Scimitar" title="Scimitar">scimitar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Rohrbach_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Rohrbach-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Of the sixty-some members of the diplomatic mission, only Castro, the ambassador, survived. He was held captive for three years, until his family paid a large ransom for his life.<sup id="cite_ref-Guénin,_p._251_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Guénin,_p._251-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Denis_of_the_Nativity&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Denis of the Nativity was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on 10 June 1900 by Pope Leo XIII, together with his companion, Redemptus of the Cross. Their <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Calendar of Saints">Calendar of Saints</a> of the Order is celebrated on the 29 November.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Prayer">Prayer</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Denis_of_the_Nativity&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Prayer">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><div class="poem">
<p><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">Deus, qui mirabilis dispositione beatos Dionysium et Redemptum per maris pericula ad palmam martyrii perduxisti: eorum intercessione concede; ut, inter mundanas varietates et sæcularia desideria, fideles usque ad mortem in confessione tui nominis maneamus. Per Dominum. Amen.</i></span><br />
<br />
O God, Who in Thy wondrous providence, led the blesseds Denis and Redemptus through the perils of the sea to the palm of martyrdom, grant through their intercession that in the midst of earthly <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/vicissitude" class="extiw" title="wiktionary:vicissitude">vicissitudes</a> and worldly desires we may remain steadfast even unto death in the confession of Thy name. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.<i></i>
