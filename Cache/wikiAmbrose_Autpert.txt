					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Ambrose_Autpert&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="Ambrose Autpert">Ambrose Autpert</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Autpert Ambrose (Ambroise)</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la"><b>Ambrosius Autpertus</b></i>) (ca. 730 – 784) was a <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> monk.
</p><p>He became abbot of <a href="/wiki/San_Vincenzo_al_Volturno" title="San Vincenzo al Volturno">San Vincenzo al Volturno</a> in South Italy in the time of <a href="/wiki/Desiderius" title="Desiderius">Desiderius</a>, king of the <a href="/wiki/Lombards" title="Lombards">Lombards</a>.  Autpert's election as <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> caused internal dissent at St. Vicenzo, and both <a href="/wiki/Pope_Stephen_III" title="Pope Stephen III">Pope Stephen III</a> and <a href="/wiki/Charlemagne" title="Charlemagne">Charlemagne</a> intervened.  The disagreement was based both on objections to Autpert's personality and to his Frankish origin.
</p><p>He wrote a considerable number of works on the Bible and religious subjects generally. Among these are commentaries on the <a href="/wiki/Apocalypse" title="Apocalypse">Apocalypse</a>, on the <a href="/wiki/Psalms" title="Psalms">Psalms</a>, and on the <a href="/wiki/Song_of_Solomon" class="mw-redirect" title="Song of Solomon">Song of Solomon</a>; <i>Lives of Saints Paldo, Tuto and Vaso</i>; <i><a href="/wiki/Assumption_of_Mary" title="Assumption of Mary">Assumption</a> of the Virgin</i>; and a <i>Combat between the Virtues and the Vices</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 2009, Pope <a href="/wiki/Benedict_XVI" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedict XVI">Benedict XVI</a> gave a homily about him in Saint Peter's square. In this homily, Autpert's death date is given as 784 (older scholarship had given a date between 778 and 779).<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
