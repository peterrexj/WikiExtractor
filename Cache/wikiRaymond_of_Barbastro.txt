<p><b>Raymond William</b> (<i>Raimundo Guillermo</i>) was born in <a href="/w/index.php?title=Durban,_Ariege&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Durban, Ariege (page does not exist)">Durban, France</a>. He entered the <a href="/wiki/Canons_Regular" class="mw-redirect" title="Canons Regular">Canons Regular</a> and in 1104<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was appointed <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Barbastro" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Barbastro">Bishop of Barbastro</a> in Spain, remaining in that position until his death of natural causes in 1126.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
He was canonised by Innocent II in 1136.
</p>
