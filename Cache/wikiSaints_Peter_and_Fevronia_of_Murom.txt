<p><b>Davyd Yuryevich</b> (Russian: <span lang="ru">Давыд Юрьевич</span>) (c. 1167 – 25 June 1228) and <b>Euphrosyne</b> (Russian: <span lang="ru">Евфросиния</span>) (c. 1175 – 25 June 1228), known as <b>Saints Peter and Fevronia of Murom</b> (Russian: <span lang="ru">Святые Петр и Феврония Муромские</span>), were the russian Prince and Princess consort of the <a href="/wiki/Principality_of_Murom" title="Principality of Murom">Principality of Murom</a>. They are some of the most renowned Russian saints and <a href="/wiki/Wonderworker" class="mw-redirect" title="Wonderworker">wonderworkers</a> venerated both in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Catholic Church">Eastern Catholic Church</a>; their feast day is celebrated every year on 25 June, <a href="/wiki/Old_Style_and_New_Style_dates" title="Old Style and New Style dates">N.S.</a> (8 July, <a href="/wiki/Old_Style_and_New_Style_dates" title="Old Style and New Style dates">O.S.</a>).
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_and_Fevronia_of_Murom&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to legend, Davyd Yuryevich was the second son of <a href="/wiki/Yuri_Dolgorukiy" title="Yuri Dolgorukiy">Duke Yuri of Murom</a> the <a href="/wiki/Grand_Prince_of_Kiev" title="Grand Prince of Kiev">Grand Prince of Kiev</a> and his second wife Helena of Constantinople, grandson of Yaroslav I of Murom and Ryazan, the first <a href="/wiki/Grand_Prince_of_Ryazan" class="mw-redirect" title="Grand Prince of Ryazan">Grand Prince of Ryazan</a>. He ascended the throne in 1203 after the death of his elder brother Vladimir Yuryevich (according to his life, the Prince Peter assumed the prince's throne after the death of his elder brother, Prince Paul). During his reign, he acted supporting Vsevolod III the <a href="/wiki/Grand_Duke_of_Vladimir" class="mw-redirect" title="Grand Duke of Vladimir">Grand Prince of Vladimir</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Some years before, Davyd was seriously ill with <a href="/wiki/Leprosy" title="Leprosy">leprosy</a>. Many doctors attempted to heal the prince; still, nobody could. During a vision, it had been revealed to the prince that the daughter of a <a href="/wiki/Beekeeper" title="Beekeeper">bee keeper</a> would be ready to heal him: the pious maiden Euphrosyne, a <a href="/wiki/Peasant" title="Peasant">peasant</a> from the rural <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%9B%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE_(%D0%A0%D1%8F%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BE%D0%BD)" class="extiw" title="ru:Ласково (Рязанский район)">Village of Laskovo</a> in <a href="/wiki/Ryazan_Governorate" title="Ryazan Governorate">Ryazan Governorate</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Euphrosyne was beautiful, pious and kind, moreover she was a wise woman, who knew the properties of herbs and knew how to treat ailments. Davyd fell in love with Euphrosyne for her piety, wisdom and kindness and made a vow to marry her after being healed; later the peasant healed the prince and have become his wife. Euphrosyne then became the princess consort of Murom. Soon the couple had three children,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> they raised them using the <a href="/wiki/Fear_of_God" title="Fear of God">fear of God</a>. They ruled the city of Murom and lived happily as one family.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_war_campaign">The war campaign</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_and_Fevronia_of_Murom&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: The war campaign">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In 1207, Davyd Yuryevich came to the aid of Vsevolod Yuryevich during his campaign on the Ryazan Governorate land near <a href="/wiki/Pronsky_District" title="Pronsky District">Pronsk District</a>. Pronsky Mikhail Vsevolodovich fled to Chernigov to his father in law Vsevolod Chermny. Residents led by Izyaslav, cousin of Mikhail Vsevolodovich, defended the city for six weeks, waiting for help from Ryazan, but experienced an acute shortage of food and water.
</p><p>After an unsuccessful attempt at the unblocking strike of the Ryazan people, the city surrendered at the mercy of the winner. Izyaslav was released in peace, and instead of him Vsevolod gave Pronsk to his brother Prince Oleg Vladimirovich, who was among the besiegers. However, in the next year (1208) upon learning of his willfulness, Vsevolod took Pronsk from Oleg Vladimirovich and gave the city to Davyd. In the same year, Oleg and his brothers drove Davyd out of Pronsk and gave him to Mikhail.
</p><p>In the struggle for the great reign of Vladimir after the death of <a href="/wiki/Vsevolod_the_Big_Nest" title="Vsevolod the Big Nest">Vsevolod III</a>, Davyd supported Yuri and Yaroslav Vsevolodovich. In 1213, he participated in the campaign of Yuri Vsevolodovich against Rostov. In 1216, the Murom squad took part in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Lipitsa" title="Battle of Lipitsa">battle of Lipitsk</a> as part of the combined forces of Vladimir, Pereyaslavl, Suzdal and some other estates on the side of Grand Duke Yuri Vsevolodovich, against the united army of <a href="/wiki/Veliky_Novgorod" title="Veliky Novgorod">Veliky Novgorod</a>, <a href="/wiki/Pskov" title="Pskov">Pskov</a>, <a href="/wiki/Smolensk" title="Smolensk">Smolensk</a>, <a href="/wiki/Toropets" title="Toropets">Toropets</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rostov" title="Rostov">Rostov</a>.
</p><p>In 1220 Davyd sent his son Svyatoslav with an army to take part in a joint campaign with the Vladimir people against the Volga Bulgars.
</p><p><br />
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_and_Fevronia_of_Murom&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the <a href="/wiki/Genealogy" title="Genealogy">Genealogy</a> of Davyd and Euphrosyne, they had children, The names of the three children are known - <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%AE%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%94%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%8B%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87" class="extiw" title="ru:Юрий Давыдович">Yuri</a>, <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A1%D0%B2%D1%8F%D1%82%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%B0%D0%B2_%D0%94%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%8B%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87_(%D0%BA%D0%BD%D1%8F%D0%B7%D1%8C_%D0%BC%D1%83%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BC%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9)" class="extiw" title="ru:Святослав Давыдович (князь муромский)">Svyatoslav</a> and <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%95%D0%B2%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BA%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D0%94%D0%B0%D0%B2%D1%8B%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BD%D0%B0" class="extiw" title="ru:Евдокия Давыдовна">Evdokia</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Along with this, the names of their four grandchildren are known; Yaroslav son of Yuri, Vasily and Ivan, sons of Svyatoslav, and Dmitry, son of Evdokia.
</p><p>The fate of the children was different. The youngest son, Svyatoslav, died in the same week as his parents, only two to three days earlier. The eldest son, Yuri, died heroically in 1237 in the battle with <a href="/wiki/Batu_Khan" title="Batu Khan">Batu Khan</a>, defending his native land.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Destiny" title="Destiny">fate</a> of Evdokia's daughter was marked by many signs of God's mercy. According to numerous sources, she married Svyatoslav, the son of Vsevolod III the Grand Duke of Vladimir and became Prince of Yuryevskaya. The chronicles mention that her father, Davyd the Prince of Murom was present at her wedding.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The Prince of Murom became directly related to the great family of the Prince of Vladimir, among whom there were many saints. One of them was Svyatoslav's mother, Maria Yasynya, who became famous for her love of children and piety. She gave birth to twelve children, after which her holy spouse began to be called Vsevolod III, having a large family. They raised their children in the fear of God and Christian piety. As a result, four of the sons are now <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> as saints by the <a href="/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church" title="Russian Orthodox Church">Russian Orthodox Church</a>.
</p><p>The beneficial spiritual influence of Saint Mary on the princely couple of Svyatoslav and Evdokia was very visible. So, it is known that Evdokia had a son, Dmitry, who after his father became the Prince of Yurievsky (1253–1269). After their death, Svyatoslav and his son Yuri began to be revered as saints, which was noted in the old calendar, Now they are officially included in the <a href="/wiki/St._Vladimir%27s_Cathedral_(St._Petersburg)" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Vladimir&#39;s Cathedral (St. Petersburg)">Cathedral of Saint Vladimir</a>.
</p><p>Evdokia did not live long in marriage with her husband. In 1228, she begged her husband to let her go to the city of Murom in <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%91%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%B1%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D1%8B%D1%80%D1%8C_(%D0%92%D0%BE%D0%B4%D1%8F%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B5)" class="extiw" title="ru:Борисоглебский монастырь (Водяное)">Borisoglebsk Monastery</a>, where her parents asceticised, took monastic vows there after the death of her parents, and in the same year died after them and her younger brother.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_and_Fevronia_of_Murom&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Towards the end of their lives, Davyd and Euphrosyne took their vows and entered monastic life, Davyd taking the <a href="/wiki/Religious_name" title="Religious name">monastic name</a> of Peter and Euphrosyne the name of Fevronia. According to the book of <a href="/wiki/Kormchaia" title="Kormchaia">Kormchaia</a> only the simultaneous cutting of spouses into <a href="/wiki/Monasticism" title="Monasticism">monasticism</a> could be regarded condescendingly as a reason for the dissolution of the marriage union.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> Both prayed that they would die the same day and their wish was fulfilled and they both died on <a href="/wiki/Easter" title="Easter">Easter Sunday</a>.
</p><p>On 25 June 1228, they both passed away in their respective <a href="/wiki/Monastic_cell" title="Monastic cell">cells</a> in <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a>. The holy couple were buried at the <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A1%D0%BE%D0%B1%D0%BE%D1%80_%D0%A0%D0%BE%D0%B6%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0_%D0%91%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%86%D1%8B_(%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BC)" class="extiw" title="ru:Собор Рождества Богородицы (Муром)">Cathedral of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary</a> in their homeland, Murom.
</p><p>According to legends, they had expressed their desire to be buried together in the same coffin. Finding the burial in one coffin incompatible with the <a href="/wiki/Religious_vows" title="Religious vows">monastic rites</a>, their bodies were placed in different coffins, but, by <a href="/wiki/Miracle" title="Miracle">miracle</a> the next day they were found together. This happened several times until their wish of being interred together was fulfilled, The simultaneous death of members of the Murom princely dynasty fell during the service in Murom, the <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A0%D1%8F%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%BD%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%8F_%D0%B5%D0%BF%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%85%D0%B8%D1%8F" class="extiw" title="ru:Рязанская епархия">Bishop of Murom and Ryazan</a>, <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%95%D0%B2%D1%84%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%81%D0%B8%D0%BD_%D0%A1%D0%B2%D1%8F%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%86" class="extiw" title="ru:Евфросин Святогорец">Bishop Yephrosyn Svyatogorets</a>, who allegedly was the presider on the rite of the monastic tonsure of the holy couple, as well as their <a href="/wiki/Christian_burial" title="Christian burial">Christian burial</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Canonization">Canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_and_Fevronia_of_Murom&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 1547, <a href="/wiki/Macarius,_Metropolitan_of_Moscow" title="Macarius, Metropolitan of Moscow">Metropolitan Macarius of Moscow</a> <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> Peter and Fevronia as a saints in the <a href="/wiki/Makaryev_Monastery" title="Makaryev Monastery">Monastery of Makaryev</a> and also the holy couple declared as the patron saint of Love and Marriage.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> After the <a href="/wiki/Soviet_Union" title="Soviet Union">Communist regime</a> in Russia the relics were rediscovered after being hidden in a <a href="/wiki/Religion_in_the_Soviet_Union" title="Religion in the Soviet Union">Soviet Anti-religious Museum.</a><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 19 September 1992 the relic was enshrined in the <a href="https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A2%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B8%D1%86%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%BC%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D1%8B%D1%80%D1%8C_(%D0%9C%D1%83%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BC)" class="extiw" title="ru:Троицкий монастырь (Муром)">Monastery of the Holy Trinity</a> in the city of <a href="/wiki/Murom" title="Murom">Murom</a>, and every pilgrim can <a href="/wiki/Veneration" title="Veneration">venerate</a> to the saints and evoke their <a href="/wiki/Intercession_of_saints" title="Intercession of saints">intercession</a>.
</p>
