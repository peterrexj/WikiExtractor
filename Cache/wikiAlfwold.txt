<p><b>Ælfwold II</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;a&#93;</a></sup> (died 1058) was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Sherborne_(ancient)" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Sherborne (ancient)">Bishop of Sherborne</a> in <a href="/wiki/Dorset" title="Dorset">Dorset</a>. He is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%C3%86lfwold_II_(bishop_of_Sherborne)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Little is known of Ælfwold apart from the information given by <a href="/wiki/William_of_Malmesbury" title="William of Malmesbury">William of Malmesbury</a>. He was at first a monk of <a href="/wiki/Winchester" title="Winchester">Winchester</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> then was consecrated Bishop of Sherborne in 1045,<sup id="cite_ref-Handbook222_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handbook222-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> succeeding his own brother <a href="/wiki/Brithwine_I" title="Brithwine I">Brithwine</a>. His frugality of life served as a powerful contrast to the contemporary custom of riotous banqueting after the example of the <a href="/wiki/Danes_(ancient_people)" class="mw-redirect" title="Danes (ancient people)">Danish</a> monarchs.
</p><p>Ælfwold showed great devotion to <a href="/wiki/Swithun" title="Swithun">Saint Swithun</a>, his old patron of Winchester, and also to Saint <a href="/wiki/Cuthbert" title="Cuthbert">Cuthbert</a>, to whose shrine at <a href="/wiki/Durham,_England" title="Durham, England">Durham</a> he made a pilgrimage. He died while singing the <a href="/wiki/Antiphon" title="Antiphon">antiphon</a> of Saint Cuthbert. He was in a sense the last Bishop of Sherborne, as after his death the see of Sherborne was united to that of <a href="/wiki/Ramsbury" title="Ramsbury">Ramsbury</a> in <a href="/wiki/Wiltshire" title="Wiltshire">Wiltshire</a>.
</p><p>Ælfwold died in 1058.<sup id="cite_ref-Handbook222_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handbook222-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a> and some <a href="/wiki/Anglican_Communion" title="Anglican Communion">Anglicans</a>.
</p>
