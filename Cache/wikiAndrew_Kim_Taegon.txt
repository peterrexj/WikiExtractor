<p><b>Andrew Kim Taegon</b> (21 August 1821 – 16 September 1846), also referred to as <b>Andrew Kim</b> in English, was the first <a href="/wiki/Korea" title="Korea">Korean</a>-born <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Catholic</a> priest and is the patron saint of Korean clergy. 
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Andrew_Kim_Taegon&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In the late 18th century, <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholicism</a> began to take root slowly in Korea,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> having been introduced by scholars who visited China and brought back Western books translated into Chinese. In 1836 Korea saw its first consecrated missionaries (members of the <a href="/wiki/Paris_Foreign_Missions_Society" title="Paris Foreign Missions Society">Paris Foreign Missions Society</a>) arrive,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> only to find out that the people there were already practicing Korean Catholics.
</p><p>Born of <a href="/wiki/Yangban" title="Yangban">Yangban</a>, Kim's parents were converts and his father was subsequently <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyred</a> for practising Christianity, a prohibited activity in heavily <a href="/wiki/Confucian" class="mw-redirect" title="Confucian">Confucian</a> Korea. After being baptized at age 15, Kim studied at a seminary in the <a href="/wiki/Portuguese_Empire" title="Portuguese Empire">Portuguese colony</a> of <a href="/wiki/Macau" title="Macau">Macau</a>. He also spent time in study at Lolomboy, <a href="/wiki/Bocaue,_Bulacan" class="mw-redirect" title="Bocaue, Bulacan">Bocaue</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bulacan" title="Bulacan">Bulacan</a>, <a href="/wiki/Philippines" title="Philippines">Philippines</a>, where today he is also venerated. He was ordained a priest in <a href="/wiki/Shanghai" title="Shanghai">Shanghai</a> after nine years (1844) by the French bishop Jean Joseph Jean-Baptiste Ferréol. He then returned to Korea to preach and evangelize. During the <a href="/wiki/Joseon_Dynasty" class="mw-redirect" title="Joseon Dynasty">Joseon Dynasty</a>, Christianity was suppressed and many Christians were persecuted and executed. Catholics had to practise their faith covertly. Kim was one of several thousand Christians who were executed during this time. In 1846, at the age of 25, he was tortured and beheaded near Seoul on the <a href="/wiki/Han_River_(Korea)" title="Han River (Korea)">Han River</a>. His last words were:
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>This is my last hour of life, listen to me attentively: if I have held communication with foreigners, it has been for my religion and my God. It is for Him that I die. My immortal life is on the point of beginning. Become Christians if you wish to be happy after death, because God has eternal chastisements in store for those who have refused to know Him.<sup id="cite_ref-london_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-london-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<p>Before Ferréol, the first bishop of Korea, died from exhaustion on 3 February 1853, he wanted to be buried beside Kim, stating, "You will never know how sad I was to lose this young native priest. I have loved him as a father loved his son; it is a consolation for me to think of his eternal happiness."<sup id="cite_ref-london_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-london-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 6 May 1984, <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> Kim along with 102 other <a href="/wiki/Korean_Martyrs" title="Korean Martyrs">Korean Martyrs</a>, including <a href="/wiki/Paul_Chong_Hasang" title="Paul Chong Hasang">Paul Chong Hasang</a>, during his trip to Korea. The feast day of Andrew Kim Taegon, Paul Chong Hasang and companions is celebrated on 20 September.
</p>
