					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=St._Bobo&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Bobo">St. Bobo</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p>Saint <b>Bobo of Provence</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Beuvon</i> or <i>Bobon</i>, <a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">Bovo</i> or <i>Bobone</i>; died 986) was a <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> warrior and <a href="/wiki/Christian_pilgrimage" title="Christian pilgrimage">pilgrim</a> from <a href="/wiki/Noyers-sur-Jabron" title="Noyers-sur-Jabron">Noyers</a> (<i>Noghiers</i>).<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBallan201034_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBallan201034-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He is known only from the anonymous biography <i><b>Vita sancti Bobonis</b></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEMurray2002376_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMurray2002376-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He built a <a href="/wiki/Castle" title="Castle">castle</a> on a hill opposite the Muslim fortress of <a href="/wiki/Fraxinet" class="mw-redirect" title="Fraxinet">Fraxinet</a> and led the Christians of <a href="/wiki/County_of_Provence" class="mw-redirect" title="County of Provence">Provence</a> to victory in battle with the Muslims in an unknown year.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBallan201026._The_&#39;&#39;Vita&#39;&#39;_calls_these_Muslims_from_Spain_&#39;&#39;hispanicolae&#39;&#39;._3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBallan201026._The_&#39;&#39;Vita&#39;&#39;_calls_these_Muslims_from_Spain_&#39;&#39;hispanicolae&#39;&#39;.-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> During the battle he had a mystical experience and vowed, if victorious, to renounce war and become a pilgrim devoted to the care of orphans and widows.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEMurray2002376_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMurray2002376-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> After the loss of his brother, he went on pilgrimage to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> and died at <a href="/wiki/Voghera" title="Voghera">Voghera</a> in <a href="/wiki/Lombardy" title="Lombardy">Lombardy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEFrance200510_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEFrance200510-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c2/Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg/220px-Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="319" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c2/Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg/330px-Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c2/Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg/440px-Saint_Bobo_of_Voghera.jpg 2x" data-file-width="682" data-file-height="989" /></a><figcaption>Saint Bobo depicted as a young knight in armour holding a cross.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Bobo is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Orthodox">Eastern Orthodox</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> churches. His feast day is May 22 and he is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of cattle.<sup id="cite_ref-LATIN-ORTHODOX_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LATIN-ORTHODOX-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
