<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; <p><b>Barlaam of Khutyn</b> (<a href="/wiki/Russian_language" title="Russian language">Russian</a>: <span lang="ru">Варлаам Хутынский</span>), also known as <b>Varlaam</b>, was a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>. Born Alexis Michalevich to a wealthy family from <a href="/wiki/Novgorod" class="mw-redirect" title="Novgorod">Novgorod</a>. After the death of his parents, he became a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Volkhov_River" class="mw-redirect" title="Volkhov River">Volkhov</a> and handed all of his inheritance to the poor. At this time he had gained many followers. So great were their numbers that he founded a monastery, the <a href="/wiki/Khutyn_Monastery" title="Khutyn Monastery">Khutyn Monastery of Saviour's Transfiguration</a>, and took the name of Barlaam (Varlaam). He died on 6 November 1192, his grave has become a site for <a href="/wiki/Christian_pilgrimage" title="Christian pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Varlaam is famous for healing the Grand Prince Vasily (from a family of Riazan boyars) when the prince was visiting Novgorod. Later he became a patron of the city of <a href="/wiki/Vologda" title="Vologda">Vologda</a>. He was also an accomplished painter, and painted several murals in the Gothic style in Novgorod, none of which survived destruction during the <a href="/wiki/Second_World_War" class="mw-redirect" title="Second World War">Second World War</a>.
</p>
