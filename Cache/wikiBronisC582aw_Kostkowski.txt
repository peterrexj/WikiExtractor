<p><b>Bronisław Kostkowski</b> (March 11, 1915 – September 27, 1942) was a <a href="/wiki/Polish_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Polish people">Polish</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> seminarian. He was born in <a href="/wiki/S%C5%82upsk" title="Słupsk">Słupsk</a>. He was imprisoned in the Nazi <a href="/wiki/Sachsenhausen_concentration_camp" title="Sachsenhausen concentration camp">Sachsenhausen concentration camp</a> and later died at the Nazi <a href="/wiki/Dachau_concentration_camp" title="Dachau concentration camp">Dachau concentration camp</a>. He is one of the <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Martyrs of World War II</a> who were <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by Pope John Paul II in 1999.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
