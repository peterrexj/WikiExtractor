<b>George Douglas</b> (<i>c</i>. 1540<sup id="cite_ref-auto_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>-1587) was one of the <a href="/wiki/Eighty-five_martyrs_of_England_and_Wales" title="Eighty-five martyrs of England and Wales">Eighty-five martyrs of England and Wales</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-BunsonBunson1999_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BunsonBunson1999-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Born in <a href="/wiki/Edinburgh" title="Edinburgh">Edinburgh</a>, he was originally a teacher by profession.<sup id="cite_ref-Watkins2015_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Watkins2015-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> His family were from <a href="/wiki/Bonjedward" title="Bonjedward">Bonjedward</a> near <a href="/wiki/Jedburgh" title="Jedburgh">Jedburgh</a> in the Scottish Borders.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Converting to <a href="/wiki/Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholicism">Catholicism</a>, he travelled to France around 1556<sup id="cite_ref-auto_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> where he was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> a <a href="/wiki/Secular_priest" class="mw-redirect" title="Secular priest">secular priest</a><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> in <a href="/wiki/Notre_Dame_de_Paris" class="mw-redirect" title="Notre Dame de Paris">Notre Dame</a>, Paris, in 1574- possibly at the testimonial of <a href="/wiki/Mary,_Queen_of_Scots" title="Mary, Queen of Scots">Mary, Queen of Scots</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Returning to the north of England, he was a priest in <a href="/wiki/York" title="York">York</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Watkins2015_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Watkins2015-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> where it seems he was 'apparelled in course canvas dublit and hose,' and in the <a href="/wiki/East_Midlands" title="East Midlands">East Midlands</a> as well.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Captured and found guilty in <a href="/wiki/York" title="York">York</a> of 'persuading <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_I_of_England" class="mw-redirect" title="Elizabeth I of England">the Queen's</a> subjects away' from <a href="/wiki/Protestantism" title="Protestantism">Protestantism</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Challoner1836_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Challoner1836-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> he was executed on 9 September 1587.<sup id="cite_ref-Watkins2015_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Watkins2015-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> on 22 November 1987.<sup id="cite_ref-BunsonBunson1999_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BunsonBunson1999-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
