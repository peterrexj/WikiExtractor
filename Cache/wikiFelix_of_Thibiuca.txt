					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">4th-century martyr</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">Not to be confused with the legendary <a href="/wiki/Felix_and_Adauctus" title="Felix and Adauctus">St Felix</a> who was supposedly martyred in 303 alongside "Adauctus"; with the <a href="/wiki/Felix,_Fortunatus,_and_Achilleus" title="Felix, Fortunatus, and Achilleus">St Felix</a> who was martyred alongside Fortunatus and Achilleus in 212; with the <a href="/wiki/Felix,_son_of_Felicitas" class="mw-redirect" title="Felix, son of Felicitas">St Felix</a> who was martyred alongside Januarius &amp; 5 other Holy Brothers, all sons of St&#160;Felicity of Rome; or with SS&#160;<a href="/wiki/Felix_and_Januarius" title="Felix and Januarius">Felix and Januarius</a> of Heraclea.</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Felix of Thibiuca</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Christian_martyrdom" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian martyrdom">Martyr</a> &amp; <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">Bishop</a></th></tr></tbody></table><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Audactus<br />Saint Fortunatus<br />Saint Januarius<br />Saint Septimus</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Christian_martyrdom" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian martyrdom">Martyrs</a></th></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Felix</b> (<abbr title="died">d.</abbr>&#160;303) was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of <a href="/wiki/Thibiuca" title="Thibiuca">Thibiuca</a> in <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Africa" title="Diocese of Africa">Africa</a> who was <a href="/wiki/Christian_martyrdom" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian martyrdom">martyred</a> during the <a href="/wiki/Great_Persecution" class="mw-redirect" title="Great Persecution">Great Persecution</a> under the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Empire" title="Roman Empire">Roman</a> <a href="/wiki/List_of_Roman_emperors" title="List of Roman emperors">emperor</a> <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a> alongside <b>Audactus</b>, <b>Fortunatus</b>, <b>Januarius</b>, and <b>Septimus</b>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Felix is said to have resisted the command of the local magistrate Magnillian (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Magnillianus</i>) to surrender his church's copies of the <a href="/wiki/Bible" title="Bible">Christian scriptures</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In one account, Felix and the others were taken to <a href="/wiki/Carthage" title="Carthage">Carthage</a> and <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">decapitated</a> on July 15.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> These <span class="nowrap"><b>Five Martyrs of Carthage</b></span> were venerated in the basilica of St&#160;Faustus.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTE&#39;&#39;DCB&#39;&#39;1882&#91;httpsbooksgooglecojpbooksid9zgMAAAAIAAJpgPA341_341&#93;_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTE&#39;&#39;DCB&#39;&#39;1882[httpsbooksgooglecojpbooksid9zgMAAAAIAAJpgPA341_341]-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Another placed his martyrdom at <a href="/wiki/Venosa" title="Venosa">Venosa</a> in <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His companions may have been <a href="/wiki/Deacon" title="Deacon">deacons</a> but, apart from their joint martyrdom with Felix, are now unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;n 1&#93;</a></sup> Their <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> was observed jointly on October 24.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEBunson_&amp;_al.2003-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Felix was formerly honored as the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Venosa" title="Venosa">Venosa</a>.
</p>
