<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the archangel, see <a href="/wiki/Michael_(archangel)" title="Michael (archangel)">Michael (archangel)</a>. <p><b>Michael de Sanctis</b> (<a href="/wiki/Catalan_language" title="Catalan language">Catalan</a>: <i lang="ca">Miquel dels Sants</i>) (29 September 1591 – 10 April 1625), sometimes called <i>Michael of the Saints</i>,<sup id="cite_ref-trinitarians.org_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-trinitarians.org-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was a <a href="/wiki/Discalced" title="Discalced">Discalced</a> <a href="/wiki/Trinitarian_Order" class="mw-redirect" title="Trinitarian Order">Trinitarian</a> born in <a href="/wiki/Vic" title="Vic">Vic</a>, a city of <a href="/wiki/Catalonia" title="Catalonia">Catalonia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a>.
</p><p>Born <b>Michael Argemir</b>, at the age of twelve, he came to <a href="/wiki/Barcelona" title="Barcelona">Barcelona</a> and asked to be received into the monastery of the Trinitarians there. After a three-year <a href="/wiki/Novitiate" title="Novitiate">novitiate</a>, he took his vows at that order's monastery of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Lambert_(martyr)" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Lambert (martyr)">St. Lambert</a> at <a href="/wiki/Zaragoza" title="Zaragoza">Zaragoza</a> in  1607. After meeting a Discalced Trinitarian one day, he felt drawn to that congregation's more austere lifestyle and, after much deliberation and the permission of his superior, he entered the congregation of the <a href="/w/index.php?title=Discalced_Trinitarians&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Discalced Trinitarians (page does not exist)">Discalced Trinitarians</a> at <a href="/wiki/Madrid" title="Madrid">Madrid</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Novice" title="Novice">novice</a>.  He then took his vows at <a href="/wiki/Alcal%C3%A1_de_Henares" title="Alcalá de Henares">Alcalá</a>, became a priest, and was twice elected superior of the monastery at <a href="/wiki/Valladolid" title="Valladolid">Valladolid</a>, where he died.
</p><p>During his life, Michael de Sanctis led a life of prayer and mortification.  He was devout towards the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Eucharist" class="mw-redirect" title="Holy Eucharist">Holy Eucharist</a>, and is said to have been experienced ecstasies several times during Consecration.
</p><p>Michael De Sanctis was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_VI" title="Pope Pius VI">Pope Pius VI</a> on 24 May 1779 and later <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_IX" title="Pope Pius IX">Pope Pius IX</a> on 8 June 1862. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated on 10 April. In images, he is usually depicted kneeling before an altar where the Blessed Sacrament is exposed.
</p><p>The municipality of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Michel-des-Saints,_Quebec" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Michel-des-Saints, Quebec">Saint-Michel-des-Saints, Quebec</a>, Canada, is named in his honor.
</p>
