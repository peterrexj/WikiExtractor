<p><b>Saint Saturius of Soria</b> (<a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>: <i lang="es">San Saturio</i>) (493–568) is a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>-<a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a>.  Born in <a href="/wiki/Soria" title="Soria">Soria</a>, Saturius, according to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> tradition, dedicated himself to a life of solitude and prayer.  He took care of his parents until their death, after which Saturius gave away his inheritance to the poor and at the age of forty, retired to a <a href="/wiki/Cave" title="Cave">cave</a> on the hill of Santa Ana (or Peñalba).  He lived for the rest of his days in complete austerity and dedicated himself to <a href="/wiki/Mortification_of_the_flesh" title="Mortification of the flesh">mortifications of the flesh</a>.
</p><p>His fame spread and Prudentius of Armentia sought his advice and wisdom.  Prudentius became Saturius' companion during the last seven years of the hermit's life and later buried him.  Prudentius would subsequently become bishop of <a href="/wiki/Tarazona" title="Tarazona">Tarazona</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saturius_of_Soria&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:152px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg/150px-Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg" decoding="async" width="150" height="202" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg/225px-Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/47/Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg/300px-Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg 2x" data-file-width="544" data-file-height="731" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Soria_Ermita_de_San_Saturio.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Hermitage_(religious_retreat)" title="Hermitage (religious retreat)">Hermitage</a> of San Saturio near Soria.</div></div></div>
<p>He was officially declared <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of Soria<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> in 1628, and was inscribed in the canon of saints by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a> in 1743.  Saturius' cave is still an object of <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a>.  Saturius' hermitage is mentioned by <a href="/wiki/Gustavo_Adolfo_B%C3%A9cquer" title="Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer">Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer</a> in this writer's <i>El Rayo de Luna</i>.
</p>
