					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">15th Bishop of Bayeux</div>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg/220px-Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="512" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg/330px-Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a3/Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg/440px-Framboldus_of_Bayeux.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1240" data-file-height="2888" /></a><figcaption>Framboldus of Bayeux.</figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Frambold</b> (in Latin: Framboldus) or sometimes Franbolt, Frambaud, Franbourd was the fifteenth <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Bayeux" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Bayeux">bishop of Bayeux</a> around 691 to 720.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
The life of Saint Frambold remains mysterious. The place and date of his birth are unknown.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Robert_C%C3%A9nalis" class="mw-redirect" title="Robert Cénalis">Robert Cénalis</a>, bishop of Avranches in the sixteenth century said of him only that "his holiness spread a lively radiance".<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> He had been a monk and abbot of the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Mans" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Mans">diocese of Mans</a>.
</p><p>Frambold is represented on a <a href="/wiki/Stained_glass_window" class="mw-redirect" title="Stained glass window">stained glass window</a> in the <a href="/wiki/North" title="North">north</a> <a href="/wiki/Transept" title="Transept">transept</a> of <a href="/wiki/Notre-Dame_de_Bayeux" class="mw-redirect" title="Notre-Dame de Bayeux">Notre-Dame de Bayeux</a> <a href="/wiki/Cathedral" title="Cathedral">cathedral</a> and in a painting on one of the <a href="/wiki/Vault_(architecture)" title="Vault (architecture)">vaults</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Choir" title="Choir">choir</a>.
</p><p>Frambold would have been buried in the Saint-Exupère church in Bayeux alongside the first bishops of Bayeux.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Controversy">Controversy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Framboldus_of_Bayeux&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Controversy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>There is no evidence for Frambold, or Framboldus, outside the 9th century episcopal list. Fisquet,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> questions his existence and Duchesne,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> does not include Framboldus in his list of actual bishops.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Framboldus_of_Bayeux&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Frambold is represented on a stained glass window in the north transept of <a href="/wiki/Bayeux_Cathedral" title="Bayeux Cathedral">Notre-Dame de Bayeux cathedral</a> and in painting on one of the <a href="/wiki/Vault_(architecture)" title="Vault (architecture)">vaults</a> of the choir.
</p><p>He is remembered in the church at <a href="/w/index.php?title=Manneville&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Manneville (page does not exist)">Manneville</a>.
</p><p>A <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> was celebrated locally on March 5 in his honor. This was suppressed by the Concordat and there was an interruption in the cult of the saint, although the tradition remained alive. During the reform of the Bayeusian liturgy in 1860, the Holy See initially opposed the resumption of worship, but a decree of June 13, 1861 allowed the feast of  Saint Frambold, to resume.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><br />
</p><p>His tomb was supposedly  found in 1853, but there remained some doubts about its authenticity and the identity of the bones it contained.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
