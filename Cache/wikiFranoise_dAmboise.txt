<p><b>Françoise d'Amboise</b> (9 May 1427<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> – 4 November 1485) was a <a href="/wiki/Frenchman" class="mw-redirect" title="Frenchman">French</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> declared "blessed" and a duchess consort of Brittany.
</p><p>She was born in the castle of <a href="/wiki/Thouars" title="Thouars">Thouars</a>. She was the daughter of the rich noble Louis d'Amboise, <a href="/wiki/Prince_of_Talmont" class="mw-redirect" title="Prince of Talmont">prince of Talmont</a> and <a href="/wiki/Viscount_of_Thouars" class="mw-redirect" title="Viscount of Thouars">Viscount of Thouars</a>, and <a href="/w/index.php?title=Louise-Marie_de_Rieux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Louise-Marie de Rieux (page does not exist)">Louise-Marie de Rieux</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Booten147_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Booten147-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> To escape from the violence of the times, she fled with her mother to the court of <a href="/wiki/Brittany" title="Brittany">Brittany</a>, which resided in <a href="/wiki/Vannes" title="Vannes">Vannes</a> and, later on, in <a href="/wiki/Nantes" title="Nantes">Nantes</a>. At the age of three she had been engaged to <a href="/wiki/Peter_II,_Duke_of_Brittany" title="Peter II, Duke of Brittany">Peter</a>, the second son of <a href="/wiki/John_V,_Duke_of_Brittany" title="John V, Duke of Brittany">John V, Duke of Brittany</a>, for political reasons. She married him at the age of fifteen, in 1442.<sup id="cite_ref-Booten147_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Booten147-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1450, after the unexpected death of Pierre's elder brother, her husband came to rule Brittany as Pierre II. Françoise d'Amboise became the <a href="/wiki/Duchess" class="mw-redirect" title="Duchess">Duchess</a> of Brittany and had a discrete but active share in governing Brittany. She came to help the poor and the sick. She had also a strong feeling about justice. Her husband died of a disease in 1457. She then entered into a conflict with King <a href="/wiki/Louis_XI_of_France" class="mw-redirect" title="Louis XI of France">Louis XI</a> who wanted to marry her. A widow without children, she founded, together with <a href="/w/index.php?title=Jean_Soreth&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Jean Soreth (page does not exist)">Jean Soreth</a>, the first <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Carmelites" title="Carmelites">Carmelites</a> in France, in 1463.
</p><p>She took the veil of a <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a> in 1468, when entering the convent of <i>the Three Maries</i> at <a href="/wiki/Vannes" title="Vannes">Vannes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Booten147_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Booten147-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> She died in <a href="/wiki/Nantes" title="Nantes">Nantes</a>, at the monastery of the Carmelite <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nuns</a>.
</p><p>In 1863, she was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by Pope Pius IX.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
