					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">English Roman Catholic priest and martyr</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Blessed</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Thomas Felton</div><br /><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">O.M.</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><span typeof="mw:File"><a href="/wiki/File:Blessed-thomas-felton.png" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a6/Blessed-thomas-felton.png/270px-Blessed-thomas-felton.png" decoding="async" width="270" height="357" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a6/Blessed-thomas-felton.png 1.5x" data-file-width="371" data-file-height="490" /></a></span><div class="infobox-caption">Fresco of Blessed Thomas Felton by Greco Bernardino within a church in the Diocese of Conversano - <p>
<b>Thomas Felton</b> (1566?–1588) was a <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Minims" title="Order of Minims">Franciscan Minim</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> martyr and son of the Roman Catholic martyr, <a href="/wiki/John_Felton_(martyr)" title="John Felton (martyr)">John Felton</a>. 
</p><p>Felton was  born around 1566 at <a href="/wiki/Bermondsey_Abbey" title="Bermondsey Abbey">Bermondsey Abbey</a>, Surrey, and was about four years old when his father was executed.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> When still young, he served as a page to Lady Lovett. He was then sent to the <a href="/wiki/English_College,_Rheims" class="mw-redirect" title="English College, Rheims">English College, Rheims</a>, where he received the first tonsure from the hands of the <a href="/wiki/Louis_II,_Cardinal_of_Guise" class="mw-redirect" title="Louis II, Cardinal of Guise">Cardinal de Guise</a>, archbishop of Rheims, in 1583.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He then entered the order of Minims, but its austerities undermined his health and he returned to England,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTECooper1889316_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTECooper1889316-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> to settle his property and make provision for his profession.
</p><p>Attempting to return to France, Felton was arrested on the coast, brought to London, and committed to the <a href="/wiki/Poultry_Compter" title="Poultry Compter">Poultry Compter</a>. About two years later his aunt, Mrs. Blount, obtained his release through the interest of some of her friends at court. He attempted to return to France, but was again intercepted and committed to <a href="/wiki/Bridewell_Palace" title="Bridewell Palace">Bridewell prison</a>. After some time he regained his liberty, and made a second attempt to get back to Rheims, but was rearrested and recommitted to Bridewell, where he was put into <a href="/wiki/Little_Ease" title="Little Ease">Little Ease</a> and otherwise tortured.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTECooper1889316_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTECooper1889316-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was brought to trial at <a href="/wiki/Newgate" title="Newgate">Newgate</a>, just after the defeat of the <a href="/wiki/Spanish_Armada" title="Spanish Armada">Spanish Armada</a>, and was asked whether, if the Spanish forces had landed, he would have taken the part of Queen <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_I_of_England" class="mw-redirect" title="Elizabeth I of England">Elizabeth</a>. His reply was that he would have taken part with God and his country. But he refused to acknowledge the queen to be the <a href="/wiki/Supreme_Head_of_the_Church_of_England" title="Supreme Head of the Church of England">Supreme Head of the Church of England</a>, and was accordingly condemned to death. The next day, 28 August 1588, he and another priest, named James Claxton (or Clarkson), were conveyed on horseback from <a href="/wiki/Bridewell" class="mw-redirect" title="Bridewell">Bridewell</a> to the place of execution, between <a href="/wiki/Brentford" title="Brentford">Brentford</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hounslow" title="Hounslow">Hounslow</a>, and were there <a href="/wiki/Hanged,_drawn_and_quartered" title="Hanged, drawn and quartered">hanged and quartered</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTECooper1889316_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTECooper1889316-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="James_Claxton">James Claxton</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thomas_Felton_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: James Claxton">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>James Claxton was from Yorkshire. He went to the English College at Douai, which had relocated temporarily to Rheims, and was ordained at Soissons 9 June 1582. He set out for England with fellow-priest Richard Evens on 24 July. At some point he was captured and exiled in 1585. He returned to England, was again arrested and sent to the <a href="/wiki/Marshalsea_prison" class="mw-redirect" title="Marshalsea prison">Marshalsea prison</a>. On 28 August 1588, he was taken with Thomas Felton to a place near Brentford, (likely "Bush Corner") and hanged.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> All told eight were executed on this day in and around London.
</p>
