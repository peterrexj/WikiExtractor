<p><b>Noël Chabanel</b> (February 2, 1613 &#8211; December 8, 1649) was a <a href="/wiki/Jesuit" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesuit">Jesuit</a> <a href="/wiki/Missionary" title="Missionary">missionary</a> at <a href="/wiki/Sainte-Marie_among_the_Hurons" title="Sainte-Marie among the Hurons">Sainte-Marie among the Hurons</a>, and one of the <a href="/wiki/Canadian_Martyrs" title="Canadian Martyrs">Canadian Martyrs</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Pouliot_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pouliot-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=No%C3%ABl_Chabanel&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Chabanel entered the Jesuit novitiate at <a href="/wiki/Toulouse" title="Toulouse">Toulouse</a> at the age of seventeen, and was a professor of <a href="/wiki/Rhetoric" title="Rhetoric">rhetoric</a> at several Jesuit colleges. He was highly esteemed for virtue and learning. In 1643, he was sent to <a href="/wiki/New_France" title="New France">New France</a> along with Fathers Leonard Garreau and Gabriel Druillettes. Although he studied the <a href="/wiki/Algonquin_language" title="Algonquin language">Algonquin language</a> for a time, he never made much headway.<sup id="cite_ref-tjc_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tjc-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was appointed to the mission at Sainte-Marie.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In his apostolic labours he was the companion of Fr. <a href="/wiki/Charles_Garnier_(missionary)" title="Charles Garnier (missionary)">Charles Garnier</a>.
</p><p>As he felt a strong repugnance to the life and habits of the <a href="/wiki/Wyandot_people" title="Wyandot people">Huron</a>, and feared it might result in him withdrawing from the work, he bound himself by vow never to leave the mission except under obedience. Chabanel was sent to assist <a href="/wiki/Jean_de_Br%C3%A9beuf" title="Jean de Brébeuf">Jean de Brébeuf</a> at the mission of Saint Louis (near the present day hamlet of <a href="/wiki/Tay,_Ontario" title="Tay, Ontario">Victoria Harbour</a>), but was replaced by in February 1649 by <a href="/wiki/Gabriel_Lalemant" title="Gabriel Lalemant">Gabriel Lalemant</a>. Chabanel was sent to help <a href="/wiki/Charles_Garnier_(missionary)" title="Charles Garnier (missionary)">Charles Garnier</a> among the <a href="/wiki/Petun" title="Petun">Petun</a>. One month later, Brébeuf and Lalemant were captured in an Iroquois raid on the St. Louis mission and taken to the nearby mission off St. Ignace where they were killed.<sup id="cite_ref-tjc_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tjc-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After the deaths of Brébeuf and Lalement, the Jesuits decided to abandon <a href="/wiki/Sainte-Marie_among_the_Hurons" title="Sainte-Marie among the Hurons">Sainte-Marie among the Hurons</a> and burned the mission rather than risk it being desecrated or taken over by Iroquois. In early December 1649, Chabanel was directed to go to <a href="/wiki/Christian_Island" title="Christian Island">St. Joseph Island</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-tjc_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tjc-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Chabanel was martyred on December 8, 1649, by what is described as a "renegade" Huron.<sup id="cite_ref-Spillane_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Spillane-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> There was a strong presumption that he was killed by the man who offered to carry him across. Father Paul Ragueneau, Provincial Superior, noted that Honarreennha was known to have believed and spread a false rumor that the French had betrayed the Huron and made a secret treaty with the Iroquois; and later admitted killing Chabanel.<sup id="cite_ref-Pouliot_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pouliot-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=No%C3%ABl_Chabanel&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Noël Chabanel was canonized by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> on 29 June 1930.
</p>
