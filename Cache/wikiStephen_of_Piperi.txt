<p><b>Saint Stephen of Piperi</b> (<a href="/wiki/Serbian_language" title="Serbian language">Serbian</a>: <span lang="sr">Свети Стефан Пиперски</span>) (died May 20, 1697) is a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Serbian_Orthodox_Church" title="Serbian Orthodox Church">Serbian Orthodox Church</a>.
</p><p>He was born into the <a href="/wiki/Nik%C5%A1i%C4%87_(tribe)" class="mw-redirect" title="Nikšić (tribe)">Nikšić</a> clan in the village of Kuti in <a href="/w/index.php?title=%C5%BDupa_(Montenegro)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Župa (Montenegro) (page does not exist)">Župa</a> of poor but devout parents, Radoje and Jaćima Krulanović. According to tradition, he first lived a life of <a href="/wiki/Asceticism" title="Asceticism">asceticism</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Mora%C4%8Da_monastery" class="mw-redirect" title="Morača monastery">Morača monastery</a> where he was <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Ottoman_Empire" title="Ottoman Empire">Turks</a> drove him out of <a href="/wiki/Mora%C4%8Da" title="Morača">Morača</a> and he settled in <a href="/w/index.php?title=Rovacki&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Rovacki (page does not exist)">Rovacki</a>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Turmanj&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Turmanj (page does not exist)">Turmanj</a> in the place which today is called <a href="/w/index.php?title=Celi%C5%A1te&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Celište (page does not exist)">Celište</a>. In 1660 he settled in <a href="/wiki/Piperi_clan" class="mw-redirect" title="Piperi clan">Piperi</a> in a cell where he remained in labor and asceticism until his death. He died peacefully on May 20, 1697. His relics still repose there and are claimed to produce <a href="/wiki/Miracle" title="Miracle">miracles</a>. His feast day is celebrated on the anniversary of his repose, on <a href="/wiki/May_20_(Eastern_Orthodox_liturgics)" title="May 20 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)">May 20</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
