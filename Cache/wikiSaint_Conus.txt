<p><b>Cono</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> monk. He was born in <a href="/wiki/Teggiano" title="Teggiano">Diano</a> (<a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a>) in the late 12th century, and became a monk in S.Maria di Cadossa Benedictine Monastery (now St. Cono sanctuary) near <a href="/wiki/Montesano_sulla_Marcellana" title="Montesano sulla Marcellana">Montesano sulla Marcellana</a>. He died very young in the early years of the 13th century with a reputation for holiness. When Cadossa monastery was closed his relics were returned to <a href="/wiki/Teggiano" title="Teggiano">Diano</a> in 1261, where he is venerated as its patron saint.
</p><p>The Saint was canonized on April 27, 1871 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_IX" title="Pope Pius IX">Pope Pius IX</a>.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (October 2009)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> The feast is celebrated on June 3. Celebrations in his honor are celebrated in <a href="/wiki/Florida,_Uruguay" title="Florida, Uruguay">Florida (Uruguay)</a>, <a href="/wiki/Teggiano" title="Teggiano">Teggiano</a>, <a href="/wiki/Buenos_Aires" title="Buenos Aires">Buenos Aires</a>, <a href="/wiki/Brooklyn" title="Brooklyn">Brooklyn</a>, <a href="/wiki/San_Mauro_Pascoli" title="San Mauro Pascoli">San Mauro Pascoli</a>, and <a href="/w/index.php?title=San_Cono_(Cessaniti)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="San Cono (Cessaniti) (page does not exist)">San Cono di Cessaniti</a>.
</p>
