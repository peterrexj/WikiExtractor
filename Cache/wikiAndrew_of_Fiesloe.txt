					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">9th-century Italian Catholic saint</div>

<p><b>Andrew the Scot</b> (also known as <b>Andrew of Tuscany</b> and <b>Andrew of Fiesole</b>) was the Irish-born student and assistant of <a href="/wiki/Donatus_of_Fiesole" title="Donatus of Fiesole">St. Donatus</a>. He served as <a href="/wiki/Archdeacon" title="Archdeacon">archdeacon</a> of <a href="/wiki/Fiesole" title="Fiesole">Fiesole</a> under Bishop Donatus.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Andrew_the_Scot&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Andrew the Scot was born in Ireland near the beginning of the ninth century to a noble family. (He later became known as “the Scot”, common in that day when speaking of someone from Ireland, which the Romans had called <i>Scotia</i>.)
</p><p>He was the brother of <a href="/wiki/Bridget_of_Fiesole" title="Bridget of Fiesole">St. Brigid the younger</a>. Both Andrew and his sister studied under <a href="/wiki/Donatus_of_Fiesole" title="Donatus of Fiesole">St. Donatus</a>. In 816 Andrew accompanied Donatus on his pilgrimage to Italy.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When Donatus and Andrew arrived at Fiesole the people were assembled to elect a new <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a>. Donatus was chosen, and after being consecrated to that office, made Andrew his archdeacon.<sup id="cite_ref-Thurston_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thurston-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>There is a miracle reported of his healing the daughter of a nobleman. The girl had been paralysed and the doctors were unable to help her so their father asked Andrew to come and pray for her. Kneeling by her couch he told her to stand for Jesus had healed her. Many other miracles were performed by him over the course of his deaconship in Fiesole: casting out demons, healing the blind, and the sick.<sup id="cite_ref-Ohanlon_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ohanlon-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f7/San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG/220px-San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="150" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f7/San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG/330px-San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f7/San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG/440px-San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG 2x" data-file-width="2592" data-file-height="1773" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:San_martino_a_mensola_00.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>San Martino a Mensola</div></div></div>
<p>During the forty-seven years of Donatus' <a href="/wiki/Episcopate" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopate">episcopate</a>, Andrew served him faithfully. The bishop encouraged Andrew to restore the church of San Martino di Mensola and to found a <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> there.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Andrew is commended for his austerity of life and boundless charity to the poor.
</p><p>He died a few years after Donatus around 880.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> His sister, seemingly miraculously, arrived from Ireland in time to assist at his deathbed.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Andrew_the_Scot&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His body is buried at St Martin's, the church he restored. When at a later date his remains were exhumed, his body was found still preserved. His relics continue to be venerated in that church.<sup id="cite_ref-Ohanlon_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ohanlon-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>St. Andrew's feast day is on the 22 of August.
</p>
