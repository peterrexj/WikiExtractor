<p>Saint <b>Ansegisus</b> (c. 770 &#8211; 20 July 833 or 834) was a monastic reformer of the <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Franks</a>.
</p><p>Born about 770, of noble parentage, at the age of eighteen he entered the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint_Wandrille" title="Abbey of Saint Wandrille">monastery of Fontenelle</a> (also called St Wandrille after the name of its founder) in the diocese of Rouen. Saint <a href="/wiki/Girowald" class="mw-redirect" title="Girowald">Girowald</a>, a relative of Ansegisus, was then Abbot of Fontanelle. Upon the recommendation of the abbot Girowald he was entrusted by the Emperor <a href="/wiki/Charlemagne" title="Charlemagne">Charlemagne</a> with the government and reform of two monasteries, St. Sixtus near Reims and St. Memmius (St. Menge) in the diocese of <a href="/wiki/Ch%C3%A2lons-sur-Marne" class="mw-redirect" title="Châlons-sur-Marne">Châlons-sur-Marne</a>, in which he was successful.<sup id="cite_ref-ott_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ott-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 817, <a href="/wiki/Louis_the_Pious" title="Louis the Pious">Louis the Pious</a> made him abbot of the famous <a href="/wiki/Luxeuil_Abbey" title="Luxeuil Abbey">Luxeuil Abbey</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-ott_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ott-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> founded by Saint <a href="/wiki/Columbanus" title="Columbanus">Columbanus</a> as early as 590. Finally, having also reformed Luxeuil, he was transferred in 823, after the death of Einhard, as abbot to Fontenelle, where he had begun his monastic life and which he reformed as successfully as the previous monasteries.<sup id="cite_ref-ott_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ott-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was responsible for compiling a number of <a href="/wiki/Capitularies" class="mw-redirect" title="Capitularies">capitularies</a>, a document of civil and ecclesiastical law,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> and also commissioned the first abbey history, the <i><a href="/wiki/Gesta_abbatum_Fontanellensium" title="Gesta abbatum Fontanellensium">Gesta abbatum Fontanellensium</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
