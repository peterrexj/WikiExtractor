<p><b>Ursmar of Lobbes</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> (died 713) was a missionary bishop in the <a href="/wiki/Meuse" title="Meuse">Meuse</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ardennes" title="Ardennes">Ardennes</a> region in present-day Belgium, Germany, Luxemburg and France. He was also the first abbot of <a href="/wiki/Lobbes_Abbey" title="Lobbes Abbey">Lobbes Abbey</a>.
</p><p>As many missionaries in the 7th and 8th century, he may have been of Irish origin. He was appointed <a href="/wiki/Abbot_of_Lobbes" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbot of Lobbes">abbot of Lobbes</a> in 691 by the <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> king <a href="/wiki/Pippin_II" class="mw-redirect" title="Pippin II">Pippin II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He is also credited with the foundation of <a href="/wiki/Aulne_Abbey" title="Aulne Abbey">Aulne Abbey</a> and <a href="/w/index.php?title=Wallers_Abbey&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Wallers Abbey (page does not exist)">Wallers Abbey</a>.
</p><p>Saint Ursmar is a Catholic saint, whose feast day is April 19.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> His <a href="/wiki/Sarcophagus" title="Sarcophagus">sarcophagus</a> is in the crypt of the parish church in <a href="/wiki/Lobbes" title="Lobbes">Lobbes</a> (as well as the sarcophagus of his successor, <a href="/wiki/Ermin_of_Lobbes" title="Ermin of Lobbes">Saint Ermin</a>. A Life was written by <a href="/wiki/Heriger_of_Lobbes" title="Heriger of Lobbes">Heriger of Lobbes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
