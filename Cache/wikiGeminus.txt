					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Ancient Greek astronomer and mathematician</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox biography vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above" style="font-size:125%;"><div class="fn" style="display:inline">Geminus</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-subheader" style="font-size:125%; font-weight:bold;"><div class="nickname" lang="el">Γεμῖνος ὁ Ῥόδιος</div></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Nationality</th><td class="infobox-data category">Greek</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Known&#160;for</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Astronomer" title="Astronomer">Astronomer</a> and mathematician</td></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-full-data"><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><b>Scientific career</b></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Academic advisors</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Posidonius" title="Posidonius">Posidonius</a></td></tr><tr style="display:none"><td colspan="2">
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Geminus</b> of <a href="/wiki/Rhodes" title="Rhodes">Rhodes</a> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">Γεμῖνος ὁ Ῥόδιος</span>), was a <a href="/wiki/Ancient_Greece" title="Ancient Greece">Greek</a> <a href="/wiki/Astronomer" title="Astronomer">astronomer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Mathematician" title="Mathematician">mathematician</a>, who flourished in the 1st century BC. An <a href="/wiki/Astronomy" title="Astronomy">astronomy</a> work of his, the <i>Introduction to the Phenomena</i>, still survives; it was intended as an introductory astronomy book for students. He also wrote a work on <a href="/wiki/Mathematics" title="Mathematics">mathematics</a>, of which only fragments quoted by later authors survive.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Geminus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Nothing is known about the life of Geminus. It is not even certain that he was born in Rhodes, but references to mountains on Rhodes in his astronomical works suggests that he worked there. His dates are not known with any certainty either. A passage in his works referring to the <i>Annus Vagus</i> (Wandering Year) of the <a href="/wiki/Egyptian_calendar" title="Egyptian calendar">Egyptian calendar</a> of 120 years before his own time, has been used to imply a date of c. 70 BC for the time of writing,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> which would be consistent with the idea that he may have been a pupil of <a href="/wiki/Posidonius" title="Posidonius">Posidonius</a>, but a date as late as 50 AD has also been suggested.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The crater <a href="/wiki/Geminus_(crater)" title="Geminus (crater)">Geminus</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Moon" title="Moon">Moon</a> is named after him.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (December 2021)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Astronomy">Astronomy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Geminus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Astronomy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The only work of Geminus to survive is his <i>Introduction to the Phenomena</i> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">Εἰσαγωγὴ εἰς τὰ Φαινόμενα</span>), often just called the <i>Isagoge</i>.  This introductory astronomy book, based on the works of earlier astronomers such as <a href="/wiki/Hipparchus" title="Hipparchus">Hipparchus</a>, was intended to teach astronomy for beginning students in the subject. In it, Geminus describes the <a href="/wiki/Zodiac" title="Zodiac">zodiac</a> and the motion of the <a href="/wiki/Sun" title="Sun">Sun</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Constellation" title="Constellation">constellations</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Celestial_sphere" title="Celestial sphere">celestial sphere</a>, days and nights, the risings and settings of the zodiacal signs, luni-solar periods and their application to <a href="/wiki/Calendars" class="mw-redirect" title="Calendars">calendars</a>, <a href="/wiki/Phases_of_the_Moon" class="mw-redirect" title="Phases of the Moon">phases of the Moon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eclipses" class="mw-redirect" title="Eclipses">eclipses</a>, star phases, terrestrial zones and geographical places, and the foolishness of making weather predictions by the stars.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He also wrote a commentary on Posidonius' work <i>On Meteorology</i>. Fragments of this commentary are preserved by <a href="/wiki/Simplicius_of_Cilicia" title="Simplicius of Cilicia">Simplicius</a> in his commentary on <a href="/wiki/Aristotle" title="Aristotle">Aristotle's</a> <i>Physics</i>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mathematics">Mathematics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Geminus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Mathematics">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Geminus also wrote extensively on <a href="/wiki/Mathematics" title="Mathematics">mathematics</a>, including a comprehensive <i>Doctrine, (or Theory) of Mathematics.</i><sup id="cite_ref-heath1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-heath1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Although this work has not survived, many extracts are preserved by <a href="/wiki/Proclus" title="Proclus">Proclus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eutocius" class="mw-redirect" title="Eutocius">Eutocius</a>, and others. He divided mathematics into two parts: <i>Mental</i> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">νοητά</span>) and <i>Observable</i> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">αἰσθητά</span>), or in other words, <a href="/wiki/Pure_mathematics" title="Pure mathematics">Pure</a> and <a href="/wiki/Applied_mathematics" title="Applied mathematics">Applied</a>. In the first category he placed <a href="/wiki/Geometry" title="Geometry">geometry</a> and <a href="/wiki/Arithmetic" title="Arithmetic">arithmetic</a> (including <a href="/wiki/Number_theory" title="Number theory">number theory</a>), and in the second category he placed <a href="/wiki/Mechanics" title="Mechanics">mechanics</a>, <a href="/wiki/Astronomy" title="Astronomy">astronomy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Optics" title="Optics">optics</a>, <a href="/wiki/Geodesy" title="Geodesy">geodesy</a>, canonics (<a href="/wiki/Harmony" title="Harmony">musical harmony</a>), and <a href="/wiki/Logistics" title="Logistics">logistics</a>. Long extracts of his work are also preserved by <a href="/wiki/Al-Nayrizi" title="Al-Nayrizi">Al-Nayrizi</a> in his commentary on <a href="/wiki/Euclid%27s_Elements" title="Euclid&#39;s Elements">Euclid's <i>Elements</i></a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Geminus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Notes">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1011085734">.mw-parser-output .reflist{font-size:90%;margin-bottom:0.5em;list-style-type:decimal}.mw-parser-output .reflist .references{font-size:100%;margin-bottom:0;list-style-type:inherit}.mw-parser-output .reflist-columns-2{column-width:30em}.mw-parser-output .reflist-columns-3{column-width:25em}.mw-parser-output .reflist-columns{margin-top:0.3em}.mw-parser-output .reflist-columns ol{margin-top:0}.mw-parser-output .reflist-columns li{page-break-inside:avoid;break-inside:avoid-column}.mw-parser-output .reflist-upper-alpha{list-style-type:upper-alpha}.mw-parser-output .reflist-upper-roman{list-style-type:upper-roman}.mw-parser-output .reflist-lower-alpha{list-style-type:lower-alpha}.mw-parser-output .reflist-lower-greek{list-style-type:lower-greek}.mw-parser-output .reflist-lower-roman{list-style-type:lower-roman}</style><div class="reflist">
<div class="mw-references-wrap"><ol class="references">
