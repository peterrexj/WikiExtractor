<p><b>Platon the <a href="/wiki/Monastery_of_Stoudios" title="Monastery of Stoudios">Studite</a></b>, also <b>Plato of Sakkoudion</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">Ὅσιος Πλάτων τῆς Μονῆς τῶν Σακκουδίων</span>), probably <a href="/wiki/Constantinople" title="Constantinople">Constantinople</a>, ca. 735 – Constantinople, 4 April 814, was a <a href="/wiki/Byzantine" class="mw-redirect" title="Byzantine">Byzantine</a> minor official who became a monk in 759. After refusing the <a href="/wiki/Metropolis_of_Nicomedia" title="Metropolis of Nicomedia">metropolitan see of Nicomedia</a> or the headship of a monastery in Constantinople, in 783 he founded the monastery of Sakkoudion on <a href="/wiki/Uluda%C4%9F" title="Uludağ">Mount Olympus</a> in <a href="/wiki/Bithynia" title="Bithynia">Bithynia</a>, of which he became the first abbot. He is notable, along with his nephew <a href="/wiki/Theodore_Stoudites" class="mw-redirect" title="Theodore Stoudites">Theodore Stoudites</a>, for his <a href="/wiki/Iconodule" class="mw-redirect" title="Iconodule">iconodule</a> stance during the <a href="/wiki/Byzantine_Iconoclasm" title="Byzantine Iconoclasm">Byzantine Iconoclasm</a> and his participation in the <a href="/wiki/Second_Council_of_Nicaea" title="Second Council of Nicaea">Second Council of Nicaea</a>, and to his firm opposition to the second marriage of Emperor <a href="/wiki/Constantine_VI" title="Constantine VI">Constantine VI</a> to his (Platon's) niece <a href="/wiki/Theodote" title="Theodote">Theodote</a> (the "<a href="/w/index.php?title=Moechian_Controversy&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Moechian Controversy (page does not exist)">Moechian Controversy</a>"). He was canonized by the Church, and his feast day is <a href="/wiki/April_4_(Eastern_Orthodox_liturgics)" title="April 4 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)">April 4</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
