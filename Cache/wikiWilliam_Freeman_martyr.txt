<p>

<b>William Freeman</b> (c. 1558 – 13 August 1595) was an English <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> priest during the <a href="/wiki/English_Reformation" title="English Reformation">Reformation</a>. He was convicted of <a href="/wiki/Treason" title="Treason">treason</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hanged,_drawn_and_quartered" title="Hanged, drawn and quartered">hanged, drawn and quartered</a> at <a href="/wiki/Warwick" title="Warwick">Warwick</a>. He is one of 136 Catholic <a href="/wiki/List_of_Catholic_martyrs_of_the_English_Reformation" title="List of Catholic martyrs of the English Reformation">martyrs</a> who were <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pius_XI" class="mw-redirect" title="Pius XI">Pius XI</a> in 1929.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=William_Freeman_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
The 1913 Catholic Encyclopedia states:</p><blockquote><p>[He was] born at <a href="/w/index.php?title=Manthorp&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Manthorp (page does not exist)">Manthorp</a> near York. His parents were <a href="/wiki/Recusant" class="mw-redirect" title="Recusant">recusant</a> Catholic, but he was conforming <a href="/wiki/Anglican" class="mw-redirect" title="Anglican">Anglican</a> for some time. He was educated at <a href="/wiki/Magdalen_College,_Oxford" title="Magdalen College, Oxford">Magdalen College, Oxford</a>, and took his degree as BA in 1581. He then lived for some years in <a href="/wiki/London" title="London">London</a>. He witnessed the execution of <a href="/wiki/Edward_Stransham" title="Edward Stransham">Edward Stransham</a> in 1586. Strongly impressed with this example, he left <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a> and was ordained priest in 1587 at <a href="/wiki/Reims" title="Reims">Reims</a>. Returning to England in 1589, he worked for six years on the borders of <a href="/wiki/Warwickshire" title="Warwickshire">Warwickshire</a>. In January, 1595, a special commission was sent down to <a href="/wiki/Stratford-on-Avon" class="mw-redirect" title="Stratford-on-Avon">Stratford-on-Avon</a> to search the house of Mrs Heaths who had engaged his services as tutor to her son. William Freeman was arrested, and spent seven months in prison. He denied his priesthood, but also refused all offers to escape. Owing to the treachery of a fellow-prisoner, William Gregory, he was at last sentenced as a seminary priest and in spite of a protest of loyalty, suffered the death of a traitor at Warwick.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
