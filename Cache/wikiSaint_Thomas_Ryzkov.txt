<div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">A Russian repentant Saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Thomas Ryzkov</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Goodman Tom, Wolfman Tom</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> late 18th century<br />Russia</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Hometown</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Tobolsk" title="Tobolsk">Tobolsk</a>, Russia</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">Tobolsk, Russia</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Saint_symbolism" title="Saint symbolism">Attributes</a></th><td class="infobox-data">Repentance</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><div style="display: inline-block; line-height: 1.2em; padding: .1em 0;">Tradition or genre</div></th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Thomas Ryzkov</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">Θωμάς Ριζκόφ</span>, <small><a href="/wiki/Romanization_of_Greek" title="Romanization of Greek">romanized</a>:&#160;</small><span title="Greek-language romanization"><i lang="el-Latn">Thomás Rizkóf</i></span>) was an <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox</a> saint from <a href="/wiki/Tobolsk" title="Tobolsk">Tobolsk</a>, Russia, born <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> late 18th century. His story is recounted in the book <i>Pascha Transforms Wolfman Tom</i> published by St Anthony's Monastery.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thomas_Ryzkov&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thomas_Ryzkov&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Thomas Ryzkov was imprisoned by judicial error at a young age in <a href="/wiki/Siberia" title="Siberia">Siberia</a> near the <a href="/wiki/Ural_Mountains" title="Ural Mountains">Ural Mountains</a> in the early 1800s. He escaped and committed multiple murders out of rage during the subsequent years near the region of <a href="/wiki/Tobolsk" title="Tobolsk">Tobolsk</a>, earning him the name Wolfman Tom.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Conversion">Conversion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thomas_Ryzkov&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Conversion">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Upon receiving a <a href="/wiki/Easter_egg" title="Easter egg">paschal egg</a> from a child, he changed his life and repented sincerely for his sins. He was granted total <a href="/wiki/Amnesty" title="Amnesty">amnesty</a> by <a href="/wiki/Tsar_Nicholas_I" class="mw-redirect" title="Tsar Nicholas I">Tsar Nicholas I</a> and lived the rest of his life in service to others, often exclaiming "<a href="/wiki/Paschal_greeting" title="Paschal greeting">Christ is Risen</a>!" His name was changed from Wolfman Tom to Goodman Tom.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thomas_Ryzkov&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>During a hunting expedition, Prince Mistislav <a href="/wiki/Kanev" title="Kanev">Kanev</a> (former marshal of <a href="/wiki/Kyiv" title="Kyiv">Kyiv</a>) witnessed Saint Thomas Ryzkov's death as a solitary <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> and proclaimed that he was a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. The prince saw angels descend from heaven to retrieve the body of the saint. Various miracles took place around the saint both during his life, during his repose, and afterwards.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
