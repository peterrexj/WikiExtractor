<p><b>Juan Nepomuceno Zegrí Moreno</b> (11 October 1831 - 17 March 1905) was a <a href="/wiki/Spanish_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Spanish people">Spanish</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> and the founder of the Mercedarian Sisters of Charity (1878).<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Moreno was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> as a priest in 1855 and was accused of misconduct in 1888 and the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Holy See</a> ordered him to leave the congregation as an investigation took place; he was vindicated in 1894.<sup id="cite_ref-OV_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OV-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CO_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> presided over Moreno's beatification on 9 November 2003.<sup id="cite_ref-HS_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Juan_Nepomuceno_Zegr%C3%AD_Moreno&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Juan Nepomuceno Zegrí Moreno was born in <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a> on 11 October 1831 to Antonio de León Zegrí Abril Martín (b. November 1808) and Josefa Moreno Escudero.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-GL_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GL-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His siblings were:
</p>
<ul><li>Francisca Josefa de León Zegrí Moreno</li>
<li>Antonio Zegrí Moreno</li>
<li>María Josefa Zegrí Moreno</li>
<li>María de las Mercedes Zegrí Moreno</li>
<li>Enriqueta Zegrí Moreno<sup id="cite_ref-GL_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GL-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup></li></ul>
<p>Juan felt a call to the <a href="/wiki/Priesthood" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood">priesthood</a> during his childhood and he commenced his studies to become a priest at the San Dionisio <a href="/wiki/Seminary" title="Seminary">seminary</a> in <a href="/wiki/Granada" title="Granada">Granada</a>. He was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> as a priest on 2 June 1855 in the <a href="/wiki/Granada_Cathedral" title="Granada Cathedral">Granada Cathedral</a> and became the parish priest at Huétor Santillán and later at San Gabriel de Loja; he also served as a canon of the <a href="/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_Cathedral" title="Málaga Cathedral">Málaga Cathedral</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Moreno also served as a <a href="/wiki/Spiritual_direction" title="Spiritual direction">spiritual director</a> to <a href="/wiki/Seminarians" class="mw-redirect" title="Seminarians">seminarians</a> and as a preacher and chaplain to <a href="/wiki/Queen_Isabel_II" class="mw-redirect" title="Queen Isabel II">Queen Isabel II</a> until her deposition. He went to Málaga following the deposition of the queen and began finalizing his plans for the establishment of a new religious order.<sup id="cite_ref-OV_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OV-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Before he was ordained he had been a friend of Bishop Juan Nepomuceno Cascallana Ordóñez.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The order - titled the Mercedarian Sisters of Charity - was founded on 16 March 1878 and was aggregated to the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_the_Blessed_Virgin_Mary_of_Mercy" title="Order of the Blessed Virgin Mary of Mercy">Mercedarians</a> on 9 June 1878.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The congregation later received the decree of praise on 25 September 1900 and full papal approval from <a href="/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIII" title="Pope Leo XIII">Pope Leo XIII</a> on 24 April 1901.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Moreno was embroiled in a scandal in 1888 when the religious of his order accused him of misconduct and it resulted in a decree from the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Holy See</a> on 7 July 1888 demanding he leave and keep his distance from the congregation.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-HS_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-HS-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> An extensive investigation followed in which Moreno kept silent and adhered to the orders of his superiors until a decree from the Holy See on 15 July 1894 vindicated him of all false allegations. Some sisters however were not inclined to accept this verdict which resulted in some in the order refusing to acknowledge Moreno as its founder.<sup id="cite_ref-OV_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OV-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died on 17 March 1905. He was re-recognized as the congregation's founder in 1925.<sup id="cite_ref-OV_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OV-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His order now operates in nations such as the <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Republic" title="Dominican Republic">Dominican Republic</a> and <a href="/wiki/South_Korea" title="South Korea">South Korea</a> and as of 2005 had 1234 religious in a total of 156 houses.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Juan_Nepomuceno_Zegr%C3%AD_Moreno&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The beatification process opened in an informative process that spanned from 5 July 1958 until it was closed on 7 June 1962. The formal opening of the cause on 5 July 1958 under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XII" title="Pope Pius XII">Pope Pius XII</a> conferred the title of <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> upon the late priest. The <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> validated the process in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> on 7 February 1992. The C.C.S. received the <a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">Positio</a> at the beginning of 1993 and passed it onto a board of historians who granted their assent to the continuation of the cause on 26 January 1993. Theologians approved it on 14 November 2000 and the C.C.S. also approved it on 4 December 2001.
</p><p>Moreno was declared to be <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> on 20 December 2001 after <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> recognized that he had lived a life of <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a>.
</p><p>The miracle required for beatification was investigated and later received C.C.S. validation in Rome on 12 February 1993 which allowed for a team of medical experts to approve the miracle in a meeting on 18 April 2002 and for theologians to follow suit on 18 October 2002. The C.C.S. voted in favor of the miracle on 7 January 2003 and the pope himself did so as well on 12 April 2003.
</p><p>John Paul II beatified Moreno on 9 November 2003.
</p>
