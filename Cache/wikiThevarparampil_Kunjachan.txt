<p><b>Thevarparambil Kunjachan</b> (born <b>Augustine Thevarparambil</b>) was an Indian <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Syro-Malabar Catholic">Syro-Malabar Catholic</a> priest who dedicated himself to the spiritual and temporal welfare of marginalized <a href="/wiki/Dalit" title="Dalit">Dalits</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thevarparampil_Kunjachan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Augustine was born on 1 April 1891, at <a href="/wiki/Ramapuram,_Kottayam" title="Ramapuram, Kottayam">Ramapuram</a> in <a href="/wiki/Travancore" title="Travancore">Travancore</a> (present-day Kerala, India). He was the son of Itty Iype and Eliswa of the Thevarparambil clan, which is a branch of the Kuzhumbil family lineage.  Augustine received his primary education at a school sponsored by the government of Travancore. He advanced to St. Ephrem's School in Mannanam where his religious faith intensified, compelling him to pursue a career as a priest. After completing in secondary education at St Ephrem's School, Augustine embarked on a journey on foot from Ramapuram to <a href="/wiki/Changanassery" title="Changanassery">Changanacherry</a> in order to enroll in St Thomas Minor Seminary. After receiving his major seminary formation at <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Our_Lady_of_Dolours,_Thrissur" title="Basilica of Our Lady of Dolours, Thrissur">Puthenpally</a> Seminary in <a href="/wiki/Varappuzha" title="Varappuzha">Varapuzha</a>, Augustine was ordained as a priest on July 16, 1915.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_career">Early career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thevarparampil_Kunjachan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early career">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After celebrating his first mass at St. Augustine Church in Ramapuram, his home parish, Augustine resided in his home village for eight years while undergoing practical training. He was known as <i>Kunjachan</i> ('little priest' in Malayalam) for his short stature. In 1923, Augustine was appointed as assistant vicar to Thomas Kuzhumpil at St. Sebastian Church in Kadanad.
</p><p>During his vocation in Kadanad, Kunjachan was often approached by the local villagers, who relied on his advice and blessings for their agricultural yield. Kunjachan would sprinkle holy water on crops, ensuring a plentiful harvest. Unfortunately, an illness forced Kunjachan to return to Ramapuram.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Missionary_work">Missionary work</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thevarparampil_Kunjachan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Missionary work">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Ramapuram, Kunjachan witnessed an entire <a href="/wiki/Caste_system_in_India" title="Caste system in India">caste</a> of people deprived of dignity, respect, and the basic necessities of life. He would devote his entire life to the uplifting of the downtrodden. It is the singular achievement of Kunjachan that he had been able to baptize by himself no less than 5,000 <a href="/wiki/Dalit" title="Dalit">Dalits</a> (earlier the term '<a href="/wiki/Harijan" class="mw-redirect" title="Harijan">Harijan</a>' was used). He is to be counted as one of the foremost missionaries among the Dalits in India. In fact, he neither made use of eloquent sermons nor of any human techniques in order to give them the "Christ" of whom he was the herald. He preached with his works, practising charity, and always sublime and more dedicated.
</p><p>Kunjachan led a very simple life for the poor and wished to be with them even after death. He spent everything he had for the poor. The Blessed who was a priest for more than 50 years, wrote in his Testament: "I do not possess anything either as landed property or as cash account... After my death, my funeral must be conducted in the most simple way. Ever since 1926, I had been staying with the Harijan (Dalit) Christians. Even after death, I would like to be with them. Therefore my dead body should be buried where the Harijan Christians are buried".
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thevarparampil_Kunjachan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After a brief period of serious illness Kunjachan died on 16 October 1973 at the age of 82. At his death the children and others told that 'a saint has passed away'. At his funeral the priest who preached the <a href="/wiki/Panegyric" title="Panegyric">panegyric</a>, spoke well of his holiness in life, apostolic zeal, kindness of heart, love for the poor and other outstanding virtues. And towards the end of the speech he said to the surprise of all, "we are participating in the funeral of a saint. We have one more mediator in heaven."
</p><p>Kunjachan had the reputation of a holy man even while he was alive. People irrespective of caste and religion, used to approach him in their manifold needs and they got favors through his prayers and blessings. Within a few days after his death his tomb at Ramapuram became a center of pilgrimage for people from far and wide.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification_and_canonization">Beatification and canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thevarparampil_Kunjachan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Beatification and canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:202px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Kunjachan_blessed_ceremony.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4d/Kunjachan_blessed_ceremony.jpg" decoding="async" width="200" height="134" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="200" data-file-height="134" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Kunjachan_blessed_ceremony.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Beatified by Varkey Vithayathil, Major Archbishop of the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Church" title="Syro-Malabar Church">Syro-Malabar Church</a> on 30 April 2006.Participated by Papal Nuncio to India Archbishop Pedro Lopez Quintana and Cardinal Telesphore Toppo, President of the Catholic Bishops Conference of India (CBCI).</div></div></div>
<p>The process of <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> and <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonization</a> started on 11 August 1987 at Ramapuram, bestowing upon him the title, '<a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a>'. The 'Positio super vita et virtutibus' was
completed after ten years and was submitted at the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> on 12 February 1997. The '<a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">positio</a>' on the heroic practice of virtues of the saintly priest was approved by Pope <a href="/wiki/John_Paul_II" class="mw-redirect" title="John Paul II">John Paul II</a> on 22 June 2004 and he was declared '<a href="/wiki/The_Venerable" title="The Venerable">The Venerable</a>'.
</p><p>Meanwhile, the process of the miraculous cure of the <a href="/wiki/Clubfoot" title="Clubfoot">clubfoot</a> of a boy, Gilson Varghese, was undertaken, and the findings were sent to Rome for consideration. After a thorough investigation of the miraculous nature of the cure, made by personnel in Rome, Pope <a href="/wiki/Benedict_XVI" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedict XVI">Benedict XVI</a> approved it, opening the way for beatification. Kunjachan was beatified by Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Varkey_Vithayathil" title="Varkey Vithayathil">Varkey Vithayathil</a>, Major Archbishop of the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Syro-Malabar Catholic Church">Syro-Malabar Church</a> on 30 April 2006 at the very same village Ramapuram where he was born, worked, died and buried.
</p><p>The Papal Nuncio to India Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Pedro_L%C3%B3pez_Quintana" title="Pedro López Quintana">Pedro López Quintana</a>, Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Telesphore_Toppo" title="Telesphore Toppo">Telesphore Toppo</a>, President of the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Bishops_Conference_of_India" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic Bishops Conference of India">Catholic Bishops Conference of India</a> (CBCI), and other bishops and priests participated in the ceremony.
</p><p>The Feast of Blessed Kunjachan is celebrated on 16 October every year.
</p><p>Kunjachan had his last times at family house Thervarparampil Ramapuram.
</p>
