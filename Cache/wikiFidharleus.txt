<p>Saint <b>Fiodhairle Ua Suanaigh</b> (or <b>Fidharleus, Fidarle, Fidairle</b>, died 762) was an Irish abbot who was considered to be a saint. His feast day is 1 October.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Fiodhairle_Ua_Suanaigh&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fiodhairle Ua Suanaigh was an 8th-century abbot of the monastery in <a href="/wiki/Rahan,_County_Offaly" title="Rahan, County Offaly">Rahan, County Offaly</a>.
He is said to have belonged to the Hy Fiachrach family, as did Saint <a href="/wiki/Colman_mac_Duagh" title="Colman mac Duagh">Colman of Kilmacduach</a>, and to have been brother to Fiodhmuine Ua Suanaigh.
The <a href="/wiki/Bollandist" title="Bollandist">Bollandists</a> refer to Colgan and give his feast day as 1 October.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18757_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18757-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
The <i><a href="/wiki/Martyrology_of_Tallaght" title="Martyrology of Tallaght">Martyrology of Tallaght</a></i> also says his feast day was 1 October.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18757_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18757-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
The <i>Martyrology of Marianus O'Gorman</i> says he was a "white-fair prince" and notes his Suanach descent and Rathen abbey.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18758_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18758-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
The 17th-century <i><a href="/wiki/Martyrology_of_Donegal" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrology of Donegal">Martyrology of Donegal</a></i> says he died in 762.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18759_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEO&#39;Hanlon18759-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Older spellings of the Rahan abbey name are Rathen, Raithen and Raithean, meaning "ferney place". 
The O'Swany (Ua Suanaigh) family were the hereditary successors to <a href="/wiki/Saint_Carthage" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Carthage">Saint Carthage</a> at the abbey from the 8th century.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEMarcella_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMarcella-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
Fiodhairle repaired the abbey after a <a href="/wiki/Vikings" title="Vikings">Viking</a> raid.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESaint_Fidharleus_CatholicSaints.info_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESaint_Fidharleus_CatholicSaints.info-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Monks_of_Ramsgate_account">Monks of Ramsgate account</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Fiodhairle_Ua_Suanaigh&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Monks of Ramsgate account">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The monks of <a href="/wiki/St_Augustine%27s_Abbey,_Ramsgate" title="St Augustine&#39;s Abbey, Ramsgate">St Augustine's Abbey, Ramsgate</a>, wrote in their <i>Book of Saints</i> (1921),
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>FIDHARLEUS (St.) Abbot (October 1)<br /> (8th century) An Irish Saint, the second founder of Rathin Abbey. He died A.D. 762.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESt._Augustine&#39;s_Abbey,_Ramsgate1921111_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESt._Augustine&#39;s_Abbey,_Ramsgate1921111-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> </p></blockquote>
<h2><span id="Butler.27s_account"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Butler's_account">Butler's account</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Fiodhairle_Ua_Suanaigh&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Butler&#039;s account">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The hagiographer <a href="/wiki/Alban_Butler" title="Alban Butler">Alban Butler</a> wrote in his <i>Lives of the fathers, martyrs, and other principal saints</i>,
</p>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r996844942"/><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>Saint Fidharleus of Ireland, Abbot.
The Irish calendars commemorate on this day Saint Fidharleus, abbot of Raithen, who departed to our Lord in 762. See Colgan, MSS.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEButlerButler184237_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEButlerButler184237-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> </p></blockquote>
