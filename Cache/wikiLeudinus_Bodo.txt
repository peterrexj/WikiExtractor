					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Leudinus_Bodo&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="Leudinus Bodo">Leudinus Bodo</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Leudinus Bodo</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was a seventh-century <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Toul" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Toul">bishop of Toul</a>, successor to <a href="/w/index.php?title=Eborinus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Eborinus (page does not exist)">Eborinus</a>, or Elbonirus.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He occurs in hagiographies.  His feast day is Sept. 11.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is traditionally known as the founder of Bodonis Monasterium (the monastery of Bodo), later called Bon-Moutier (Bonmoutier, Bon Moustiers).<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Bonmoutier is in the modern <a href="/wiki/Val-et-Ch%C3%A2tillon" title="Val-et-Châtillon">Val-et-Châtillon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Vosges" title="Vosges">Vosges</a>.
</p><p>He is said to have been born around 625, in <a href="/wiki/Bassigny" class="mw-redirect" title="Bassigny">Bassigny</a>, to <a href="/wiki/Gundoin" class="mw-redirect" title="Gundoin">Gundoin</a> and Saratrude of the <a href="/wiki/Etichonids" title="Etichonids">Etichonids</a>, a family of the <a href="/wiki/Austrasia" title="Austrasia">Austrasian</a> nobility. His sister was <a href="/wiki/Sadalberga" title="Sadalberga">Sadalberga</a>, who founded the monastery at <a href="/wiki/Laon" title="Laon">Laon</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> He founded also the <a href="/w/index.php?title=Abbey_of_%C3%89tival&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Abbey of Étival (page does not exist)">Abbey of Étival</a> (Stivagium, Abbaye Saint-Pierre d'Étival<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>),<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> dated to 663<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> and the <a href="/w/index.php?title=Abbey_of_Othonville&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Abbey of Othonville (page does not exist)">Abbey of Othonville</a>, and died around 678.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
