<p><b>Cyril</b> (also Cyrillus or Cyryl) is a masculine <a href="/wiki/Given_name" title="Given name">given name</a>. It is derived from the <a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a> name <span title="Greek-language text"><span lang="el">Κύριλλος</span></span> (<i>Kýrillos</i>), meaning 'lordly, masterful', which in turn derives from Greek <span title="Ancient Greek (to 1453)-language text"><span lang="grc">κυριος</span></span> (<i><a href="/wiki/K%C3%BDrios" class="mw-redirect" title="Kýrios">kýrios</a></i>) '<a href="/wiki/Lord" title="Lord">lord</a>'. There are various variant forms of the name <i>Cyril</i> such as <i>Cyrill</i>, <i>Cyrille</i>, <i>Ciril</i>, <i>Kirill</i>, <i>Kiryl</i>, <i>Kirillos</i>, <i><a href="/wiki/Kyrylo" title="Kyrylo">Kyrylo</a></i>, <i>Kiril</i>, <i>Kiro</i>, <i>Kyril</i> and <i>Kyrill</i>.
</p><p>It may also refer to:
