<p><b>Wilfrid II</b> (died on 29 April in either 745 or 746), name also spelled <b>Wilfrith</b>, also known as <b>Wilfrid the Younger</b>, was the last <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_York#Angles,_Vikings_and_Medieval_times" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of York">Bishop of York</a>, as the see was converted to an <a href="/wiki/Archbishopric" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishopric">archbishopric</a> during the time of his successor.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Wilfrid_II_(bishop_of_York)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Wilfrid was a monk at <a href="/wiki/Whitby" title="Whitby">Whitby</a> and studied there when <a href="/wiki/Hilda_of_Whitby" title="Hilda of Whitby">Hilda</a> was <a href="/wiki/Abbess" title="Abbess">abbess</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-BSE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BSE-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> In 718 he was consecrated as <a href="/wiki/Coadjutor_bishop" title="Coadjutor bishop">coadjutor bishop</a> to <a href="/wiki/John_of_Beverley" title="John of Beverley">John of Beverley</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Handbook224_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handbook224-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Wilfrid was described as a very holy man, and interested in education.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh624_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh624-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Wilfrid resigned the <a href="/wiki/Diocese" title="Diocese">bishopric</a> in 732.<sup id="cite_ref-Handbook224_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handbook224-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He died on 29 April in either 745 or 746,<sup id="cite_ref-BSE_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BSE-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and was buried at <a href="/wiki/Ripon_Cathedral" title="Ripon Cathedral">Ripon</a>, but it may have been his body that was later translated to Canterbury in the mistaken belief that it was that of the earlier Wilfrid.<sup id="cite_ref-Blair560_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blair560-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> The younger Wilfrid is considered a saint, with his feast day being 29 April.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh624_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh624-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> However, he was never the object of strong cult, and only occasional mentions of him occur in <a href="/wiki/Martyrologies" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrologies">martyrologies</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-ODS538_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ODS538-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
