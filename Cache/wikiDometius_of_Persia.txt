<p>Saint <b>Dometius</b> (<i>Domitius</i>) <b>the Persian</b> (died 363) is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>.  According to tradition, he was martyred by <a href="/wiki/Lapidation" class="mw-redirect" title="Lapidation">lapidation</a> during the reign of <a href="/wiki/Julian_the_Apostate" class="mw-redirect" title="Julian the Apostate">Julian the Apostate</a> with two companions.<sup id="cite_ref-stpat_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stpat-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He was killed at <a href="/wiki/Nisibis" class="mw-redirect" title="Nisibis">Nisibis</a> in <a href="/wiki/Mesopotamia" title="Mesopotamia">Mesopotamia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-stpat_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stpat-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The name Domitius appears three times in the <i><a href="/wiki/Roman_Martyrology" title="Roman Martyrology">Roman Martyrology</a></i> on different <a href="/wiki/Feast_days" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast days">feast days</a> (August 7, March 23, July 5); “it is uncertain that they were indeed the same person.”<sup id="cite_ref-stpat_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stpat-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Dometius of Persia was depicted in an 8th-century <a href="/wiki/Fresco" title="Fresco">fresco</a> in the church of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Antiqua" title="Santa Maria Antiqua">Santa Maria Antiqua</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ó_Carragáin_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ó_Carragáin-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  This may indicate that there were Greek-speaking monks at Santa Maria Antiqua,<sup id="cite_ref-Ó_Carragáin_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ó_Carragáin-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> as evidenced by these frescoes, which not only depicted Dometius but also Saints <a href="/wiki/Barachisius" class="mw-redirect" title="Barachisius">Barachisius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Euthymius_the_Great" title="Euthymius the Great">Euthymius</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Sabas_of_Palestine" class="mw-redirect" title="Sabas of Palestine">Sabas of Palestine</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Ó_Carragáin_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ó_Carragáin-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
