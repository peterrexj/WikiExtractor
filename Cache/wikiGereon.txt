<p><b>Gereon of Cologne</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Géréon</i>), who may have been a <a href="/wiki/Soldier" title="Soldier">soldier</a>, was <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyred</a> at <a href="/wiki/Cologne" title="Cologne">Cologne</a> by <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">beheading</a>, probably in the early 4th century.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gereon&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the Roman Martyrology, "In Cologne in Germany, the Saints Gereon and his companions, martyrs, who with sincere piety, courageously offered their necks to the sword."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> That brief outline is the official account of the church, that is, the martyrdom by beheading, in the famous German city, of a group of Christians headed by a certain Gereon. In fact, nothing more can be said about them with historical certainty. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gereon&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to his legend, Gereon (called the "Golden Saint") was said to be a soldier<sup id="cite_ref-koln_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koln-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> of the <a href="/wiki/Theban_Legion" title="Theban Legion">Theban Legion</a>.  <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a>, writing in the 6th century, said that Gereon and his companions were a detachment of fifty men of the Theban Legion who were massacred at <a href="/wiki/Agaunum" title="Agaunum">Agaunum</a> by order of <a href="/wiki/Maximian" title="Maximian">Emperor Maximian</a> for refusing to sacrifice to <a href="/wiki/Roman_mythology" title="Roman mythology">pagan gods</a> to obtain victory in battle.
</p><p>Some of his companions' names are stated as being Cassius, Gregorius Maurus, Florentius, Innocentius (Innocent), Constantinus, and Victor.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Saint_Bede" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Bede">Bede</a> mentions that their feast was included in the <a href="/wiki/Sarum_Rite" class="mw-redirect" title="Sarum Rite">Sarum</a> <a href="/wiki/Calendar" title="Calendar">calendar</a>, as well as the calendars of <a href="/wiki/Barking,_London" title="Barking, London">Barking</a> and <a href="/wiki/Durham,_England" title="Durham, England">Durham</a>. Later medieval legends increased the number of Gereon's companions to 290 or 319, and <a href="/wiki/Norbert_of_Xanten" title="Norbert of Xanten">Norbert of Xanten</a> is said to have discovered, through a vision, the spot at Cologne where the <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Ursula" title="Saint Ursula">Ursula</a> and her companions, of Gereon, and of other martyrs lay hidden.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Gereon became a popular <a href="/wiki/Military_saint" title="Military saint">military saint</a> and is often represented in art as a <a href="/wiki/Legionary" title="Legionary">Roman soldier</a> or <a href="/wiki/Knight" title="Knight">medieval knight</a>. Along with other saints who were beheaded, he is invoked by those suffering from <a href="/wiki/Migraine_headache" class="mw-redirect" title="Migraine headache">migraine headaches</a>. <a href="/wiki/H%C3%A9linand_of_Froidmont" class="mw-redirect" title="Hélinand of Froidmont">Hélinand of Froidmont</a>'s <i>Martyrium</i> mentions Saint Gereon.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gereon&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/St._Gereon%27s_Basilica" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Gereon&#39;s Basilica">St. Gereon's Basilica</a>, in Cologne, is dedicated to him.<sup id="cite_ref-koln_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-koln-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Stefan_Lochner" title="Stefan Lochner">Stefan Lochner</a> painted a triptych in the 15th century which, in the centre piece, shows in almost life-size figures the worshipping of the Magi, and the side panels of which represent Ursula with her companions, and Gereon with his warriors. In 1810 the triptych was moved from the town hall to the choir chapel of the cathedral.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Saint-G%C3%A9r%C3%A9on" title="Saint-Géréon">Saint-Géréon</a> is a small town located in the department of Loire-Atlantique of the French region Pays de la Loire.<sup id="cite_ref-ehess_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ehess-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The martyr is depicted on the 13th century seal of the Convent of St. Gereon, Cologne.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
