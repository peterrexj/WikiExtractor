<p><b>Saint Romanus of Condat</b> (<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;390</span> – <span title="circa">c.</span><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;463</span>) is a saint of the fifth century.  At the age of thirty five he decided to live as a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> in the area of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Claude,_Jura" title="Saint-Claude, Jura">Condat</a>.  His younger brother <a href="/wiki/Lupicinus_of_Condat" title="Lupicinus of Condat">Lupicinus</a> followed him there.  They became leaders of a community of monks that included <a href="/wiki/Saint_Eugendus" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Eugendus">Saint Eugendus</a>.  
</p><p>Romanus and Lupicinus founded several monasteries.  These included <a href="/wiki/Condat_Abbey" title="Condat Abbey">Condat Abbey</a>, which was the nucleus of the later town of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Claude,_Jura" title="Saint-Claude, Jura">Saint-Claude, Jura</a>), Lauconne (later <a href="/wiki/Saint-Lupicin" title="Saint-Lupicin">Saint-Lupicin</a>, as Lupicinus was buried there), La Balme (Beaume) (later <a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint-Romain-de-Roche&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Saint-Romain-de-Roche (page does not exist)">Saint-Romain-de-Roche</a>), where Romanus was buried, and <a href="/wiki/Romainm%C3%B4tier_Abbey" class="mw-redirect" title="Romainmôtier Abbey">Romainmôtier</a> (<i>Romanum monasterium</i>), now in the <a href="/wiki/Canton_of_Vaud" class="mw-redirect" title="Canton of Vaud">canton of Vaud</a> in <a href="/wiki/Switzerland" title="Switzerland">Switzerland</a>.
</p><p>Romanus was <a href="/wiki/Ordination" title="Ordination">ordained</a> a priest by <a href="/wiki/St._Hilary_of_Arles" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Hilary of Arles">St. Hilary of Arles</a> in 444.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sources_on_Romanus">Sources on Romanus</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Romanus_of_Condat&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Sources on Romanus">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Two lives of him are in existence: one by <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a> in the <i>Liber vitae patrum</i> (<a href="/wiki/Mon._Germ._Hist." class="mw-redirect" title="Mon. Germ. Hist.">Mon. Germ. Hist.</a>: Script. Merov., I, 663), and an anonymous <i><a href="/wiki/Vita_patrum_Iurensium" title="Vita patrum Iurensium">Vita Sanctorum Romani, Lupicini, Eugendi</a></i> [ibid., III, 131 sqq.; cf. Benoît, "Histoire de St-Claude", I (Paris, 1890); Besson, "Recherches sur les origines des évêchés de Genève, Lausanne, et Sion" (Fribourg, 1906), 210 sqq.].
</p>
