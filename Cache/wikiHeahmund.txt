<p><b>Heahmund</b> was a medieval <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Sherborne_(ancient)" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Sherborne (ancient)">Bishop of Sherborne</a>.
</p><p>Heahmund was <a href="/wiki/Consecration#Christianity" title="Consecration">consecrated</a> in 867 or 868.<sup id="cite_ref-Handbook222_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handbook222-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He died at the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Meretun" title="Battle of Meretun">Battle of Meretun</a> in 871. As his death is assigned to 22 March in the English calendar of saints the battle and his death can be dated 22 March 871.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was buried at <a href="/wiki/Keynsham" title="Keynsham">Keynsham</a> in <a href="/wiki/Somerset" title="Somerset">Somerset</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Heahmund is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
