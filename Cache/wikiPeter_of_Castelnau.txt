<p><b>Pierre de Castelnau</b> (? - died 15 January 1208), French ecclesiastic, made papal legate in 1199 to address the Cathar heresy, he was subsequently murdered in 1208. Following his death <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a> beatified him by papal order, excommunicated Count Raymond VI of Toulouse, and declared the Albigensian crusade.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pierre_de_Castelnau&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Pierre was born in the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Montpellier" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Montpellier">diocese of Montpellier</a>. He became archdeacon of <a href="/wiki/Maguelone_Cathedral" title="Maguelone Cathedral">Maguelonne</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEOldenbourg201512_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEOldenbourg201512-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and in 1199 was appointed by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a> as one of the <a href="/wiki/Papal_legate" title="Papal legate">papal legates</a> for the suppression of the <a href="/wiki/Cathar" class="mw-redirect" title="Cathar">Cathar</a> heresy in <a href="/wiki/Languedoc" title="Languedoc">Languedoc</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESumption197868_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESumption197868-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 1202, he made profession as a <a href="/wiki/Cistercian" class="mw-redirect" title="Cistercian">Cistercian</a> monk at the <a href="/wiki/Fontfroide_Abbey" title="Fontfroide Abbey">abbey of Fontfroide</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESumption197868_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESumption197868-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Narbonne" title="Narbonne">Narbonne</a>, and by 1203 was confirmed as <a href="/wiki/Papal_legate" title="Papal legate">papal legate</a> and chief <a href="/wiki/Inquisitor" title="Inquisitor">inquisitor</a>, first in <a href="/wiki/Languedoc" title="Languedoc">Languedoc</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEGraham-Leigh200576_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGraham-Leigh200576-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> and afterwards at <a href="/wiki/Viviers,_Ard%C3%A8che" title="Viviers, Ardèche">Viviers</a> and Montpellier.
</p><p>In 1207, Pierre was appointed  was in the <a href="/wiki/Rh%C3%B4ne" title="Rhône">Rhone valley</a> and in <a href="/wiki/Provence" title="Provence">Provence</a>, where he became involved in the strife between the <a href="/wiki/Count_of_Baux" class="mw-redirect" title="Count of Baux">count of Baux</a> and <a href="/wiki/Raymond_VI,_Count_of_Toulouse" title="Raymond VI, Count of Toulouse">Raymond VI, Count of Toulouse</a>. Castelnau was assassinated on 15 January 1208,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEMadaule1967165_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMadaule1967165-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> possibly by an agent of Raymond,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESumption197815_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESumption197815-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> but this was never proven.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTETaylor201189_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTETaylor201189-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Nevertheless, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a> held Raymond responsible and Pierre's murder was the immediate cause of Raymond's <a href="/wiki/Excommunication" title="Excommunication">excommunication</a> and the start of the <a href="/wiki/Albigensian_Crusade" title="Albigensian Crusade">Albigensian Crusade</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESumption197877_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESumption197877-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEGraham-Leigh20052_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEGraham-Leigh20052-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Pierre was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a>, through papal order, in 1208 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTERyan20043-4_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTERyan20043-4-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> The relics of Pierre de Castelnau are interred in the church of the ancient <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint-Gilles" title="Abbey of Saint-Gilles">Abbey of St-Gilles</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESumption197815_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESumption197815-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
