<p><b>Pierre Henri Dorie</b> (1839–1866) was a French missionary of the <a href="/wiki/Paris_Foreign_Missions_Society" title="Paris Foreign Missions Society">Paris Foreign Missions Society</a>, who was martyred in <a href="/wiki/Korea" title="Korea">Korea</a> in 1866.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His feast day is March 7,<sup id="cite_ref-martyrology_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-martyrology-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> and he is also venerated along with the rest of the 103 <a href="/wiki/Korean_Martyrs" title="Korean Martyrs">Korean Martyrs</a> on September 20.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pierre-Henri_Dorie&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Henri Dorie was born on 23 September 1839 in <a href="/wiki/Talmont-Saint-Hilaire" title="Talmont-Saint-Hilaire">Saint-Hilaire-de-Talmont</a>.
</p><p>Following the arrest and execution of Bishop <a href="/wiki/Sim%C3%A9on-Fran%C3%A7ois_Berneux" title="Siméon-François Berneux">Siméon-François Berneux</a> on 7 March 1866, all but three of the French missionaries in Korea were also captured and executed: among them were Bishop <a href="/wiki/Marie-Nicolas-Antoine_Daveluy" title="Marie-Nicolas-Antoine Daveluy">Antoine Daveluy</a>, Father Just de Bretenières, Father <a href="/wiki/Louis_Beaulieu" title="Louis Beaulieu">Louis Beaulieu</a>, Father Dorie, Father Pierre Aumaître, Father Martin-Luc Huin, all of them members of the Paris Foreign Missions Society.
</p><p>The persecutions triggered the <a href="/wiki/French_campaign_against_Korea_(1866)" class="mw-redirect" title="French campaign against Korea (1866)">French Campaign against Korea</a> in October–November 1866.
</p><p>Like the other martyrs, Pierre Henri Dorie was <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> on 6 May 1984 under the name Peter Henricus Dorie.
</p>
