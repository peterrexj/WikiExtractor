<p><b>Christian</b>, also known as <b>Gilla Críst Ua Morgair</b> (<a href="/wiki/Irish_language" title="Irish language">Modern Irish</a>: <b>Croistan Ó Morgair</b>), was a bishop and brother of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Malachy" title="Saint Malachy">St. Malachy of Armagh</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> In 1126, Christian was named the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Clogher" title="Bishop of Clogher">Bishop of Clogher</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a>, a position he held until his death on 12 June 1138. He was buried at the Abbey of St. Peter and Paul in <a href="/wiki/Armagh" title="Armagh">Armagh</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Visio_Tnugdali"><a href="/wiki/Visio_Tnugdali" title="Visio Tnugdali">Visio Tnugdali</a></span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Christian_of_Clogher&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Visio Tnugdali">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
The Visio Tnugdali, written <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;1149</span>, makes reference to Christian as follows: </p><blockquote><p>When <a href="/wiki/Ruadh%C3%A1n_of_Lorrha" title="Ruadhán of Lorrha">Saint Ruadan</a> had fallen silent, Tundale looked happily about him and saw <a href="/wiki/Saint_Patrick" title="Saint Patrick">Saint Patrick of Ireland</a>, dressed in shining robes alongside many bishops decked out in their finest regalia. They were all joyful and there was no sound of any sighing! Among that blessed company Tundale could see four bishops whom he recognised... The third bishop that Tundale recognised was Malachias O'Moore's brother, <b>the former bishop of Clogher</b>, a wealthy but a retiring man.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
