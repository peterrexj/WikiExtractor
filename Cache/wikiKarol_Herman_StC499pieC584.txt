					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Polish Roman Catholic priest and martyr</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox biography vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above" style="font-size:125%;"><div class="fn" style="display:inline">Karol Herman Stępień</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:B%C5%82._o._Karol_Herman_St%C4%99pie%C5%84.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Bł. o. <p><b>Karol Herman Stępień</b> (1910–1943) was a Polish Roman Catholic martyr.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Karol_Herman_St%C4%99pie%C5%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Stępień was born on October 21, 1910 in <a href="/wiki/Lodz" class="mw-redirect" title="Lodz">Lodz</a>, Poland.<sup id="cite_ref-andreasresch_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-andreasresch-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He grew up in a poor family in Lodz.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Stępień was educated in Lodz.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He attended the Franciscan seminary in <a href="/wiki/Lviv" title="Lviv">Lviv</a>, graduating in 1929.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He then attended the <a href="/wiki/Pontifical_University_of_St._Bonaventure" title="Pontifical University of St. Bonaventure">Pontifical University of St. Bonaventure</a> in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was ordained as a <a href="/wiki/Franciscan" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan">Franciscan</a> priest in 1937 in Rome.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Stępień returned to Poland, where he earned a Master's degree in Theology from <a href="/wiki/Lviv_University" class="mw-redirect" title="Lviv University">Lviv University</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Vocation">Vocation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Karol_Herman_St%C4%99pie%C5%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Vocation">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He served as a Francisco priest in <a href="/wiki/Radomsko" title="Radomsko">Radomsko</a> and <a href="/wiki/Vilnius" title="Vilnius">Vilnius</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 1940, he was asked by Bishop Kazimierz Bukraba of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Pinsk" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Pinsk">Roman Catholic Diocese of Pinsk</a> to go to <a href="/w/index.php?title=Piar%C5%A1ai&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Piaršai (page does not exist)">Piaršai</a> to help their parish priest, Achille Puchala.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When the Nazis invaded in 1943, Stepien decided to stay and keep preaching.<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He declared: "Pastors cannot leave the believers!".<sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Karol_Herman_St%C4%99pie%C5%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On July 19, 1943, the Nazis took Stępień, Puchala and their parishioners to a barn in <a href="/w/index.php?title=Borowikowszczyzna&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Borowikowszczyzna (page does not exist)">Borowikowszczyzna</a>, which they set on fire, thus murdering them all together.<sup id="cite_ref-andreasresch_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-andreasresch-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-swzygmunt_2-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-swzygmunt-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-nominis_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Karol_Herman_St%C4%99pie%C5%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Stępień was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by Pope <a href="/wiki/John_Paul_II" class="mw-redirect" title="John Paul II">John Paul II</a> on June 13, 1999 in <a href="/wiki/Warsaw" title="Warsaw">Warsaw</a>, Poland.<sup id="cite_ref-andreasresch_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-andreasresch-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-nominis_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
