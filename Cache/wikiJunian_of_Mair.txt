<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Junian</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">587<br />France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">13 August</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Junian</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Saint Junien</i>) was a 6th-century <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> and <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a>.  He was the founder of Mairé, or Mariacum, Abbey at <a href="/wiki/Mair%C3%A9-Levescault" title="Mairé-Levescault">Mairé-Levescault</a> in <a href="/wiki/Poitou" title="Poitou">Poitou</a>, <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a> and is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Poitou" title="Poitou">Poitou</a> ploughmen.
</p><p>He was born at the beginning of the 6th century, in 500 or 501, in the area of "Briosso" (now called <a href="/w/index.php?title=Brian%C3%A7ais&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Briançais (page does not exist)">Briançais</a> or <a href="/w/index.php?title=Brioux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Brioux (page does not exist)">Brioux</a>) near or on an estate named "Champagné" which still exists (<a href="/wiki/Champagn%C3%A9-le-Sec" title="Champagné-le-Sec">Champagné-le-Sec</a>),<sup id="cite_ref-junien_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-junien-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and close to the village of <a href="/wiki/Mair%C3%A9" title="Mairé">Mairé</a> in the commune of <a href="/wiki/Perign%C3%A9" class="mw-redirect" title="Perigné">Perigné</a>, Deux-Sèvres.
</p><p>He was educated by his parents who were <a href="/wiki/Gallo-Roman" class="mw-redirect" title="Gallo-Roman">Gallo-Roman</a> nobility. Although his name is undoubtedly of <a href="/wiki/Paganism" title="Paganism">Pagan</a> origin, coming from the <a href="/wiki/Roman_god" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman god">Roman gods</a> <a href="/wiki/Hera" title="Hera">Juno</a>/<a href="/wiki/Jupiter_(god)" class="mw-redirect" title="Jupiter (god)">Jupiter</a>, he was dedicated to the service of God from his earliest childhood and was instructed in all the <a href="/wiki/Sacred" class="mw-redirect" title="Sacred">sacred</a> and <a href="/wiki/Human_sciences" class="mw-redirect" title="Human sciences">human sciences</a> which were taught at that time.
</p><p>Nothing else is known about his early life but we re-encounter Junien as a young man living a life of austerity as a hermit in a place called Bois Trappeau in the commune of <a href="/wiki/Ardilleux" title="Ardilleux">Ardilleux</a> in Deux-Sèvres.
</p><p>Junian acquired disciples and in 559, King <a href="/wiki/Clotaire_I" class="mw-redirect" title="Clotaire I">Clotaire I</a> made him a gift of land on which to found a <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> dedicated to the rule of <a href="/wiki/Benedict_of_Nursia" title="Benedict of Nursia">Saint Benoît</a> - the religious community at <a href="/wiki/Mair%C3%A9-l%27Evescault" class="mw-redirect" title="Mairé-l&#39;Evescault">Mairé-l'Evescault</a>, south of <a href="/wiki/Deux-S%C3%A8vres" title="Deux-Sèvres">Deux-Sèvres</a>. This isolated place evoked memories of his childhood home and Junien named it "Mariacus" - subsequently "Mairé". The "L'Evescault" was added after a great religious festival in <a href="/wiki/Poitiers" title="Poitiers">Poitiers</a> to which Junien was invited by Queen <a href="/wiki/Radagonde" class="mw-redirect" title="Radagonde">Radagonde</a> who raised him to the same rank as the other <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishops</a> or "Les Evêques" who were present. Hence the name Mairé-L'Evescault.
</p><p>He remained a friend of Queen Radegund who herself founded the abbey of <a href="/wiki/Holy_Cross_Abbey_(Poitiers)" title="Holy Cross Abbey (Poitiers)">Sainte-Croix de Poitiers</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-junien_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-junien-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Junian and Radegonde died on the same day, 13 August 587.<sup id="cite_ref-junien_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-junien-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Junian's <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> at Mairé was partially destroyed during the wars of <a href="/wiki/Pippin_III" class="mw-redirect" title="Pippin III">Pepin the Short</a> and <a href="/wiki/Charlemagne" title="Charlemagne">Charlemagne</a> but survived for another thousand years. It was finally demolished during the <a href="/wiki/French_Revolution" title="French Revolution">French Revolution</a>, the stone being re-used for local construction and the lands reverting to their farming origins.
</p>
