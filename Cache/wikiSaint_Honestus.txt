<p><b>Saint Honestus</b> (<a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>: <i lang="es">San Honesto</i>, <a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Saint Honest</i>) was, according to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> tradition, a disciple of <a href="/wiki/Saturninus_of_Toulouse" class="mw-redirect" title="Saturninus of Toulouse">Saturninus of Toulouse</a> and a native of <a href="/wiki/N%C3%AEmes" title="Nîmes">Nîmes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Saturninus and Honestus evangelized in <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a>, and Honestus was martyred at <a href="/wiki/Pampeluna" class="mw-redirect" title="Pampeluna">Pampeluna</a> during the persecutions of <a href="/wiki/Aurelian" title="Aurelian">Aurelian</a>. Elaboration of this legend states that Honestus was a nobleman of Nîmes who was appointed "apostle to <a href="/wiki/Navarre" title="Navarre">Navarre</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Basque_Country_(historical_territory)" class="mw-redirect" title="Basque Country (historical territory)">Basque Country</a>."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Further elaboration of his legend states that at Pampeluna, he converted the senator Firmus and his family to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a>, while Firmus's son, <a href="/wiki/Fermin" title="Fermin">Saint Firminus</a>, was christened by Saint Saturninus.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  Variants of this legend state that Honestus baptized Firminus himself.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
