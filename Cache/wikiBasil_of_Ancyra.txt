<p><b>Basil of Ancyra</b> (Βασίλειος), was a Christian priest in <a href="/wiki/Ancyra" class="mw-redirect" title="Ancyra">Ancyra</a>, <a href="/wiki/Galatia" title="Galatia">Galatia</a> during the 4th century.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Very meager information about his life is preserved in a metaphrastic work: “Life and Deeds of the Martyred Priest Basil.” He fought against the <a href="/wiki/Paganism" title="Paganism">pagans</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Arianism" title="Arianism">Arians</a>. Basil defended <a href="/wiki/Marcellus_of_Ancyra" title="Marcellus of Ancyra">Bishop Marcellus</a> against the prelate being deposed by the Arians.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Suda" title="Suda">Suda</a> write that he was the bishop of Ancyra and physician by trade.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Basil was caught up in the persecution of <a href="/wiki/Julian_the_Apostate" class="mw-redirect" title="Julian the Apostate">Julian the Apostate</a>. He was arrested, tortured, and executed on June 28/29, 362.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is commemorated as a martyr on March 22 in the West<sup id="cite_ref-CO_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and East.<sup id="cite_ref-OE_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OE-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He is sometimes confused with the other Basil of Ancyra who was not a priest and who is commemorated on January 1.<sup id="cite_ref-OE_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OE-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
