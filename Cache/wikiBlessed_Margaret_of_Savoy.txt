<p><b>Margaret of Savoy</b> (June 21, 1382<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> or 1390<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> –  November 23, 1464) was Marchioness of <a href="/wiki/Montferrat" title="Montferrat">Montferrat</a>, and a <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Order" title="Dominican Order">Dominican</a> <a href="/wiki/Religious_Sister" class="mw-redirect" title="Religious Sister">Sister</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Margaret_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Margaret was the eldest of the four children—all daughters—born to <a href="/wiki/Amadeo,_Prince_of_Achaea" class="mw-redirect" title="Amadeo, Prince of Achaea">Amadeo of Savoy</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lord_of_Piedmont" class="mw-redirect" title="Lord of Piedmont">Lord of Piedmont</a><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> (and titular <a href="/wiki/Principality_of_Achaea" title="Principality of Achaea">Prince of Achaea</a>), and his wife Catherine of Geneva.
</p><p>Her paternal grandparents were <a href="/wiki/James_of_Piedmont" title="James of Piedmont">Giacomo of Savoy</a>, titular Prince of Achaea, and his second wife Sibylle of Baux. Her maternal grandparents were <a href="/wiki/Amadeus_III,_Count_of_Geneva" class="mw-redirect" title="Amadeus III, Count of Geneva">Amadeus III, Count of Geneva</a>, and Mahaut d'Auvergne. Their claim to the throne of Achaea came from her great-grandfather <a href="/wiki/Philip_I_of_Piedmont" title="Philip I of Piedmont">Philip of Savoy</a>, eldest son of <a href="/wiki/Thomas_III_of_Piedmont" title="Thomas III of Piedmont">Thomas III of Piedmont</a> and Guia of Burgundy. Philip married <a href="/wiki/Isabella_of_Villehardouin" title="Isabella of Villehardouin">Isabella of Villehardouin</a>, Princess of Achaea, and co-reigned with her from 1301 to 1307. They were both deposed by <a href="/wiki/Philip_I_of_Taranto" class="mw-redirect" title="Philip I of Taranto">Philip I of Taranto</a> but continued to claim the title. However Giacomo was a son of Philip by his second wife Catherine de la Tour du Pin and his claim to be the heir of Isabella of Villehardouin was disputed.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Marriage">Marriage</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Margaret_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Marriage">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On January 17, 1403, Margaret married <a href="/wiki/Theodore_II,_Marquess_of_Montferrat" class="mw-redirect" title="Theodore II, Marquess of Montferrat">Theodore II, Marquess of Montferrat</a>, a member of the <a href="/wiki/Palaiologos" title="Palaiologos">Palaiologos</a> dynasty and male-line descendant of <a href="/wiki/Andronikos_II_Palaiologos" title="Andronikos II Palaiologos">Andronikos II Palaiologos</a>. Theodore was the widower of Jeanne of Bar,<sup id="cite_ref-clugnet_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-clugnet-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> daughter of <a href="/wiki/Robert_I,_Duke_of_Bar" class="mw-redirect" title="Robert I, Duke of Bar">Robert I, Duke of Bar</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Marie_of_Valois_(1344-1404)" class="mw-redirect" title="Marie of Valois (1344-1404)">Marie Valois</a>. Her piety increased after she heard the preaching of <a href="/wiki/Vincent_Ferrer" title="Vincent Ferrer">Vincent Ferrer</a>, who spent several months in <a href="/wiki/Montferrat" title="Montferrat">Montferrat</a>. This marriage lasted for fifteen years but was childless. Theodore died in 1418.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Monastic_life">Monastic life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Margaret_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Monastic life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When she was left a widow, she decided to abandon the world. Leaving the direction of the affairs of the marquisate to <a href="/wiki/John_Jacob,_Marquess_of_Montferrat" class="mw-redirect" title="John Jacob, Marquess of Montferrat">Jean-Jacques</a>, the son of her husband by his first marriage, she retired to Alba where she joined the <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_St._Dominic" class="mw-redirect" title="Third Order of St. Dominic">Third Order of St. Dominic</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-clugnet_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-clugnet-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>A little later, <a href="/wiki/Filippo_Maria_Visconti" title="Filippo Maria Visconti">Filippo Maria Visconti</a>, <a href="/wiki/List_of_rulers_of_Milan" title="List of rulers of Milan">Duke of Milan</a>, asked her hand in marriage and begged <a href="/wiki/Pope_Martin_V" title="Pope Martin V">Pope Martin V</a> to relieve her of her vow. But Margaret opposed a formal refusal to this request and with several young women of rank, she founded a monastery and placed it under the rule of the order of St. Dominic. Redoubling her mortifications she remained a model of piety and died at <a href="/wiki/Alba,_Italy" class="mw-redirect" title="Alba, Italy">Alba</a>, 23 November 1464.<sup id="cite_ref-clugnet_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-clugnet-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Margaret_of_Savoy&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On 13 December 1464, her remains were placed in a simple tomb; in 1481 they were transferred to a different and much more beautiful sepulchre built in her monastery at the expense of <a href="/wiki/William_VIII_of_Montferrat" class="mw-redirect" title="William VIII of Montferrat">William VIII of Montferrat</a>. They are presently held in the church of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Maddalena,_Alba" title="Santa Maria Maddalena, Alba">Santa Maria Maddalena, Alba</a>.
</p><p>Four noble <i>beati</i> of the Savoy family are depicted on the vault of a bay in the south aisle of the church of <a href="/wiki/San_Michele_Maggiore" class="mw-redirect" title="San Michele Maggiore">San Michele</a> in <a href="/wiki/Pavia" title="Pavia">Pavia</a>. Margaret is shown dressed as a nun holding three arrows.
</p>
