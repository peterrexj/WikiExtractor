					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">This article is about the Christian martyr. For other uses, see <a href="/wiki/Epicharis_(disambiguation)" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Epicharis (disambiguation)">Epicharis (disambiguation)</a>.</div>
<p><b>Epicharis</b> is the name of two <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> martyrs.  
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="250">250</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Epicharis_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: 250">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her feast day is 9 January (the day of her martyrdom) in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Born in Africa, she was martyred in 250 with Felix, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Jucundus_(died_250)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Jucundus (died 250) (page does not exist)">Jucundus</a>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Secundus_(died_250)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Secundus (died 250) (page does not exist)">Secundus</a>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Vitalis_(died_250)&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Vitalis (died 250) (page does not exist)">Vitalis</a>, and seven other companions. An <a href="/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Zaragoza" class="mw-redirect" title="Archdiocese of Zaragoza">Epictetus</a>, a bishop, was recorded by St. <a href="/wiki/Cyprian" title="Cyprian">Cyprian</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="300">300</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Epicharis_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: 300">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Said to be the wife of a Roman senator, she was martyred in Byzantium or Asia Minor in 300.  Her feast day is <a href="/wiki/September_27_(Eastern_Orthodox_liturgics)" title="September 27 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)">September 27</a> in the Eastern Orthodox and Roman Catholic churches.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Some sources give her as a lady of a senatorial family, who was scourged and then smitten with the sword in Rome in the persecution of Diocletian.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
