					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Angelelmus</b> (Angelelme, Angelaume) was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Auxerre" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Auxerre">bishop of Auxerre</a> from 813 to 828.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was <a href="/wiki/Bavaria" title="Bavaria">Bavarian</a>, and became abbot of the monastery of <a href="/wiki/Gervasius_and_Protasius" title="Gervasius and Protasius">SS Gervais and Protase</a>, Auxerre.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is a Catholic and Orthodox<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> saint, with feast day on July 7.
</p>
