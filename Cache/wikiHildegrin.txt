					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Hildegrin&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="Hildegrin">Hildegrin</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Hildegrim</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> (<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;750</span> – 19 June 827) was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Ch%C3%A2lons" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Châlons">Bishop of Châlons</a> from 804 to 810 and the second abbot of <a href="/wiki/Werden_Abbey" title="Werden Abbey">Werden Abbey</a>, after his elder brother <a href="/wiki/Ludger" title="Ludger">Ludger</a>, from 809 until his death.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Hildegrim_of_Ch%C3%A2lons&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Like his brother Ludger, Hildegrim was of Christian <a href="/wiki/Frisia" title="Frisia">Frisian</a> noble descent. He presumably stayed at the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Benedict" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Saint Benedict">Benedictine</a> abbey of <a href="/wiki/Monte_Cassino" title="Monte Cassino">Monte Cassino</a> and received a thorough <a href="/wiki/Liberal_arts_education" title="Liberal arts education">liberal arts education</a> at the <a href="/wiki/St._Martin%27s_Cathedral,_Utrecht" title="St. Martin&#39;s Cathedral, Utrecht">Utrecht Cathedral</a> School, founded by Bishop <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Utrecht" title="Gregory of Utrecht">Gregory</a>, and in <a href="/wiki/York" title="York">York</a> under <a href="/wiki/Alcuin" title="Alcuin">Alcuin</a>. Mentioned as <a href="/wiki/Deacon" title="Deacon">deacon</a> in 793, he was ordained a priest three years later.
</p><p>About 804 he became Bishop of Châlons. Upon Ludger's death in 809, he succeeded him as Abbot of Werden and <a href="/wiki/St_Ludger%27s_Abbey" class="mw-redirect" title="St Ludger&#39;s Abbey">Helmstedt</a> in 809.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is also traditionally named as the first <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Halberstadt" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Halberstadt">Bishop of Halberstadt</a>, a position now discounted by scholars;<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> nevertheless, Hildegrim is known to have been active in spreading Christianity as a missionary into the <a href="/wiki/Osterwieck" title="Osterwieck">Osterwieck</a> and <a href="/wiki/Halberstadt" title="Halberstadt">Halberstadt</a> region after the <a href="/wiki/Saxon_Wars" title="Saxon Wars">Saxon Wars</a> of <a href="/wiki/Charlemagne" title="Charlemagne">Charlemagne</a>.
</p><p>Hildegrim is buried in the crypt of Werden Abbey church. He is a Catholic and Orthodox<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> saint, remembered on June 19.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
