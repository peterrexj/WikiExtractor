					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:172px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Canon_van_der_Paele_(_Donatian)_Van_Eyck.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0a/Canon_van_der_Paele_%28_Donatian%29_Van_Eyck.jpg/170px-Canon_van_der_Paele_%28_Donatian%29_Van_Eyck.jpg" decoding="async" width="170" height="409" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0a/Canon_van_der_Paele_%28_Donatian%29_Van_Eyck.jpg/255px-Canon_van_der_Paele_%28_Donatian%29_Van_Eyck.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0a/Canon_van_der_Paele_%28_Donatian%29_Van_Eyck.jpg 2x" data-file-width="319" data-file-height="768" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Canon_van_der_Paele_(_Donatian)_Van_Eyck.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Detail of St. Donatian, from <a href="/wiki/Jan_van_Eyck" title="Jan van Eyck">Jan van Eyck</a>'s <i><a href="/wiki/Virgin_and_Child_with_Canon_van_der_Paele" title="Virgin and Child with Canon van der Paele">Virgin and Child with Canon van der Paele</a></i> (1436)</div></div></div>
<p><b>Donatien of Reims</b> (also known as Donatien or Donat) was a 4th-century <a href="/wiki/French_people" title="French people">French</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a><sup id="cite_ref-nominis.cef.fr_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis.cef.fr-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and the 8th <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Reims" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Reims">Bishop of Reims</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died in AD 389, and in AD 863 the <a href="/wiki/Baldwin_I,_Count_of_Flanders" class="mw-redirect" title="Baldwin I, Count of Flanders">count of Flanders Baldwin I</a> transferred his relics to the Church <a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint-Agricol_de_Reims&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Saint-Agricol de Reims (page does not exist)">Saint-Agricol de Reims</a> at <a href="/wiki/Bruges" title="Bruges">Bruges</a>, where his cult is still active.
He is revered as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> and his <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is locally celebrated on 14 October.<sup id="cite_ref-nominis.cef.fr_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nominis.cef.fr-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Donatian_of_Reims&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A legend has it that he was thrown as a child into a river, where a holy man took five candles and placed them on a <a href="/wiki/Water_wheel" title="Water wheel">water wheel</a> which showed where the child had gone, and he was able to recover the child.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
Saint Donatien is represented holding a wheel bearing candles.
</p>
