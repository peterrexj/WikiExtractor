<p><b>John Roche</b> (also known as John Neele or Neale) was a <a href="/wiki/Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>, born in <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a>, who died in <a href="/wiki/London,_England" class="mw-redirect" title="London, England">London, England</a> on 30 August 1588.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Roche_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He helped <a href="/wiki/Margaret_Ward" title="Margaret Ward">Margaret Ward</a> arrange the escape of Richard Watson from <a href="/wiki/Bridewell_Palace" title="Bridewell Palace">Bridewell Prison</a> when the boatman she had originally asked to help her refused to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Roche exchanged clothes with the prisoner and was arrested in his place.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Offered his freedom if he would ask <a href="/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_I" class="mw-redirect" title="Queen Elizabeth I">Queen Elizabeth I</a>'s pardon and promise to attend a <a href="/wiki/Protestant" class="mw-redirect" title="Protestant">Protestant</a> church, he refused, and was hanged at <a href="/wiki/Tyburn,_London" class="mw-redirect" title="Tyburn, London">Tyburn, London</a> on 30 August 1588, along with <a href="/wiki/Margaret_Ward" title="Margaret Ward">Ward</a>, <a href="/wiki/Edward_Shelley" class="mw-redirect" title="Edward Shelley">Edward Shelley</a>, <a href="/wiki/Richard_Martin_(martyr)" title="Richard Martin (martyr)">Richard Martin</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Richard_Leigh_(martyr)" title="Richard Leigh (martyr)">Richard Leigh</a> and Richard Lloyd (alias Flower).<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Roche_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> Roche in 1929. A school in the <a href="/wiki/London_Borough_of_Tower_Hamlets" title="London Borough of Tower Hamlets">London Borough of Tower Hamlets</a> was named after him.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Iconography">Iconography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Roche_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Iconography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Usually shown in <a href="/wiki/Working-class" class="mw-redirect" title="Working-class">working-class</a> <a href="/wiki/Elizabethan" class="mw-redirect" title="Elizabethan">Elizabethan</a> dress and holding an oar or a miniature boat, he is the patron of sailors, mariners and boatmen.
</p>
