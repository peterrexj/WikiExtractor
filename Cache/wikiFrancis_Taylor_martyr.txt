<p><b>Francis Taylor</b> (<a href="/wiki/Irish_language" title="Irish language">Irish</a>: Proinnsias Táiliúr; Beannaithe, <a href="/wiki/Swords,_Dublin" title="Swords, Dublin">Swords</a>, c. 1550 &#8211; <a href="/wiki/Dublin" title="Dublin">Dublin</a>, 29 January 1621) was a <a href="/wiki/Lord_Mayor_of_Dublin" title="Lord Mayor of Dublin">Mayor of Dublin</a>, Ireland, who was incarcerated because of his <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Catholicism</a>. He has been declared a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> for his faith and <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by the Catholic Church.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francis_Taylor_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born in <a href="/wiki/Swords,_Dublin" title="Swords, Dublin">Swords</a>, <a href="/wiki/County_Dublin" title="County Dublin">County Dublin</a>, Taylor moved to the <a href="/wiki/City_of_Dublin" class="mw-redirect" title="City of Dublin">City of Dublin</a> and married the daughter of a prominent family, being the granddaughter of a Mayor of Dublin. He himself was elected Dublin's mayor in 1595. Taylor was imprisoned for his Catholic faith in 1613, and died there on 29 January 1621, after seven years of refusing to accept his freedom by giving up his religion.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francis_Taylor_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> beatified Taylor on 27 September 1992, as part of a group of 17 <a href="/wiki/Irish_Catholic_Martyrs" title="Irish Catholic Martyrs">Irish Catholic Martyrs</a> who were victims of religious persecution due to their Catholic faith during that era.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>A statue of Taylor and of his wife's grandmother, the Blessed <a href="/wiki/Margaret_Ball" title="Margaret Ball">Margaret Ball</a>, who had died in that same prison for her faith in 1584, stands outside <a href="/wiki/St._Mary%27s_Pro-Cathedral" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Mary&#39;s Pro-Cathedral">St. Mary's Pro-Cathedral</a> in Dublin.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (June 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
