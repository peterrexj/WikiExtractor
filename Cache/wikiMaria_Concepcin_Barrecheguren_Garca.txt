<p><b>Maria Concepción Barrecheguren García</b> (27 November 1905 – 13 May 1927), also known as <b>Conchita Barrecheguren</b>, was a young <a href="/wiki/Spaniards" title="Spaniards">Spanish</a> woman. She was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on May 6, 2023, by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Maria_Concepci%C3%B3n_Barrecheguren_Garc%C3%ADa&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Sierva_de_Dios_Conchita_Barrecheguren.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0d/Sierva_de_Dios_Conchita_Barrecheguren.jpg/220px-Sierva_de_Dios_Conchita_Barrecheguren.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="306" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0d/Sierva_de_Dios_Conchita_Barrecheguren.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="288" data-file-height="400" /></a><figcaption>Conchita Barrecheguren in her bed, with a statue of the <a href="/wiki/Christ_Child" title="Christ Child">Christ Child</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>Maria Concepción Barrecheguren García was born on November 27, 1905, in Granada. She was the only child of the couple Francisco Barrecheguren and Concha García, both from wealthy and religious families. She was baptized on December 8, 1905, on the Solemnity of the Immaculate Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary. When she was just over a year and a half old, she was diagnosed with <a href="/wiki/Enterocolitis" title="Enterocolitis">acute enterocolitis</a>, so much so that her life was feared. The girl soon recovered, being attributed to <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Lourdes" title="Our Lady of Lourdes">Our Lady of Lourdes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> From an early age, Conchita had health problems, as a result of which she did not attend school. His father was responsible for educating him in social and catechetical principles. The quality of the teachings transmitted by her father allowed her to receive the <a href="/wiki/Confirmation" title="Confirmation">Sacrament of Confirmation</a> and the <a href="/wiki/First_Communion" title="First Communion">First Communion</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Since childhood, Conchita wanted to lead a consecrated religious life. However, later illnesses formed an obstacle to her joining the monastic life.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In 1917, she was diagnosed with an intestinal infection, which caused her a lot of pain and required a special diet. Conchita patiently accepted her condition, seeing in it an opportunity to better surrender herself to God.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1924, she was stricken with a mental illness inherited from her mother. With fragile health, she was admitted to the Sanatorium of La Purissima, in Granada. Isolated from society, she devoted herself to <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9r%C3%A8se_of_Lisieux" title="Thérèse of Lisieux">Saint Thérèse of Lisieux</a>. While she was hospitalized, she testified to her faith by praying and showing trust in God. She also used to chronicle reporting and writing personal notes. Despite poor health, in August 1926 she made a pilgrimage to <a href="/wiki/Lisieux" title="Lisieux">Lisieux</a>, where she contracted <a href="/wiki/Tuberculosis" title="Tuberculosis">tuberculosis</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Seeking to improve from the new illness she contracted, she moved with her parents to the Carmine di San Valentino, in <a href="/wiki/Alhambra" title="Alhambra">Alhambra</a>, but the illness got worse.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The last months of Conchita's life were marked by total dependence on caregivers. All were unanimous in reporting their joy and patience when sick. The rest of her life was also marked by the many times she received the Eucharist.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Conchita died on May 13, 1927, aged 22, due to tuberculosis.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> She was initially buried in her family's tomb in Grenada. On May 13, 1978, the remains were transferred to the Carmelo di Conchita, in Alhambra and, since November 29, 2007, she haas been buried in the Redemptorist Sanctuary of Our Lady of Perpetual Help.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Maria_Concepci%C3%B3n_Barrecheguren_Garc%C3%ADa&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The beatification cause for Conchita Barrecheguren began in 1938. A <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Granada" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Granada">archdiocesan</a> inquiry into her writings and testimonies about her life was completed in 1956 and sent to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> for examination by the <a href="/wiki/Dicastery_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" title="Dicastery for the Causes of Saints">Dicastery for the Causes of Saints</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-auto_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-auto-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On May 5, 2020, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> recognized Conchita heroic virtues, giving her the title of Venerable. On the same day, her father Francisco was also declared venerable. On May 21, 2022, a decree was signed recognizing the miracle obtained through her intercession.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On May 6, 2023, she was beatified in the <a href="/wiki/Granada_Cathedral" title="Granada Cathedral">Granada Cathedral</a>. The celebrant chosen to represent Pope Francis was Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Marcello_Semeraro" title="Marcello Semeraro">Marcello Semeraro</a>, who inscribed her on the list of Blesseds of the Catholic Church.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His liturgical memory is celebrated on May 13.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
