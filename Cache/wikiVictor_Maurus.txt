<p><b>Victor the Moor</b> (in <a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <b>Victor Maurus</b>) (born 3rd century in <a href="/wiki/Mauretania" title="Mauretania">Mauretania</a>; died ca. 303 in <a href="/wiki/Milan" title="Milan">Milan</a>) was a native of <a href="/wiki/Mauretania" title="Mauretania">Mauretania</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>, according to tradition, and is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Victor_Maurus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Victor, born into a Christian family, was a soldier in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Empire" title="Roman Empire">Roman</a> <a href="/wiki/Praetorian_Guard" title="Praetorian Guard">Praetorian Guard</a> under <a href="/wiki/Maximian" title="Maximian">Maximian</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Holweck_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Holweck-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> In the "Acts", which date back to the 8th century, it is said that Victor refused to continue his military service. Dragged to the Hippodrome of the Circus in the presence of Maximian Herculean and his adviser Anulinus, he refused to betray his beliefs despite the torments to which he was subjected. Whipped and imprisoned, after an almost miraculous escape, he was again captured. He was dragged into a nearby elm wood and decapitated around the year 303.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Victor_Maurus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_(last_decade_of_15th_century).jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5e/Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_%28last_decade_of_15th_century%29.jpg/220px-Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_%28last_decade_of_15th_century%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="293" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5e/Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_%28last_decade_of_15th_century%29.jpg/330px-Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_%28last_decade_of_15th_century%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5e/Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_%28last_decade_of_15th_century%29.jpg/440px-Museo_del_Duomo_-_Milan_-_St_Victor_-_Milanese_sculptor_%28last_decade_of_15th_century%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2448" data-file-height="3264" /></a><figcaption>Statue of St Victor in Museo del Duomo, Milan. Unknown Milanese sculptor, last decade of 15th century</figcaption></figure>
<p>His bones were later buried at an ancient basilica on the site of a former Roman mausoleum. They were later moved to the oratory of <i>San Vittore in Ciel d'Oro</i>, originally a free-standing chapel, commissioned by bishop <a href="/wiki/Maternus_(bishop_of_Milan)" title="Maternus (bishop of Milan)">Maternus</a> to hold the relics of Saint Victor. It is now part of the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Sant%27Ambrogio" title="Basilica of Sant&#39;Ambrogio">Basilica of Sant'Ambrogio</a>, built by <a href="/wiki/Ambrose" title="Ambrose">Ambrose</a>, fourth-century <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of <a href="/wiki/Milan" title="Milan">Milan</a>, and initially called the "Basilica Martyrum".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Victor's cause was promoted by Ambrose. <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a> claimed <a href="/wiki/Miracle" title="Miracle">miracles</a> occurred at Victor's grave.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (July 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> In 1576, Bishop <a href="/wiki/Charles_Borromeo" title="Charles Borromeo">Charles Borromeo</a> had the relics returned to the rebuilt <a href="/wiki/San_Vittore_al_Corpo,_Milan" title="San Vittore al Corpo, Milan">San Vittore al Corpo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Holweck_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Holweck-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Forensic examinations conducted in 2018 indicated a male in his mid-twenties, with clear signs of decaptitation.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Numerous churches have been dedicated to him in the city itself and throughout the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Milan" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Milan">Diocese of Milan</a> and its neighbours.
</p><p>His memorial day is May 8 in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Evangelical_Lutheran_Church_in_America" title="Evangelical Lutheran Church in America">Evangelical Lutheran Church in America</a>. Victor is the patron saint of prisoners and exiles.
</p>
