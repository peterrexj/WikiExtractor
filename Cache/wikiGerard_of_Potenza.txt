<p>Saint <b>Gerard of Potenza</b>, also <b>Gerard La Porta</b> (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">Gerardo di Potenza</i>, <i>Gerardo La Porta</i>) (d. 30 October 1119) was a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> saint and a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Potenza" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Potenza">bishop of Potenza</a> in <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gerard_of_Potenza&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Gerard was born in <a href="/wiki/Piacenza" title="Piacenza">Piacenza</a> into a noble family. He travelled into southern Italy in search of holy sites, but when he reached <a href="/wiki/Potenza" title="Potenza">Potenza</a> he decided to dedicate himself to the apostolic life. Such was his drive that when the bishop died, the people and clergy chose Gerard as his successor.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was proclaimed bishop at <a href="/wiki/Acerenza" title="Acerenza">Acerenza</a> and was in post for eight years.
</p><p>After Gerard's death <a href="/wiki/Pope_Callixtus_II" title="Pope Callixtus II">Pope Callixtus II</a> declared him a saint <i>viva voce</i> in 1120. His <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> are kept in a <a href="/wiki/Sarcophagus" title="Sarcophagus">sarcophagus</a> in <a href="/wiki/Potenza_Cathedral" title="Potenza Cathedral">Potenza Cathedral</a>, which is dedicated to him.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gerard_of_Potenza&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Saint Gerard's principal feast day is 30 May, the day of the <a href="/wiki/Translation_(relics)" class="mw-redirect" title="Translation (relics)">translation</a> of his relics; 30 October, the day of his death, is also kept.
</p>
