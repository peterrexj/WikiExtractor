<p><b>Alfonso Rodríguez Olmedo</b> (10 March 1598 – 15 November 1628) was a <a href="/wiki/Spanish_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Spanish people">Spanish</a> <a href="/wiki/Society_of_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Society of Jesus">Jesuit</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_priest" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic priest">priest</a> who was sent as a missionary among the <a href="/wiki/Guarani_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Guarani people">Guarani people</a> in Paraguay. He is honored as a martyr and saint by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-va_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-va-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alfonso_Rodr%C3%ADguez_Olmedo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rodríguez was born in the city of <a href="/wiki/Zamora,_Spain" title="Zamora, Spain">Zamora</a>, <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a> on March 10, 1598. He did his novitiate in <a href="/wiki/Villagarc%C3%ADa_de_Campos" title="Villagarcía de Campos">Villagarcía de Campos</a>, <a href="/wiki/Valladolid" title="Valladolid">Valladolid</a> and was later sent to <a href="/wiki/Paraguay" title="Paraguay">Paraguay</a>. He left <a href="/wiki/Lisbon" title="Lisbon">Lisbon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a> on an expedition led by Father Juan de Viana.<sup id="cite_ref-terr_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-terr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1626, he was sent to the <a href="/wiki/Guaycuru_peoples" title="Guaycuru peoples">Guaycuru</a> missions, which were in front of Asunción, on the other side of the <a href="/wiki/Paraguay_River" title="Paraguay River">Paraguay River</a>. He was the first missionary to learn the local language,<sup id="cite_ref-terr_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-terr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> and his mission was to preach and to facilitate the spread of the faith and ensure decent living conditions for indigenous people.<sup id="cite_ref-va_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-va-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1628, he went to the <a href="/wiki/Guaran%C3%AD_people" title="Guaraní people">Guaraní</a> missions of <a href="/wiki/Paran%C3%A1_River" title="Paraná River">Paraná</a> and then to Itaipú. He was appointed to accompany <a href="/wiki/Roque_Gonz%C3%A1lez_y_de_Santa_Cruz" title="Roque González y de Santa Cruz">Roque González y de Santa Cruz</a> in the founding of the town of Todos los Santos de Caaró, on the eastern bank of the Uruguay River. But Ñezú, a sorcerer and chieftain of Igní, opposed the project and gave the order to kill the missionaries.<sup id="cite_ref-terr_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-terr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> On November 15, 1628, while they were gathered in the plaza to witness the installation of a bell, the indigenous people appeared suddenly and in the midst of the confusion, they hit González on the head. Upon hearing the noise, Rodríguez left the church and was also beaten to death by a stone mallet called an <i><a href="/w/index.php?title=Itaiz%C3%A1&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Itaizá (page does not exist)">itaizá</a></i>. Their bodies were dragged to the church and burned with paintings and images.<sup id="cite_ref-terr_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-terr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Juan_de_Castillo_(Jesuit)" title="Juan de Castillo (Jesuit)">Juan de Castillo</a> suffered the same fate a few days later, being assassinated on November 17, 1628.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Juan_Eusebio_Nieremberg" title="Juan Eusebio Nieremberg">Juan Eusebio Nieremberg</a>, who was a fellow classmate of the three martyrs, wrote their biographies in 1644.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alfonso_Rodr%C3%ADguez_Olmedo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rodríguez was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> on 28 January 1934. He and his companions, Juan de Castillo and Roque González y de Santa Cruz, were later <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> in Asunción, Paraguay.
</p>
