<p><b>Maura of Troyes</b> was a French <a href="/wiki/Virginity" title="Virginity">virgin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. Born into <a href="/wiki/Nobility" title="Nobility">nobility</a> in <a href="/wiki/Troyes" title="Troyes">Troyes</a>, she devoted herself to prayer, remaining <a href="/wiki/Chastity" title="Chastity">chaste</a>, and is credited with at least two <a href="/wiki/Miracle" title="Miracle">miracles</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Maura_of_Troyes&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Maura was born to a noble family in <a href="/wiki/Troyes" title="Troyes">Troyes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Francia" title="Francia">Francia</a>, in 827, and from a young age devoted herself to prayer.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> As a young girl, she converted her father Mauranus who had previously lived a worldly life, to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> After his death, Maura continued to live at home, praying, serving the poor, and caring for her mother, who was named either Seluca or Sedulia.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Her example was also credited with her brother Eutropius' strong faith, and he later went on to become bishop of Troyes.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When not praying or serving the poor, Maura greatly enjoyed meeting the material needs of local priests and the <a href="/wiki/Troyes_Cathedral" title="Troyes Cathedral">Troyes Cathedral</a>: she would make sacred vestments, trim the candles, fill oil lamps and prepare wax and other things for the <a href="/wiki/Altar" title="Altar">altar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> In fact, Bishop <a href="/wiki/Prudentius_of_Troyes" title="Prudentius of Troyes">Prudentius of Troyes</a>, a personal friend, wore an <a href="/wiki/Alb" title="Alb">alb</a> spun and woven by her.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Maura spent every morning in church, praying.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> She fasted Wednesday and Friday, sustaining herself only on small amounts of bread and water.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Sometimes, she would walk several miles to visit her <a href="/wiki/Spiritual_direction" title="Spiritual direction">spiritual director</a> at his <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> She was known to produce copious amounts of tears while praying, considered a gift from God.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> She was also said to have performed miracles, but asked the people she helped not to make her gifts known.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>She died on 21 September 850, at the age of 23.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> She was buried in the cemetery of Château-Nore-de-Troyes.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Miracles_and_sainthood">Miracles and sainthood</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Maura_of_Troyes&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Miracles and sainthood">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At least three posthumous miracles are attributed to Maura of Troyes. After her death, her body was washed with water, but the water was claimed to have changed into milk.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> A young man was believed to have been cured of "a burning fever" after drinking the milk.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> A young woman, whose husband disliked a large <a href="/wiki/Birthmark" title="Birthmark">birthmark</a> on her cheek, also drank the milk and the birthmark disappeared.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> She was declared a saint by the community, as was the normal process before the establishment of the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>St. Maura of Troyes is honored in the <a href="/wiki/Anglican_Communion" title="Anglican Communion">Anglican Communion</a>, <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_2-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:5_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> Her feast day is celebrated on 21 September.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:5_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:5-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
