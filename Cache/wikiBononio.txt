					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Italian Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Bononio</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">August 30, 1026</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">Canonized</a></th><td class="infobox-data">1026 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_XIX" title="Pope John XIX">Pope John XIX</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">August 30</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Saint_symbolism" title="Saint symbolism">Attributes</a></th><td class="infobox-data">depicted as an abbot</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Bononio</b> (or <b>Bononius</b>) (died  August 30, 1026) was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.
</p><p>Bononio was born in Bologna sometime in the latter part of the tenth century. He became a monk at an early age, and while on a pilgrimage to the East settled in Egypt to live as a hermit during the reign of Fatamid Caliph <a href="/wiki/Al-Aziz_Billah" title="Al-Aziz Billah">Al-Aziz Billah</a>. Noted for both his asceticism and charitable works, Bononio acquired some influence at court, and was permitted to build a few churches.  When Peter, bishop of Vercelli, was captured by <a href="/wiki/Arabs" title="Arabs">Arab</a> forces after the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Stilo" title="Battle of Stilo">Battle of Stilo</a>, Bononio assisted in the bishop's release, and then retired to live as a hermit in the Sinai.<sup id="cite_ref-Pomi_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pomi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In gratitude for Bononio's assistance, when Peter returned to Italy, he named him abbot of the monastery of <a href="/wiki/Lucedio" class="mw-redirect" title="Lucedio">Lucedio</a>.  At Lucedio, Bononio restored discipline amongst the monks and provided for the surrounding population. He died in Lucedio on August 30, 1026. Bononio was canonized by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_XIX" title="Pope John XIX">Pope John XIX</a>. The saint's festival is celebrated on that day in the Piedmont liturgical calendar.<sup id="cite_ref-Pomi_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pomi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> The village of San Bononio is part of the municipality of <a href="/wiki/Curino" title="Curino">Curino</a>. It celebrates its patron's feast with a three-day festival of concerts, art exhibitions, and lanterns on the canal.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The story that Bononio was a disciple of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Romuald" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Romuald">Saint Romuald</a> is based on a much later spurious <i>vita</i> fabricated by a Camaldolese abbot.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
