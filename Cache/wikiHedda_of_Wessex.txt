<p><b>Hædde</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;a&#93;</a></sup> (died 705) was a medieval monk and <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Winchester" title="Bishop of Winchester">Bishop of Winchester</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=H%C3%A6dde&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hædde is believed to have been born in <a href="/wiki/Headingley" title="Headingley">Headingley</a>, <a href="/wiki/Leeds" title="Leeds">Leeds</a>, and became a monk of <a href="/wiki/Whitby_Abbey" title="Whitby Abbey">Whitby Abbey</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-benedictine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-benedictine-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-hunt362_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hunt362-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
He became bishop in 676 and died about 7 July 705,<sup id="cite_ref-Handbook223_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Handbook223-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> although the <i><a href="/wiki/Anglo-Saxon_Chronicle" title="Anglo-Saxon Chronicle">Anglo-Saxon Chronicle</a></i> states that he died in 703.<sup id="cite_ref-Kirby41_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirby41-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> In the law code of King <a href="/wiki/Ine_of_Wessex" title="Ine of Wessex">Ine of Wessex</a>, the bishop is mentioned as contributing to the laws.<sup id="cite_ref-Conversion235_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Conversion235-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> After his death, he was revered as a saint with a feast day of 7 July,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> and his large diocese was split in two,<sup id="cite_ref-Kirby107_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirby107-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> part of the area forming the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Sherborne" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Sherborne">Diocese of Sherborne</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
