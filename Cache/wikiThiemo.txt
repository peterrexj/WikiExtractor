<p><b>Thiemo</b> (<i>Thimo</i>, also called <i>Dietmar</i> or <i>Theodemarus</i>; <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;1040</span>&#160;&#8211;&#32;28 September 1101/02) was <a href="/wiki/Archbishopric_of_Salzburg" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishopric of Salzburg">Archbishop of Salzburg</a> from 1090 until his death. He is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Christian_martyrs" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian martyrs">Christian martyr</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thiemo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A scion of the <a href="/wiki/Duchy_of_Bavaria" title="Duchy of Bavaria">Bavarian</a> comital House of <a href="/wiki/Vornbach_Abbey" title="Vornbach Abbey">Vornbach</a> (Formbach), Thiemo reportedly was a talented painter and sculptor. He entered the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Benedict" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Saint Benedict">Benedictine</a> abbey of <a href="/wiki/Niederaltaich_Abbey" title="Niederaltaich Abbey">Niederaltaich</a> and in 1077 became abbot of the <a href="/wiki/St_Peter%27s_Abbey,_Salzburg" title="St Peter&#39;s Abbey, Salzburg">St. Peter's Monastery</a> in <a href="/wiki/Salzburg" title="Salzburg">Salzburg</a>. Under Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Gebhard_of_Salzburg" title="Gebhard of Salzburg">Gebhard</a>, he was caught up in the fierce <a href="/wiki/Investiture_Controversy" title="Investiture Controversy">Investiture Controversy</a> as a papal supporter in opposition to German king <a href="/wiki/Henry_IV,_Holy_Roman_Emperor" title="Henry IV, Holy Roman Emperor">Henry IV</a>. While Henry had the archbishop expelled, Thiemo likewise, in 1081, went into exile, at first to <a href="/wiki/M%C3%B6nchsdeggingen" title="Mönchsdeggingen">Mönchsdeggingen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hirsau_Abbey" title="Hirsau Abbey">Hirsau Abbey</a> in <a href="/wiki/Duchy_of_Swabia" title="Duchy of Swabia">Swabia</a>, later to <a href="/wiki/Admont_Abbey" title="Admont Abbey">Admont</a>, <a href="/wiki/March_of_Styria" title="March of Styria">Styria</a>. 
</p><p>In 1086 Thiemo was able to return to Salzburg, together with Gebhard, whom he succeeded after his death two years later. Elected archbishop on 25 March 1090, he received the <a href="/wiki/Holy_orders" title="Holy orders">holy orders</a> on April 7, confirmed by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Urban_II" title="Pope Urban II">Pope Urban II</a>.
</p><p>In 1095 Thiemo attended the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Piacenza" title="Council of Piacenza">Council of Piacenza</a>, while a domestic conflict with anti-bishop Count Berthold of <a href="/wiki/Moosburg" class="mw-redirect" title="Moosburg">Moosburg</a>, who had been appointed by Henry IV in 1085, continued. He was defeated by Berthold's troops in 1097 and escaped to <a href="/wiki/Duchy_of_Carinthia" title="Duchy of Carinthia">Carinthia</a>, where he was arrested at <a href="/wiki/Friesach" title="Friesach">Friesach</a> by the forces of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Gurk" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Gurk">Gurk bishop</a>. Freed by a loyal monk, Thiemo found a refuge in the diocese of befriended Bishop <a href="/wiki/Gebhard_(III)_of_Constance" class="mw-redirect" title="Gebhard (III) of Constance">Gebhard of Constance</a> at <a href="/wiki/Petershausen_Abbey" title="Petershausen Abbey">Petershausen Abbey</a>.
</p><p>In 1101 Thiemo decided to join Duke <a href="/wiki/William_IX,_Duke_of_Aquitaine" title="William IX, Duke of Aquitaine">William IX of Aquitaine</a> on his <a href="/wiki/Crusade_of_1101" title="Crusade of 1101">crusade</a> to <a href="/wiki/Palestine_(region)" title="Palestine (region)">Palestine</a> and did not return. Several traditions concerning his death exist. He may have been taken captive by the <a href="/wiki/Sultanate_of_R%C3%BBm" class="mw-redirect" title="Sultanate of Rûm">Seljuqs of Rûm</a> at <a href="/wiki/Ere%C4%9Fli,_Konya" title="Ereğli, Konya">Ereğli</a> (Heraclea) in <a href="/wiki/Anatolia" title="Anatolia">Anatolia</a> in September 1101 or was imprisoned by the <a href="/wiki/Fatimid_Caliphate" title="Fatimid Caliphate">Fatimid Caliphate</a> at <a href="/wiki/Ashkelon" title="Ashkelon">Ashkelon</a> in the following year. His martyrdom is described being tortured and killed by pulling the intestines out of his body with a spindle. 
</p><p>He was never formally <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> but is commemorated as a martyr by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Name_day" title="Name day">name day</a> is 28 September.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
