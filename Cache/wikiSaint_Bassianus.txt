<p><b>Bassianus of Lodi</b> (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">Bassiano di Lodi</i>, <a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Bassianus Laudensis</i>; c. 320 – 413) was an Italian saint, the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Lodi,_Lombardy" title="Lodi, Lombardy">Lodi</a> and <a href="/wiki/Pizzighettone" title="Pizzighettone">Pizzighettone</a> in Italy.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bassianus_of_Lodi&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born in <a href="/wiki/Syracuse,_Sicily" title="Syracuse, Sicily">Syracuse, Sicily</a>, to Sergius, <a href="/wiki/Prefect" title="Prefect">prefect</a> of the city, Bassianus was sent to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> in order to complete his studies.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> There, he was converted to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a> by a priest named Giordano much against his parents' will.<sup id="cite_ref-Akker_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akker-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Bassianus's father, who wanted his son to <a href="/wiki/Apostatize" class="mw-redirect" title="Apostatize">apostatize</a>, asked him to return to Syracuse. Bassianus refused and fled to a relative, Urso, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Ravenna" title="Bishop of Ravenna">Bishop of Ravenna</a>. There Bassianus took up the life of a hermit near <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Sant%27Apollinare_in_Classe" title="Basilica of Sant&#39;Apollinare in Classe">Sant'Apollinare in Classe</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Akker_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akker-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Lodi" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Lodi">bishop of Lodi</a> died around 373, Bassianus was asked to succeed him. He was consecrated bishop by <a href="/wiki/Ambrose" title="Ambrose">Ambrose of Milan</a> and Urso of Ravenna.<sup id="cite_ref-Akker_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Akker-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Bassianus built a church dedicated to the <a href="/wiki/Apostles_in_the_New_Testament" title="Apostles in the New Testament">Apostles</a>, consecrating it in 381 in the presence of Ambrose and <a href="/wiki/Saint_Felix_of_Como" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Felix of Como">Saint Felix of Como</a>.  He participated in the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Aquileia_in_381" class="mw-redirect" title="Council of Aquileia in 381">Council of Aquileia in 381</a><sup id="cite_ref-Paulinus_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Paulinus-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and may perhaps have participated in the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Milan" class="mw-redirect" title="Council of Milan">Council of Milan</a> (390), in which <a href="/wiki/Jovinian" title="Jovinian">Jovinian</a> was condemned.
</p><p>Bassianus's signature is found together with Ambrose's in a letter sent to <a href="/wiki/Pope_Siricius" title="Pope Siricius">Pope Siricius</a>. In 397, Bassianus was present at the death of his friend Ambrose.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Bassianus died around 413.<sup id="cite_ref-Paulinus_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Paulinus-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was known as a caring shepherd and was believed to have the gift of healing the sick.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bassianus_of_Lodi&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 1158, when <a href="/wiki/Milan" title="Milan">Milanese</a> forces destroyed Lodi, his <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> were taken to Milan. They were returned in 1163, when Lodi was rebuilt by <a href="/wiki/Frederick_Barbarossa" title="Frederick Barbarossa">Frederick Barbarossa</a>, and enshrined in the <a href="/wiki/Lodi_Cathedral" title="Lodi Cathedral">Cathedral of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary</a> in Lodi.<sup id="cite_ref-Cambielli_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cambielli-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Bassianus is the patron saint of Lodi and is invoked against leprosy.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> His feast day is January 19, the occasion of the St. Bassianus’ Fair in Lodi.<sup id="cite_ref-Cambielli_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cambielli-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
