<p><b>Herve</b> (<small>French pronunciation:&#160;</small><span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)" class="IPA" lang="fr-Latn-fonipa"><a href="/wiki/Help:IPA/French" title="Help:IPA/French">[ɛʁv]</a></span>&#32;<span class="nowrap" style="font-size:85%">(<span class="unicode haudio"><span class="fn"><span style="white-space:nowrap;margin-right:.25em;"><a href="/wiki/File:LL-Q150_(fra)-Manestra-Herve.wav" title="About this sound"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Loudspeaker.svg/11px-Loudspeaker.svg.png" decoding="async" width="11" height="11" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Loudspeaker.svg/17px-Loudspeaker.svg.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Loudspeaker.svg/22px-Loudspeaker.svg.png 2x" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20" /></a></span><a href="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/39/LL-Q150_%28fra%29-Manestra-Herve.wav" class="internal" title="LL-Q150 (fra)-Manestra-Herve.wav">listen</a></span></span>)</span>; <a href="/wiki/Limburgish_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Limburgish language">Limburgish</a>: <i lang="li">Herf</i>; <a href="/wiki/Walloon_language" title="Walloon language">Walloon</a>: <i lang="wa">Heve</i>) is a <a href="/wiki/City_status_in_Belgium" title="City status in Belgium">city</a> and <a href="/wiki/Municipalities_of_Belgium" title="Municipalities of Belgium">municipality</a> of <a href="/wiki/Wallonia" title="Wallonia">Wallonia</a> located in the <a href="/wiki/Li%C3%A8ge_Province" title="Liège Province">province of Liège</a>, <a href="/wiki/Belgium" title="Belgium">Belgium</a>. On January 1, 2018 Herve had a total population of 17,598. The total area is 56.84&#160;km<sup>2</sup> (21.95 square miles) which gives a <a href="/wiki/Population_density" title="Population density">population density</a> of 310 inhabitants per square kilometre (800/sq&#160;mi).
</p><p>It is famed for its <a href="/wiki/Herve_cheese" class="mw-redirect" title="Herve cheese">Herve cheese</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Municipal_merger">Municipal merger</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Herve&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Municipal merger">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Since January 1, 1977, the municipality consists of the following districts: <a href="/w/index.php?title=Battice&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Battice (page does not exist)">Battice</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolland" class="extiw" title="fr:Bolland">fr</a>&#93;</span>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Chaineux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Chaineux (page does not exist)">Chaineux</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaineux" class="extiw" title="fr:Chaineux">fr</a>&#93;</span>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Charneux&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Charneux (page does not exist)">Charneux</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charneux" class="extiw" title="fr:Charneux">fr</a>&#93;</span>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Grand-Rechain&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Grand-Rechain (page does not exist)">Grand-Rechain</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; </p><p>Herve is currently constituted of 11 villages: Battice, Bolland, Bruyères, Chaineux, Charneux, Grand-Rechain, Herve, José, Julémont, Manaihant, Xhendelesse.
</p><p>There are a number of smaller villages in the Herve region, such as <a href="/wiki/Hacboister" title="Hacboister">Hacboister</a> (district of Bolland).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Architecture">Architecture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Herve&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Architecture">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><i>The Church of St John the Baptist</i>: built in the 17th century. The tower, with a height of 49&#160;m (161 feet), dates back to the 13th century.
