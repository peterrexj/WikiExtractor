<a href="/wiki/Limburgish_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Limburgish language">Limburgish</a>: <i lang="li">Herf</i>; <a href="/wiki/Walloon_language" title="Walloon language">Walloon</a>: <i lang="wa">Heve</i>) is a <a href="/wiki/City_status_in_Belgium" title="City status in Belgium">city</a> and <a href="/wiki/Municipalities_of_Belgium" title="Municipalities of Belgium">municipality</a> of <a href="/wiki/Wallonia" title="Wallonia">Wallonia</a> located in the <a href="/wiki/Li%C3%A8ge_Province" title="Liège Province">province of Liège</a>, <a href="/wiki/Belgium" title="Belgium">Belgium</a>. On January 1, 2018 Herve had a total population of 17,598. The total area is 56.84&#160;km<sup>2</sup> (21.95 square miles) which gives a <a href="/wiki/Population_density" title="Population density">population density</a> of 310 inhabitants per square kilometre (800/sq&#160;mi).
</p><p>It is famed for its <a href="/wiki/Herve_cheese" class="mw-redirect" title="Herve cheese">Herve cheese</a>.
</p>

</p><p>Herve is currently constituted of 11 villages: Battice, Bolland, Bruyères, Chaineux, Charneux, Grand-Rechain, Herve, José, Julémont, Manaihant, Xhendelesse.
</p><p>There are a number of smaller villages in the Herve region, such as <a href="/wiki/Hacboister" title="Hacboister">Hacboister</a> (district of Bolland).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Architecture">Architecture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Herve&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Architecture">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><i>The Church of St John the Baptist</i>: built in the 17th century. The tower, with a height of 49&#160;m (161 feet), dates back to the 13th century.
