<p><b>Theodosius the Cenobiarch</b> (<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 423&#8211;529) was a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a>, <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> who was a founder and organizer of the <a href="/wiki/Cenobitic" class="mw-redirect" title="Cenobitic">cenobitic</a> way of monastic life. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is on <a href="/wiki/January_11_(Eastern_Orthodox_liturgics)" title="January 11 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)">January 11</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He was born in Mogarissos, a village in <a href="/wiki/Cappadocia" title="Cappadocia">Cappadocia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Basil_of_Caesarea" title="Basil of Caesarea">Saint Basil</a>'s province. Theodosius' parents Proheresius and Eulogia were both very pious.  Later Eulogia would become a nun taking her son Theodosius as her spiritual father.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Monastic_beginnings">Monastic beginnings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Monastic beginnings">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When he was younger he felt a desire to imitate Abraham by leaving his parents, friends, relatives and everything else for the love of God.  Theodosius set out for <a href="/wiki/Jerusalem" title="Jerusalem">Jerusalem</a> at the time of the Holy <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Ecumenical_Council" class="mw-redirect" title="Fourth Ecumenical Council">Fourth Ecumenical Council</a> of Chalcedon held in 451.
</p><p>When Theodosius reached <a href="/wiki/Antioch" title="Antioch">Antioch</a>, he went to see <a href="/wiki/Saint_Simeon_Stylites" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Simeon Stylites">Saint Symeon the Stylite</a>, to ask for his prayers and blessing.  Arriving at the pillar of Saint Symeon he was miraculously greeted by name and was invited to ascend the column.  Theodosius climbed the pillar and prostrated himself before Saint Symeon who embraced him with his blessing and prophesied great spiritual glory for Theodosius.
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_(south_east_groin-vault)_-_Theodosios.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Hosios Loukas Crypt (south east groin-vault) - Theodosios.jpg" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/90/Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28south_east_groin-vault%29_-_Theodosios.jpg/220px-Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28south_east_groin-vault%29_-_Theodosios.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="228" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/90/Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28south_east_groin-vault%29_-_Theodosios.jpg/330px-Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28south_east_groin-vault%29_-_Theodosios.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/90/Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28south_east_groin-vault%29_-_Theodosios.jpg/440px-Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28south_east_groin-vault%29_-_Theodosios.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1068" data-file-height="1109" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_(south_east_groin-vault)_-_Theodosios.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div></div></div></div>
<p>When Theodosius reached Jerusalem he spent time visiting and venerating the Holy Places. He then decided it would be best to obtain discipline for himself before he settled in solitude. Theodosius began his monastic labors under the hermit abbot Longinus, settling near the <a href="/wiki/Tower_of_David" title="Tower of David">Tower of David</a>. During this time there lived a wealthy and pious woman named Ikelia, who built a church near a place called "<a href="/wiki/Church_of_the_Seat_of_Mary_(Kathisma)" class="mw-redirect" title="Church of the Seat of Mary (Kathisma)">The Old Kathisma</a>" and dedicated it to the Theotokos. Ikelia requested to the elder Longinus that Theodosius settle in that place to which he agreed. After some time Theodosius had many visitors and pilgrims who distracted and deprived him of his solitude.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Life_as_a_hermit">Life as a hermit</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Life as a hermit">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Eventually Theodosius could no longer handle his admirers and left the Old Kathisma so that he might better dedicate himself to God. He settled on the peak of the mountain and lived in a cave. There is a tradition from the ancient fathers that this was the same cave the <a href="/wiki/Three_Magi" class="mw-redirect" title="Three Magi">three Magi</a> spent the night after they had worshiped the Lord, and that an angel was sent there and ordered them to return to their own country by another way, which they did.
</p><p>While dwelling in the cave Theodosius performed great feats of asceticism. He prayed at all times and he even suspended a rope that it might prop him up in case sleep overcame him, and thus he stood through the night in prayer. He remembered never to satisfy his hunger, but ate only enough so that he would not fall ill from extreme fasting. He ate only <a href="/wiki/Date_(fruit)" class="mw-redirect" title="Date (fruit)">dates</a>, <a href="/wiki/Carob" title="Carob">carob</a>, wild vegetables and <a href="/wiki/Legume" title="Legume">legumes</a> soaked in water. If the land was unproductive and arid he soaked the hearts of palms and ate them only from excessive need. He never tasted bread for thirty years.
</p>
<h3><span id="Communal_life.2C_founding_of_future_monastery"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Communal_life,_founding_of_future_monastery">Communal life, founding of future monastery</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Communal life, founding of future monastery">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Theodosius_Monastery_(9198074875).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/St._Theodosius_Monastery_%289198074875%29.jpg/220px-St._Theodosius_Monastery_%289198074875%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="168" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/St._Theodosius_Monastery_%289198074875%29.jpg/330px-St._Theodosius_Monastery_%289198074875%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/St._Theodosius_Monastery_%289198074875%29.jpg/440px-St._Theodosius_Monastery_%289198074875%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1549" data-file-height="1182" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Theodosius_Monastery_(9198074875).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Monastery of St. Theodosius</div></div></div>
<p>Theodosius formed a small community of monks near <a href="/wiki/Bethlehem" title="Bethlehem">Bethlehem</a>, which later became the <a href="/wiki/Monastery_of_St._Theodosius" class="mw-redirect" title="Monastery of St. Theodosius">Monastery of St. Theodosius</a>. The community grew rapidly, with monks of several cultures and languages, and became very well known for its work with the sick, elderly, and mentally impaired. When Theodosius' friend and countryman <a href="/wiki/Sabbas_the_Sanctified" title="Sabbas the Sanctified">Sabbas</a> was appointed <a href="/wiki/Archimandrite" title="Archimandrite">archimandrite</a> of all the isolated monks in Palestine by Patriarch Salustius of Jerusalem, Theodosius was made the leader of all those monks who lived in community. This is the origin of his being called "the Cenobiarch", which translates as chief of those living a life in common.
</p><p>When Theodosius was at the age of ninety, <a href="/wiki/Anastasius_I_(emperor)" class="mw-redirect" title="Anastasius I (emperor)">Emperor Anastasius</a> was fighting the Christians and he exiled Theodosius, who appears to have taken refuge in Lebanon, and the Maronite tradition tells that he was the one who established the monastery of Qannoubine, meaning the monastery of "common living" in <a href="/wiki/Kadisha_Valley" title="Kadisha Valley">Kadisha Valley</a>, and the tradition has preserved this name through generations to this day.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Anti-miaphysitic_stance">Anti-miaphysitic stance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Anti-miaphysitic stance">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Theodosius was a staunch opponent of <a href="/wiki/Miaphysitism" title="Miaphysitism">Miaphysitism</a>, which caused the <a href="/wiki/Anastasius_I_(emperor)" class="mw-redirect" title="Anastasius I (emperor)">Emperor Anastasius</a> to remove him from office temporarily.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Theodosius_the_Cenobiarch&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Theodosius died near <a href="/wiki/Jerusalem" title="Jerusalem">Jerusalem</a>, at about 105 years old.
</p>
