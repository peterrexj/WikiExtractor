<p><b>Saint Basil of Ostrog</b> (<a href="/wiki/Serbian_language" title="Serbian language">Serbian</a>: <span lang="sr">Свети Василије Острошки</span>/<i>Sveti Vasilije Ostroški</i>, <small>pronounced&#160;</small><span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)" class="IPA" lang="sh-Latn-fonipa"><a href="/wiki/Help:IPA/Serbo-Croatian" title="Help:IPA/Serbo-Croatian">[sʋê:ti ʋǎsiːlije ǒstroʃki]</a></span>, 28 December 1610 – 29 April 1671), also known as Vasilije,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was a <a href="/wiki/Serbian_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Serbian Orthodox">Serbian Orthodox</a> bishop of <a href="/wiki/Eparchy_of_Zahumlje_and_Herzegovina" title="Eparchy of Zahumlje and Herzegovina">Zahumlje</a> who is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Basil_of_Ostrog&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Basil_of_Ostrog&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><b>Stojan Jovanović</b> (<a href="/wiki/Serbian_Cyrillic_alphabet" title="Serbian Cyrillic alphabet">Serbian Cyrillic</a>: <span lang="sr-Cyrl">Стојан Јовановић</span>) was born on 28 December 1610, in the village of <a href="/wiki/Mrkonji%C4%87i" title="Mrkonjići">Mrkonjići</a> at the bottom of the <a href="/wiki/Popovo_field" class="mw-redirect" title="Popovo field">Popovo field</a> in the Ljubinje nahija (<a href="/wiki/Herzegovina" title="Herzegovina">Herzegovina</a>), at the time part of the <a href="/wiki/Ottoman_Empire" title="Ottoman Empire">Ottoman Empire</a>. His father was Petar Stojanović and his mother was Ana. He was a diligent and obedient child, and he inherited his graciousness and benevolence from his parents. Having raised cattle on the Herzegovinian hills and mountain slopes, he shared all of his food with poorer people. The Ottomans started to notice him, unenthusiastically monitoring this gifted young man. His parents, in order to avoid any danger, took him to the nearby <a href="/wiki/Zavala_Monastery" title="Zavala Monastery">Zavala Monastery</a>, where his paternal uncle, the <a href="/wiki/Hieromonk" title="Hieromonk">hieromonk</a> Serafim served as the <a href="/wiki/Hegumen" title="Hegumen">hegumen</a> of the monastery. There he would study, and be protected.<sup id="cite_ref-Mileusnić2000_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mileusnić2000-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Studies">Studies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Basil_of_Ostrog&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Studies">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Having a rich library at his hands, the young Vasilije especially liked the cells dug into the rocks above the monastery church of the Presentation of the Blessed Virgin Mary. His character quickly made him a master of the Bible and basic Christian mysteries. After a while his uncle sent his protégé to the Monastery of the Most Holy Mother of God, the <a href="/wiki/Tvrdo%C5%A1_Monastery" title="Tvrdoš Monastery">Tvrdoš Monastery</a> in <a href="/wiki/Trebinje" title="Trebinje">Trebinje</a>, for further spiritual and theological studies.<sup id="cite_ref-Mileusnić2000_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Mileusnić2000-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Bishop_of_Zahumlje">Bishop of Zahumlje</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Basil_of_Ostrog&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Bishop of Zahumlje">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>St. Basil's modesty made him reluctant to occupy high positions.  However, his piety, humility and dedication to the Church made him well suited for the role and he was elected as <a href="/wiki/Eparchy_of_Zahumlje_and_Herzegovina" title="Eparchy of Zahumlje and Herzegovina">Bishop of Zahumlje and Skenderija</a>, which he reluctantly accepted in 1639. He retired from the position ten years later in 1649.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Basil_of_Ostrog&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:152px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki,_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki%2C_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg/150px-Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki%2C_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg" decoding="async" width="150" height="208" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki%2C_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg/225px-Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki%2C_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki%2C_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg/300px-Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki%2C_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg 2x" data-file-width="4329" data-file-height="6000" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Sv._Vasilije_Ostro%C5%A1ki,_manastir_Bijeljina.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Monastery_of_St._Basil_of_Ostrog,_Bijeljina" title="Monastery of St. Basil of Ostrog, Bijeljina">Monastery of St. Basil of Ostrog</a> in <a href="/wiki/Bijeljina" title="Bijeljina">Bijeljina</a></div></div></div>
<p>After his death in 1671 he was buried at the <a href="/wiki/Ostrog_Monastery" title="Ostrog Monastery">Ostrog Monastery</a> he had founded in <a href="/wiki/Montenegro" title="Montenegro">Montenegro</a>, and his tomb in a cave-church soon became a site of <a href="/wiki/Christian_pilgrimage" title="Christian pilgrimage">pilgrimage for Christians</a> (both <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Orthodox</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a>) and Muslims drawn by reports of miracles occurring through the intercession of the saint. The <a href="/wiki/Ostrog_Monastery" title="Ostrog Monastery">Monastery of Ostrog</a> is now one of the major pilgrimage sites in the Balkans, and large numbers of pilgrims gather particularly at <a href="/wiki/Pentecost" title="Pentecost">Pentecost</a>. St. Basil of Ostrog is commemorated in the Serbian orthodox liturgical calendar on April 29 (May 12 in the Gregorian Calendar).
</p><p>The Orthodox seminary in <a href="/wiki/East_Sarajevo" class="mw-redirect" title="East Sarajevo">East Sarajevo</a> (<a href="https://sr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogoslovski_fakultet_Univerziteta_u_Isto%C4%8Dnom_Sarajevu" class="extiw" title="sr:Bogoslovski fakultet Univerziteta u Istočnom Sarajevu">Pravoslavni bogoslovski fakultet Sveti Vasilije Ostroški</a>), part of the <a href="/wiki/University_of_East_Sarajevo" title="University of East Sarajevo">University of East Sarajevo</a>, is named after him. A church in <a href="/wiki/Nalje%C5%BEi%C4%87i" title="Nalježići">Nalježići</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Grbalj" title="Grbalj">Grbalj</a> region is named after him.
</p>
