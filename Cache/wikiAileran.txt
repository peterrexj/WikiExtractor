					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Irish scholar and saint</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Ailerán</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:Saint_Aileran.webp" class="image"><img alt="Saint Aileran.webp" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7d/Saint_Aileran.webp/250px-Saint_Aileran.webp.png" decoding="async" width="250" height="239" data-file-width="250" data-file-height="239" /></a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Residence</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">29 December 664</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">29 December</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><span class="nowrap">Major works</span></th><td class="infobox-data"><i>Ancestry of Our Lord Jesus Christ</i></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Ailerán</b>, also known as <b>Ailerán sapiens</b> (Ailerán the Wise) was an <a href="/wiki/Irish_people" title="Irish people">Irish</a> <a href="/wiki/Scholarly_method" title="Scholarly method">scholar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> who died on 29 December 664 or 665. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 29 December.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ailer%C3%A1n&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ailerán was one of the most distinguished scholars at the School of <a href="/wiki/Clonard_Abbey" title="Clonard Abbey">Clonard</a> in the 7th century.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His early life is not recorded, but he was attracted to Clonard by the fame of Saint <a href="/wiki/Finnian_of_Clonard" title="Finnian of Clonard">Finnián</a> and his disciples. He became <a href="/wiki/Reader_(liturgy)" title="Reader (liturgy)">lector</a> of the school<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> in 650. He died of the <a href="/wiki/Yellow_Plague" class="mw-redirect" title="Yellow Plague">Yellow Plague</a>, and his death is recorded in the <a href="/wiki/Annals_of_Ulster" title="Annals of Ulster">Annals of Ulster</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Because of his knowledge of the works of <a href="/wiki/Origen" title="Origen">Origen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Philo" title="Philo">Philo</a>, <a href="/wiki/St._Jerome" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Jerome">St. Jerome</a>, <a href="/wiki/Augustine_of_Hippo" title="Augustine of Hippo">St. Augustine</a>, and others, he was well versed in patristic literature.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Works">Works</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ailer%C3%A1n&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Works">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to <a href="/wiki/John_Colgan" title="John Colgan">John Colgan</a>, numerous works can be ascribed to Ailerán, including the <i>Fourth Life of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Patrick" title="Saint Patrick">Saint Patrick</a></i>, a Latin litany, and the <i>Lives</i> of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Brigid" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Brigid">Saint Brigid</a> and Saint <a href="/wiki/F%C3%A9ich%C3%ADn_of_Fore" class="mw-redirect" title="Féichín of Fore">Féichín of Fore</a>. Ailerán's best known work is his mystical interpretation of the <i>Ancestry of Our Lord Jesus Christ</i>, according to the <a href="/wiki/Genealogy_of_Jesus" title="Genealogy of Jesus">genealogy of Jesus</a> in <a href="/wiki/Gospel_of_Matthew" title="Gospel of Matthew">Saint Matthew's Gospel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> This was published in the <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> edition of the <a href="/wiki/Church_Fathers" title="Church Fathers">Fathers</a>, and the editors said that they published it although Aileran was not a Benedictine, because he " unfoulded the meaning of the Sacred Scripture with so much learning and ingenuity that every student of the Sacred Volume and especially preachers of the Divine Word will regard the publication as most acceptable."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Another work of his is titled <i>A Short Moral Explanation of the Sacred Names</i>, which could be a fragment of a larger work.
</p>
