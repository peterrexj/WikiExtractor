<p><b>Mercurius</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">Ἅγιος Μερκούριος</span>, <a href="/wiki/Coptic_language" title="Coptic language">Coptic</a>: <span lang="cop">Ⲫⲓⲗⲟⲡⲁⲧⲏⲣ Ⲙⲉⲣⲕⲟⲩⲣⲓⲟⲥ</span>; <a href="/wiki/Syriac_language" title="Syriac language">Syriac</a>: <span lang="syr" dir="rtl">ܡܳܪܩܘ̇ܪܝܘ̇ܣ</span>; 224/225 – 250 AD) was a Roman soldier of <a href="/wiki/Scythian" class="mw-redirect" title="Scythian">Scythian</a> descent who became a <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> saint and <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was born in the city of <a href="/wiki/Saratl%C4%B1,_G%C3%BCla%C4%9Fa%C3%A7" title="Saratlı, Gülağaç">Eskentos</a> in <a href="/wiki/Cappadocia_(Roman_province)" title="Cappadocia (Roman province)">Cappadocia</a>, in Eastern <a href="/wiki/Anatolia" title="Anatolia">Asia Minor</a> (modern-day <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>). According to <a href="/wiki/Christian_tradition" title="Christian tradition">Christian tradition</a>, he was the soldier who killed <a href="/wiki/Julian_(emperor)" title="Julian (emperor)">Julian the apostate</a> during his <a href="/wiki/Julian%27s_Persian_expedition" title="Julian&#39;s Persian expedition">campaign in Persia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Saint Mercurius was also widely known by his <a href="/wiki/Arabic" title="Arabic">Arabic</a>-language name <b>Abu-Sayfain</b>, <b>Abu-Sifin</b> or <b>Abu-Sefein</b> in <a href="/wiki/Egyptian_Arabic" title="Egyptian Arabic">Egyptian Arabic</a> (<a href="/wiki/Arabic_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Arabic language">Arabic</a>: <span lang="ar" dir="rtl">أبو سيفين</span>, <small><a href="/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic" title="Romanization of Arabic">romanized</a>:&#160;</small><span title="Arabic-language romanization"><i lang="ar-Latn">Abū Seyfaīn</i></span>; <a href="/wiki/Coptic_language" title="Coptic language">Coptic</a>: <span lang="cop">ⲁⲃⲩⲥⲉⲫⲁⲓⲛ</span>, <small>romanized:&#160;</small><span title="Coptic-language romanization"><i lang="cop-Latn">Abû-Sefaīn</i></span>) which means "father of two swords", referring to the second sword given to him by the <a href="/wiki/Michael_(archangel)" title="Michael (archangel)">Archangel Michael</a>.
</p><p>St. Mercurius was born around 225 A.D. in Cappadocia (Eastern Asia Minor) into a family of <a href="/wiki/Scythian" class="mw-redirect" title="Scythian">Scythian</a> descent.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> His parents were converts to Christianity and they called him "Philopater" or "Philopatyr" (a <a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a> name which means 'Lover of the Father'). They raised him in a Christian manner. When he grew to adulthood (at the age of 17), he enlisted in the Roman army in the reign of <a href="/wiki/Decius" title="Decius">Emperor Decius</a>. He gained a great reputation among his superiors as a swordsman and a tactician in many battles. During this period, it is said that the Emperor grew very close to him.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (January 2018)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Traditional_biography">Traditional biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Mercurius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Traditional biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Mercurius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f3/Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg/220px-Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="306" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f3/Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg/330px-Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f3/Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg/440px-Anonymous_-_Christ_appearing_to_Saints_Mercurius_and_Catherine_of_Alexandria_Who_Trample_Enemie_-_1871.112_-_Yale_University_Art_Gallery.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1379" data-file-height="1920" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Fresco" title="Fresco">Fresco</a> of Christ appearing to Saints Mercurius and <a href="/wiki/Catherine_of_Alexandria" title="Catherine of Alexandria">Catherine of Alexandria</a> in the act of <a href="/wiki/Tyrannicide" title="Tyrannicide">tyrannicide</a>, <a href="/wiki/Yale_University_Art_Gallery" title="Yale University Art Gallery">Yale University Art Gallery</a>, <a href="/wiki/Connecticut" title="Connecticut">Connecticut</a>, <a href="/wiki/United_States" title="United States">United States</a></figcaption></figure><p>
Some accounts state that Philopater was born in Eskentos in Cappadocia. However, others refer to Rome as his place of birth.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Mercurius was the son of Yares, an officer in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_army" title="Roman army">Roman army</a>.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (September 2021)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> One day, while Yares was hunting in the forest with his father, the two were attacked by an animal. The animal jumped on Yares' father, causing Yares to faint. While Yares was unconscious, he had a vision with a brilliant light and a voice saying:</p><blockquote><p>Yares, I am your God who loves you. I know that you have a good heart and that you hate the pagan idols. I want to inform you that your son, Philopatyr, will become like a tree bearing good fruits, and because of him, I will bless you and your wife. Philopatyr will be my witness and will defy all prejudice in my name.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<p>Yares, his wife, and his son were baptized shortly after. All three were given new names. Yares became <i>Noah</i>, his wife became <i>Saphina</i>, and Mercurius became <i>Philopater</i>. News of their <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptism</a> spread quickly in the city and the prince ordered them to be arrested and thrown to wild animals. However, the animals did not harm them and the prince decided to release Noah and his family.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>
When the Barbarians attacked, Noah went to fight them. He was taken prisoner and was brought to their territory, where he was kept for seventeen months. When the war finally ended, he went back to his city and joined his family, but died shortly after.</p><figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/34/Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg/170px-Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg" decoding="async" width="170" height="377" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/34/Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg/255px-Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/34/Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg/340px-Byzantine_icon_St-Mercurius_1295.jpg 2x" data-file-width="525" data-file-height="1164" /></a><figcaption>A <a href="/wiki/Byzantine_art" title="Byzantine art">Byzantine Greek fresco</a> of St. Mercurius, dated 1295, from <a href="/wiki/Ohrid" title="Ohrid">Ohrid</a>, <a href="/wiki/North_Macedonia" title="North Macedonia">North Macedonia</a></figcaption></figure>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Military_career_of_Saint_Mercurius">Military career of Saint Mercurius</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Mercurius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Military career of Saint Mercurius">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After the death of Mercurius' father Noah, the <a href="/wiki/Pagan" class="mw-redirect" title="Pagan">pagan</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Emperor" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Emperor">Roman Emperor</a> <a href="/wiki/Decius" title="Decius">Decius</a> (ruled 249–251) chose him to replace his father. Described as very strong and courageous, Mercurius earned the respect of his fellow soldiers and gained renown as a swordsman.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> When the Barbarians attacked Rome, Decius went out to fight them but became afraid when he saw how many there were. Mercurius then came to him and said, "Do not be afraid, because God will kill our enemies and will bring us victory."<sup id="cite_ref-cocn_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cocn-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After several days of fighting, the <a href="/wiki/Michael_(archangel)" title="Michael (archangel)">Archangel Michael</a> appeared to Mercurius holding a shining sword. The saint took the sword from the archangel, hence the name <i>Abu Sayfain</i> - "the father of two swords": a <a href="/wiki/Military" title="Military">military</a> sword and a <a href="/wiki/Divinity" title="Divinity">divine</a> sword.<sup id="cite_ref-cocn_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cocn-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> He conquered the Barbarians. When Decius heard news of the triumphant victory, he named Mercurius as a prince.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Martyrdom_of_Saint_Mercurius">Martyrdom of Saint Mercurius</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Mercurius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Martyrdom of Saint Mercurius">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bf/Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg/180px-Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg" decoding="async" width="180" height="270" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bf/Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg/270px-Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bf/Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg/360px-Yuhanna-Mercurius.jpg 2x" data-file-width="792" data-file-height="1186" /></a><figcaption>Coptic Icon of St Mercurius by <a href="/wiki/Yuhanna_al-Armani" title="Yuhanna al-Armani">Yuhanna al-Armani</a> in <a href="/wiki/The_Hanging_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="The Hanging Church">The Hanging Church</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cairo" title="Cairo">Cairo</a>.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Nonetheless, in 249, Decius began his persecution of Christians, compelling everyone to offer sacrifices to his pagan gods. The Archangel Michael appeared to Mercurius and told him to remember God and not be fearful of persecution. The saint was encouraged and spent the whole night praying fervently, confessing his weakness to God.
</p><p>The Emperor sent messengers to summon Mercurius to the palace, saying: "Dear Mercurius, let us go offer incense to the gods who helped us attain victory in the war." As they were leaving, Mercurius slipped through the crowd and went away. However, one of the guards reported his absence, and the Emperor called Mercurius and asked him: "Is it true that you refused to worship the idols who helped us during the war?"<sup id="cite_ref-amsm_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amsm-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> Mercurius declared himself a Christian, saying, "I do not worship anyone except my Lord and my God, Jesus Christ."<sup id="cite_ref-cocn_10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cocn-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The Emperor tried to persuade him to give up his faith but failed. He then ordered Mercurius to be stripped of his rank and tortured.<sup id="cite_ref-amsm_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amsm-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> Fearing a revolt because the people loved Mercurius, the emperor had him bound in iron fetters and sent him to <a href="/wiki/Caesarea_Maritima" title="Caesarea Maritima">Caesarea</a>. Mercurius was beheaded on 4 December 250 AD.<sup id="cite_ref-amsm_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-amsm-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> He was only 25 years old.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_traditions">Other traditions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Mercurius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Other traditions">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/02/Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg/180px-Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg" decoding="async" width="180" height="316" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/02/Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg/270px-Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/02/Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg/360px-Triptych_Harbaville_Louvre_OA3247_n5.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1140" data-file-height="2000" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Arethas_(martyr)" title="Arethas (martyr)">St. Arethas</a> with Saint Eustratius. In the roundels, Mercurius and <a href="/wiki/Thomas_the_Apostle" title="Thomas the Apostle">Thomas the Apostle</a> (from the <a href="/wiki/Harbaville_Triptych" title="Harbaville Triptych">Harbaville Triptych</a>).</figcaption></figure>
<p>After the end of the first persecution, the place of the saint's burial was revealed. Mercurius appeared to a poor man in the city and told him that he was "Mercurius, the Martyr of the Lord". "My body is buried in <a href="/wiki/Cappadocia" title="Cappadocia">Cappadocia Gardens</a>, under the old house on the way to the royal palace. My body looks as white as snow, because Jesus was present at the time of my <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyrdom</a>", said the saint.
</p><p>The next morning, the man went to dig under the old house. He began to smell the scent of perfume, seeing the body of the saint. The news spread quickly and many people came to take a look at the blessed body. They moved it temporarily to the local church until they built a new church bearing his name where Mercurius' body was buried with respect and devotion.
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_(central_groin_vault)_-_Eustace,_Mercurios,_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e5/Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28central_groin_vault%29_-_Eustace%2C_Mercurios%2C_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg/220px-Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28central_groin_vault%29_-_Eustace%2C_Mercurios%2C_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="265" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e5/Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28central_groin_vault%29_-_Eustace%2C_Mercurios%2C_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg/330px-Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28central_groin_vault%29_-_Eustace%2C_Mercurios%2C_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e5/Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28central_groin_vault%29_-_Eustace%2C_Mercurios%2C_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg/440px-Hosios_Loukas_Crypt_%28central_groin_vault%29_-_Eustace%2C_Mercurios%2C_Nicetas_and_Nestor.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2946" data-file-height="3552" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Mosaic" title="Mosaic">Mosaic</a> depicting St. Mercurius Abu-Sifin, <a href="/wiki/Hosios_Loukas" title="Hosios Loukas">Hosios Loukas Monastery</a>, <a href="/wiki/Boeotia" title="Boeotia">Boeotia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Greece" title="Greece">Greece</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>A few years later, the <a href="/wiki/Catholicos_of_Armenia" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholicos of Armenia">Catholicos of Armenia</a> and the Chief <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Armenian_Apostolic_Church" title="Armenian Apostolic Church">Armenian Apostolic Church</a>, visited Egypt and met with the <a href="/wiki/Patriarch_of_Alexandria" title="Patriarch of Alexandria">Patriarch of Alexandria</a>, the leader of the <a href="/wiki/Coptic_Orthodox_Church" title="Coptic Orthodox Church">Coptic Orthodox Church</a>. The latter asked him if Egypt could have part of the relics of Saint Mercurius to be placed in the church that bears his name in Egypt. On <a href="/wiki/Paoni_9_(Coptic_Orthodox_liturgics)" class="mw-redirect" title="Paoni 9 (Coptic Orthodox liturgics)">9 Paoni</a> (16 June), part of the blessed relics of Saint Mercurius were transferred to Egypt.
</p><p>According to one tradition, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Basil" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Basil">Saint Basil</a> once prayed before an icon on which Mercurius was portrayed as a soldier carrying a spear. He asked God not to permit the emperor <a href="/wiki/Julian_the_Apostate" class="mw-redirect" title="Julian the Apostate">Julian the Apostate</a> (361-363) to return from his war against the Persians and resume his oppression of Christians. The image of the holy <a href="/wiki/Great_Martyr" class="mw-redirect" title="Great Martyr">Great Martyr</a> Mercurius depicted on the icon became invisible, only to reappear later with a bloodied spear. Julian the Apostate, on his Persian campaign, was mortally wounded by the spear of an unknown saracen soldier.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Manastir_Sase10.JPG" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/Manastir_Sase10.JPG/260px-Manastir_Sase10.JPG" decoding="async" width="260" height="195" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/Manastir_Sase10.JPG/390px-Manastir_Sase10.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/Manastir_Sase10.JPG/520px-Manastir_Sase10.JPG 2x" data-file-width="4000" data-file-height="3000" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Fresco" title="Fresco">Fresco</a> depicting St. Mercurius Abu-Sifin (center), <a href="/wiki/Sase_Monastery" title="Sase Monastery">Sase Monastery</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bratunac" title="Bratunac">Bratunac</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina" title="Bosnia and Herzegovina">Bosnia</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>The nuns and their abbess, <a href="/wiki/Mother_Irini" title="Mother Irini">Tenmav Irini</a> at the Abu Sefein Monastery in Cairo credit the saint's intercession for the reversal of a government decision to confiscate their property for the Defense Ministry.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to local <a href="/wiki/Folklore" title="Folklore">folklore</a>, after Tamav Irini prayed for Abu Sefein's intercession, he appeared, in one way or another, to <a href="/wiki/Egyptian_president" class="mw-redirect" title="Egyptian president">Egyptian president</a> <a href="/wiki/Hosni_Mubarak" title="Hosni Mubarak">Hosni Mubarak</a>. Egyptian locals claim him to have become a <a href="/wiki/Crypto-Christianity" title="Crypto-Christianity">crypto-christian</a> to this day.
</p>
