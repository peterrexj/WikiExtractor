					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Basilla of Rome</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Martyr</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">257<br />Rome</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">May 20<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Basilla of Rome</b>, also known as <b>Basilissa</b> and <b>Babilla</b>, was a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> and <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> of the 3rd century. According to myth she was born into a Roman noble family and was a niece of the emperor <a href="/wiki/Gallienus" title="Gallienus">Gallienus</a>. She was beheaded in 257 under the Roman emperor <a href="/wiki/Valerian_(emperor)" title="Valerian (emperor)">Valerian</a> because she refused to marry Pompeius (or Pompey), a <a href="/wiki/Patrician_(ancient_Rome)" title="Patrician (ancient Rome)">patrician</a> and <a href="/wiki/Paganism" title="Paganism">pagan</a> described as "a man of equal rank"<sup id="cite_ref-saintlywomen_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-saintlywomen-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> to her, after she converted to Christianity. She was baptized by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Cornelius" title="Pope Cornelius">Pope Cornelius</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-holweck_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-holweck-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-delaney_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-delaney-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-bookofsaints_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bookofsaints-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Her maid accused her of being a Christian, and Pompeius betrayed her to Valerian when "she remained steadfast in her refusal to marry him".<sup id="cite_ref-delaney_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-delaney-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Basilla was buried in the cemetery and catacombs of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Hermes" title="Saint Hermes">Saint Hermes</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Via_Salaria" title="Via Salaria">Salarian Way</a> near <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> 
Hagiographer Agnes Dunbar states that Basilla was buried in a cemetery that she owned, which may have been named for her or for the other martyrs buried there. In the 9th century, her body was moved to the <a href="/wiki/Santa_Prassede" title="Santa Prassede">Basilica of Santa Prassede</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-saintlywomen_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-saintlywomen-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 1654, her <a href="/wiki/Relic" title="Relic">relics</a> were found and buried at the Hôtel-Dieu de Bayeux. Saint Basillia's feast day is May 20.<sup id="cite_ref-holweck_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-holweck-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-delaney_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-delaney-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-bookofsaints_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bookofsaints-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
