					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Trojanus of Saintes</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> (died c.530) was a sixth-century <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Saintes" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Saintes">bishop of Saintes</a>, in France. He is mentioned in semi-legendary terms by <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He is identified with the author of a surviving letter to <a href="/w/index.php?title=Eumerius_of_Nantes&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Eumerius of Nantes (page does not exist)">Eumerius of Nantes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is a Catholic saint, known also as <b>Trojan</b> and <b>Troyen</b>; his feast day is November 30.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
