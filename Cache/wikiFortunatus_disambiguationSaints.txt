<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Saints">Saints</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Fortunatus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Saints">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Fortunatus_the_Apostle" class="mw-redirect" title="Fortunatus the Apostle">Fortunatus the Apostle</a>, one of the 70 Disciples of Jesus Christ, companion of <a href="/wiki/Achaicus_of_Corinth" title="Achaicus of Corinth">Achaicus of Corinth</a></li>
<li>Fortunatus (1st century), martyred with <a href="/wiki/Orontius_of_Lecce" title="Orontius of Lecce">SS Orontius and Justus</a></li>
<li>Fortunatus (died c. 70), a deacon martyred with <a href="/wiki/Hermagoras_of_Aquileia" title="Hermagoras of Aquileia">Hermagoras of Aquileia</a></li>
<li>Fortunatus (died 212), martyred with <a href="/wiki/Felix,_Fortunatus,_and_Achilleus" title="Felix, Fortunatus, and Achilleus">SS Felix and Achilleus</a></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Fortunatus_of_Casei" title="Fortunatus of Casei">Fortunatus of Casei</a> (died 286), a martyr</li>
<li>Fortunatus (died 303), a deacon martyred with SS&#160;<a href="/wiki/Felix_of_Thibiuca" title="Felix of Thibiuca">Felix of Thibiuca</a>, Audactus, Januarius, and Septimus</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Fortunatus_of_Naples" title="Fortunatus of Naples">Fortunatus of Naples</a>, 4th century bishop of Naples</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Fortunatus_of_Spoleto" title="Fortunatus of Spoleto">Fortunatus of Spoleto</a> (died 400), a priest near Montefalco</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Fortunatus_of_Todi" title="Fortunatus of Todi">Fortunatus of Todi</a> (died 537), bishop of Todi</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Venantius_Fortunatus" title="Venantius Fortunatus">Venantius Fortunatus</a> (died in the early 7th century), a poet and bishop of Poitiers</li></ul>
