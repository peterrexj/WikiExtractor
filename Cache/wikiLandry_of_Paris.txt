<p>Saint <b>Landry</b> or <b>Landericus of Paris</b> (<span style="white-space:nowrap;"><abbr title="died">d.</abbr>&#8201;<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;661</span></span>) was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Paris" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Paris">bishop of Paris</a> and is recognized as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> by the Roman Catholic Church. Landry built a hospital dedicated to St. Christopher, which later became the <a href="/wiki/H%C3%B4tel-Dieu_de_Paris" class="mw-redirect" title="Hôtel-Dieu de Paris">Hôtel-Dieu de Paris</a>. His feast day is 10 June.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Landry_of_Paris&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Landry was chief clerk of the Royal Chancery. In 650, he succeeded Audobertus as Bishop of Paris.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> During the famine of 650-51, Bishop Landry sold all of his personal possessions, as well as some of the furniture and sacred vessels of the church, to feed the poor.
<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is credited with building the first major <a href="/wiki/Hospital" title="Hospital">hospital</a> in the city, dedicating it to <a href="/wiki/Saint_Christopher" title="Saint Christopher">Saint Christopher</a>, (now the Hôtel-Dieu).<sup id="cite_ref-Harper_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Harper-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>In 653, Landry, with 23 other bishops, subscribed to the charter <a href="/wiki/Clovis_II" title="Clovis II">Clovis II</a> gave to <a href="/wiki/Saint_Denis_Basilica" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Denis Basilica">Saint-Denis Abbey</a>, exempting it from episcopal jurisdiction. It is believed that he built the original church of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Germain_l%27Auxerrois" title="Saint-Germain l&#39;Auxerrois">Saint-Germain l'Auxerrois</a>, which became the parish church of the kings of France.<sup id="cite_ref-Harper_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Harper-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died in 661.<sup id="cite_ref-Harper_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Harper-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Landry_of_Paris&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810,_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ef/Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810%2C_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg/260px-Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810%2C_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg" decoding="async" width="260" height="234" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ef/Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810%2C_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg/390px-Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810%2C_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ef/Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810%2C_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg/520px-Eglise_Saint-Landry_vers_1810%2C_d%27apr%C3%A8s_un_dessin_communiqu%C3%A9_par_M._Romain_Boulenger.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1428" data-file-height="1287" /></a><figcaption>Eglise Saint-Landry, <span title="circa">c.</span><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;1810</span></figcaption></figure>
<p>He was buried at the Church of Saint-Germain-l'Auxerrois, where there is a chapel named after him and most of his <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> are kept, except for two bones that were given to the parish of Saint-Landry in 1408, which was originally a chapel near the saint’s house in which he was accustomed to pray. He is honored with an office in the new Paris Breviary.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His feast day is 10 June.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Miracles were recorded of him.  One of them reads:
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>We have seen and known that a man which men call Raoul Gracard was smitten suddenly, and had the head much great and swollen, and was so red in the face of him that all folk that saw him deemed and held him for a <a href="/wiki/Leper" class="mw-redirect" title="Leper">leper</a>.  Which man with great haste came to the presence of Saint Landry, and there he confessed him much devoutly, receiving much benignly his penance, and after he came to the <a href="/wiki/Sudary" class="mw-redirect" title="Sudary">sudary</a> of the saint and with great devotion kissed it, and when he had done his offering and vow with much great faith and hope he returned, and <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/uneath" class="extiw" title="wiktionary:uneath">unnethe</a> [hardly] he was come to his house when he became as whole as ever he was.</p><div class="templatequotecite">—&#8202;<cite><a href="/wiki/Jacobus_de_Voragine" title="Jacobus de Voragine">Jacobus de Voragine</a>, <i>The Golden Legend</i><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup></cite></div></blockquote>
<p><a href="/wiki/St._Landry_Catholic_Church" title="St. Landry Catholic Church">St. Landry Catholic Church</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Opelousas,_Louisiana" title="Opelousas, Louisiana">Opelousas, Louisiana</a> is dedicated to Landry of Paris.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> A statue of Saint Landry stands behind the altar, and a stained glass window with his image at the southwest corner of the church.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (April 2019)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>  The civil parish of St. Landry, located in south Louisiana, is named after the church. 
</p><p>Port St-Landry was Paris' first dock.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
