					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Christian martyr saints of Thessalonica</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saints</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Agape, Chionia, and Irene</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:Agape,_Chionia,_and_Irene.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Agape, Chionia, and Irene.jpg" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5b/Agape%2C_Chionia%2C_and_Irene.jpg/250px-Agape%2C_Chionia%2C_and_Irene.jpg" decoding="async" width="250" height="449" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5b/Agape%2C_Chionia%2C_and_Irene.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="334" data-file-height="600" /></a><div class="infobox-caption">Agape, Chionia, and Irene</div></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">Aquileia</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">304 AD<br />Thessalonica</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">April 3 (Western Churches), April 16 (Eastern Churches)</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Agape</b>, <b>Chionia</b> and <b>Irene</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span lang="el">Αγάπη, Χιονία και Ειρήνη</span>) were sisters and <a href="/wiki/Christians" title="Christians">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a> from <a href="/wiki/Aquileia" title="Aquileia">Aquileia</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> martyred at Thessalonica in 304 AD. Agape and Chionia were charged with refusing to eat <a href="/wiki/Sacrificial_offering" class="mw-redirect" title="Sacrificial offering">sacrificial offerings</a>, whilst Irene was killed for keeping Christian books in violation of existing law. All were condemned to be <a href="/wiki/Death_by_burning" title="Death by burning">burned alive</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Agape,_Chionia,_and_Irene&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Orphaned at a young age, the sisters Agape, Chionia, and Irene led pious lives under the direction of the priest Xeno. They declined a number of offers of marriage. In 303, Emperor <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a> issued a decree making it a capital offense to possess Christian scriptures. The sisters hid their copies.<sup id="cite_ref-greg_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Eventually, they were arrested for offending the <a href="/wiki/Imperial_cult_of_ancient_Rome" class="mw-redirect" title="Imperial cult of ancient Rome">Imperial cult</a> by not eating food that had been sacrificed to the gods.<sup id="cite_ref-greg_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> They were brought before Emperor Diocletian, who could not persuade them to renounce their faith, and as he was leaving for Macedonia, brought them with him. There they were taken to the court of <a href="/wiki/Dulcitius" title="Dulcitius">Dulcitius</a>, governor of Thessalonica.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The sisters repulsed the governor's indecent advances. Annoyed with Dulcititus as ineffectual, Diocletian turned the three young women over to Count Sisinus for trial. He imprisoned Irene, the youngest; and making no headway in getting the older two to recant, ordered them to be burned. Afterwards the decedents appeared to be merely asleep as neither their clothes nor bodies had been scorched.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> After the deaths, their house was searched and the scriptures found and publicly burned.<sup id="cite_ref-greg_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-greg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Sisinus ordered Irene to be taken to a brothel, but on the way the escort was intercepted by two soldiers who told them to abandon her on a mountain. When they returned Sisinus grew angry as he had given no such orders. He pursued Irene and she was wounded in the throat with an arrow, at which point she died.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Four other individuals were tried with the sisters: Agatho, Casia, Philippa and Eutychia. Of these, one woman was remanded as she was pregnant. The fates of the other three are unknown.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Agape,_Chionia,_and_Irene&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The story of their martyrdom is the subject of a 10th-century medieval Latin drama by the secular canoness, <a href="/wiki/Hrotsvitha" title="Hrotsvitha">Hrotsvitha</a> of Gandersheim. 
</p><p>The island of <a href="/wiki/Santorini" title="Santorini">Santorini</a> is named after a cathedral established honoring Irene in the island village of Perissa.<sup id="cite_ref-rdnaturalwonders_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rdnaturalwonders-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
