<p><b>Francisco de Jesus Marto</b> (11 June 1908 – 4 April 1919) and <b>Jacinta de Jesus Marto</b> (11 March 1910<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> – 20 February 1920)<sup id="cite_ref-Shrine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Shrine-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> were siblings from <a href="/wiki/Aljustrel_(F%C3%A1tima)" title="Aljustrel (Fátima)">Aljustrel</a>, a small hamlet near <a href="/wiki/F%C3%A1tima,_Portugal" title="Fátima, Portugal">Fátima, Portugal</a>, who with their cousin <a href="/wiki/Sister_L%C3%BAcia" title="Sister Lúcia">Lúcia dos Santos</a> (1907&#8211;2005) reportedly witnessed three apparitions of the <a href="/wiki/Guardian_Angel_of_Portugal" class="mw-redirect" title="Guardian Angel of Portugal">Angel of Peace</a> in 1916 and several <a href="/wiki/Marian_apparition" title="Marian apparition">apparitions</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary">Blessed Virgin Mary</a> at <a href="/wiki/Cova_da_Iria" title="Cova da Iria">Cova da Iria</a> in 1917. The title <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Our Lady of Fátima">Our Lady of Fátima</a> was given to the Virgin Mary as a result, and the <a href="/wiki/Sanctuary_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Sanctuary of Fátima">Sanctuary of Fátima</a> became a major centre of world <a href="/wiki/Christian_pilgrimage" title="Christian pilgrimage">Christian pilgrimage</a>. 
</p><p>The two Marto children were solemnly <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Sanctuary_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Sanctuary of Fátima">Sanctuary of Our Lady of Fátima</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a> on 13 May 2017, the centennial of the first Apparition of <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Our Lady of Fátima">Our Lady of Fátima</a>. They are the youngest Catholic saints, with Jacinta being the youngest saint who did not die a martyr.<sup id="cite_ref-NBC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NBC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:162px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Francisco_Marto2.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7e/Francisco_Marto2.jpg/160px-Francisco_Marto2.jpg" decoding="async" width="160" height="266" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7e/Francisco_Marto2.jpg/240px-Francisco_Marto2.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/7e/Francisco_Marto2.jpg 2x" data-file-width="250" data-file-height="415" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Francisco_Marto2.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Francisco Marto</div></div></div>  
<p>The youngest children of Manuel and Olimpia Marto, Francisco and Jacinta were typical of Portuguese village children of that time. They were illiterate.<sup id="cite_ref-Religious_Phenomena_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Religious_Phenomena-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to the memoirs of their cousin <a href="/wiki/Sister_L%C3%BAcia" title="Sister Lúcia">Sister Lúcia</a>, Francisco had a placid disposition, was somewhat musically inclined, and liked to be by himself to think. Jacinta was affectionate with a sweet singing voice and a gift for dancing. Following their experiences, their fundamental personalities remained the same. Francisco preferred to pray alone, saying that this would "console Jesus for the sins of the world".<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Inline_citation#When_you_must_use_inline_citations" title="Wikipedia:Inline citation"><span title="The text near this tag needs a citation. (November 2022)">This quote needs a citation</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> Jacinta said she was deeply affected by a terrifying vision of Hell shown to the children at the third apparition, and deeply convinced of the need to save sinners through penance and sacrifice as the Virgin had told the children to do. All three children, but particularly Francisco and Jacinta, practiced stringent <a href="/wiki/Mortification_of_the_flesh" title="Mortification of the flesh">self-mortifications</a> to this end.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> The Congregation for the Causes of Saints, in the report that confirmed Jacinta as <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a>, observed that she seemed to have an "insatiable hunger for immolation."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> By this the Congregation was referring to immolation as offering in sacrifice.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Apparitions">Apparitions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Apparitions">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The brother and sister, who tended to their families’ sheep with their cousin Lúcia in the fields of Fátima, Portugal, reported seeing several apparitions of an angel in 1916. Lúcia later recorded the words of several prayers she said they learned from this angel.<sup id="cite_ref-Religious_Phenomena_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Religious_Phenomena-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Sister Lúcia wrote in her memoirs that she and her cousins saw the first apparition of Mary on 13 May 1917. At the time of the apparition, Francisco was 8 years old, and Jacinta was 7.<sup id="cite_ref-nd_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nd-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>During the first apparition, the children said Mary asked them to pray the Rosary and to make sacrifices, offering them for the conversion of sinners. She also asked them to return to that spot on the thirteenth of each month for the next six months.<sup id="cite_ref-Foley_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Foley-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Illness_and_death">Illness and death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Illness and death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The siblings were victims of the great <a href="/wiki/1918_flu_pandemic" class="mw-redirect" title="1918 flu pandemic">1918 influenza</a> epidemic that swept through Europe that year. In October 1918, Jacinta told Lucia that Mary had appeared to her and promised to take them to heaven soon. Both lingered for many months, insisting on walking to church to make Eucharistic devotions and prostrating themselves to pray for hours, <a href="/wiki/Proskynesis" title="Proskynesis">kneeling with their heads on the ground</a> as they said the angel had instructed them to do.<sup id="cite_ref-nd_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nd-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg/220px-Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="155" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg/330px-Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/fd/Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg/440px-Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1024" data-file-height="720" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Fatima_children_with_rosaries.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>From left to right: Jacinta Marto, <a href="/wiki/Sister_L%C3%BAcia" title="Sister Lúcia">Lúcia dos Santos</a> and Francisco Marto, holding their <a href="/wiki/Rosary" title="Rosary">rosaries</a> in 1917</div></div></div>
<p>Francisco declined hospital treatment on 3 April 1919, and died at home the next day. In an attempt to save her life, which she insisted was futile, Jacinta was moved to <a href="/wiki/Our%C3%A9m" title="Ourém">Ourém</a> Hospital. Her condition steadily worsened and, in an attempt to transfer her to the children's hospital in <a href="/wiki/Lisbon" title="Lisbon">Lisbon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Hospital_de_Dona_Estef%C3%A2nia" title="Hospital de Dona Estefânia">Queen Stephanie's Hospital</a> (which at the time only allowed for children from the city to be treated there), she was moved first to the care of the small Orphanage of Our Lady of Miracles, in the Lisbon neighbourhood of <a href="/wiki/Estrela_(Lisbon)" title="Estrela (Lisbon)">Estrela</a>. She developed purulent <a href="/wiki/Pleurisy" title="Pleurisy">pleurisy</a> and endured an operation in which two of her ribs were removed. Because of the condition of her heart, she could not be fully anesthetized, and suffered terrible pain, which she said would help to convert many sinners. On 19 February 1920, Jacinta asked the hospital <a href="/wiki/Chaplain" title="Chaplain">chaplain</a> who heard her <a href="/wiki/Confession_(religion)" title="Confession (religion)">confession</a> to bring her <a href="/wiki/Holy_Communion" class="mw-redirect" title="Holy Communion">Holy Communion</a> and administer <a href="/wiki/Extreme_Unction" class="mw-redirect" title="Extreme Unction">Extreme Unction</a> because she was going to die "the next night". He told her that her condition was not that serious and that he would return the next day. The next day Jacinta was dead; she had died, as she had often said she would, alone.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1920, shortly before her death at age nine, Jacinta Marto reportedly discussed the <a href="/wiki/Alliance_of_the_Hearts_of_Jesus_and_Mary" title="Alliance of the Hearts of Jesus and Mary">Alliance of the Hearts of Jesus and Mary</a> with a then 12-year-old <a href="/wiki/Sister_L%C3%BAcia" title="Sister Lúcia">Lúcia dos Santos</a> and said:
</p>
<blockquote>
<p>When you are to say this, don't go and hide. Tell everybody that God grants us graces through the Immaculate Heart of Mary; that people are to ask her for them; and that the Heart of Jesus wants the Immaculate Heart of Mary to be venerated at his side. Tell them also to pray to the Immaculate Heart of Mary for peace, since God entrusted it to her.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Jacinta and Francisco are both buried at the <a href="/wiki/Sanctuary_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Sanctuary of Fátima">Basilica of Our Lady of the Rosary of Fátima</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification_and_canonization">Beatification and canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Beatification and canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>The cause for the siblings' canonization began in 1946.<sup id="cite_ref-nd_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-nd-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> Exhumed in 1935, Jacinta's face was found to be <a href="/wiki/Incorruptibility" title="Incorruptibility">incorrupt</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup> Francisco's had decomposed. By 1951, when she was again exhumed for her reburial in the Basilica, Jacinta had begun to decompose also.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1937 <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> decided that causes for minors should not be accepted as they could not fully understand <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a> or practice it repeatedly, both of which are essential for canonization. For the next four decades, no sainthood processes for children were pursued. In 1979 the bishop of Leiria-Fátima asked all the world's bishops to write to the Pope, petitioning him to make an exception for Francisco, who had died at age 10, and Jacinta, who had died at age 9. More than 300 bishops sent letters to the Pope, writing that "the children were known, admired and attracted people to the way of sanctity. Favors were received through their intercession."<sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Inline_citation#When_you_must_use_inline_citations" title="Wikipedia:Inline citation"><span title="The text near this tag needs a citation. (October 2022)">This quote needs a citation</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> The bishops also said that the children's canonization was a pastoral necessity for the children and teenagers of the day.
</p><p>In 1979, the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> convened a general assembly. Cardinals, bishops, theologians and other experts debated whether it was possible for children to display heroic virtue. Eventually, they decided that, like the very few children who have a genius for music or mathematics, "in some supernatural way, some children could be spiritual prodigies."<sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Inline_citation#When_you_must_use_inline_citations" title="Wikipedia:Inline citation"><span title="The text near this tag needs a citation. (October 2022)">This quote needs a citation</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> They were declared venerable by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> in 1989.
</p><p>On 13 May 2000, they were declared "blessed" in a decree from the Congregation for the Causes of Saints.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> Jacinta is the youngest non-martyred child ever to be beatified.
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:277px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_(13_de_Maio_de_2017).png" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_%2813_de_Maio_de_2017%29.png/275px-Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_%2813_de_Maio_de_2017%29.png" decoding="async" width="275" height="209" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_%2813_de_Maio_de_2017%29.png/413px-Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_%2813_de_Maio_de_2017%29.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c3/Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_%2813_de_Maio_de_2017%29.png/550px-Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_%2813_de_Maio_de_2017%29.png 2x" data-file-width="1002" data-file-height="760" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Canoniza%C3%A7%C3%A3o_de_Francisco_e_Jacinta_Marto_(13_de_Maio_de_2017).png" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Canonization of Jacinta and Francisco Marto by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a>, at the <a href="/wiki/Sanctuary_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Sanctuary of Fátima">Sanctuary of Fátima</a> on 13 May 2017</div></div></div>
<p>When Pope John Paul II arrived in Fátima for the first time, in 1982, he said that he had come "because, on this exact date last year in <a href="/wiki/St._Peter%27s_Square" title="St. Peter&#39;s Square">St. Peter's Square</a>, in Rome, there was <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II_assassination_attempt" class="mw-redirect" title="Pope John Paul II assassination attempt">an attempt on the life of your Pope</a>, which mysteriously coincided with the anniversary of the first vision at Fátima, that of 13 May 1917. The coincidence of these dates was so great that it seemed to be a special invitation for me to come here."<sup id="cite_ref-seyer_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-seyer-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Another miracle was found to have been attributed to their intercession and the process that investigated the presumed miracle was validated on 8 February 2013. On 23 March 2017, it was announced that Pope Francis would canonize them while visiting Portugal on 12 and 13 May.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup> The pope solemnly canonized the children on 13 May 2017 during the centennial of the first apparition.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">&#91;20&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>
They are the Catholic Church's youngest saints who did not die as martyrs, with Jacinta the youngest.<sup id="cite_ref-NBC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NBC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21">&#91;21&#93;</a></sup></p><div style="clear:both;"></div>
