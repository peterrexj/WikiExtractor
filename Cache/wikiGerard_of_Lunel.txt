					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Gerard of Lunel</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">~1275<br />Lunel, France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">1298<br />Monte Santo, Italy</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">August 1, 1742 by <a href="/wiki/Benedict_XIV" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedict XIV">Benedict XIV</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">May 6; May 25</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">Patronage</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Potenza_Picena" title="Potenza Picena">Potenza Picena</a> (formerly called Monte Santo); invoked against <a href="/wiki/Epilepsy" title="Epilepsy">epilepsy</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and headaches<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Gerard of Lunel</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Gérard de Lunel</i>) (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">San Gerio, Girio</i>) (ca. 1275–1298), also known as <i>Roger of Lunel</i> and as <b>Saint Géri</b> (<i>Gerius</i>),<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> was a French <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>.  Born to the French nobility, he became a <a href="/wiki/Franciscan" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan">Franciscan</a> tertiary at the age of five.
</p><p>From his maternal grandfather, Raymond Guasselin, Gerard received half of the Barony of <a href="/wiki/Lunel,_H%C3%A9rault" title="Lunel, Hérault">Lunel</a>, which consisted of 15 villages. When his grandfather died in 1294, Gerard received other assets that were administered by his father, since the future saint was underage at the time.<sup id="cite_ref-San_Gerio_Girio_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-San_Gerio_Girio-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>  In 1295, when <a href="/wiki/Philip_IV_of_France" title="Philip IV of France">Philip the Fair</a> wished to possess a <a href="/wiki/Mediterranean" class="mw-redirect" title="Mediterranean">Mediterranean</a> port, he offered to assume control over Lunel in exchange for the county of <a href="/wiki/Roquefort" title="Roquefort">Roquefort</a>, also situated in <a href="/wiki/Languedoc" title="Languedoc">Languedoc</a>, but in the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Avignon" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Avignon">diocese of Avignon</a> rather than <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Montpellier" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Montpellier">Montpellier</a>.  Gerard thus became a count rather than a baron.  When he was 18, however, Gerard wished to live in solitude as a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>.  He and his brother <b>Effrenaud</b> (Effrendo) decided to live as hermit in two caves for two years.<sup id="cite_ref-San_Gerio_Girio_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-San_Gerio_Girio-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to his legend, when continuous rains swelled the river so much that the two brothers remained trapped in their cave and thus in danger of starving, two snakes carried bread to them.  The rain then stopped, and Gerard and his brother traveled to a distant castle to receive <a href="/wiki/Eucharist" title="Eucharist">communion</a>.  The miracle became known, and many people visited Gerard in his cave.<sup id="cite_ref-San_Gerio_Girio_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-San_Gerio_Girio-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Wishing to escape the fame that they were earning as a result of their apparent holiness, they decided to make a <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a> to the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Land" title="Holy Land">Holy Land</a>.  They spent two years in Rome, visiting its shrines and churches.  In Rome, Gerard learned that there lived a holy man named Liberius at <a href="/wiki/Ancona" title="Ancona">Ancona</a>, who had visited the Holy Land.  Wishing to visit him, the two brothers began to travel in the direction of Ancona, but Gerard suddenly suffered a pain in his head.<sup id="cite_ref-San_Gerio_Girio_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-San_Gerio_Girio-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Gerard suffered a collapse at Monte Santo (present-day <a href="/wiki/Potenza_Picena" title="Potenza Picena">Potenza Picena</a>),<sup id="cite_ref-San_Gerio_Girio_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-San_Gerio_Girio-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> near <a href="/wiki/Ancona" title="Ancona">Ancona</a>.  Effrenaud, leaving Gerard in a cottage, went to get help but by the time he came back, Gerard had died.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gerard_of_Lunel&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A communal council at Monte Santo in 1371 approved a day of festivity in honor of Gerard as May 25.<sup id="cite_ref-San_Gerio_Girio_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-San_Gerio_Girio-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
