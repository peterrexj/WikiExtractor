<p><b>Aghanus</b> (<a href="/wiki/Azerbaijani_language" title="Azerbaijani language">Azerbaijani</a>: <i lang="az">Ağanus</i>; <a href="/wiki/Armenian_language" title="Armenian language">Armenian</a>: <span lang="hy">Աղանուս</span>) is a village in the <a href="/wiki/Lachin_District" title="Lachin District">Lachin District</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azerbaijan" title="Azerbaijan">Azerbaijan</a>. According to Minister of Territorial Administration and Infrastructure of Nagorno Karabakh, Hayk Khanumyan, the village is located in the new corridor between <a href="/wiki/Armenia" title="Armenia">Armenia</a> and <a href="/wiki/Nagorno-Karabakh" title="Nagorno-Karabakh">Nagorno-Karabakh</a>, controlled by <a href="/wiki/Peacekeeping_operations_in_Nagorno-Karabakh" title="Peacekeeping operations in Nagorno-Karabakh">Russian peacekeepers</a>, that replaced the <a href="/wiki/Lachin_corridor" title="Lachin corridor">Lachin corridor</a> in August 2022.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=A%C4%9Fanus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The village was located in the <a href="/wiki/Armenian-occupied_territories_surrounding_Nagorno-Karabakh" title="Armenian-occupied territories surrounding Nagorno-Karabakh">Armenian-occupied territories surrounding Nagorno-Karabakh</a>, coming under the control of ethnic Armenian forces during the <a href="/wiki/First_Nagorno-Karabakh_War" title="First Nagorno-Karabakh War">First Nagorno-Karabakh War</a> in the early 1990s, subsequently becoming part of the <a href="/wiki/Political_status_of_Nagorno-Karabakh" title="Political status of Nagorno-Karabakh">breakaway</a> <a href="/wiki/Republic_of_Artsakh" title="Republic of Artsakh">Republic of Artsakh</a> as part of its <a href="/wiki/Kashatagh_Province" title="Kashatagh Province">Kashatagh Province</a>. The village was returned to Azerbaijan as part of the <a href="/wiki/2020_Nagorno-Karabakh_ceasefire_agreement" title="2020 Nagorno-Karabakh ceasefire agreement">2020 Nagorno-Karabakh ceasefire agreement</a>.
</p>
