<p><b>Mary of the Divine Heart</b> (<a href="/wiki/M%C3%BCnster" title="Münster">Münster</a>, 8 September 1863 – <a href="/wiki/Porto" title="Porto">Porto</a>, 8 June 1899), born <b>Maria Droste zu Vischering</b>, was a German <a href="/wiki/Uradel" title="Uradel">noblewoman</a> and <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Religious_sister" title="Religious sister">religious sister</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_of_Our_Lady_of_Charity_of_the_Good_Shepherd" title="Congregation of Our Lady of Charity of the Good Shepherd">Congregation of Our Lady of Charity of the Good Shepherd</a>. She is best known for having influenced <a href="/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIII" title="Pope Leo XIII">Pope Leo XIII</a> to consecrate the world to the <a href="/wiki/Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Sacred Heart of Jesus">Sacred Heart of Jesus</a>. Pope Leo XIII called the solemn consecration "the greatest act of my pontificate".<sup id="cite_ref-bainvel_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bainvel-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Birth">Birth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Birth">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:252px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Germany,_Nordrhein-Westfalen,_M%C3%BCnster_(1),_Erbdrostenhof.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Germany%2C_Nordrhein-Westfalen%2C_M%C3%BCnster_%281%29%2C_Erbdrostenhof.JPG/250px-Germany%2C_Nordrhein-Westfalen%2C_M%C3%BCnster_%281%29%2C_Erbdrostenhof.JPG" decoding="async" width="250" height="120" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Germany%2C_Nordrhein-Westfalen%2C_M%C3%BCnster_%281%29%2C_Erbdrostenhof.JPG/375px-Germany%2C_Nordrhein-Westfalen%2C_M%C3%BCnster_%281%29%2C_Erbdrostenhof.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/41/Germany%2C_Nordrhein-Westfalen%2C_M%C3%BCnster_%281%29%2C_Erbdrostenhof.JPG/500px-Germany%2C_Nordrhein-Westfalen%2C_M%C3%BCnster_%281%29%2C_Erbdrostenhof.JPG 2x" data-file-width="4512" data-file-height="2160" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Germany,_Nordrhein-Westfalen,_M%C3%BCnster_(1),_Erbdrostenhof.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Erbdrostenhof Palace (in <a href="/wiki/M%C3%BCnster" title="Münster">Münster</a>, <a href="/wiki/Germany" title="Germany">Germany</a>), the birthplace of Maria Droste zu Vischering</div></div></div>
<p>Maria Anna Johanna Franziska Theresia Antonia Huberta <a href="/wiki/Droste_zu_Vischering" title="Droste zu Vischering">Droste zu Vischering</a> was born with her twin brother Max (Maximilian Droste zu Vischering) on 8 September 1863, the feast of the <a href="/wiki/Nativity_of_Mary" title="Nativity of Mary">Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Erbdrostenhof" title="Erbdrostenhof">Erbdrostenhof Palace</a>, in <a href="/wiki/M%C3%BCnster" title="Münster">Münster</a>, the capital city of <a href="/wiki/Westphalia" title="Westphalia">Westphalia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Germany" title="Germany">Germany</a>. She was a daughter of one of the wealthiest and noblest<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Dates_and_numbers" title="Wikipedia:Manual of Style/Dates and numbers"><span title="This term requires quantification. (August 2022)">quantify</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> German families, which distinguished itself by its fidelity to the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a> during the persecution of the <i><a href="/wiki/Kulturkampf" title="Kulturkampf">Kulturkampf</a></i>. Her parents were Klemens Heidenreich Franz Hubertus Eusebius Maria, the <a href="/wiki/Graf" title="Graf">count</a> Droste zu Vischering, and Helene Clementine Maria Anna Sybille Huberta Antonia, the countess of Galen.<sup id="cite_ref-philippine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-philippine-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Because of the fragility of her health, Vischering was baptized immediately at birth.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_years">Early years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early years">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Maria Droste zu Vischering spent her childhood with her family in the Castle of Darfeld, in <a href="/wiki/Rosendahl" title="Rosendahl">Rosendahl</a> near Münster, and was a child full of life. Her mother stated, "She doesn't mind her dress. She is running into the mud, jumping into the wet grass and the bushes until she is so dirty that she has to change every stitch."<sup id="cite_ref-congregation_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-congregation-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:252px;"><a href="/wiki/File:20160505_Schloss_Darfeld,_Rosendahl_(05293).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/95/20160505_Schloss_Darfeld%2C_Rosendahl_%2805293%29.jpg/250px-20160505_Schloss_Darfeld%2C_Rosendahl_%2805293%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="250" height="121" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/95/20160505_Schloss_Darfeld%2C_Rosendahl_%2805293%29.jpg/375px-20160505_Schloss_Darfeld%2C_Rosendahl_%2805293%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/95/20160505_Schloss_Darfeld%2C_Rosendahl_%2805293%29.jpg/500px-20160505_Schloss_Darfeld%2C_Rosendahl_%2805293%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="4805" data-file-height="2318" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:20160505_Schloss_Darfeld,_Rosendahl_(05293).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Castle of Darfeld, where Maria Droste zu Vischering spent her childhood</div></div></div>
<p>Vischering was taught at home by governesses. On 25 April 1875 Maria and her brother Max received their <a href="/wiki/First_Communion" title="First Communion">First Communion</a>. In April 1879, Vischering continued her education at the boarding school of the Sacré-Coeur Sisters in <a href="/wiki/Riedenburg" title="Riedenburg">Riedenburg</a>, Bavaria. While there, she heard a homily on Psalm 45: "Listen, my daughter … Forget your people and your father’s house, that the king might desire your beauty."  Vischering decided that she should become a religious. During the spring of 1879, while she was listening to a particular devotion of one of the Sacré-Coeur Sisters, Maria Droste zu Vischering reached an important conclusion: "I began to understand that without the spirit of sacrifice the love of the Heart of Jesus is merely an illusion."<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (February 2016)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>

<p>While at school, she contracted pneumonia and, shortly before her 18th birthday, returned home to recover.<sup id="cite_ref-congregation_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-congregation-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In 1883, at the chapel of the Castle of Darfeld, she is said to have had an interior locution of Jesus Christ who told her: "Thou shalt be the wife of My Heart". On 5 August of that same year, on the <a href="/wiki/Silver_Jubilee" class="mw-redirect" title="Silver Jubilee">Silver Jubilee</a> of her parents' marriage, Maria told them of her desire to become a religious.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Religious_life">Religious life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Religious life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:162px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto,_Portugal_(1894).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/25/Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281894%29.jpg/160px-Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281894%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="160" height="356" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/25/Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281894%29.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="219" data-file-height="487" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto,_Portugal_(1894).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Sister Mary of the Divine Heart as Mother Superior of the Convent in <a href="/wiki/Porto" title="Porto">Porto</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a> (1896)</div></div></div>
<p>In 1888, she visited with her mother the Hospital of Darfeld and there found a girl who had given scandal. Maria Droste zu Vischering reached out to the unfortunate. That episode can be considered her first contact with the charism of the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_of_Our_Lady_of_Charity_of_the_Good_Shepherd" title="Congregation of Our Lady of Charity of the Good Shepherd">Sisters of the Good Shepherd</a>. In the parish church, a short time later, she heard again the voice of Jesus, who told her: "You must enter in the convent of the Good Shepherd". On 21 November 1888, at the age of 25, she joined that congregation. When she received the <a href="/wiki/Religious_habit" title="Religious habit">religious habit</a>, she received the <a href="/wiki/Religious_name" title="Religious name">religious name</a> <i>Mary of the Divine Heart</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>For Sister Mary, the devotion to the Heart of Christ always merged with the devotion to the Blessed Sacrament: "I could never separate the devotion to the Heart of Jesus from the devotion to the Blessed Sacrament, and I will never be able to explain how and how much the Sacred Heart of Jesus deigned to favor me in the Blessed Sacrament of the Eucharist."
</p><p>In 1891, she devoted herself to the girls sent to the Good Shepherd Sisters in Münster for rehabilitation and care. With an ardent love for youth ministry, she maintained that "the most needy, the most miserable, the most forsaken are the children I love best."<sup id="cite_ref-philippine_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-philippine-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Mary of the Divine Heart spent only five years in Münster because she was called by obedience to a special mission. In 1894, at the age of 31, she was sent to <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a>, where she was initially sent as assistant of the mother superior of the convent in <a href="/wiki/Lisbon" title="Lisbon">Lisbon</a>. From February to May 1894, Sister Mary remained in the Portuguese capital but then was appointed to the office of the <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Special:Search/mother_superior" class="extiw" title="wikt:Special:Search/mother superior">Mother Superior</a> of the convent in <a href="/wiki/Porto" title="Porto">Porto</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-philippine_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-philippine-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In Porto, Sister Mary reported several <a href="/wiki/Visions_of_Jesus_and_Mary" title="Visions of Jesus and Mary">messages from Jesus Christ</a> in which she was asked to contact the pope to request the consecration of the world to the Sacred Heart of Jesus.
</p>

<p>On 10 June 1898 her confessor at the Good Shepherd monastery wrote to <a href="/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIII" title="Pope Leo XIII">Pope Leo XIII</a> to state that Mary of the Divine Heart had received a message from Christ requesting the Pope to consecrate the entire world to the Sacred Heart. The Pope initially did not believe her and took no action. However, on 6 January 1899, she wrote another letter asking that in addition to the consecration, the first Fridays of the month be observed in honour of the Sacred Heart. In the letter, she also referred to the recent illness of the Pope and stated that Christ had assured her that Pope Leo XIII would live until he had performed the consecration to the Sacred Heart. The theologian Laurent Volken states that this had an emotional impact on Leo XIII, despite the theological issues concerning the consecration of non-Christians.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Pope Leo XIII commissioned an inquiry on the basis of her revelation and church tradition. In his 1899 encyclical letter <i><a href="/wiki/Annum_sacrum" title="Annum sacrum">Annum sacrum</a></i>, he decreed that the consecration of the entire human race to the Sacred Heart of Jesus should take place on 11 June 1899. In the encyclical, he referred to the illness about which Vischering had written:
"There is one further reason that urges us to realize our design: We do not want it to pass by unnoticed. It is personal in nature but just as important: God the author of all Good has saved us by healing us recently from a dangerous disease."<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Pope Leo XIII also composed the <a href="/wiki/Prayer_of_Consecration_to_the_Sacred_Heart" class="mw-redirect" title="Prayer of Consecration to the Sacred Heart">Prayer of Consecration to the Sacred Heart</a> and included it in the encyclical. <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_X" title="Pope Pius X">Pope Pius X</a> later decreed for the consecration of the human race, performed by Pope Leo XIII, to be renewed each year.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death_and_incorruptibility">Death and incorruptibility</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Death and incorruptibility">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:252px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto,_Portugal_(1899).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d2/Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281899%29.jpg/250px-Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281899%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="250" height="158" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d2/Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281899%29.jpg/375px-Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281899%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d2/Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto%2C_Portugal_%281899%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="450" data-file-height="285" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Maria_Droste_zu_Vischering_-_Porto,_Portugal_(1899).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Death of Sister Mary of the Divine Heart in the Convent of the Good Shepherd in <a href="/wiki/Porto" title="Porto">Porto</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a> (1899)</div></div></div>
<p>Sister Mary of the Divine Heart died on Thursday 8 June 1899, the eve of the <a href="/wiki/Feast_of_the_Sacred_Heart" title="Feast of the Sacred Heart">Solemnity of the Most Sacred Heart of Jesus</a>), three days before the world consecration that Pope Leo XIII had scheduled for the following Sunday.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Mary of the Divine Heart's <a href="/wiki/Incorruptibility" title="Incorruptibility">incorrupt body</a> is exposed for public veneration in the <a href="/wiki/Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_(Ermesinde)" title="Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus (Ermesinde)">Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Ermesinde" title="Ermesinde">Ermesinde</a>, in northern Portugal.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> The church is adjacent to the Convent of the Good Shepherd Sisters.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> There is also a <a href="/wiki/Relic" title="Relic">relic</a> of her body exposed for public veneration at the <a href="/wiki/Christ_the_King_(Almada)" title="Christ the King (Almada)">Sanctuary of Christ the King</a> in <a href="/wiki/Almada" title="Almada">Almada</a>, near Lisbon, Portugal.
</p>

<p>In 1964, Mary of the Divine Heart was declared <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">venerable</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>. On 1 November 1975 she was  <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI" title="Pope Paul VI">Pope Paul VI</a>. Waldery Hilgeman is the postulator of the cause of <a href="/wiki/Canonisation" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonisation">canonisation</a>.
</p>

<p>In his revelations to Mary of the Divine Heart, Jesus is said to have revealed to her two promises:
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Promise_of_obtaining_graces_through_the_intercession_of_Sister_Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart">Promise of obtaining graces through the intercession of Sister Mary of the Divine Heart</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Promise of obtaining graces through the intercession of Sister Mary of the Divine Heart">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>"Know this, My daughter, that by the charity of My Heart I desire to pour out floods of graces through your heart over the hearts of others. This is why people will get close to you with confidence. … No one, even the most hardened sinner, will leave your presence without having received, in one way or another, consolation, relief, or a special grace."<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Promise_of_obtaining_graces_in_the_Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_in_Ermesinde">Promise of obtaining graces in the Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus in Ermesinde</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mary_of_the_Divine_Heart&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9" title="Edit section: Promise of obtaining graces in the Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus in Ermesinde">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:247px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e0/Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg/245px-Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg" decoding="async" width="245" height="223" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e0/Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg/368px-Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e0/Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg/490px-Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="455" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_Ermesinde_Portugal.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Church_of_the_Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus_(Ermesinde)" title="Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus (Ermesinde)">Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus</a> in <a href="/wiki/Ermesinde" title="Ermesinde">Ermesinde</a>, <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a></div></div></div>
<p>"For a long time, as you know, I have wanted to build a church on the Good Shepherd property. Uncertain as to whom this church should be dedicated, I have prayed and consulted many people without reaching a decision. On the First Friday of this month, I asked Our Lord to enlighten me. After the Holy Communion, He said to me: 'I desire that the church be consecrated to My Heart. You must erect here a place of reparation; from My part I will make it a place of graces. I will distribute copiously graces to all who live in this house [the Convent], those who live here now, those who will live here after, and even to the people of their relations'. Then He told me that He wished this church, above all, to be a place of reparation for sacrileges and to obtain graces for the clergy."<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
