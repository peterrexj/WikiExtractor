<p><b>Saint Castulus</b> (died 286) is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>.  According to tradition, he was the <a href="/wiki/Chamberlain_(office)" title="Chamberlain (office)">chamberlain</a> (or officer, valet) of Emperor <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a> and the husband of <a href="/wiki/Irene_of_Rome" title="Irene of Rome">Saint Irene of Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-heiligenlexikon.de_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-heiligenlexikon.de-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Castulus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A convert to the <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> religion, he sheltered Christians in his home and arranged for religious services inside the palace of the emperor.  Among those he sheltered were <a href="/wiki/Mark_and_Marcellian" title="Mark and Marcellian">Mark and Marcellian</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  He is one of the saints associated with the life and legend of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Sebastian" title="Saint Sebastian">Saint Sebastian</a>.
</p><p>With his friend <a href="/wiki/Saint_Tiburtius" title="Saint Tiburtius">Saint Tiburtius</a>, he converted many men and woman to Christianity and brought them to <a href="/wiki/Pope_Saint_Caius" class="mw-redirect" title="Pope Saint Caius">Pope Saint Caius</a> to be <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptized</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-ACI_Prensa_-_Santos_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ACI_Prensa_-_Santos-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>  He was betrayed by an <a href="/wiki/Apostate" class="mw-redirect" title="Apostate">apostate</a> named Torquatus and taken before Fabian, <a href="/wiki/Prefect#Ancient_Rome" title="Prefect">prefect</a> of the city.<sup id="cite_ref-ACI_Prensa_-_Santos_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ACI_Prensa_-_Santos-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>     
</p><p>Castulus was tortured and executed by being <a href="/wiki/Buried_alive" class="mw-redirect" title="Buried alive">buried alive</a> in a <a href="/wiki/Sand_pit" class="mw-redirect" title="Sand pit">sand pit</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Via_Labicana" title="Via Labicana">Via Labicana</a>.  According to tradition, <a href="/wiki/Irene_of_Rome" title="Irene of Rome">Irene</a> subsequently buried the body of the martyred <a href="/wiki/Saint_Sebastian" title="Saint Sebastian">Saint Sebastian</a>.  She was later martyred herself, around 288 AD.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration_of_St._Castulus">Veneration of St. Castulus</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Castulus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration of St. Castulus">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A church dedicated to him at <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, built on the site of his martyrdom, existed from at least the seventh century.
</p><p>Castulus was venerated in <a href="/wiki/Bavaria" title="Bavaria">Bavaria</a> after <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> of his were taken to <a href="/wiki/Moosburg" class="mw-redirect" title="Moosburg">Moosburg</a>.  Duke <a href="/wiki/Heinrich_der_L%C3%B6we" class="mw-redirect" title="Heinrich der Löwe">Heinrich der Löwe</a> started the construction of the Kastulus <a href="/wiki/Minster_(cathedral)" class="mw-redirect" title="Minster (cathedral)">Minster</a> in 1171.
</p><p>In 1604, some relics were also brought to <a href="/wiki/Landshut" title="Landshut">Landshut</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>  His relics still rest in Landshut's <a href="/wiki/St._Martin%27s_Church_(Landshut)" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Martin&#39;s Church (Landshut)">church of St. Martin's</a> and Kostel Sv. Haštala (Church of St.Castulus), Prague.
</p>
