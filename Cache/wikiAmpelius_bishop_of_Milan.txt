<p><b>Ampelius</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Ampelius</i>, <a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">Ampelio</i>) was <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Milan" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Milan">Archbishop of Milan</a> from 671 to 676. He is honoured as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Cazzani_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cazzani-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ampelius_(bishop_of_Milan)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Almost nothing is known about the life and the <a href="/wiki/Episcopate" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopate">episcopate</a> of Ampelius.  He ruled the diocese of Milan in a period marked by the troubles due to the <a href="/wiki/Lombards" title="Lombards">Lombards</a>. Ampelius is remembered as man able to do miracles, but we have no detail about his acts.<sup id="cite_ref-DCA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DCA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Goffredo da Bussero in the 15th century informs us that his feast day was the 8 February, possibly the date of his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Cazzani_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cazzani-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His feast day is now celebrated on July 7 in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Rite" title="Roman Rite">Roman Rite</a> and on July 8 in the <a href="/wiki/Ambrosian_Rite" title="Ambrosian Rite">Ambrosian Rite</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-MA_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MA-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> 
He was buried in the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_San_Simpliciano" title="Basilica of San Simpliciano">Basilica of St. Simplician</a> where his relics are still venerated under the main altar.<sup id="cite_ref-DCA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DCA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
