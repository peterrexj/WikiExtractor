<p><b>Pope Cyril V of Alexandria (Abba Kyrillos V)</b>, <a href="/wiki/List_of_Coptic_Orthodox_Popes_of_Alexandria" class="mw-redirect" title="List of Coptic Orthodox Popes of Alexandria">112th Pope of Alexandria &amp; Patriarch of the See of St. Mark</a> for 52 years, 9 months and 6 days. He was the longest-serving Pope in the history of the <a href="/wiki/Coptic_Orthodox_Church" title="Coptic Orthodox Church">Coptic Orthodox Church</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was born as Youhanna (John) in 1824 or 1830/1831 according to different accounts and he died on 7 August 1927.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="A_monk">A monk</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pope_Cyril_V_of_Alexandria&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: A monk">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He joined the Al Baramous Monastery in the <a href="/wiki/Nitrian_Desert" title="Nitrian Desert">Nitrian Desert</a>, where he served as abbot prior to his elevation to Pope.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Coptic_Pope">The Coptic Pope</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pope_Cyril_V_of_Alexandria&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: The Coptic Pope">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/General_Congregation_Council" title="General Congregation Council">General Congregation Council</a> (Elmagles Elmelly Ela'am) elected him Pope, with seat in the <a href="/wiki/Saint_Mark%27s_Coptic_Orthodox_Cathedral_(Azbakeya)" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Mark&#39;s Coptic Orthodox Cathedral (Azbakeya)">Saint Mark's Coptic Orthodox Cathedral</a> in Azbakeya in <a href="/wiki/Cairo" title="Cairo">Cairo</a> throughout his papacy. The secretary of the council was <a href="/wiki/Boutros_Ghali" title="Boutros Ghali">Boutros pasha Ghali</a> بطرس غالي, later Prime Minister of Egypt. Cyril spent most of his papacy at loggerheads with the council and objecting to its interference in church matters.<sup id="cite_ref-Alahram_weekley_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Alahram_weekley-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> At the beginning of his papacy there was a dispute with the council, which Cyril won.
</p><p>In general, his papacy was an era of regeneration for the Coptic Orthodox Church and he continued the work begun by Pope <a href="/wiki/Pope_Cyril_IV_of_Alexandria" title="Pope Cyril IV of Alexandria">Cyril IV</a> (1854–1861) in educational reform.<sup id="cite_ref-Alahram_weekley_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Alahram_weekley-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Notable men of the Coptic Church during his papacy included saint <a href="/wiki/Abraam_Bishop_of_Fayoum" class="mw-redirect" title="Abraam Bishop of Fayoum">Anba Abraam, Bishop of Fayoum</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Habib_Girgis" title="Habib Girgis">Habib Girgis</a>.
</p><p>In 1881 the <a href="/wiki/Ethiopia" title="Ethiopia">Ethiopian</a> Emperor <a href="/wiki/Yohannes_IV_of_Ethiopia" class="mw-redirect" title="Yohannes IV of Ethiopia">Yohannes IV</a> asked Pope Cyril V to ordain a metropolitan and three Bishops for the Ethiopian Empire. Cyril chose the four monks who had left El-Muharraq Monastery with Anba Abraam: Abouna Petros, Abouna Marqos, Abouna Matewos and Abouna Luqas.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When news of his death reached Ethiopia, Empress <a href="/wiki/Zewditu" title="Zewditu">Zewditu</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ras_(title)" title="Ras (title)">Ras</a> <a href="/wiki/Haile_Selassie_of_Ethiopia" class="mw-redirect" title="Haile Selassie of Ethiopia">Tafari</a> ordered requiem masses to be said throughout Ethiopia, and that government offices be closed for three days.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
