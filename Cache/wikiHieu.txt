<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Hieu</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Abbess</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 7th century</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">12 March, c. 7th century<br /><a href="/wiki/Healaugh,_Tadcaster" class="mw-redirect" title="Healaugh, Tadcaster">Healaugh</a>, <a href="/wiki/Yorkshire" title="Yorkshire">Yorkshire</a>, <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">2 September</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Hieu</b> was a 7th-century <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Irish</a> <a href="/wiki/Abbess" title="Abbess">abbess</a> who worked in <a href="/wiki/Northumbria" title="Northumbria">Northumbria</a>. She was foundress of <a href="/wiki/Abbey" title="Abbey">abbeys</a> at <a href="/wiki/Hartlepool" title="Hartlepool">Hartlepool</a> and <a href="/wiki/Healaugh,_Tadcaster" class="mw-redirect" title="Healaugh, Tadcaster">Healaugh</a> in <a href="/wiki/Yorkshire" title="Yorkshire">Yorkshire</a> <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a>. Hieu was also the first of the saintly <a href="/wiki/Recluse" title="Recluse">recluses</a> of Northumbria,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and the first known woman to rule a <a href="/wiki/Double_monastery" title="Double monastery">double monastery</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Hieu&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Nothing is known of her early life, until she met <a href="/wiki/Aidan_of_Lindisfarne" title="Aidan of Lindisfarne">Aidan of Lindisfarne</a> who appointed her <a href="/wiki/Abbess" title="Abbess">abbess</a> of <a href="/wiki/Hartlepool_Abbey" title="Hartlepool Abbey">Hartlepool Abbey</a> and subsequently a <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> at Healaugh near <a href="/wiki/Tadcaster" title="Tadcaster">Tadcaster</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>She died at Healaugh on 12 March of an unknown year in the 7th century.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> It is possible that the towns of Hartlepool (Hereteu) and Healaugh are named after her.
</p><p>Hieu's <a href="/wiki/Memorial_(liturgy)" title="Memorial (liturgy)">memorial</a> is kept on September 2.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
