<p><b>Abundius and Abundantius</b> (died c. 304) are <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyrs</a> who were killed during the <a href="/wiki/Diocletian_persecution" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocletian persecution">Diocletian persecution</a>. Their <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated on September 16.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Abundius_and_Abundantius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Abundius was a <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> who had earlier <a href="/wiki/Resurrect" class="mw-redirect" title="Resurrect">resurrected</a> <a href="/wiki/John_and_Marcianus" title="John and Marcianus"><b>John</b>, the son of <b>Marcianus</b></a>. Upon hearing of this, the emperor ordered these three, with Abundantius, a deacon, to be <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">beheaded</a>. The executions were carried out at the 26th <a href="/wiki/Milestone" title="Milestone">milestone</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Via_Flaminia" title="Via Flaminia">Via Flaminia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Abundius_and_Abundantius&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Their bodies were later transferred to the church of <a href="/wiki/Cosmas_and_Damian" class="mw-redirect" title="Cosmas and Damian">Cosmas and Damian</a> in Rome. The bodies of <a href="/wiki/John_and_Marcianus" title="John and Marcianus">Marcianus and John</a> were found around 1001 and transferred to <a href="/wiki/Civita_Castellana" title="Civita Castellana">Civita Castellana</a>. There, they were elected to be the city's principal <a href="/wiki/Patron_saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Patron saints">patron saints</a>. In 1583, the relics of Abundius and Abundantius were transferred to the SS. Nome di Gesu, where they were placed under the church's <a href="/wiki/Altar" title="Altar">high altar</a>. <a href="/wiki/Aloysius_Gonzaga" title="Aloysius Gonzaga">Aloysius Gonzaga</a> heard <a href="/wiki/Mass_(liturgy)" title="Mass (liturgy)">mass</a> there before becoming a <a href="/wiki/Jesuit" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesuit">Jesuit</a>.
</p>
