					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">Not to be confused with <a href="/wiki/Lantfred" title="Lantfred">Lantfred</a>.</div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Frankish_empire.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/00/Frankish_empire.jpg/220px-Frankish_empire.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="156" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/00/Frankish_empire.jpg/330px-Frankish_empire.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/00/Frankish_empire.jpg/440px-Frankish_empire.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1755" data-file-height="1241" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Frankish_empire.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Expansion of Frankish rule (Alamannia rendered as Swabia)</div></div></div>
<p><b>Lantfrid</b> (also <i>Landfrid</i> or <i>Lanfred</i>, <a href="/wiki/Latinisation_of_names" title="Latinisation of names">Latinised</a> <i>Lantfridus</i> or <i>Lanfredus</i>) (died 730) was duke of <a href="/wiki/Alamannia" title="Alamannia">Alamannia</a> under <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> sovereignty  from 709 until his death. He was the son of duke <a href="/wiki/Gotfrid" title="Gotfrid">Gotfrid</a>. Lantfrid's brother was <a href="/wiki/Theudebald_(Alamannic_ruler)" class="mw-redirect" title="Theudebald (Alamannic ruler)">Theudebald</a>.
</p><p>Following Gotfrid's death in 709 and the accession of Lantfrid and his brother Theudebald, the <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> <i><a href="/wiki/Mayor_of_the_Palace" class="mw-redirect" title="Mayor of the Palace">maior domus</a></i> <a href="/wiki/Pepin_of_Herstal" title="Pepin of Herstal">Pepin of Herstal</a> invaded Alamannia and fought against yet another duke, <a href="/wiki/Willehari" title="Willehari">Willehari</a>, whose territory was restricted to the <a href="/wiki/Ortenau" title="Ortenau">Ortenau</a> in western Alamannia.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> This campaign can be seen as an attempt by Pepin to impose royal authority on the duchy following the death of Gotfrid and also to assert his right to influence or even control the succession within the duchy. Pepin’s campaign against Willehari might therefore have taken place to assist Lantfrid and Theudebald in their claim to the duchy.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> However, both Lantfrid and Theudebald were hostile to Pepin’s successor.
</p><p>After Pipin’s death in 714, Lantfrid dissolved all links with the royal court and its new <i>maior domus</i> Charles Martell.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Alamannic resistance against Frankish supremacy was tenacious. In 722 <a href="/wiki/Charles_Martel" title="Charles Martel">Charles Martel</a> forcefully subdued the duchies of Alamannia and <a href="/wiki/History_of_Bavaria" title="History of Bavaria">Bavaria</a> but in the following year both duchies again rose against <a href="/wiki/Carolingian" class="mw-redirect" title="Carolingian">Carolingian</a> supremacy.<sup id="cite_ref-R._Schieffer,_p._42_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-R._Schieffer,_p._42-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 724 <a href="/wiki/Saint_Pirmin" title="Saint Pirmin">Pirmin</a> founded the <a href="/wiki/Reichenau_Abbey" title="Reichenau Abbey">Reichenau Abbey</a> under the protection of Charles Martel, Lantfrid and Theudebald considered this a provocation and in 727 Theudebald  <i>ob odium Karoli</i> ("out of hatred against Charles") evicted first Pirmin and, in 732, his successor <a href="/w/index.php?title=Heddo&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Heddo (page does not exist)">Heddo</a> from Reichenau. The <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_St._Gall" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of St. Gall">Abbey of St. Gall</a>, however, founded 719 by the Alamannic monk <a href="/wiki/Otmar" class="mw-redirect" title="Otmar">Otmar</a>, was favoured by the dukes of Alamannia and the regional aristocracy.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Between 724 and 730 Lantfrid usurped the right to issue a law-code whereas up until then law-giving had been one of the major activities of the Merovingian kings.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> He instigated a revision of the Laws of the Alamans (<i><a href="/wiki/Lex_Alamannorum" title="Lex Alamannorum">Lex Alamannorum Lantfridus</a></i>),<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> a sign and an expression of his claim to be an independent ruler. Even though in the law-code the king is mentioned as <i>dominus</i>, there is no reference to the role of the <i>maior domus</i>, which is an indication of Lantfrid’s loyalty towards the Merovingian dynasty.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 730 Charles Martell invaded the duchy with an army once again. Lantfrid's death is recorded for the same year. Whether this occurred as a result of the fighting is not clear.<sup id="cite_ref-R._Schieffer,_p._42_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-R._Schieffer,_p._42-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His brother Theudebald succeeded to the duchy as sole ruler.
</p>
