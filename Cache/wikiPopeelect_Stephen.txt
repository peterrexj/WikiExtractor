<p><b>Pope-elect Stephen</b> (died 25 March 752) was a Roman priest <a href="/wiki/Papal_selection_before_1059" title="Papal selection before 1059">selected</a> in March 752 to succeed <a href="/wiki/Pope_Zachary" title="Pope Zachary">Pope Zachary</a>. Because he died before he was <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_consecration" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopal consecration">consecrated</a>, he is considered only a <span class="nowrap">pope<a href="/wiki/-elect" title="-elect">-elect</a></span> rather than a legitimate <a href="/wiki/Pope" title="Pope">pope</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Election_to_the_Papacy">Election to the Papacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pope-elect_Stephen&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Election to the Papacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 745, Stephen was made a <a href="/wiki/Cardinal-priest" class="mw-redirect" title="Cardinal-priest">cardinal-priest</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Zachary" title="Pope Zachary">Pope Zachary</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Titular_church" title="Titular church">titular church</a> was <a href="/wiki/San_Crisogono" class="mw-redirect" title="San Crisogono">San Crisogono</a>. Zachary died in mid-March 752. On 23 March, Stephen was <a href="/wiki/Papal_selection_before_1059" title="Papal selection before 1059">selected</a> to become the new <a href="/wiki/Pope" title="Pope">pope</a>. He died of a <a href="/wiki/Stroke" title="Stroke">stroke</a> only days later, before being <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_consecration" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopal consecration">consecrated</a> as <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Rome" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Rome">bishop of Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-CE_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CE-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>According to the <a href="/wiki/Canon_law_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Canon law (Catholic Church)">canon law</a> of the time, a pope's <a href="/wiki/Pontificate" title="Pontificate">pontificate</a> started upon his consecration.<sup id="cite_ref-AP2012_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-AP2012-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Later canon law considered that a man became pope the moment he accepted his election, and Pope-elect Stephen was then anachronistically called Pope Stephen II.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His name was removed from the list of popes in the <i><a href="/wiki/Annuario_Pontificio" title="Annuario Pontificio">Annuario Pontificio</a></i> in 1961.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
