<p>Prince <b>Jan Franciszek Czartoryski</b> or <b>Blessed Michał</b> (19 February 1897&#8211;6 September 1944) was a Polish <a href="/wiki/Szlachcic" class="mw-redirect" title="Szlachcic">noble</a>, and a <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Order" title="Dominican Order">Dominican</a> friar.
</p><p>John Czartoryski was born in Pelkinie (Jaroslaw) on 19 February 1897.<sup id="cite_ref-santi_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was an activist of the young organisation "Odrodzenie" ("Rebirth") in <a href="/wiki/Lw%C3%B3w" class="mw-redirect" title="Lwów">Lwów</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/Warsaw_Uprising" title="Warsaw Uprising">Warsaw Uprising</a> he was the chaplain of the <a href="/wiki/Armia_Krajowa" class="mw-redirect" title="Armia Krajowa">Armia Krajowa</a> Group "Konrad", and was shot by <a href="/wiki/Germany" title="Germany">German</a> troops.
</p><p>Czartoryski was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on 13 June 1998 among the <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Polish Martyrs of World War II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-santi_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Feast Day is 6 September.
</p>
