<p>The feminine name <b>Jutta</b> (pronounced "yutta") is the German form of <a href="/wiki/Judith_(name)" class="mw-redirect" title="Judith (name)">Judith</a>. There is also an alternative theory that it could be derived from the Germanic name <i>Eutha</i>, meaning "mankind, child, descendant", or from a short form of <i>Henrietta</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
