					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Irish monk</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For other Irish saints of this name, see <a href="/wiki/Cumm%C3%A9ne" title="Cumméne">Cumméne</a>.</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg/220px-Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="150" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg/330px-Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/86/Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg/440px-Bobbio-Abtei-Langobard.Grabplatte-jpg.jpg 2x" data-file-width="800" data-file-height="546" /></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Cumianus</b> (<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 641 – <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 736) was an <a href="/wiki/Irish_people" title="Irish people">Irish</a> monk who became abbot of <a href="/wiki/Bobbio_Abbey" title="Bobbio Abbey">San Colombano di Bobbio</a> around 715. He left Ireland as an old man. The intricately carved lid of his sarcophagus, containing a lengthy epitaph, was made by one Master John and commissioned by King <a href="/wiki/Liutprand,_King_of_the_Lombards" title="Liutprand, King of the Lombards">Liutprand, King of the Lombards</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The inscription on the tomb reads as follows; it is written in <a href="/wiki/Latin_rhythmic_hexameter" title="Latin rhythmic hexameter">rhythmic hexameters</a>, a kind of hexameter in which word accent is taken into account rather than syllable length:<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<dl><dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">Hic sacra beati membra Cumiani solvuntur,</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">cuius coelum penetrans anima cum angelis gaudet.</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">iste fuit magnus dignitate, genere, forma.</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">hunc misit Scotia fines ad Italicos senem:</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">locatus Ebobio Domini constrictus amore,</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">ubi venerandi dogma Columbani servando</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">vigilans, jejunans, indefessus, sedulo orans</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">Olympiades quatuor uniusque curriculo anni</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">sic vixit feliciter, ut felix modo credatur,</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">mitis, prudens, pius, fratribus pacificus cunctis.</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">huic aetatis anni fuerunt nonies deni,</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">lustrum quoque unum mense(n)sque quatuor simul.</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">at pater egregie potens intercessor existe</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">pro gloriosissimo Luitprando rege, qui tuum</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">pretioso lapide tympum decoravit devotus,</i></span></dd>
<dd><span title="Latin-language text"><i lang="la">sit ut manifestum, almum ubi tegitur corpus.</i></span></dd></dl>
<dl><dd>"Here lie the sacred limbs of Cumian;</dd>
<dd>whose soul, entering heaven, rejoices with the angels.</dd>
<dd>He was great in dignity, nobility, and beauty.</dd>
<dd>Ireland sent him as an old man to the lands of Italy:</dd>
<dd>located in Bobbio, constrained by love of the Lord,</dd>
<dd>where, by preserving the teaching of the venerable <a href="/wiki/Columbanus" title="Columbanus">Columban</a>,</dd>
<dd>keeping watch, fasting, tireless, constantly praying,</dd>
<dd>for four <a href="/wiki/Olympiads" class="mw-redirect" title="Olympiads">olympiads</a> and the course of one year</dd>
<dd>he lived so felicitously that he is believed to be only fortunate,</dd>
<dd>gentle, wise, pious, peaceful to all brothers.</dd>
<dd>He lived for 90 years</dd>
<dd>and one period of five years and four months altogether.</dd>
<dd>But, most excellent Father, be a powerful intercessor</dd>
<dd>for the most glorious King Liutprand, who</dd>
<dd>has devotedly decorated your tomb with precious stone,</dd>
<dd>so that it may be manifest where your kindly body is buried."</dd></dl>
