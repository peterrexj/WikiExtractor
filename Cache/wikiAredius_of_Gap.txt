<p><b>Aredius of Gap</b> (Arigius, Arey) (c. 575, <a href="/wiki/Chalon-sur-Sa%C3%B4ne" title="Chalon-sur-Saône">Chalon-sur-Saône</a> – c. 605) was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Gap" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Gap">bishop of Gap</a>.
</p><p>He is a Catholic and Orthodox<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> saint, with feast day May 1.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
