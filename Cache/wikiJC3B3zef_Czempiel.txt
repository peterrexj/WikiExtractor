<p><b>Józef Czempiel</b> (born 21 September 1883 in <a href="/wiki/Piekary_%C5%9Al%C4%85skie" title="Piekary Śląskie">Piekary Śląskie</a> – 4 May 1942) was a Polish Roman Catholic priest, activist. He was murdered in <a href="/wiki/Dachau_concentration_camp" title="Dachau concentration camp">Dachau concentration camp</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 13 June 1999, Czempiel was among <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Polish martyrs of World War II</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> in Warsaw by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a>.
</p>
