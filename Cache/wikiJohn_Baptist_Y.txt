<table class="infobox" style="width: 22em"><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><tbody><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-subheader" style="font-size: 125%; background-color: #b0c4de;">John Baptist Yi Kwang-nyol</td></tr><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label" style="font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Hangul" title="Hangul">Hangul</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><div style="display:inline;font-size:1rem;"><span title="Korean-language text"><span lang="ko-Hang">이광렬 요한</span></span></div></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label" style="font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Revised_Romanization_of_Korean" title="Revised Romanization of Korean">Revised Romanization</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><span title="Revised Romanization of Korean transliteration"><i lang="ko-Latn">I Gwang-ryeol Yohan</i></span></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label" style="font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/McCune%E2%80%93Reischauer" title="McCune–Reischauer">McCune–Reischauer</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><span title="McCune–Reischauer transliteration"><i lang="ko-Latn">Yi Kwangnyŏl Yohan</i></span></td></tr><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/></tbody></table>
<p><b>John Baptist Yi Kwang-nyol</b> (c. 1800 – July 20, 1839) was one of the 103 <a href="/wiki/Korean_Martyrs" title="Korean Martyrs">Korean Martyrs</a>. His feast day is July 20,<sup id="cite_ref-martyrology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-martyrology-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and he is also venerated along with the rest of The <a href="/wiki/Korean_martyrs" class="mw-redirect" title="Korean martyrs">Korean martyrs</a> on September 20.
</p><p>John embraced Roman Catholicism with his elder brother when he was 28 years old. His fervor attracted the attention of the <a href="/wiki/Catechist" class="mw-redirect" title="Catechist">catechists</a>, who sent him to Beijing to negotiate affairs of religion. The priests of the capital, in admiration of his piety, conferred upon him <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptism</a> and the other sacraments. John Baptist, after his return to his country, abstained from eating meat throughout the rest of his life, and made a resolution to live in <a href="/wiki/Celibacy" title="Celibacy">celibacy</a>. He was noted for his holy exterior appearance. He was martyred at the age of 39, severely tortured with torturing instruments and at last beheaded with an axe.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
