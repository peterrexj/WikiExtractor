<p><b>St. Leonides of Alexandria</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: <span title="Ancient Greek (to 1453)-language text"><span lang="grc">Λεωνίδης</span></span>) was a <a href="/wiki/Greeks" title="Greeks">Greek</a> <a href="/wiki/Early_christianity" class="mw-redirect" title="Early christianity">early Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> who lived in the second and early third centuries <a href="/wiki/Anno_Domini" title="Anno Domini">AD</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Leonides_of_Alexandria&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to the Christian historian <a href="/wiki/Eusebius" title="Eusebius">Eusebius</a>, Leonides' son was the early Church father <a href="/wiki/Origen" title="Origen">Origen</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Eusebius also says that he was of Greek nationality.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> In the same passage Eusebius tells us that Leonides was martyred during the persecution of the Roman <a href="/wiki/Emperor" title="Emperor">emperor</a> <a href="/wiki/Septimius_Severus" title="Septimius Severus">Septimius Severus</a> in the year 202 AD.  
</p><p>Condemned to death by the Egyptian prefect <a href="/w/index.php?title=Lactus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Lactus (page does not exist)">Lactus</a>, he was beheaded, and his property seized.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Leonides_of_Alexandria&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name of Leonides' wife is unknown, but she bore at least six children after Origen.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Porphyry_(philosopher)" title="Porphyry (philosopher)">Porphyry</a>, <a href="/wiki/Neoplatonism" title="Neoplatonism">a Neoplatonist</a>, claims Origen's parents were <a href="/wiki/Paganism" title="Paganism">pagans</a>.
</p><p>Leonides catechized his children well. Origen attempted to follow his father in martyrdom, but he was detained by his mother - it is said that she hid his clothes so that Origen could not leave the house.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Feast">Feast</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Leonides_of_Alexandria&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Feast">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Catholic feast of Leonides is celebrated on April 22.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
