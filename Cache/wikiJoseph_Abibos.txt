<p><b>Joseph Abibos</b> is the name of a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> who founded a monastery in <a href="/wiki/Alaverdi,_Armenia" title="Alaverdi, Armenia">Alaverdi</a> and served as <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a>. A native of Syria, he was a disciple of <a href="/wiki/Thirteen_Assyrian_Fathers" title="Thirteen Assyrian Fathers">John of Zedazeni</a> and one of the <a href="/wiki/Thirteen_Assyrian_Fathers" title="Thirteen Assyrian Fathers">Thirteen Assyrian Fathers</a>. He is mentioned in <a href="/wiki/Bessarion" title="Bessarion">Bessarion's</a> <i><a href="/wiki/The_Saints_of_Georgia" title="The Saints of Georgia">The Saints of Georgia</a></i>. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated on September 15.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><br />
</p>
