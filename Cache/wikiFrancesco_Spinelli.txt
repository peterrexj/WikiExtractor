<p><b>Francesco Spinelli</b> (14 April 1853 - 6 February 1913) was an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> and the founder of the Sisters Adorers of the Blessed Sacrament.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Spinelli became close contemporaries of <a href="/wiki/Geltrude_Comensoli" title="Geltrude Comensoli">Geltrude Comensoli</a> and <a href="/wiki/Luigi_Maria_Palazzolo" title="Luigi Maria Palazzolo">Luigi Maria Palazzolo</a> and had a previous collaboration with Comensoli in which the two established a religious institute in <a href="/wiki/Bergamo" title="Bergamo">Bergamo</a> before a rift between members caused Spinelli to distance himself from its work and leave.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> cause opened in 1952 under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XII" title="Pope Pius XII">Pope Pius XII</a> with the beatification being celebrated four decades later on 21 June 1992.<sup id="cite_ref-CO_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> confirmed Spinelli's canonization which took place on 14 October 2018.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francesco_Spinelli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Francesco Spinelli was born in <a href="/wiki/Milan" title="Milan">Milan</a> on 14 April 1853; he was <a href="/wiki/Baptized" class="mw-redirect" title="Baptized">baptized</a> on the following 15 April.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> He - with his parents and siblings - moved from Milan to <a href="/wiki/Cremona" title="Cremona">Cremona</a> when he was still a child and would spend his summers at <a href="/wiki/Vergo" title="Vergo">Vergo</a> where in 1871 he was cured of a severe spinal problem.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> In his childhood he liked to hold puppet shows for fellow children while he visited the poor and ill with his mother on frequent occasions.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His call to the religious life was fostered due to the support of his mother and his <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> uncle Pietro Cagliaroli. His friend <a href="/wiki/Luigi_Maria_Palazzolo" title="Luigi Maria Palazzolo">Luigi Maria Palazzolo</a> also encouraged him towards the path of the priesthood which he studied for in <a href="/wiki/Bergamo" title="Bergamo">Bergamo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> He was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> to the priesthood in Bergamo on 14 August 1875 (which he received from the Bishop Pietro Luigi Speranza) and not long after travelled to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> to take part in the Jubilee that <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_IX" title="Pope Pius IX">Pope Pius IX</a> convoked. He also went to the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_di_Santa_Maria_Maggiore" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore">Basilica di Santa Maria Maggiore</a> where he spent brief moments in reflection at the crib of the <a href="/wiki/Jesus_Christ" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesus Christ">Infant Jesus</a>. This provided him with the inspiration he needed to found a religious congregation of his own after a vision in which he saw women worshiping <a href="/wiki/Jesus" title="Jesus">Jesus</a> in <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_Adoration" class="mw-redirect" title="Eucharistic Adoration">Eucharistic Adoration</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His return from Rome saw him hold educational activities at an evening school at a place his friend Palazzolo established and also assisted his priest uncle Pietro Cagliaroli with his parish activities.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 15 December 1882 he co-founded the Sacramentine Sisters in Bergamo with <a href="/wiki/Geltrude_Comensoli" title="Geltrude Comensoli">Geltrude Comensoli</a> that would devote itself to the <a href="/wiki/Eucharist" title="Eucharist">Eucharist</a> and to <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_Adoration" class="mw-redirect" title="Eucharistic Adoration">Eucharistic Adoration</a>; the first <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a> opened on Via San Antonino. Spinelli was forced to leave it on 4 March 1889 after it failed due to a range of calamities including financial difficulties (the bishop decided to sever Spinelli's connection to the order as a result).<sup id="cite_ref-CO_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-EC_6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> He arrived in <a href="/wiki/Rivolta_d%27Adda" title="Rivolta d&#39;Adda">Rivolta d'Adda</a> in Cremona penniless and pained due to what happened back in Bergamo. It was the diocesan bishop who offered him to come to Cremona to exercise his pastoral duties.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 1892 he founded the Sisters Adorers of the Blessed Sacrament that later received the diocesan approval of the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Cremona" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Cremona">Bishop of Cremona</a> Geremia Bonomelli in 1897.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died in 1913.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The papal decree of praise for his order was issued on 11 December 1926 and later received full pontifical approval on 27 February 1932 from <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a>. The congregation he founded now operates in nations such as <a href="/wiki/Argentina" title="Argentina">Argentina</a> and <a href="/wiki/Senegal" title="Senegal">Senegal</a> and as of 2005 has up to 436 religious in a total of 59 houses.<sup id="cite_ref-SA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 30 August 1958 the Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Patriarch_of_Venice" title="Patriarch of Venice">Patriarch of Venice</a> Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli - the future <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_XXIII" title="Pope John XXIII">Pope John XXIII</a> - visited Spinelli's tomb and wrote in his journal: "Going from <a href="/wiki/Lodi,_Lombardy" title="Lodi, Lombardy">Lodi</a> and arrived in Rivolta d'Adda where I admired the general house of the Sisters Adorers founded by <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> Francesco Spinelli at whose tomb I was glad to pray".<sup id="cite_ref-CO_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Roncalli referred to Spinelli as Venerable despite the fact that the formal recognition had not been conferred upon the late priest at that stage.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Canonization">Canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Francesco_Spinelli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:242px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._(44398703435).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e3/Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._%2844398703435%29.jpg/240px-Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._%2844398703435%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="240" height="160" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e3/Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._%2844398703435%29.jpg/360px-Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._%2844398703435%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e3/Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._%2844398703435%29.jpg/480px-Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._%2844398703435%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="6720" data-file-height="4480" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Ceremonia_de_Canonizaci%C3%B3n_de_Monse%C3%B1or_Romero._(44398703435).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Spinelli's canonization Mass held in 2018 in Saint Peter's Square.</div></div></div>
<p>The beatification process commenced in the Cremona diocese in an informative process (1928-30 with a concurrent process from 1930 to 1931) that had been tasked in documenting his life and the manner in which his life adhered to the <a href="/wiki/Cardinal_virtues" title="Cardinal virtues">cardinal virtues</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Theological_virtues" title="Theological virtues">theological virtues</a>. Theologians also voiced approval for his writings which were found to be in line with the tradition of the faith. These processes occurred despite the fact that the formal introduction of the cause did not come until 25 January 1952 under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XII" title="Pope Pius XII">Pope Pius XII</a> in which he was titled as a <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> as the first official stage in the process. An apostolic process was later held from 1953 until 1957 at which point the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_of_Rites" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation of Rites">Congregation of Rites</a> validated both processes on 22 June 1962.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">Positio</a> was received in Rome in 1998 which enabled the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> to start their own investigation into the cause and the contents of the dossier. Theologians approved the cause on 14 April 1989 as did the C.C.S. members on 9 January 1990. <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> declared him to be Venerable on 3 March 1990 after recognizing his <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a>.
</p><p>Spinelli's beatification depended upon a healing to receive papal confirmation which needed to be deemed miraculous in nature. One such case was investigated in the diocese it originated in before the contents of that investigation were sent to the Congregation for the Causes of Saints for additional assessment. Medical experts confirmed the healing to have no scientific explanation on 9 January 1992 with theologians confirming the healing was a result of Spinelli's intercession; the C.C.S. confirmed the findings of both boards on 7 April 1992. John Paul II later confirmed the investigated healing to be a miracle attributed to him on 2 June 1992. The pontiff beatified Spinelli on 21 June 1992 at a shrine in <a href="/wiki/Caravaggio,_Lombardy" title="Caravaggio, Lombardy">Caravaggio</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>There needed to be one final confirmed miracle in order for him to be canonized as a saint. There came a case in the 2000s from <a href="/wiki/Kinshasa" title="Kinshasa">Kinshasa</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Democratic_Republic_of_Congo" class="mw-redirect" title="Democratic Republic of Congo">Democratic Republic of Congo</a> that was subjected to a diocesan investigation from 10 to 16 August 2014 and was sent to the Congregation for the Causes of Saints for further assessment. Medical experts confirmed this healing on 21 September 2017 as did theologians later on 30 November 2017.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> The C.C.S. later confirmed this miracle on 6 February 2018; <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> later approved it on 6 March and Spinelli was canonized as a saint on 14 October 2018.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The current <a href="/wiki/Postulator" title="Postulator">postulator</a> assigned to the cause is Giovangiuseppe Califano.
</p>
