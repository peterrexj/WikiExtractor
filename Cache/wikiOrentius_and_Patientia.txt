<p><b>Saint Lawrence</b> or <b>Laurence</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Laurentius</i>, <small>lit.</small> "<a href="/wiki/Laurel_wreath" title="Laurel wreath">laurelled</a>"; 31 December AD 225<sup id="cite_ref-Janice_Bennett_2002._Page_61_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Janice_Bennett_2002._Page_61-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> – 10 August 258) was one of the seven deacons of the city of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Sixtus_II" title="Pope Sixtus II">Pope Sixtus II</a> who were <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyred</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Persecution_of_Christians" title="Persecution of Christians">persecution of the Christians</a> that the <a href="/wiki/Valerian_(emperor)" title="Valerian (emperor)">Roman Emperor Valerian</a> ordered in 258.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>St. Lawrence is thought to have been born on 31 December AD 225,<sup id="cite_ref-Janice_Bennett_2002._Page_61_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Janice_Bennett_2002._Page_61-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> in <a href="/wiki/Valencia" title="Valencia">Valencia</a> (or less probably, in <a href="/wiki/Huesca" title="Huesca">Huesca</a>), the town from which his parents came in the later region of <a href="/wiki/Aragon" title="Aragon">Aragon</a> that was then part of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_province" title="Roman province">Roman province</a> of <a href="/wiki/Hispania_Tarraconensis" title="Hispania Tarraconensis">Hispania Tarraconensis</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Janice_Bennett_2002._Pages_15_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Janice_Bennett_2002._Pages_15-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The martyrs Orentius (Modern Spanish: <span title="Spanish-language text"><i lang="es">San Orencio</i></span>) and Patientia (Modern Spanish: <span title="Spanish-language text"><i lang="es">Santa Paciencia</i></span>) are traditionally held to have been his parents.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Sts._Orentius_and_Patientia_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sts._Orentius_and_Patientia-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Lawrence encountered the future <a href="/wiki/Pope_Sixtus_II" title="Pope Sixtus II">Pope Sixtus II</a>, a famous teacher born in Greece, in Caesaraugusta (<a href="/wiki/Zaragoza" title="Zaragoza">Zaragoza</a>), and  they travelled together from  <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a> to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>. When Sixtus became the <a href="/wiki/Pope" title="Pope">Pope</a> in 257, he ordained the young Lawrence who was only 22, as a <a href="/wiki/Deacon" title="Deacon">deacon</a>, and later  and appointed him as "<a href="/wiki/Archdeacon" title="Archdeacon">archdeacon</a> of Rome", the first among the seven deacons who served in the <a href="/wiki/Cathedral_church" class="mw-redirect" title="Cathedral church">cathedral church</a>. This was a position of great trust that included the care of the treasury and riches of the Church and the distribution of alms to the indigent.<sup id="cite_ref-slocc_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-slocc-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:302px;"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7f/St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg/300px-St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg" decoding="async" width="300" height="226" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7f/St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg/450px-St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/7/7f/St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg/600px-St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg 2x" data-file-width="6125" data-file-height="4608" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Lawrence_Distributing_the_Treasures_of_the_Church_-_Bernardo_Strozzi_-_Google_Cultural_Institute.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><i>St. Lawrence Distributing the Treasures of the Church</i> by <a href="/wiki/Bernardo_Strozzi" title="Bernardo Strozzi">Bernardo Strozzi</a></div></div></div>
<p><a href="/wiki/Cyprian" title="Cyprian">St. Cyprian</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">Bishop</a> of <a href="/wiki/Carthage" title="Carthage">Carthage</a>, noted that the at the time, the norm was that  Christians who were denounced were executed, and all their goods confiscated by the Imperial treasury. At the beginning of August 258, the <a href="/wiki/Emperor_Valerian" class="mw-redirect" title="Emperor Valerian">Emperor Valerian</a> issued an edict that all <a href="/wiki/Bishops" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishops">bishops</a>, <a href="/wiki/Priests" class="mw-redirect" title="Priests">priests</a>, and deacons should immediately be put to death. Pope Sixtus II was captured on 6 August 258, at the <a href="/wiki/Catacomb_of_Callixtus" title="Catacomb of Callixtus">cemetery of St. Callixtus</a> while celebrating the <a href="/wiki/Liturgy" title="Liturgy">liturgy</a> and executed immediately.<sup id="cite_ref-moraglia_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-moraglia-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After the death of Sixtus, the <a href="/wiki/Prefect" title="Prefect">prefect</a> of Rome demanded that Lawrence turn over the riches of the Church, and <a href="/wiki/St._Ambrose" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Ambrose">St. Ambrose</a> wrote that Lawrence asked for three days to gather the wealth.<sup id="cite_ref-ReferenceA_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ReferenceA-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>,  distribute as much Church property to the poor, presented himself and the city's poor to the Prefect as the true wealth of the church, and was martyred on the 10th. <sup id="cite_ref-SHMI_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SHMI-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Martyrdom">Martyrdom</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Martyrdom">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_(1614)_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_%281614%29_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg/220px-The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_%281614%29_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="312" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_%281614%29_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg/330px-The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_%281614%29_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c4/The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_%281614%29_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg/440px-The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_%281614%29_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2767" data-file-height="3922" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:The_Martyrdom_of_St._Lawrence_by_Rubens_(1614)_-_Alte_Pinakothek_-_Munich_-_Germany_2017.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The Martyrdom of Saint Lawrence by Rubens (1614).</div></div></div>
<p>As a deacon in Rome, Lawrence was responsible for the material goods of the Church and the distribution of alms to the poor.<sup id="cite_ref-SHMI_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SHMI-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Ambrose of Milan related that when the treasures of the Church were demanded of Lawrence by the prefect of Rome, he brought forward the poor, to whom he had distributed the treasure as alms.<sup id="cite_ref-kirsch_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kirsch-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> "Behold in these poor persons the treasures which I promised to show you; to which I will add pearls and precious stones, those widows and consecrated virgins, which are the Church's crown."<sup id="cite_ref-slocc_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-slocc-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> The prefect was so angry that he had a great gridiron prepared with hot coals beneath it and had Lawrence placed on it, hence Lawrence's association with the gridiron. After the martyr had suffered pain for a long time, the legend concludes, he cheerfully declared: "I'm well done on this side. Turn me over!"<sup id="cite_ref-SHMI_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SHMI-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-foley_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foley-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> From this, St. Lawrence derives his patronage of <a href="/wiki/Cook_(profession)" title="Cook (profession)">cooks</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chef" title="Chef">chefs</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Comedian" title="Comedian">comedians</a>.
</p><p>Lawrence was sentenced at <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Miranda" class="mw-redirect" title="San Lorenzo in Miranda">San Lorenzo in Miranda</a> and imprisoned in San Lorenzo in Fonte, where he baptized fellow prisoners. He was martyred in <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Panisperna" title="San Lorenzo in Panisperna">San Lorenzo in Panisperna</a>, and was buried in <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_fuori_le_Mura" class="mw-redirect" title="San Lorenzo fuori le Mura">San Lorenzo fuori le Mura</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Chronography_of_354" class="mw-redirect" title="Chronography of 354">Almanac of Filocalus</a> for 354 states that he was buried in the <a href="/wiki/Catacomb" class="mw-redirect" title="Catacomb">Catacomb</a> of <a href="/wiki/Cyriaca" title="Cyriaca">Cyriaca</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Via_Tiburtina" title="Via Tiburtina">Via Tiburtina</a><sup id="cite_ref-kirsch_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kirsch-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> by <a href="/wiki/Hippolytus_of_Rome" title="Hippolytus of Rome">Hippolytus</a> and <a href="/wiki/Justin_the_Confessor" title="Justin the Confessor">Justin the Confessor</a>, a presbyter. One of the early sources for his martyrdom was the description of <a href="/wiki/Prudentius" title="Prudentius">Aurelius Prudentius Clemens</a> in his <i>Peristephanon</i>, Hymn 2.
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:352px;"><a href="/wiki/File:San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_(Venice)_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/ff/San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_%28Venice%29_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg/350px-San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_%28Venice%29_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg" decoding="async" width="350" height="199" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/ff/San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_%28Venice%29_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg/525px-San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_%28Venice%29_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/ff/San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_%28Venice%29_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg/700px-San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_%28Venice%29_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3744" data-file-height="2126" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:San_Giacomo_dall%27Orio_(Venice)_-_Martire_di_San_Lorenzo_di_Palma_il_giovane.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/San_Giacomo_dell%27Orio" title="San Giacomo dell&#39;Orio">San Giacomo dall'Orio (Venice)</a> - <i>Martyrdom of San Lorenzo</i>- by <a href="/wiki/Palma_il_giovane" class="mw-redirect" title="Palma il giovane">Palma il giovane</a></div></div></div>
<p>Despite the Church being in possession of the actual gridiron, historian Patrick J. Healy opines that the traditional account of how Lawrence was martyred is "not worthy of credence,"<sup id="cite_ref-Healy_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healy-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources" title="Wikipedia:Citing sources"><span title="This citation requires a reference to the specific page or range of pages in which the material appears. (August 2020)">page&#160;needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> as the slow, lingering death cannot be reconciled "with the express command contained in the edict regarding bishops, priests, and deacons (<i>animadvertantur</i>) which ordinarily meant decapitation."<sup id="cite_ref-Healy_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healy-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources" title="Wikipedia:Citing sources"><span title="This citation requires a reference to the specific page or range of pages in which the material appears. (August 2020)">page&#160;needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> A theory of how the tradition arose is proposed that as the result of a mistake in transcription, the omission of the letter "p"&#160;– "by which the customary and solemn formula for announcing the death of a martyr&#160;– <i>passus est</i> ["he suffered," that is, was martyred]&#160;– was made to read <i>assus est</i> [he was roasted]."<sup id="cite_ref-Healy_11-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healy-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources" title="Wikipedia:Citing sources"><span title="This citation requires a reference to the specific page or range of pages in which the material appears. (August 2020)">page&#160;needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> The <i>Liber Pontificalis</i>, which is held to draw from sources independent of the existing traditions and <i>Acta</i> regarding Lawrence, uses <i>passus est</i> concerning him, the same term it uses for Pope Sixtus II, who was martyred by decapitation during the same persecution 4 days earlier.<sup id="cite_ref-Healy_11-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Healy-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources" title="Wikipedia:Citing sources"><span title="This citation requires a reference to the specific page or range of pages in which the material appears. (August 2020)">page&#160;needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Constantine_I_(emperor)" class="mw-redirect" title="Constantine I (emperor)">Emperor Constantine I</a> is held to have erected a small oratory in honour of Lawrence, which was a station on the itineraries of the graves of the Roman martyrs by the seventh century. <a href="/wiki/Pope_Damasus_I" title="Pope Damasus I">Pope Damasus I</a> rebuilt or repaired the church, now the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_di_San_Lorenzo_fuori_le_Mura" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura">Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura</a>, while the Minor Basilica of <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Panisperna" title="San Lorenzo in Panisperna">San Lorenzo in Panisperna</a> was erected over the site of his martyrdom. The gridiron of the martyrdom was placed by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Paschal_II" title="Pope Paschal II">Pope Paschal II</a> in the Minor Basilica of <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Lucina" title="San Lorenzo in Lucina">San Lorenzo in Lucina</a>.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2020)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Associated_Roman_churches">Associated Roman churches</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Associated Roman churches">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Stephen_the_Martyr_(Omaha),_chapel_window_4,_St_Lawrence.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/36/St._Stephen_the_Martyr_%28Omaha%29%2C_chapel_window_4%2C_St_Lawrence.jpg/220px-St._Stephen_the_Martyr_%28Omaha%29%2C_chapel_window_4%2C_St_Lawrence.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="474" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/36/St._Stephen_the_Martyr_%28Omaha%29%2C_chapel_window_4%2C_St_Lawrence.jpg/330px-St._Stephen_the_Martyr_%28Omaha%29%2C_chapel_window_4%2C_St_Lawrence.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/36/St._Stephen_the_Martyr_%28Omaha%29%2C_chapel_window_4%2C_St_Lawrence.jpg/440px-St._Stephen_the_Martyr_%28Omaha%29%2C_chapel_window_4%2C_St_Lawrence.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1953" data-file-height="4206" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Stephen_the_Martyr_(Omaha),_chapel_window_4,_St_Lawrence.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>St. Lawrence in stained glass window by <a href="/wiki/Franz_Mayer_%26_Co." class="mw-redirect" title="Franz Mayer &amp; Co.">Franz Mayer &amp; Co.</a>  He is holding a palm branch, a symbol of martyrdom, and a griddle, the instrument of his death.</div></div></div>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> erected six churches on the sites in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> traditionally associated with his martyrdom:
</p>
<ul><li>Minor Basilica of St Lawrence in Damaso (<a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Damaso" title="San Lorenzo in Damaso">Basilica Minore di San Lorenzo in Damaso</a>): site where he performed his duties as <a href="/wiki/Deacon" title="Deacon">deacon</a> of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>;</li>
<li>Minor Basilica of St Mary in Domnica alla Navicella (<a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_in_Domnica" title="Santa Maria in Domnica">Basilica Minore di Santa Maria in Domnica alla Navicella</a>): site where he customarily distributed alms to the indigent;</li>
<li>Annexed Church of St Lawrence in Miranda (<a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Miranda" class="mw-redirect" title="San Lorenzo in Miranda">Chiesa Annessa San Lorenzo de’ Speziali in Miranda</a>): site of his sentencing and condemnation by the Prefect of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>;</li>
<li>Annexed Church of St Lawrence in Fonte (Chiesa Annessa San Lorenzo in Fonte): site of his imprisonment by the centurion Ippolito and of the fountain in which the Saint <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptized</a> his fellow prisoners;</li>
<li>Church of St Lawrence in Panisperna (<a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Panisperna" title="San Lorenzo in Panisperna">Chiesa di San Lorenzo in Panisperna</a>): site of his actual martyrdom/death and the oven used to roast him to death; and</li>
<li>Papal Minor Basilica of St Lawrence outside the Walls (<a href="/wiki/Basilica_di_San_Lorenzo_fuori_le_Mura" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura">Basilica Minore Papale di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura</a>: site of his burial and sepulchre.</li></ul>
<p>Also in Rome are three other significant churches that are dedicated to Saint Lawrence but not associated with his life:
</p>
<ul><li>Minor Basilica of St Lawrence in Lucina (<a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Lucina" title="San Lorenzo in Lucina">Basilica Minore di San Lorenzo in Lucina</a>), which possesses the relics of the gridiron on which and the chains with which he was martyred;<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>Church of St. Lawrence in Palatio ad Sancta Sanctorum, Pontifical Sanctuary of the Holy Stairs (Chiesa di San Lorenzo in Palatio ad Sancta Sanctorum, <a href="/wiki/Sancta_Sanctorum_(Lateran,_Rome)" title="Sancta Sanctorum (Lateran, Rome)">Pontificio Santuario della Scala Santa</a>), proximate to the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_St._John_Lateran" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica of St. John Lateran">Archbasilica of St. John in Laterano</a>, which was originally a private Papal chapel when the edifice that houses it was a Papal palace, and which housed some of the most precious relics of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>, hence the title "Sancta Sanctorum" ("Holy of Holies"); and</li>
<li>Church of St Lawrence in Piscibus (Chiesa di San Lorenzo in Piscibus), which is proximate to the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_St._Peter" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica of St. Peter">Basilica of St. Peter</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Miracles">Miracles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Miracles">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The life and miracles of Lawrence were collected in <i>The Acts of St Lawrence</i> but those writings have been lost. The earliest existing documentation of miracles associated with him is in the writings of <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a> (538–594), who mentions the following:
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>A priest named Fr. Sanctulus was rebuilding a church of St. Lawrence, which had been attacked and burnt, and hired many workmen to accomplish the job. At one point during the construction, he found himself with nothing to feed them. He prayed to St. Lawrence for help, and looking in his basket he found a fresh, white loaf of bread. It seemed to him too small to feed the workmen, but in faith he began to serve it to the men. While he broke the bread, it so multiplied that his workmen fed from it for ten days.<sup id="cite_ref-slocc_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-slocc-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template noprint noexcerpt Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:NOTRS" class="mw-redirect" title="Wikipedia:NOTRS"><span title="This claim needs references to better sources. (April 2018)">better&#160;source&#160;needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup></p></blockquote>
<p>The mediaeval Church of <a href="/wiki/Assumption_of_Mary" title="Assumption of Mary">St Mary Assumed</a> (Chiesa di Santa Maria Assunta) in the small commune of <a href="/wiki/Amaseno" title="Amaseno">Amaseno</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lazio" title="Lazio">Lazio</a>, Italy houses the famous reliquary of the <i><a href="/wiki/Ampulla" title="Ampulla">ampulla</a></i> containing relics of Lawrence, namely a quantum of his blood, a fragment of his flesh, some fat and ashes. Tradition holds that annually, on the Feast of St. Lawrence, and sometimes on other occasions, the blood in the ampulla miraculously liquefies during the Feast and re-coagulates by the following day.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template noprint noexcerpt Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:NOTRS" class="mw-redirect" title="Wikipedia:NOTRS"><span title="This claim needs references to better sources. (April 2018)">better&#160;source&#160;needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg/220px-Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="165" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg/330px-Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg/440px-Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2032" data-file-height="1524" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Saint_Lawrence_stone.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The stone on which St Lawrence's body was laid after death, in <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_fuori_le_mura" title="San Lorenzo fuori le mura">San Lorenzo fuori le mura</a></div></div></div>
<p>Due to his conspiring to hide and protect the written documents of the Church, Lawrence is known as the patron saint of archivists and librarians.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Roman_Catholic_Church">Roman Catholic Church</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Roman Catholic Church">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Lawrence is one of the most widely venerated saints of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>. Legendary details of his death were known to Damasus, Prudentius, Ambrose, and Augustine. Devotion to him was widespread by the fourth century. His liturgical celebration on 10 August has the rank of <a href="/wiki/Religious_festival" title="Religious festival">feast</a> in the <a href="/wiki/General_Roman_Calendar" title="General Roman Calendar">General Roman Calendar</a>, consistent with the oldest of Christian calendars, e. g. the <a href="/wiki/Almanac_of_Philocalus" class="mw-redirect" title="Almanac of Philocalus">Almanac of Philocalus</a> for the year 354, the inventory of which contains the principal feasts of the Roman martyrs of the middle of the fourth century. He remains one of the saints enumerated in the "Roman Canon" of the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Mass" class="mw-redirect" title="Holy Mass">Holy Mass</a> as celebrated in the <a href="/wiki/Latin_Church" title="Latin Church">Latin Church</a>.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2020)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>Lawrence is especially honoured in the city of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, of which he is considered the third patron after <a href="/wiki/St._Peter" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Peter">St. Peter</a> and <a href="/wiki/St._Paul" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Paul">St. Paul</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup> The church built over his tomb, the Papal Minor <a href="/wiki/Basilica_di_San_Lorenzo_fuori_le_Mura" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura">Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura</a>, became one of the seven principal churches of Rome and a favourite place of Roman pilgrimages.<sup id="cite_ref-foley_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-foley-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> The area proximate to the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_di_San_Lorenzo_fuori_le_Mura" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura">Basilica di San Lorenzo fuori le Mura</a> is named the "<a href="/wiki/Quartiere_San_Lorenzo" class="mw-redirect" title="Quartiere San Lorenzo">Quartiere San Lorenzo</a>".<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2020)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>Because the <a href="/wiki/Perseids" title="Perseids">Perseid Meteor Shower</a> typically occurs annually in mid-August on or proximate to his feast day, some refer to the shower as the "Tears of St Lawrence".<sup id="cite_ref-slocc_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-slocc-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:252px;"><a href="/wiki/File:San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/09/San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png/250px-San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png" decoding="async" width="250" height="188" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/09/San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png/375px-San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/09/San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png/500px-San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png 2x" data-file-width="2304" data-file-height="1728" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:San_lorenzo%27s_grill.png" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The shrine containing the <a href="/wiki/Gridiron_(cooking)" title="Gridiron (cooking)">gridiron</a> that was used to roast St Lawrence to death according to tradition is in the Church of <a href="/wiki/San_Lorenzo_in_Lucina" title="San Lorenzo in Lucina">San Lorenzo in Lucina</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>His intercession to God is invoked by <a href="/wiki/Librarians" class="mw-redirect" title="Librarians">librarians</a>, <a href="/wiki/Archivists" class="mw-redirect" title="Archivists">archivists</a>, <a href="/wiki/Comedians" class="mw-redirect" title="Comedians">comedians</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cook_(profession)" title="Cook (profession)">cooks</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tanner_(occupation)" class="mw-redirect" title="Tanner (occupation)">tanners</a> as their patron. He is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Ampleforth_Abbey" title="Ampleforth Abbey">Ampleforth Abbey</a>, whose Benedictine monks founded one of the world's leading public schools for Catholics, located in <a href="/wiki/North_Yorkshire" title="North Yorkshire">North Yorkshire</a> (North East <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a>).<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2020)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>On his feast the reliquary containing his burnt head is displayed in the <a href="/wiki/St._Peter%27s_Basilica" title="St. Peter&#39;s Basilica">St. Peter's Basilica</a> for veneration.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2020)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Anglican_Communion">Anglican Communion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Anglican Communion">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Within Anglicanism Lawrence's name is traditionally spelled <i>Laurence or Lawrence</i>. His feast is on 10 August which is in the calendar of the <i><a href="/wiki/Book_of_Common_Prayer" title="Book of Common Prayer">Book of Common Prayer</a></i>, the volume of prayers which, in its 1662 format, was the founding liturgical document of a majority of Anglican provinces. In the <i>Book of Common Prayer</i> the feast is titled "S Laurence, Archdeacon of Rome and Martyr". His feast on 10 August has been carried into the contemporary calendars of most Anglican provinces,<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup> Laurence is <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints_(Church_of_England)" title="Calendar of saints (Church of England)">remembered</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Church_of_England" title="Church of England">Church of England</a><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> with a <a href="/wiki/Lesser_Festival_(Anglicanism)" title="Lesser Festival (Anglicanism)">Lesser Festival</a> under the title "Laurence, deacon, martyr, 258" on <a href="/wiki/August_10" title="August 10">10 August</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Anglo-Catholicism" title="Anglo-Catholicism">Anglo-Catholics</a> venerate Lawrence, who is the patron of many Anglican parish churches, including 228 in <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">&#91;20&#93;</a></sup> A major church in <a href="/wiki/Sydney" title="Sydney">Sydney</a>, Australia, in the former civil parish of St Laurence, is known as "<a href="/wiki/Christ_Church_St_Laurence" title="Christ Church St Laurence">Christ Church St Laurence</a>". The Anglican charitable society, <a href="/wiki/Brotherhood_of_St_Laurence" title="Brotherhood of St Laurence">Brotherhood of St Laurence</a> also bears his name.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2020)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Lawrence&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to Francesco Moraglia the role of <a href="/wiki/Deacon" title="Deacon">deacon</a> is distinguished by service of the poor. He is destined both to the service of the table (<a href="/wiki/Works_of_mercy#Corporal_works_of_mercy" title="Works of mercy">corporal works of mercy</a>) and to the service of the word (<a href="/wiki/Works_of_mercy#Spiritual_works_of_mercy" title="Works of mercy">spiritual works of mercy</a>). "The beauty, power and the heroism of [d]eacons such as Lawrence help to discover and come to a deeper meaning of the special nature of the diaconal ministry."<sup id="cite_ref-moraglia_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-moraglia-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/49/El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg/220px-El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="135" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/49/El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg/330px-El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/49/El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg/440px-El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg 2x" data-file-width="5895" data-file-height="3615" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:El_Escorial_View_from_the_north-west.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/El_Escorial" title="El Escorial">El Escorial</a>, near Madrid, laid out in a grid pattern resembling a gridiron.</div></div></div>
<p>Many churches, schools, parishes, towns, and geographic features throughout the world are named for Lawrence of Rome. Depending on locality they are named <i>St. Lawrence</i>, <i>St. Laurence</i>, <i>San Lorenzo</i>, <i>St. Laurent</i>, <i>St. Lorenz</i> or similarly in other languages. <a href="/wiki/El_Escorial" title="El Escorial">San Lorenzo del Escorial</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> built by King <a href="/wiki/Philip_II_of_Spain" title="Philip II of Spain">Philip II of Spain</a>, commemorates his victory at the <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_St._Quentin_(1557)" title="Battle of St. Quentin (1557)">Battle of St. Quentin (1557)</a> on the Feast of St. Lawrence.<sup id="cite_ref-Fodor&#39;s_Review_2008_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fodor&#39;s_Review_2008-21">&#91;21&#93;</a></sup> The monastery and the attached palace, college, and library are laid out in a grid pattern that resembles the gridiron of Lawrence's martyrdom. The gridiron of Lawrence is also thought the basis of the design of the <a href="/wiki/Certosa_di_Padula" title="Certosa di Padula">Certosa di San Lorenzo di Padula</a>, which is a monastery in <a href="/wiki/Padula" title="Padula">Padula</a>, <a href="/wiki/Province_of_Salerno" title="Province of Salerno">Salerno</a>, <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a>. Two universities bear his name: <a href="/wiki/St._Lawrence_University" title="St. Lawrence University">St. Lawrence University</a> (non-Catholic) in <a href="/wiki/Canton,_St._Lawrence_County" class="mw-redirect" title="Canton, St. Lawrence County">Canton, St. Lawrence County</a>, <a href="/wiki/New_York_(state)" title="New York (state)">New York</a>, <a href="/wiki/United_States" title="United States">United States</a>, and <a href="/wiki/St._Lawrence_University_(Uganda)" title="St. Lawrence University (Uganda)">St. Lawrence University</a> in <a href="/wiki/Kampala" title="Kampala">Kampala</a>, <a href="/wiki/Uganda" title="Uganda">Uganda</a>. There is a school named after him in <a href="/wiki/Kolkata" title="Kolkata">Kolkata</a> named <a href="/w/index.php?title=St.Lawrence_High_School,Kolkata&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="St.Lawrence High School,Kolkata (page does not exist)">St.Lawrence High School,Kolkata</a>.
</p><p>Canada's patron saint is St. Lawrence. On his second voyage, French explorer <a href="/wiki/Jacques_Cartier" title="Jacques Cartier">Jacques Cartier</a>, arriving in the river <a href="/wiki/Estuary" title="Estuary">estuary</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Great_Lakes" title="Great Lakes">North American Great Lakes</a> on the Feast of St. Lawrence in 1535, named it the <i><a href="/wiki/Gulf_of_St._Lawrence" title="Gulf of St. Lawrence">Gulf of St. Lawrence</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22">&#91;22&#93;</a></sup>
The river emptying into the gulf was named the <i><a href="/wiki/St._Lawrence_River" title="St. Lawrence River">St. Lawrence River</a></i>. Many names in what are now <a href="/wiki/Qu%C3%A9bec" class="mw-redirect" title="Québec">Québec</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Maritime_Provinces" class="mw-redirect" title="Maritime Provinces">Maritime Provinces</a> of <a href="/wiki/Canada" title="Canada">Canada</a> are references to this important seaway, e. g., the <i><a href="/wiki/Laurentian_mountains" class="mw-redirect" title="Laurentian mountains">Laurentian mountains</a></i> north of the city of <a href="/wiki/Montreal" title="Montreal">Montreal</a>, <a href="/wiki/Saint-Laurent_(borough)" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Laurent (borough)">Saint-Laurent (borough)</a>, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Lawrence_Boulevard" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Lawrence Boulevard">Saint Lawrence Boulevard</a> which spans the width of the <a href="/wiki/Island_of_Montreal" title="Island of Montreal">Island of Montreal</a>, and <a href="/wiki/St._Lawrence_County,_New_York" title="St. Lawrence County, New York">St. Lawrence County, New York</a>, <a href="/wiki/United_States" title="United States">United States</a> near <a href="/wiki/Lake_Ontario" title="Lake Ontario">Lake Ontario</a>. 
In the province of Ontario, <a href="/wiki/St._Lawrence,_Toronto" title="St. Lawrence, Toronto">St. Lawrence</a> is a prominent, historic neighbourhood in old centre of <a href="/wiki/Toronto" title="Toronto">Toronto</a> (formerly named York), now most known for the expansive St. Lawrence Market. The Laurentian Mountains gave rise to the name for the Laurential Plateau, or the Canadian Shield. In <a href="/wiki/Switzerland" title="Switzerland">Switzerland</a>, Saint Lawrence is represented on the <a href="/wiki/Coat_of_arms" title="Coat of arms">coat of arms</a> of the city of <a href="/wiki/B%C3%BClach" title="Bülach">Bülach</a> with a gridiron.
</p><p>The rescue operation for the miners trapped in the <a href="/wiki/2010_Copiap%C3%B3_mining_accident" title="2010 Copiapó mining accident">2010 Copiapó mining accident</a> in <a href="/wiki/Chile" title="Chile">Chile</a> was named <i>Operacíon San Lorenzo</i> after Lawrence, patron saint of miners.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">&#91;23&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/Freemasonry" title="Freemasonry">Freemasonry</a> the <i>Order of St. Lawrence the Martyr</i> is a masonic degree whose ritual is based upon the story of Lawrence. It is one of the constituent degrees of the <a href="/wiki/Allied_Masonic_Degrees" title="Allied Masonic Degrees">Allied Masonic Degrees</a>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Bernalillo,_New_Mexico" title="Bernalillo, New Mexico">Bernalillo, New Mexico</a> celebrates three days of devotions to the Saint, in honor a devotional promise made by Spanish settlers during the <a href="/wiki/Pueblo_Revolt" title="Pueblo Revolt">1692 Pueblo Revolt</a>. Among the festivities are a set of dances performed by <a href="/wiki/Matachines" title="Matachines">matachines</a>. An image of the saint is kept in the house of a local family throughout the year, and a vigil and feast are held from 9–11 August. It is one of the oldest dancing processions in the New World.<sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">&#91;24&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
