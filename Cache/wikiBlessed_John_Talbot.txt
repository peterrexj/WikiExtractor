					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">English Catholic recusant and martyr</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">

</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Blessed</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">John Talbot</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;1550</span><br /><a href="/wiki/Yorkshire" title="Yorkshire">Yorkshire</a>, England</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">9 August 1600<br /><a href="/wiki/Durham,_England" title="Durham, England">Durham</a>, England</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">9 August</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>John Talbot</b> (died 9 August 1600) was an English Catholic <a href="/wiki/Recusant" class="mw-redirect" title="Recusant">recusant</a> and martyr. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Talbot_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>John Talbot was born at <a href="/wiki/Thornton-le-Street" title="Thornton-le-Street">Thornton-le-Street</a>, North Yorkshire; suffered at Durham, 9 August 1600. He had already been persecuted for his adherence to the Catholic faith, having been convicted of recusancy in 1588.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>   
</p><p>As a result of his Catholic faith, Talbot suffered severe persecution, including multiple arrests, fines and confiscation of his property. He had to endure these punishments on a daily basis - a severe test of his faith and loyalty.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Talbot was present at prayer with the priest, <a href="/wiki/Thomas_Palasor" title="Thomas Palasor">Thomas Palasor</a>, John Norton and several companions at a house in Lamesley, Gateshead when he was arrested in 1600. It was Talbot who tried to prevent the arrest of the priest. For this, he too was arrested as a traitor.
</p><p>Talbot was imprisoned and confined in a dungeon and shortly thereafter, sentenced to be <a href="/wiki/Hanged,_drawn_and_quartered" title="Hanged, drawn and quartered">hanged, drawn and quartered</a> for the crime of harbouring a Catholic priest. On Wednesday 9 August 1600, Talbot was brought, together with Fr Thomas Palasor and John Norton, to the gallows site in Durham, on the crest of the hill at the north side of Durham City. He was given the chance to save his life if he would renounce the Catholic faith and attend Protestant services - he refused. Talbot was tortured on the rack, hanged until not quite dead, then disembowelled, before being beheaded.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was beatified by Pope John Paul II at the Vatican on 22 November 1987 as one of the <a href="/wiki/Eighty-five_martyrs_of_England_and_Wales" title="Eighty-five martyrs of England and Wales">Eighty-five martyrs of England and Wales</a>. John Talbot's <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 9 August. He is also venerated on 4 May as one of the martyrs of England and Wales.
</p>
