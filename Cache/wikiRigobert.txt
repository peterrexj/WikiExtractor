					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">French bishop and Catholic saint (died c.743)</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For other people named Rigobert, see <a href="/wiki/Rigobert_(name)" title="Rigobert (name)">Rigobert (name)</a>.</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Rigobert</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">?</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 743</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">January 4</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Rigobert</b> (died c. 743) was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> monk and later abbot of the <a href="/w/index.php?title=Abbaye_Saint-Pierre_d%27Orbais&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Abbaye Saint-Pierre d&#39;Orbais (page does not exist)">Abbaye Saint-Pierre d'Orbais</a> who subsequently succeeded <a href="/wiki/Rieul_of_Reims" title="Rieul of Reims">Saint Rieul</a> as <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Reims" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Reims">bishop of Reims</a> in 698. He is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.
</p><p>Rigobert baptized <a href="/wiki/Charles_Martel" title="Charles Martel">Charles Martel</a>, but Charles afterwards had him brutally driven from the see<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and replaced, for political reasons, by the warlike and unpriestly <a href="/wiki/Milo_(bishop_of_Trier)" title="Milo (bishop of Trier)">Milo</a>, who was already Archbishop of Trier.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Rigobert took refuge in Aquitaine and then retired to Gernicourt, in the Diocese of <a href="/wiki/Soissons" title="Soissons">Soissons</a>, where he led a life in the exercises of penance and prayer.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died about the year 743, and was buried in the church of Saint Peter at Gernicourt, which he had built. <a href="/wiki/Hincmar" title="Hincmar">Hincmar</a> translated his relics to the abbey of Saint Theodoric, and later, to the church of Saint Dionysius at Reims. <a href="/wiki/Fulk_(archbishop_of_Reims)" title="Fulk (archbishop of Reims)">Fulk</a>, Hincmar's successor, removed them into the metropolitan <a href="/wiki/Reims_Cathedral" title="Reims Cathedral">Church of Our Lady of Reims</a>, in which the greater part is preserved in a rich shrine, though a portion is kept in the church of Saint Dionysius at Reims, and another portion in the cathedral of Paris, where a chapel bears his name.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is 4 January.&lt;ref&gt; <a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="https://catholicreadings.org/saint-rigobert-of-rheims/">Saint Rigobert of Rheims</a>; Catholic Daily Readings; accessed 2022-12-08
</p>
