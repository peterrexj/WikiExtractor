<p><b>Viktor of Xanten</b> was a 4th century <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> recognized by the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a>. Since the 12th century, his presumed <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">bones</a> have been kept in a <a href="/wiki/Shrine" title="Shrine">shrine</a>, which is embedded in the high altar of the <a href="/wiki/Xanten_Cathedral" title="Xanten Cathedral">Xanten Cathedral</a>. His feast day is October 10. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Narrative">Narrative</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Viktor_of_Xanten&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Narrative">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tradition states that Viktor, as a <a href="/wiki/Prefect" title="Prefect">Praefectus cohortis</a> of a <a href="/wiki/Cohort_(military_unit)" title="Cohort (military unit)">Cohort</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Theban_Legion" title="Theban Legion">Theban Legion</a>, was not caught up in the decimation at Agaunum but then fell victim to the persecution under Emperor Maximian together with other companions in Xanten.<sup id="cite_ref-Schäfer_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Schäfer-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was executed in the <a href="/wiki/Amphitheater" class="mw-redirect" title="Amphitheater">amphitheater</a> of <i>Castra Vetera</i> (the site of present-day <a href="/wiki/Xanten" title="Xanten">Xanten</a>) for refusing to sacrifice to the <a href="/wiki/Roman_gods" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman gods">Roman gods</a>. He was closely associated with <a href="/wiki/Ursus_of_Solothurn" title="Ursus of Solothurn">Ursus of Solothurn</a>, and is said to have been a relative of <a href="/wiki/Verena_of_Zurzach" title="Verena of Zurzach">Saint Verena</a>.
</p><p>Victor was first mentioned by <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a> in connection with the discovery of the bones of a Mallosus in the village of Birten, today a suburb of Xanten.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The place name "Xanten" is also derived as "ze santen", which thus refers to a widely known and revered burial place.<sup id="cite_ref-Schäfer_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Schäfer-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> According to legend, Helena of Constantinople recovered the bones of Victor and his legion and erected a chapel in their honour. 
</p><p>According to other traditions, he was a companion of <a href="/wiki/Gereon_of_Cologne" class="mw-redirect" title="Gereon of Cologne">Gereon of Cologne</a>. The tradition of Victor as a member of the Theban Legion is mixed with that of Victor of Agaunum and <a href="/wiki/Victor_of_Solothurn" title="Victor of Solothurn">Victor of Solothurn</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Schäfer_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Schäfer-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
