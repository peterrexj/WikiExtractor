<p><b>Callistus Caravario</b> (18 June 1903 ― 25 February 1930) was a <a href="/wiki/Salesians_of_Don_Bosco" title="Salesians of Don Bosco">Salesian</a> priest serving in <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a>, who along with <a href="/wiki/Luigi_Versiglia" title="Luigi Versiglia">Luigi Versiglia</a> was martyred in <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a> on 25 February 1930.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Callistus_Caravario&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Caravario was born in <a href="/wiki/Cuorgn%C3%A8" title="Cuorgnè">Cuorgnè</a>, <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a> on 18 June 1903.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He joined Salesians on the advice of Father Garelli and entered the Novitiate.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mission_in_China">Mission in China</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Callistus_Caravario&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Mission in China">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Callistus was inspired by the words of Bishop <a href="/wiki/Luigi_Versiglia" title="Luigi Versiglia">Luigi Versiglia</a> who spoke of the missions to the Brothers on his visit to <a href="/wiki/Turin" title="Turin">Turin</a> in 1922 and wanted to join the mission in China. After a prolonged insisting to join the mission in China finally he was sent to China. He was sent to <a href="/wiki/Macau" title="Macau">Macao</a> and then he spent two years in <a href="/wiki/Timor" title="Timor">Timor</a>. He returned to <a href="/wiki/Shaoguan" title="Shaoguan">Shaoguan</a> on 18 May 1929 and was ordained a priest by Versiglia. He was entrusted with the mission at <a href="/wiki/Lianzhou" title="Lianzhou">Lianzhou</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Martyrdom">Martyrdom</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Callistus_Caravario&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Martyrdom">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On 13 February 1930, Caravario was accompanying Versiglia on his pastoral visit to his mission in Lianzhou and there were some young boys and girls with them. Their boat was stopped by a group of <a href="/wiki/Bolsheviks" title="Bolsheviks">Bolshevik</a> pirates on 25 February 1930, the pirates wanted to take the girls. Versiglia and Callistus tried to stop them but they were hit by the guns by the pirates and fell unconscious. Versiglia and Callistus were taken ashore and shot by the pirates.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Their bodies were found on 27 February 1930.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:1_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sainthood">Sainthood</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Callistus_Caravario&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Sainthood">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Caravario was declared a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI" title="Pope Paul VI">Pope Paul VI</a> in 1976 and was beatified by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> in 1983. He was <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> on 1 October 2000 along with <a href="/wiki/Luigi_Versiglia" title="Luigi Versiglia">Luigi Versiglia</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">John Paul II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:3_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:4_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:4-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
