					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Italian Catholic prelate, died 1214 CE</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Albert of Jerusalem</div><br /><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Canons_Regular_of_the_Lateran" title="Canons Regular of the Lateran">C.R.L.</a></div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Latin_Patriarch_of_Jerusalem" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem">Patriarch of Jerusalem</a><br /> <a href="/wiki/Carmelite_Rule_of_St._Albert" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite Rule of St. Albert">Law-Giver of Carmel</a></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Gualtieri" title="Gualtieri">Gualtieri</a>, <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">14 September 1214<br /><a href="/wiki/Acre,_Israel" title="Acre, Israel">Acre</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Jerusalem" title="Kingdom of Jerusalem">Kingdom of Jerusalem</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Albert of Jerusalem</b>  (<i>Albertus Hierosolymitanus; Albertus Vercelensis,</i> also <i>Saint Albert</i>, <i>Albert of Vercelli</i> or <i>Alberto Avogadro</i>;<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> died 14 September 1214) was a <a href="/wiki/Canon_law_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Canon law (Catholic Church)">canon lawyer</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. He was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Bobbio" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Bobbio">Bishop of Bobbio</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Vercelli" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Vercelli">Bishop of Vercelli</a>, and served as mediator and diplomat under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Clement_III" title="Pope Clement III">Pope Clement III</a>. Innocent III appointed him <a href="/wiki/Latin_Patriarch_of_Jerusalem" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem">Patriarch of Jerusalem</a> in 1204 or 1205. In Jerusalem, he contributed the <a href="/wiki/Carmelite_Rule_of_St._Albert" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite Rule of St. Albert">Carmelite Rule of St. Albert</a> to the newly-founded <a href="/wiki/Carmelite_Order" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite Order">Carmelite Order</a>. He is honoured as a saint in the Roman Catholic Church and commemorated by the Carmelites on 17 September.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Albert_of_Vercelli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born at Castel <a href="/wiki/Gualtieri" title="Gualtieri">Gualtieri</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Italy_(Holy_Roman_Empire)" title="Kingdom of Italy (Holy Roman Empire)">Italy</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> he was educated in <a href="/wiki/Theology" title="Theology">theology</a> and law. He entered the <a href="/wiki/Canons_Regular_of_the_Lateran#Background" title="Canons Regular of the Lateran">Canons Regular of the Holy Cross</a> at Mortara and was elected prior in 1180.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
He became <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Bobbio" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Bobbio">Bishop of Bobbio</a> in 1184, and a year later was appointed <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Vercelli" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Vercelli">Bishop of Vercelli</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Campbell_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Campbell-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He served the <a href="/wiki/Papacy" class="mw-redirect" title="Papacy">Papacy</a> as a mediator and diplomat between <a href="/wiki/Pope_Clement_III" title="Pope Clement III">Pope Clement III</a> and <a href="/wiki/Frederick_I,_Holy_Roman_Emperor" class="mw-redirect" title="Frederick I, Holy Roman Emperor">Holy Roman Emperor Frederick Barbarossa</a>. He served as <a href="/wiki/Papal_legate" title="Papal legate">papal legate</a> in 1199 and helped end the war between <a href="/wiki/Parma" title="Parma">Parma</a> and <a href="/wiki/Piacenza" title="Piacenza">Piacenza</a>.
</p><p>In 1205 he was made Patriarch of Jerusalem by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a>, whom he also served as papal legate in the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Land" title="Holy Land">Holy Land</a>. As patriarch he helped found the <a href="/wiki/Carmelite_Order" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite Order">Carmelite Order</a> around 1209, in particular by his composition of what came to be called the <a href="/wiki/Carmelite_Rule_of_St._Albert" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite Rule of St. Albert">Carmelite Rule of St. Albert</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Campbell_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Campbell-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>  This order was based on <a href="/wiki/Mount_Carmel" title="Mount Carmel">Mount Carmel</a>, across the Bay of Haifa from <a href="/wiki/Acre_(city)" class="mw-redirect" title="Acre (city)">Acre</a> where he resided as patriarch.
Additionally he mediated in disputes between the <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Jerusalem" title="Kingdom of Jerusalem">Kingdom of Jerusalem</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Cyprus" title="Kingdom of Cyprus">Kingdom of Cyprus</a> and between the <a href="/wiki/Knights_Templar" title="Knights Templar">Knights Templar</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Armenian_Kingdom_of_Cilicia" title="Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia">Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia</a>.
</p><p>In 1214 he had been invited to the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Lateran_Council" class="mw-redirect" title="Fourth Lateran Council">Fourth Lateran Council</a>, but the Master of the Hospital of the Holy Spirit, whom he had rebuked and deposed for immorality, stabbed him to death on 14 September while taking part in a procession on the <a href="/wiki/Feast_of_the_Cross" title="Feast of the Cross">Feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Campbell_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Campbell-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
He was succeeded by <a href="/wiki/Raoul_of_Merencourt" class="mw-redirect" title="Raoul of Merencourt">Raoul of Merencourt</a>.
</p>
