<p><b>Herulph</b> (†815) (Herulphe, Hariolf, Hariolfus) was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_St._Gall" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of St. Gall">Abbey of St. Gall</a> who founded <a href="/wiki/Ellwangen_Abbey" title="Ellwangen Abbey">Ellwangen Abbey</a>. He is a Catholic saint; his feast day is December 13.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Herulph&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Herulph was born about 730 in <a href="/wiki/Ellwangen" title="Ellwangen">Ellwangen</a> near Stuttgart. He was the son of the Count of Ellwangen, and related to <a href="/wiki/Gozbald" title="Gozbald">Gozbald</a>, archchaplain and chancellor to <a href="/wiki/Louis_the_German" title="Louis the German">Louis the German</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-goldberg_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-goldberg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Herulph and his brother Erlolf became <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> monks of the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_St._Gall" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of St. Gall">Abbey of St. Gall</a> in Switzerland.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Around 750 he and his brother founded the monastery of Ellwangen. Herulph became the first abbot.
</p><p>In 759, Erlolf became <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Langres" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Langres">Bishop of Langres</a> in France and sent relics of SS. Speosippus, Eleosippus and Meleosippus to Ellwangen. In 773, he sent Herulph relics of SS. Sulpicius and Servilian, which he had obtained from <a href="/wiki/Pope_Adrian_I" title="Pope Adrian I">Pope Adrian I</a>, and they became the main patron saints of the first monastic church.<sup id="cite_ref-Odden_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Odden-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Herulph assisted at the <a href="/wiki/Lateran_Council_(769)" title="Lateran Council (769)">Lateran Council (769)</a>, and became chorbishop of Langres in 772. Erlolf
died in 785 and was buried in the monastery church of St Vitus at Ellwangen. He is commemorated on August 13. Herulph resigned as bishop around 778 and retired to Ellwangen, where he died in 815. He also was buried at St. Vitus.
</p><p>A <i>Vita Hariolfi</i> was written by the Benedictine monk, <a href="/wiki/Ermanrich_of_Passau" title="Ermanrich of Passau">Ermanrich of Passau</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-goldberg_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-goldberg-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
