<p><b>Saint Leobinus</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Lubin</i>) (died 14 March 557)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 
<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> was a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>, <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a>. Born in a <a href="/wiki/Peasant" title="Peasant">peasant</a> family, he became a hermit and a monk of <a href="/wiki/Micy_Abbey" title="Micy Abbey">Micy Abbey</a> before being ordained a priest. He was then elected abbot of <a href="/wiki/Brou,_Eure-et-Loir" title="Brou, Eure-et-Loir">Brou</a> and in 544, became <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Chartres" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Chartres">Bishop of Chartres</a>, succeeding <a href="/w/index.php?title=Etherius&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Etherius (page does not exist)">Etherius</a> with the consent of king <a href="/wiki/Childebert_I" title="Childebert I">Childebert I</a>.
</p>
