<p>The <b>French Southern and Antarctic Lands</b><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Terres australes et antarctiques françaises</i>, <b>TAAF</b>) is an <a href="/wiki/Overseas_territory_(France)" title="Overseas territory (France)">overseas Territory</a> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Territoire d'outre-mer</i> or <span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">TOM</i></span>) of <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>. It consists of:
</p>
<ol><li><i><a href="/wiki/Ad%C3%A9lie_Land" title="Adélie Land">Adélie Land</a></i> (<span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">Terre Adélie</i></span>), the French claim on the continent of <a href="/wiki/Antarctica" title="Antarctica">Antarctica</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Crozet_Islands" title="Crozet Islands">Crozet Islands</a> (<span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">Îles Crozet</i></span>), a group in the southern Indian Ocean, south of Madagascar.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Kerguelen_Islands" title="Kerguelen Islands">Kerguelen Islands</a> (<span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">Archipel des Kerguelen</i></span>), a group of <a href="/wiki/Volcanic_island" title="Volcanic island">volcanic islands</a> in the southern <a href="/wiki/Indian_Ocean" title="Indian Ocean">Indian Ocean</a>, southeast of <a href="/wiki/Africa" title="Africa">Africa</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Saint_Paul_and_Amsterdam_Islands" title="Saint Paul and Amsterdam Islands">Saint Paul and Amsterdam Islands</a> (<span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">Îles Saint Paul et Amsterdam</i></span>), a group to the north of the Kerguelen Islands.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Scattered_Islands_in_the_Indian_Ocean" title="Scattered Islands in the Indian Ocean">Scattered Islands</a> (<span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">Îles Éparses</i></span>), a dispersed group of islands around the coast of <a href="/wiki/Madagascar" title="Madagascar">Madagascar</a>.</li></ol>
<p>The territory is sometimes referred to as the <b>French Southern Lands</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Terres australes françaises</i>) or the <b>French Southern Territories</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-ISOTF_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ISOTF-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> usually to emphasize non-recognition of French <a href="/wiki/Sovereignty" title="Sovereignty">sovereignty</a> over <a href="/wiki/Ad%C3%A9lie_Land" title="Adélie Land">Adélie Land</a> as part of the <a href="/wiki/Antarctic_Treaty_System" title="Antarctic Treaty System">Antarctic Treaty System</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FactbookFS_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FactbookFS-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The entire territory has no known permanently settled inhabitants. Approximately 150 (in the winter) to 310 (in the summer) people are usually present in the French Southern and Antarctic Lands at any time, but they are mainly made up of military personnel, officials, scientific researchers and support staff.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 5 July 2019, the <a href="/wiki/Crozet_Islands" title="Crozet Islands">Crozet Islands</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Kerguelen_Islands" title="Kerguelen Islands">Kerguelen Islands</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Saint_Paul_and_Amsterdam_Islands" title="Saint Paul and Amsterdam Islands">Saint Paul and Amsterdam Islands</a> were inscribed as a <a href="/wiki/UNESCO" title="UNESCO">UNESCO</a> <a href="/wiki/World_Heritage_Site" title="World Heritage Site">World Heritage Site</a> as the "French Austral Lands and Seas" because of their pristine wilderness, biodiversity, and enormous bird colonies.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1097763485"><table class="box-Expand_section plainlinks metadata ambox mbox-small-left ambox-content" role="presentation"><tbody><tr><td class="mbox-image"><span typeof="mw:File"><a href="/wiki/File:Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg" class="mw-file-description"><img alt="[icon]" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1c/Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg/20px-Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg.png" decoding="async" width="20" height="14" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1c/Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg/30px-Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1c/Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg/40px-Wiki_letter_w_cropped.svg.png 2x" data-file-width="44" data-file-height="31" /></a></span></td><td class="mbox-text"><div class="mbox-text-span">This section <b>needs expansion</b>. You can help by <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=">adding to it</a>.  <span class="date-container"><i>(<span class="date">September 2022</span>)</i></span></div></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Several of the islands within this territory were originally discovered as part of the <a href="/wiki/Cape_Route" title="Cape Route">Sea Route to India</a>. The Portuguese discovered <a href="/wiki/%C3%8Ele_Saint-Paul" title="Île Saint-Paul">Saint Paul Island</a> in the sixteenth century; in the seventeenth century, the Dutch became the first to land on (and name) <a href="/wiki/%C3%8Ele_Amsterdam" title="Île Amsterdam">Amsterdam Island</a>. The French discovered the <a href="/wiki/Crozet_Islands" title="Crozet Islands">Crozet Islands</a> some time later, in the eighteenth century; the other islands were also first explored by Europeans within this time frame.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Administration">Administration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Administration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1033289096"><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">See also: <a href="/wiki/List_of_superior_administrators_of_the_French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands" title="List of superior administrators of the French Southern and Antarctic Lands">List of superior administrators of the French Southern and Antarctic Lands</a></div>
<p>The French Southern and Antarctic Lands have formed a <span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr"><a href="/wiki/Overseas_territory_(France)" title="Overseas territory (France)">territoire d'outre-mer</a></i></span> (an overseas territory) of <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a> since 1955. Formerly, they were administered from <a href="/wiki/Paris" title="Paris">Paris</a> by an <span title="French-language text"><i lang="fr">administrateur supérieur</i></span> assisted by a secretary-general; since December 2004, however, their administrator has been a <i><a href="/wiki/Prefect_(France)" title="Prefect (France)">préfet</a></i>, currently <a href="/wiki/Florence_Jeanblanc-Risler" title="Florence Jeanblanc-Risler">Florence Jeanblanc-Risler</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-JORF20200916_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-JORF20200916-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> with headquarters in <a href="/wiki/Saint-Pierre,_R%C3%A9union" title="Saint-Pierre, Réunion">Saint Pierre</a> on <a href="/wiki/R%C3%A9union" title="Réunion">Réunion</a> Island.
</p><p>The TAAF administration, the French Polar Institute Paul-Émile Victor (IPEV) and the <a href="/wiki/French_Navy" title="French Navy">French Navy</a> jointly operate the icebreaker <a href="/wiki/L%27Astrolabe_(2016_icebreaker)" title="L&#39;Astrolabe (2016 icebreaker)"><i>Astrolabe</i></a> which is based out of Reunion. The vessel is used both to bring personnel and supplies to the <a href="/wiki/Dumont_d%27Urville_Station" title="Dumont d&#39;Urville Station">Dumont d'Urville Station</a> and for research and patrol duties. <sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The territory is divided into five districts:

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geography">Geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Geography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1097763485"><table class="box-Unreferenced_section plainlinks metadata ambox ambox-content ambox-Unreferenced" role="presentation"><tbody><tr><td class="mbox-image"><div class="mbox-image-div"><span typeof="mw:File"><a href="/wiki/File:Question_book-new.svg" class="mw-file-description"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/9/99/Question_book-new.svg/50px-Question_book-new.svg.png" decoding="async" width="50" height="39" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/9/99/Question_book-new.svg/75px-Question_book-new.svg.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/9/99/Question_book-new.svg/100px-Question_book-new.svg.png 2x" data-file-width="512" data-file-height="399" /></a></span></div></td><td class="mbox-text"><div class="mbox-text-span">This section <b>does not <a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citing_sources" title="Wikipedia:Citing sources">cite</a> any <a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability" title="Wikipedia:Verifiability">sources</a></b>.<span class="hide-when-compact"> Please help <a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands&amp;action=edit">improve this section</a> by <a href="/wiki/Help:Referencing_for_beginners" title="Help:Referencing for beginners">adding citations to reliable sources</a>. <figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg/220px-Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="146" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg/330px-Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a9/Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg/440px-Pringlea_antiscorbutica_Mayes_fake.jpg 2x" data-file-width="7036" data-file-height="4670" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Kerguelen_cabbage" class="mw-redirect" title="Kerguelen cabbage">Kerguelen cabbages</a> on Île Mayès, Kerguelen</figcaption></figure>
<p>The territory includes the <a href="/wiki/Crozet_Islands" title="Crozet Islands">Crozet Islands</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Kerguelen_Islands" title="Kerguelen Islands">Kerguelen Islands</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Saint_Paul_and_Amsterdam_Islands" title="Saint Paul and Amsterdam Islands">Saint Paul and Amsterdam Islands</a> in the southern <a href="/wiki/Indian_Ocean" title="Indian Ocean">Indian Ocean</a> near 43°S, 67°E, along with <a href="/wiki/Ad%C3%A9lie_Land" title="Adélie Land">Adélie Land</a>, the sector of <a href="/wiki/Antarctica" title="Antarctica">Antarctica</a> <a href="/wiki/Territorial_claims_in_Antarctica" title="Territorial claims in Antarctica">claimed by France</a>, named by the French explorer <a href="/wiki/Jules_Dumont_d%27Urville" title="Jules Dumont d&#39;Urville">Jules Dumont d'Urville</a> after his wife.
</p><p>Adélie Land (about 432,000&#160;km<sup>2</sup> or 167,000&#160;sq&#160;mi) and the islands, totaling 7,781&#160;km<sup>2</sup> (3,004&#160;sq&#160;mi), have no indigenous inhabitants, though in 1997 there were about 100 researchers whose numbers varied from winter (July) to summer (January).
</p><p><a href="/wiki/%C3%8Ele_Amsterdam" title="Île Amsterdam">Amsterdam Island</a> and <a href="/wiki/%C3%8Ele_Saint-Paul" title="Île Saint-Paul">Saint Paul Island</a> are extinct <a href="/wiki/Volcano" title="Volcano">volcanoes</a> and have been delineated as the <a href="/wiki/Amsterdam_and_Saint-Paul_Islands_temperate_grasslands" title="Amsterdam and Saint-Paul Islands temperate grasslands">Amsterdam and Saint-Paul Islands temperate grasslands</a> <a href="/wiki/Ecoregion" title="Ecoregion">ecoregion</a>. The highest point in the territory is Mont Ross on Kerguelen Island at 1,850&#160;m (6,070&#160;ft). There are very few airstrips on the islands, only existing on islands with weather stations, and the 1,232&#160;km (766&#160;mi) of coastline have no ports or harbors, only offshore anchorages.
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg/220px-Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="149" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg/330px-Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cd/Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg/440px-Kerguelen_VolcanDuDiable.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3190" data-file-height="2157" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Volcan_du_Diable" title="Volcan du Diable">Volcan du Diable</a> on Grande Terre, Kerguelen</figcaption></figure>
<p>The islands in the Indian Ocean are supplied by the special ship <i><a href="/wiki/Marion_Dufresne_(1994)" title="Marion Dufresne (1994)">Marion Dufresne</a></i> sailing out of <a href="/wiki/Le_Port,_R%C3%A9union" title="Le Port, Réunion">Le Port</a> in <a href="/wiki/R%C3%A9union_Island" class="mw-redirect" title="Réunion Island">Réunion Island</a>. Terre Adélie is supplied by <i><a href="/wiki/L%27Astrolabe_(2016_icebreaker)" title="L&#39;Astrolabe (2016 icebreaker)">L'Astrolabe</a></i> sailing out of <a href="/wiki/Hobart" title="Hobart">Hobart</a> in <a href="/wiki/Tasmania" title="Tasmania">Tasmania</a>.
</p><p>However, the territory has a <a href="/wiki/Merchant_navy" title="Merchant navy">merchant marine fleet</a> totaling (in 1999) 2,892,911 <a href="/wiki/Gross_Register_Tonnage" class="mw-redirect" title="Gross Register Tonnage">GRT</a>/5,165,713&#160;tonnes <a href="/wiki/Deadweight_tonnage" title="Deadweight tonnage">deadweight</a> (DWT), including seven bulk carriers, five cargo ships, ten chemical tankers, nine container ships, six liquefied gas carriers, 24 petroleum tankers, one refrigerated cargo ship, and ten <a href="/wiki/Roll-on/roll-off" title="Roll-on/roll-off">roll-on/roll-off</a> (RORO) carriers. This fleet is maintained as a subset of the French register that allows French-owned ships to operate under more liberal taxation and manning regulations than permissible under the main French register. This register, however, is to vanish,<sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Dates_and_numbers#Chronological_items" title="Wikipedia:Manual of Style/Dates and numbers"><span title="The time period mentioned near this tag is ambiguous. (December 2014)">when?</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> replaced by the International French Register (<i>Registre International Français</i>, RIF).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Flora_and_fauna">Flora and fauna</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=French_Southern_and_Antarctic_Lands&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Flora and fauna">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1033289096"><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">See also: <a href="/wiki/List_of_mammals_of_French_Southern_Territories" title="List of mammals of French Southern Territories">List of mammals of French Southern Territories</a></div>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"><table class="infobox"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above">Designations</th></tr><tr class="mergedrow"><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header"><link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"><div style="border:4px solid #FFE153; line-height: 1.5; text-align: center;">

<a href="/wiki/Ramsar_Convention" title="Ramsar Convention">Ramsar Wetland</a></div></th></tr><tr class="mergedrow"><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Official name</th><td class="infobox-data">Réserve Naturelle Nationale des Terres Australes Francaises</td></tr><tr class="mergedrow"><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Designated</th><td class="infobox-data">15 September 2008</td></tr><tr class="mergedrow"><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Reference&#160;no.</th><td class="infobox-data">1837<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup></td></tr><tr style="display:none"><th colspan="2">

<p>Due to their isolation, the French islands in the southern Indian Ocean comprise one of the last remaining large wilderness areas on Earth.<sup id="cite_ref-unesco_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-unesco-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup> Furthermore, the islands are positioned along the <a href="/wiki/Antarctic_Convergence" title="Antarctic Convergence">Antarctic Convergence</a>, where <a href="/wiki/Upwelling" title="Upwelling">upwelling</a> creates nutrient-rich waters.<sup id="cite_ref-iucn_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iucn-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup> As a result, birds and marine mammals gather on the islands in great abundance. More than 50 million birds of 47 species breed on the islands, including more than half the breeding population of 16 different species.<sup id="cite_ref-unesco_15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-unesco-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup> The largest populations of <a href="/wiki/King_penguins" class="mw-redirect" title="King penguins">king penguins</a> and the endangered <a href="/wiki/Indian_yellow-nosed_albatross" title="Indian yellow-nosed albatross">Indian yellow-nosed albatross</a> on Earth are found on the Crozet Islands and Amsterdam Island, respectively.<sup id="cite_ref-iucn_16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iucn-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup> Other threatened bird species with important populations on the islands include <a href="/wiki/Eaton%27s_pintail" title="Eaton&#39;s pintail">Eaton's pintail</a>, <a href="/wiki/MacGillivray%27s_prion" title="MacGillivray&#39;s prion">MacGillivray's prion</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Amsterdam_albatross" title="Amsterdam albatross">Amsterdam albatross</a>, which is one of four bird species <a href="/wiki/Endemic" class="mw-redirect" title="Endemic">endemic</a> to the island group.<sup id="cite_ref-iucn_16-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iucn-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup> The French Southern Lands also hold the second largest population of <a href="/wiki/Southern_elephant_seals" class="mw-redirect" title="Southern elephant seals">southern elephant seals</a> on Earth, numbering roughly 200,000, and the third largest population of the <a href="/wiki/Antarctic_fur_seal" title="Antarctic fur seal">Antarctic fur seal</a>. 
</p><p>Because of their isolation and subpolar location, the French Southern Lands are relatively <a href="/wiki/Depauperate_ecosystem" title="Depauperate ecosystem">depauperate</a> of vegetation, with both Saint-Paul and Crozet having no native tree or shrub species.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> However, eight of the 36 higher plant species are endemic.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup> Some species of endemic invertebrates have also been recorded on the islands, including moths and flies which have lost their wings in the absence of predators.<sup id="cite_ref-iucn_16-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-iucn-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<p>The territory's natural resources are limited to fish and crustaceans. Economic activity is limited to servicing <a href="/wiki/Meteorology" title="Meteorology">meteorological</a> and <a href="/wiki/Geophysics" title="Geophysics">geophysical</a> research stations and French and other fishing fleets.<sup id="cite_ref-OEC_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OEC-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The main fish resources are <a href="/wiki/Patagonian_toothfish" title="Patagonian toothfish">Patagonian toothfish</a> and <a href="/wiki/Spiny_lobster" title="Spiny lobster">spiny lobster</a>. Both are poached by foreign fleets; because of this, the <a href="/wiki/French_Navy" title="French Navy">French Navy</a>, and occasionally other services, patrol the zone and arrest poaching vessels. Such arrests can result in heavy fines and/or the seizure of the ship.
</p><p>France previously sold licenses to foreign fisheries to fish the Patagonian toothfish; because of overfishing, it is now restricted to a small number of fisheries from Réunion Island.
</p><p>The territory takes in revenues of about <a href="/wiki/Euro" title="Euro">€</a>16 million a year.
</p>

<p>The French Southern Territories (i.e. the TAAF excluding Adélie Land) have been given the following <a href="/wiki/Country_code" title="Country code">country codes</a>: FS (<a href="/wiki/List_of_FIPS_country_codes" title="List of FIPS country codes">FIPS</a>) and <a href="/wiki/ISO_3166-2:TF" title="ISO 3166-2:TF">TF</a> (<a href="/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2" title="ISO 3166-1 alpha-2">ISO 3166-1 alpha-2</a>).
</p>
