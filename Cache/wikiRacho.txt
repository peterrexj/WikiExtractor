					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Racho&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="Racho">Racho</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p>
Saint <b>Racho</b> (or <b>Ragnobert</b>) <b>of Autun</b> (died c. 660)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> and an <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Orthodox</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>.  He was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Autun" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Autun">bishop of Autun</a>, with a feast day on 25 January. A <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Cluny" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Cluny">Cluniac priory</a>, Saint-Racho-lès-Autun,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> under the protection of his name was established in southern <a href="/wiki/County_of_Burgundy" title="County of Burgundy">Burgundy</a> during the first flush of the <a href="/wiki/Cluniac_reform" class="mw-redirect" title="Cluniac reform">Cluniac reform</a> movement during the tenure of <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Cluny" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of Cluny">Odilon of Cluny</a> (994–1049).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/French_commune" class="mw-redirect" title="French commune">commune</a> of <a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint-Racho,_Sa%C3%B4ne-et-Loire&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Saint-Racho, Saône-et-Loire (page does not exist)">Saint-Racho, Saône-et-Loire</a> owes its historical origins to the <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a>.
</p><p>Racho holds a tenuous place in authentic history as the predecessor in the <a href="/wiki/Diocese" title="Diocese">diocese</a> of Autun of <a href="/wiki/Leodegar" title="Leodegar">Leodegar</a>, a fully historical figure who was the great opponent of <a href="/wiki/Ebroin" title="Ebroin">Ebroin</a>— the mayor of the Palace of <a href="/wiki/Neustria" title="Neustria">Neustria</a>— and the leader of the faction of <a href="/wiki/Austrasia" title="Austrasia">Austrasian</a> great nobles in the struggles for <a href="/wiki/Hegemony" title="Hegemony">hegemony</a> over the waning <a href="/wiki/Merovingian" class="mw-redirect" title="Merovingian">Merovingian</a> dynasty. His torture and death made him a martyr and saint of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> and the Orthodox Church, which embraced the cause of the Austrasian mayors of the palace, the <a href="/wiki/Arnulfing" class="mw-redirect" title="Arnulfing">Arnulfings</a>, in the following century, anointed as the <a href="/wiki/Carolingian" class="mw-redirect" title="Carolingian">Carolingian</a> dynasty.
</p>
