					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><dl><dd><i>"Sigirannus” is also the name of a 7th-century abbot of St. Cales in the department of <a href="/wiki/Sarthe" title="Sarthe">Sarthe</a>.</i></dd></dl>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Sigiramnus</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Abbot and Confessor</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data"><span title="circa">c.</span> 655 AD</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">December 4; December 5<sup id="cite_ref-katolsk_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-katolsk-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Sigiramnus</b> (also <i>Sigirannus</i> and similar spellings; <a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Siran, Cyran</i>; died <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 655 AD), also known as <b>Saint Cyran</b>, was an abbot and confessor of the 7th century.  A nobleman of <a href="/wiki/Berry_(province)" class="mw-redirect" title="Berry (province)">Berry</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> he studied at <a href="/wiki/Tours" title="Tours">Tours</a> and then joined the royal court of <a href="/wiki/Clothaire_II" class="mw-redirect" title="Clothaire II">Clothaire II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He served as cup-bearer but always wore a <a href="/wiki/Hair-shirt" class="mw-redirect" title="Hair-shirt">hair-shirt</a> underneath his garments, devoting himself to prayer.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His father, count of <a href="/wiki/Bourges" title="Bourges">Bourges</a> (and later <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Tours" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Tours">bishop of Tours</a>), wanted Sigiramnus to marry the daughter of a nobleman.<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Refusing to marry, Sigiramnus took holy orders at the church of St. Martin at Tours in 625 AD, serving as <a href="/wiki/Archdeacon" title="Archdeacon">archdeacon</a> at <a href="/wiki/Tours" title="Tours">Tours</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He refused to gain high position in the secular world, and after his father died, he gave away his goods and money to the poor; he was locked away as a lunatic for this.<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 640, after he was released, he made a <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a> to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> with Flavius, an <a href="/wiki/Irish_people" title="Irish people">Irish</a> bishop.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  According to one account, as they crossed the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Tours" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Tours">diocese of Tours</a>, he insisting on working in the fields with the serfs after he was “seized with compassion at the peasants covered with dust and sweat.”<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>   
</p><p>When Sigiramnus returned to <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>, he founded two monasteries with land given to him by Clothaire in the diocese of Bourges: <a href="/wiki/Saint-Pierre_de_Longoret" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Pierre de Longoret">Saint-Pierre de Longoret</a> (Longoretum, Lonrey) and <a href="/wiki/M%C3%A9obecq" title="Méobecq">Méobecq</a> (Millepecus), in the forest of Brenne region of the <a href="/wiki/Berry_(province)" class="mw-redirect" title="Berry (province)">Berry province</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-zeno_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-zeno-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Longoret was later renamed <a href="/wiki/Saint-Michel-en-Brenne" title="Saint-Michel-en-Brenne">Saint-Michel-en-Brenne</a> after him.  He served as abbot of Longoret until his death in 655 AD.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Sigiramnus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A life of Sigiramnus was written in the ninth or tenth centuries; the author of this <i>Life</i> claims to have compiled it from an earlier text.<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The monastery of Saint-Cyran was dissolved in 1712.<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  <a href="/wiki/Jean_du_Vergier_de_Hauranne" title="Jean du Vergier de Hauranne">Jean du Vergier de Hauranne</a> (1581–1643), known as the Abbé of Saint-Cyran, took his title from this monastery.<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Sigiramnus’ <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> were kept at the abbey of Saint-Cyran until 1860, when <a href="/wiki/Eug%C3%A9nie_de_Montijo" title="Eugénie de Montijo">Eugénie de Montijo</a>, <a href="/wiki/List_of_French_consorts" class="mw-redirect" title="List of French consorts">Empress consort of the French</a>, encased them in a <a href="/wiki/Reliquary" title="Reliquary">reliquary</a> and gave it to the church of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Michel-en-Brenne" title="Saint-Michel-en-Brenne">Saint-Michel-en-Brenne</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-butler2_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
