					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Tutilo&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="Tutilo">Tutilo</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Medieval composer and monk (died 915)</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg/220px-St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="208" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg/330px-St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/46/St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg/440px-St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img01.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2575" data-file-height="2434" /></a><figcaption>Two ivory tablets attributed to Tuotilo</figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Tuotilo</b> (died 27 April 915) was a Frankish monk at the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint_Gall" title="Abbey of Saint Gall">Abbey of Saint Gall</a>. He was a composer, and according to <a href="/wiki/Ekkehard_IV" title="Ekkehard IV">Ekkehard IV</a> a century later, also a poet, musician, painter and sculptor.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Various <a href="/wiki/Trope_(music)" title="Trope (music)">trope</a> melodies can be assigned to Tuotilo, but works of other mediums are attributed with less certainty. He was a student of <a href="/w/index.php?title=Iso_of_St._Gallen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Iso of St. Gallen (page does not exist)">Iso of St. Gallen</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iso_of_St._Gallen" class="extiw" title="de:Iso of St. Gallen">de</a>&#93;</span> and friends with the fellow monk <a href="/wiki/Notker_the_Stammerer" title="Notker the Stammerer">Notker the Stammerer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life_and_career">Life and career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Tuotilo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life and career">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg/220px-St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="173" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg/330px-St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/22/St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg/440px-St_Gallen_Tuotilotafeln_Kopie_img05.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2650" data-file-height="2087" /></a><figcaption>Detail of an ivory tablet attributed to Tuotilo</figcaption></figure>
<p>Born in <a href="/wiki/Alamannia" title="Alamannia">Alemannic</a> Germany,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;n 1&#93;</a></sup> he is said to have been a large and powerfully built man, and an excellent boxer. Always cheerful and in excellent spirits, he was a general favorite.<sup id="cite_ref-bradley_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bradley-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He received his education at St. Gall's, from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Iso_of_St._Gallen&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Iso of St. Gallen (page does not exist)">Iso of St. Gallen</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iso_of_St._Gallen" class="extiw" title="de:Iso of St. Gallen">de</a>&#93;</span> and the Irishman Moengall, teachers in the monastic school.<sup id="cite_ref-kampers_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kampers-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was the friend of <a href="/wiki/Notker_of_St._Gall" class="mw-redirect" title="Notker of St. Gall">Notker of St. Gall</a>, with whom he studied music under Moengal. Educated at the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_St._Gall" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of St. Gall">Abbey of St. Gall</a>, he remained to become a monk there.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Tuotilo was a composer, poet, musician, <a href="/wiki/Painting" title="Painting">painter</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sculptor" class="mw-redirect" title="Sculptor">sculptor</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEPlanchart2001§_para._1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to the British librarian John W Bradley, Tuotilo was said to be "a good speaker, had a fine musical voice, was a capital carver in wood, and an accomplished illuminator. Like most of the earlier monks of St. Gall, he was a clever musician, equally skillful with the trumpet and the harp. Besides being teacher of music in the upper school to the sons of the nobility, he was a classical tutor and could preach both in Latin and Greek. His chief accomplishments, however, were music and painting, and on these his reputation mainly rests. He was much in request and by the permission of his abbot travelled to distant places. One of his celebrated sculptures was the image of the Blessed Virgin for the cathedral at Metz. In addition, he was a mathematician and astronomer, and constructed an astrolabe or orrery, which showed the courses of the planets".<sup id="cite_ref-bradley_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bradley-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Works">Works</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Tuotilo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Works">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Music">Music</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Tuotilo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Music">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">Further information: <a href="/wiki/Easter_Drama" title="Easter Drama">Easter Drama</a></div>
<p>Tuotilo played several instruments, including the harp. The history of the <a href="/wiki/Ecclesiastical" title="Ecclesiastical">ecclesiastical</a> <a href="/wiki/Drama" title="Drama">drama</a> begins with the trope sung as Introit of the <a href="/wiki/Mass_(liturgy)" title="Mass (liturgy)">Mass</a> on <a href="/wiki/Easter_Sunday" class="mw-redirect" title="Easter Sunday">Easter Sunday</a>. It has come down to us in a St. Gallen <a href="/wiki/Manuscript" title="Manuscript">manuscript</a> dating from the time of Tuotilo. According to the works catalogue of <a href="/wiki/Ekkehard_IV" title="Ekkehard IV">Ekkehard IV</a>, <i>Casus sancti Galli</i>, Tuotilo is the author of five <a href="/wiki/Trope_(music)" title="Trope (music)">tropes</a>; further research ascribed five additional tropes to him.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Some of them are available in modern editions.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Art">Art</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Tuotilo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Art">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>James Midgley Clark points out that the most interesting items at the St. Gallen Abbey in Switzerland are the ivory tablets attributed to Tuotilo, which form the cover of the <i><a href="/wiki/Evangelium_Longum" title="Evangelium Longum">Evangelium Longum</a></i>.<sup id="cite_ref-mellby_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mellby-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Tuotilo's <a href="/wiki/Paintings" class="mw-redirect" title="Paintings">paintings</a> can be found at <a href="/wiki/Konstanz" title="Konstanz">Konstanz</a>, <a href="/wiki/Metz" title="Metz">Metz</a>, St. Gallen, and <a href="/wiki/Mainz" title="Mainz">Mainz</a>.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Tuotilo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tuotilo was buried at a chapel dedicated to <a href="/wiki/Catherine_of_Alexandria" title="Catherine of Alexandria">Saint Catherine</a> in St. Gall, which was later renamed for him.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> His feast day is celebrated on 28 March.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> St. Tuotilo is the patron saint of speech therapists.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (August 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
