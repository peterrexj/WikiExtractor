					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color: #DFB0DF;"><div style="display:inline" class="fn">Eoban</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-subheader role">Bishop of Utrecht</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Church</th><td class="infobox-data org"><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Diocese</th><td class="infobox-data label"><a href="/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Utrecht_(695%E2%80%931580)" class="mw-redirect" title="Archdiocese of Utrecht (695–1580)">Archdiocese of Utrecht</a></td></tr><tr class="note"><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">In office</th><td class="infobox-data">753–754</td></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:#DDDDDD;">Personal details</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">5 June 754</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Eoban</b> (died 5 June 754 at <a href="/wiki/Dokkum" title="Dokkum">Dokkum</a>) was a companion of <a href="/wiki/St._Boniface" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Boniface">St. Boniface</a>, and was martyred with him on his final mission. In Germany, he is revered as a bishop and martyr.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Eoban&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Little is known of Eoban apart from what the <i>Vita Bonifatii</i> says.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Apparently, he was an English priest who came to Germany together with St. Boniface. In 753, Willibald's <i>Vita</i> lists him as a <a href="/wiki/Chorbishop" title="Chorbishop">Chorbishop</a>. The <i><a href="/w/index.php?title=Fulda_martyrology&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Fulda martyrology (page does not exist)">Fulda martyrology</a></i> mentions him as a bishop,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> as does the <i>Vita tertia Bonifatii</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to the <i>Vita Bonifatii auctore Willibaldo</i>, on the morning of 5 June 754, Boniface and 50 others, presumably including Eoban (none of the companions are mentioned by name in the <i>Vita</i>), were killed at <a href="/wiki/Dokkum" title="Dokkum">Dokkum</a> (The Netherlands) by pagan Frisians.<sup id="cite_ref-farmer_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-farmer-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His cult was of some importance to English Catholics; he is included in the paintings made by <a href="/wiki/Niccol%C3%B2_Circignani" title="Niccolò Circignani">Niccolò Circignani</a> of English saints and martyrs in the <a href="/wiki/English_College,_Rome" title="English College, Rome">English College, Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-farmer_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-farmer-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration_in_Erfurt">Veneration in Erfurt</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Eoban&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration in Erfurt">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After 756 the relics of the bishops of Utrecht Eoban and Adalar were transferred to <a href="/wiki/Fulda" title="Fulda">Fulda</a>, and buried next to St. Boniface. Before 1100 they had been then transferred to <a href="/wiki/Erfurt" title="Erfurt">Erfurt</a>. <sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Around this time the veneration of the companions of St. Boniface began in Erfurt.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Sarcophagus" title="Sarcophagus">Sarcophagus</a> containing the relics of the saints Adalar and Eoban in the <a href="/wiki/Erfurt_Cathedral" title="Erfurt Cathedral">Erfurt Cathedral</a> is dated from about 1350.
</p><p>His feast day as a saint is 7 July.
</p>
