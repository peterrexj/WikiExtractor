<p><b>Saint Zacchaeus of Jerusalem</b> (died 116 AD?) is a 2nd-century Christian saint venerated by the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> and <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Orthodox">Eastern Orthodox</a> churches. Also known also Zacharias, he was the fourth <a href="/wiki/Greek_Orthodox_Patriarch_of_Jerusalem" title="Greek Orthodox Patriarch of Jerusalem">Bishop of Jerusalem</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is August 23.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to <a href="/wiki/Eusebius" title="Eusebius">Eusebius</a>, he was a <a href="/wiki/Jewish_Christian" title="Jewish Christian">Jewish Christian</a>. Little is known about his life, although he is recognized as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. 
His episcopacy was about the years 112 to 116, when he probably died.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
