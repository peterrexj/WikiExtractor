<p><b>George Errington</b> of <a href="/wiki/Hurst_Castle" title="Hurst Castle">Hurst Castle</a> – from the minor gentry branch of Bingfield, St John Lee, Northumberland – was an English <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Lay_preacher" title="Lay preacher">layman</a> who is honoured as a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> by the Catholic Church.
</p><p>Errington was convicted of attempted conversion to the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>, in a plot by an <a href="/wiki/Anglican" class="mw-redirect" title="Anglican">Anglican</a> minister who claimed interest in this. Convicted of treason for this under the <a href="/wiki/Penal_law_(British)#English_statutes_on_religious_nonconformity" title="Penal law (British)">Penal Laws</a> enacted under Queen <a href="/wiki/Elizabeth_I" title="Elizabeth I">Elizabeth I</a>, he was condemned to death. For this he suffered <a href="/wiki/Hanging,_drawing_and_quartering" class="mw-redirect" title="Hanging, drawing and quartering">hanging, drawing and quartering</a> at <a href="/wiki/York" title="York">York</a> on 29 November 1596. Two years before his own death, Errington had ridden with <a href="/wiki/John_Boste" title="John Boste">John Boste</a> on his last journey from York to <a href="/wiki/Durham,_England" title="Durham, England">Durham</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Martyred with Errington were <a href="/wiki/Henry_Abbot_(martyr)" title="Henry Abbot (martyr)">Henry Abbot</a>, <a href="/wiki/William_Knight_(martyr)" title="William Knight (martyr)">William Knight</a> and <a href="/wiki/William_Gibson_(martyr)" title="William Gibson (martyr)">William Gibson</a>, who had all been caught up in the plot by the minister. Except for Abbot who was executed and beatified separately, they were all beatified by Pope John Paul II as among the <a href="/wiki/Eighty-five_martyrs_of_England_and_Wales" title="Eighty-five martyrs of England and Wales">Eighty-five martyrs of England and Wales</a>.
</p>
