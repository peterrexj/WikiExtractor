<p><b>Maxima of Rome</b> (<span class="rt-commentedText nowrap"><span class="IPA nopopups noexcerpt" lang="en-fonipa"><a href="/wiki/Help:IPA/English" title="Help:IPA/English">/<span style="border-bottom:1px dotted"><span title="/ˈ/: primary stress follows">ˈ</span><span title="&#39;m&#39; in &#39;my&#39;">m</span><span title="/æ/: &#39;a&#39; in &#39;bad&#39;">æ</span><span title="&#39;k&#39; in &#39;kind&#39;">k</span><span title="&#39;s&#39; in &#39;sigh&#39;">s</span><span title="/ɪ/: &#39;i&#39; in &#39;kit&#39;">ɪ</span><span title="&#39;m&#39; in &#39;my&#39;">m</span><span title="/ə/: &#39;a&#39; in &#39;about&#39;">ə</span></span>/</a></span></span>) was a slave and friend of <a href="/wiki/Ansanus" title="Ansanus">Saint Ansanus of Siena</a>. She was <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyred</a> by being beaten to death in the persecutions of <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a>, circa 304. Locally recognized as saint, her feast day is September 2.<sup id="cite_ref-patron_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
