<p><b>Saint Zoilus</b> (died 304 AD) is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.  Christian tradition states that he was a young man martyred with nineteen others at <a href="/wiki/C%C3%B3rdoba,_Spain" title="Córdoba, Spain">Córdoba, Spain</a> under <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Zoilus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His name is mentioned by <a href="/wiki/Prudentius" title="Prudentius">Prudentius</a> and his name appears in the <i><a href="/wiki/Martyrologium_Hieronymianum" title="Martyrologium Hieronymianum">Martyrologium Hieronymianum</a></i> as well as the <i>Roman Martyrology</i>.
</p><p>Their <a href="/wiki/Relic" title="Relic">relics</a> were enshrined at the abbey named after him: the Benedictine abbey of San Zoilo de Carrión at <a href="/wiki/Carri%C3%B3n_de_los_Condes" title="Carrión de los Condes">Carrión de los Condes</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Province_of_Palencia" title="Province of Palencia">Province of Palencia</a>.  There was also a monastery near Córdoba dedicated to him.  Some of the subsequent <a href="/wiki/Martyrs_of_C%C3%B3rdoba" title="Martyrs of Córdoba">Martyrs of Córdoba</a> were associated with this monastery.
</p><p>His feast was also celebrated at <a href="/wiki/Chester" title="Chester">Chester</a>;<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> he was anciently and incorrectly considered to have reigned by the city's inhabitants.
</p>
