<p><b>Saint Metrophanes</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: Μητροφάνης;&#160;? – 326) was the <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of <a href="/wiki/Byzantium" title="Byzantium">Byzantium</a> from 306 to 314. He may have retired from his episcopacy and died as late as 326.
</p><p>There is a tradition that, before his death, the Emperor <a href="/wiki/Constantine_I" class="mw-redirect" title="Constantine I">Constantine I</a> bestowed upon him the honorary title of <a href="/wiki/Patriarch" title="Patriarch">Patriarch</a>; however, Byzantium did not become the capital of the Empire until 330 (when it was renamed <a href="/wiki/New_Rome" title="New Rome">New Rome</a> or <a href="/wiki/Constantinople" title="Constantinople">Constantinople</a>), and the <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_see" title="Episcopal see">see</a> was not elevated to a patriarchate until 451.
Metrophanes I could not partake in the <a href="/wiki/First_Ecumenical_Council" class="mw-redirect" title="First Ecumenical Council">first Ecumenical Council</a>, which was held at <a href="/wiki/Nicaea" title="Nicaea">Nicaea</a>, due to age and ill health (he was already bedridden). He sent instead <a href="/wiki/Alexander_of_Constantinople" title="Alexander of Constantinople">Alexander</a>, the first among his <a href="/wiki/Presbyters" class="mw-redirect" title="Presbyters">presbyters</a>, an honest man, whom he destined as his successor. For it is said that, when the <a href="/wiki/First_Council_of_Nicaea" title="First Council of Nicaea">Council</a> had ended and the <a href="/wiki/King" title="King">king</a> with the god-bearing fathers returned, he was told by God that Alexander, and after him <a href="/wiki/Paul_I_of_Constantinople" title="Paul I of Constantinople">Paul</a>, pleased God, and are good for this position.
</p><p>Metrophanes has been <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>, and is revered in both the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is June 4.
</p><p>His predecessor as Bishop of Byzantium was Probus, son of Dometius, and Metrophanes may have been the son of Probus.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
