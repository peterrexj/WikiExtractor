<p><b>Visma Solutions</b> is a Finnish joint stock company, which offers <a href="/wiki/Software_as_a_Service" class="mw-redirect" title="Software as a Service">SaaS</a> <a href="/wiki/Business_software" title="Business software">business software</a> for companies.  The company produces <a href="/wiki/Professional_services_automation" title="Professional services automation">professional services automation</a> software, <a href="/wiki/E-accounting" title="E-accounting">E-accounting</a> software, budgeting and forecasting software, e-invoicing service software, and an online signature service. Visma Solutions is part of the <a href="/wiki/Visma" title="Visma">Visma group</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-LinkedIn_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LinkedIn-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Visma_Solutions&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Visma Severa was founded in 2004 by Ari-Pekka Salovaara and Jari Kärkkäinen, who were studying at Lappeenranta University of Technology at the time.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 2010 company was acquired by Visma and from 2013 on has been part of Visma Solutions.<sup id="cite_ref-Talouselämä_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Talouselämä-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Visma Solutions was founded in the end of year 2012, when former joint stock company called Netvisor was named as Visma Solutions. Netvisor was founded by Markku Nylund, while he studied at <a href="/wiki/Lappeenranta_University_of_Technology" class="mw-redirect" title="Lappeenranta University of Technology">Lappeenranta University of Technology</a>. In 2000 Netvisor was merged with Bittivisio and the first version of the software was launched in 2002.<sup id="cite_ref-Tuloslaskelma_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tuloslaskelma-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Two later Cap Gemini Ernst &amp; Young bought Bittivisio, but sold Netvisor’s business already in 2005 to Solanum Networks Economic Administration.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> From 2011 on Netvisor has been part of Visma group and in the end of 2012 the company’s name was changed to Visma Solutions.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Talouselämä_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Talouselämä-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In November 2020, Visma acquired Danish software company Ditmer.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Products">Products</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Visma_Solutions&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Products">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Severa" class="mw-redirect" title="Severa">Visma Severa</a> <a href="/wiki/Professional_services_automation" title="Professional services automation">PSA</a> software, which is optimized for organizations such as PR, advertising, design, IT, engineering, law, architecture and accounting firms.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup></li></ul>
<ul><li>Visma Netvisor is e-accounting software. The software has been developed over 10 years and it is one of the pioneers in the field of e-accounting and SaaS software.<sup id="cite_ref-LinkedIn_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LinkedIn-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>Maventa is an e-invoicing service suitable for SME’s to multinational corporations.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>ValueFrame is a <a href="/wiki/Professional_services_automation" title="Professional services automation">PSA</a> software suited for architects, advertising and communication agencies and consultants.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li>Visma Sign is a legally binding <a href="/wiki/Electronic_signature" title="Electronic signature">online signature service</a>. Visma Sign can be integrated to online forms for immediate signing, or used to send signing invitations via email and text messages.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup></li></ul>
