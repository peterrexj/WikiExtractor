</p>
<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Beverley_Minster.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/Beverley_Minster.jpg/190px-Beverley_Minster.jpg" decoding="async" width="190" height="143" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/Beverley_Minster.jpg/285px-Beverley_Minster.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/Beverley_Minster.jpg/380px-Beverley_Minster.jpg 2x" data-file-width="640" data-file-height="480" /></a><figcaption>Beverley Minster where he lived</figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Bercthun of  Beverley</b> (died 15 May 733) also known as <b>Bertin</b> and <b>Britwin</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> <i>Berhthu</i> or <i>Beorhthun</i>,  was an eighth century <a href="/wiki/Anglo_Saxon" class="mw-redirect" title="Anglo Saxon">Anglo Saxon</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was a (<a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a>) <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> of <a href="/wiki/Beverley" title="Beverley">Beverley</a>, a <a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/disciple" class="extiw" title="wiktionary:disciple">disciple</a> of <a href="/wiki/John_of_Beverley" title="John of Beverley">John of Beverley</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bede" title="Bede">Bede</a>'s informant about much of Bedes history regarding Beverley. Latter in life he became the <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">Abbot</a> of Beverley.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was also known from the <a href="/wiki/Secgan" class="mw-redirect" title="Secgan">secgan</a> <a href="/wiki/Hagiographies" class="mw-redirect" title="Hagiographies">hagiographies</a>.
</p><p>A <a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="http://www.bl.uk/onlinegallery/onlineex/illmanus/other/largeimage74683.html">Bust of Bercthun</a> is thought to be kept in the <a href="/wiki/British_Library" title="British Library">British Library</a>.
</p><p>In 733 <a href="/wiki/Anno_Domini" title="Anno Domini">AD</a> he died on the 15 May, on which day his <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast</a> was locally kept.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> A feast day was celebrated locally in his honour on the date of his death.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
