<p>Saint <b>Hypatius the Wonderworker, Hypatius of <a href="/wiki/%C3%87ank%C4%B1r%C4%B1" title="Çankırı">Gangra</a></b> (<span title="Ancient Greek (to 1453)-language text"><span lang="grc">Ὑπάτιος Γαγγρῶν</span></span>) – Hieromartyr; titular Bishop of <a href="/wiki/Gangra" class="mw-redirect" title="Gangra">Gangra</a>, <a href="/wiki/Asia_Minor" class="mw-redirect" title="Asia Minor">Asia Minor</a>; present at the <a href="/wiki/First_Ecumenical_Council" class="mw-redirect" title="First Ecumenical Council">First Ecumenical Council</a> where he supported Saint <a href="/wiki/Athanasius_of_Alexandria" title="Athanasius of Alexandria">Athanasius the Great</a> against the <a href="/wiki/Arianism" title="Arianism">Arian heresy</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a> celebrates him on <a href="/wiki/March_31_(Eastern_Orthodox_liturgics)" title="March 31 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)">March 31</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> celebrates him on 14 November.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>There is not much information about his life. On the road from <a href="/wiki/Constantinople" title="Constantinople">Constantinople</a> to Gangra in year 326, followers of Novatus and Felicissimus attacked him in a desolate place, and threw him into a muddy swamp. A woman who was amongst the attackers, struck him on the head with a rock – delivering a killing blow. Immediately after, she went mad, and started hitting herself with the same stone. She was healed only after they brought her back to the saint's burial place. 
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg/220px-Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="151" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg/330px-Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4b/Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg/440px-Menologion_of_Basil_064.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1078" data-file-height="740" /></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p>His body was found by some Christians who ran to the city of <a href="/wiki/Gangra" class="mw-redirect" title="Gangra">Gangra</a>, and the inhabitants of the city came and buried him, their beloved <a href="/wiki/Archpriest" title="Archpriest">archpriest</a>. After his death, the <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> of the saint became famous for numerous miracles. For that reason, he is called a <i>Wonderworker</i>.
</p>
