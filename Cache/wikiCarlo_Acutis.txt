<p><b>Carlo Acutis</b> (3 May 1991 – 12 October 2006) was an English-born Italian <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> youth and website designer<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> who is best known for documenting <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_miracle" title="Eucharistic miracle">Eucharistic miracles</a> around the world and cataloguing them onto a <a href="/wiki/Website" title="Website">website</a> which he created before his death from <a href="/wiki/Leukemia" title="Leukemia">leukemia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was noted for his cheerfulness, computer skills, and deep devotion to the <a href="/wiki/Eucharist" title="Eucharist">Eucharist</a>, which became a core theme of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on 10 October 2020, two days before the 14th anniversary of his death.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Birth_and_baptism">Birth and baptism</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Birth and baptism">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Carlo Acutis was born in <a href="/wiki/London,_England" class="mw-redirect" title="London, England">London, England</a>, on 3 May 1991, to Andrea Acutis and Antonia Salzano, members of a wealthy Italian family.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-alwasys_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-alwasys-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-vite_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vite-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202116,_18-9_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202116,_18-9-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> The Acutis family had a prominent position in the Italian insurance industry.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202118_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202118-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> The Salzanos ran a publishing company.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202119_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202119-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> Acutis' maternal great-grandmother was born in the United States and came from a family of landowners in New York.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202120_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202120-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His baptism took place on 18 May 1991 in the church of <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Dolours,_Chelsea" title="Our Lady of Dolours, Chelsea">Our Lady of Dolours, Chelsea</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-rcdow_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-rcdow-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> His paternal grandfather, Carlo, was his godfather, and his maternal grandmother, Luana, was his godmother.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202116_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202116-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His first nanny was a young Scottish woman who once gave him a small amount of alcohol to help him sleep.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202118_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202118-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Neither parent was religious, and they had worked in London and Germany before Acutis was born.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7NZyz_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7NZyz-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Move_to_Italy">Move to Italy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Move to Italy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The family moved to <a href="/wiki/Milan" title="Milan">Milan</a> in September 1991, not long after Acutis' birth.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7NZyz_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7NZyz-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202119_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202119-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> Both parents began working in their families' businesses<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202118_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202118-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> and Acutis was cared for by a new Irish nanny.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202119_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202119-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Aside from a few visits to a daycare center, most of Acutis' early care came from nannies.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202141_14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202141-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup> At once such visit to a daycare center, he began to be hassled by other children.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202119_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202119-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> He was now under the care of a Polish nanny who thought Acutis was "too good" and she tried to teach him to set boundaries so that the other children would not take his toys.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202119_9-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202119-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> He once responded to her that "Jesus would not be happy if I lost my temper."<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202119_9-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202119-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In the summer, Acutis was sent to stay with his mother's parents in <a href="/wiki/Centola" title="Centola">Centola</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202120_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202120-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> After spending the day at the beach, he would join a number of old women in the local parish church to pray the rosary.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202122_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202122-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup> His family owned a boat at <a href="/wiki/Santa_Margherita_Ligure" title="Santa Margherita Ligure">Santa Margherita Ligure</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202182_16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202182-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="School_years">School years</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: School years">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Acutis' first grade school, the San Carlo Institute, was one of the most elite schools in Milan.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202123,_191_17-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202123,_191-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> He entered in September 1997 but the school was a distance from their home and, three months later he transferred to Marcelline Tommaseo Institute, a school closer to his home.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202123,_191_17-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202123,_191-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> During his walks to school, he took a particular interest in the home caretakers, most of whom were not Italian, he met along the way.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202123-4_18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202123-4-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup> After learning their names, he would stop to greet them personally each morning.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202124_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202124-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Starting in September 2002 he received his middle school education at the Marcelline Tommaseo Institute, which is run by the <a href="/wiki/Saint_Marcellina" title="Saint Marcellina">Sisters of St. Marcellina</a>, and went to the <a href="/wiki/Jesuits" title="Jesuits">Jesuit</a> <a href="/wiki/Leo_XIII_Institute,_Milan" title="Leo XIII Institute, Milan">Instituto Leone XIII</a> high school.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202146,_191,_192_20-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202146,_191,_192-20">&#91;20&#93;</a></sup> He was an average student, but liked to read and independent study in areas that interested him, including computer science.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202146-7_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202146-7-21">&#91;21&#93;</a></sup> He also taught himself to play the saxophone.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202147_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202147-22">&#91;22&#93;</a></sup> He had a tutor who helped him with his homework and ended up following him to church.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202148_23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202148-23">&#91;23&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Outside school, he did volunteer work with the homeless and destitute. He also liked films, comic editing, and playing <a href="/wiki/PlayStation" title="PlayStation">PlayStation</a> video games.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (April 2023)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> He struggled with his weight, often overindulging on <a href="/wiki/Nutella" title="Nutella">Nutella</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202177_24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202177-24">&#91;24&#93;</a></sup> As a <a href="/wiki/Acts_of_reparation" title="Acts of reparation">sacrifice</a>, he would give up sweets or his favorite movies, like the <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_F%C3%A1tima#Marian_apparitions" title="Our Lady of Fátima">children of Fatima</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202164_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202164-25">&#91;25&#93;</a></sup> He admired the poverty of the <a href="/wiki/Franciscans" title="Franciscans">Franciscans</a> and sought to emulate it.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021100_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021100-26">&#91;26&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Acts_of_kindness">Acts of kindness</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Acts of kindness">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Acutis was a lover of nature.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202178-9_27-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202178-9-27">&#91;27&#93;</a></sup> His father bought him a device to pick up trash while hiking so that he would not fill his pockets with trash and cigarette butts.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202178-9_27-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202178-9-27">&#91;27&#93;</a></sup> While at the beach, he used an inflatable boat, snorkel, and fins to retrieve trash in the ocean.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202179_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202179-28">&#91;28&#93;</a></sup> He also became very angry when he encountered young people who were stomping on lizards.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202179_28-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202179-28">&#91;28&#93;</a></sup> An animal lover, he had two cats, four dogs, and fish at home, and asked his parents to take in every stray animal he found.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202179_28-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202179-28">&#91;28&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On the social side, Acutis would worry about friends of his whose parents were divorcing and would invite them to his home to support them.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202173_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202173-29">&#91;29&#93;</a></sup> He defended peers at school when bullies mocked them.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202173_29-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202173-29">&#91;29&#93;</a></sup> He would sometimes see two friends at school fighting, and then invite them back to his house after school to help them reconcile.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202168_30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202168-30">&#91;30&#93;</a></sup> He also spoke up for girls who were being whistled at or harassed, and told the boys doing so to stop.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021145_31-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021145-31">&#91;31&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When his grandparents gave him several games for his birthday one year, Acustis visited the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor_Capuchin" title="Order of Friars Minor Capuchin">Capuchins</a> in Milan and donated them to children who had none.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202199_32-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202199-32">&#91;32&#93;</a></sup> In the evenings, Acutis would ask his grandmother to make a snack for a man who begged in the park near their house.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202199_32-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202199-32">&#91;32&#93;</a></sup> When he dropped it off, Acutis would also give the beggar some of his pocket money to buy coffee in the morning.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202199_32-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202199-32">&#91;32&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Family_life">Family life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Family life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Acutis's parents were not able to have other children during his lifetime, though they wanted them and met with doctors to assist them in conceiving.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202150,_188_33-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202150,_188-33">&#91;33&#93;</a></sup> His mother, Antonia, is said to attribute to his intercession the fact that, at the age of 44, she gave birth to twins, Michele and Francesca, born precisely four years to the day after his death.<sup id="cite_ref-mom_34-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mom-34">&#91;34&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188_35-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188-35">&#91;35&#93;</a></sup> During his lifetime his cousin Flavia was his best friend.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202150_36-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202150-36">&#91;36&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The Acutises employed a <a href="/wiki/Brahmin" title="Brahmin">Brahmin</a> immigrant from India to work in their household.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202128_37-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202128-37">&#91;37&#93;</a></sup> Rajesh Mohur and Acutis quickly became friends.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202128-9_38-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202128-9-38">&#91;38&#93;</a></sup> In time, after speaking with Acutis about Christianity, Mohur asked to be baptized.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202131_39-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202131-39">&#91;39&#93;</a></sup> A friend of Mohur's, Seeven Kistnen, also converted and was baptized after meeting with Acutis and hearing him speak about the faith.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202131-2_40-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202131-2-40">&#91;40&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Acutis's mother, Antonia, grew up in a secular family.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021151_41-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021151-41">&#91;41&#93;</a></sup> She was confirmed while she was in college and was married in the church, but she did not attend mass before Acutis was born.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021187_42-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021187-42">&#91;42&#93;</a></sup> It was her son's faith, and his insistent questions, that brought her back to the faith.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021151_41-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021151-41">&#91;41&#93;</a></sup> It was similar for his father.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021159_43-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021159-43">&#91;43&#93;</a></sup> Antonia was asked, and agreed, to lead a catechism class after Acutis made his first communion, though she did not feel qualified to do so.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021187_42-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021187-42">&#91;42&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The Acutis family would sometimes invite priests to dinner, including a high-ranking official at the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Doctrine_of_the_Faith" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith">Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202125_44-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202125-44">&#91;44&#93;</a></sup> The family also supported scholarships for underprivileged students at the <a href="/wiki/Pontifical_Oriental_Institute" title="Pontifical Oriental Institute">Pontifical Oriental Institute</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Pontifical_Biblical_Institute" title="Pontifical Biblical Institute">Pontifical Biblical Institute</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202125_44-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202125-44">&#91;44&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Although he immensely enjoyed travel and visited a number of different countries, the town of <a href="/wiki/Assisi" title="Assisi">Assisi</a> remained a favorite.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021148_45-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021148-45">&#91;45&#93;</a></sup> His parents bought a second home in the city.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021149_46-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021149-46">&#91;46&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Though Acutis was considered a computer genius, his parents were not as skilled and said they did not know how to set up parental controls on his computer.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021132_47-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021132-47">&#91;47&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Religious_education_and_devotion">Religious education and devotion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Religious education and devotion">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When Acutis was three years old, his maternal grandfather, Antonio Salzano, died.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202143_48-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202143-48">&#91;48&#93;</a></sup> Several days before his death, Acutis was present when his grandfather received the <a href="/wiki/Anointing_of_the_sick" title="Anointing of the sick">anointing of the sick</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202144_49-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202144-49">&#91;49&#93;</a></sup> The grandfather was said to have appeared to him in a dream asking to be prayed for. Shortly after his death, Acutis put on his coat while his grandmother was caring for him and asked to be taken to church.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202145_50-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202145-50">&#91;50&#93;</a></sup> When she asked him why, Acutis said he wanted to pray for his grandfather who, Acutis said, "went to see Jesus."<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202145_50-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202145-50">&#91;50&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When Acutis displayed an interest in Catholic religious practice, his questions were answered by the family's Polish babysitter.<sup id="cite_ref-kock_51-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kock-51">&#91;51&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-kath_52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kath-52">&#91;52&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Parish_life">Parish life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Parish life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When Acutis was 12 years old, he became a catechist at his parish, Santa Maria Segreta.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109,_124_53-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109,_124-53">&#91;53&#93;</a></sup> In the structure in Italy at the time, religious education was typically conducted by young team leaders in youth groups as opposed to an adult standing at the head of a classroom.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109_54-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109-54">&#91;54&#93;</a></sup> The pastor or other adult would provide the lesson, and the young person would impart it to their peers.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109_54-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109-54">&#91;54&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His parish priest said of Acutis that
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>Carlo was a young man who was exceptionally transparent. He really wanted to progress in loving his parent's, God, his classmates, and those who loved him less. He wanted to apply himself in his studies to educate himself in his catechism class as well as in school and computer science.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202170_55-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202170-55">&#91;55&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sacraments">Sacraments</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9" title="Edit section: Sacraments">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On June 16, 1998, Acutis receives his <a href="/wiki/First_Communion" title="First Communion">First Communion</a> at the age of seven at the <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a> of <a href="/wiki/Sant%27Ambrogio_ad_Nemus,_Milan" title="Sant&#39;Ambrogio ad Nemus, Milan">Sant'Ambrogio ad Nemus</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202191,_191_56-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202191,_191-56">&#91;56&#93;</a></sup> After that he made an effort, either before or after Mass, to reflect before the <a href="/wiki/Church_tabernacle" title="Church tabernacle">tabernacle</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021_57-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021-57">&#91;57&#93;</a></sup> Acutis became a frequent communicant, would often attend <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_Adoration" class="mw-redirect" title="Eucharistic Adoration">Eucharistic Adoration</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021100_26-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021100-26">&#91;26&#93;</a></sup> and would make a weekly <a href="/wiki/Sacrament_of_Penance" title="Sacrament of Penance">confession</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202170_55-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202170-55">&#91;55&#93;</a></sup> He was confirmed on May 24, 2003, at Saint-Marie Secrete Church.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021192_58-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021192-58">&#91;58&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Pilgrimages">Pilgrimages</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: Pilgrimages">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>While in <a href="/wiki/Meteora" title="Meteora">Meteora</a>, Acutis visited an <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Orthodox">Eastern Orthodox</a> church, met <a href="/wiki/Hieromonk" title="Hieromonk">hieromonks</a>, and watched the faithful kiss the icons on the <a href="/wiki/Iconostasis" title="Iconostasis">iconostasis</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202161_59-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202161-59">&#91;59&#93;</a></sup> Acutis visited <a href="/wiki/Sanctuary_of_Our_Lady_of_Lourdes" title="Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes">Lourdes</a> for the first time in February 2005.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202161_59-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202161-59">&#91;59&#93;</a></sup> The following year, in February 2006, he traveled to <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_F%C3%A1tima" title="Our Lady of Fátima">Fátima</a> for the final time.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202161_59-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202161-59">&#91;59&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His father once proposed that the family visit the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Land" title="Holy Land">Holy Land</a>, but Acutis responded that he would rather stay in Milan: "Why go to where Jesus was 2,000 years ago, whereas He is here now?"<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021104_60-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021104-60">&#91;60&#93;</a></sup> While he wanted to visit the Holy Land, he instead decided to make a pilgrimage to each of the churches in Milan, seeing as many of them were often empty.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202173_29-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202173-29">&#91;29&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He attended the <a href="/wiki/Rimini_Meeting" title="Rimini Meeting">Rimini Meeting</a> in 2002 with his parents.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021127_61-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021127-61">&#91;61&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Devotion_to_saints">Devotion to saints</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=11" title="Edit section: Devotion to saints">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He is said to have had several models as guides for his life, especially <a href="/wiki/Francis_of_Assisi" title="Francis of Assisi">Francis of Assisi</a>, as well as <a href="/wiki/Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto" title="Francisco and Jacinta Marto">Francisco and Jacinta Marto</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dominic_Savio" title="Dominic Savio">Dominic Savio</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tarcisius" title="Tarcisius">Tarcisius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bernadette_Soubirous" title="Bernadette Soubirous">Bernadette Soubirous</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Mary_Magdalene_de%27_Pazzi" title="Mary Magdalene de&#39; Pazzi">Mary Magdalene de' Pazzi</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202198_62-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202198-62">&#91;62&#93;</a></sup> He would often visit church alone.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202150_36-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202150-36">&#91;36&#93;</a></sup> He would frequently pray to his guardian angel and had a special devotion to <a href="/wiki/St._Michael_the_Archangel" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Michael the Archangel">St. Michael the Archangel</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021189_63-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021189-63">&#91;63&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Websites">Websites</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=12" title="Edit section: Websites">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Those around him considered him a "<a href="/wiki/Computer_geek" class="mw-redirect" title="Computer geek">computer geek</a>" on account of his passion and skill with computers and the internet.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was skilled in using <a href="/wiki/Dreamweaver" class="mw-redirect" title="Dreamweaver">Dreamweaver</a>, <a href="/wiki/Java_(programming_language)" title="Java (programming language)">Java</a>, <a href="/wiki/C%2B%2B" title="C++">C++</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Ubuntu" title="Ubuntu">Ubuntu</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021124_64-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021124-64">&#91;64&#93;</a></sup> He often helped others with technical issues.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021132-3_65-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021132-3-65">&#91;65&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When he was 14, his parish priest asked him to create a webpage for his parish, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Santa_Maria_Segreta&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Santa Maria Segreta (page does not exist)">Santa Maria Segreta</a> in Milan.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021124_64-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021124-64">&#91;64&#93;</a></sup> After this, a priest at his high school asked him to create a website to promote volunteering, on which Acutis spent most of the summer of 2006 working.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126_66-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126-66">&#91;66&#93;</a></sup> For this work, he won a national competition named "Savrai volontario" (You will be a volunteer).<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126_66-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126-66">&#91;66&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Eucharistic_miracles">Eucharistic miracles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=13" title="Edit section: Eucharistic miracles">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Having seen the need for a new way to transmit the faith to a younger generation,<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109_54-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021109-54">&#91;54&#93;</a></sup> Acutis applied himself to creating a website dedicated to cataloging each reported <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_miracle" title="Eucharistic miracle">Eucharistic miracle</a> in the world. He admired <a href="/wiki/Giacomo_Alberione" class="mw-redirect" title="Giacomo Alberione">Giacomo Alberione</a>'s initiatives to use the media to evangelize and proclaim the <a href="/wiki/Gospel" title="Gospel">Gospel</a> and aimed to do likewise with the website he had created.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (April 2023)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>Acutis was inspired by the Italian designs of <a href="/wiki/Lancia" title="Lancia">Lancia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ferrari" title="Ferrari">Ferrari</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Maserati" title="Maserati">Maserati</a>, which are works of art in addition to being automobiles.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126_66-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126-66">&#91;66&#93;</a></sup> After attending the Rimini Meeting, which took place not far from the factories that produce the cars, he began to wonder why the Church could not present Christ and the Church better.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126_66-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021126-66">&#91;66&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Acutis launched the website in 2004<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021192_58-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021192-58">&#91;58&#93;</a></sup> and worked on it for two and a half years, involving his entire family in the project.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128_67-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128-67">&#91;67&#93;</a></sup> It was unveiled on October 4, 2006, the Feast of St. Francis, only days before his death.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128_67-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128-67">&#91;67&#93;</a></sup> Acutis was not able to attend the debut of his exhibition at Rome's <a href="/wiki/San_Carlo_al_Corso" title="San Carlo al Corso">Church of San Carlo Borromeo</a> as he was hospitalized.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021129_68-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021129-68">&#91;68&#93;</a></sup> It was also presented at his high school, the Leo XIII Institute.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128_67-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128-67">&#91;67&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Final_days">Final days</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=14" title="Edit section: Final days">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Illness">Illness</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=15" title="Edit section: Illness">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On October 1, 2006, Acutis developed an inflammation of the throat.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021160_69-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021160-69">&#91;69&#93;</a></sup> His parents brought him to a doctor who diagnosed him with <a href="/wiki/Parotitis" title="Parotitis">parotitis</a> and dehydration, which a second doctor, a family friend, confirmed.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021160_69-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021160-69">&#91;69&#93;</a></sup> A few days later, the pain became worse and there was blood in his urine.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161_70-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161-70">&#91;70&#93;</a></sup> By October 8, a Sunday, Acutis was too weak to get out of bed to go to Mass.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161_70-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161-70">&#91;70&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Acutis was brought to a clinic that specialized in blood diseases and was diagnosed him with <a href="/wiki/Acute_promyelocytic_leukemia" title="Acute promyelocytic leukemia">acute promyelocytic leukemia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161_70-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161-70">&#91;70&#93;</a></sup> He was given almost no chance of recovery.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021162_71-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021162-71">&#91;71&#93;</a></sup> He was rushed to the intensive care unit and given a respirator mask to assist with his breathing.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021163_72-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021163-72">&#91;72&#93;</a></sup> After a sleepless night, Acutis was transported to San Gerardo Hospital north of Milan, one of only three hospitals in Italy that was equipped to treat his illness.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021163_72-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021163-72">&#91;72&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The hospital chaplain was called in and performed the <a href="/wiki/Anointing_of_the_sick" title="Anointing of the sick">anointing of the sick</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021164_73-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021164-73">&#91;73&#93;</a></sup> When a nurse came in to care for him, he asked her not to wake his parents as they were already very tired and he did not want to worry them more.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021164_73-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021164-73">&#91;73&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Acutis <a href="/wiki/Acts_of_reparation" title="Acts of reparation">offered his suffering</a> both for <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI" title="Pope Benedict XVI">Pope Benedict XVI</a> and for the Catholic Church, saying: "I offer to the Lord the sufferings that I will have to undergo for the Pope and for the Church."<sup id="cite_ref-generation_74-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-generation-74">&#91;74&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161_70-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021161-70">&#91;70&#93;</a></sup> He had asked his parents to take him on pilgrimages to the sites of all the known Eucharistic miracles in the world, but his declining health prevented this from happening.
</p><p>The doctors treating his final illness had asked him if he was in great pain, a question to which he responded that "there are people who suffer much more than me".<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021164_73-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021164-73">&#91;73&#93;</a></sup> His final words to his mother were
</p>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r996844942"><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>Mom, don't be afraid. Since Jesus has became a man, death has become the passage towards life, and we don't need to flee it. Let us prepare ourselves to experience something extraordinary in the eternal life.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021152_75-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021152-75">&#91;75&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Death_and_burial">Death and burial</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=16" title="Edit section: Death and burial">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Acutis fell into a coma and was rushed to the intensive care unit where he underwent a treatment to cleanse his blood.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021165_76-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021165-76">&#91;76&#93;</a></sup> He then underwent a <a href="/wiki/Cerebral_hemorrhage" class="mw-redirect" title="Cerebral hemorrhage">cerebral hemorrhage</a> and was pronounced brain dead on October 11.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021165_76-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021165-76">&#91;76&#93;</a></sup> Acutis died on October 12, 2006, at 6:45&#160;p.m.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021165_76-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021165-76">&#91;76&#93;</a></sup> He was 15 years old at the time of his death.
</p><p>After the mortuary cleaned his body, Acutis' parents brought his body home where a flood of people came over the next four days to pay their last respects.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021166-7_77-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021166-7-77">&#91;77&#93;</a></sup> Acutis's body had the <a href="/wiki/Odor_of_sanctity" class="mw-redirect" title="Odor of sanctity">odor of sanctity</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021166_78-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021166-78">&#91;78&#93;</a></sup> At his funeral, a crowd of strangers attended.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021167_79-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021167-79">&#91;79&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Many young people who had fallen away from the church returned for his funeral, and then for a memorial mass three months later.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021168_80-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021168-80">&#91;80&#93;</a></sup> The Friends of Carlo Acutis was formed to organize annual <a href="/wiki/Requiem_mass" class="mw-redirect" title="Requiem mass">requiem masses</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021168_80-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021168-80">&#91;80&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>At his funeral was a woman with a cancerous tumor.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188_35-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188-35">&#91;35&#93;</a></sup> A few days after the funeral, her doctor told her the tumor had disappeared.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188_35-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188-35">&#91;35&#93;</a></sup> Also at the funeral was a 44-year-old woman who wanted to have children, but was not able to give birth.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188_35-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188-35">&#91;35&#93;</a></sup> Nine months after the funeral, she had a child.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188_35-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188-35">&#91;35&#93;</a></sup> Both women attribute their miracles to Acutis' intercession.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188_35-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021188-35">&#91;35&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Exhumation_and_removals">Exhumation and removals</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=17" title="Edit section: Exhumation and removals">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>It was Acutis' final wish to die and be buried in Assisi.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021175_81-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021175-81">&#91;81&#93;</a></sup> A year after his death, his body was transferred to the Assisi cemetery in accordance with his wishes.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-last_82-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-last-82">&#91;82&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On April 6, 2018, his body was brought to <a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Maggiore,_Assisi" title="Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi">the Sanctuary of the Spoliation</a> so that the faithful could visit his final resting spot and venerate his body.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021175-6_83-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021175-6-83">&#91;83&#93;</a></sup> The procession began on Friday night and ended on Saturday morning, traveling a distance of 1.5 miles.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021176_84-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021176-84">&#91;84&#93;</a></sup> Overnight, the procession stopped at the <a href="/wiki/Cathedral_of_San_Rufino" class="mw-redirect" title="Cathedral of San Rufino">Cathedral of San Rufino</a> where the diocesan choir sang a <i>Non io, ma Dio,</i> ("Not me, but God"), a hymn especially composed for the occasion by <a href="/w/index.php?title=Marco_Mammoli&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Marco Mammoli (page does not exist)">Marco Mammoli</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021176-7_85-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021176-7-85">&#91;85&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On June 23, 2018, his body was exhumed and found to be <a href="/wiki/Incorruptibility" title="Incorruptibility">uncorrupted</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021168_80-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021168-80">&#91;80&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=18" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Following the Catholic Church's recognition of a miracle in 2020, attributed to Acutis, Antonia told the press that her son had appeared to her in dreams saying that he will not only be beatified but also canonized a saint in the future.<sup id="cite_ref-beato_86-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-beato-86">&#91;86&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>There is a website for his canonization cause, another for educators, young people, and prayer groups, and sites for each of the four exhibitions that he inspired.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021131_87-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021131-87">&#91;87&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Photo_exhibition_of_eucharistic_miracles">Photo exhibition of eucharistic miracles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=19" title="Edit section: Photo exhibition of eucharistic miracles">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In memory of Acutis, bishops <a href="/wiki/Raffaello_Martinelli" title="Raffaello Martinelli">Raffaello Martinelli</a> and <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Comastri" title="Angelo Comastri">Angelo Comastri</a> have helped to organize a traveling photo exhibition of all the Eucharistic miracle sites. It has since traveled to dozens of different countries across five continents.<sup id="cite_ref-photo_88-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-photo-88">&#91;88&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The preface to the print version of the exhibit was written by Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Comastri" title="Angelo Comastri">Angelo Comastri</a><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128_67-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021128-67">&#91;67&#93;</a></sup> and has been translated into 18 languages.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021130_89-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021130-89">&#91;89&#93;</a></sup> It has traveled to more than 10,000 places, including churches, congressional palaces, youth clubs, and welcome centers, in addition to the canonization of <a href="/wiki/Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto" title="Francisco and Jacinta Marto">Francisco and Jacinta Marto</a> in <a href="/wiki/Fatima,_Portugal" class="mw-redirect" title="Fatima, Portugal">Fatima, Portugal</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021130_89-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021130-89">&#91;89&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Parish_of_Blessed_Carlo_Acutis">Parish of Blessed Carlo Acutis</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=20" title="Edit section: Parish of Blessed Carlo Acutis">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On 15 December 2020, under the provisions of Canon Law, Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Bernard_Longley" title="Bernard Longley">Bernard Longley</a> established a new parish in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Birmingham" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Birmingham">Archdiocese of Birmingham</a> under the patronage of the Blessed Carlo Acutis.<sup id="cite_ref-decree_90-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-decree-90">&#91;90&#93;</a></sup> The parish incorporates the three churches of <a href="/wiki/St_Peter_and_St_Paul%27s_Church,_Wolverhampton" title="St Peter and St Paul&#39;s Church, Wolverhampton">St Peter &amp; Paul</a>, St Michael and St Bernadette in <a href="/wiki/Wolverhampton" title="Wolverhampton">Wolverhampton</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-parish_91-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-parish-91">&#91;91&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-feast_92-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-feast-92">&#91;92&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In July 2021, the parishes of St. John Berchmans in <a href="/wiki/Logan_Square,_Chicago" title="Logan Square, Chicago">Logan Square</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chicago" title="Chicago">Chicago</a>, and St. Hedwig in Bucktown combined under the patronage of Blessed Carlo Acutis.<sup id="cite_ref-history_93-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-history-93">&#91;93&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Biographies">Biographies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=21" title="Edit section: Biographies">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Several biographies of Acutis were written following his death. One, <i>The Eucharist: My Highway to Heaven</i> was written by <a href="/w/index.php?title=Nicola_Gori&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Nicola Gori (page does not exist)">Nicola Gori</a>, a reporter at <a href="/wiki/L%27Osservatore_Romano" title="L&#39;Osservatore Romano">L'Osservatore Romano</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021169_94-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021169-94">&#91;94&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other">Other</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=22" title="Edit section: Other">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In April 2022, the first life-sized statue of Acutis in the United Kingdom was erected at <a href="/wiki/Carfin_Grotto" title="Carfin Grotto">Carfin Grotto</a>, <a href="/wiki/North_Lanarkshire" title="North Lanarkshire">North Lanarkshire</a>, <a href="/wiki/Scotland" title="Scotland">Scotland</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-statue_95-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-statue-95">&#91;95&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-grotto_96-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grotto-96">&#91;96&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Acutis is quoted as saying that "We are all born originals, but many of us die as photocopies."<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202150_36-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202150-36">&#91;36&#93;</a></sup> In the document that concluded the <a href="/wiki/Synod_on_Young_People" class="mw-redirect" title="Synod on Young People">Synod on Young People</a> in 2018, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> used the phrase and praised Acutis thusly:
</p>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r996844942"><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>Carlo did not fall into a trap. He saw that many young people, if they seem to be different, end up, in reality, looking like each other, by running behind what powerful people impose on them via mechanisms of consumption and stupor. In this way, they do not let the gifts that the Lord has made for them flow into them. They do not offer the world these personal and unique gifts that the Lord has sown in each one of them.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer202151-2_97-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer202151-2-97">&#91;97&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<p>Pope Francis has also spoken about Acutis as a model for people who live normal lives to grow in holiness.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021171_98-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021171-98">&#91;98&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=23" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Assisi,_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG/220px-Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="165" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG/330px-Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG/440px-Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG 2x" data-file-width="2816" data-file-height="2112" /></a><figcaption><a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Maggiore,_Assisi" title="Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi">Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi</a>, Acutis's burial place</figcaption></figure>
<p>The call for him to be beatified began not long after Acutis's death.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> On October 12, 2012, the sixth anniversary of his death, the <a href="/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Milan" class="mw-redirect" title="Archdiocese of Milan">Archdiocese of Milan</a> opened the <a href="/wiki/Cause_for_canonization" class="mw-redirect" title="Cause for canonization">cause for canonization</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021170_99-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021170-99">&#91;99&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The campaign gained momentum on May 13, 2013, when the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> issued a <a href="/wiki/Nihil_obstat" title="Nihil obstat">nihil obstat</a> stating there was nothing preventing the cause from moving forward.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021171_98-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021171-98">&#91;98&#93;</a></sup> He was then named a <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a>, the first stage on the path towards sainthood.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CS_100-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-100">&#91;100&#93;</a></sup>  The <a href="/wiki/Lombardy" title="Lombardy">Lombardy</a> Episcopal Conference approved the petition for the official canonization cause to proceed at a meeting in 2013.<sup id="cite_ref-CS_100-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-100">&#91;100&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The opening of the diocesan investigation was held on 15 February 2013, with Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Scola" title="Angelo Scola">Angelo Scola</a> inaugurating the process, and concluding it on 24 November 2016.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021174_101-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021174-101">&#91;101&#93;</a></sup> Scola said Acutis was not called to be "a movie star, but a star in Heaven" and that Acutis is "a new treasure in the Ambrosian church."<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021174_101-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021174-101">&#91;101&#93;</a></sup> The formal introduction to the cause occurred on 13 May 2013, and Acutis became titled a "Servant of God". <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> next confirmed his life as one of <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a> on 5 July 2018, and declared him <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-patron_102-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-102">&#91;102&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEConquer2021178_103-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEConquer2021178-103">&#91;103&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 14 November 2019, the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Vatican's</a> Medical Council of the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> expressed a positive opinion about a miracle in <a href="/wiki/Brazil" title="Brazil">Brazil</a> attributed to Acutis's intercession.<sup id="cite_ref-filipe_104-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-filipe-104">&#91;104&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-whiz_105-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-whiz-105">&#91;105&#93;</a></sup> Luciana Vianna had taken her son, Mattheus, who was born with a pancreatic defect that made eating difficult, to a prayer service. Beforehand, Vianna had already prayed a <a href="/wiki/Novena" title="Novena">novena</a> asking for the teenager Acutis's intercession. During the service, her son had asked that he should not "throw up as much". Immediately following the service, Mattheus told his mother that he felt healed and asked for solid food when he came home. Until then he had been on an all-liquid diet.<sup id="cite_ref-Miracle_106-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Miracle-106">&#91;106&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-osv_107-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-osv-107">&#91;107&#93;</a></sup> After a detailed investigation, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> confirmed the miracle's authenticity in a decree on 21 February 2020, leading to Acutis's <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a>.
</p><p>Within a month of the decree, the beatification ceremony was postponed due to the <a href="/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Italy" title="COVID-19 pandemic in Italy">COVID-19 pandemic in Italy</a>, during which the country was placed on lockdown. It was rescheduled for 10 October 2020 and was held in the Upper Church of the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Saint_Francis_of_Assisi" title="Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi">Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi</a> in Assisi, Italy, with Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Agostino_Vallini" title="Agostino Vallini">Agostino Vallini</a> presiding on the Pope's behalf.<sup id="cite_ref-declared_108-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-declared-108">&#91;108&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-indian_109-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-indian-109">&#91;109&#93;</a></sup> As of 2019<sup class="plainlinks noexcerpt noprint asof-tag update" style="display:none;"><a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit">&#91;update&#93;</a></sup>, the <a href="/wiki/Postulator" title="Postulator">postulator</a> for Acutis's cause is Nicola Gori.<sup id="cite_ref-CS_100-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-100">&#91;100&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Venerable_110-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Venerable-110">&#91;110&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Since the beatification ceremony on 10 October 2020, silent crowds have been filing past the exposed relics of the blessed youth in the one-time cathedral of Assisi, the church of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Maggiore,_Assisi" title="Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi">Santa Maria Maggiore</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-lucie_111-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-lucie-111">&#91;111&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
