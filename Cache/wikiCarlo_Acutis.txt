<p><b>Carlo Acutis</b> (3 May 1991 – 12 October 2006) was an English-born Italian <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> youth and amateur computer programmer,<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> who is best known for documenting <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_miracle" title="Eucharistic miracle">Eucharistic miracles</a> around the world and cataloguing them onto a <a href="/wiki/Website" title="Website">website</a> which he created before his death from <a href="/wiki/Leukemia" title="Leukemia">leukemia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was noted for his cheerfulness, computer skills, and deep devotion to the <a href="/wiki/Eucharist" title="Eucharist">Eucharist</a>, which became a core theme of his life.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on 10 October 2020, two days before the 14th anniversary of his death.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Carlo Acutis was born in <a href="/wiki/London,_England" class="mw-redirect" title="London, England">London, England</a>, on 3 May 1991 to a wealthy Italian family.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> His baptism took place on 18 May 1991 in the church of <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Dolours,_Chelsea" title="Our Lady of Dolours, Chelsea">Our Lady of Dolours, Chelsea</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> His parents, Andrea Acutis and Antonia Salzano, who were not especially religious, had worked in London and Germany, finally settled in <a href="/wiki/Milan" title="Milan">Milan</a> in September 1991, not long after their first son's birth.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> In 1995, when Acutis was four years old, his maternal grandfather died and was said to have appeared to him in a dream asking to be prayed for. When the child displayed an interest in Catholic religious practice, his questions were answered by the family's Polish babysitter.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> Three years later he requested to receive his <a href="/wiki/First_Communion" title="First Communion">First Communion</a> at the age of seven. After consulting a prelate and providing instruction, the family arranged this at the <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a> of <a href="/wiki/Sant%27Ambrogio_ad_Nemus,_Milan" title="Sant&#39;Ambrogio ad Nemus, Milan">Sant'Ambrogio ad Nemus</a>. After that he made the effort, either before or after Mass, to reflect before the <a href="/wiki/Church_tabernacle" title="Church tabernacle">tabernacle</a>. Acutis became a frequent communicant and would make a weekly <a href="/wiki/Sacrament_of_Penance" title="Sacrament of Penance">confession</a>. He is said to have had several models as guides for his life, especially <a href="/wiki/Francis_of_Assisi" title="Francis of Assisi">Francis of Assisi</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> as well as <a href="/wiki/Francisco_and_Jacinta_Marto" title="Francisco and Jacinta Marto">Francisco and Jacinta Marto</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dominic_Savio" title="Dominic Savio">Dominic Savio</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tarcisius" title="Tarcisius">Tarcisius</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Bernadette_Soubirous" title="Bernadette Soubirous">Bernadette Soubirous</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was educated in Milan at the <a href="/wiki/Jesuits" title="Jesuits">Jesuit</a> <a href="/wiki/Leo_XIII_Institute,_Milan" title="Leo XIII Institute, Milan">Instituto Leone XIII</a> high school. On the social side, Acutis would worry about friends of his whose parents were divorcing and would invite them to his home to support them. He defended disabled peers at school when bullies mocked them. Outside school, he did volunteer work with the homeless and destitute. He also liked films, comic editing and playing <a href="/wiki/PlayStation" title="PlayStation">PlayStation</a> video games. Although he greatly enjoyed travel, the town of <a href="/wiki/Assisi" title="Assisi">Assisi</a> remained a particular favorite.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Those around him considered him a "<a href="/wiki/Computer_geek" class="mw-redirect" title="Computer geek">computer geek</a>" on account of his passion and skill with computers and the internet.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Acutis applied himself to creating a website dedicated to cataloguing each reported <a href="/wiki/Eucharistic_miracle" title="Eucharistic miracle">Eucharistic miracle</a> in the world. He completed this in 2005, having started compiling the catalogue at the age of eleven. He admired <a href="/wiki/Giacomo_Alberione" class="mw-redirect" title="Giacomo Alberione">Giacomo Alberione</a>'s initiatives to use the media to evangelize and proclaim the <a href="/wiki/Gospel" title="Gospel">Gospel</a> and aimed to do likewise with the website he had created. 
</p><p>When Carlo developed <a href="/wiki/Leukemia" title="Leukemia">leukemia</a>, he offered his suffering both for <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI" title="Pope Benedict XVI">Pope Benedict XVI</a> and for the Catholic Church, saying: "I offer to the Lord the sufferings that I will have to undergo for the Pope and for the Church."<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> He had asked his parents to take him on pilgrimages to the sites of all the known Eucharistic miracles in the world, but his declining health prevented this from happening.
The doctors treating Carlo's final illness had asked him if he was in great pain, a question to which he responded that "there are people who suffer much more than me".<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Carlo died on 12 October 2006 at 06:45 p.m. He was 15 years old at the time of his death. He was buried in Assisi in accordance with his wishes.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Acutis's mother, Antonia, is said to attribute to his intercession the fact that, at the age of 44, she gave birth to twins, born exactly four years to the day after his death.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup> Following the Catholic Church's recognition of a miracle in 2020, attributed to Acutis, Antonia told the press that her son had appeared to her in dreams saying that he will not only be beatified but also canonised a saint in the future.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Photo_exhibition_of_eucharistic_miracles">Photo exhibition of eucharistic miracles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Photo exhibition of eucharistic miracles">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In memory of Acutis, bishops <a href="/wiki/Raffaello_Martinelli" title="Raffaello Martinelli">Raffaello Martinelli</a> and <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Comastri" title="Angelo Comastri">Angelo Comastri</a> have helped to organize a travelling photo exhibition of all the Eucharistic miracle sites. It has since travelled to dozens of different countries across five continents.<sup id="cite_ref-293fI_15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-293fI-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Parish_of_Blessed_Carlo_Acutis">Parish of Blessed Carlo Acutis</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Parish of Blessed Carlo Acutis">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On 15 December 2020, under the provisions of Canon Law, Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Bernard_Longley" title="Bernard Longley">Bernard</a> established a new parish in the Archdiocese of Birmingham under the patronage of the Blessed Carlo Acutis.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup> The parish incorporates the three churches of <a href="/wiki/St_Peter_and_St_Paul%27s_Church,_Wolverhampton" title="St Peter and St Paul&#39;s Church, Wolverhampton">St Peter &amp; Paul</a>, St Michael and St Bernadette in <a href="/wiki/Wolverhampton" title="Wolverhampton">Wolverhampton</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> <sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In July 2021, the parishes of St. John Berchmans in Logan Square, Chicago, and St. Hedwig in Bucktown combined under the patronage of Blessed Carlo Acutis.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other">Other</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Other">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In April 2022, the first life-sized statue of Acutis in the United Kingdom was erected at <a href="/wiki/Carfin_Grotto" title="Carfin Grotto">Carfin Grotto</a>, <a href="/wiki/North_Lanarkshire" title="North Lanarkshire">North Lanarkshire</a>, Scotland.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">&#91;20&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Assisi,_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG/220px-Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="165" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG/330px-Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ac/Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG/440px-Assisi%2C_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG 2x" data-file-width="2816" data-file-height="2112" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Assisi,_santa_maria_maggiore.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Maggiore,_Assisi" title="Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi">Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi</a>, Acutis's burial place</div></div></div>
<p>The call for him to be beatified began not long after Acutis's death.<sup id="cite_ref-CN_4-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> The campaign gained momentum in 2013 after he was named a <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a>, the first stage on the path towards sainthood.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_3-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CS_21-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-21">&#91;21&#93;</a></sup>  The <a href="/wiki/Lombardy" title="Lombardy">Lombardy</a> Episcopal Conference approved the petition for the official canonization cause to proceed at a meeting in 2013.<sup id="cite_ref-CS_21-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-21">&#91;21&#93;</a></sup> The opening of the diocesan investigation was held on 15 February 2013, with Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Angelo_Scola" title="Angelo Scola">Angelo Scola</a> inaugurating the process, and concluding it on 24 November 2016. The formal introduction to the cause took place on 13 May 2013, and Acutis became titled a "Servant of God". <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> next confirmed his life as one of <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a> on 5 July 2018, and declared him <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-kdu53_22-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kdu53-22">&#91;22&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>On 14 November 2019, the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Vatican's</a> Medical Council of the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> expressed a positive opinion about a miracle in <a href="/wiki/Brazil" title="Brazil">Brazil</a> attributed to Acutis's intercession.<sup id="cite_ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23">&#91;23&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24">&#91;24&#93;</a></sup> Luciana Vianna had taken her son, Mattheus, who was born with a pancreatic defect that made eating difficult, to a prayer service. Beforehand, Vianna had already prayed a <a href="/wiki/Novena" title="Novena">novena</a> asking for the teenager Acutis's intercession. During the service her son had simply asked that he should not "throw up as much". Immediately following the service, Mattheus told his mother that he felt healed and asked for solid food when he came home. Until then he had been on an all-liquid diet.<sup id="cite_ref-Miracle_25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Miracle-25">&#91;25&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-With_26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-With-26">&#91;26&#93;</a></sup> After a detailed investigation, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> confirmed the miracle's authenticity in a decree on 21 February 2020, leading to Acutis's <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a>. 
</p><p>Within a month of the decree, the beatification ceremony was postponed due to the <a href="/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Italy" title="COVID-19 pandemic in Italy">COVID-19 pandemic in Italy</a>, during which the country was placed on lockdown. It was rescheduled for 10 October 2020 and was held in the Upper Church of the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Saint_Francis_of_Assisi" title="Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi">Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi</a> in Assisi, Italy, with Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Agostino_Vallini" title="Agostino Vallini">Agostino Vallini</a> presiding on the Pope's behalf.<sup id="cite_ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27">&#91;27&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-indian_28-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-indian-28">&#91;28&#93;</a></sup> As of 2019<sup class="plainlinks noexcerpt noprint asof-tag update" style="display:none;"><a class="external text" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Carlo_Acutis&amp;action=edit">&#91;update&#93;</a></sup>, the <a href="/wiki/Postulator" title="Postulator">postulator</a> for Acutis's cause is Nicola Gori.<sup id="cite_ref-CS_21-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CS-21">&#91;21&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Venerable_29-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Venerable-29">&#91;29&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Since the beatification ceremony on 10 October 2020, silent crowds have been filing past the exposed relics of the blessed youth in the one-time cathedral of Assisi, the church of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Maria_Maggiore,_Assisi" title="Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi">Santa Maria Maggiore</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30">&#91;30&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
