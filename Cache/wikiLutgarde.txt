					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Luitgard</b> is a German female name.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Origin">Origin</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Luitgard&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Origin">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The name comes from <a href="/wiki/Old_High_German" title="Old High German">Old High German</a> and means "[female] guardian of the people"  (German: <i>Beschützerin des Volks</i>). This derives, in its older form, <i>Liutgard</i>, from <i>liut</i> which means "people" (Modern German: <i>Leute</i>), "member of a people",<sup id="cite_ref-Grimm1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grimm1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and <i>gard</i> which means "protection" or "guardianship", from which the German word <i>Garten</i> and the English word "garden" are also derived.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Name_day">Name day</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Luitgard&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Name day">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Its <a href="/wiki/Name_day" title="Name day">name day</a> is 16 October, the same date as that of the Blessed <a href="/wiki/Luitgard_of_Wittichen" title="Luitgard of Wittichen">Luitgard of Wittichen</a>.
</p>
