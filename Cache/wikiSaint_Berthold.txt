					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Berthold of Mount Carmel</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Le bienheureux Berthold du Mont Carmel.jpg" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg/220px-Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="293" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg/330px-Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg/440px-Le_bienheureux_Berthold_du_Mont_Carmel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="500" data-file-height="666" /></a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">Limoges, France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">1195 AD</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">March 29</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Berthold of Calabria</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">Berthold de Malifaye</i>; <a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Bertoldus Calabriensis</i>; died 1195) was a <a href="/wiki/Calabria" title="Calabria">Calabrian</a> <a href="/wiki/Crusades" title="Crusades">crusader</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> who established a hermit colony on <a href="/wiki/Mount_Carmel" title="Mount Carmel">Mount Carmel</a> in 1185.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was introduced into <a href="/wiki/Carmelite" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite">Carmelite</a> literature around the 15th century as <b>Saint Berthold of Mount Carmel</b> and is said to have been a general of the Order before <a href="/wiki/Saint_Brocard" title="Saint Brocard">Brocard</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-staring_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-staring-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Berthold was a son of the Count of <a href="/wiki/Limoges" title="Limoges">Limoges</a> and was born in Malifaye in southwest France. The label "Calabrian" was a contemporary euphemism for "Westerner."<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Berthold was a nephew of <a href="/wiki/Aymeric_of_Malifaye" class="mw-redirect" title="Aymeric of Malifaye">Aymeric of Malifaye</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Latin_Patriarchate_of_Antioch" title="Latin Patriarchate of Antioch">Latin patriarch of Antioch</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-stevens_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stevens-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Berthold went to the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Land" title="Holy Land">Holy Land</a> as part of the <a href="/wiki/Crusades" title="Crusades">Crusades</a> and was in <a href="/wiki/Antioch" title="Antioch">Antioch</a> when it was besieged by the <a href="/wiki/Saracens" class="mw-redirect" title="Saracens">Saracens</a>. During this time he had a vision of <a href="/wiki/Christ" class="mw-redirect" title="Christ">Christ</a> denouncing the soldiers' evil methods. At the time, <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermits</a> from the West were scattered throughout <a href="/wiki/Palestine_(region)" title="Palestine (region)">Palestine</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-stevens_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stevens-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Some accounts hold that in 1185 he came to Mount Carmel, built a small chapel there and gathered a community of hermits who would live at his side in imitation of the prophet <a href="/wiki/Elijah" title="Elijah">Elijah</a>. This community was believed to have given rise to the <a href="/wiki/Carmelites" title="Carmelites">Order of the Carmelites</a>, but this is not supported by evidence and is discounted by historians of the Order. Berthold lived out his days on Mount Carmel, ruling the community he had founded for forty-five years until his death in 1195.<sup id="cite_ref-stevens_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stevens-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Tradition holds that he was accepted as leader of the hermits by Brocard.  His feast day is celebrated on March 29.
</p>
