					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Mykola Konrad</b> (<a href="/wiki/Ukrainian_language" title="Ukrainian language">Ukrainian</a>: <span lang="uk">Микола Конрад</span>) was a <a href="/wiki/Ukrainian_Greek_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Ukrainian Greek Catholic">Ukrainian Greek Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> who became a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> in 1941.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mykola_Konrad&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Конрад Микола.jpg" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/e/e2/%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg/220px-%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="314" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/e/e2/%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg/330px-%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/e/e2/%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg 2x" data-file-width="420" data-file-height="600" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:%D0%9A%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B4_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%B0.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div></div></div></div>
<p>Konrad was born on 16 May 1876 in the village of Strusiv in <a href="/wiki/Ternopil_Oblast" title="Ternopil Oblast">Ternopil Region</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-v_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  He studied philosophy and theology in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, where he defended his <a href="/wiki/Dissertation" class="mw-redirect" title="Dissertation">dissertation</a> and received his <a href="/wiki/Doctorate" title="Doctorate">doctorate</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-u_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-u-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> a priest in 1899.<sup id="cite_ref-b_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He taught for a time in <a href="/wiki/High_schools" class="mw-redirect" title="High schools">high schools</a> in <a href="/wiki/Berezhany" title="Berezhany">Berezhany</a> and <a href="/wiki/Terebovlya" class="mw-redirect" title="Terebovlya">Terebovlya</a>. In 1929 he founded Obnova ("Renewal") the first communion of Ukrainian Catholic students,<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> and in 1930 he was asked by <a href="/wiki/Metropolitan_bishop" title="Metropolitan bishop">Metropolitan</a> <a href="/wiki/Andriy_Sheptytsky" class="mw-redirect" title="Andriy Sheptytsky">Andriy Sheptytsky</a> to teach at the Lviv Theological Academy.<sup id="cite_ref-u_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-u-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He was then appointed <a href="/wiki/Parish" title="Parish">parish</a> priest in the village of Stradch.<sup id="cite_ref-v_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 26 June 1941 he was murdered along with <a href="/wiki/Volodymyr_Pryjma" title="Volodymyr Pryjma">Volodymyr Pryjma</a>, in a forest near Stradch as they were returning from the house of a sick woman who had requested the <a href="/wiki/Sacrament_of_reconciliation" class="mw-redirect" title="Sacrament of reconciliation">sacrament of reconciliation</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-v_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-u_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-u-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-b_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-b-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> on 27 June 2001.<sup id="cite_ref-v_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-v-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Influence">Influence</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mykola_Konrad&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Influence">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In an interview, Mykola Markevych said, "Doctor Konrad, a professor at the Academy, my catechist... O, he was a distinguished person. An ideal man. He was very involved with youth; he had a heart for youth- and for his people. He wanted  us to be patriots, good and aware students. That was Father Konrad."<sup id="cite_ref-:0_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
