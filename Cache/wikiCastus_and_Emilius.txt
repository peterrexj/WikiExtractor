					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p>Saints <b>Castus and Emilius</b> (died 250 AD) are venerated as saints and martyrs by the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Orthodox">Eastern Orthodox</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> Churches.  
</p><p>They are mentioned by <a href="/wiki/Cyprian" title="Cyprian">St. Cyprian</a> as having been martyred sometime during the <a href="/wiki/Decian_persecution" title="Decian persecution">Decian persecution</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and were praised by <a href="/wiki/Augustine_of_Hippo" title="Augustine of Hippo">Augustine of Hippo</a>.  When they were imprisoned, Castus and Emilius denied that they were <a href="/wiki/Christians" title="Christians">Christians</a> under torture and were released.  When they were arrested a second time, they refused to abjure Christianity and were burned to death.  Their <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is <a href="/wiki/May_22_(Eastern_Orthodox_liturgics)" title="May 22 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)">May 22</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
