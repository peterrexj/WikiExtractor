					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Blessed</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Conrad of Ascoli</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">1234<br />Ascoli, Italy</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">19 April 1289<br />Ascoli, Italy</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data">Roman Catholicism</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">April 19</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Conrad of Ascoli</b> was an Italian <a href="/wiki/Friar_Minor" class="mw-redirect" title="Friar Minor">Friar Minor</a> and missionary; his feast day is April 19.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Conrad_of_Ascoli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Conrad was born at <a href="/wiki/Ascoli_Piceno" title="Ascoli Piceno">Ascoli</a> in the <a href="/wiki/March_of_Ancona" title="March of Ancona">March of Ancona</a> in 1234. He belonged to the noble family of Miliani and from his earliest years made <a href="/wiki/Penance" title="Penance">penance</a> the predominating element of his life. He entered the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor" title="Order of Friars Minor">Order of Friars Minor</a> at <a href="/wiki/Ascoli_Piceno" title="Ascoli Piceno">Ascoli</a> together with his townsman and lifelong friend, <a href="/wiki/Girolamo_d%27Ascoli" class="mw-redirect" title="Girolamo d&#39;Ascoli">Girolamo d'Ascoli</a>, afterwards <a href="/wiki/Minister_general" class="mw-redirect" title="Minister general">minister general</a>, and later pope under the title of <a href="/wiki/Pope_Nicholas_IV" title="Pope Nicholas IV">Nicholas IV</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Later together they went to the <a href="/wiki/Cloister" title="Cloister">cloister</a> in Assisi to complete their training in the religious life and then to <a href="/wiki/Perugia" title="Perugia">Perugia</a> for continue their education. Conrad received a doctor's degree in theology and was sent to Rome to teach.<sup id="cite_ref-donovan_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-donovan-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Conrad had a great devotion to the Holy Trinity.
</p><p>In 1274 he obtained permission to go to Africa, where he preached throughout the different provinces of <a href="/wiki/Libya" title="Libya">Libya</a>. Noted for his austerities, he was recalled from Africa, probably for reasons of health, to accompany his friend Girolamo, now papal legate, on a mission to the King of France, then at war with Spain. Conrad subsequently became lector of theology at Paris. When not engaged in teaching, Conrad preached to the people or ministered to the sick in hospitals.<sup id="cite_ref-Blessed_Conrad_of_Ascoli_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blessed_Conrad_of_Ascoli-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1288 he was summoned to Rome by the new pope, Nicholas IV, who wished to make him cardinal, but Conrad died on the way after reaching his native city on 19 April 1289, being then fifty-five years of age.<sup id="cite_ref-donovan_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-donovan-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Conrad_of_Ascoli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Nicholas IV was deeply grieved because of the loss of his saintly friend, and declared that Conrad's death was a great loss to the Catholic Church. The people of <a href="/wiki/Ascoli_Piceno" title="Ascoli Piceno">Ascoli</a> built a tomb over the remains of Conrad.
</p><p>In 1371 his body was removed to the new church of the Franciscans. But it was found incorrupt and gave forth a <a href="/wiki/Odour_of_Sanctity" class="mw-redirect" title="Odour of Sanctity">sweet odour</a>. <a href="/wiki/Pius_VI" class="mw-redirect" title="Pius VI">Pius VI</a> approved the cultus of <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Blessed</a> Conrad.<sup id="cite_ref-donovan_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-donovan-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His liturgical feast is kept in the Order of Friars Minor on 19 April.<sup id="cite_ref-Blessed_Conrad_of_Ascoli_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Blessed_Conrad_of_Ascoli-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
