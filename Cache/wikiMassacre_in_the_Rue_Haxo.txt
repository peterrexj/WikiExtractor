<p>The <b>Massacre in the Rue Haxo</b> (<a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>: <i lang="fr">le massacre de la rue Haxo</i>) was a massacre of priests and gendarmes by <a href="/wiki/Communards" title="Communards">communards</a> during the <i><a href="/wiki/Semaine_sanglante" title="Semaine sanglante">semaine sanglante</a></i> ("bloody week") at the end of the <a href="/wiki/Paris_Commune" title="Paris Commune">Paris Commune</a> in May 1871.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Massacre_in_the_Rue_Haxo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg/220px-Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="175" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg/330px-Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0f/Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg/440px-Place_of_the_massacre_of_rue_Haxo_in_May_1871_during_the_Paris_commune.jpg 2x" data-file-width="4422" data-file-height="3510" /></a><figcaption>Location of the massacre of rue Haxo (<a href="/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Atget" title="Eugène Atget">Eugène Atget</a>)</figcaption></figure>
<p>The communards associated the Catholic Church with conservatism and imperialism and enforced a separation between Church and state.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In April, the Commune had arrested some 200 clergy to serve as hostages against reprisals from the Versailles government, and to use in possible prisoner exchanges. In particular, leaders of the Commune hoped to be able to exchange the archbishop of Paris, <a href="/wiki/Georges_Darboy" title="Georges Darboy">Georges Darboy</a>, for <a href="/wiki/Louis_Auguste_Blanqui" title="Louis Auguste Blanqui">Louis Auguste Blanqui</a>, but this offer was rebuffed by <a href="/wiki/Adolphe_Thiers" title="Adolphe Thiers">Adolphe Thiers</a>, president of the <a href="/wiki/French_Third_Republic" title="French Third Republic">Third Republic</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Versailles troops entered the city on 21 May, and by 24 May had retaken much of the city. <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9ophile_Ferr%C3%A9" title="Théophile Ferré">Théophile Ferré</a> signed an order of execution for six of the hostages at <a href="/wiki/La_Roquette_Prison" class="mw-redirect" title="La Roquette Prison">la Roquette Prison</a>, specifically including the archbishop; they were executed by firing squad.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Massacre">Massacre</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Massacre_in_the_Rue_Haxo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Massacre">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On 26 May, 50 further hostages from la Roquette Prison were executed, this time publicly in rue Haxo.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 27 May, some of them attempted to escape; they were immediately killed.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In total, 110 were killed, of whom 75 were clergy and 35 were soldiers.<sup id="cite_ref-:3_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:3-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<p>A chapel was built on the site in 1894, and in 1938 the church in rue Haxo was inaugurated as Notre-Dame des Otages.<sup id="cite_ref-:2_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:2-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 25 November 2021, the <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> recognized five victims of the 26 May killings as <a href="/wiki/Christian_martyr" title="Christian martyr">martyrs</a>: Henri Planchat, Ladislas Radigue, Polycarpe Tuffier, Marcellin Rouchouze, and Frézal Tardieu.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> They were <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on April 22, 2023 by the <a href="/wiki/Cardinal_(Catholic_Church)" title="Cardinal (Catholic Church)">Cardinal</a> <a href="/wiki/Marcello_Semeraro" title="Marcello Semeraro">Marcello Semeraro</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
