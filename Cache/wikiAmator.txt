					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Catholic bishop and saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the film, see <a href="/wiki/Camera_Buff" title="Camera Buff">Camera Buff</a>.</div>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1033289096"/><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">"Saint Martha (French)" redirects here. For the legends of St Martha of Bethany in France, see <a href="/wiki/Martha#Legends" title="Martha">Martha §&#160;Legends</a>.</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Amator of Auxerre</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG" class="image"><img alt="Chapelle de Locadour - 3654.JPG" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG/220px-Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="332" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG/330px-Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e1/Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG/440px-Chapelle_de_Locadour_-_3654.JPG 2x" data-file-width="3426" data-file-height="5164" /></a><div class="infobox-caption">St. Amador, Chapelle de Locadour, Kervignac</div></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">1 May 418</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">Canonized</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Pre-congregation" class="mw-redirect" title="Pre-congregation">Pre-Congregation</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">1 May</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Saint_symbolism" title="Saint symbolism">Attributes</a></th><td class="infobox-data">bishop with axe and tree</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Amator</b> <span class="languageicon">(in French)</span> <b>Amadour</b> or <b>Amatre</b>  was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Auxerre" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Auxerre">bishop of Auxerre</a> from 388 until his death on 1 May 418 and venerated as a saint in the Catholic Church. Amator's feast day is celebrated on 1 May.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Amator_of_Auxerre">Amator of Auxerre</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amator&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Amator of Auxerre">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Amator studied <a href="/wiki/Theology" title="Theology">theology</a> under Valerian, Bishop of Auxerre, but married a holy woman of <a href="/wiki/Langres" title="Langres">Langres</a>, venerated locally as <b>Saint Martha</b>, in order to please his parents. After their <a href="/wiki/Wedding" title="Wedding">wedding</a>, they mutually agreed to live together as brother and sister. By mutual consent, Martha subsequently became a <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a> and Amator received the clerical tonsure.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He later succeeded <a href="/wiki/Helladius_of_Auxerre" title="Helladius of Auxerre">Eladius</a> as Bishop of Auxerre in 388 and governed the see until his death 30 years later. During this 30-year <a href="/wiki/Episcopacy" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopacy">episcopacy</a>, he built two <a href="/wiki/Church_(building)" title="Church (building)">churches</a> and converted the remaining <a href="/wiki/Paganism" title="Paganism">pagans</a> in his <a href="/wiki/Diocese" title="Diocese">diocese</a>.  He introduced the <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Quiricus_and_Saint_Julietta" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Quiricus and Saint Julietta">Saint Cyricus</a> into <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>, thus propagating this saint's cult.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Germanus_of_Auxerre" title="Germanus of Auxerre">Germanus</a> was one of the six dukes, entrusted by the emperor with the government of the Gallic provinces. He resided at Auxerre. At length he incurred the displeasure of Bishop Amator by hanging hunting trophies on a certain tree, which in earlier times had been the scene of pagan worship. Amator remonstrated with him in vain. One day when the duke was absent, the bishop had the tree cut down and the trophies burnt. Fearing the anger of the duke, who wished to kill him, he fled and appealed to the prefect Julius for permission to confer the tonsure on Germain. This being granted, Amator, who felt that his own life was drawing to a close, returned. When the duke came to the church, Amator caused the doors to be barred and gave him the tonsure against his will, telling him to live as one destined to be his successor, and forthwith made him a deacon. When in a short time Amator died, Germain was unanimously chosen to succeed him as bishop.<sup id="cite_ref-macerlean_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-macerlean-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Germanus subsequently wrote a <a href="/wiki/Biography" title="Biography">biography</a> of Amator. In the <a href="/wiki/Middle_Ages" title="Middle Ages">Middle Ages</a>, a certain Stephen also composed a life of Amator, but it is considered a work of <a href="/wiki/Fiction" title="Fiction">fiction</a>. 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/J._B._Bury" title="J. B. Bury">J. B. Bury</a> suggests that Amator ordained <a href="/wiki/Saint_Patrick" title="Saint Patrick">Saint Patrick</a> to the diaconate at Auxerre; and that Patrick was later ordained priest by Amator's successor <a href="/wiki/Germanus_of_Auxerre" title="Germanus of Auxerre">Germanus of Auxerre</a> in a church in Auxerre dedicated to Amator.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Amator died in 418 and was buried in the church which he had built in honour of the Martyr Saint Symphorian, and which later bore his own name.<sup id="cite_ref-monks_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-monks-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Iconography">Iconography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amator&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Iconography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>He is depicted as a bishop with an <a href="/wiki/Axe" title="Axe">axe</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tree" title="Tree">tree</a>.
</p><p>Amator is sometimes confused with a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> of legend whose feast day is 20 August.  A tradition in <a href="/wiki/Autun" title="Autun">Autun</a> also designates him as its first bishop, with an occupancy date of 250, tying him to the seven bishops sent to evangelise Gaul in the time of <a href="/wiki/Decius" title="Decius">Decius</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Amator_and_Rocamadour">Amator and Rocamadour</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amator&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Amator and Rocamadour">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1033289096"/><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">Main articles: <a href="/wiki/Rocamadour" title="Rocamadour">Rocamadour</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint_Amadour" title="Saint Amadour">Saint Amadour</a></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/be/Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG/220px-Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="293" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/be/Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG/330px-Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/be/Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG/440px-Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="2560" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Rocam_sanctdetail2_082005.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The sanctuary of Rocamadour</div></div></div>
<p>The commune of <a href="/wiki/Rocamadour" title="Rocamadour">Rocamadour</a> is said to have been named after Amator, who is believed to have been the founder of the ancient sanctuary. The <i>crypt Saint-Amadour</i> is situated here.  The church of Notre Dame (1479) contains a wooden <a href="/wiki/Black_Madonna" title="Black Madonna">Black Madonna</a> reputed to have been carved by Amator.
</p><p>Amadour was identified with the <a href="/wiki/Bible" title="Bible">Biblical</a> <a href="/wiki/Zacchaeus" title="Zacchaeus">Zacheus</a>, the tax collector of <a href="/wiki/Jericho" title="Jericho">Jericho</a> mentioned in Luke 19:1-10. He is also thought to have been a servant in the house of the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Family" title="Holy Family">Holy Family</a>, who married <a href="/wiki/Saint_Veronica" title="Saint Veronica">Veronica</a>, who wiped <a href="/wiki/Jesus_Christ" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesus Christ">Jesus'</a> face on the way to <a href="/wiki/Calvary" title="Calvary">Calvary</a>. Driven out of <a href="/wiki/Palestine_(region)" title="Palestine (region)">Palestine</a> by persecution, Amadour and Veronica embarked in a frail <a href="/wiki/Skiff" title="Skiff">skiff</a> and, guided by an <a href="/wiki/Angel" title="Angel">angel</a>, landed on the coast of <a href="/wiki/Aquitaine" title="Aquitaine">Aquitaine</a>, where they met Bishop <a href="/wiki/Saint_Martial" title="Saint Martial">Martial</a>, who was preaching the <a href="/wiki/Gospel" title="Gospel">Gospel</a> in the south-west of <a href="/wiki/Gaul" title="Gaul">Gaul</a>.  After journeying to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, where he witnessed the <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyrdoms</a> of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter" title="Saint Peter">Peter</a> and <a href="/wiki/Paul_of_Tarsus" class="mw-redirect" title="Paul of Tarsus">Paul</a>, Amadour, having returned to France, on the death of his spouse, withdrew to a wild spot in <a href="/wiki/Quercy" title="Quercy">Quercy</a> where he built a chapel in honour of the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin">Blessed Virgin</a>, near which he died a little later.<sup id="cite_ref-clugnet_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-clugnet-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Amator's <i>Acts</i> cannot be dated as being older than the 12th century. It is now well established that Martial, Amadour's contemporary in the legend, lived in the 3rd not the 1st century, and Rome has never included him among the members of the <a href="/wiki/Apostolic_College" class="mw-redirect" title="Apostolic College">Apostolic College</a>. The untrustworthiness of the legend has led some recent authors to suggest that Amadour was an unknown <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> or the actual bishop of Auxerre of the same name.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Portuguese_Amator">The Portuguese Amator</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amator&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: The Portuguese Amator">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Confusion is added by the fact that there is also a Amator (<a href="/wiki/Portuguese_language" title="Portuguese language">Portuguese</a>: <i lang="pt"><a href="/wiki/Amador_of_Portugal" title="Amador of Portugal">Amador of Portugal</a></i>) whose feast day is celebrated on 27 March. He was a hermit of <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a>, and several churches in the <a href="/wiki/Diocese" title="Diocese">diocese</a> of <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Guarda,_Portugal" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Guarda, Portugal">Guarda</a> and elsewhere in Portugal are dedicated to him. A "San Amador" is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spanish</a> town of <a href="/wiki/Martos" title="Martos">Martos</a>.    
</p>
