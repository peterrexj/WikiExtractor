<p><b>Blessed Thomas Holford Catholic College</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Secondary_school" title="Secondary school">secondary school</a> based in <a href="/wiki/Altrincham,_Greater_Manchester" class="mw-redirect" title="Altrincham, Greater Manchester">Altrincham, Greater Manchester</a>. The school specialises in <a href="/wiki/Maths" class="mw-redirect" title="Maths">maths</a> and <a href="/wiki/Computing" title="Computing">computing</a>, and is named after <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Thomas_Holford" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Thomas Holford">Blessed Thomas Holford</a>, a 16th-century <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> from <a href="/wiki/Cheshire" title="Cheshire">Cheshire</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The college has a <a href="/wiki/Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic">Catholic</a> identity,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> and all pupils are required to wear <a href="/wiki/School_uniform" title="School uniform">uniform</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Curriculum">Curriculum</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Thomas_Holford_Catholic_College&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Curriculum">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The college puts emphasis on maths and computing, and follows the <a href="/wiki/Key_Stage" title="Key Stage">Key Stage</a> process. At <a href="/wiki/Key_Stage_3" title="Key Stage 3">Key Stage 3</a> in year 7 &amp; 8, the pupils take the following subjects:
</p>
<ul><li>English (3 hours)</li>
<li>Maths (4 hours)</li>
<li>Science (3 hours)</li>
<li>Religious Education (2.5 hours)</li>
<li>Design Technology (3 hours) - Woodwork, food technology, graphic design, art, music, PSHCE</li>
<li>Computer Science (1 hour)</li>
<li>French/Spanish (2 hours)</li>
<li>History (2 hours)</li>
<li>Geography (2 hours)</li>
<li>Physical Education (2 hours)</li>
<li>Drama (1 hour)</li></ul>
<p>For <a href="/wiki/GCSE" class="mw-redirect" title="GCSE">GCSE</a>, the pupils take core subjects including English language, English literature, Science, Mathematics, RE, computer science or BTEC and PE although not to complete as a GCSE. Pupils choose from a variety of additional subjects, including <a href="/wiki/History" title="History">history</a>, <a href="/wiki/Geography" title="Geography">geography</a>, <a href="/wiki/Art" title="Art">art</a>, <a href="/wiki/Health_and_social_care" title="Health and social care">health and social care</a>, <a href="/wiki/Physical_education" title="Physical education">physical education</a>, <a href="/wiki/Food_technology" title="Food technology">food technology</a>, <a href="/wiki/Business_studies" title="Business studies">business studies</a>, <a href="/wiki/Classics" title="Classics">classics</a>, <a href="/wiki/Music" title="Music">music</a>, <a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>, <a href="/wiki/Drama" title="Drama">drama</a>, <a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a>, <a href="/wiki/Language" title="Language">German</a>, <a href="/wiki/Language" title="Language">Japanese</a>, <a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>. <a href="/wiki/Business_And_Technology_Education_Council_(BTEC)" class="mw-redirect" title="Business And Technology Education Council (BTEC)">BTEC</a> courses for sport are also available. Pupils have to take at least one, but not more than three, of History, Geography, Spanish and French. Out of all the other subjects a maximum of two can be chosen.<sup id="cite_ref-prospectus_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-prospectus-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Academic_performance_and_Ofsted_judgements">Academic performance and <a href="/wiki/Ofsted" title="Ofsted">Ofsted</a> judgements</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Thomas_Holford_Catholic_College&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Academic performance and Ofsted judgements">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 2019, the school was above national average for Section 48.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="margin-left:0.1em; white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Please_clarify" title="Wikipedia:Please clarify"><span title="The text near this tag may need clarification or removal of jargon. (May 2023)">clarification needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup><sup id="cite_ref-BBC_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-BBC-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>It was judged Outstanding by <a href="/wiki/Ofsted" title="Ofsted">Ofsted</a> in 2012 and 2009. In 2022 its inspection rating was 'Requires Improvement'.<sup id="cite_ref-ofsted_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ofsted-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Football_Academy">Football Academy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Blessed_Thomas_Holford_Catholic_College&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Football Academy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The school has a <a href="/wiki/UEFA" title="UEFA">UEFA</a>-standard <a href="/wiki/FieldTurf" title="FieldTurf">FieldTurf</a> <a href="/wiki/Artificial_turf" title="Artificial turf">artificial grass</a> football pitch which was opened in April 2007 by <a href="/wiki/Bobby_Charlton" title="Bobby Charlton">Bobby Charlton</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> The pitch, which cost <a href="/wiki/%C2%A3" class="mw-redirect" title="£">£</a>1 million to install, was used by the <a href="/wiki/England_national_football_team" title="England national football team">England national football team</a> for training prior to an away game against <a href="/wiki/Russia_national_football_team" title="Russia national football team">Russia</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Luzhniki_Stadium" title="Luzhniki Stadium">Luzhniki Stadium</a> in <a href="/wiki/Moscow" title="Moscow">Moscow</a>, as it uses the same surface as the Russian pitch.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
