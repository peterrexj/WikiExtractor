<p><b>Abraham of <a href="/wiki/Scetes" class="mw-redirect" title="Scetes">Scetes</a></b> was a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> who became a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Coptic_Orthodox_Church_of_Alexandria" class="mw-redirect" title="Coptic Orthodox Church of Alexandria">Coptic Church</a>.
</p><p>He was born the son of a wealthy landowner in Egypt, and became a monk under Jonas. He is alleged to have had a vision of <a href="/wiki/Jesus" title="Jesus">Christ</a> riding the chariot of the <a href="/wiki/Cherub" title="Cherub">Cherubim</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He died after eighteen years of suffering from an illness at Djirdjeh. His cell, called Dshabih, later became a famous <a href="/wiki/Shrine#Christianity" title="Shrine">shrine</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Calendar of Saints">feast day</a> in the Coptic Church is January 4.
</p>
