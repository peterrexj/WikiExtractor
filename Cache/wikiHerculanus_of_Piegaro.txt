<p><b>Herculanus of Piegaro</b> (died 1451) is an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <i>beatus</i>, <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> in 1860.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Born at <a href="/wiki/Piegaro" title="Piegaro">Piegaro</a>, he became an important <a href="/wiki/Franciscan" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan">Franciscan</a> preacher.  He entered the Franciscan Convent of the Strict Observance at <a href="/wiki/Sarteano" title="Sarteano">Sarteano</a>. He was said to have emboldened the citizens of <a href="/wiki/Lucca" title="Lucca">Lucca</a> to resist an attack by the Florentines in 1430. He died on 28 May 1451 at Castronovo in Tuscany. His feast day is June 1.  His body is said to have remained <a href="/wiki/Incorrupt" class="mw-redirect" title="Incorrupt">incorrupt</a> after his death when 5 years after his passing he was moved to a shrine in the local Franciscan church.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
