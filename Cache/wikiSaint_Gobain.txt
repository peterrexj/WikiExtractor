<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_(DxO_copie).jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/01/%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_%28DxO_copie%29.jpg/220px-%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_%28DxO_copie%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="165" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/01/%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_%28DxO_copie%29.jpg/330px-%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_%28DxO_copie%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/01/%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_%28DxO_copie%29.jpg/440px-%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_%28DxO_copie%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="4608" data-file-height="3456" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:%C3%89glise_Saint-Gobain_de_Saint-Gobain_(DxO_copie).jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Church of Saint-Gobain, Aisne</div></div></div>
<p><b>Saint Gobain</b> (died 670), also known as Goban, was an <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Irish</a> <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> and spiritual student of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Fursey" title="Saint Fursey">Saint Fursey</a> at <a href="/wiki/Burgh_Castle" title="Burgh Castle">Burgh Castle</a>, <a href="/wiki/Norfolk" title="Norfolk">Norfolk</a>, <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a>.
</p><p>Born in <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a>, he was a brother of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Wasnon" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Wasnon">Saint Wasnon</a>, (to whom a church is dedicated in <a href="/wiki/Cond%C3%A9-sur-l%27Escaut" title="Condé-sur-l&#39;Escaut">Condé-sur-l'Escaut</a>). Gobain accompanied Fursey to <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>. Some accounts have him staying at the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint_Vincent,_Laon" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of Saint Vincent, Laon">Abbey of Saint Vincent</a> in Picardy,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> or the abbey of Corbény in Champagne,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> before settling in a hermitage in the forest of Voas, near the present <a href="/wiki/Saint-Gobain,_Aisne" title="Saint-Gobain, Aisne">Saint-Gobain</a>. There he brought forth a spring by thrusting his pilgrim's staff into the ground.
</p><p>In 670, Gobain was beheaded by marauders, and buried in his oratory, which became a place of pilgrimage. His feast day is observed on 20 June.
</p>
