<p>In addition to <a href="/wiki/Quadratus_of_Athens" title="Quadratus of Athens">Quadratus of Athens</a> (<b>Quadratus</b> the Apologist), there are several <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a> with the name <b>Quadratus</b> (sometimes spelled <b>Codratus</b>):
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quadratus_of_Africa">Quadratus of Africa</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quadratus_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Quadratus of Africa">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Quadratus of Africa was martyred in <a href="/wiki/Africa_Proconsularis" class="mw-redirect" title="Africa Proconsularis">Africa Proconsularis</a> on an unknown date.  His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is celebrated on May 26.  He was the subject of a <a href="/wiki/Panegyric" title="Panegyric">panegyric</a> by <a href="/wiki/Saint_Augustine_of_Hippo" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Augustine of Hippo">Saint Augustine of Hippo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quadratus_of_Corinth">Quadratus of Corinth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quadratus_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Quadratus of Corinth">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Quadratus or Codratus was born on a mountain near Corinth, where his mother had fled to escape a persecution, during the third century, against Christians. He was a hermit and healer. During the Decian persecution, unable to persuade Quadratus and his friends Cyprian, Dionysius, Anectus, Paul and Crescens to deny Christ, the military prefect ordered the martyrs to be thrown to wild beasts, but the beasts did not touch them. They were then beheaded with a sword.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> His feast day is March 10.<sup id="cite_ref-serbianorthodoxchurch.net_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-serbianorthodoxchurch.net-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quadratus_of_Herbipolis">Quadratus of Herbipolis</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quadratus_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Quadratus of Herbipolis">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His feast day is May 7.  He was martyred around 257 at Herbipolis in <a href="/wiki/Asia_Minor" class="mw-redirect" title="Asia Minor">Asia Minor</a> (modern-day <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>) during the reign of <a href="/wiki/Valerian_(emperor)" title="Valerian (emperor)">Valerian</a>.  He had also been imprisoned at <a href="/wiki/Nicomedia" title="Nicomedia">Nicomedia</a>, <a href="/wiki/%C4%B0znik" title="İznik">Nicaea</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Apamea_(Syria)" class="mw-redirect" title="Apamea (Syria)">Apamea</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quadratus_of_Nicomedia">Quadratus of Nicomedia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quadratus_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Quadratus of Nicomedia">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Quadratus or Codratus was a nobleman who was martyred at <a href="/wiki/Nicomedia" title="Nicomedia">Nicomedia</a> (modern-day <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>) during the reign of Valerian.  His feast day is March 10.<sup id="cite_ref-serbianorthodoxchurch.net_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-serbianorthodoxchurch.net-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span id="Quadratus.2C_Theodosius.2C_Emmanuel_and_Companions"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Quadratus,_Theodosius,_Emmanuel_and_Companions">Quadratus, Theodosius, Emmanuel and Companions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quadratus_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Quadratus, Theodosius, Emmanuel and Companions">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>This Quadratus or Codratus was a martyred bishop in <a href="/wiki/Anatolia" title="Anatolia">Anatolia</a> (modern-day <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>). He was arrested and put to death with forty-two other martyrs, including Emmanuel and Theodosius, during the persecution of the Christians by <a href="/wiki/Diocletian" title="Diocletian">Diocletian</a> in 304. Their feast day is March 26.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
