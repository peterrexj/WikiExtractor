<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p>

<p><b>Raynald</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> monk and <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Nocera" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Nocera">Bishop of Nocera Umbra</a>. Born around 1150, in the village of Postignano, near <a href="/wiki/Nocera_Umbra" title="Nocera Umbra">Nocera Umbra</a>, <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a>, to parents of <a href="/wiki/Germans" title="Germans">German</a> descent.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He entered the <a href="/wiki/Benedictines" title="Benedictines">Benedictine</a> congregation of <a href="/wiki/Fonte_Avellana" title="Fonte Avellana">Fonte Avellana</a>. He was at the monastery of S. Maria di Vallemergo in 1199, along with Trasimundus, the future bishop of Senigallia.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was elected Prior of the monastery of Santa Croce di Fonte Avellana, though the date is unknown. On 7 February 1218 Prior Rinaldo and his monastery were taken under the protection of the Holy See by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Honorius_III" title="Pope Honorius III">Pope Honorius III</a>, renewing the privilege granted by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_II" title="Pope Innocent II">Pope Innocent II</a> in 1139.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In 1218, he accompanied Bishop Gerard of Ancona and Bishop Trasimundus of Senigallia to Fano, to negotiate the release of the imprisoned Bishop Riccardo.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> In September 1218, he visited the monastery of S. Esuperanzio in Cingoli, where he acted as witness to a grant to the monastery. He then visited Senigallia, where his congregation received a gift, and finally returned to his own monastery at Fonte Avellano.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was appointed <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Nocera" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Nocera">Bishop of Nocera Umbra</a> in 1213.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> He was a close friend of St. <a href="/wiki/Francis_of_Assisi" title="Francis of Assisi">Francis of Assisi</a>. He was one of the seven bishops who consecrated the church of the Porziuncula at Assisi, and proclaimed the indulgence.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> He served as Bishop of Nocera until his death in 1217.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
