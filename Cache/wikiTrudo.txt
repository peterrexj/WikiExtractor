					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">"Saint Trond" redirects here. <style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Trudo</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">~698 AD</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">23 November</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Trudo (Tron, Trond, Trudon, Trutjen, Truyen)</b> (died ca. 698) was a saint of the seventh century.  He is called the "Apostle of <a href="/wiki/Hesbaye" title="Hesbaye">Hesbaye</a>" (partly in the provinces of  <a href="/wiki/Province_of_Brabant" title="Province of Brabant">Brabant</a> and <a href="/wiki/Limburg_(Belgium)" title="Limburg (Belgium)">Limburg</a>, <a href="/wiki/Belgium" title="Belgium">Belgium</a>).  His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is celebrated on 23 November.
</p><p>Devoted from his earliest youth to the service of God, Trudo came to St. <a href="/wiki/Remaclus" title="Remaclus">Remaclus</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Li%C3%A8ge" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Liège">Bishop of Liège</a> (<i><a href="/wiki/Acta_Sanctorum" title="Acta Sanctorum">Acta Sanctorum</a></i>, I Sept., 678) and was sent by him to <a href="/wiki/Chlodulph" class="mw-redirect" title="Chlodulph">Chlodulph</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Metz" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Metz">Bishop of Metz</a>. Here he received his education at the Church of St. Stephen, to which he always showed a strong affection and donated his later foundation. After his ordination he returned to his native district, preached the Gospel, and built a church at <i>Sarchinium</i>, on the River Cicindria. It was blessed about 656 by <a href="/wiki/Theodard_of_Maastricht" title="Theodard of Maastricht">Theodard of Maastricht</a>, in honour of Sts. <a href="/wiki/Quintinus" class="mw-redirect" title="Quintinus">Quintinus</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint_Remigius" title="Saint Remigius">Remigius</a>. Disciples gathered about him and in course of time a monastery developed, the later <a href="/wiki/Sint-Truiden_Abbey" title="Sint-Truiden Abbey">Sint-Truiden Abbey</a>. The convent for women, established by him at <a href="/wiki/Odeghem" class="mw-redirect" title="Odeghem">Odeghem</a> near <a href="/wiki/Bruges" title="Bruges">Bruges</a>, later also bore his name (<i><a href="/wiki/Gallia_Christiana" title="Gallia Christiana">Gallia Christiana</a></i>, Paris, 1887, V, 281) (see <a href="/wiki/Sint-Truiden" title="Sint-Truiden">Sint-Truiden</a>).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Trudo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After death he was buried in the church erected by himself. A translation of his <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a>, together with those of St. <a href="/wiki/Eucherius_of_Orl%C3%A9ans" title="Eucherius of Orléans">Eucherius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Orl%C3%A9ans" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Orléans">Bishop of Orléans</a>, who had died there in exile in 743, was made in 880 by <a href="/w/index.php?title=Franco,_Bishop_of_Li%C3%A8ge&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Franco, Bishop of Liège (page does not exist)">Franco, Bishop of Liège</a>. On account of the threatened inroads of the <a href="/wiki/Normans" title="Normans">Normans</a> the relics were later hidden in a subterranean <a href="/wiki/Crypt" title="Crypt">crypt</a>. After the great conflagration of 1085 they were lost, but again discovered in 1169, and on 11 August that year an official recognition and translation was made by Bishop Rudolph III. On account of these translations the dates 5 and 12 August, and 1 and 2 September, are noted in the martyrologies. The <i>Analecta Bollandiana</i> (V, 305) give an old office of the saint in verse.
</p><p>The life was written by Donatus, a deacon of Metz, at the order of his bishop, Angibram (769-91). It was rewritten by Theodoric, Abbot of <a href="/wiki/St_Trond" class="mw-redirect" title="St Trond">St Trond</a> (d. 1107).
</p>
