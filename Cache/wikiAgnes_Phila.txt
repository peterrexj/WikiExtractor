<p>The <b>Martyrs of Songkhon</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> (<a href="/wiki/Thai_language" title="Thai language">Thai</a>: <span lang="th">มรณสักขีแห่งสองคอน</span>) (also called <b>Seven Blessed Martyrs of Songkhon</b>) are seven <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Thai_people" title="Thai people">Thais</a> executed in the village Songkhon in Pong Kham subdistrict, <a href="/wiki/Wan_Yai_District" class="mw-redirect" title="Wan Yai District">Wan Yai District</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Mukdahan_Province" class="mw-redirect" title="Mukdahan Province">Mukdahan Province</a>, northeastern <a href="/wiki/Thailand" title="Thailand">Thailand</a>, in December 1940 by local police forces. The killings occurred during the <a href="/wiki/Franco-Thai_War" title="Franco-Thai War">Franco-Thai War</a> and police falsely believed they were spying for the French. The victims were <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> on October 22, 1989.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup class="noprint Inline-Template" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources" title="Wikipedia:Reliable sources"><span title="The material near this tag may rely on an unreliable source. (April 2022)">unreliable source?</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>The names of the martyrs are:
</p>
<ul><li><b>Philip Siphong Onphitak</b>, <a href="/wiki/Catechist" class="mw-redirect" title="Catechist">catechist</a>, 33 years old</li>
<li><b>Agnes Phila</b>, 31 years old, belonged to <a href="/wiki/Lovers_of_the_Holy_Cross" title="Lovers of the Holy Cross">Lovers of the Holy Cross</a> Congregation</li>
<li><b>Lucia Khambang</b>, 23 years old, belonged to Lovers of the Holy Cross Congregation</li>
<li><b>Agatha Phutta</b>, 59 years old</li>
<li><b>Cecilia Butsi</b>, 16 years old</li>
<li><b>Bibiana Khampai</b>, 15 years old</li>
<li><b>Maria Phon</b>, 14 years old</li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Letter_of_Sister_Agnes_Phila_to_the_police_before_the_execution">Letter of Sister Agnes Phila to the police before the execution</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Martyrs_of_Songkhon&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Letter of Sister Agnes Phila to the police before the execution">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<blockquote><p>To the Chief Police in Songkhon</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>Yesterday evening you received your order to wipe out, definitely, the Name of God, the Only Lord of our lives and minds. We adore Him only, Sir. A few days earlier, you had mentioned to us that you would not wipe out the Name of God and we were rather pleased with that in such a way that we put away our religious habits which showed that we were His handmaids. But it not so today. We do profess that the religion of Christ is the only true religion. Therefore, we would like to give our answer to your question, asked yesterday evening which we did not have a chance to respond because we were unprepared for it. Now we would like to give you our answer. We are asking you to carry out your order with us. Please do not delay any longer. Please carry out your order. Please open the door of heaven to us so that we can confirm that outside the Religion of Christ no none can go to heaven. Please do it. We are well prepared. When we will be gone we will remember you. Please take pity on our souls. We will be thankful to you and will be grateful to you for it. And on the last day we will see each other face to face.</p></blockquote>
<blockquote><p>Do wait and see, please. We keep your commands, oh God, we wish to be witnesses to You, dear God. We are: Agnes, Lucia, Phuttha, Budsi, Buakhai, Suwan. We would like to bring little Phuma along with us because we love her so much. We have already made up our minds, dear Sir.</p></blockquote>
