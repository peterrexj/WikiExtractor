<div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Bishop of Eichstatt</div>
<p><b>Gundekar</b> (1019–1075), (also <b>Gundechar</b>, <b>Gundakar</b>, <b>Gunzo</b>) was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Eichst%C3%A4tt" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Eichstätt">bishop of Eichstätt</a> from 1057 to 1075.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He is known for his historical work <i>Vitae Pontificum Eystettensium</i> on his predecessors.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is a Catholic <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">blessed</a> and his <a href="/wiki/Feast_Day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast Day">feast day</a> is August 2.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
