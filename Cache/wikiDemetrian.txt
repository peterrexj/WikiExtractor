					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Demetrian</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">Sika, <a href="/wiki/Cyprus" title="Cyprus">Cyprus</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">c. 912<br />Cyprus</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>,<br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">6 November</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">Patronage</a></th><td class="infobox-data">Prisoners</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Demetrian</b> (d. 912?) is a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> from <a href="/wiki/Cyprus" title="Cyprus">Cyprus</a>. In the 9th and 10th centuries, he served the <a href="/wiki/Christian_Church" title="Christian Church">Christian Church</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> and an <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a>, and ultimately as the <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of the ancient city of <a href="/wiki/Khytri" class="mw-redirect" title="Khytri">Khytri</a>. He is venerated for his apparently miraculous rescue of Christian Cypriots who had been enslaved by <a href="/wiki/Saracen" title="Saracen">Saracen</a> invaders.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Demetrian&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Contemporaneous documentation of Demetrian's life is limited to a single manuscript.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Complicating his record, he has at times been called by the common names <i>Demetrio</i> or <i>Demetrius</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-CSinfo_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CSinfo-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The lone manuscript holds that Demetrian was the son of a priest,<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> born in a small village named Sika in <a href="/wiki/Cyprus_in_the_Middle_Ages" title="Cyprus in the Middle Ages">medieval Cyprus</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-COorg_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-COorg-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> As a young man, he wed a woman who died only a few months after marriage.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> After her death, he devoted himself to the Christian Church.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-COorg_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-COorg-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Demetrian served in a monastery dedicated to <a href="/wiki/Anthony_the_Great" title="Anthony the Great">Saint Anthony</a>, and he was eventually ordained as that institution's abbot, or <i><a href="/wiki/Hegoumenos" class="mw-redirect" title="Hegoumenos">hegoumenos</a></i>, a position he is said to have held for forty years.<sup id="cite_ref-CSinfo_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CSinfo-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-COorg_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-COorg-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Church leaders then elevated him to <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> of the city of <a href="/wiki/Khytri" class="mw-redirect" title="Khytri">Khytri</a>, but at first he resisted the appointment. Not wanting to exchange his quiet monastic life for a busy episcopal office in the city, he is said to have fled, and was hidden in a cave by a friend.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Ultimately, however, his friend became uncomfortable with the situation, and Demetrian was convinced to return to the city and embrace his appointment.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He served as bishop of Khytri for some twenty-five years, from 885 until his death, and is acknowledged by the <i><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Encyclopedia" title="Catholic Encyclopedia">Catholic Encyclopedia</a></i> as that office's most famous inhabitant.<sup id="cite_ref-Pétridès_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Pétridès-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Near the end of his life, Demetrian saw Cyprus overrun by Saracen invaders. Many Christians were taken captive and marched toward <a href="/wiki/Baghdad" title="Baghdad">Baghdad</a> to become slaves. Despite his advanced age, Demetrian followed them on the road and pleaded desperately for their release. Seemingly miraculously, the Saracens were persuaded and they released the captives to him.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Butler_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Demetrian&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A longstanding devotional following of Saint Demetrian continues through the present day in Cyprus.<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-COorg_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-COorg-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His annual <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated on 6 November.<sup id="cite_ref-Walsh_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Walsh-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Prisoner" title="Prisoner">prisoners</a>, Demetrian is also invoked in a more general fashion by a preamble to <a href="/wiki/Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic">Catholic</a> prayer: "Loving Father, through the intercession of Saint Demetrian, rescue me from the anxieties that hold me captive".<sup id="cite_ref-Magni_1-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Magni-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
