<p><b>Benvenutus Scotivoli</b> (died 22 March 1282) was an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> prelate who served as the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Osimo" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Osimo">Bishop of Osimo</a> from 1264 until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-SW_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SW-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CO_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CO-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Pope_Martin_IV" title="Pope Martin IV">Pope Martin IV</a> canonized him as a saint in 1284.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Benvenutus_Scotivoli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Benvenutus Scotivoli was born sometime in the 1200s in <a href="/wiki/Ancona" title="Ancona">Ancona</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SW_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SW-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Scotivoli studied at the <a href="/wiki/University_of_Bologna" title="University of Bologna">Bologna college</a> and studied <a href="/wiki/Jurisprudence" title="Jurisprudence">jurisprudence</a> with <a href="/wiki/Sylvester_Gozzolini" title="Sylvester Gozzolini">Silvestro Gozzolini</a> at which point he was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> to the <a href="/wiki/Priesthood" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood">priesthood</a> when he returned to his hometown.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He was made the archdeacon of Ancona and later its apostolic administrator on 1 August 1263 before <a href="/wiki/Pope_Urban_IV" title="Pope Urban IV">Pope Urban IV</a> appointed him as the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Osimo" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Osimo">Bishop of Osimo</a> on 13 March 1264. Scotivoli ordained <a href="/wiki/Nicholas_of_Tolentino" title="Nicholas of Tolentino">Nicholas of Tolentino</a> to the priesthood in 1269.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 15 January 1270 he ordered the San Fiorenzo <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a> in Posciavalle to sell all of its assets while a diocesan synod on 7 February 1273 saw him ban the sale of all church properties.<sup id="cite_ref-SW_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SW-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Scotivoli - on 24 February 1274 - rescinded the excommunication of <a href="/wiki/Ripatransone" title="Ripatransone">Ripatransone</a> on the orders of <a href="/wiki/Pope_Gregory_X" title="Pope Gregory X">Pope Gregory X</a> that the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Fermo" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Fermo">Bishop of Fermo</a> had instituted before that.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died on 22 March 1284 and was buried in the <a href="/wiki/Osimo_Cathedral" title="Osimo Cathedral">Osimo Cathedral</a> though his remains were moved down to the crypt in July 1590.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Canonization">Canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Benvenutus_Scotivoli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Pope_Martin_IV" title="Pope Martin IV">Pope Martin IV</a> canonized him in 1284 and he has been the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of Osimo since 1755 when the civic authorities recognized him as such.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Franciscan_discrepancies">Franciscan discrepancies</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Benvenutus_Scotivoli&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Franciscan discrepancies">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Inspection of his tomb revealed a dark <a href="/wiki/Capuche" title="Capuche">capuche</a> sewn to a lambskin and it led to the biographer Jean Baldi asserting that Scotivoli was a <a href="/wiki/Franciscan" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan">Franciscan</a> which became an accepted proposition. But in 1765 the Osimo priest Pannelli contended he was not a Franciscan but the saint is still recognized on the Franciscan calendar.<sup id="cite_ref-EC_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
