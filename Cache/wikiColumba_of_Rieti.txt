<p><b>Columba of Rieti</b>, (2 February 1467 – 20 May 1501) was an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Religious_sister" title="Religious sister">religious sister</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_St._Dominic" class="mw-redirect" title="Third Order of St. Dominic">Third Order of St. Dominic</a> who was noted as a <a href="/wiki/Christian_mysticism" title="Christian mysticism">mystic</a>. She was renowned for her spiritual counsel, devotion to the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Sacrament" title="Blessed Sacrament">Blessed Sacrament</a>, and fantastic miracles were attributed to her. She was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a> in 1625.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Columba_of_Rieti&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Columba_of_Rieti&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Columba was born <b>Angelella Guardagnoli</b>, the daughter of a poor family in the <a href="/wiki/Umbria" title="Umbria">Umbrian</a> city of <a href="/wiki/Rieti" title="Rieti">Rieti</a>. Legend states that when she was born, <a href="/wiki/Angel" title="Angel">angels</a> gathered around her house, singing, and that during her <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptism</a>, a dove flew down to the font. From then on, no one referred to her as Angelella, but as Columba (<i>dove</i>).<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEJones_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> As a small girl, Columba learned to <a href="/wiki/Spinning_(textiles)" title="Spinning (textiles)">spin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Sewing" title="Sewing">sew</a> repairing the clothes of the local Dominican <a href="/wiki/Friar" title="Friar">friars</a>. She was educated by Dominican <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nuns</a>. As a <a href="/wiki/Adolescence" title="Adolescence">teenager</a>, she prayed to discern her vocation in life and received a vision of <a href="/wiki/Christ_King" class="mw-redirect" title="Christ King">Christ on a throne</a> surrounded by <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a>. She took this as a sign to dedicate herself to God, and so she made a private vow of <a href="/wiki/Chastity" title="Chastity">chastity</a>, and spent her time in prayer. When it was revealed that her parents had <a href="/wiki/Arranged_marriage" title="Arranged marriage">arranged a marriage for her</a>, she cut off her hair and sent it to her suitor as a way of letting him know where her real interest lay.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEJones_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Eating_disorder">Eating disorder</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Columba_of_Rieti&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Eating disorder">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Columba fasted regularly, a form of religiously-motivated self-starvation or abstaining from food. <sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Anorexia mirabilis was well known in Columba's time, when it was not seen as a <a href="/wiki/Medical_emergency" title="Medical emergency">medical emergency</a> or <a href="/wiki/Mental_illness" class="mw-redirect" title="Mental illness">mental illness</a> as it would be today, instead revered as a symbol of one's <a href="/wiki/Piety" title="Piety">piety</a>. Ultimately, the disease caused her death in 1501, at the age of 34.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Additionally, Columba was known to engage in acts designed to cause her physical pain, such as at the wearing of a <a href="/wiki/Hairshirt" class="mw-redirect" title="Hairshirt">hairshirt</a> and sleeping on <a href="/wiki/Thorns,_spines,_and_prickles" title="Thorns, spines, and prickles">thorns</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> It's possible that she may have been influenced by <a href="/wiki/Catherine_of_Siena" title="Catherine of Siena">Catherine of Siena</a>, an earlier Italian <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Order" title="Dominican Order">Dominican</a> saint, who suffered from anorexia mirabilis and died at the age of 33.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Also, like Catherine, Columba cut her hair short to avoid marriage.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Religious_career">Religious career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Columba_of_Rieti&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Religious career">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Columba became a Dominican tertiary at age 19. She was reputedly given to ecstasies, during one of which <a href="/wiki/Astral_projection" title="Astral projection">her spirit toured the Holy Land</a>. She was much sought after as a spiritual counselor. It is said that citizens from the city of <a href="/wiki/Narni" title="Narni">Narni</a> tried to kidnap her so she could be <i>their</i> miracle worker, but she escaped. The same townsfolk were later to fight to retain their own townswoman and mystic, <a href="/wiki/Lucy_of_Narni" class="mw-redirect" title="Lucy of Narni">Lucy of Narni</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEJones_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEAshley_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAshley-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Upon an interior prompting that she should leave Rieti, Columba wandered away, having no concept of where she was going. Along the way she was arrested in <a href="/wiki/Foligno" title="Foligno">Foligno</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Vagrancy_(people)" class="mw-redirect" title="Vagrancy (people)">vagrant</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> there ordered her to go to Perugia and to found a Third Order <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a>, which she did, but only against the strong objections of the citizens of Foligno and Rieti who wanted her for their own towns.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEAshley_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAshley-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> She worked with the poor extensively in Perugia, so much so that her sanctity reportedly incensed <a href="/wiki/Lucrezia_Borgia" title="Lucrezia Borgia">Lucrezia Borgia</a> for years. At one point Borgia had even issued a complaint accusing Columba of <a href="/wiki/Witchcraft" title="Witchcraft">practicing magic</a>. On the other hand, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Alexander_VI" title="Pope Alexander VI">Pope Alexander VI</a>, Lucrezia's father, held Columba in high regard. He consulted her and received a severe admonition to repent from her.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEMcNamara2007_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMcNamara2007-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Columba spent eleven years as <a href="/wiki/Prioress" class="mw-redirect" title="Prioress">prioress</a> in Perugia, dying on 20 May 1501, at the age of 34.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEAshley_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEAshley-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Legend says that at the moment of her death, her friend and fellow Dominican tertiary, <a href="/wiki/Osanna_of_Mantua" title="Osanna of Mantua">Osanna of Mantua</a>, saw Columba's soul as a "radiance rising to heaven." The whole city turned out for her funeral, which was paid for by the city fathers.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEJones_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEJones-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEMcNamara2007_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMcNamara2007-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>She was beatified on 25 February 1625 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Urban_VIII" title="Pope Urban VIII">Pope Urban VIII</a>, and her <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated within the <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Order" title="Dominican Order">Dominican Order</a> on the anniversary of her death.
</p>
