<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Anstrudis of Laon</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">unknown<br /><a href="/wiki/Laon" title="Laon">Laon</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">688</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">17 October</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Anstrudis</b> (Anstrude, Austru, or Austrude) (b. unknown - 688) was the daughter of Saint <a href="/w/index.php?title=Blandinus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Blandinus (page does not exist)">Blandinus</a> and Saint <a href="/wiki/Sadalberga" title="Sadalberga">Sadalberga</a>, the founder of the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_St._John,_Laon" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of St. John, Laon">Abbey of St. John</a> at Laon. She was also the sister of Saint <a href="/w/index.php?title=Baldwin_of_Laon&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Baldwin of Laon (page does not exist)">Baldwin</a><span class="noprint" style="font-size:85%; font-style: normal;">&#160;&#91;<a href="https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baudouin_de_Laon" class="extiw" title="fr:Baudouin de Laon">fr</a>&#93;</span>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Anstrudis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In Merovingian Gaul, founding a monastery was a noble family's way of expressing and reinforcing its power. The founder gave the land, and retained the right to appoint the abbot or abbess, but also guaranteed its protection. Regine Le Jan describes it as part of the family's <i>honor</i>. The ruling abbot/abbess was frequently a family member and controlled access to the premises, a matter of some importance during a time of recurrent feuds and power struggles between neighboring families.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> They served as a power base for families, and as such were not exempt from the political disturbances of the time. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Anstrudis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>When St. Sadalberga withdrew from the world to become abbess at the convent, Anstrudis went with her. Sadalberga died in 655. Before her death, in order to ensure the stability of the abbey, Sadalberga determined to turn over its direction to her daughter as soon as Anstrudis reached the age of twenty. Anstrudis was then consecrated abbess.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> She was noted for the care for her sisters, her all-night vigils, and her self-imposed austerities. Except on Sundays and on <a href="/wiki/Christmas_Day" class="mw-redirect" title="Christmas Day">Christmas Day</a> she never took any nourishment but one moderate refection at three o’clock in the afternoon, and on fast-days after sunset.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Her tenure as abbess was marked by the unsettled political conditions of the period.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Anstrudis was caught up in the dynastic struggle between <a href="/wiki/Dagobert_II" title="Dagobert II">Dagobert II</a> of <a href="/wiki/Austrasia" title="Austrasia">Austrasia</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ebroin" title="Ebroin">Ebroin</a>, mayor of the place of <a href="/wiki/Neustria" title="Neustria">Neustria</a>, who supported <a href="/wiki/Theuderic_III" title="Theuderic III">Theuderic III</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Fox_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Fox-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Her brother Baldwin was treacherously assassinated while attempting to negotiate a settlement of some dispute regarding the convent. She herself was accused of wrongdoing by Ebroin. However, he was at length softened by her intrepid constancy and virtue and innocence, and from a persecutor became her patron and friend. <a href="/wiki/Pepin_of_Herstal" title="Pepin of Herstal">Pepin</a>, when Mayor of the palace, declared himself her strenuous protector.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Anstrudis died in 688 of natural causes. Her feast day is celebrated on 17 October. Anstrudis is remembered in the Gallican and Benedictines calendars.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Helianthus_decapetalus" title="Helianthus decapetalus">ten-petaled sunflower</a>, which blooms in late summer or early fall is identified with Anstrudis.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
