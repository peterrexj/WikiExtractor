<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geography">Geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Geography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bron lies 10&#160;km (6.2&#160;mi) east-southeast of central <a href="/wiki/Lyon" title="Lyon">Lyon</a>. It is the sixth-largest <a href="/wiki/Suburb" title="Suburb">suburb</a> of the city of <a href="/wiki/Lyon" title="Lyon">Lyon</a>, and is adjacent to its east side.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The earliest traces of life in Bron can be found in the cemetery and date from 71 BC. The town as it is today did not take shape until approximately 1812.
</p><p>In mid-August 1944, prisoners from <a href="/wiki/Montluc_prison" title="Montluc prison">Montluc prison</a> were taken to <a href="/wiki/Lyon%E2%80%93Bron_Airport" title="Lyon–Bron Airport">Bron Airfield</a> where 109 of them, including 72 Jews, were killed in what would become known as <i>Le Charnier de Bron</i> ("The <a href="/wiki/Charnel_house" title="Charnel house">Charnel house</a> of Bron").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Bron was spared much of the damage caused by the riots in many of France's suburbs in the 1990s, such as in <a href="/wiki/V%C3%A9nissieux" title="Vénissieux">Vénissieux</a> and <a href="/wiki/Villeurbanne" title="Villeurbanne">Villeurbanne</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sights">Sights</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Sights">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Fort_de_Bron" title="Fort de Bron">Fort de Bron</a>, erected between 1872 and 1876, is part of the second belt of fortifications around Lyon.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Transport">Transport</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Transport">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bron is served by the following <a href="/wiki/Transports_en_commun_lyonnais" title="Transports en commun lyonnais">TCL</a> (Lyon <a href="/wiki/Public_transport" title="Public transport">public transport</a>) services:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Rapid_transit" title="Rapid transit">Metro</a>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Lyon_Metro_Line_D" title="Lyon Metro Line D">Line D</a> (Gare de <a href="/wiki/Vaise" title="Vaise">Vaise</a> to Gare de <a href="/wiki/V%C3%A9nissieux" title="Vénissieux">Vénissieux</a>) – served by 2 stations (Mermoz-Pinel and Parilly) located on the boundary with Lyon.</li></ul></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Tram" title="Tram">Tram</a>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Tramways_in_Lyon#Line_T2" class="mw-redirect" title="Tramways in Lyon">Line T2</a> (<a href="/wiki/Perrache_(quarter)" title="Perrache (quarter)">Perrache</a> to <a href="/wiki/Saint-Priest,_Rh%C3%B4ne" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Priest, Rhône">Saint-Priest</a> Bel-Air) – served by 8 stations.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Tramways_in_Lyon#Line_T5" class="mw-redirect" title="Tramways in Lyon">Line T5</a> (Grange-Blanche to Parc du Chêne, continuing to <a href="/wiki/Eurexpo" title="Eurexpo">Eurexpo</a> on exhibition days) – served by 7 stations.</li></ul></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Bus" title="Bus">Bus</a>

<li>C15 (Laurent Bonnevay – Bachut Mairie du 8e)</li>

<p><b>Bron Airport</b><br /><a href="/wiki/Lyon-Bron_Airport" class="mw-redirect" title="Lyon-Bron Airport">Lyon-Bron Airport</a> (technically located in the communes of <a href="/wiki/Chassieu" title="Chassieu">Chassieu</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint-Priest,_Rh%C3%B4ne" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Priest, Rhône">Saint-Priest</a>) has existed since 1920, although much of its commercial activity was diverted to Satolas (<a href="/wiki/Saint-Exup%C3%A9ry_International_Airport" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Exupéry International Airport">Saint-Exupéry International Airport</a>), Lyon's main <a href="/wiki/Airport" title="Airport">airport</a>, in 1975.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Education">Education</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Education">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bron is home to part of the <a href="/wiki/Lumi%C3%A8re_University_Lyon_2" title="Lumière University Lyon 2">University of Lyon 2</a>. The Bron campus is located on the south-eastern edge of the town, close to the <a href="/wiki/Parc_de_Parilly" title="Parc de Parilly">Parc de Parilly</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint-Priest,_Rh%C3%B4ne" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Priest, Rhône">Saint-Priest</a>.
</p>
