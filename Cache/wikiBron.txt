<p><b>Bron</b> (<small>French pronunciation:&#160;&#8203;</small><span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)" class="IPA" lang="fr-Latn-fonipa"><a href="/wiki/Help:IPA/French" title="Help:IPA/French">[bʁɔ̃]</a></span>) is a <a href="/wiki/Communes_of_France" title="Communes of France">commune</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Metropolis_of_Lyon" class="mw-redirect" title="Metropolis of Lyon">Metropolis of Lyon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Auvergne-Rh%C3%B4ne-Alpes" title="Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes">Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes</a> <a href="/wiki/Regions_of_France" title="Regions of France">region</a>, eastern <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geography">Geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Geography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bron lies 10&#160;km (6.2&#160;mi) east-southeast of central <a href="/wiki/Lyon" title="Lyon">Lyon</a>. It is the sixth-largest <a href="/wiki/Suburb" title="Suburb">suburb</a> of the city of <a href="/wiki/Lyon" title="Lyon">Lyon</a>, and is adjacent to its east side.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bron&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: History">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The earliest traces of life in Bron can be found in the cemetery and date from 71 BC. The town as it is today did not take shape until approximately 1812.
</p><p>In mid-August 1944, prisoners from <a href="/wiki/Montluc_prison" title="Montluc prison">Montluc prison</a> were taken to <a href="/wiki/Lyon%E2%80%93Bron_Airport" title="Lyon–Bron Airport">Bron Airfield</a> where 109 of them, including 72 Jews, were killed in what would become known as <i>Le Charnier de Bron</i> ("The <a href="/wiki/Charnel_house" title="Charnel house">Charnel house</a> of Bron").<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Bron was spared much of the damage caused by the riots in many of France's suburbs in the 1990s, such as in <a href="/wiki/Venissieux" class="mw-redirect" title="Venissieux">Venissieux</a> and <a href="/wiki/Villeurbanne" title="Villeurbanne">Villeurbanne</a>.
</p>
