<p><b>Gabriele Allegra</b> (<a href="/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language">Chinese</a>&#58; <span lang="zh">雷永明</span>, 26 December 1907 – 26 January 1976) was a <a href="/wiki/Franciscan" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan">Franciscan</a> <a href="/wiki/Friar" title="Friar">Friar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Biblical_scholar" class="mw-redirect" title="Biblical scholar">Biblical scholar</a>. He is best known for accomplishing the first complete translation of the <a href="/wiki/Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Bible" title="Bible">Bible</a> into the <a href="/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language">Chinese language</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Studium_Biblicum_Translation" class="mw-redirect" title="Studium Biblicum Translation">Studium Biblicum Translation</a> is often considered the definitive Chinese Bible among Catholics. He awaits <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonization</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Giovanni Stefano Allegra was born the eldest of eight children, in <a href="/wiki/San_Giovanni_la_Punta" title="San Giovanni la Punta">San Giovanni la Punta</a> in the province of <a href="/wiki/Catania" title="Catania">Catania</a>, Italy. He entered the Franciscan <a href="/wiki/Minor_seminary" title="Minor seminary">minor seminary</a> at S. Biagio in <a href="/wiki/Acireale" title="Acireale">Acireale</a> in 1918, taking the name "Gabriele Maria", and the <a href="/wiki/Novitiate" title="Novitiate">novitiate</a> in <a href="/wiki/Bronte,_Catania" class="mw-redirect" title="Bronte, Catania">Bronte</a> in 1923. He then studied at the Franciscan International College of St. Anthony in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> from 1926, now known as the <a href="/wiki/Pontifical_University_Antonianum" class="mw-redirect" title="Pontifical University Antonianum">Pontifical University Antonianum</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-stampa_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stampa-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The future course of his life was determined in 1928 when he attended the celebrations of the 6th centenary of another Franciscan, <a href="/wiki/Giovanni_di_Monte_Corvino" class="mw-redirect" title="Giovanni di Monte Corvino">Giovanni di Monte Corvino</a>, who had attempted a first translation of the <a href="/wiki/Bible" title="Bible">Bible</a> in <a href="/wiki/Beijing" title="Beijing">Beijing</a> in the 14th century. On that day, at the age of 21, Allegra was inspired to translate the Bible into Chinese; a task that took the next 40 years of his life. He was ordained a priest in 1930 and soon thereafter received orders to sail for mainland <a href="/wiki/China" title="China">China</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-stampa_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stampa-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mission_to_China">Mission to China</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Mission to China">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:AllegraChina1.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/4/4b/AllegraChina1.jpg/143px-AllegraChina1.jpg" decoding="async" width="143" height="216" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/4/4b/AllegraChina1.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="171" data-file-height="258" /></a><figcaption>Young Allegra (left) as he first arrived in China</figcaption></figure>
<p>Allegra arrived at the mission in <a href="/wiki/Hunan" title="Hunan">Hunan</a>, southern China, in July 1931 and started to learn Chinese. With the help of his Chinese teacher, he prepared a first draft of the translation of the <a href="/wiki/Bible" title="Bible">Bible</a> around 1937. He was fatigued from the translation effort and had to return to Italy for three years where he continued his studies in biblical languages and biblical archaeology.<sup id="cite_ref-stampa_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-stampa-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1940 he left Italy again and sailed from <a href="/wiki/San_Francisco" title="San Francisco">San Francisco</a> for Japan on his way to China. In <a href="/wiki/Kobe" title="Kobe">Kobe</a>, he met the French <a href="/wiki/Jesuit" class="mw-redirect" title="Jesuit">Jesuit</a> priest <a href="/wiki/Teilhard_de_Chardin" class="mw-redirect" title="Teilhard de Chardin">Teilhard de Chardin</a> for the first time. He attempted to return to Hunan again, but the <a href="/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War" title="Second Sino-Japanese War">Second Sino-Japanese War</a> had already started and he was forced to go further north to Beijing instead. This had an unfortunate side-effect in that during his trip through the Japanese-occupied territories, he lost more than half of the translated text during the war events.<sup id="cite_ref-newsva_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-newsva-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Because Allegra was an Italian citizen, and the chaplain to the Italian Embassy, the Japanese occupiers of China did not intern him for long, and he could continue his translation work. As of 1942, he became actively involved in assisting other missionaries to survive their internment in the Japanese <a href="/wiki/Internment_camp" class="mw-redirect" title="Internment camp">internment camp</a> at Weihsien in northern China, and managed to obtain the release of several prisoners.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Bible_in_Chinese">The Bible in Chinese</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: The Bible in Chinese">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:ChineseJesus.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/ChineseJesus.jpg/143px-ChineseJesus.jpg" decoding="async" width="143" height="191" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8a/ChineseJesus.jpg/215px-ChineseJesus.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/ChineseJesus.jpg 2x" data-file-width="250" data-file-height="334" /></a><figcaption>Chinese <a href="/wiki/Depiction_of_Jesus" title="Depiction of Jesus">depiction of Jesus</a> in <a href="/wiki/Gospel_of_Mark" title="Gospel of Mark">Mark:10</a>, <a href="/wiki/Beijing" title="Beijing">Beijing</a>, 1879.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Allegra organized a team of Chinese Franciscan friars to work with him on the translation of the Bible and inaugurated the <a href="/wiki/Studium_Biblicum_Franciscanum" title="Studium Biblicum Franciscanum">Studium Biblicum Franciscanum</a> in Beijing in 1945, dedicating it to <a href="/wiki/Duns_Scotus" title="Duns Scotus">Duns Scotus</a>. But as the <a href="/wiki/Chinese_Civil_War" title="Chinese Civil War">Chinese Civil War</a> ended, the <a href="/wiki/Chinese_Communist_Party" title="Chinese Communist Party">Chinese Communist Party</a> took over China and Allegra and his team had to leave for <a href="/wiki/Kowloon" title="Kowloon">Kowloon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Hong_Kong" title="Hong Kong">Hong Kong</a> in 1948.<sup id="cite_ref-newsva_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-newsva-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1948 the first three volumes of the <a href="/wiki/Old_Testament" title="Old Testament">Old Testament</a> were published by the <a href="/wiki/Studium_Biblicum_Franciscanum" title="Studium Biblicum Franciscanum">Studium Biblicum Franciscanum</a> in Chinese and over the next 12 years eight more volumes with explanatory notes were produced by the team, including the <a href="/wiki/New_Testament" title="New Testament">New Testament</a>. In 1954 along with four Chinese friars he went to the Studium Biblicum in <a href="/wiki/Jerusalem" title="Jerusalem">Jerusalem</a> to study original biblical texts for about a year. He lived mostly in Hong Kong thereafter, and he organized the 1st Ecumenical Bible Exhibition in Hong Kong in 1965.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Christmas_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Christmas day">Christmas day</a> in 1968 witnessed the culmination of his 40-year effort with the first publication of the one-volume Chinese Bible.<sup id="cite_ref-friars_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-friars-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> In 1975 the Chinese Bible Dictionary was published.
</p><p>Allegra died in Hong Kong on 26 January 1976.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="His_life_and_writings">His life and writings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: His life and writings">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His archived letters show his determination to translate the Bible into Chinese and his fascination with the study of scripture. Yet, at times his letters show the softer side of a man who missed the sound of church bells in Rome. Nonetheless he chose to work in the Orient to the end of his life.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Work_to_the_end">Work to the end</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Work to the end">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<figure class="mw-halign-right" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:AllegraMacau.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/3/38/AllegraMacau.jpg/125px-AllegraMacau.jpg" decoding="async" width="125" height="178" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/3/38/AllegraMacau.jpg 1.5x" data-file-width="181" data-file-height="258" /></a><figcaption>Gabriele Allegra with lepers on <a href="/wiki/Macau" title="Macau">Macau</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>He was known for working too hard, often resulting in the deterioration of his health. He used to say, "The most enviable fate for a Franciscan who doesn't obtain the grace of martyrdom, is to die while he is working".<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2015)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>In another letter Allegra wrote: "The work upon the Bible is hard and intense, but I must work because if I stop, I will never get up again."<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2015)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>Although the translation of the Bible was the main focus of Allegra's work, and he has usually been viewed as primarily a Scripture scholar, he took time to help the poor and the sick, particularly the <a href="/wiki/Leper" class="mw-redirect" title="Leper">lepers</a>. He frequently visited his "beloved lepers" in <a href="/wiki/Macau" title="Macau">Macau</a>, spending many of the holidays with them.
</p><p>In Allegra's later years he suffered severely from heart trouble and high blood pressure. A rest and recovery period was recommended in Italy, but he chose to return to the Studium Biblicum in Hong Kong to work to the end. He wrote: "Everybody thinks that I'm sick: I can still work, so let's go on! The ideal is worth more than life!"<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2015)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Duns_Scotus">Duns Scotus</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Duns Scotus">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Although Allegra's main focus was the translation of the Bible, he was also well read on other biblical and philosophical matters. He was an expert on the philosophy of <a href="/wiki/Duns_Scotus" title="Duns Scotus">Duns Scotus</a> and introduced <a href="/wiki/Teilhard_de_Chardin" class="mw-redirect" title="Teilhard de Chardin">Teilhard de Chardin</a> to some aspects of it that shaped de Chardin's thoughts on the subject. His expertise on that topic was internationally respected and <a href="/wiki/Oxford_University" class="mw-redirect" title="Oxford University">Oxford University</a> invited him to give the 700th centenary lecture on Duns Scotus.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_interests">Other interests</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Other interests">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>With the publication of the Chinese Bible in 1968, Allegra found time to focus on his other interests, which included the detailed study and analysis of the book <a href="/wiki/The_Poem_of_the_Man_God" class="mw-redirect" title="The Poem of the Man God">The Poem of the Man God</a> by the Italian writer and mystic <a href="/wiki/Maria_Valtorta" title="Maria Valtorta">Maria Valtorta</a>, on the life of <a href="/wiki/Christ" class="mw-redirect" title="Christ">Christ</a>.
</p><p>Allegra was an active member of the <a href="/wiki/Marian_Movement_of_Priests" title="Marian Movement of Priests">Marian Movement of Priests</a> and completed the translation of the writings of the Catholic priest <a href="/wiki/Stefano_Gobbi" title="Stefano Gobbi">Stefano Gobbi</a> into Chinese, shortly before he died.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2015)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Books_and_memoirs">Books and memoirs</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Books and memoirs">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Allegra wrote two books, one on the primacy of Christ, the other on the Virgin Mary's Immaculate Heart. He started writing his memoirs in 1975, but died while working on them in Hong Kong in 1976.
</p><p>The Church of San Biagio in <a href="/wiki/Acireale" title="Acireale">Acireale</a>, where he had entered the Franciscan seminary, holds some of his relics
.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/64/Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg/220px-Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="147" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/64/Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg/330px-Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/64/Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg/440px-Gabriele_Allegra_Shrine.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3456" data-file-height="2304" /></a><figcaption>Devotion to Allegra in the Roman Catholic Cathedral of Hong Kong</figcaption></figure>
<p>From his early days, Allegra was viewed as a favorite son of the Catholic Church. <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XII" title="Pope Pius XII">Pope Pius XII</a> said of him: "Tell this young priest that he has my special blessing and that I will pray for him every day. He will meet with many difficulties, but let him not lose courage. Nothing is impossible for him who prays, wills and studies. I shall not live to see this work completed, but I shall pray for him in heaven.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The cause for Allegra's <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonization</a> was started in 1984 by Bishop <a href="/wiki/John_Wu_(cardinal)" class="mw-redirect" title="John Wu (cardinal)">John Wu</a> in Hong Kong, 8 years after his death. He was declared <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Holy See</a> in 1994, and the promulgation of a decree of one miracle attributed to Allegra, required to conclude the beatification process, was approved in 2002.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> His decree of <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> was promulgated by the Holy See on that same day, but the beatification ceremony, which was set for October 26 of that year, was postponed. However, on the feast of the Assumption in 2012, the Roman Curia announced through the Sicilian Franciscan Holy Name Province, that Allegra would be beatified on September 29, 2012, at the Cathedral of Arcireale, Catania in Sicilia.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> He is, thus far, the only biblical scholar of the 20th century who has been beatified.
</p><p>In 2009 a web cast in English and Chinese was named after Allegra.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy">Legacy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gabriele_Allegra&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: Legacy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Blessed Gabriele M. Allegra OFM Fraternity is part of the International College of St. Anthony (CISA) in Rome, Italy.
</p>
