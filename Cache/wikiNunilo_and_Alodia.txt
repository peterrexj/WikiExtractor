The base of the carving reads "Nonyla", but her sister's name has been broken off.</div></div></div>
<p>Saints <b>Nunilo</b> and <b>Alodia</b> (died <i>c</i>. 842/51) were a pair of child <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyrs</a> from <a href="/wiki/Huesca" title="Huesca">Huesca</a>. Born of a <a href="/wiki/Interfaith_marriage" title="Interfaith marriage">mixed marriage</a>, they eschewed the <a href="/wiki/Islam" title="Islam">Islam</a> of their father in favour of their mother's <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a>. They were executed by the Muslim authorities of Huesca in accordance with <a href="/wiki/Sharia_law" class="mw-redirect" title="Sharia law"><i>sharia</i> law</a> as <a href="/wiki/Apostate" class="mw-redirect" title="Apostate">apostates</a>. Their <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 22 October.
</p><p>The girls were arrested during the persecutions conducted by <a href="/wiki/Abd_ar-Rahman_II" title="Abd ar-Rahman II">Abd ar-Rahman II</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Emir_of_C%C3%B3rdoba" class="mw-redirect" title="Emir of Córdoba">Emir of Córdoba</a>. When they refused to disavow their faith they were placed in a <a href="/wiki/Brothel" title="Brothel">brothel</a> and later beheaded. Their relics were revered at the <a href="/wiki/Monastery_of_Leyre" title="Monastery of Leyre">monastery of Leyre</a> in the tenth and eleventh centuries, when a portal was fashioned bearing their image, which still survives.
</p><p>The <i>Translatio sanctarum Nunilonis et Alodiae</i>, a short account of the translation of their <a href="/wiki/Relic" title="Relic">relics</a> to the monastery of Leyre in 851, survives in two tenth-century manuscripts. The children's relics were translated from Huesca to Leyre by Oneca, the wife of <a href="/wiki/%C3%8D%C3%B1igo_Arista" title="Íñigo Arista">Íñigo Arista</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kingdom_of_Navarre" title="Kingdom of Navarre">King of Navarre</a>; they were later contained in the <a href="/wiki/Leyre_Casket" title="Leyre Casket">Leyre Casket</a>, an ornate ivory casket produced in 1004/5 in Muslim Cordoba.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> There are some discrepancies between the account of the martyrdom in the <i>Translatio</i> and that recorded by <a href="/wiki/Eulogius_of_C%C3%B3rdoba" title="Eulogius of Córdoba">Eulogius of Córdoba</a>.
</p>
