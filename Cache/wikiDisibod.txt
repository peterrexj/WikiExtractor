<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Disibod,_Ausschnitt.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c5/St._Disibod%2C_Ausschnitt.jpg/220px-St._Disibod%2C_Ausschnitt.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="294" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c5/St._Disibod%2C_Ausschnitt.jpg/330px-St._Disibod%2C_Ausschnitt.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c5/St._Disibod%2C_Ausschnitt.jpg 2x" data-file-width="352" data-file-height="470" /></a><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Saint Disibod</b> (619–c.700) was an <a href="/wiki/Irish_monk" class="mw-redirect" title="Irish monk">Irish monk</a> and hermit, first mentioned in a <a href="/wiki/Martyrologium" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrologium">martyrologium</a> by <a href="/wiki/Hrabanus_Maurus" class="mw-redirect" title="Hrabanus Maurus">Hrabanus Maurus</a> (9th century). <a href="/wiki/Hildegard_of_Bingen" title="Hildegard of Bingen">Hildegard of Bingen</a> around 1170 composed a <i>Vita</i> of Saint Disibod <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He is commemorated on 8 September.
</p><p>According to Hildegard's <i>Vita sancti Dysibodi</i>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Disibod came to the <a href="/wiki/Frankish_Empire" class="mw-redirect" title="Frankish Empire">Frankish Empire</a> in 640 as a missionary, accompanied by his disciples Giswald, Clemens and Sallust. They were active in the <a href="/wiki/Vosges_mountains" class="mw-redirect" title="Vosges mountains">Vosges</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ardennes" title="Ardennes">Ardennes</a>, until, guided by a dream, Disibod built a cell at the confluence of the rivers <a href="/wiki/Nahe_(Rhine)" title="Nahe (Rhine)">Nahe</a> and <a href="/wiki/Glan_(Nahe)" title="Glan (Nahe)">Glan</a>, the location of the later <a href="/wiki/Disibodenberg" title="Disibodenberg">monastery of Disibodenberg</a>.
</p><p><span class="geo-inline-hidden noexcerpt"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1156832818">.mw-parser-output .geo-default,.mw-parser-output .geo-dms,.mw-parser-output .geo-dec{display:inline}.mw-parser-output .geo-nondefault,.mw-parser-output .geo-multi-punct,.mw-parser-output .geo-inline-hidden{display:none}.mw-parser-output .longitude,.mw-parser-output .latitude{white-space:nowrap}</style><span class="plainlinks nourlexpansion"><a class="external text" href="https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?pagename=Disibod&amp;params=49.777_N_7.701_E_"><span class="geo-nondefault"><span class="geo-dms" title="Maps, aerial photos, and other data for this location"><span class="latitude">49°46′37″N</span> <span class="longitude">7°42′04″E</span></span></span><span class="geo-multi-punct">&#xfeff; / &#xfeff;</span><span class="geo-default"><span class="geo-dec" title="Maps, aerial photos, and other data for this location">49.777°N 7.701°E</span><span style="display:none">&#xfeff; / <span class="geo">49.777; 7.701</span></span></span></a></span></span>
