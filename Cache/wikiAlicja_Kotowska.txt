<p><b>Alicja Jadwiga Kotowska</b> (<span style="display:none">(<span class="bday">1899-11-20</span>)</span>20 November 1899, Warsaw – 11 November 1939, near <a href="/wiki/Wielka_Pia%C5%9Bnica" title="Wielka Piaśnica">Wielka Piaśnica</a>) was a Polish <a href="/wiki/Religious_sister" title="Religious sister">religious sister</a> who was head of the <a href="/wiki/Resurrectionist_Congregation" title="Resurrectionist Congregation">Resurrectionist</a> convent in <a href="/wiki/Wejherowo" title="Wejherowo">Wejherowo</a> between 1934 and 1939. She was arrested by the <a href="/wiki/Gestapo" title="Gestapo">Gestapo</a> on 24 October 1939 during prayer and murdered alongside over 300 other Poles and Jews on 11 November in one of the <a href="/wiki/Massacres_in_Pia%C5%9Bnica" title="Massacres in Piaśnica">Piaśnica massacres</a>. Witnesses reported seeing her comfort Jewish children while being transported.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Bojarska_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bojarska-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> She was beatified by the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> in 1999 as one of the <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Martyrs of World War II</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alicja_Kotowska&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Kotowska was born on 20 November 1899 to a devout Catholic family, the second of eight children.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> During World War I she worked as a nurse. She took her vows on 2 February 1924, but continued her academic studies in addition to her duties, earning a Masters degree in chemistry in 1929. She later worked as a teacher and headmistress of a school.<sup id="cite_ref-:1_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:1-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alicja_Kotowska&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was arrested by the <a href="/wiki/Gestapo" title="Gestapo">Gestapo</a> on 24 October 1939 during prayer and murdered alongside over 300 other Poles and Jews on 11 November in one of the <a href="/wiki/Massacres_in_Pia%C5%9Bnica" title="Massacres in Piaśnica">Piaśnica massacres</a>. Witnesses reported seeing her comfort Jewish children while being transported.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
