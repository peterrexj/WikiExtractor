<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox"><caption class="infobox-title">Ebba</caption><tbody><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Gender</th><td class="infobox-data">female</td></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background:#ADD8E6">Origin</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Word/name</th><td class="infobox-data">Germanic</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Meaning</th><td class="infobox-data">modern form of Old English Æbbe.</td></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background:#ADD8E6">Other names</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Related names</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Ebbe" title="Ebbe">Ebbe</a> (masculine version)</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Ebba</b> is a feminine given name, the feminine version of <a href="/wiki/Ebbe" title="Ebbe">Ebbe</a>, which is a diminutive form of the <a href="/wiki/Germanic_languages" title="Germanic languages">Germanic</a> name <a href="/wiki/Eberhard" title="Eberhard">Eberhard</a> or Everhard, meaning "<a href="/wiki/Strong_(disambiguation)" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Strong (disambiguation)">strong</a>." Alternately, it may be a form of an <a href="/wiki/Old_English_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Old English language">Old English</a> name Æbbe, of unknown derivation, which was the name of several early saints.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> The name was the 10th most popular name given to girls born in <a href="/wiki/Sweden" title="Sweden">Sweden</a> in 2009.
</p>
