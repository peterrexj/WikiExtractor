<p><b>Saint Ermengol</b> (also <i>Armengol</i> or <i>Armengod</i>) or <b>Hermengaudius</b> (died 1035) was the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Urgell" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Urgell">bishop of Urgell</a> from 1010.
</p><p>Possibly born in the village of <a href="/wiki/Ayguat%C3%A9bia" class="mw-redirect" title="Ayguatébia">Ayguatébia</a>, he was the son of Bernat I, viscount of Conflent, and of his wife Guisla de Lluçià, and also the nephew and successor of <a href="/wiki/Sal%C2%B7la,_Bishop_of_Urgell" class="mw-redirect" title="Sal·la, Bishop of Urgell">Bishop Sal·la</a> and a member of the family of the <a href="/wiki/County_of_Conflent" title="County of Conflent">counts of Conflent</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman1-3_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman1-3-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>By 1002 Ermengol was an archdeacon and the following year the Bishop of Urgell, Sal·la, had made arrangements for Emermengol to succeed him.<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman1-3_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman1-3-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He began his episcopate by reforming the cathedral canons, along the lines of the life of Saint <a href="/wiki/Augustine_of_Hippo" title="Augustine of Hippo">Augustine of Hippo</a>, and granting them land in <a href="/wiki/Vallespir" title="Vallespir">Vallespir</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cerdanya" title="Cerdanya">Cerdanya</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Alt_Urgell" title="Alt Urgell">Alt Urgell</a>. In 1012, he travelled to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> for an audience with <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_VIII" title="Pope Benedict VIII">Pope Benedict VIII</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> who confirmed the possessions of his bishopric and its jurisdiction, including over <a href="/wiki/County_of_Ribagorza" title="County of Ribagorza">Ribagorza</a>. In 1017, Ermengol was involved in the establishment of the bishopric of <a href="/wiki/Roda_de_Is%C3%A1bena" title="Roda de Isábena">Roda</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman7-8_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman7-8-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> consecrated Borrell as bishop and received recognition by that bishop of his superiority in the local hierarchy. He is credited with the recaptured and rechristianization of the city of <a href="/wiki/Guissona" title="Guissona">Guissona</a> in 1024.<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman7-8_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman7-8-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was often at odds with the nobility of <a href="/wiki/Urgell" title="Urgell">Urgell</a><sup id="cite_ref-Bowman7-8_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman7-8-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> and he assisted in the construction of many public works. He built the cathedral of Urgell,<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman7-8_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman7-8-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> which was consecrated in 1040 by his successor, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Eribau&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Eribau (page does not exist)">Eribau</a>. For these public acts, he is celebrated annually in the <a href="/w/index.php?title=Fair_of_Saint_Ermengol&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Fair of Saint Ermengol (page does not exist)">Fair of Saint Ermengol</a>. He died in 1035 while assisting in the construction of a bridge near <a href="/wiki/El_Pont_de_Bar" title="El Pont de Bar">El Pont de Bar</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman1-3_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman1-3-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> The year following his death he was venerated and a few years later was canonised.<sup id="cite_ref-Bowman1-3_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bowman1-3-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His feast day is 3 November.
</p>
