<p><b>Conon</b> (3 June 1139 &#8211; 28 March 1236) was a <a href="/wiki/Basilian_monks" title="Basilian monks">Basilian</a> <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> at <a href="/wiki/Naso,_Sicily" title="Naso, Sicily">Naso</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sicily" title="Sicily">Sicily</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Religious_life">Religious life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Conon_of_Naso&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Religious life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A famous tale from the life of Conon tells that he made a <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a> to <a href="/wiki/Jerusalem" title="Jerusalem">Jerusalem</a>, and whilst there he received a vision. In this vision, Conon witnessed a priest he knew being suffocated by a snake. Having returning from the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Land" title="Holy Land">Holy Land</a>, he went directly to this priest and told him what he had seen. The priest at once confessed to Conon that he was taking church funds and keeping them for himself. Conon then persuaded his fellow priest to change his ways.
Another tale tells of how Conon healed a Sicilian boy of his <a href="/wiki/Apoplexy" title="Apoplexy">apoplexy</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy_and_veneration">Legacy and veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Conon_of_Naso&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legacy and veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 1571, <a href="/wiki/Naso,_Sicily" title="Naso, Sicily">Naso</a> was in the midst of a dire famine. The people of the city prayed for the intercession of Conon, their <a href="/wiki/Patron" class="mw-redirect" title="Patron">patron</a>. Conon then appeared to a ship captain, who brought grain to Naso, and thus the people of Naso survived the famine.
</p>
