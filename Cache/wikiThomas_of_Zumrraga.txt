<p><b>Thomas of Zumárraga and Lazcano</b> (<a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>: <i lang="es">Tomás de Zumárraga y Lazcano</i>; 9 March 1577 – 12 September 1622)<sup id="cite_ref-OPN_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Missionary" title="Missionary">Missionary</a> from <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a>. He was beatified in May 1867 by Pope <a href="/wiki/Pius_IX" class="mw-redirect" title="Pius IX">Pius IX</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-OPN_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Thomas_of_Zum%C3%A1rraga&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Thomas was born in Vitoria, in 1577. In 1594, he was professed in the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Preachers" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Preachers">Order of Preachers</a> in Vitoria. After he entered the <a href="/wiki/Convento_de_San_Esteban,_Salamanca" title="Convento de San Esteban, Salamanca">Convento de San Esteban</a> de <a href="/wiki/Salamanca" title="Salamanca">Salamanca</a>, he received the name of <b>Fray Tomás del Espíritu Santo</b> (Brother Thomas of the Holy Spirit).<sup id="cite_ref-H_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-H-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In 1601, he was sent to <a href="/wiki/Mexico" title="Mexico">Mexico</a> en route to the <a href="/wiki/Philippines" title="Philippines">Philippines</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-OPN_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Shortly after arriving, in 1603, Fray Francisco Morales, superior of the Dominicans in <a href="/wiki/Manila" title="Manila">Manila</a>, sent him to <a href="/wiki/Japan" title="Japan">Japan</a> to found a mission.
</p><p>He hid in the mountains, in the area of <a href="/wiki/Nagasaki" title="Nagasaki">Nagasaki</a> and <a href="/wiki/%C5%8Cmura,_Nagasaki" title="Ōmura, Nagasaki">Ōmura</a>. He was captured on 23 July 1617. After five years of imprisonment, he was burnt alive on 12 September 1622.<sup id="cite_ref-OPN_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OPN-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
