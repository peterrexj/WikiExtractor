<p>Saint <b>Triphyllius</b> (sometimes called <b>Tryphillius</b>) was born in <a href="/wiki/Constantinople" title="Constantinople">Constantinople</a> in the early fourth century. He was educated in law at the <a href="/wiki/Law_School_of_Beirut" class="mw-redirect" title="Law School of Beirut">school of Beirut</a>. He converted to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a> and was named bishop of <a href="/wiki/Nicosia" title="Nicosia">Nicosia</a>. Triphyllius was a follower of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Spyridon" title="Saint Spyridon">Saint Spyridon of Trimythous</a>. He was also an ardent supporter of St. <a href="/wiki/Athanasius_of_Alexandria" title="Athanasius of Alexandria">Athanasius of Alexandria</a> against the <a href="/wiki/Arianism" title="Arianism">Arians</a>, and consequently he was persecuted by them. St. Jerome considered him one of the most eloquent Church figures of the era.<sup id="cite_ref-OCA_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-OCA-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Oex_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Oex-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-joh_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-joh-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-cat_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-cat-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
