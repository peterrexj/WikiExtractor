					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Italian Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Amantius of Como</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Canzo" title="Canzo">Canzo</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">8 April 448<br /><a href="/wiki/Como" title="Como">Como</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">8 April</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Amantius of Como</b> (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">Sant'Amanzio di Como</i>) (died April 8, 448 AD) is venerated as the third <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Como" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Como">bishop of Como</a>.  He was preceded by <a href="/wiki/Felix_of_Como" title="Felix of Como">Felix of Como</a> and <a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Provinus&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Saint Provinus (page does not exist)">Saint Provinus</a>.  He was succeeded by <a href="/wiki/Saint_Abundius" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Abundius">Saint Abundius</a>.  His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 8 April.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amantius_of_Como&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>A late legend, based on a naive reading of the toponym <i><a href="/wiki/Canzo" title="Canzo">Cantium</a></i>, says he was born in <a href="/wiki/Canterbury" title="Canterbury">Canterbury</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-santibeati_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santibeati-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and served as an imperial dignitary before becoming a bishop. The legend continues he was a relative of <a href="/wiki/Theodosius_II" title="Theodosius II">Theodosius II</a> through his mother. He is credited with building the original <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Sant%27Abbondio" title="Basilica of Sant&#39;Abbondio">Basilica of Sant'Abbondio</a> outside of the <a href="/wiki/City_walls" class="mw-redirect" title="City walls">city walls</a> of Como.  The basilica was built to house several <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> associated with <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter" title="Saint Peter">Saint Peter</a> and <a href="/wiki/Paul_of_Tarsus" class="mw-redirect" title="Paul of Tarsus">Saint Paul</a>, which Amantius had brought from <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-santibeati_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santibeati-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Amantius_of_Como&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His relics were preserved at Sant'Abbondio until July 2, 1590, when they were transferred to the Chiesa del Gesù in Como.  The relics were later transferred to the church of San Fedele in Como, where they remain today.<sup id="cite_ref-santibeati_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santibeati-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
