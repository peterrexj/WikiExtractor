<p><b>Euphrasia Eluvathingal</b> (born <b>Rosa Eluvathingal;</b> 17 October 1877 – 29 August 1952) was an <a href="/wiki/Indian_people" title="Indian people">Indian</a> <a href="/wiki/Carmelites" title="Carmelites">Carmelite</a> <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Church" title="Syro-Malabar Church">Syro-Malabar Church</a>, which is an <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Catholic_Churches" title="Eastern Catholic Churches">Eastern Catholic Church</a> in <a href="/wiki/Kerala" title="Kerala">Kerala</a>. Euphrasia is said to have had a vision of the <a href="/wiki/Holy_Family" title="Holy Family">Holy Family</a>, at which point the illness she had long felt ceased. She was canonised as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> on 23 November 2014 in <a href="/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City">Vatican City</a>. Since the beheading of St. John the Baptist is celebrated on August 29, the feast of St. Euphrasia is postponed to <a href="/wiki/Liturgical_calendar_of_the_Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Church" title="Liturgical calendar of the Syro-Malabar Catholic Church">August 30
</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was born Rosa Eluvathingal on 17 October 1877 in a <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Syro-Malabar Catholic">Syro-Malabar Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint_Thomas_Christians" title="Saint Thomas Christians">Nasrani</a> family in <a href="/wiki/Kattoor,_Thrissur" title="Kattoor, Thrissur">Kattoor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Irinjalakuda" title="Irinjalakuda">Irinjalakuda</a>, <a href="/wiki/Thrissur_district" title="Thrissur district">Thrissur district</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Kerala" title="Kerala">Kerala</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Religious_life">Religious life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Religious life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Ollur025.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/Ollur025.jpg/220px-Ollur025.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="147" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/Ollur025.jpg/330px-Ollur025.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b7/Ollur025.jpg/440px-Ollur025.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2816" data-file-height="1880" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Ollur025.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The bed where Euphrasia died in St Mary's convent, <a href="/wiki/Ollur" title="Ollur">Ollur</a>, <a href="/wiki/Thrissur" title="Thrissur">Thrissur</a>, shown in the museum.</div></div></div>
<p>She endeavoured to lead a life of constant prayer and of devotion to the <a href="/wiki/Sacred_Heart_of_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Sacred Heart of Jesus">Sacred Heart of Jesus</a>, becoming known to many people as the <i>Praying Mother</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Miracles">Miracles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Miracles">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The first reported miracle was curing a carpenter from bone cancer.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Thomas Tharakan from <a href="/wiki/Anchery" title="Anchery">Anchery</a> in <a href="/wiki/Ollur" title="Ollur">Ollur</a>, a furniture polishing worker, was diagnosed with cancer by the <a href="/wiki/Jubilee_Mission_Medical_College_and_Research_Institute" title="Jubilee Mission Medical College and Research Institute">Jubilee Mission Medical College and Research Institute</a> in <a href="/wiki/Thrissur" title="Thrissur">Thrissur</a>. Thomas was admitted to the hospital for one week. Later before the surgery, a scan by the doctor showed no sign of tumour, despite an earlier scan report showing clear evidence of a tumour. Thomas's sister, Rosy, later claimed that cure was the result of her prayer to Euphrasia.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The second reported miracle happened to a seven-year-old child named Jewel from Aloor in <a href="/wiki/Thrissur_District" class="mw-redirect" title="Thrissur District">Thrissur District</a>. The child had a tumour in his neck which made it difficult for him to swallow any food. Doctors at Dhanya Hospital in Potta, Thrissur District, had said that this disease was incurable. As Jewel's family came from a poor background, their only option was to pray for divine intercession. After his grandmother prayed to Euphrasia, doctors noticed that his tumour began to shrink.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Dr Sasikumar of Dhanya Hospital examined him once again and found the tumour to have disappeared. Many other doctors examined the boy and stated that there was no medical basis for this event.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Stages_of_canonisation">Stages of canonisation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Stages of canonisation">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Servant_of_God">Servant of God</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Servant of God">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On 27 September 1986 the process of canonisation began in Ollur. On 13 August 1987 Father Lucas Vithuvatikal was appointed as Postulator. He made the oath as Postulator in the presence of Mar <a href="/wiki/Joseph_Kundukulam" title="Joseph Kundukulam">Joseph Kundukulam</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Metropolitan_Archbishop_of_Thrissur" class="mw-redirect" title="Metropolitan Archbishop of Thrissur">Metropolitan Archbishop of Thrissur</a> on 29 August 1987 and Euphrasia was declared a "Servant of God" on the same day.<sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Venerable">Venerable</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Venerable">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Sister Perigrin was appointed as Vice-Postulator on 9 September 1987 and in 1988 a Diocesan Tribunal for the <i>Cause of Euphrasia</i> was established by Kundukulam, established an <i>apostolic miracle</i> on 8 January 1989. and concluded its work on 19 June 1991. On 30 January 1990 the tomb of Euphrasia was opened and her remains were transferred to a newly built tomb inside the chapel of St. Mary's Convent. Her case was submitted to the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, on 20 April 1994, and on 5 July 2002 <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> declared her "Venerable".<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Blessed">Blessed</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Blessed">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>She was beatified on 3 December 2006 in <a href="/wiki/St._Anthony%27s_Forane_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Anthony&#39;s Forane Church">St. Anthony's Forane Church</a>, Ollur, with the declaration of the Major Archbishop, <a href="/wiki/Varkey_Vithayathil" title="Varkey Vithayathil">Varkey Vithayathil</a>, on behalf of <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI" title="Pope Benedict XVI">Pope Benedict XVI</a>.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (April 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> <a href="/wiki/Apostolic_Nunciature_to_India" title="Apostolic Nunciature to India">Apostolic Nuncio to India</a> Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Pedro_L%C3%B3pez_Quintana" title="Pedro López Quintana">Pedro López Quintana</a> and Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Jacob_Thoomkuzhy" title="Jacob Thoomkuzhy">Jacob Thoomkuzhy</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Archeparchy_of_Thrissur" title="Syro-Malabar Catholic Archeparchy of Thrissur">Syro-Malabar Catholic Archeparchy of Thrissur</a> joined 30 prelates and 500 priests for the beatification events.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (April 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Saint">Saint</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Euphrasia_Eluvathingal&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Saint">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On 3 April 2014, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> authorised the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> to promulgate the decrees concerning the miracle attributed to Euphrasia's intercession. This confirmed the Pope's approval of Euphrasia's canonisation. At a special Mass held at <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter%27s_Square" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Peter&#39;s Square">St Peter's Square</a> at <a href="/wiki/Vatican_City" title="Vatican City">Vatican City</a> on 23 November 2014, Pope Francis canonised Euphrasia as a saint. Mother Sancta, Mother General of Congregation of the Mother of Carmel (CMC), carried the relics of Euphrasia to the altar.<sup id="cite_ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">&#91;20&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
