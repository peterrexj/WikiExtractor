<p><b>Leonardo Kimura</b><span style="font-weight: normal"> (<span title="Japanese-language text"><span lang="ja">レオナルド 木村（レ゜オナルド）</span></span>, 1575 – 18 November 1619)</span> <a href="/wiki/Society_of_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Society of Jesus">SJ</a>, - blessed by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>, <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>, victim of <a href="/wiki/Anti-Catholicism" title="Anti-Catholicism">anti-Catholic</a> persecution in <a href="/wiki/Japan" title="Japan">Japan</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FS1_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS1-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SB_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SB-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Leonardo_Kimura&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The development of <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a> in Japan started in 1549 thanks to <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">St.</a> <a href="/wiki/Francis_Xavier" title="Francis Xavier">Francis Xavier</a>. Years later, he encountered a number of problems.<sup id="cite_ref-FS0_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS0-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> The growing number of followers changed the attitude of the authorities, who were afraid that their position would become weakened.<sup id="cite_ref-FS0_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS0-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Other reasons for the <a href="/wiki/Shogun" title="Shogun">shoguns</a> and <a href="/wiki/Daimyo" title="Daimyo">daimyos</a> to withdraw their support for the mission included the conflicts between Spanish and Portuguese merchants, as well as disputes between the missionaries themselves about methods of evangelization.<sup id="cite_ref-FS0_7-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS0-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> All these factors led to further waves of persecution.<sup id="cite_ref-FS0_7-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS0-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> After a period of increased missionary activity by the Catholic Church, it is estimated that 400,000 people professed this religion in Japan in 1613.<sup id="cite_ref-FS1_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS1-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> That year, the Shogun <a href="/wiki/Tokugawa_Hidetada" title="Tokugawa Hidetada">Tokugawa Hidetada</a> issued a decree under which, under the threat of losing their lives, all missionaries were to leave the country, and the practice and teaching of the religion were forbidden.<sup id="cite_ref-FS1_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS1-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NO_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NO-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Leonardo_Kimura&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Leonardo Kimura came from a noble Christian family.<sup id="cite_ref-NO_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NO-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His grandfather was baptized by St. Francis Xavier.<sup id="cite_ref-NO_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NO-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-sqpn_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> He was educated at the local Jesuit school, and from 1588 he worked as a <a href="/wiki/Catechesis" title="Catechesis">catechist</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-sqpn_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He joined the Society of Jesus in November 1602.<sup id="cite_ref-NO_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NO-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> By his own choice, he remained a religious brother, initially as a cook and tailor, and later as a catechist.<sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-sqpn_9-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NO_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NO-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> In December 1616, he was arrested on false allegations of murder.<sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> During the trial, he revealed his religious affiliation, for which he was sent to prison, where he undertook his apostolate and converted 96 people.<sup id="cite_ref-sqpn_9-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn-9">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NO_8-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NO-8">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> His attitude contributed to recognition by his fellow prisoners as a spiritual leader.<sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> On November 18, 1619, he was asked by a governor called Gonroku<sup id="cite_ref-SB_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SB-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> if he belonged to the Society of Jesus and if so, why did he not comply with the binding order to leave Japan, and he replied:
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>I have remained to preach faith in Jesus Christ and I want to preach it to my last breath.<sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> </p></blockquote>
<p>Later that same day, he was <a href="/wiki/Death_by_burning" title="Death by burning">burned at the stake</a> along with four other Christians in Nishizaka hill, Nagasaki.<sup id="cite_ref-FS2_5-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FS2-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-NSA_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NSA-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">The anniversary of his death (dies natalis)</a> is the day he is <a href="/wiki/Memorial_(liturgy)" title="Memorial (liturgy)">remembered</a> in the Catholic Church.<sup id="cite_ref-SB_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SB-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Leonardo Kimura was among the <a href="/wiki/205_Martyrs_of_Japan" title="205 Martyrs of Japan">205 Martyrs of Japan</a> <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> on May 7, 1867, by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_IX" title="Pope Pius IX">Pope Pius IX</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-NSA_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-NSA-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
