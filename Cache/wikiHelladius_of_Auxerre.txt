					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Helladius of Auxerre</b> (died 387) was a <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Auxerre" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Auxerre">bishop of Auxerre</a>. <a href="/wiki/St._Amator" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Amator">St. Amator</a> (died 418) was ordained deacon and <a href="/wiki/Tonsure" title="Tonsure">tonsured</a> by Helladius, which provides the earliest example of ecclesiastical tonsure mentioned in the religious history of <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>. He is commemorated on May 8.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
