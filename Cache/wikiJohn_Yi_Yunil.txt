					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Korean Catholic</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint John Yi Yun-il</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1126788409">.mw-parser-output .plainlist ol,.mw-parser-output .plainlist ul{line-height:inherit;list-style:none;margin:0;padding:0}.mw-parser-output .plainlist ol li,.mw-parser-output .plainlist ul li{margin-bottom:0}</style><div class="plainlist"><ul><li>January 21</li><li>September 20 (Roman calendar, along with The Korean Martyrs)</li></ul></div></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>John Yi Yun-il</b> (1822 – January 21, 1867) was a <a href="/wiki/Korea" title="Korea">Korean</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> who was killed during the 19th-century Korean persecution of <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christians</a>. He was a family man who made his living as a farmer and who also served as a <a href="/wiki/Catechist" class="mw-redirect" title="Catechist">catechist</a>, i.e. a teacher of Christian religion.<sup id="cite_ref-martyrology_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-martyrology-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Yi_Yun-il&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>John Yi Yun-il was killed at <a href="/w/index.php?title=Gwandeokjeong&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Gwandeokjeong (page does not exist)">Gwandeokjeong</a>, a military training center in the city of <a href="/wiki/Daegu" title="Daegu">Daegu</a> in modern-day <a href="/wiki/South_Korea" title="South Korea">South Korea</a>, in the course of the <a href="/wiki/Joseon_Dynasty" class="mw-redirect" title="Joseon Dynasty">Joseon Dynasty</a>'s <a href="/w/index.php?title=Catholic_Persecution_of_1866&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Catholic Persecution of 1866 (page does not exist)">persecution of Catholics</a> that began in 1866.<sup id="cite_ref-ucanews_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ucanews-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Though <a href="/wiki/Flogging" class="mw-redirect" title="Flogging">flogged</a> until the flesh of his limbs was torn, he remained strong in the Christian faith, and he was finally <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">decapitated</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-martyrology_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-martyrology-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sainthood">Sainthood</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=John_Yi_Yun-il&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Sainthood">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He is venerated in the Catholic Church as a <a href="/wiki/Christian_martyrs" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian martyrs">martyr</a> and was <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> as one of 103 Korean martyr <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a> by <a href="/wiki/John_Paul_II" class="mw-redirect" title="John Paul II">John Paul II</a> on May 6, 1984.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is one of two patron saints of the <a href="/wiki/Archdiocese_of_Daegu" class="mw-redirect" title="Archdiocese of Daegu">Archdiocese of Daegu</a>, along with <a href="/wiki/Our_Lady_of_Lourdes" title="Our Lady of Lourdes">Our Lady of Lourdes</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-gcatholic_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-gcatholic-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> His body is preserved in a chapel in the Archdiocese of Daegu. At the time of John's death, the site of his execution, Gwandeokjeong was used for the execution of criminals. Due to the number of Christian martyrs who were executed there, the site is now treated by the Archdiocese as a sacred place.<sup id="cite_ref-ucanews_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ucanews-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>John Yi Yun-il's feast day is January 21,<sup id="cite_ref-martyrology_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-martyrology-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> the day of his death, and he is also venerated along with the rest of the 103 Korean martyrs on September 20.<sup id="cite_ref-butler_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-butler-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
