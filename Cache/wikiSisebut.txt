					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">This article is about the Visigothic king. <div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">King of the Visigoths 612–621</div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Sisebut1.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/45/Sisebut1.jpg/220px-Sisebut1.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="220" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/45/Sisebut1.jpg/330px-Sisebut1.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/45/Sisebut1.jpg 2x" data-file-width="359" data-file-height="359" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Sisebut1.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Golden <a href="/wiki/Tremissis" title="Tremissis">tremissis</a> of <i>Sisebutus rex</i></div></div></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1a/Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg/220px-Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="332" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1a/Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg/330px-Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/1a/Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg/440px-Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2649" data-file-height="4000" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Toledo_-_Sisebuto.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Imagined portrayal of Sisebut in a statue in <a href="/wiki/Toledo,_Spain" title="Toledo, Spain">Toledo</a></div></div></div>
<p><b>Sisebut</b> (<a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a>: <i lang="la">Sisebutus</i>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>: <i lang="es">Sisebuto</i>; also <i>Sisebuth</i>, <i>Sisebur</i>, <i>Sisebod</i> or <i>Sigebut</i>) (<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 565 – February 621) was <a href="/wiki/Visigothic_Kingdom" title="Visigothic Kingdom">King of the Visigoths</a> and ruler of <a href="/wiki/Hispania" title="Hispania">Hispania</a> and <a href="/wiki/Septimania" title="Septimania">Septimania</a> from 612 until his death.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Sisebut&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He campaigned successfully against the remains of <a href="/wiki/East_Roman_Empire" class="mw-redirect" title="East Roman Empire">East Roman</a> power in <a href="/wiki/Spania" title="Spania">Spania</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-collins75_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collins75-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> strengthened Visigothic control over the <a href="/wiki/Basques" title="Basques">Basques</a> and <a href="/wiki/Cantabri" title="Cantabri">Cantabrians</a>, developed friendly relations with the <a href="/wiki/Lombards" title="Lombards">Lombards</a> of <a href="/wiki/Italy" title="Italy">Italy</a>, and reinforced the fleet which had been established by his predecessor <a href="/wiki/Leovigild" class="mw-redirect" title="Leovigild">Leovigild</a>.
</p><p>Sisebut was known for his devout piety to <a href="/wiki/Chalcedonian_Christianity" title="Chalcedonian Christianity">Chalcedonian Christianity</a>. In 612, upon his accession to the throne, he forced his <a href="/wiki/Jewish" class="mw-redirect" title="Jewish">Jewish</a> subjects to convert to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In 616, he ordered that those Jews who refused to convert to Christianity be punished with the lash. He was closely associated and amicable with the scholar and encyclopaedist <a href="/wiki/Isidore_of_Seville" title="Isidore of Seville">Isidore</a>, bishop of <a href="/wiki/Seville" title="Seville">Seville</a>, and is usually regarded as the author of a Latin poem on astronomy, <i>Carmen de Luna</i> or <i>Praefatio de Libro Rotarum</i>,<sup id="cite_ref-collins75_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-collins75-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> dedicated to a friend who is identified with Isidore.
</p><p>He had a son, who succeeded him as <a href="/wiki/Reccared_II" title="Reccared II">Reccared II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
