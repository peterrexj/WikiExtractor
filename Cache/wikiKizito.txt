<p><b>Saint Kizito</b> (1872 – June 3, 1886) was one of the <a href="/wiki/Martyrs_of_Uganda" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrs of Uganda">Martyrs of Uganda</a> and the youngest <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> slain by the King <a href="/wiki/Mwanga_II_of_Buganda" title="Mwanga II of Buganda">Mwanga II of Buganda</a>. He was baptized on 25/26 May 1886, by <a href="/wiki/Charles_Lwanga" title="Charles Lwanga">Charles Lwanga</a>, the leader of Uganda's Christian community at the time, at <a href="/wiki/Munyonyo_Martyrs_Shrine" class="mw-redirect" title="Munyonyo Martyrs Shrine">Munyonyo</a>, and burned alive on 3 June 1886 in <a href="/wiki/Namugongo" title="Namugongo">Namugongo</a>. He was <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> on 18 October 1964 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI" title="Pope Paul VI">Pope Paul VI</a> in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is on June 3rd. He is considered as the patron saint of children and primary schools.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
