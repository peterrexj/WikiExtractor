<p>Saint <b>Abran</b> (<a href="/wiki/Breton_language" title="Breton language">Breton</a> for 'Abraham'), was a 6th-century Irish <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> in Brittany.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Abran&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Abran was born in <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a> and was a brother of <a href="/wiki/Gibrian" title="Gibrian">Gibrian</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Abran and Gibrain  traveled to <a href="/wiki/Brittany" title="Brittany">Brittany</a> with their siblings. The five brothers and three sisters chose a life of devotion to <a href="/wiki/Trinity" title="Trinity">God</a> in <a href="/wiki/Consecrated_life" title="Consecrated life">consecrated religious life</a>. Abran lived in a <a href="/wiki/Hermitage_(religious_retreat)" title="Hermitage (religious retreat)">hermitage</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Marne_River" class="mw-redirect" title="Marne River">Marne River</a>, which had been given to him by <a href="/wiki/Saint_Remigius" title="Saint Remigius">Saint Remigius</a>.
</p><p>Abran and his siblings are all considered <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a> for their positive <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> influence upon the <a href="/wiki/Breton_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Breton people">Breton people</a>.
</p><p>Saint Abran's <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 8 May on the <a href="/wiki/Western_Rite_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Western Rite Orthodox">Western Rite Orthodox</a> and <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> calendars.
</p>
