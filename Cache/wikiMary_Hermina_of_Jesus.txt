					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">French Roman Catholic saint</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; <p>Saint <b>Marie-Hermine of Jesus</b> (1866–1900, born <b>Irma Grivot</b>) was a French nun and Mother Superior who died for her faith in China during the <a href="/wiki/Boxer_Rebellion" title="Boxer Rebellion">Boxer Rebellion</a> and was canonised in 2000. She and six other nuns had gone to China to create a small hospital and to staff an orphanage.<sup id="cite_ref-csi_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-csi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> She is one of the group known as the <a href="/wiki/Martyr_Saints_of_China" title="Martyr Saints of China">Martyr Saints of China</a> who were canonised by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> 1 October 2000.<sup id="cite_ref-vatican_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vatican-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-hagiog_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hagiog-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Marie-Hermine_of_Jesus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was born Irma Grivot on 28 April 1866 in <a href="/wiki/Beaune" title="Beaune">Beaune</a>, France, and she had only a basic education. Her father made barrels and he and her mother did not support her ambition to lead a religious life. She first took a job as a tutor to gain her independence and she joined the <a href="/wiki/Franciscan_Missionaries_of_Mary" title="Franciscan Missionaries of Mary">Franciscan Missionaries of Mary</a> in a prenovitiate in 1894 at <a href="/wiki/Vanves" title="Vanves">Vanves</a>. She went on to her novitiate at <a href="/wiki/Le_Ch%C3%A2telet" title="Le Châtelet">Le Châtelet</a> and this took some time as she was frequently ill.<sup id="cite_ref-csi_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-csi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6b/Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg/220px-Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="275" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6b/Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg/330px-Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6b/Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg/440px-Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg 2x" data-file-width="576" data-file-height="719" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Shanxi_martyrs_taken_in_maybe_1898.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The seven missionary nuns/Shanxi martyrs</div></div></div>
<p>In 1899 she was the mother superior of a group of seven sisters from the order who left Marseilles on 12 May 1899. They went to <a href="/wiki/Taiyuan" title="Taiyuan">Taiyuan</a>, China, arriving on 4 May 1899, to set up an orphanage at the mission there under bishop <a href="/wiki/Gregorio_Grassi" title="Gregorio Grassi">Gregorio Grassi</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-fmm_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fmm-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> The seven nuns were from France, Italy, Belgium and the Netherlands.<sup id="cite_ref-mysg_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mysg-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Marie_de_la_Paix_Giuliani" title="Marie de la Paix Giuliani">Marie de la Paix Giuliani</a> who was Italian and who was the youngest of the nuns became Mother Superior Marie-Hermine of Jesus's assistant.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> The nuns who left China were aware of the dangers as the Boxers had already killed other missionaries.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-martyrs_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-martyrs-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> They would be there during the <a href="/wiki/Boxer_Rebellion" title="Boxer Rebellion">Boxer Rebellion</a> which was a violent, anti-Christian, and anti-imperialist insurrection that ran from 1899 to 1901.<sup id="cite_ref-boxer_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-boxer-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 27 June 1900 the missionaries realised they were in peril and their Bishops advised that the sisters should remove their habits and escape in plain clothes. Marie-Hermine of Jesus as Mother Superior protested that the sisters should be allowed to stay and take the risk of being killed for their faith.<sup id="cite_ref-mysg_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mysg-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>On 5 July 1900, the Christians at the mission were told that they would be killed if they did not renounce their faith. Four days later the priests, nuns, seminarians and Christian lay workers were all murdered as part of what was callerd the <a href="/wiki/Taiyuan_massacre" title="Taiyuan massacre">Taiyuan massacre</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-fmm_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-fmm-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> It is estimated that 250 foreigners died during the Boxer rebellion. Some of these were embassy staff, but most were missionaries. It is thought that 100,000 Chinese people may have died.<sup id="cite_ref-boxer_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-boxer-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Marie-Hermine was beatified by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XII" title="Pope Pius XII">Pope Pius XII</a> on 24 November 1946 and canonised by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> on 1 October 2000 as one of a group of 120 <a href="/wiki/Martyr_Saints_of_China" title="Martyr Saints of China">Martyr Saints of China</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-csi_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-csi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-seven_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-seven-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
