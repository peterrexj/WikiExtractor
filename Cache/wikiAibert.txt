					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Aibert of Crespin</div><br /><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">O.S.B.</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">1060<br />Espein, Diocese of Tournai</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">April 7, 1140 (aged 80)<br /><a href="/wiki/Tournai" title="Tournai">Tournai</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholicism">Catholicism</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">April 7</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Aibert</b> (or <b>Aybert</b>) <b>of Crespin</b>, (also <b>Aibert of Tournai</b>) was a <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Benedict" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of Saint Benedict">Benedictine</a> <a href="/wiki/Monastic" class="mw-redirect" title="Monastic">monastic</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> revered for his intense life of prayer, <a href="/wiki/Asceticism" title="Asceticism">asceticism</a> and devotion to <a href="/wiki/Mary,_mother_of_Jesus" title="Mary, mother of Jesus">Mary</a>. His biography was written by a contemporary, Robert, Archdeacon of Oostrevand.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Aibert&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Aibert was born in 1060 in the village of Espain in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Tournai" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Tournai">Diocese of Tournai</a>, in present-day <a href="/wiki/Belgium" title="Belgium">Belgium</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His father was a knight, Aldbald of Espen, near Tournai.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Aibert fasted frequently, eating only bread or an apple when working in the fields.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_1866,_71_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler_1866,_71-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Moved by a wandering <a href="/wiki/Minstrel" title="Minstrel">minstrel</a> singing a lament about <a href="/wiki/Theobald_of_Provins" title="Theobald of Provins">Theobald of Provins</a>, Aibert began to live a life of asceticism under the direction of a hermit named John who lived in a wood near <a href="/wiki/Crespin_Abbey" title="Crespin Abbey">Crespin Abbey</a>. While Aibert and John shared a single cell, they survived primarily on uncooked herbs and, occasionally, bread.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_1866,_71_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler_1866,_71-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> "They chose to eat neither foods of animal origins, nor foods that had been cooked."<sup id="cite_ref-Roberts_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roberts-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Aibert joined the abbot on a pilgrimage to Rome, and upon his return in 1090, entered the monastery, becoming a Benedictine.  He spent twenty-five years at Crespin Abbey, being selected as <a href="/wiki/Provost_(religion)" title="Provost (religion)">provost</a> and cellarer.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Aibert was known for his veneration of the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary">Blessed Virgin Mary</a> and is reported to have said 150 <a href="/wiki/Hail_Mary" title="Hail Mary">Hail Marys</a> daily, 100 with <a href="/wiki/Genuflection" title="Genuflection">genuflexions</a> and 50 with prostrations. (At that time, the Hail Mary consisted of: "Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee; blessed art thou amongst women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb.")<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1115, Aibert was allowed to return to the solitary life of a hermit. Aibert built a hermitage in the wilderness and was sought out by those seeking spiritual advice and healing.<sup id="cite_ref-Roberts_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Roberts-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Sometime after returning to the wilderness, Aibert was ordained to the priesthood by Bishop <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Cambrai#Bishops" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Cambrai">Burchard of Cambrai</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_1866,_72_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler_1866,_72-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>  Aibert is noted to have said two <a href="/wiki/Mass_(liturgy)" title="Mass (liturgy)">Masses</a> each day, one for the living and one for the dead.<sup id="cite_ref-monks_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-monks-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He died in 1140<sup id="cite_ref-monks_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-monks-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> at the age of 80. His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is April 7.
</p>
