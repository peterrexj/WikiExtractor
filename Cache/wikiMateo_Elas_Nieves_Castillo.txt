<p><b>Mateo Elías Nieves Castillo</b> (21 September 1882 – 10 March 1928) was a Mexican Roman Catholic priest who was also a member of the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Augustine" title="Order of Saint Augustine">Order of Saint Augustine</a> who assumed the name of <b>Elias del Socorro</b> when he became a member of the order.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> beatified him on 12 October 1997 without a miracle because he was killed "in odium fidei" and therefore did not require a miracle. Instead it was proved that he was killed in hatred of the faith. A miracle is now under investigation for prospective sainthood.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mateo_El%C3%ADas_Nieves_Castillo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Mateo Elías Nieves Castillo was born on 21 September 1882 as a sick child to Ramón and Rita and was baptized on the same day due to fears that he would not live for long.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He almost died of <a href="/wiki/Tuberculosis" title="Tuberculosis">tuberculosis</a> at the age of twelve and his father soon died after he rallied from his ailment.
</p><p>Castillo was admitted to the Augustinian college in <a href="/wiki/Yuriria" title="Yuriria">Yuriria</a> at the age of 22 which was older than the standard admission age. He took his vows in 1911 and took the name of "Elias del Socorro". He was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> to the <a href="/wiki/Priesthood" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood">priesthood</a> on 9 April 1916 and served as a vicar in <a href="/wiki/Ca%C3%B1ada_de_Caracheo" class="mw-redirect" title="Cañada de Caracheo">Cañada de Caracheo</a> in 1921.
</p><p>At the time of the government persecution of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a> he refused to submit and moved to the hillside where he served his parish during the night so as not to be caught. He was arrested and was shot to death on the side of the road. His final words were: "Long live <a href="/wiki/Christ_the_King" title="Christ the King">Christ the King</a>!"<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mateo_El%C3%ADas_Nieves_Castillo&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The beatification cause for the <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> commenced in <a href="/wiki/Morelia" title="Morelia">Morelia</a> in October 1957 and concluded its work in June 1959. The process was ratified in 1992 and the <a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">Positio</a> - documentation on his murder being "in odium fidei" - was sent to the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> in 1994.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> approved the decision that Castillo died in hatred of the faith and beatified him on 12 October 1997.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>A miracle attributed to him was discovered and was investigated. The process was ratified in 2010. This miracle will have to be approved before his canonization can proceed.
</p>
