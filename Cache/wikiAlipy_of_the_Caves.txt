					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the seventh century ascetic, see <a href="/wiki/Saint_Alypius,_stylite" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Alypius, stylite">Saint Alypius, stylite</a>.</div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Painter_Alipius.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/26/Painter_Alipius.jpg/220px-Painter_Alipius.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="377" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/26/Painter_Alipius.jpg/330px-Painter_Alipius.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/26/Painter_Alipius.jpg/440px-Painter_Alipius.jpg 2x" data-file-width="583" data-file-height="1000" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Painter_Alipius.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Icon" title="Icon">Iconographic</a> sketch of Saint Alipy by <a href="/wiki/Viktor_Vasnetsov" title="Viktor Vasnetsov">Viktor Vasnetsov</a> (<a href="/wiki/Tretyakov_Gallery" title="Tretyakov Gallery">Tretyakov Gallery</a>, <a href="/wiki/Moscow" title="Moscow">Moscow</a>).</div></div></div>
<p><b>Alipy of the Caves</b> (? - 1114) - (also known as 'Venerable Alypius') <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Orthodox">Eastern Orthodox</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>, <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> and famous painter of <a href="/wiki/Icon" title="Icon">icons</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Cave_monastery" class="mw-redirect" title="Cave monastery">cave monastery</a> of <a href="/wiki/Kiev_Pechersk_Lavra" class="mw-redirect" title="Kiev Pechersk Lavra">Kiev Pechersk Lavra</a>. Saint Alipy was a disciple of Greek icon painters from <a href="/wiki/Constantinople" title="Constantinople">Constantinople</a><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and considered to be the first <a href="/wiki/Icon" title="Icon">icon</a> painter of <a href="/wiki/Kievan_Rus" class="mw-redirect" title="Kievan Rus">Kievan Rus</a>.
</p><p>According to medieval sources, Alipy created his icons with the help of God and angels. The saint took part in creation of <a href="/wiki/Mosaic" title="Mosaic">mosaic</a> painting in <a href="/w/index.php?title=Cathedral_of_the_Dormition,_Kyiv_Pechersk_Lavra&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Cathedral of the Dormition, Kyiv Pechersk Lavra (page does not exist)">Dormition Cathedral</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Lavra" title="Lavra">Lavra</a>. Presumably, the artist also participated in the painting of murals in St. Michael's Cathedral in Kiev. One of the icons painted by St Alypius survived and is now preserved in the <a href="/wiki/State_Tretyakov_Gallery" class="mw-redirect" title="State Tretyakov Gallery">State Tretyakov Gallery</a> in <a href="/wiki/Moscow" title="Moscow">Moscow</a>. This is the Sven Icon<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> of the <a href="/wiki/Theotokos" title="Theotokos">Theotokos</a> (or The Sven Caves Icon of the Mother of God) (feast days: 3 May and 17 August).
</p><p>The saint died on 17 August around the year 1114. When his body was discovered, it was found that the fingers of his right hand were still formed in the Orthodox manner of making the <a href="/wiki/Sign_of_the_Cross" class="mw-redirect" title="Sign of the Cross">Sign of the Cross</a>.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> of Saint Alipy is celebrated in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a> on 17 August (for those churches which follow the <a href="/wiki/Julian_Calendar" class="mw-redirect" title="Julian Calendar">Julian Calendar</a>, 17 August currently falls on 30 August of the modern <a href="/wiki/Gregorian_Calendar" class="mw-redirect" title="Gregorian Calendar">Gregorian Calendar</a>). He is also celebrated, in common with other saints of his monastery on 28 September (11 October), the "<a href="/wiki/Synaxis" title="Synaxis">Synaxis</a> of the Holy Fathers of Kiev whose relics lie in the <a href="/wiki/Near_Caves" title="Near Caves">Near Caves</a> of <a href="/wiki/Anthony_of_Kiev" title="Anthony of Kiev">Saint Anthony</a>".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His <a href="/wiki/Relic" title="Relic">relics</a> are preserved in Kiev Pechersk Lavra.
</p>
