<p><b>Pope Athanasius II of Alexandria</b>, <a href="/wiki/List_of_Coptic_Orthodox_Popes_of_Alexandria" class="mw-redirect" title="List of Coptic Orthodox Popes of Alexandria">28th Pope of Alexandria &amp; Patriarch of the See of St. Mark</a>.
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Peter_III_of_Alexandria" title="Peter III of Alexandria">Pope Peter III</a> of Alexandria died, the bishops, elders and people agreed to ordain Athanasius Patriarch. He retained the post until his death three years and nine months later.
</p><p>He is commemorated in the <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Calendar of Saints">Calendar of Saints</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Coptic_Orthodox_Church_of_Alexandria" class="mw-redirect" title="Coptic Orthodox Church of Alexandria">Coptic Church</a> on the 20th day of <a href="/wiki/Thout" title="Thout">Thout</a>, the day of his death.<sup id="cite_ref-Tout_20_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tout_20-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
