<p><b>Pelagius of Constance</b> was, a child <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> put to death in <a href="/wiki/Pannonia" title="Pannonia">Pannonia</a> during the persecution of <a href="/wiki/Roman_Emperor" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Emperor">Roman Emperor</a> <a href="/wiki/Numerian" title="Numerian">Numerian</a>. He was first revered in <a href="/wiki/Novigrad,_Istria_County" title="Novigrad, Istria County">Novigrad</a> (Cittanova) in <a href="/wiki/Istria" title="Istria">Istria</a>, <a href="/wiki/Croatia" title="Croatia">Croatia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pelagius_of_Constance&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Pelagius is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Konstanz" title="Konstanz">Konstanz</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Cathedral_of_Constance" class="mw-redirect" title="Cathedral of Constance">Cathedral of Konstanz</a>, owing to the transfer of <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> that were believed to be his to that city before or around 904 AD. He usually does not have a face in his depictions.
</p>
