					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">English Roman Catholic priest</div>
<p><b>Edward Stransham</b> (c. 1557 at <a href="/wiki/Oxford" title="Oxford">Oxford</a><sup id="cite_ref-Wainewright_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wainewright-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> – executed 21 January 1586, at <a href="/wiki/Tyburn" title="Tyburn">Tyburn</a>) was an English <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> priest. He is a Catholic martyr, <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> in 1929.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Edward_Stransham&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Edward Stransham was born at Oxford around 1557. He was educated at <a href="/wiki/St_John%27s_College,_Oxford" title="St John&#39;s College, Oxford">St John's College, Oxford</a>, becoming B.A. in 1575-6. He arrived at <a href="/wiki/Douai_College" class="mw-redirect" title="Douai College">Douai College</a> in 1577, and went with the college to <a href="/wiki/Reims" title="Reims">Reims</a> in 1578, but returned to England due to illness.<sup id="cite_ref-Wainewright_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wainewright-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1579, however, he returned to Reims with four potential students, and was ordained priest at <a href="/wiki/Soissons" title="Soissons">Soissons</a> in December 1580. Although ill he left for England 30 June 1581, as it was thought his native air might do him good.<sup id="cite_ref-Wainewright_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wainewright-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> With him went fellow priest <a href="/wiki/Nicholas_Woodfen" title="Nicholas Woodfen">Nicholas Woodfen</a>, of the London Diocese, ordained priest at Reims, 25 March 1581.
</p><p>In 1583 Stransham came back to Reims with ten Oxford converts. After five months there he went to Paris, where he remained about eighteen months at death's door from <a href="/wiki/Tuberculosis" title="Tuberculosis">consumption</a>.
</p><p>He was arrested in <a href="/wiki/Bishopsgate_Street_Without" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishopsgate Street Without">Bishopsgate Street Without</a>, London, 17 July 1585, while saying Mass, and was condemned at the next assizes for being a priest.
</p><p>He was hanged, drawn and quartered at Tyburn.  His beatification took place in 1929, under Pope Pius XI.
</p>
