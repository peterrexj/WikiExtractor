<p><b>László Batthyány-Strattmann</b> (<a href="/wiki/German_language" title="German language">German</a>: <i lang="de">Ladislaus Batthyány-Strattmann</i>; 28 October 1870 &#8211; 22 January 1931) was a <a href="/wiki/Nobility_and_royalty_of_the_Kingdom_of_Hungary" class="mw-redirect" title="Nobility and royalty of the Kingdom of Hungary">Hungarian aristocrat</a> and <a href="/wiki/Physician" title="Physician">physician</a>. Until 1914, he was known as <b>László Batthyány</b>. A devout <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholicism">Roman Catholic</a>, he became known as the "doctor of the poor" and was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by the church in 2003.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_Batthy%C3%A1ny-Strattmann&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>László <a href="/wiki/Batthyany" class="mw-redirect" title="Batthyany">Batthyány-Strattmann</a> was born on 28 October 1870, in <a href="/wiki/Dunakiliti" title="Dunakiliti">Dunakiliti</a>, <a href="/wiki/Austria-Hungary" title="Austria-Hungary">Austria-Hungary</a>, into a very old <a href="/wiki/Batthy%C3%A1ny" title="Batthyány">Hungarian aristocratic family</a>, the sixth of ten brothers. The family moved to Austria in 1876. His childhood was marred by the fact that his father left his family and converted to <a href="/wiki/Protestantism" title="Protestantism">Protestantism</a> in order to marry another woman.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Ladislaus' mother died when he was twelve years old.
</p><p>According to the will of his father, he first prepared himself to care for the vast property of the Batthyánys. He first studied <a href="/wiki/Agriculture" title="Agriculture">agriculture</a> in Vienna, later also a great number of other subjects, including <a href="/wiki/Chemistry" title="Chemistry">chemistry</a>, <a href="/wiki/Philosophy" title="Philosophy">philosophy</a> and <a href="/wiki/Music" title="Music">music</a>. In this chaotic period in his life, he also fathered an <a href="/wiki/Illegitimacy" class="mw-redirect" title="Illegitimacy">illegitimate</a> daughter.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Career_and_family">Career and family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_Batthy%C3%A1ny-Strattmann&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Career and family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG/170px-G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG" decoding="async" width="170" height="255" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG/255px-G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cc/G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG/340px-G%C3%BCssing_-_Denkmal_Batthyany-Strattmann.JPG 2x" data-file-width="2994" data-file-height="4490" /></a><figcaption>Batthyany-Strattmann memorial in Güssing</figcaption></figure>
<p>Batthyány became a student of <a href="/wiki/Medicine" title="Medicine">medicine</a> in 1896, and gained his degree of <a href="/wiki/Doctor_of_Medicine" title="Doctor of Medicine">M.D.</a> in 1900.<sup id="cite_ref-va_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-va-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He trained as a <a href="/wiki/General_practitioner" title="General practitioner">general practitioner</a>, but soon specialized in <a href="/wiki/Surgery" title="Surgery">surgery</a>, and later in <a href="/wiki/Ophthalmology" title="Ophthalmology">ophthalmology</a>. This period was also accompanied by a renewal of his religious faith. He met Countess Maria Theresia Coreth zu Coredo und Starkenberg, a devout Roman Catholic, whom he married in Vienna on 10 November 1898. The couple had thirteen children.
</p><p>In 1902 Batthyány opened a private hospital with twenty-five beds in <a href="/wiki/Kittsee" title="Kittsee">Kittsee</a>, <a href="/wiki/Austria" title="Austria">Austria</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-va_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-va-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> where he worked as a general practitioner, later specializing as a surgeon and oculist. During the <a href="/wiki/World_War_I" title="World War I">First World War</a>, the hospital was enlarged to take in wounded soldiers for treatment.
</p><p>In 1915, Batthyány and his family moved to the castle of <a href="/wiki/K%C3%B6rmend" title="Körmend">Körmend</a> in Hungary, which he had inherited upon the death of his kinsman <a href="/wiki/Prince_Edmund_Batthyany-Strattmann" title="Prince Edmund Batthyany-Strattmann">Prince Edmund Batthyany-Strattmann</a> in 1914;<sup id="cite_ref-va_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-va-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> he also inherited the title of "Prince" (German <i><a href="/wiki/F%C3%BCrst" title="Fürst">Fürst</a></i>, Hungarian <i>herceg</i>) and adopted the additional surname of "Strattmann". At Körmend he continued to practise as a doctor, becoming known for treating poor patients for no payment, which gained him the title of "doctor of the poor". Batthyány turned a wing of the castle into a hospital for ophthalmological patients.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=L%C3%A1szl%C3%B3_Batthy%C3%A1ny-Strattmann&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>At the age of 60, Batthyány was diagnosed with <a href="/wiki/Bladder_cancer" title="Bladder cancer">cancer of the bladder</a> and admitted to the Löw <a href="/wiki/Sanatorium" title="Sanatorium">sanatorium</a> in <a href="/wiki/Vienna" title="Vienna">Vienna</a>, <a href="/wiki/Austria" title="Austria">Austria</a>. After fourteen months of illness, he died in January 1931.
</p>

<p>The beatification process was begun in 1944 as a joint effort of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Vienna" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Vienna">Archbishop of Vienna</a> (Austria) and the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Szombathely" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Szombathely">bishop of Szombathely</a> (Hungary). The process became forgotten for some time afterwards, before it was taken up again in 1982 due to the initiative of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Diocese_of_Eisenstadt" title="Roman Catholic Diocese of Eisenstadt">bishop of Eisenstadt</a> (Austria), Stefan László. On 11 July 1992 László Batthyány-Strattmann was declared a <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> - a necessary step for beatification. He was beatified on 23 March 2003 by Pope <a href="/wiki/John_Paul_II" class="mw-redirect" title="John Paul II">John Paul II</a>. Five years later, on 23 March 2008, the fifth anniversary of his beatification, the Dr. Ladislaus Batthyány-Strattmann confraternity of prayer for the canonization of the poor's doctor was invested by the archbishop of Vienna <a href="/wiki/Christoph_Sch%C3%B6nborn" title="Christoph Schönborn">Christoph Cardinal Schönborn</a> as a private <a href="/wiki/Associations_of_the_faithful" class="mw-redirect" title="Associations of the faithful">association</a>, being entitled under the justification of <a href="/wiki/Canon_law" title="Canon law">canon law</a>.
</p>
