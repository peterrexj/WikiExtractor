<p><i><b>Athanasia</b></i> is a <a href="/wiki/Genus" title="Genus">genus</a> of flowering plants in the <a href="/wiki/Asteraceae" title="Asteraceae">daisy family</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<dl><dt>Species</dt></dl>
<p><i>Athanasia</i> is native to southern <a href="/wiki/Africa" title="Africa">Africa</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-mmmnm_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-mmmnm-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> The name is derived from the <a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a> a-, 'without', and thanatos 'death', alluding to the persistent dry involucral bracts.
