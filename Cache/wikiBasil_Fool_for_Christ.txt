<p><b>Basil the Blessed</b> (known also as <b>Basil, fool for Christ</b>; <b>Basil, Wonderworker of Moscow</b>; or <b>Blessed Basil of Moscow, fool for Christ</b> <a href="/wiki/Russian_language" title="Russian language">Russian</a>: <span lang="ru">Василий Блаженный</span>, Vasily Blazhenny) is a <a href="/wiki/Russian_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Russian Orthodox">Russian Orthodox</a> <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> of the type known as <i><a href="/wiki/Yurodivy" class="mw-redirect" title="Yurodivy">yurodivy</a></i> or "holy fool".
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Basil_Fool_for_Christ&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Vasbla4.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f2/Vasbla4.jpg/220px-Vasbla4.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="296" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f2/Vasbla4.jpg/330px-Vasbla4.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/f2/Vasbla4.jpg 2x" data-file-width="380" data-file-height="512" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Vasbla4.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/St_Basil%27s_Cathedral" class="mw-redirect" title="St Basil&#39;s Cathedral">Intercession Cathedral</a> in <a href="/wiki/Moscow" title="Moscow">Moscow</a> is named after the saint.</div></div></div>
<p>He was born to <a href="/wiki/Serf" class="mw-redirect" title="Serf">serfs</a> in December 1468 at the portico of the <a href="/wiki/Yelokhovo_Cathedral" title="Yelokhovo Cathedral">Epiphany Cathedral at Yelokhovo</a> (now in Moscow).<sup id="cite_ref-oca_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> His father was named Jacob and his mother Anna.
</p><p>Originally an <a href="/wiki/Apprentice" class="mw-redirect" title="Apprentice">apprentice</a> <a href="/wiki/Shoemaker" class="mw-redirect" title="Shoemaker">shoemaker</a>, he went to Moscow when he was sixteen. There he helped those who were ashamed to ask for alms, but were in need of help. He adopted an eccentric lifestyle of <a href="/wiki/Shoplifting" title="Shoplifting">shoplifting</a> and giving to the poor to shame the <a href="/wiki/Miser" title="Miser">miserly</a> and help those in need.  He went naked and weighed himself down with chains.  He rebuked <a href="/wiki/Ivan_the_Terrible" title="Ivan the Terrible">Ivan the Terrible</a> for not paying attention in church. Basil was said to have the gift of prophecy.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When he died on August 2, 1552 or 1557, <a href="/wiki/Macarius,_Metropolitan_of_Moscow" title="Macarius, Metropolitan of Moscow">St. Macarius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Metropolitan_of_Moscow" class="mw-redirect" title="Metropolitan of Moscow">Metropolitan of Moscow</a>, served his funeral with many clergy. He is buried in <a href="/wiki/St._Basil%27s_Cathedral" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Basil&#39;s Cathedral">St. Basil's Cathedral</a> in Moscow, which was commissioned by Ivan for commemoration of his conquest of Kazan' and was named for the saint later. Basil was formally <a href="/wiki/Canonisation" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonisation">canonised</a> in 1588.<sup id="cite_ref-oca_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oca-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  His <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is celebrated on August 2 (August 15, <a href="/wiki/Gregorian_Calendar" class="mw-redirect" title="Gregorian Calendar">N.S.</a>).
</p>
