<p><b>Saint Irene of Tomar</b> (<a href="/wiki/Portuguese_language" title="Portuguese language">Portuguese</a>: <i lang="pt"><b>Santa Iria</b></i>) (c.635 – c.653) was a Christian who was <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyred</a> for her faith in <a href="/wiki/Visigoth" class="mw-redirect" title="Visigoth">Visigothic</a> <a href="/wiki/Portugal" title="Portugal">Portugal</a>. Her parents, wishing to protect her, sent her to a convent school and a private tutor. The only times she left her house was to attend <a href="/wiki/Mass_(liturgy)" title="Mass (liturgy)">Mass</a> or pray at the sanctuary of Saint Peter. The legend of her life is possibly little more than that.<sup id="cite_ref-ecsv10_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ecsv10-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Irene_of_Tomar&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Legend says that Irene was born in Nabância, present-day <a href="/wiki/Tomar" title="Tomar">Tomar</a>, Portugal. She was born into an influential family, and her parents, wishing to protect her, sent her to a convent school and a private <a href="/wiki/Tutor" class="mw-redirect" title="Tutor">tutor</a>. The only times she left her house was to attend <a href="/wiki/Mass_(liturgy)" title="Mass (liturgy)">Mass</a> or pray at the sanctuary of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter" title="Saint Peter">Saint Peter</a>. A young <a href="/wiki/Nobility" title="Nobility">nobleman</a> named Britald saw her once and fell in love with her. Every time she left to go to church, he would follow her. Eventually he came forward with his proposal to court her; however, Irene made it clear that she would never marry him. When Britald became depressed, Irene hastened to make it clear to him that the reason for her celibacy was that she had given herself to God as a <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-ecsv10_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ecsv10-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Meanwhile, her tutor, a monk named Remigius (or Remígio) made improper advances to her, and when she declined, quit and spread vicious rumors about her. When asked why he was no longer tutoring the girl, he replied that he had left upon having learned that she was pregnant. In addition, he managed to give her a drink that brought about her belly to inflate. All these news circulated around the town, and eventually Britald learned of her supposed infidelity. Enraged, he hired a <a href="/wiki/Mercenary_soldier" class="mw-redirect" title="Mercenary soldier">mercenary soldier</a> to kill her. So, as Irene was returning home from visiting an elderly <a href="/wiki/Physical_disability" title="Physical disability">disabled</a> man, the assassin approached from behind and killed her with a single stroke of his <a href="/wiki/Sword" title="Sword">sword</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-ecsv10_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ecsv10-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Irene_of_Tomar&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Her body was thrown into river Nabão, that flows into <a href="/wiki/Tagus" title="Tagus">River Tagus</a>. Later, it was recovered <a href="/wiki/Incorruptibility" title="Incorruptibility">uncorrupted</a> from this river by <a href="/wiki/Benedictines" title="Benedictines">Benedictines</a> near the town of Scalabis. Legend says that her uncle abbot Celius had received from Christ a revelation about the true story of her niece and the location of her body. The monks gave her a proper burial, and spread her <a href="/wiki/Cult_(religious_practice)" title="Cult (religious practice)">cultus</a>. Eventually, so great was the reverence paid to the virgin saint, that the name of the town of <a href="/wiki/Scalabis" title="Scalabis">Scalabis</a> was changed to <a href="/wiki/Santar%C3%A9m,_Portugal" title="Santarém, Portugal">Santarém</a> ("Saint Irene").<sup id="cite_ref-ecsv10_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ecsv10-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Thus, Irene is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of Tomar and namesake Santarém.
Feast Day is 20 October.
</p>
