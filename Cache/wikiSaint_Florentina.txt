<p><b>Florentina of Cartagena</b> (died ca. 612) is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.  Born towards the middle of the sixth century in <a href="/wiki/Cartagena,_Spain" title="Cartagena, Spain">Cartagena</a>, <a href="/wiki/Hispania" title="Hispania">Hispania</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> she and her family were actively engaged in furthering the best interests of <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a>.
</p><p>Florentina was the sister of three Iberian bishops in the time of the Visigothic dominion (<a href="/wiki/Leander_of_Seville" title="Leander of Seville">Leander</a>, <a href="/wiki/Isidore_of_Seville" title="Isidore of Seville">Isidore of Seville</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Fulgentius_of_Cartagena" title="Fulgentius of Cartagena">Fulgentius</a>). She was younger than her brother Leander, later Archbishop of Seville, but older than Isidore, who succeeded Leander as archbishop of the same see. All four have been <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> by the Catholic Church.<sup id="cite_ref-web_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-web-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Losing their parents at an early age, she was placed under the guardianship of her brother, Leander, who had since taken monastic vows, and it was through his influence that Florentina embraced the <a href="/wiki/Ascetic" class="mw-redirect" title="Ascetic">ascetic</a> life.<sup id="cite_ref-aoca_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-aoca-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> She associated with herself a number of virgins, who also desired to forsake the world, and formed them into a religious community. Later sources declare their residence to have been the convent of S. Maria de Valle near <a href="/wiki/Ecija" class="mw-redirect" title="Ecija">Ecija</a> (Astigis), of which city her brother Fulgentius was bishop.<sup id="cite_ref-Kirsch_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirsch-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>  She later became abbess of the community. 
</p><p>Sometime before the year 600, her brother Leander, who died either in the year 600 or 601, wrote for her guidance an extant work dealing with a nun's rule of life and with contempt for the world.  In it the author lays down the rules according to which cloistered consecrated virgins should regulate their lives. He strongly advises them to avoid interaction with women living in the world, and with men, especially youths; recommends strict temperance in eating and drinking, gives advice concerning the reading of and meditation on Holy Scripture, enjoins equal love and friendship for all those living together in community, and exhorts his sister earnestly to remain true to her holy state.<sup id="cite_ref-Kirsch_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirsch-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Florentina regulated her life according to the advice of her brother, entered with fervour into the spirit of the religious life, and was honoured as a saint after her death. She died sometime early in the seventh century.<sup id="cite_ref-Kirsch_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Kirsch-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Florentina&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Florentina is venerated as the patroness of the diocese of <a href="/wiki/Plasencia" title="Plasencia">Plasencia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-aoca_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-aoca-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Her feast falls on 20 June. The name is written Florentia in the Roman martyrology, but Florentina is without doubt the correct form.
</p><p>An part of her bones were buried in the cathedral of Murcia (Spain), where they continue to be venerated. However, most of her remains are preserved in Berzocana (Spain), where she is venerated as well as her brother Fulgentius.
</p>
