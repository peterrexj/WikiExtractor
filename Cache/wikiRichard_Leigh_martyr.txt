<p class="mw-empty-elt">

<p><b>Richard Leigh</b> (c. 1557 &#8211; 1588) was an English <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> born in <a href="/wiki/Cambridge" title="Cambridge">Cambridge</a>, the scion of <a href="/wiki/Cheshire" title="Cheshire">Cheshire</a> <a href="/wiki/Landed_gentry" title="Landed gentry">gentry</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lord_of_the_manor" title="Lord of the manor">squires</a> of the <a href="/wiki/High_Legh" title="High Legh">West Hall, High Legh</a> since the 11th century.<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style></p><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Blessed</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Richard Leigh</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">Cambridgeshire, England</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">30 August 1588<br />Tyburn, London, England</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">30 August</td></tr></tbody></table>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Richard_Leigh_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:132px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_(Mixe).svg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b0/Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_%28Mixe%29.svg/130px-Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_%28Mixe%29.svg.png" decoding="async" width="130" height="143" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b0/Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_%28Mixe%29.svg/195px-Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_%28Mixe%29.svg.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b0/Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_%28Mixe%29.svg/260px-Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_%28Mixe%29.svg.png 2x" data-file-width="600" data-file-height="660" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Blason_Fr_famille_Labetz_(Mixe).svg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Leigh coat of arms</div></div></div>
<p>Richard Leigh was the son of Richard Leigh, who attended <a href="/wiki/Cambridge_University" class="mw-redirect" title="Cambridge University">Cambridge University</a>, and Clemence Holcroft, daughter of <a href="/wiki/John_Holcroft_(16th-century_MP)" title="John Holcroft (16th-century MP)">Sir John Holcroft</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was the subject of a childhood <a href="/wiki/Arranged_marriage" title="Arranged marriage">arranged marriage</a> at <a href="/wiki/Middleton,_Greater_Manchester" title="Middleton, Greater Manchester">Middleton</a>, 22 September 1562 with Anne Belfield (married in 1574 <a href="/wiki/Assheton_baronets" title="Assheton baronets">William Assheton</a>, <a href="/wiki/Steward_(office)" title="Steward (office)">steward</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Manorialism" title="Manorialism">manor</a> of <a href="/wiki/Rochdale_(ancient_parish)" title="Rochdale (ancient parish)">Rochdale</a>), daughter of Ralph Belfield, of <a href="/wiki/Clegg_Hall" title="Clegg Hall">Clegg Hall</a>, who had died without a male <a href="/wiki/Heir" class="mw-redirect" title="Heir">heir</a> in 1552. Her sister, Elizabeth, was married on the same day to <a href="/wiki/Barlow_Hall" title="Barlow Hall">Alexander Barlow (later Sir Alexander Barlow)</a> and both marriages were subsequently <a href="/wiki/Annulled" class="mw-redirect" title="Annulled">annulled</a> on the grounds of being so young that "doth not remember that he ever was marryed."<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Leigh attended <a href="/wiki/Shrewsbury_School" title="Shrewsbury School">Shrewsbury School</a> before studying <a href="/wiki/Divinity" title="Divinity">divinity</a> at <a href="/wiki/English_College,_Douai" title="English College, Douai">Reims</a> and at <a href="/wiki/English_College,_Rome" title="English College, Rome">Rome</a>, where he was <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> in 1586.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He returned to England but before reaching Cheshire, was arrested in London and banished. He returned to England again and was arrested on 4 July 1588, about two weeks before the arrival of the <a href="/wiki/Spanish_Armada" title="Spanish Armada">Spanish Armada</a>. In the aftermath of the failed invasion, there was an immediate reaction against Catholics.
</p><p>Imprisoned in the <a href="/wiki/Tower_of_London" title="Tower of London">Tower of London</a>, he was condemned at the <a href="/wiki/Old_Bailey" title="Old Bailey">Old Bailey</a> for being a <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> and hanged at <a href="/wiki/Tyburn,_London" class="mw-redirect" title="Tyburn, London">Tyburn</a> on 30 August 1588, with five others who have been declared <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a>: <a href="/wiki/Richard_Martin_(martyr)" title="Richard Martin (martyr)">Richard Martin</a>, <a href="/wiki/John_Roche_(martyr)" title="John Roche (martyr)">John Roche</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sir_William_Shelley" title="Sir William Shelley">Edward Shelley</a>, <a href="/wiki/Richard_Flower_(martyr)" class="mw-redirect" title="Richard Flower (martyr)">Richard Lloyd (alias Flower)</a> and <a href="/wiki/Margaret_Ward" title="Margaret Ward">Margaret Ward</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Wainewright_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wainewright-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Leigh was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> in 1929.
</p>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r1066479718"/><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Blessed Richard Flowers (Lloyd)</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">c.1566<br />Anglesey, Wales</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">30 August 1588<br />Tyburn, London, England</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">30 August</td></tr></tbody></table>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Richard_Flower">Richard Flower</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Richard_Leigh_(martyr)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Richard Flower">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The individual commonly known as Richard Flower was born Richard Lloyd, probably around 1566, to a notable family of <a href="/wiki/Anglesey" title="Anglesey">Anglesey</a>. He also went under the names Fludd and Graye.<sup id="cite_ref-Wainewright_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Wainewright-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> By 1584, he is mentioned in government interrogation reports as "the chiefest reliever of priests". The law at that time declared that anyone who knowingly "shall receive, relieve, aid, or comfort a <a href="/wiki/Seminary_priest" title="Seminary priest">Seminary priest</a>, are felons..."<sup id="cite_ref-Burton_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burton-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> Lloyd was accused of providing aid to a priest named William Horner, in the parish of St. Dunstan's, <a href="/wiki/Farringdon_Without" title="Farringdon Without">Farringdon Without</a>. According to <a href="/wiki/Christopher_Grene" title="Christopher Grene">Christopher Grene</a>, Lloyd gave Horner, alias Forest, a quart of wine. Grene says that since at the time of Lloyd's trial, Horner was only a supposed priest, being neither under arrest, condemned, nor outlawed, the court was unsure if he even was a priest. Lloyd was executed at Tyburn 30 August 1588, at about twenty-two years of age.<sup id="cite_ref-Burton_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Burton-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
