					<div id="contentSub"><div id="mw-content-subtitle"><span class="mw-redirectedfrom">(Redirected from <a href="/w/index.php?title=Veremundus&amp;redirect=no" class="mw-redirect" title="Veremundus">Veremundus</a>)</span></div></div>
					
					
					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For the later Asturian monarchs with the same name, see <a href="/wiki/Bermudo_(disambiguation)" class="mw-redirect mw-disambig" title="Bermudo (disambiguation)">Bermudo</a>.</div>
<p><b>Veremund</b> or <b>Veremundus</b> (later vernacular <i>Vermudo</i> or <i>Bermudo</i>) was a <a href="/wiki/Suevi" class="mw-redirect" title="Suevi">Suevic</a> <a href="/wiki/King_of_Galicia" class="mw-redirect" title="King of Galicia">king of Galicia</a> around 500. His existence is conjectured on the basis of a sixth-century inscription discovered at <a href="/w/index.php?title=Salvador_de_Vair%C3%A3o&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Salvador de Vairão (page does not exist)">Salvador de Vairão</a>. The date in the inscription is interpreted as either 485 or 535. Some scholars have dated the inscription to the eighth century, arguing that the <i>Veremundus</i> of the inscription is King <a href="/wiki/Bermudo_I_of_Asturias" title="Bermudo I of Asturias">Bermudo I of Asturias</a>.
</p><p>Veremund's reign would fall within a period of obscurity for the region of Galicia following the death of the valuable chronicler <a href="/wiki/Hydatius" title="Hydatius">Hydatius</a> (469) and the Sueves' conversion to <a href="/wiki/Arianism" title="Arianism">Arianism</a> (466). If the king existed, he was undoubtedly an Arian.
</p>
