<p><b>Edward Klinik</b> (21 July 1919 – 24 August 1942) was a <a href="/wiki/Polish_people" title="Polish people">Polish</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> anti-Nazi resistance fighter. One of the <a href="/w/index.php?title=Pozna%C5%84_Five&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Poznań Five (page does not exist)">Poznań Five</a> (Poznańska Piątka), he was guillotined in a prison in <a href="/wiki/Dresden" title="Dresden">Dresden</a> for his resistance work.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He is one of the <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Martyrs of World War II</a> who were <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by Pope <a href="/wiki/John_Paul_II" class="mw-redirect" title="John Paul II">John Paul II</a> in 1999.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
