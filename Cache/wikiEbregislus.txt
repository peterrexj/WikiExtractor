					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox biography vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above" style="font-size:125%;"><div class="fn" style="display:inline">Eberigisil</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">End of 6th century</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Occupation</th><td class="infobox-data role"><a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Cologne" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Cologne">Bishop of Cologne</a></td></tr></tbody></table>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_(cropped).JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/96/Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_%28cropped%29.JPG/220px-Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_%28cropped%29.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="320" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/96/Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_%28cropped%29.JPG/330px-Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_%28cropped%29.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/96/Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_%28cropped%29.JPG/440px-Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_%28cropped%29.JPG 2x" data-file-width="1366" data-file-height="1989" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Glasfenster_von_Saint_Evergislus_(cropped).JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Eberigisil</div></div></div>
<p><b>Eberigisil</b><sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;a&#93;</a></sup> (died before 593<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>) was <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Cologne" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Cologne">Bishop of Cologne</a>, being the fifth well-attested bishop, and the first with a <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> name.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Evergislu's tenure was marked by the unrest brought about by the migration of peoples, which dominated both city and country. Evergislus tried to build up religious life and ecclesiastical peace. 
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Eberigisil&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He is mentioned by <a href="/wiki/Gregory_of_Tours" title="Gregory of Tours">Gregory of Tours</a> but always using the past tense, so it is assumed that Eberigisil died before 594. Gregory says that Eberigisil built a church in honor of the martyr Mallosus in Birten, near <a href="/wiki/Xanten" title="Xanten">Xanten</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In 590 he settled disputes in a nunnery in <a href="/wiki/Poitiers" title="Poitiers">Poitiers</a> on behalf of <a href="/wiki/Childebert_II" title="Childebert II">Childebert II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Heilige_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Heilige-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Heriger_of_Lobbes" title="Heriger of Lobbes">Heriger of Lobbes</a> mentions him as a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Maastricht" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Maastricht">bishop of Maastricht</a> but this may well be a mix up.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Eberigisil&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>According to an 11th-century legend, Eberigisil came from a wealthy family in the Flemish town of <a href="/wiki/Tongeren" title="Tongeren">Tongeren</a>. When Bishop <a href="/wiki/Severin_of_Cologne" title="Severin of Cologne">Severin of Cologne</a> was visiting there (Tongern belonged to the Cologne ecclesiastical province at the time), he took the boy, who was considered pious, with him to the episcopal city on the Rhine, educated him and ordained him a deacon. When Severin died, Evergislus became his successor. On a later visit to his hometown of Tongeren, he is said to have been murdered by robbers and buried in the local <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Our_Lady,_Tongeren" title="Basilica of Our Lady, Tongeren">Church of Our Lady</a>. From a historical point of view, however, this narrative is not possible, since Severin was bishop of Cologne around 397.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Eberigisil&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 954 Archbishop <a href="/wiki/Bruno_the_Great" title="Bruno the Great">Bruno I</a> transferred the supposedrelics of Evergisilus«&#160;from <a href="/wiki/Tongeren" title="Tongeren">Tongeren</a> to <a href="/wiki/St._Cecilia%27s_Church,_Cologne" title="St. Cecilia&#39;s Church, Cologne">St. Cecilia's Church, Cologne</a>. Eberigisil was a co-patron of St. Cecilia's Church,<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> which is now a museum. Today his relics rest in the nearby <a href="/wiki/St_Peter%27s_Church_(Cologne)" title="St Peter&#39;s Church (Cologne)">parish church of St. Peter</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Heilige_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Heilige-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He is revered as a Catholic saint, honoured on 24 October, and is the patron saint of glaziers, and glass painters. 
</p><p>The parish church in <a href="/wiki/Bornheim_(Rheinland)" title="Bornheim (Rheinland)">Brenig</a> is dedicated to St. Evergislus.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Eberigisil is often depicted in episcopal regalia with a palm leaf.<sup id="cite_ref-Heilige_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Heilige-5">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
