<p><b>Osanna of Cattaro</b> (<a href="/wiki/Serbian_language" title="Serbian language">Serbian</a>: <span lang="sr">Озана Которска</span>, <small><a href="/wiki/Romanization_of_Serbian" title="Romanization of Serbian">romanized</a>:&#160;</small><span title="Serbian-language romanization"><i lang="sr-Latn">Ozana Kotorska</i></span>; 25 November 1493 – 27 April 1565) was a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Visionary" title="Visionary">visionary</a> and <a href="/wiki/Anchorite" title="Anchorite">anchoress</a> from <a href="/wiki/Kotor" title="Kotor">Cattaro</a>. She was a teenage <a href="/wiki/Religious_conversion" title="Religious conversion">convert</a> from <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodoxy" title="Eastern Orthodoxy">Orthodoxy</a><sup id="cite_ref-patron_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> of <a href="/wiki/Serbs_of_Montenegro" title="Serbs of Montenegro">Serbian</a> descent from <a href="/wiki/Zeta_under_the_Crnojevi%C4%87i" title="Zeta under the Crnojevići">Montenegro</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Luković-1965_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Luković-1965-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> She became a <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_St._Dominic" class="mw-redirect" title="Third Order of St. Dominic">Dominican tertiary</a> and was posthumously venerated as a saint in Kotor. She was later beatified in 1934.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Osanna_of_Cattaro&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Osanna was born in the village of <a href="/w/index.php?title=Relezi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Relezi (page does not exist)">Relezi</a><sup id="cite_ref-hr_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Luković-1965_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Luković-1965-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> in <a href="/wiki/Zeta_(state)" class="mw-redirect" title="Zeta (state)">Zeta</a> to an <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox</a> priestly family named Kosić and was <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptized</a> "Jovana" in that tradition.<sup id="cite_ref-srazzi_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-srazzi-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Brajović_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Brajović-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> Her father was priest Pero Kosić and her uncle was Marko Kosić, a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> with the name of "Makarije" who later became Eastern Orthodox Bishop of Zeta. Her grandfather Aleksa Kosić and her great grandfather Đuro Kosić were also Orthodox priests. She was a <a href="/wiki/Shepherd" title="Shepherd">shepherdess</a> in her youth, and developed the habit of spending her solitary hours in <a href="/wiki/Prayer" title="Prayer">prayer</a>. A story says that one day while watching the flocks, she saw a child lying asleep on the grass. Attracted by its beauty, she went to pick up the baby, but it disappeared, leaving Osanna with a feeling of great loneliness.<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Osanna continued to witness these apparitions. When she was 14 years old, her visions began to be followed by an odd desire to travel to the coastal <a href="/wiki/Republic_of_Venice" title="Republic of Venice">Venetian</a> town of <a href="/wiki/Kotor" title="Kotor">Cattaro</a> in <a href="/wiki/Albania_Veneta" class="mw-redirect" title="Albania Veneta">Albania Veneta</a> (<a href="/wiki/Bay_of_Kotor" title="Bay of Kotor">Bay of Kotor</a>, modern-day <a href="/wiki/Montenegro" title="Montenegro">Montenegro</a>), where she felt she could pray better. Her mother did not understand, and grudgingly arranged a position for Osanna as a servant to the wealthy Catholic <a href="/wiki/Bu%C4%87a" title="Buća">Buća</a> family, who allowed the girl as much time as she wished for church visits. In Cattaro, Osanna abandoned Eastern Orthodoxy and converted to <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholicism">Roman Catholicism</a>, and took the name Katarina (Catherine Cosie).<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-patron_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Osanna learned to read and write during her free time. She read religious books in both <a href="/wiki/Latin_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Latin language">Latin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>, especially the <a href="/wiki/Bible" title="Bible">Bible</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-hr_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In her late teens, Osanna felt a call to live the life of an <a href="/wiki/Anchorite" title="Anchorite">anchoress</a>. Though she was considered very young for such a calling, her spiritual director had her walled up in a cell built near Saint Bartholomew's church in Cattaro. It had a window through which Osanna could hear <a href="/wiki/Mass_in_the_Catholic_Church" title="Mass in the Catholic Church">Mass</a>, and another window to which people would occasionally come to ask for prayers or give food. Katarina made the customary promises of stability and the door was sealed.<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After an <a href="/wiki/Earthquake" title="Earthquake">earthquake</a> destroyed her first hermitage, she moved to a cell at Saint Paul's church, and became a <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_Saint_Dominic" title="Third Order of Saint Dominic">Dominican tertiary</a>, taking the name Osanna in memory of <a href="/wiki/Osanna_of_Mantua" title="Osanna of Mantua">Blessed Osanna of Mantua</a>. She would follow the Dominican Rule for the last 52 years of her life. A group of Dominican <a href="/wiki/Religious_sister" title="Religious sister">sisters</a> took up residence near her, consulting her for guidance, and came to consider her their leader. Osanna soon had so many followers that a <a href="/wiki/Convent" title="Convent">convent</a> was founded for them.<sup id="cite_ref-patron_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In her tiny cell, it is said that Osanna received many visions. These included the <a href="/wiki/Child_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Child Jesus">Christ as an infant</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary">Blessed Virgin Mary</a>, and several <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a>. Once <a href="/wiki/Devil_in_Christianity" title="Devil in Christianity">the Devil</a> appeared to her in the form of the Blessed Virgin and told her to modify her <a href="/wiki/Penance" title="Penance">penances</a>. By obedience to her <a href="/wiki/Confessor" title="Confessor">confessor</a>, Osanna managed to penetrate this disguise and vanquish this enemy.<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-patron_4-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>A convent of sisters founded at Cattaro regarded her as their foundress because of her prayers, although she never actually saw the place. When the city was attacked on August 9, 1539 by <a href="/wiki/Barbarossa_(Ottoman_admiral)" class="mw-redirect" title="Barbarossa (Ottoman admiral)">Khair ad-Din Barbarossa</a>, and Cattaro was threatened, the citizens of Cattaro ran to her for help.<sup id="cite_ref-hr_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> They credited their deliverance to her prayers and counsel. Again, her prayers were credited to saving them from the <a href="/wiki/Black_Death" title="Black Death">plague</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-patrick_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patrick-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Osanna_of_Cattaro&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Incorruptibility" title="Incorruptibility">incorrupt</a> body of Osanna was kept in the Church of St. Paul until 1807, when the <a href="/wiki/French_Army" title="French Army">French Army</a> converted the church into a warehouse. Her body was then brought to the Church of St. Mary. The people of Kotor venerated her as a saint. In 1905, the process for her beatification began in Kotor and was successfully completed in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>. In 1927, Pope Pius XI approved her <a href="/wiki/Cult_(religious_practice)" title="Cult (religious practice)">cultus</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-hr_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> and in 1934, she was formally <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-patron_4-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-patron-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Over the course of her life, the people of Kotor came to call her "the trumpet of the Holy Spirit" and the "teacher of mysticism." People from all walks of life came to her for advice, and she interceded particularly for peace in the town and among feuding families. Therefore, she was also called "the Virgin Reconciler" and the "Angel of Peace."<sup id="cite_ref-hr_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hr-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
