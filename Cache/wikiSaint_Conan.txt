<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Conan</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;">Bishop</th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">7th century</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data"><span style="display:none">(<span class="dday deathdate">648-01-13</span>)</span>13 January 648<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholicism">Roman Catholicism</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">13 January</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Conan</b> (<span class="rt-commentedText nowrap"><span class="IPA nopopups noexcerpt" lang="en-fonipa"><a href="/wiki/Help:IPA/English" title="Help:IPA/English">/<span style="border-bottom:1px dotted"><span title="/ˈ/: primary stress follows">ˈ</span><span title="&#39;k&#39; in &#39;kind&#39;">k</span><span title="/oʊ/: &#39;o&#39; in &#39;code&#39;">oʊ</span><span title="&#39;n&#39; in &#39;nigh&#39;">n</span><span title="/ən/: &#39;on&#39; in &#39;button&#39;">ən</span></span>/</a></span></span>; 7th century – January 684) was a bishop of the <a href="/wiki/Isle_of_Man" title="Isle of Man">Isle of Man</a> and an Irish missionary.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Conan&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Conan is not to be confused with St Conindrius (died 17 November 560), who is said to have been a disciple of <a href="/wiki/St_Patrick" class="mw-redirect" title="St Patrick">Saint Patrick</a>, and to have lived to a very advanced age.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Bollandists" class="mw-redirect" title="Bollandists">Bollandists</a> place Saint Conan amongst the early <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Sodor_and_Man" title="Bishop of Sodor and Man">bishops of Man</a>, and <a href="/wiki/John_Colgan" title="John Colgan">John Colgan</a> gives an account of his life and labours. Unfortunately, the history of the Isle of Man in the fifth and sixth centuries is very obscure, and it is difficult to verify biographical details.  However, Conan, who is also described as "Bishop of Inis-Patrick" left a distinct impression of his zeal for souls in the <a href="/wiki/Isle_of_Man" title="Isle of Man">Isle of Man</a>. Some authorities give the date of his death as 26 January, but Colgan, quoting from the ancient Irish martyrologies, gives 13 January, on which day Conan's feast is observed.<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Saint Conan is believed to have taught <a href="/wiki/Saint_Fiacre" title="Saint Fiacre">Saint Fiacre</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>There are also several minor Irish saints who share the name Conan, including Saint <a href="/w/index.php?title=Conan_of_Assaroe&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Conan of Assaroe (page does not exist)">Conan of Assaroe</a> (8 March), and Saint <a href="/w/index.php?title=Conan_of_Ballinamore&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Conan of Ballinamore (page does not exist)">Conan of Ballinamore</a> (26 April).<sup id="cite_ref-grattan_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-grattan-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
