<p><b>Saint Antiochus of Sulcis</b> (died c. 127 AD) was an early <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> of <a href="/wiki/Sardinia" title="Sardinia">Sardinia</a>.  The <a href="/wiki/Island" title="Island">island</a> and town of <a href="/wiki/Sant%27Antioco" title="Sant&#39;Antioco">Sant'Antioco</a> are named after him. 
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History_and_legend">History and legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Antiochus_of_Sulcis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History and legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Antiochus is a figure associated with the Sardinian <a href="/wiki/Mining" title="Mining">mines</a> from which the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Empire" title="Roman Empire">Romans</a> extracted <a href="/wiki/Minerals" class="mw-redirect" title="Minerals">minerals</a> and <a href="/wiki/Precious_metals" class="mw-redirect" title="Precious metals">precious metals</a>; the Romans condemned <a href="/wiki/Prisoners_of_war" class="mw-redirect" title="Prisoners of war">prisoners of war</a> and Christians to work these mines.  
</p><p>Antiochus' legend states that he was condemned to work the mines on the island that now bears his name.  The island, inhospitable and isolated during this period, was named <i>Plumbaria</i> at the time, after its source of <a href="/wiki/Lead" title="Lead">lead</a> (<i>plumbum</i>).  His legend also states that he was a physician during the reign of <a href="/wiki/Hadrian" title="Hadrian">Hadrian</a>.  He had converted many people in <a href="/wiki/Cappadocia" title="Cappadocia">Cappadocia</a> and <a href="/wiki/Galatia" title="Galatia">Galatia</a> to the Christian religion, and was therefore tortured and sent into <a href="/wiki/Exile" title="Exile">exile</a> by the authorities.  Antiochus, however, converted his jailer <b>Cyriacus</b> in Sardinia, and had built a small underground <a href="/wiki/Oratory_(worship)" title="Oratory (worship)">oratory</a> on Plumbaria, and was thus condemned to death there.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (February 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Antiochus_of_Sulcis&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Some sources state that he was martyred with Cyriacus at <a href="/wiki/Elaiussa_Sebaste" title="Elaiussa Sebaste">Sebaste</a> rather than at Sardinia.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
