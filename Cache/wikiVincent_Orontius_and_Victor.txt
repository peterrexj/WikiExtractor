<p><b>Saints Vincent, Orontius, and Victor</b> (d. 305 AD) are venerated as martyrs by the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic</a> and <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Orthodox</a> Churches. Tradition states that Vincent and Orontius were brothers from <a href="/wiki/Cimiez" title="Cimiez">Cimiez</a>.  They were Christians who evangelized in the <a href="/wiki/Pyrenees" title="Pyrenees">Pyrenees</a> and were killed at <a href="/wiki/Puigcerda" class="mw-redirect" title="Puigcerda">Puigcerda</a> with Saint Victor.<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated1_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Vincent should not be confused with the more famous <a href="/wiki/Vincent_of_Saragossa" title="Vincent of Saragossa">Vincent of Saragossa</a>, who is honored on the same <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Vincent,_Orontius,_and_Victor&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Their <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> were enshrined at <a href="/wiki/Embrun,_Hautes-Alpes" title="Embrun, Hautes-Alpes">Embrun</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-autogenerated1_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-autogenerated1-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> in a sanctuary built by <a href="/wiki/Palladius_of_Embrun" title="Palladius of Embrun">Palladius of Embrun</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
