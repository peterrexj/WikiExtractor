<p><b>Martín Antonio Gómez Rodríguez</b> (born 23 January 1989) is a <a href="/wiki/Panama" title="Panama">Panamanian</a> <a href="/wiki/Association_football" title="Association football">football</a> player who is currently playing for the <a href="/wiki/Liga_Paname%C3%B1a_de_F%C3%BAtbol" title="Liga Panameña de Fútbol">Liga Panameña de Fútbol</a> side <a href="/wiki/San_Francisco_F.C." title="San Francisco F.C.">San Francisco</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Club_career">Club career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mart%C3%ADn_G%C3%B3mez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Club career">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Gómez played for <a href="/wiki/Atl%C3%A9tico_Chiriqu%C3%AD" title="Atlético Chiriquí">Atlético Chiriquí</a> before moving to <a href="/wiki/San_Francisco_F.C." title="San Francisco F.C.">San Francisco</a> in 2011.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="International_career">International career</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Mart%C3%ADn_G%C3%B3mez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: International career">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He made his debut for the <a href="/wiki/Panama_national_football_team" title="Panama national football team">Panama</a> on March 13, 2009, against <a href="/wiki/Trinidad_%26_Tobago_national_football_team" class="mw-redirect" title="Trinidad &amp; Tobago national football team">Trinidad &amp; Tobago</a>.
</p><p>His final international was an August 2011 <a href="/wiki/Friendly_match" class="mw-redirect" title="Friendly match">friendly match</a> against <a href="/wiki/Bolivia_national_football_team" title="Bolivia national football team">Bolivia</a>, although he was named as an emergency replacement for <a href="/wiki/Ismael_D%C3%ADaz_(Panamanian_footballer)" class="mw-redirect" title="Ismael Díaz (Panamanian footballer)">Ismael Díaz</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Copa_Am%C3%A9rica_Centenario" title="Copa América Centenario">2016 Copa América Centenario</a>.
</p>
