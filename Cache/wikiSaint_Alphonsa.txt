<p><b>Alphonsa of the Immaculate Conception</b> or <b>Saint Alphonsa</b>, <a href="/wiki/Naming_ceremony" title="Naming ceremony">christened</a> at birth as <b>Anna Muttathupadathu</b> (19 August 1910– 28 July 1946),  was a <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a> and an <a href="/wiki/Educator" class="mw-redirect" title="Educator">educator</a> by <a href="/wiki/Religious_vocation" class="mw-redirect" title="Religious vocation">vocation (profession)</a>. She was also known for being a <a href="/wiki/Victim_soul" title="Victim soul">victim soul</a>, <a href="/wiki/Visionary" title="Visionary">visionary</a> and <a href="/wiki/Prophecy" title="Prophecy">prophetess</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Kottayam_district" title="Kottayam district">Kottayam pergunna</a> of the erstwhile <a href="/wiki/Travancore" title="Travancore">Travancore</a> province of <a href="/wiki/British_India" class="mw-redirect" title="British India">British India</a>, in the present-day <a href="/wiki/Kerala,_India" class="mw-redirect" title="Kerala, India">Kerala, India</a>. She is the first woman of <a href="/wiki/South_Asians" class="mw-redirect" title="South Asians">Indian origin</a> to be <a href="/wiki/Canonisation" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonisation">canonised</a> as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> after decades of enquiry by the <a href="/wiki/Sacred_Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Sacred Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Sacred Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> she is also the first saint of the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Church" title="Syro-Malabar Church">Syro-Malabar Church</a>, an <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Catholic">Eastern Catholic</a> community of <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Christianity" title="Eastern Christianity">Eastern Christianity</a>.
</p><p>In the 1990s, a <a href="/wiki/Postal_stamp" class="mw-redirect" title="Postal stamp">postal stamp</a> commemorating Alphonsa was published by Kerala state's former minister, <a href="/wiki/K_Karunakaran" class="mw-redirect" title="K Karunakaran">K Karunakaran</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Alphonsa was born as Annakutty Muttathupadathu in a <a href="/wiki/Malankara_Nasrani" class="mw-redirect" title="Malankara Nasrani">Malankara Nasrani</a> family to Joseph Muttathupadathil and Mary Puthukari<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> in Kudamalloor, near <a href="/wiki/Kottayam" title="Kottayam">Kottayam</a>, on 19 August 1910.<sup id="cite_ref-bangalore_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bangalore-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> She was <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptised</a> on 27 August. <i>Alphonsamma</i>, as she was locally known, was born in <a href="/wiki/Arpookara" title="Arpookara">Arpookara</a>, a village in the <a href="/wiki/Princely_state" title="Princely state">princely state</a> of <a href="/wiki/Travancore" title="Travancore">Travancore</a>, what would become <a href="/wiki/Kerala_state" class="mw-redirect" title="Kerala state">Kerala state</a>. The village lies within the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Archeparchy_of_Changanassery" class="mw-redirect" title="Syro-Malabar Catholic Archeparchy of Changanassery">Archeparchy of Changanassery</a>.
</p><p>Her parents nicknamed her <i>Annakutty</i> (little Anna). She had a difficult childhood characterised by the <a href="/wiki/Loss_of_a_parent" class="mw-redirect" title="Loss of a parent">loss of a parent</a>. Her mother died early, so her maternal aunt helped to raise her. Hagiographies describe her early life as one of hardships at the hands of her stern aunt and <a href="/wiki/Foster_mother" class="mw-redirect" title="Foster mother">foster mother</a> and one of <a href="/wiki/Bullying" title="Bullying">teasing</a> by schoolmates.<sup id="cite_ref-Corinne_G_Dempsey_1999_pp._150-176_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corinne_G_Dempsey_1999_pp._150-176-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Anna was educated by her great-uncle, Joseph Muttathupadathu. When Anna was three-years-old, she contracted <a href="/wiki/Eczema" class="mw-redirect" title="Eczema">eczema</a> and suffered for over a year.<sup id="cite_ref-sqpn1_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn1-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In 1916, Anna started school in Arpookara. She received her <a href="/wiki/First_Communion" title="First Communion">First Communion</a> on 27 November 1917. When the first school cycle ended in 1920, the time had come to transfer to Muttuchira, to the house of her aunt Anna Murickan, to whom her mother had entrusted get, before she died. She was raised in the Murickan family, which was an age-old and prominent family in the area. As a child her grandmother would tell her stories of <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a>, teach prayers&amp; <a href="/wiki/Christian_songs" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian songs">Christian songs</a>. As an <a href="/wiki/Adolescent" class="mw-redirect" title="Adolescent">adolescent</a> Anna received many marriage proposals from reputed families. However, she had a feeling in her mind that she wants to be a <a href="/wiki/Bride_of_Christ" title="Bride of Christ">bride of Christ</a> and do good things for people.<sup id="cite_ref-:0_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-:0-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> In these years, <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9r%C3%A8se_of_Lisieux" title="Thérèse of Lisieux">Thérèse of Lisieux</a> appeared and told her that she will one day become a saint. There are many similarities in the lives of Anna and Thérèse, and she was Anna's all-time favorite <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> and role model.
</p><p>Anna sacrificed her material fortune and wanted to dedicate her life to Jesus Christ. In 1923, Anna's feet were burnt when she fell into a pit of burning <a href="/wiki/Chaff" title="Chaff">chaff</a>; local hagiographies describe this as a self-inflicted injury in order to avoid her foster mother's attempt to arrange a marriage for her and thereby to fulfill her desire for becoming a <a href="/wiki/Religious_sister_(Catholic)" class="mw-redirect" title="Religious sister (Catholic)">religious sister</a> instead.<sup id="cite_ref-Corinne_G_Dempsey_1999_pp._150-176_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corinne_G_Dempsey_1999_pp._150-176-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> This accident left her partially disabled for the rest of her life.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Sister_Alphonsa">Sister Alphonsa</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Sister Alphonsa">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When it became possible, Anna joined the <a href="/wiki/Franciscan_Clarist_Congregation" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan Clarist Congregation">Franciscan Clarist Congregation</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Religious_congregation" title="Religious congregation">religious congregation</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Third_Order_of_St._Francis" class="mw-redirect" title="Third Order of St. Francis">Third Order of St. Francis</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-vatican_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vatican-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> and through them, completed her schooling.
</p><p>Anna arrived at the Clarist convent at <a href="/wiki/Bharananganam" title="Bharananganam">Bharananganam</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kottayam_district" title="Kottayam district">Kottayam district</a>, on Pentecost Sunday 1927.<sup id="cite_ref-bangalore_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bangalore-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> She received the <a href="/wiki/Postulant" title="Postulant">postulant</a>'s veil on 2 August 1928 and took the name <b>Alphonsa of the Immaculate Conception</b> in honor of <a href="/wiki/Alphonsus_Liguori" title="Alphonsus Liguori">Alphonsus Liguori</a>, whose feast day it was.<sup id="cite_ref-syro-malabar_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-syro-malabar-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> In May 1929 Alphonsa was assigned to teach at Malayalam High School at Vazhappally. Her foster mother died in 1930. She resumed her studies at <a href="/wiki/Changanacherry" class="mw-redirect" title="Changanacherry">Changanacherry</a>, while working as a temporary teacher at a school at Vakakkad. On 19 May 1930 Alphonsa entered the <a href="/wiki/Novitiate" title="Novitiate">novitiate</a> of the congregation at Bharananganam. On 11 August 1931 she completed the novitiate and took her first vows. And became a religious in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Health_decline">Health decline</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Health decline">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The period 1930–1935 was characterised by grave illness.<sup id="cite_ref-syro-malabar_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-syro-malabar-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Alphonsa took her permanent vows on 12 August 1936.<sup id="cite_ref-bangalore_4-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bangalore-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> Two days later she returned to Bharananganam from Changanacherry. She then taught high school at St. Alphonsa Girl's High School, but was often sick and unable to teach.<sup id="cite_ref-sqpn1_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn1-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> For most of her years as a Clarist sister she endured serious illness.
</p><p>In December 1936, it is claimed that she was cured from her ailments through the intervention of the <a href="/wiki/Kuriakose_Elias_Chavara" title="Kuriakose Elias Chavara">Kuriakose Elias Chavara</a><sup id="cite_ref-syro-malabar_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-syro-malabar-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> (who was beatified at the same ceremony as she), but on 14 June 1939 she was struck by a severe attack of <a href="/wiki/Pneumonia" title="Pneumonia">pneumonia</a> which left her weakened. On 18 October 1940, a thief entered her room in the middle of the night. This traumatic event caused her to suffer amnesia and weakened her again.
</p><p>Her health continued to deteriorate over a period of months. She received <a href="/wiki/Extreme_unction" class="mw-redirect" title="Extreme unction">extreme unction</a> on 29 September 1941. The next day it is believed that she regained her memory, though not complete health. Her health improved over the next few years, until in July 1945 she developed gastroenteritis and liver problems that caused violent convulsions and vomiting.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> During the last year of her life she came to know Sebastian Valopilly (later Bishop of Kerala) who frequently brought her communion. This bishop became famous in Kerala for championing the cause of poor people from all religious backgrounds who had come to live in Thalassery as a result of shortages elsewhere.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After a series of serious health problems, she died on 28 July 1946, at a young age of 35. She was buried at St. Mary's Catholic Church, <a href="/wiki/Bharananganam" title="Bharananganam">Bharananganam</a>, <a href="/wiki/Travancore" title="Travancore">Travancore</a> (present day <a href="/wiki/Kottayam_district" title="Kottayam district">Kottayam district</a>) in the <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Diocese_of_Palai" class="mw-redirect" title="Syro-Malabar Catholic Diocese of Palai">Diocese of Palai</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-kct_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-kct-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> Her death news made her loved ones sad. Many people came to see Alphonsa's funeral.<sup id="cite_ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Claims of her miraculous intervention began almost immediately upon her death and often involved the children of the convent school where she used to teach. On 2 December 1953, Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Eug%C3%A8ne_Tisserant" title="Eugène Tisserant">Eugène Tisserant</a> inaugurated the diocesan process for her beatification and Alphonsa was declared a <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a>.
</p><p>In 1985, <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> formally approved a miracle attributed to her intercession and on 9 July she became "Venerable Sister Alphonsa".<sup id="cite_ref-la_13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-la-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification">Beatification</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Beatification">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Alphonsa was beatified along with <a href="/wiki/Kuriakose_Elias_Chavara" title="Kuriakose Elias Chavara">Kuriakose Elias Chavara</a> at Kottayam, on 8 February 1986 by Pope John Paul II during his apostolic pilgrimage to India.
</p><p>During his speech at <a href="/wiki/Nehru_Stadium,_Kottayam" title="Nehru Stadium, Kottayam">Nehru Stadium</a>, the Pope said:
</p>
<blockquote><p> From early in her life, Sister Alphonsa experienced great suffering. With the passing of the years, the heavenly Father gave her an ever fuller share in the Passion of his beloved Son. We recall how she experienced not only physical pain of great intensity, but also the spiritual suffering of being misunderstood and misjudged by others. But she constantly accepted all her sufferings with serenity and trust in God. ... She wrote to her spiritual director: "Dear Father, as my good Lord Jesus loves me so very much, I sincerely desire to remain on this sick bed and suffer not only this, but anything else besides, even to the end of the world. I feel now that God has intended my life to be an oblation, a sacrifice of suffering" (20 November 1944). She came to love suffering because she loved the suffering Christ. She learned to love the Cross through her love of the crucified Lord.<sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Miracles">Miracles</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Miracles">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Hundreds of miraculous cures are claimed from her intervention, many of them involving straightening of clubbed feet, possibly because of her having lived with deformed feet herself.  Two of these cases were submitted to the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> as proof of her miraculous intervention. The continuing cures are chronicled in the magazine <i>PassionFlower</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-sqpn1_6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-sqpn1-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>
Bishop Sebastian reported: </p><blockquote><p> About ten years ago, when I was in a small village in Wayanad outside Manatavady, I saw a boy walking with some difficulty, using a stick. As he approached me I noted that both of his feet were turned upside down. I had a stack of holy cards in my pocket with Alphonsa's picture on them, so I pulled one of them out and gave it to the boy. When I told the boy that he should pray to this woman for the cure of his feet, the boy – he was quite smart for a ten-year-old boy – replied: "But I'm a Muslim, and, besides, I was born this way." I replied that God is very powerful, so let's pray. A few months later, a boy and a gentleman appeared at the house here. I didn't recognize them at first but soon learned that it was the Muslim boy with his father, here to tell me that his feet had been cured through their prayers to Sister Alphonsa. They showed me the calluses on the tops of his feet, and you could see the marks which had been made from the years of his walking with his feet turned under. Before they left, the three of us had our pictures taken.<sup id="cite_ref-Corinne_G_Dempsey_1999_pp._150-176_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Corinne_G_Dempsey_1999_pp._150-176-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup></p></blockquote>
<p>The boy had reportedly taken Alphonsa's picture card and asked Alphonsa to help fix his feet. Several days afterwards one of his feet supposedly turned around. He and the other members of his family then prayed for the cure of the second foot, which also supposedly turned around later.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Canonization">Canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=9" title="Edit section: Canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>On Sunday, 12 October 2008, <a href="/wiki/Pope_Benedict_XVI" title="Pope Benedict XVI">Pope Benedict XVI</a> announced her <a href="/wiki/Canonisation" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonisation">canonization</a> at a ceremony at <a href="/wiki/Saint_Peter%27s_Square" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Peter&#39;s Square">Saint Peter's Square</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup> Indians from across the world, especially people from Kerala, gathered at the ceremony in Rome. Among them was a 10-year-old Kerala boy Jinil Joseph whose clubfoot – a birth defect – was, in the judgment of Vatican officials, miraculously healed after prayers to Alphonsa in 1999.<sup id="cite_ref-la_13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-la-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The final ceremony for the canonization began with the holy relics of Alphonsa being presented to the Pope by Sister Celia, Mother General of the <a href="/wiki/Franciscan_Clarist_Congregation" class="mw-redirect" title="Franciscan Clarist Congregation">Franciscan Clarist Congregation</a>, the congregation to which Alphonsa belonged. Celia was accompanied by Vice Postulator Francis Vadakkel and former Kerala minister K. M. Mani, all holding lit candles.  Speaking in English, the Pope declared Alphonsa a saint, after reading excerpts from the Bible. The Pope himself read out the biography of Alphonsa after the ceremony.
</p><p>In the homily, Pope Benedict XVI recalled Alphonsa's life as one of "extreme physical and spiritual suffering".
</p>
<blockquote><p>  This exceptional woman ... was convinced that her cross was the very means of reaching the heavenly banquet prepared for her by the Father, , By accepting the invitation to the wedding feast, and by adorning herself with the garment of God's grace through prayer and penance, she conformed her life to Christ's and now delights in the "rich fare and choice wines" of the heavenly kingdom. </p></blockquote>
<p>"(Her) heroic virtues of patience, fortitude and perseverance in the midst of deep suffering remind us that <a href="/wiki/God" title="God">God</a> always provides the strength we need to overcome every trial," the pope stated before the ceremony ended.<sup id="cite_ref-vatican_7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-vatican-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The canonization was greeted with the bursting of firecrackers and the toll of church bells. St Mary's Forane church at Kudmaloor, her home parish, also celebrated a special Mass.<sup id="cite_ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17">&#91;17&#93;</a></sup> The grave at St Mary's Forane Church in <a href="/wiki/Bharananganam" title="Bharananganam">Bharananganam</a> where the Franciscan Clarist Sister was buried had a chapel built there, which houses her mortal remains.
</p><p>Reserve bank of India Issued a 5 Rupees Commemorative Coin in 2009 marking the birth centenary of Alphonsa.<sup id="cite_ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18">&#91;18&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Shrine">Shrine</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: Shrine">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Her tomb at St. Mary's Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, <a href="/wiki/Bharananganam" title="Bharananganam">Bharananganam</a>, has become a pilgrimage site as miracles have been reported by some of the faithful.
</p><p>St. Alphonsa Church Valiyakolly is known as the "Bharanaganam of North Kerala" (unofficial). This church is under the diocese of Thamarassery. Valiyakolly church is the first church named after Alphonsa in the diocese. And hence this church is one of the main pilgrimage centers of Saint Alphonsa in North Kerala. Novena on the name of Saint Alphonsa is being conducted here on every Friday evening<sup id="cite_ref-hindu1_19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hindu1-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Feast">Feast</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=11" title="Edit section: Feast">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Thousands of people converge on the small town of Bharananganam when they celebrate the feast of Saint Alphonsa from 19 to 28 July each year; her tomb has been designated as a pilgrimage site with numerous miracles being reported by pious devotees.<sup id="cite_ref-hindu1_19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-hindu1-19">&#91;19&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Documentary_video">Documentary video</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Alphonsa_of_the_Immaculate_Conception&amp;action=edit&amp;section=12" title="Edit section: Documentary video">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2><p>
In 2008, Sibi Yogiaveedan directed a <a href="/wiki/Documentary_film" title="Documentary film">documentary</a> video of Alphonsa, which known as "Vishudha (saint) Alphonsama- The passion flower". In this video the director tries to bring Alphonsa's <a href="/wiki/Childhood" class="mw-redirect" title="Childhood">childhood</a> friends and relatives. Which made the documentary video more interesting because they are sharing the real life experience with Alphonsa. In addition, Alphonsa's character was played by <a href="/wiki/Nikhila_Vimal" title="Nikhila Vimal">Nikhila Vimal</a>. The video was made in <a href="/wiki/Malayalam" title="Malayalam">Malayalam</a> language. For this reason, English <a href="/wiki/Subtitles" title="Subtitles">subtitles</a> have been included.<sup id="cite_ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20">&#91;20&#93;</a></sup>  </p><div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a5/Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg/220px-Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="293" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a5/Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg/330px-Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a5/Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg/440px-Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1920" data-file-height="2560" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Tomb_of_Saint_Alphonsa.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Tomb of Saint Alphonsa, St. Mary's Syro-Malabar Church, Bharananganam, Kerala, India</div></div></div>
