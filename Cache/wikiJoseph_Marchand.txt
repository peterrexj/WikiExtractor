<p><b>Joseph Marchand</b> (17 August 1803 – 30 November 1835) was a French missionary in <a href="/wiki/Vietnam" title="Vietnam">Vietnam</a> and a member of the <a href="/wiki/Paris_Foreign_Missions_Society" title="Paris Foreign Missions Society">Paris Foreign Missions Society</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bunson_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bunson-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He is now a <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> saint, celebrated on 30 November.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Personal_life">Personal life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Joseph_Marchand&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Personal life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Joseph_Marchand.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5f/Joseph_Marchand.jpg/220px-Joseph_Marchand.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="276" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5f/Joseph_Marchand.jpg/330px-Joseph_Marchand.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5f/Joseph_Marchand.jpg/440px-Joseph_Marchand.jpg 2x" data-file-width="496" data-file-height="623" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Joseph_Marchand.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Joseph Marchand.</div></div></div>
<p>Marchand was born in <a href="/wiki/Passavant,_Doubs" title="Passavant, Doubs">Passavant</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Doubs" title="Doubs">Doubs</a> department of France. At the age of 25 he joined the <a href="/wiki/Paris_Foreign_Missions_Society" title="Paris Foreign Missions Society">Paris Foreign Mission</a>, whose primary goal was (and still is) to evangelize countries in Asia.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Vietnam">Vietnam</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Joseph_Marchand&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Vietnam">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>In 1833, he was invited to join the <a href="/wiki/L%C3%AA_V%C4%83n_Kh%C3%B4i_revolt" title="Lê Văn Khôi revolt">Lê Văn Khôi revolt</a> led by <a href="/wiki/L%C3%AA_V%C4%83n_Kh%C3%B4i" title="Lê Văn Khôi">Lê Văn Khôi</a>, son of the late governor of southern Vietnam <a href="/wiki/L%C3%AA_V%C4%83n_Duy%E1%BB%87t" title="Lê Văn Duyệt">Lê Văn Duyệt</a>. He vowed to overthrow Emperor <a href="/wiki/Minh_M%E1%BA%A1ng" title="Minh Mạng">Minh Mạng</a> and replace him with <a href="/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ph%C3%BAc_M%E1%BB%B9_%C4%90%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng" title="Nguyễn Phúc Mỹ Đường">My Duong</a>, the son of Minh Mạng's late elder brother <a href="/wiki/Nguy%E1%BB%85n_Ph%C3%BAc_C%E1%BA%A3nh" title="Nguyễn Phúc Cảnh">Nguyễn Phúc Cảnh</a>, who were both Catholics. Khoi appealed to other Catholics to join in overthrowing Minh Mạng and installing a Catholic emperor. They quickly seized the <a href="/wiki/Citadel_of_Saigon" title="Citadel of Saigon">Citadel of Saigon</a> in an uprising lasting two years.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (September 2017)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>In 1835, he was arrested and later executed in <a href="/wiki/Hu%E1%BA%BF" title="Huế">Huế</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> subsequently becoming a Catholic martyr after having his flesh pulled from his bones by tongs,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> known as <a href="/wiki/Slow_slicing" class="mw-redirect" title="Slow slicing">death by a thousand cuts</a>.
</p><p>Marchand was canonized by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> in 1988. His feast day is 30 November and his joint feast day with the <a href="/wiki/Vietnamese_Martyrs" title="Vietnamese Martyrs">Vietnamese Martyrs</a> is 24 November.<sup id="cite_ref-Bunson_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bunson-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
