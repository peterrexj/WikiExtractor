<p><b>Ezequiél Moreno y Díaz</b>, OAR was a Spanish Catholic friar in the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Augustinian_Recollects" title="Order of Augustinian Recollects">Order of Augustinian Recollects</a> and now venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a>. He was born on April 9, 1848, in <a href="/wiki/Alfaro,_La_Rioja" title="Alfaro, La Rioja">Alfaro, La Rioja</a>, <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a> and later served as a missionary to the <a href="/wiki/Philippines" title="Philippines">Philippines</a>. He also became the <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">Bishop</a> of <a href="/wiki/Pinara" title="Pinara">Pinara</a> and later of <a href="/wiki/Pasto,_Colombia" title="Pasto, Colombia">Pasto</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Colombia" title="Colombia">Colombia</a>.
</p><p>His nephew Julián Moreno is also venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Blessed</a> because of his martyrdom in <a href="/wiki/Motril" title="Motril">Motril</a>. Moreno is popularly invoked as the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron</a> of <a href="/wiki/Cancer" title="Cancer">cancer</a> patients.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life_and_entry_to_the_Order_of_Augustinian_Recollects">Early life and entry to the Order of Augustinian Recollects</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life and entry to the Order of Augustinian Recollects">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Born in the city of <a href="/wiki/Alfaro,_La_Rioja" title="Alfaro, La Rioja">Alfaro, La Rioja</a>, Spain to Félix Moreno and María Josefa Díaz, he was the third of six children along with Eustaquio, Juana, Valentina and Benigna. His fourth sister, named María de las Candelas, died.
</p><p>Moreno first wore the habit of the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Augustinian_Recollects" title="Order of Augustinian Recollects">Recoletos</a> in <a href="/wiki/Monteagudo,_Navarre" title="Monteagudo, Navarre">Monteagudo</a>, <a href="/wiki/Navarra" class="mw-redirect" title="Navarra">Navarra</a>, <a href="/wiki/Spain" title="Spain">Spain</a> on September 21, 1864. Later, he made his solemn vow in Marcialla (also in <a href="/wiki/Navarra" class="mw-redirect" title="Navarra">Navarra</a>) on September 22, 1868.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Philippine_missions">Philippine missions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Philippine missions">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arrival_and_ordination_in_Manila">Arrival and ordination in Manila</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Arrival and ordination in Manila">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The monastery in Monteagudo was known for dispatching missionaries to both the Americas and the <a href="/wiki/Philippines" title="Philippines">Philippines</a>. As expected, Moreno sailed for <a href="/wiki/Manila" title="Manila">Manila</a>, the capital of the <a href="/wiki/Captaincy_General_of_the_Philippines" title="Captaincy General of the Philippines">Captaincy General of the Philippines</a>, arriving there on February 10, 1870.
</p><p>In the middle that year, Moreno sailed on to the <a href="/wiki/Visayas" title="Visayas">Visayan</a> town of <a href="/wiki/Jaro,_Iloilo" class="mw-redirect" title="Jaro, Iloilo">Jaro</a>, <a href="/wiki/Iloilo" title="Iloilo">Iloilo</a> where he received and finished his <a href="/wiki/Minor_orders" title="Minor orders">minor orders</a>. A year later, he returned to Manila to receive his <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">sacerdotal orders</a> from the <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Manila" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Manila">Archbishop of Manila</a>, Gregorio Melitón Martínez. Today, the letter of the archbishop informing him of his sacerdotal ordination is one of the best-preserved manuscripts from the period.
After ordination, he was immediately sent to his first mission in <a href="/wiki/Calapan,_Oriental_Mindoro" class="mw-redirect" title="Calapan, Oriental Mindoro">Calapan, Oriental Mindoro</a>. There, he became proficient in <a href="/wiki/Tagalog_language" title="Tagalog language">Tagalog</a> that at his <a href="/wiki/Canonisation" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonisation">canonisation</a>, <a href="/wiki/Filipino_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Filipino people">Filipino</a> Catholics remarked that the language is now "a language of the <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saints</a>."
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Provincial_assignments">Provincial assignments</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Provincial assignments">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Two years after his arrival in the colony, he undertook the arduous task of being <a href="/wiki/Military_Chaplain" class="mw-redirect" title="Military Chaplain">Military Chaplain</a> to a <a href="/wiki/Penal_colony" title="Penal colony">penal colony</a>, now <a href="/wiki/Iwahig_Prison_and_Penal_Farm" title="Iwahig Prison and Penal Farm">Iwahig Prison and Penal Farm</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Puerto_Princesa_City" class="mw-redirect" title="Puerto Princesa City">Puerto Princesa City</a>. He was among the founders of the town of <a href="/wiki/Aborlan,_Palawan" class="mw-redirect" title="Aborlan, Palawan">Aborlan, Palawan</a> and Barangay Inagawan in <a href="/wiki/Puerto_Princesa" title="Puerto Princesa">Puerto Princesa</a>. Moreno was struck by <a href="/wiki/Malaria" title="Malaria">malaria</a>, which forced him to leave the island.
</p><p>When he was able to recover from the disease, he was again made in charge of a mission in Calapan. Though only 28 years old, both the Archbishop of Manila and his <a href="/wiki/Recollect" class="mw-redirect" title="Recollect">Recollect</a> <a href="/wiki/Prior_(ecclesiastical)" title="Prior (ecclesiastical)">Prior</a> assigned him as <a href="/wiki/Vicar_Forane" class="mw-redirect" title="Vicar Forane">Vicar Forane</a> of <a href="/wiki/Mindoro" title="Mindoro">Mindoro</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Return_to_Manila_and_surrounding_areas">Return to Manila and surrounding areas</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Return to Manila and surrounding areas">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In 1876, he was transferred to what is now <a href="/wiki/Las_Pi%C3%B1as" title="Las Piñas">Las Piñas</a> and worked zealously for the development of the lives of the people in the area. The town suffered many major calamities during his pastorship, yet the people did not lose hope because of his example. This saintly way of life that he exemplified caused the people of Las Piñas to oppose his transfer to <a href="/wiki/Santo_Tomas,_Batangas" title="Santo Tomas, Batangas">Santo Tomás</a>, <a href="/wiki/Batangas" title="Batangas">Batangas</a>.
</p><p>The appeal, as strong as it was, was not successful and Moreno was transferred to Santo Tomás. However, he was recalled to Manila because he was appointed General Preacher of the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_Augustinian_Recollects" title="Order of Augustinian Recollects">Order of Augustinian Recollects</a>. The people of Santo Tomas also made an appeal to suspend the order but like what happened in Las Piñas, their request was not granted. He assumed the post in October 1880 and as Preacher General, was assigned <a href="/wiki/Parish_priest" class="mw-redirect" title="Parish priest">parish priest</a> of <a href="/wiki/Santa_Cruz_Church_(Manila)" title="Santa Cruz Church (Manila)">Santa Cruz Church</a>, assuming the post in February 1881.
</p><p>A year later, he was assigned to take charge of the Recollect <a href="/wiki/Hacienda" title="Hacienda">hacienda</a> in the towns of <a href="/wiki/Imus" title="Imus">Imus</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bacoor" title="Bacoor">Bacoor</a> in <a href="/wiki/Cavite" title="Cavite">Cavite</a>. During his stay, the towns were struck by <a href="/wiki/Cholera" title="Cholera">cholera</a> and the priest worked hard to administer the <a href="/wiki/Last_Rites" class="mw-redirect" title="Last Rites">Last Rites</a> to as many of the faithful as possible that of the 3,200 victims, only three died without receiving the Last Rites.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Election_as_superior_of_Monteagudo">Election as superior of Monteagudo</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Election as superior of Monteagudo">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His saintly life made him elected as Superior to the Seminary in Monteagudo, Navarra, Spain, in 1885. Through this post, Moreno imparted his missionary zeal to many missionaries to the Americas and the Philippines.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Colombian_missions">Colombian missions</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Colombian missions">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>It was in 1888 that he once more crossed the Atlantic Ocean and became the head of the Recollect mission in <a href="/wiki/Colombia" title="Colombia">Colombia</a>. He served as Vicar apostolic of <a href="/wiki/Casanare_Department" title="Casanare Department">Casanare</a> and was named bishop of Pinara, Colombia on October 23, 1893. He became Bishop of <a href="/wiki/Pasto,_Colombia" title="Pasto, Colombia">Pasto, Colombia</a> on December 2, 1893. He was noted for his generous charity to the faithful of his diocese and his ardent attacks against the liberal party from the pulpit.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Involvement_in_Colombian_politics">Involvement in Colombian politics</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ezequi%C3%A9l_Moreno_y_D%C3%ADaz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Involvement in Colombian politics">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

<p>As most of the Colombian Church hierarchy, Ezequiel Moreno aligned himself with the <a href="/wiki/Colombian_Conservative_Party" title="Colombian Conservative Party">Colombian Conservative Party</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/Thousand_Days_War" class="mw-redirect" title="Thousand Days War">Thousand Days War</a>, he used his writings and preaching sermons to attack the <a href="/wiki/Colombian_Liberal_Party" title="Colombian Liberal Party">Colombian Liberal Party</a> and to urge Catholics to fight the liberals and to "defend their religion with <a href="/wiki/Remington_Arms" title="Remington Arms">Remingtons</a> and <a href="/wiki/Machete" title="Machete">machetes</a>", promising automatic <a href="/wiki/Absolution" title="Absolution">absolution</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
