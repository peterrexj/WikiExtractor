					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Catholic saint and Archbishop of Lyon (d. 494)</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p><p><b>Saint Rusticus</b> (c. 455 – 25 April 501), the successor of Saint <a href="/wiki/Lupicinus_of_Lyon" title="Lupicinus of Lyon">Lupicinus of Lyon</a> (491-494), served as <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Lyon" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Lyon">Archbishop of Lyon</a> from 494 to April 501.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Later <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> and venerated in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>, his <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 25 April.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Rusticus_(archbishop_of_Lyon)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He and his brother <a href="/wiki/St._Viventiolus" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Viventiolus">St. Viventiolus</a> were the sons of Aquilinus (c. 430-c. 470), a nobleman at Lyon. Aquilinus was the son of Tullia (born 410), the daughter of <a href="/wiki/Eucherius_of_Lyon" title="Eucherius of Lyon">Saint Eucherius</a> and his wife Gallia. Tullia's husband, whose name is unknown, was the son of <a href="/wiki/Decimus_Rusticus" title="Decimus Rusticus">Decimus Rusticus</a> and his wife Artemia, and was a <i><a href="/wiki/Vicarius" title="Vicarius">vicarius</a></i> of a province in <a href="/wiki/Gaul" title="Gaul">Gaul</a> between 423 and 448 under Apollinaris, the father of Aquilinus' schoolfellow and friend, <a href="/wiki/Sidonius_Apollinaris" title="Sidonius Apollinaris">Sidonius Apollinaris</a> (c. 400).
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Bishop">Bishop</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Rusticus_(archbishop_of_Lyon)&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Bishop">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rusticus served for many years as a magistrate.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Around 494 he succeeded <a href="/wiki/Lupicinus_of_Lyon" title="Lupicinus of Lyon">Lupicinus of Lyon</a> as bishop. Shortly after his consecration, Rusticus sent some financial aid to <a href="/wiki/Pope_Gelasius_I" title="Pope Gelasius I">Pope Gelasius I</a>. Gelasius wrote back in February 494, recommending to the bishop's good offices <a href="/wiki/Epiphanius_of_Pavia" title="Epiphanius of Pavia">Epiphanius of Pavia</a>, who was on his way to Gaul to see to the ransom of certain captives held by the Burgundian king <a href="/wiki/Gundobad" title="Gundobad">Gundobad</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> According to <a href="/wiki/Magnus_Felix_Ennodius" title="Magnus Felix Ennodius">Ennodius</a>, among those freed were 400 from Lyon.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>

<p>Married before 480 to Hiberie de Limoges (born c. 460), daughter of <a href="/wiki/Ruricius" title="Ruricius">Ruricius</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Limoges" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Limoges">Bishop of Limoges</a> (then <a href="/wiki/Limoges" title="Limoges">Augustoritum</a>) and his wife Hiberia, daughter of an <a href="/wiki/Arverni" title="Arverni">Arvernian</a> senator Ommatius.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> Rusticus and his wife had three children:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Sacerdos_of_Lyon" title="Sacerdos of Lyon">St. Sacerdos</a>, <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Lyon" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Lyon">Archbishop of Lyon</a><sup id="cite_ref-Heinzelmann_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Heinzelmann-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Leontius,_Archbishop_of_Lyon" class="mw-redirect" title="Leontius, Archbishop of Lyon">Leontius, Archbishop of Lyon</a></li>
<li>Artemia,<sup id="cite_ref-Heinzelmann_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Heinzelmann-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> the wife of Florentinus, born in 485, a <a href="/wiki/Senator" class="mw-redirect" title="Senator">Senator</a>, who were the parents of:
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Gondulf_of_Provence" title="Gondulf of Provence">Gondulf of Provence</a>, <a href="/wiki/Duke" title="Duke">Duke</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Metz" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Metz">Bishop of Metz</a></li>
