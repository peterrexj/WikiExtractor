					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saints Justin, Florentius, Felix and Justa</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">Siponto?</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">~310 AD<br />Furci</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">July 25 (not mentioned in recent martyrologies; Saint Justa has her own feast day of August 1<sup id="cite_ref-santiebeati.it_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santiebeati.it-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>)</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Justin of Siponto</b>, as well as <b>Saints Florentius, Felix, and Justa</b>, are venerated as <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christian</a> <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyrs</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>. Information about them is fragmentary but their names were inserted into various <a href="/wiki/Martyrologies" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrologies">martyrologies</a>. Tradition states that Justin was a <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a>, that Florentius and Felix were his siblings, and that Justa was his niece. The earliest document about them dates from the 15th century; this is their legendary <i>passio</i>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Justin_of_Siponto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>They lived in <a href="/wiki/Siponto" title="Siponto">Siponto</a> at the beginning of the fourth century.  Justin as the most erudite and eloquent of the three brothers.  He was appointed <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Siponto" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Siponto">bishop of Siponto</a>.  Florentius, meanwhile, married and had a daughter named Justa, named after Justin, who had <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">baptized</a> her.  After a decade or so, the four left Siponto and went to <a href="/wiki/Chieti" title="Chieti">Chieti</a>, where they preached and performed miracles.  <a href="/wiki/Pagan" class="mw-redirect" title="Pagan">Pagan</a> priests, alarmed by this, contacted <a href="/wiki/Maximian" title="Maximian">Maximian</a>; the authorities ordered the four to sacrifice to <a href="/wiki/Jove" class="mw-redirect" title="Jove">Jove</a>.  Justin escaped to a mountain named <i>Tubernium</i> while Florentius, Felix, and Justa were arrested and taken to Forconium (<a href="/wiki/Furci" title="Furci">Furci</a>).  Florentius and Felix were <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">decapitated</a> immediately, on July 25, 310.  Justa was thrown into a burning furnace, but after emerging unharmed, was shot with <a href="/wiki/Arrow" title="Arrow">arrows</a> on August 1 of that year.  She was buried in a place two miles from Furci where a <a href="/wiki/Basilica" title="Basilica">basilica</a> would later be built.
</p><p>Justin, meanwhile, buried the bodies of his brothers beside that of his niece and died of natural causes at the age of eighty-four.  He was buried near <a href="/wiki/Offida" title="Offida">Offida</a> (province of <a href="/wiki/Ascoli_Piceno" title="Ascoli Piceno">Ascoli Piceno</a>), where a basilica was also built.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Justin_of_Siponto&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The varying dates of celebration and places with which they were associated has led scholars to believe that they are purely legendary.<sup id="cite_ref-santiebeati.it_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santiebeati.it-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Justin was confused with the cult of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Justin_of_Chieti" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Justin of Chieti">Saint Justin of Chieti</a>, whose feast day falls on January 1, and May 11 locally.  Florentius and Felix were sometimes identified as <a href="/wiki/Military_saint" title="Military saint">soldier saints</a>, probably through confusion with <a href="/wiki/Felix_of_Nola" title="Felix of Nola">Felix of Nola</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-santiebeati.it_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santiebeati.it-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>   Of Justa it is known that there was a <a href="/wiki/Crypt" title="Crypt">crypt</a> dedicated to her at <a href="/wiki/Bazzano,_Valsamoggia" title="Bazzano, Valsamoggia">Bazzano</a>, where her body was conserved.  The church there was expanded in the 13th century.  Her cult, with a feast day of August 1, was centered in the <a href="/wiki/Abruzzo" title="Abruzzo">Abruzzo</a> and <a href="/wiki/Campania" title="Campania">Campania</a> regions.  There were three churches dedicated to her in <a href="/wiki/Penne,_Abruzzo" title="Penne, Abruzzo">Penne</a>, <a href="/wiki/Sulmona" title="Sulmona">Sulmona</a>, and Chieti.  The four saints all had their own feast day of July 25 in previous Roman Martyrologies; currently they are not mentioned in this martyrology.<sup id="cite_ref-santiebeati.it_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-santiebeati.it-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
