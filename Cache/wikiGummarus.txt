<p><b>Saint Gummarus of Lier</b> (also known as Gommaire, Gommer or Gummery) is a Belgian saint. He was the son of the Lord of Emblem (near <a href="/wiki/Lier,_Belgium" title="Lier, Belgium">Lier, Belgium</a>). An official in the court of his relative <a href="/wiki/Pepin_the_Younger" class="mw-redirect" title="Pepin the Younger">Pepin the Younger</a> or <a href="/wiki/Pepin_of_Herstal" title="Pepin of Herstal">Pepin of Herstal</a> according to some other sources,<sup id="cite_ref-visitlier_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-visitlier-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> after a number of years in military service he retired to live the life of a hermit.<sup id="cite_ref-Jones_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Jones-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> The town of Lier grew up around his hermitage.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gummarus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Gummarus was a native of Emblehem, referring to an area including Lier and not just the town of Emblem,<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>  in Brabant, and a relative of <a href="/wiki/Pippin_the_Younger" class="mw-redirect" title="Pippin the Younger">Pippin the Younger</a>, who called him to his court and entrusted him with important offices. The king arranged a marriage between Gummarus and a wealthy noblewoman named Guinmarie, extravagant and haughty.<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>  His wife appears to have been shrewish as well as abusive to their household servants in his absence. They had no children.
</p><p>Gummarus accompanied Pepin on a number of military campaigns,<sup id="cite_ref-monks_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-monks-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> and spent eight years in the field, in Cardekho, <a href="/wiki/Saxony" title="Saxony">Saxony</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Aquitaine" title="Aquitaine">Aquitaine</a>. Upon his return from the military, Gummarus tried to reconcile with his wife and remedy the injustices she had laid upon the people in their service. That he might have a place of quiet and retirement, and in order to attend his private devotions, he built a chapel called Nivesdunc.
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ca/Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg/220px-Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="267" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ca/Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg/330px-Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/ca/Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg/440px-Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3776" data-file-height="4575" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Beeld_van_Sint-Gummarus.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Statue of Sint-Gummarus, inside the Sint-Gummaruschurch in Lier</div></div></div>
<p>Gummarus and his wife eventually separated. He became a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> at Nivesdunc and the town of <a href="/wiki/Lier,_Belgium" title="Lier, Belgium">Lier, Belgium</a> grew up around the site of the hermitage where he died in 774.<sup id="cite_ref-monks_6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-monks-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
Other sources place his time of death around 714.<sup id="cite_ref-visitlier_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-visitlier-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The latter is more likely the true time of death as Gummarus was said to have met saint <a href="/wiki/Rumbold_of_Mechelen" class="mw-redirect" title="Rumbold of Mechelen">Rumbold of Mechelen</a><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-tradition_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-tradition-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CathOL_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CathOL-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> who has been determined to have died between 580 and 655.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2004date1_11-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2004date1-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2004date2_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2004date2-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/754" title="754">754</a><sup id="cite_ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13">&#91;13&#93;</a></sup> or <a href="/wiki/815" title="815">815</a>  he was recognized as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a><sup id="cite_ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14">&#91;14&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gummarus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG/220px-Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG" decoding="async" width="220" height="253" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG/330px-Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG/440px-Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG 2x" data-file-width="2592" data-file-height="2984" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Mechelen_St_Rombouts_stained_glass_windows_01.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Saint Rumbold meets Saint Gummarus, Cathedral of Mechelen</div></div></div>
<p>St. Gummarus is the patron saint of Lier. A number of miracles were attributed to his intercession. He is commemorated by the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Butler_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Butler-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> and the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a>, including <a href="/wiki/Western_Rite_Orthodoxy" title="Western Rite Orthodoxy">Western Rite Orthodox</a> on 11 October.<sup id="cite_ref-Jones_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Jones-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The site of his hermitage is now St. Peter's chapel. The collegiate Church of St. Gummarus was built in <a href="/wiki/Brabantine_Gothic" title="Brabantine Gothic">Brabantine Gothic</a> in 1378. Every year on the first Sunday after October 10, the city holds the Sint-Gummarus Fair, which includes a procession in which the saint's relics are carried through the streets of Lier.<sup id="cite_ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15">&#91;15&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Brouwerij Cornelissen of Limburg, Belgium brews an award-winning Sint Gummarus Tripel.<sup id="cite_ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16">&#91;16&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Iconography">Iconography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Gummarus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Iconography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Gummarus was assumed to have been a close companion of the Christian missionary <a href="/wiki/Rumbold_of_Mechelen" class="mw-redirect" title="Rumbold of Mechelen">Rumbold of Mechelen</a>, who worked in Brabant, and is sometimes credited with helping Rumbold found an abbey at Lier. For this reason they are often depicted together in paintings and stained glass windows. However a 2004 examination of relics believed to be those of Rumbold suggests that he likely died over fifty years before Gummarus was born, so they would not have been contemporaries.<sup id="cite_ref-2004date1_11-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2004date1-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
