<p><b>Roman Sitko</b> (30 March 1880 – 12 October 1942) was a <a href="/wiki/Polish_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Polish people">Polish</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Priesthood_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood (Catholic Church)">priest</a>. In 1941 he was arrested by the <a href="/wiki/Nazi" class="mw-redirect" title="Nazi">Nazis</a> and murdered at <a href="/wiki/Auschwitz-Birkenau" class="mw-redirect" title="Auschwitz-Birkenau">Auschwitz-Birkenau</a> concentration camp. He is considered a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> and was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> on 13 June 1999.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Roman_Sitko&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Roman Sitko was born on 30 March 1880 in Czarna Sędziszowska, <a href="/wiki/Poland" title="Poland">Poland</a>. In 1900 he started his studies for the priesthood and was ordained in <a href="/wiki/Tarn%C3%B3w" title="Tarnów">Tarnów</a> in 1904. Between 1907 and 1921 he worked as a priest in <a href="/wiki/Mielec" title="Mielec">Mielec</a>.
</p><p>Sitko was arrested on 22 May 1941 by the Nazis. He was imprisoned for months before being sent to Auschwitz-Birkenau in August 1942.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Roman_Sitko&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After his murder Sitko was recognized as one of the <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Martyrs of World War II</a>. He was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by Pope John Paul II on 13 June 1999. His feast is celebrated on 12 October.
