<p><b>Pope Donus</b> (died on 11 April 678) was the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Rome" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Rome">bishop of Rome</a> from 676 to his death. Few details survive about him or his achievements beyond what is recorded in the <i><a href="/wiki/Liber_Pontificalis" title="Liber Pontificalis">Liber Pontificalis</a></i>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Election">Election</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pope_Donus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Election">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Donus was the son of a Roman named Maurice.<sup id="cite_ref-Attwater_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Attwater-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He became <a href="/wiki/Pope" title="Pope">pope</a> on 2 November 676, having been <a href="/wiki/Papal_selection_before_1059" title="Papal selection before 1059">selected</a> to succeed <a href="/wiki/Adeodatus_II" class="mw-redirect" title="Adeodatus II">Adeodatus II</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> By that time, Donus was already elderly.<sup id="cite_ref-Attwater_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Attwater-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Pontificate">Pontificate</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Pope_Donus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Pontificate">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Donus expanded the clergy of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> with twelve new priests and five deacons. He also consecrated six bishops for various <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_see" title="Episcopal see">sees</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  One of these may have been Vitalianus of Arezzo.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He had the <a href="/wiki/Atrium_(architecture)" title="Atrium (architecture)">atrium</a> of <a href="/wiki/Old_St._Peter%27s_Basilica" title="Old St. Peter&#39;s Basilica">Old St. Peter's Basilica</a> paved with large blocks of white marble, and restored other churches of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, notably the church of St. Euphemia on the Appian Way and the <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_St._Paul_Outside_the_Walls" class="mw-redirect" title="Basilica of St. Paul Outside the Walls">Basilica of St. Paul Outside the Walls</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Donus was shocked to discover a colony of <a href="/wiki/Nestorian_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Nestorian Church">Nestorian</a> monks in Boetianum, a Syrian monastery in Rome. He gave their monastery to Roman monks and dispersed them through the various religious houses of the city in the hope that they would accept <a href="/wiki/Chalcedonian_Christianity" title="Chalcedonian Christianity">Chalcedonian Christianity</a>. The Nestorians were possibly refugees fleeing the <a href="/wiki/Muslim_conquest_of_the_Levant" title="Muslim conquest of the Levant">Muslim conquest of the Levant</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>During the pontificate of Donus, Archbishop Reparatus of Ravenna returned to the obedience of the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Holy See</a>, thus ending the schism created by Archbishop Maurus, who had aimed at making Ravenna <a href="/wiki/Autocephalous" class="mw-redirect" title="Autocephalous">autocephalous</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> Donus' relations with Constantinople tended towards the conciliatory.  On 10 August 678, Emperor <a href="/wiki/Constantine_IV" title="Constantine IV">Constantine IV</a> addressed him as "the most holy and blessed archbishop of our ancient Rome and the universal pope," hoping to attract him to engage in negotiations with the <a href="/wiki/Patriarch_of_Constantinople" class="mw-redirect" title="Patriarch of Constantinople">patriarch of Constantinople</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Monothelites" class="mw-redirect" title="Monothelites">Monothelites</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>  He ordered that <a href="/wiki/Pope_Vitalianus" class="mw-redirect" title="Pope Vitalianus">Pope Vitalianus</a>' name be put back in the <a href="/wiki/Diptych" title="Diptych">diptychs</a> of those bishops in communion with Constantinople, an act which caused him a great deal of trouble from the Monothelites and Patriarch <a href="/wiki/Theodore_I_of_Constantinople" title="Theodore I of Constantinople">Theodore I of Constantinople</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Donus died on 11 April 678  and was buried the same day in Old St. Peter's Basilica. He was succeeded by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Agatho" title="Pope Agatho">Agatho</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
