<p><b>Joséphine Leroux</b> (23 January 1747 – 23 October 1794), born <b>Anne-Josepha Leroux</b>, was a French <a href="/wiki/Poor_Clares" title="Poor Clares">Poor Clare</a> <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a>, executed during the French Revolution.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Jos%C3%A9phine_Leroux&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>She was born in <a href="/wiki/Cambrai" title="Cambrai">Cambrai</a>, <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>. At the age of twenty-two, she entered the Poor Clare monastery in <a href="/wiki/Valenciennes" title="Valenciennes">Valenciennes</a>, taking the name Joséphine. Her sister Marie was an Ursuline, also in Valenciennes.<sup id="cite_ref-Belleville_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Belleville-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When the <a href="/wiki/Monasteries" class="mw-redirect" title="Monasteries">monasteries</a> and <a href="/wiki/Convents" class="mw-redirect" title="Convents">convents</a> were suppressed during the <a href="/wiki/French_Revolution" title="French Revolution">French Revolution</a>, she fled to her family in <a href="/wiki/Mons" title="Mons">Mons</a>, <a href="/wiki/County_of_Hainaut" title="County of Hainaut">Hainaut</a>, as did her sister. When Austrian forces took the city, there was a brief period of peace, and Joséphine returned to Valenciennes in 1793. Since her own Poor Clare monastery had been destroyed, she and her sister resumed religious life at the <a href="/wiki/Ursulines" title="Ursulines">Ursuline</a> Convent.<sup id="cite_ref-Belleville_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Belleville-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The revolutionary army retook the city and in 1794, she and several other nuns were arrested on the grounds that they were emigres who had returned without permission and were running a religious school. They were condemned for high treason. On October 23, 1794 she and her sister, two other Ursulines, and two Bridgettine nuns were <a href="/wiki/Guillotined" class="mw-redirect" title="Guillotined">guillotined</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Belleville_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Belleville-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> The nuns went to their deaths singing the <a href="/wiki/Litany_of_Loretto" class="mw-redirect" title="Litany of Loretto">Litany of Loretto</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Joséphine Leroux was <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by Pope Benedict XV in 1920.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
