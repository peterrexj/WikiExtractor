<p><b>Saint Domninus of Fidenza</b> (<a href="/wiki/Italian_language" title="Italian language">Italian</a>: <i lang="it">San Donnino di Fidenza</i>) is an <a href="/wiki/Italians" title="Italians">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> saint.  According to tradition, he died in 304 AD and was a native of <a href="/wiki/Parma" title="Parma">Parma</a>.  The cathedral at <a href="/wiki/Fidenza" title="Fidenza">Fidenza</a> (a town once called <i>Borgo San Donnino</i>) is dedicated to him.  The <i><a href="/wiki/Hieronymian_Martyrology" class="mw-redirect" title="Hieronymian Martyrology">Hieronymian Martyrology</a></i> commemorates Domninus, but does not include any further information about him, and his feast day is cited as occurring on October 9.  He is not commemorated in the <a href="/wiki/Martyrologies" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyrologies">martyrologies</a> of <a href="/wiki/Bede" title="Bede">Bede</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ado_(archbishop)" class="mw-redirect" title="Ado (archbishop)">Ado</a>, <a href="/wiki/Notker_of_St_Gall" class="mw-redirect" title="Notker of St Gall">Notker</a>, or the <i><a href="/w/index.php?title=Parvum_Romanum&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Parvum Romanum (page does not exist)">Parvum Romanum</a></i>.
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/42/Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg/220px-Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="103" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/42/Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg/330px-Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/42/Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg/440px-Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg 2x" data-file-width="1848" data-file-height="867" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Carpineti_san_donnino_inverno.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Church of San Donnino in <a href="/wiki/Carpineti" title="Carpineti">Carpineti</a></div></div></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:123px;"><a href="/wiki/File:SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ad/SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg/121px-SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg" decoding="async" width="121" height="168" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ad/SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg/181px-SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/ad/SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg/242px-SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg 2x" data-file-width="706" data-file-height="980" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:SanDonnino_fidenza.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Domninus of Fidenza.</div></div></div>
<p>His legend states that Domninus was <a href="/wiki/Chamberlain_(office)" title="Chamberlain (office)">Chamberlain</a> to <a href="/wiki/Maximian" title="Maximian">Emperor Maximian</a> and keeper of the royal crown, and converted to <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a>, thereby incurring the emperor's wrath.  Pursued by imperial forces, he rode through <a href="/wiki/Piacenza" title="Piacenza">Piacenza</a> holding a cross.  He was caught and <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">beheaded</a> on the banks of the <a href="/wiki/Stirone" title="Stirone">Stirone</a>, outside of Fidenza, or the <a href="/wiki/Via_Aemilia" title="Via Aemilia">Via Aemilia</a>.  It is recounted that Domninus <a href="/wiki/Cephalophore" title="Cephalophore">picked up his severed head</a> and placed it on the future site of the cathedral of San Donnino.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Domninus_of_Fidenza&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> are enshrined in <a href="/wiki/Fidenza_Cathedral" title="Fidenza Cathedral">Fidenza Cathedral</a>, adding some plausibility to the tradition that he suffered martyrdom in this region.  The ancient <a href="/wiki/Basilica" title="Basilica">basilica</a> at Fidenza, rebuilt in the 12th century, includes a sculpted frieze sub-divided into five scenes representing the life of the saint.  The sculptures are attributed to the school of <a href="/wiki/Benedetto_Antelami" title="Benedetto Antelami">Benedetto Antelami</a>.
</p><p>In art, Domninus is depicted in <a href="/wiki/Military" title="Military">military</a> attire, and holds the palm of martyrdom.  Domninus' cult was popular in <a href="/wiki/Northern_Italy" title="Northern Italy">Northern Italy</a>. He has been from earliest times invoked against <a href="/wiki/Rabies" title="Rabies">rabies</a>; his <i>Passio</i> records that after water and wine was blessed and the saint invoked, anyone who drank this would be cured from rabies.
