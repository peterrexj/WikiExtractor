<p><b>Hilary Paweł Januszewski, O.Carm</b> (June 11, 1907, in <a href="/wiki/Krajenki" title="Krajenki">Krajenki</a> – March 25, 1945, in <a href="/wiki/Dachau_concentration_camp" title="Dachau concentration camp">Dachau concentration camp</a>), was a Polish priest, <a href="/wiki/Carmelite" class="mw-redirect" title="Carmelite">Carmelite</a> <a href="/wiki/Friar" title="Friar">friar</a> of the Ancient Observance and <a href="/wiki/Catholic_priest" class="mw-redirect" title="Catholic priest">Catholic priest</a>, who was sent by the Nazi authorities to occupied Poland to the <a href="/wiki/Dachau_concentration_camp" title="Dachau concentration camp">Dachau concentration camp</a>, where he managed to survive until 1945.
</p><p>Januszewski studied in Rome receiving a <a href="/wiki/Licentiate_in_Theology" class="mw-redirect" title="Licentiate in Theology">Licentiate in Theology</a> in 1935 from the <a href="/wiki/Pontifical_University_of_Saint_Thomas_Aquinas" title="Pontifical University of Saint Thomas Aquinas">Pontifical University of St. Thomas Aquinas, <i>Angelicum</i></a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In February 1945, in the course of a large outbreak of <a href="/wiki/Typhoid" class="mw-redirect" title="Typhoid">typhoid</a>, Januszewski volunteered to serve those who were dying in an isolated makeshift building because, as he said, he was more needed there. He contracted the disease himself and died there.
</p><p>Januszewski is one of <a href="/wiki/108_Martyrs_of_World_War_II" title="108 Martyrs of World War II">108 Martyrs of World War II</a> who were <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> as a group by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> in 1999.
</p>
