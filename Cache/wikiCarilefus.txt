<p><b>Carilef</b> (French <i>Calais</i>, Latin <i>Calevisus</i>; died 541) was a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> who founded the monastery of <a href="/w/index.php?title=Aniole&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Aniole (page does not exist)">Aniole</a>.  The town of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Calais" title="Saint-Calais">Saint-Calais</a> takes its name from him. According to his ancient biography, the <i>Vita Carileffi</i>, King <a href="/wiki/Childebert_I" title="Childebert I">Childebert I</a> granted him lands after encountering the hermit in a forest where the king was hunting.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Lomer_(saint)" title="Lomer (saint)">Laumer</a> was his successor and <a href="/w/index.php?title=Siviard&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Siviard (page does not exist)">Siviard</a>, another successor, wrote the <i>Vita</i>.
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a>, his feast is celebrated on 1 July.
</p>
