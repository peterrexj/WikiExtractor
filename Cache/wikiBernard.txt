<p><b>Bernard</b> (<i><a href="/wiki/Bernhard" title="Bernhard">Bernhard</a></i>) is a <a href="/wiki/French_language" title="French language">French</a> and <a href="/wiki/West_Germanic" class="mw-redirect" title="West Germanic">West Germanic</a> masculine given name.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> It is also a surname.
</p><p>The name is attested from at least the 9th century. West Germanic <i>Bernhard</i> is composed from the two elements <i>bern</i> "bear" and <i>hard</i> "brave, hardy".<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Its native Old English reflex was <i>Beornheard</i>, which was replaced by the French form <i>Bernard</i> that was brought to England after the <a href="/wiki/Norman_Conquest" title="Norman Conquest">Norman Conquest</a>. The name <i>Bernhard</i> was notably popular among <a href="/wiki/Old_Frisian" title="Old Frisian">Old Frisian</a> speakers.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> Its wider use was popularized due to Saint <a href="/wiki/Bernhard_of_Clairvaux" class="mw-redirect" title="Bernhard of Clairvaux">Bernhard of Clairvaux</a> (canonized in 1174). Bernard is the second most common surname in <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geographical_distribution">Geographical distribution</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bernard&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Geographical distribution">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>As of 2014, 42.2% of all known bearers of the surname <i>Bernard</i> were residents of <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a> (frequency 1:392), 12.5% of the <a href="/wiki/United_States" title="United States">United States</a> (1:7,203), 7.0% of <a href="/wiki/Haiti" title="Haiti">Haiti</a> (1:382), 6.6% of <a href="/wiki/Tanzania" title="Tanzania">Tanzania</a> (1:1,961), 4.8% of <a href="/wiki/Canada" title="Canada">Canada</a> (1:1,896), 3.6% of <a href="/wiki/Nigeria" title="Nigeria">Nigeria</a> (1:12,221), 2.7% of <a href="/wiki/Burundi" title="Burundi">Burundi</a> (1:894), 1.9% of <a href="/wiki/Belgium" title="Belgium">Belgium</a> (1:1,500), 1.6% of <a href="/wiki/Rwanda" title="Rwanda">Rwanda</a> (1:1,745), 1.2% of <a href="/wiki/Germany" title="Germany">Germany</a> (1:16,397), 1.2% of <a href="/wiki/Jamaica" title="Jamaica">Jamaica</a> (1:595), 1.1% of <a href="/wiki/Ghana" title="Ghana">Ghana</a> (1:6,179), 1.0% of <a href="/wiki/England" title="England">England</a> (1:13,691) and 1.0% of <a href="/wiki/Madagascar" title="Madagascar">Madagascar</a> (1:6,098).
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>, the frequency of the surname was higher than national average (1:392) in the following regions:<sup id="cite_ref-bernard2014_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-bernard2014-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<ul><li>1. <a href="/wiki/Collectivity_of_Saint_Martin" title="Collectivity of Saint Martin">Collectivity of Saint Martin</a> (1:60)</li>
<li>2. <a href="/wiki/Saint_Barth%C3%A9lemy" title="Saint Barthélemy">Saint Barthélemy</a> (1:189)</li>
<li>3. <a href="/wiki/Bourgogne-Franche-Comt%C3%A9" title="Bourgogne-Franche-Comté">Bourgogne-Franche-Comté</a> (1:260)</li>
<li>4. <a href="/wiki/Nouvelle-Aquitaine" title="Nouvelle-Aquitaine">Nouvelle-Aquitaine</a> (1:283)</li>
<li>5. <a href="/wiki/Auvergne-Rh%C3%B4ne-Alpes" title="Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes">Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes</a> (1:296)</li>
<li>6. <a href="/wiki/Brittany_(administrative_region)" title="Brittany (administrative region)">Brittany</a> (1:333)</li>
<li>7. <a href="/wiki/Pays_de_la_Loire" title="Pays de la Loire">Pays de la Loire</a> (1:336)</li>
