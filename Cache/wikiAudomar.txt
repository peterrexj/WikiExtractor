					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Bishop of Thérouanne</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">Not to be confused with <a href="/wiki/Saint_Othmar" title="Saint Othmar">Saint Othmar</a>.</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Saint Audomar</div></th></tr><tr><td colspan="2" class="infobox-image"><a href="/wiki/File:Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_(cropped).jpg" class="image"><img alt="Estrée - Statue-1 (cropped).jpg" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_%28cropped%29.jpg/150px-Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_%28cropped%29.jpg" decoding="async" width="150" height="234" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_%28cropped%29.jpg/225px-Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_%28cropped%29.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/59/Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_%28cropped%29.jpg/300px-Estr%C3%A9e_-_Statue-1_%28cropped%29.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3385" data-file-height="5285" /></a><div class="infobox-caption">Statue de Saint Audomar (Omer).</div></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Neustria" title="Neustria">Neustria</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">~670 AD</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a><br /><a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox_Church" title="Eastern Orthodox Church">Eastern Orthodox Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">9 September</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Saint Audomar</b> (died <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 670), better known as <b>Saint Omer</b>, was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Th%C3%A9rouanne" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Thérouanne">bishop of Thérouanne</a>, after whom nearby <a href="/wiki/Saint-Omer" title="Saint-Omer">Saint-Omer</a> in northern <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a> was named.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Audomar&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was born of a distinguished family of <a href="/wiki/Coutances" title="Coutances">Coutances</a>, then under the <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> realm of <a href="/wiki/Neustria" title="Neustria">Neustria</a>, towards the close of the 6th or the beginning of the 7th century.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>After the death of his mother, he entered with his father the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Luxeuil" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbey of Luxeuil">abbey of Luxeuil</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Diocese_of_Besan%C3%A7on" class="mw-redirect" title="Diocese of Besançon">Diocese of Besançon</a>, probably about 615. Under the direction of Eustachius, Omer studied the <a href="/wiki/Scriptures" class="mw-redirect" title="Scriptures">Scriptures</a>, in which he acquired remarkable proficiency.<sup id="cite_ref-oboyle_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oboyle-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Dagobert_I" title="Dagobert I">King Dagobert</a> requested the appointment of a bishop for the important city of <a href="/wiki/Th%C3%A9rouanne" title="Thérouanne">Thérouanne</a>, the capital of the ancient territory of the <a href="/wiki/Morini" title="Morini">Morini</a> in <a href="/wiki/Neustria" title="Neustria">Neustria</a>, he was appointed and consecrated in 637.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Though the Morini had received <a href="/wiki/Christianity" title="Christianity">Christianity</a> from <a href="/wiki/Victoricus,_Fuscian,_and_Gentian" title="Victoricus, Fuscian, and Gentian">Saint Fuscian and Saint Victoricus</a>, and later Antmund and <a href="/wiki/Adalbert_(mystic)" class="mw-redirect" title="Adalbert (mystic)">Adelbert</a>, nearly every vestige of Christianity had disappeared. In 649 or 651, Audomar received twelve estates, including <a href="/wiki/Sithiu" class="mw-redirect" title="Sithiu">Sithiu</a> where he established three missionaries who came from Austrasia and spoke the local language.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> The Abbot of Luxeuil sent to his assistance several monks, among whom are mentioned <a href="/wiki/Saint_Bertin" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Bertin">Saint Bertin</a>, Saint <a href="/wiki/Mommolin_of_Noyon" title="Mommolin of Noyon">Mommolin</a> and Saint Ebertran.<sup id="cite_ref-oboyle_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-oboyle-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He founded the Abbey of Saint Peter (now the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint_Bertin" title="Abbey of Saint Bertin">Abbey of Saint Bertin</a>) in Sithiu, soon to rival the old monastery of Luxeuil for the number of learned and zealous men educated there. In 649 he erected the Church of Our Lady of Sithiu, with a small monastery adjoining, which he turned over to the monks of Saint Bertin, on condition that he would be buried at the site and that the church would serve as a burial place for the monks.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The exact date of his death is unknown, but he is believed to have died about the year 670.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Audomar&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The place of his burial is uncertain; most probably he was laid to rest in the church of Our Lady, later <a href="/wiki/Saint-Omer_Cathedral" title="Saint-Omer Cathedral">Saint-Omer Cathedral</a>, where there is a 13th-century <a href="/wiki/Cenotaph" title="Cenotaph">cenotaph</a> dedicated to him.
</p><p>His feast is celebrated on 9 September.
</p>
