<p><b>Candidus</b> (d. 287 AD) was a commander of the <a href="/wiki/Theban_Legion" title="Theban Legion">Theban Legion</a>. The Theban Legion was composed of Christians from <a href="/wiki/Upper_Egypt" title="Upper Egypt">Upper Egypt</a>. He is venerated as a <a href="/wiki/Christian_saint" class="mw-redirect" title="Christian saint">Christian saint</a> and <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend">Legend</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Candidus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Legend">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Candidus was first mentioned as a member of the Theban Legion by <a href="/wiki/Eucherius_of_Lyon" title="Eucherius of Lyon">Eucherius</a>, Bishop of Lyons (434 AD). The 13th-century  <i><a href="/wiki/Golden_Legend" title="Golden Legend">Golden Legend</a></i> states: "And the noble man, <a href="/wiki/Saint_Maurice" title="Saint Maurice">Maurice</a>, was duke of this holy legion; and they that governed under him, which bare the banners, were named Saint Candidus, Saint Innocent, Saint Exsuperius, Saint Victor, and Saint Constantine, all these were captains."<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Candidus is called Maurice's <i>senator militum</i> or <a href="/wiki/Staff_officer" class="mw-redirect" title="Staff officer">staff officer</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  He opposed <a href="/wiki/Maximian" title="Maximian">Maximian</a>, who had ordered them to harass the local Christians in his name, stating that "we are your soldiers, but we are also servants of the true God. We cannot renounce Him who is our Creator and Master, and also yours even though you reject Him."<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Candidus, along with Maurice, the other staff officers and 6,600 soldiers, is said to have been <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyred</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Switzerland" title="Switzerland">Swiss</a> town of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Maurice-en-Valais" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Maurice-en-Valais">Saint Maurice-en-Valais</a>, then called <i><a href="/wiki/Agaunum" title="Agaunum">Agaunum</a></i>.  His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is September 22.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Remains">Remains</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Candidus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Remains">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Candidus' <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> were stored in a 6th-century reliquary at the <a href="/wiki/Abbaye_de_Saint-Maurice_d%27Agaune" class="mw-redirect" title="Abbaye de Saint-Maurice d&#39;Agaune">Abbaye de Saint-Maurice d'Agaune</a>. His skull is kept in an embossed silver <a href="/wiki/Bust_(sculpture)" title="Bust (sculpture)">bust</a> in the Abbey's Treasury.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Some of Candidus' <a href="/wiki/Relics" class="mw-redirect" title="Relics">relics</a> are stored at Our Lady of Malibu Catholic Church, given by the Archdiocese of Los Angeles to the newly formed parish in 1960 under founding rector, Joseph Burbage. The relics were rededicated by Archbishop Jose Gomez in November, 2014. It has been confirmed that his relics can also be found at St. Bonaventure Catholic Church in Huntington Beach, CA.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Other_saints_with_this_name">Other saints with this name</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Saint_Candidus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Other saints with this name">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1132942124">.mw-parser-output .portalbox{padding:0;margin:0.5em 0;display:table;box-sizing:border-box;max-width:175px;list-style:none}.mw-parser-output .portalborder{border:solid #aaa 1px;padding:0.1em;background:#f9f9f9}.mw-parser-output .portalbox-entry{display:table-row;font-size:85%;line-height:110%;height:1.9em;font-style:italic;font-weight:bold}.mw-parser-output .portalbox-image{display:table-cell;padding:0.2em;vertical-align:middle;text-align:center}.mw-parser-output .portalbox-link{display:table-cell;padding:0.2em 0.2em 0.2em 0.3em;vertical-align:middle}@media(min-width:720px){.mw-parser-output .portalleft{clear:left;float:left;margin:0.5em 1em 0.5em 0}.mw-parser-output .portalright{clear:right;float:right;margin:0.5em 0 0.5em 1em}}</style><ul role="navigation" aria-label="Portals" class="noprint portalbox portalborder portalright">
<li class="portalbox-entry"><span class="portalbox-image"><span class="noviewer" typeof="mw:File"><span><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/48/Gloriole_blur.svg/32px-Gloriole_blur.svg.png" decoding="async" width="32" height="28" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/48/Gloriole_blur.svg/48px-Gloriole_blur.svg.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/48/Gloriole_blur.svg/64px-Gloriole_blur.svg.png 2x" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="28" /></span></span></span><span class="portalbox-link"><a href="/wiki/Portal:Saints" title="Portal:Saints">Saints portal</a></span></li></ul>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Cyrion_and_Candidus" title="Cyrion and Candidus">Candidus of Armenia</a>, Armenian martyr venerated on 10 March.</li>
<li><a href="/w/index.php?title=Candidus_of_Rome&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Candidus of Rome (page does not exist)">Candidus of Rome</a>, a Roman martyr buried on the <a href="/wiki/Esquiline_Hill" title="Esquiline Hill">Esquiline Hill</a>, whose feast day is October 3.<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/w/index.php?title=Candidus_the_Martyr&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Candidus the Martyr (page does not exist)">Candidus the Martyr</a>, Martyr whose feast day is February 2.<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/w/index.php?title=Candidus_of_Maastricht&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Candidus of Maastricht (page does not exist)">Candidus of Maastricht</a>, 4th or 5th-century <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Maastricht" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Maastricht">Bishop of Maastricht</a>, successor of Saint Servatius, although he may not have been officially bishop. His relics were translated in 1039. His feast day is <a href="/wiki/7_June" class="mw-redirect" title="7 June">7 June</a>, the same as Saint Valentinus of Tongeren. In the <a href="/wiki/Art_%26_History_Museum" title="Art &amp; History Museum">Art &amp; History Museum</a> in <a href="/wiki/Brussels" title="Brussels">Brussels</a> is a 12th-century reliquary gable, which was originally in <a href="/wiki/Basilica_of_Saint_Servatius" title="Basilica of Saint Servatius">St Servatius'</a> in <a href="/wiki/Maastricht" title="Maastricht">Maastricht</a>. This church still owns a stone <a href="/wiki/Sarcophagus" title="Sarcophagus">sarcophagus</a> that once contained his relics (amongst others), and two 19th-century copies of the Brussels gable, one of which is now part of reliquary shrine.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup></li></ul>
