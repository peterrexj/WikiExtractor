<p><b>Peter Sanz</b> (22 September 1680 &#8211; 26 May 1747) (<a href="/wiki/Catalan_language" title="Catalan language">Catalan</a>: <i lang="ca">Pere Sans i Jordá</i>, <a href="/wiki/Spanish_language" title="Spanish language">Spanish</a>: <i lang="es">Pedro Sans i Jordá</i>) was a <a href="/wiki/Catalan_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Catalan people">Catalan</a> <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Order" title="Dominican Order">Dominican</a> <a href="/wiki/Friar" title="Friar">friar</a> who was sent as a missionary <a href="/wiki/Bishop_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop (Catholic Church)">bishop</a> to China. He was declared a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> and <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_life">Early life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Sanz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Early life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sanz was born 22 September 1680 in <a href="/wiki/Asc%C3%B3" title="Ascó">Ascó</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ribera_d%27Ebre" title="Ribera d&#39;Ebre">Ribera d'Ebre</a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Catalonia" title="Catalonia">Catalan</a> region of Spain. In 1697 he <a href="/wiki/Profession_(religious)" class="mw-redirect" title="Profession (religious)">professed</a> <a href="/wiki/Religious_vows" title="Religious vows">religious vows</a> as a member of the Dominican Order in <a href="/wiki/Lleida" title="Lleida">Lerida</a>. After completing his theological studies, he was <a href="/wiki/Holy_Orders" class="mw-redirect" title="Holy Orders">ordained</a> a <a href="/wiki/Priesthood_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood (Catholic Church)">priest</a> on 22 September 1704.<sup id="cite_ref-PeterSanz_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PeterSanz-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Mission_in_China">Mission in China</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Sanz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Mission in China">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sanz later volunteered and was accepted to serve in China. He was sent to the Philippines in 1713 to prepare for this mission, where he studied the <a href="/wiki/Chinese_language" title="Chinese language">Chinese language</a> for two years. He then entered China with a small band of fellow friars, where he began a ministry which lasted over 30 years.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In January 1728, the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Evangelization_of_Peoples" title="Congregation for the Evangelization of Peoples">Sacred Congregation for the Propagation of the Faith</a> named him as <a href="/wiki/Coadjutor_bishop" title="Coadjutor bishop">Coadjutor</a> <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Fuzhou" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Fuzhou">Vicar Apostolic of Fujian</a>, for which he was <a href="/wiki/Consecration_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Consecration (Catholic Church)">consecrated</a> a bishop on 22 February 1730 with the new <a href="/wiki/Titular_see" title="Titular see">titular see</a> of <a href="/wiki/Maurocastrum" title="Maurocastrum">Maurocastrum</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Nanjing" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Nanjing">Bishop of Nanking</a>, <a href="/w/index.php?title=Emmanuel_de_Jesus-Maria-Joseph&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Emmanuel de Jesus-Maria-Joseph (page does not exist)">Emmanuel de Jesus-Maria-Joseph</a>, with <a href="/wiki/Jo%C3%A3o_de_Casal" title="João de Casal">João de Casal</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Macau" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Macau">Bishop of Macau</a>, and <a href="/w/index.php?title=Francisco_da_Purifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o_da_Rocha_Froes&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Francisco da Purificação da Rocha Froes (page does not exist)">Francisco da Purificação da Rocha Froes</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Beijing" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Beijing">Bishop of Beijing</a>, serving as <a href="/wiki/Co-consecrators" class="mw-redirect" title="Co-consecrators">co-consecrators</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-PeterSanz_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PeterSanz-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He succeeded to the office of Vicar in January 1732, upon the death of Magino Ventallol, who had been unable to be consecrated a bishop during the thirteen years of his administration.<sup id="cite_ref-PeterSanz_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PeterSanz-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Martyrdom">Martyrdom</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Sanz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Martyrdom">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Sanz was arrested by imperial authorities in 1741, along with four other friars. They suffered torture and a long imprisonment in <a href="/wiki/Fuzhou" title="Fuzhou">Fuzhou</a>. Finally, on 26 May 1747, Sanz was <a href="/wiki/Decapitation" title="Decapitation">beheaded</a> at Fuzhou. In October 1748, word came that one of his companions had been named his <a href="/wiki/Coadjutor_bishop" title="Coadjutor bishop">coadjutor bishop</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Holy_See" title="Holy See">Holy See</a>. The other friars were executed immediately upon that news.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification_and_canonization">Beatification and canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Peter_Sanz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Beatification and canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He and his companions were <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatified</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Leo_XIII" title="Pope Leo XIII">Pope Leo XIII</a> on May 14, 1893. They were included among a group of 120 saints known collectively as the <a href="/wiki/Martyr_Saints_of_China" title="Martyr Saints of China">Martyr Saints of China</a> who were canonized on 1 October 2000 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a>. The group was given the <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> of 9 July.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>Previously, their <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> was kept on June 3, which is still the date kept by Dominicans who follow the <a href="/wiki/General_Roman_Calendar_of_1960" title="General Roman Calendar of 1960">General Roman Calendar of 1960</a> as part of the <a href="/wiki/Traditional_Catholicism" class="mw-redirect" title="Traditional Catholicism">traditional</a> <a href="/wiki/Dominican_Rite" title="Dominican Rite">Dominican Rite</a>, which was in place before the changes that occurred after Vatican II.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> The keeping of the <a href="/wiki/General_Roman_Calendar_of_1960" title="General Roman Calendar of 1960">General Roman Calendar of 1960</a> is in accordance with the universal permissions which were clarified in <a href="/wiki/Summorum_Pontificum" title="Summorum Pontificum">Summorum Pontificum</a>.
</p>
