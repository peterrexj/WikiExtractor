<div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Bishop of Armagh (d. 852)</div>
<p>

<b>Diarmaid of Armagh</b> (died <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;852</span>) was <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Armagh_(Roman_Catholic)" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Armagh (Roman Catholic)">Bishop of Armagh</a>. He was made Bishop of Armagh in 834, but was driven from his see by the usurper Foraunan in 835. However, he claimed his rights and collected his <a href="/wiki/Cess" title="Cess">cess</a> in <a href="/wiki/Connacht" title="Connacht">Connacht</a>, in 836. He lived in a stormy age, as the <a href="/wiki/Scandinavian_history" class="mw-redirect" title="Scandinavian history">Scandinavian</a> rovers under <a href="/wiki/Turgesius" title="Turgesius">Turgesius</a> seized <a href="/wiki/Armagh" title="Armagh">Armagh</a> in 841 and leveled the churches. The <i><a href="/wiki/Annals_of_Ulster" title="Annals of Ulster">Annals of Ulster</a></i> describe him as one of  "the wisest of the doctors of Europe". He is also known as Saint Dermot, and his <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Calendar of Saints">feast</a> is celebrated on 24 April .
