<p><b>Varghese Payyappilly</b> was a <a href="/wiki/Syro-Malabar" class="mw-redirect" title="Syro-Malabar">Syro-Malabar</a> priest from the <a href="/wiki/India" title="India">Indian</a> state of <a href="/wiki/Kerala" title="Kerala">Kerala</a> and the founder of the congregation of <a href="/wiki/Sisters_of_the_Destitute" title="Sisters of the Destitute">Sisters of the Destitute</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-times_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-times-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was declared <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> on 14 April 2018.<sup id="cite_ref-CNA_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CNA-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Family">Family</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Family">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Kathanar was born as Kunjuvaru on 8 August 1876 to Payyappilly Lonan and Kunjumariam in the Palakkappilly branch of Payyappilly Nasrani family at Perumanoor, <a href="/wiki/Thevara" title="Thevara">Thevara</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Sisters_of_the_Destitute_–_Founder_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sisters_of_the_Destitute_–_Founder-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-obit-priests_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-obit-priests-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> Payyappilly is an ancient <a href="/wiki/Saint_Thomas_Christian" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Thomas Christian">Saint Thomas Christian</a> family in India, baptized by <a href="/wiki/Thomas_the_Apostle" title="Thomas the Apostle">Thomas the Apostle</a> in the first century.<sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> The word Payyappilly, translated as "beautiful building" or "beautiful house", is derived from the <a href="/wiki/Syriac_language" title="Syriac language">Syriac</a> ܦܐܝܐ <i>Payya</i> "beautiful, radiant" and the <a href="/wiki/Malayalam" title="Malayalam">Malayalam</a> word പിള്ളി <i>Pilly</i>, a variant of പള്ളി <i>Pally</i>, "church, building". <a href="/wiki/Mary_Celine_Payyappilly" title="Mary Celine Payyappilly">Mary Celine Payyappilly</a> is another <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> from the Payyappilly family.<sup id="cite_ref-Indian_Express_7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Indian_Express-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Payyappilly was educated in the parish school at Perumanoor and at St. Albert's School, <a href="/wiki/Ernakulam" title="Ernakulam">Ernakulam</a>. He received his religious training from the Mangalapuzha Seminary at Puthenpally and the <a href="/wiki/Papal_Seminary" title="Papal Seminary">Papal Seminary</a> at <a href="/wiki/Kandy" title="Kandy">Kandy</a>, Sri Lanka. He was ordained a priest on 21 December 1907 and was known as Payyappilly Varghese Kathanar. He learned the <a href="/wiki/Syriac_language" title="Syriac language">Syriac language</a> under the guidance of <a href="/wiki/Aloysius_Pazheparambil" title="Aloysius Pazheparambil">Aloysius Pazheparambil</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-soc_8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-soc-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Payyappilly served as parish priest in <a href="/wiki/Kadamakkudy" class="mw-redirect" title="Kadamakkudy">Kadamakkudy</a> (1909–11), <a href="/wiki/Alangad" title="Alangad">Alangad</a> (1911–13) and <a href="/wiki/Arakuzha" title="Arakuzha">Arakuzha</a> (1920–22). During his tenure in <a href="/wiki/Marth_Mariam_Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Forane_Church,_Arakuzha" class="mw-redirect" title="Marth Mariam Syro-Malabar Catholic Forane Church, Arakuzha">Marth Mariam Syro-Malabar Catholic Forane Church, Arakuzha</a> he began the St. Mary's Higher Secondary School there. His efforts transformed the parish and helped to solve many long-lasting family problems. He made some land purchases for the church, thus making it self-sufficient.<sup id="cite_ref-soc_8-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-soc-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> He also purchased 12 acres (49,000&#160;m<sup>2</sup>) of land in <a href="/wiki/M._C._Road" class="mw-redirect" title="M. C. Road">M. C. Road</a> for constructing <a href="/wiki/St._Joseph%27s_Syro-Malabar_Catholic_Church,_Meenkunnam" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Joseph&#39;s Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Meenkunnam">St. Joseph's Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Meenkunnam</a>.
</p><p>He was manager of St. Mary's High School, <a href="/wiki/Aluva" title="Aluva">Aluva</a>, <a href="/wiki/Travancore" title="Travancore">Travancore</a>, between 1913–1920 and 1922–1929.<sup id="cite_ref-soc_8-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-soc-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> According to <a href="/wiki/Joseph_Parecattil" title="Joseph Parecattil">Joseph Parecattil</a>, who was a pupil at the time, the school was a seedbed of priestly vocations during Varghese Kathanar's tenure.<sup id="cite_ref-jp_9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jp-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Payyappilly served as a member of the Diocesan Council, as Director of Apostolic Union as well as Priests' Provident Fund. He was a good mediator and people approached him seeking solutions to their problems.<sup id="cite_ref-jp_9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-jp-9">&#91;9&#93;</a></sup> He was held in great honour by the church authorities and equally by the officials in the education department and government officers.<sup id="cite_ref-soc_8-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-soc-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup> His concern and care for the poor and the suffering were noted particularly in the way he helped the victims of a flood in 1924.<sup id="cite_ref-times_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-times-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He transformed his parish into a center for the homeless<sup id="cite_ref-Vatican_News_10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vatican_News-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup> and turned St. Mary's High School into a shelter for the suffering people and brought food for them in a hired boat.<sup id="cite_ref-Sisters_of_the_Destitute_–_Founder_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sisters_of_the_Destitute_–_Founder-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Sisters_of_the_Destitute">Sisters of the Destitute</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Sisters of the Destitute">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>On 19 March 1927, Payyappilly founded the Sisters of the Destitute in Chunangamvely.<sup id="cite_ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11">&#91;11&#93;</a></sup> This organisation was intended to continue what he saw as Christ's redemptive mission among the poor. He found abandoned people, brought them to the shelter of the Home for the Aged and nursed them. The S.D. was originally named <i>Little Sisters of the Poor</i> but in 1933 the name was changed to <i>Sisters of the Destitute</i> in order not to be confused with another congregation having the same title.<sup id="cite_ref-soc_8-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-soc-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Payyappilly fell ill in September 1929 and eventually was hospitalised. He died of <a href="/wiki/Typhoid" class="mw-redirect" title="Typhoid">typhoid</a> on 5 October 1929 and was buried in St. John Nepumsian Syro-Malabar Catholic Church, Konthuruthy.<sup id="cite_ref-soc_8-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-soc-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Stages_of_canonization">Stages of canonization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Stages of canonization">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Servant_of_God">Servant of God</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Servant of God">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Cause of the Beatification of Payyappilly was initiated on 25 August 2009. He was declared as <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> by <a href="/wiki/Varkey_Vithayathil" title="Varkey Vithayathil">Varkey Vithayathil</a>, <a href="/wiki/Major_Archbishop" class="mw-redirect" title="Major Archbishop">Major Archbishop</a> of the Syro-Malabar Catholic Church on 6 September 2009.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Venerable">Venerable</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Varghese_Payyappilly&amp;action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Venerable">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>In February 2011, his remains were exhumed, identified and transferred to a new tomb. The Miracle Tribunal that had been established to study the miraculous healing of Sr. Mercina through Payyappilly's intercession was closed in May 2012<sup id="cite_ref-Archdiocesan_Tribunal_Closing_12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Archdiocesan_Tribunal_Closing-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup> and the Archdiocesan Tribunal was closed in November 2012.<sup id="cite_ref-Archdiocesan_Tribunal_Closing_12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Archdiocesan_Tribunal_Closing-12">&#91;12&#93;</a></sup> His case was submitted to the <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a>, <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>, and on 14 April 2018 <a href="/wiki/Pope_Francis" title="Pope Francis">Pope Francis</a> declared him "Venerable".<sup id="cite_ref-Vatican_News_10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Vatican_News-10">&#91;10&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
