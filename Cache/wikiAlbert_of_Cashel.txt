</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:172px;"><a href="/wiki/File:IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/df/IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg/170px-IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg" decoding="async" width="170" height="128" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/df/IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg/255px-IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/df/IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg/340px-IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg 2x" data-file-width="2592" data-file-height="1944" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:IMGRockCashel_4756w.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Rock_of_Cashel" title="Rock of Cashel">Rock of Cashel</a></div></div></div>
<p><b>Albert of Cashel</b> was an eighth century <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> and <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">Patron</a> of <a href="/wiki/Cashel,_County_Tipperary" title="Cashel, County Tipperary">Cashel, Ireland</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Albert_of_Cashel&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Traditionally held to be an Englishman who worked in Ireland and then <a href="/wiki/Bavaria" title="Bavaria">Bavaria</a>, Albert went to Jerusalem and died in Regensburg on his return journey.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was an <a href="/wiki/Evangelism" title="Evangelism">evangelist</a> working mainly around the city of Cashel, and may have been a <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Cashel" title="Archbishop of Cashel">bishop</a> there.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> In a legendary 12th century biography he is called <i>natione Anglus, conversatione Angelicus</i> - “by race an <a href="/wiki/Angle" title="Angle">Angle</a>, in manners an angel". He continued his <a href="/wiki/Cashel,_County_Tipperary#Ancient_history" title="Cashel, County Tipperary">work as an Evangelist</a> in <a href="/wiki/Bavaria" title="Bavaria">Bavaria</a> with <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a> <a href="/wiki/Erhard_of_Regensburg" title="Erhard of Regensburg">Erhard of Regensburg</a> and is reported to have suffered from arthritis in his back and hips.
</p><p>He made a <a href="/wiki/Pilgrimage" title="Pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a> to Jerusalem, with <a href="/wiki/Erhard_of_Regensburg" title="Erhard of Regensburg">Erhard of Regensburg</a>. He traveled to Jerusalem but died in 800 <a href="/wiki/Anno_Domini" title="Anno Domini">AD</a> at <a href="/wiki/Regensburg" title="Regensburg">Regensburg</a> on the return journey. Albert's grave is in <a href="/wiki/Niederm%C3%BCnster" class="mw-redirect" title="Niedermünster">Niedermünster</a> in <a href="/wiki/Regensburg" title="Regensburg">Regensburg</a><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> and he was <a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">canonized</a> 19 June 1902 by Pope <a href="/wiki/Leo_XIII" class="mw-redirect" title="Leo XIII">Leo XIII</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Controversy">Controversy</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Albert_of_Cashel&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Controversy">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His existence has been questioned by some,<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> others question his role as <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Cashel" title="Archbishop of Cashel">Archbishop of Cashel</a>, stating that this <a href="/wiki/Diocese" title="Diocese">diocese</a> did not exist<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Cashel#History" title="Archbishop of Cashel">until 1118 AD</a>.
</p>
