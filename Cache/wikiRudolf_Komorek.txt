<p><b>Rudolf Komórek</b> (11 October 1890 – 11 December 1949) was a <a href="/wiki/Poles" title="Poles">Polish</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a> and a professed member of the <a href="/wiki/Salesians_of_Don_Bosco" title="Salesians of Don Bosco">Salesians of Don Bosco</a> while also serving as part of the missions in <a href="/wiki/Brazil" title="Brazil">Brazil</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He served during <a href="/wiki/World_War_I" title="World War I">World War I</a> as a hospital chaplain and began the process of joining the order of <a href="/wiki/John_Bosco" title="John Bosco">Saint John Bosco</a> following his time as a <a href="/wiki/Prisoner_of_war" title="Prisoner of war">prisoner of war</a> in <a href="/wiki/Trento" title="Trento">Trento</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>The cause for sainthood commenced on 31 January 1964 in which he became a <a href="/wiki/Servant_of_God" title="Servant of God">Servant of God</a> and <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> named him to be <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> on 6 April 1995.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Rudolf_Kom%C3%B3rek&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Rudolf Komórek was born on 11 October 1890 in <a href="/wiki/Bielitz" class="mw-redirect" title="Bielitz">Bielitz</a> as the third of seven children of Jan Komórek, a blacksmith, and Agnieszka Gach, seamstress and <a href="/wiki/Midwife" title="Midwife">midwife</a>. His sisters were Maria, Wanda and Valerie and his brothers were Jan, Leopold and Robert. He was <a href="/wiki/Baptized" class="mw-redirect" title="Baptized">baptized</a> on 30 November 1890.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Komórek attended school as a child from 1896 until 1901 when he attended a German high school in his hometown. He passed examinations on 13 July 1909 with distinctions and commenced his studies for the <a href="/wiki/Priesthood" class="mw-redirect" title="Priesthood">priesthood</a> on 30 September 1909 in <a href="/wiki/Wroc%C5%82aw" title="Wrocław">Wrocław</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He received the sacrament of Confirmation on 20 July 1910 and went on to receive the tonsure on 11 July 1911 from Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Georg_von_Kopp" title="Georg von Kopp">Georg von Kopp</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Komórek received the minor orders on 11 March 1913 and the title of sub-deacon on the following 13 March. Cardinal Kopp <a href="/wiki/Ordained" class="mw-redirect" title="Ordained">ordained</a> the seminarian to the <a href="/wiki/Diaconate" class="mw-redirect" title="Diaconate">diaconate</a> on 15 March 1913 while ordaining him to the priesthood on the following 22 July.
</p>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:182px;"><a href="/wiki/File:AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg/180px-AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg.png" decoding="async" width="180" height="50" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg/270px-AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg.png 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/ea/AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg/360px-AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg.png 2x" data-file-width="218" data-file-height="60" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:AUT_KuK_Kriegsbande_BAR.svg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The ribbon of the award granted to him.</div></div></div>
<p>Komórek served as the vicar of a local parish from 1 August 1913 until such time when he was appointed to a new position and served in <a href="/wiki/Zabrzeg" title="Zabrzeg">Zabrzeg</a> from 1 March until 31 May 1914. On 1 May 1914 he became a chaplain of the 100 Infantry Regiment as a captain due to the tensions that resulted in <a href="/wiki/World_War_I" title="World War I">World War I</a> and on 1 June moved from <a href="/wiki/Zabrze" title="Zabrze">Zabrze</a> to <a href="/wiki/Skocz%C3%B3w" title="Skoczów">Skoczów</a> while later relocating to <a href="/wiki/Krak%C3%B3w" title="Kraków">Kraków</a> tending to the wounded in hospitals. On 13 May 1916 – at his request – he was sent to the frontlines in <a href="/wiki/Leogang" title="Leogang">Leogang</a> and later in <a href="/wiki/Tyrol_(state)" title="Tyrol (state)">Tyrol</a>; his efforts saw him honored by the <a href="/wiki/Red_Cross" class="mw-redirect" title="Red Cross">Red Cross</a> and being awarded the <a href="/wiki/Military_Merit_Cross_(Austria-Hungary)" title="Military Merit Cross (Austria-Hungary)">Cross of Merit</a> by <a href="/wiki/Austria" title="Austria">Austrian</a> officials.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Italian forces arrested him and kept him as a <a href="/wiki/Prisoner_of_war" title="Prisoner of war">prisoner of war</a> for two months in <a href="/wiki/Trento" title="Trento">Trento</a>; his release allowed him to return home to <a href="/wiki/Poland" title="Poland">Poland</a>.
</p><p>He later joined he <a href="/wiki/Salesians_of_Don_Bosco" title="Salesians of Don Bosco">Salesians of Don Bosco</a> – upon asking for the permission of Cardinal <a href="/wiki/Adolf_Bertram" title="Adolf Bertram">Adolf Bertram</a> – and commenced his <a href="/wiki/Novitiate" title="Novitiate">novitiate</a> on 30 October 1922 near <a href="/wiki/Wadowice" title="Wadowice">Wadowice</a>. Komórek made his temporary vows on 1 November 1932.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> He was called served in the missions in <a href="/wiki/Brazil" title="Brazil">Brazil</a> and arrived there in October 1924 while arriving in <a href="/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro" title="Rio de Janeiro">Rio de Janeiro</a> on the following 27 November.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Komórek made his solemn profession as a Salesian on 28 January 1930 in one of their houses and served as a curate in a local parish from 1929 to 1934 while serving in the same position in another location from 1934 to 1936.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_2-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>In January 1941 a persistent cough worried his superiors which led to the diagnosis of <a href="/wiki/Tuberculosis" title="Tuberculosis">tuberculosis</a>. Komórek died of that ailment in <a href="/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Jos%C3%A9_dos_Campos" title="São José dos Campos">São José dos Campos</a> on 11 December 1949 at 11:20pm while holding a large <a href="/wiki/Crucifix" title="Crucifix">Crucifix</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-SM_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SM-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was buried in Brazil.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Beatification_process">Beatification process</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Rudolf_Kom%C3%B3rek&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Beatification process">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The beatification process opened under <a href="/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI" title="Pope Paul VI">Pope Paul VI</a> in <a href="/wiki/Taubat%C3%A9" title="Taubaté">Taubaté</a> on 31 January 1964 in an informative process that later concluded its work on 20 June 1969;<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_2-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> theologians approved his writings as being in line with the faith on 14 June 1974. The <a href="/wiki/Congregation_for_the_Causes_of_Saints" class="mw-redirect" title="Congregation for the Causes of Saints">Congregation for the Causes of Saints</a> granted the decree of validation for the process on 20 April 1990 in <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a>.
</p><p>The C.C.S. received the <a href="/wiki/Positio" title="Positio">Positio</a> in 1992 and was transferred to their consulting theologians on 29 November 1994 in which the cause received approval; the C.C.S. themselves approved the process on 14 March 1995. <a href="/wiki/Pope_John_Paul_II" title="Pope John Paul II">Pope John Paul II</a> declared Komórek to be <a href="/wiki/Venerable" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerable">Venerable</a> on 6 April 1995 after confirming his life of <a href="/wiki/Heroic_virtue" title="Heroic virtue">heroic virtue</a>.
</p><p>The miracle required for potential beatification – from 1999 – was investigated and later received C.C.S. validation on 10 February 2006 while a medical board met and approved the miracle on 14 January 2010.
</p><p>The current <a href="/wiki/Postulator" title="Postulator">postulator</a> assigned to the cause is the Rev. Pierluigi Cameroni and the current vice-postulator is the Bishop Hilario Moser.
</p>
