<p>Saint <b>Asterius of Ostia</b> (d. 3rd century AD) was a <a href="/wiki/Martyred" class="mw-redirect" title="Martyred">martyred</a> <a href="/wiki/Priest" title="Priest">priest</a>.  Information on this saint is based on the apocryphal <i>Acts of Saint Callixtus</i>.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  According to tradition, he was a priest of <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> who recovered the body of <a href="/wiki/Pope_Callixtus_I" title="Pope Callixtus I">Pope Callixtus I</a> after it had been tossed into a well around 222 AD.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Asterius buried Callixtus' body at night but was arrested for this action by the prefect Alexander and then killed by being thrown off a bridge into the <a href="/wiki/Tiber_River" class="mw-redirect" title="Tiber River">Tiber River</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>According to tradition, his body washed up at Ostia and was buried there.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Asterius_of_Ostia&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Asterius was venerated from at least the 4th or 5th centuries.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>   A saint with the same name, along with that of his daughter, were <a href="/wiki/Translation_(relics)" class="mw-redirect" title="Translation (relics)">translated</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Sergius_II" title="Pope Sergius II">Pope Sergius II</a> between 844 and 847 and rest in the Church of <a href="/wiki/San_Martino_ai_Monti" title="San Martino ai Monti">San Martino ai Monti</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Esquiline" class="mw-redirect" title="Esquiline">Esquiline</a>, according to <a href="/wiki/Anastasius_the_Librarian" class="mw-redirect" title="Anastasius the Librarian">Anastasius the Librarian</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  However, the “<a href="/wiki/Bollandists" class="mw-redirect" title="Bollandists">Bollandists</a> think that this is the body of another Asterius.”<sup id="cite_ref-Sabine_1-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Sabine-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
