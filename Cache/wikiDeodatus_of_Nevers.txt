<p><b>Deodatus</b> (<b>Dié, Didier, Dieudonné, Déodat, Adéodat</b>) <b>of Nevers</b> (d. June 19, ca. 679 AD) was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Nevers" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Nevers">bishop of Nevers</a> from 655.  Deodatus lived with <a href="/wiki/Saint_Arbogast" title="Saint Arbogast">Arbogast</a> in the monastery of <a href="/wiki/Ebersheim,_Bas-Rhin" title="Ebersheim, Bas-Rhin">Ebersheim</a>, established by <a href="/wiki/Childeric_II" title="Childeric II">Childeric II</a> near <a href="/wiki/S%C3%A9lestat" title="Sélestat">Sélestat</a> in the forest of <a href="/wiki/Haguenau" title="Haguenau">Haguenau</a>.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Exploits">Exploits</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Deodatus_of_Nevers&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Exploits">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Deodatus' establishment of the monastery Juncturae (Jointures) in the present town of <a href="/wiki/Saint-Di%C3%A9" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint-Dié">Saint-Dié</a> followed his appointment as the bishop of Nevers.  He placed Jointures under the Rule of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Columban" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Columban">Saint Columban</a> (later changed to that of <a href="/wiki/Rule_of_Saint_Benedict" title="Rule of Saint Benedict">Saint Benedict</a>).
</p><p>He baptized the son of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Hunna" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Hunna">Saint Hunna</a> (Una), who was also named Deodatus and who is also venerated as a saint.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  Hunna's son became a monk at Ebersheim. 
</p><p>After 664 Deodatus renounced his see to withdraw to the so-called valley of "Galilaea" in the <a href="/wiki/Vosges_Mountains" class="mw-redirect" title="Vosges Mountains">Vosges</a>, where he lived as a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> in a cell.
</p><p>Tradition states that he died in the arms of <a href="/wiki/Saint_Hidulphus" class="mw-redirect" title="Saint Hidulphus">Saint Hidulphus</a>, bishop of <a href="/wiki/Treves" class="mw-redirect" title="Treves">Treves</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Deodatus_of_Nevers&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The town of Saint-Dié grew up around the monastery of Jointures.  However, some sources connect the name with an earlier saint, Deodatus of Blois (d. 525).<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
