<p><b>Saint Quintian</b> (<i>Quintianus, Quinctianus, Quintien</i>) (died ca. 525) was a <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Rodez" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Rodez">bishop of Rodez</a> and a bishop of <a href="/wiki/Clermont-Ferrand" title="Clermont-Ferrand">Clermont-Ferrand</a> (<i>Arvernes</i>) in the sixth century, and participated in the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_Agde" title="Council of Agde">Councils of Agde</a> (508) and <a href="/wiki/First_Council_of_Orl%C3%A9ans" title="First Council of Orléans">Orleans</a> (511).
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quintian_of_Rodez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Tradition makes him a native of Africa and a priest of <a href="/wiki/Carthage" title="Carthage">Carthage</a> who fled to <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a> due to the persecutions of the <a href="/wiki/Vandals" title="Vandals">Vandals</a>.  At the death of <a href="/w/index.php?title=Amantius_of_Rodez&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Amantius of Rodez (page does not exist)">Saint Amantius</a> (<i>Amans</i>) in 487, Quintian succeeded him as bishop of <a href="/wiki/Rodez" title="Rodez">Rodez</a>.  During the war between the <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Franks</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Visigoth" class="mw-redirect" title="Visigoth">West Goths</a>, he was a zealous supporter of <a href="/wiki/Clovis_I" title="Clovis I">Clovis I</a>. To avoid the persecutions of the <a href="/wiki/Arianism" title="Arianism">Arian</a> <a href="/wiki/Visigoths" title="Visigoths">Visigoths</a>, he fled Rodez and proceeded to <a href="/wiki/Auvergne_(province)" class="mw-redirect" title="Auvergne (province)">Auvergne</a>, where he was hospitably received by Bishop <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Clermont-Ferrand" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Clermont-Ferrand">Euphrasius</a> (<i>Eufrèse</i>). King <a href="/wiki/Theodoric_I" title="Theodoric I">Theodoric I</a> appointed Quinctianus successor to Sidonius Apollinaris (grandson of the famous <a href="/wiki/Sidonius_Apollinaris" title="Sidonius Apollinaris">Sidonius Apollinaris</a>), <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Clermont" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Clermont">Bishop of Clermont</a>. On the death of the latter, Quinctianus succeeded to the See of Clermont, which he occupied until his death on November 13, 525 or 526
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Quintian_of_Rodez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>His feast is celebrated on November 13, except at Rodez, where it is kept on June 14. In the "Roman Martyrology" his name stands under both dates.
</p>
