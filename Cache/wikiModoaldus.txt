<p><b>Saint Modoald</b>, also known as <b>Romoald</b>, was the <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Trier" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Trier">archbishop of Trier</a> from 626 to 645. He is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Reichsabtei" class="mw-redirect" title="Reichsabtei">Reichsabtei</a> <a href="/wiki/Helmarshausen" title="Helmarshausen">Helmarshausen</a> and his liturgical feast is on 12 May.
</p><p>Modoald was born in <a href="/wiki/Aquitaine" title="Aquitaine">Aquitaine</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-Arduino_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arduino-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> the son of <a href="/wiki/Arnulf_of_Metz" title="Arnulf of Metz">Arnulf</a>, later <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Metz" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Metz">Bishop of Metz</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-aoca_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-aoca-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He had been at the Court of the Merovingian King <a href="/wiki/Dagobert_I" title="Dagobert I">Dagobert I</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> when the King had him made Archbishop of Trier.<sup id="cite_ref-Arduino_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Arduino-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Germanus_of_Granfelden" title="Germanus of Granfelden">Germanus of Granfelden</a> was from a wealthy senatorial family of <a href="/wiki/Trier" title="Trier">Trier</a>. His father entrusted him at a young age to Bishop Modoald, for his education.<sup id="cite_ref-Boillon_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Boillon-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Modoald established the community of Saint-Symphorien, on the <a href="/wiki/Moselle" title="Moselle">Moselle</a>; and placed it under the supervision of his sister Saint Severa.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
