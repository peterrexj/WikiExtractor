<p><b>Ivan Merz</b> (16 December 1896 &#8211; 10 May 1928) was a <a href="/wiki/Croatians" class="mw-redirect" title="Croatians">Croatian</a> lay academic, beatified by Pope <a href="/wiki/John_Paul_II" class="mw-redirect" title="John Paul II">John Paul II</a> on a visit at <a href="/wiki/Banja_Luka" title="Banja Luka">Banja Luka</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bosnia_and_Herzegovina" title="Bosnia and Herzegovina">Bosnia and Herzegovina</a> on June 22, 2003.  Ivan Merz promoted the liturgical movement in <a href="/wiki/Croatia" title="Croatia">Croatia</a> and together with <a href="/wiki/Ivo_Protulipac" title="Ivo Protulipac">Ivo Protulipac</a> created a movement for the young people, “The Croatian union of the Eagles” (“Hrvatski orlovski savez)”, inspired by the “Eucharistic Crusade,” which he had encountered in <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy_in_the_Philippines">Legacy in the Philippines</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ivan_Merz&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Legacy in the Philippines">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Director of the Confraternity, Dave Ceasar Dela Cruz was elected as the Vice Postulator of the Cause for the Canonization of Blessed Ivan Merz <sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> on 19 March 2008 by the Vatican through the Congregation for the Causes of Saints.
</p>
