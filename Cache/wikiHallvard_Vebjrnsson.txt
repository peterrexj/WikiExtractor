<p><b>Hallvard Vebjørnsson</b> (<i>Hallvard Den Hellige</i>) (<abbr title="circa">c.</abbr> 1020–1043), commonly referred to as <b>Saint Hallvard</b> (<i>Sankt Hallvard</i>), is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of <a href="/wiki/Oslo" title="Oslo">Oslo</a>. He is considered a <a href="/wiki/Martyr" title="Martyr">martyr</a> because of his defence of an innocent <a href="/wiki/Thrall" title="Thrall">thrall</a> woman. His religious feast day is 15 May.
</p><p>The connection of St. Hallvard to the city of <a href="/wiki/Oslo" title="Oslo">Oslo</a> was evidenced by the fact that his image was recorded in the city's seal since the Middle Ages. The municipality's highest honor, the St. Hallvard Medal (<i>St. Hallvard-medaljen</i>), was named after him in 1950.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Hallvard_Vebj%C3%B8rnsson&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Little is known of his life, and all traditional stories relate to his death near <a href="/wiki/Drammen" title="Drammen">Drammen</a>. Although the exact year of birth and place of his birth are unknown, he is commonly believed to have been born ca. 1020   According to tradition, his father was the farmer Vebjørn.  His parents were wealthy farmers and owned the farm Husaby in <a href="/wiki/Lier,_Norway" title="Lier, Norway">Lier</a>. His mother, Torny Gudbrandsdatter, was reportedly related to <a href="/wiki/Olaf_II_of_Norway" title="Olaf II of Norway">St. Olaf</a>, the patron saint of <a href="/wiki/Norway" title="Norway">Norway</a>.  It is said that she was the daughter of Gudbrand Kula from <a href="/wiki/Oppland" title="Oppland">Oppland</a>,  who was also the father of <a href="/wiki/%C3%85sta_Gudbrandsdatter" title="Åsta Gudbrandsdatter">Åsta Gudbrandsdatter</a>, St. Olaf's mother.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:252px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/53/Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG/250px-Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG" decoding="async" width="250" height="188" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/53/Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG/375px-Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/53/Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG/500px-Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG 2x" data-file-width="3264" data-file-height="2448" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Hallvardskatedralen_2012-4.JPG" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Ruins of <a href="/wiki/St._Hallvard%27s_Cathedral" title="St. Hallvard&#39;s Cathedral">St. Hallvard's Cathedral</a> in Oslo</div></div></div>

Nidaros Cathedral" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/37/Domkirka_statuer_20.jpg" decoding="async" width="80" height="265" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="265" /></a></div>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Death">Death</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Hallvard_Vebj%C3%B8rnsson&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Death">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Hallvard defended a <a href="/wiki/Pregnant_woman" class="mw-redirect" title="Pregnant woman">pregnant woman</a>, most likely a <a href="/wiki/Thrall" title="Thrall">thrall</a>, who had been given sanctuary from three men accusing her of theft. Hallvard believed in her innocence and took her on his boat. Hallvard, together with the woman, were killed by arrows from the men. The woman was buried on the beach. Hallvard, however, was bound with a <a href="/wiki/Millstone" title="Millstone">millstone</a> around his neck, and the men attempted to sink his body in <a href="/wiki/Drammensfjord" title="Drammensfjord">Drammensfjord</a> but it refused to remain submerged and as a result their crimes were discovered. A local village buried him in a Christian manner and people came to regard him as a martyr to their faith.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Veneration">Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Hallvard_Vebj%C3%B8rnsson&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:222px;"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Hallvard_.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/94/St._Hallvard_.jpg/220px-St._Hallvard_.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="317" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/94/St._Hallvard_.jpg/330px-St._Hallvard_.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/94/St._Hallvard_.jpg/440px-St._Hallvard_.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3195" data-file-height="4598" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:St._Hallvard_.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Saint Hallvard</div></div></div>
<p><a href="/wiki/St._Hallvard%27s_Cathedral" title="St. Hallvard&#39;s Cathedral">St. Hallvard's Cathedral</a> (<i>Hallvardskatedralen</i>)  in Oslo was dedicated to his name  and his remains were relocated to the facility which was finished in 1130.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (February 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>The Cathedral was built on the hill just north of the area that is now the <a href="/wiki/Old_Town,_Oslo" title="Old Town, Oslo">Old Town market square</a> in Oslo (intersection of Bispegata –Oslo gate). For almost 500 years this was the most important church in the city. Besides being the bishop's seat and religious center, the cathedral was the coronation church, the royal wedding church and the royal burial chapel. The facility was in use as a church until about 1655.  It fell into disrepair in the 17th century and is today a ruin situated in <a href="/wiki/Minneparken" title="Minneparken">Minneparken</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8">&#91;8&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>15 May is celebrated as St. Hallvard's Day. This is also known as Oslo Day.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (February 2022)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
