<p><b>Melan</b> is a village and a former municipality in the <a href="/wiki/Dib%C3%ABr_County" title="Dibër County">Dibër County</a>, northeastern <a href="/wiki/Albania" title="Albania">Albania</a>. At the 2015 local government reform it became a subdivision of the municipality <a href="/wiki/Dib%C3%ABr,_Albania" class="mw-redirect" title="Dibër, Albania">Dibër</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> The population at the 2011 census was 3,649.<sup id="cite_ref-census11_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-census11-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
