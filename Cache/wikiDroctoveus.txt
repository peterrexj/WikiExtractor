					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;">Saint</div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Droctoveus</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data"><abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;530</span><br />Auxerre, France</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Residence</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Saint-Germain-des-Pr%C3%A9s_(abbey)" title="Saint-Germain-des-Prés (abbey)">Saint Germain-des-Pres</a> in Paris</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data"><abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;580</span></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">10 March</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>Saint <b>Droctoveus</b> (or <b>Droctonius, Droctovée, Droctovius, Drote, Drottoveo, Drotté</b>; <abbr title="circa">c.</abbr><span style="white-space:nowrap;">&#8201;530–580</span>) was a <a href="/wiki/Franks" title="Franks">Frankish</a> abbot, the first abbot of what became <a href="/wiki/Saint-Germain-des-Pr%C3%A9s_(abbey)" title="Saint-Germain-des-Prés (abbey)">Saint Germain-des-Pres</a> in Paris.
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Droctovaeus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The oldest account of Drottoveo's life was written by his contemporary <a href="/wiki/Venantius_Fortunatus" title="Venantius Fortunatus">Venantius Fortunatus</a> in chapter XI of the 1.IX of his <i>Carmina</i> (Songs).
A <i>Life</i> was lost after the raids of the <a href="/wiki/Normans" title="Normans">Normans</a>, who set fire to the monastery of Saint Vincent in Paris in 845 and again in 857.
A monk named Gislemaro of the monastery of Saint Vincent wrote an account between 841 and 847 based on oral testimony, but it has little value.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEFusconi_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEFusconi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Droctoveus was born around 530.
He became a monk and disciple of Saint <a href="/wiki/Germain_of_Paris" title="Germain of Paris">Germain of Paris</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEFusconi_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEFusconi-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
He became abbot of the <a href="/wiki/Abbey_of_Saint-Symphorien,_Autun" title="Abbey of Saint-Symphorien, Autun">Abbey of Saint-Symphorien, Autun</a>, and was then appointed first abbot of Saint Vincent, later known as <a href="/wiki/Saint-Germain-des-Pr%C3%A9s_(abbey)" title="Saint-Germain-des-Prés (abbey)">Saint Germain-des-Pres</a> in Paris.
He died around 580 of natural causes.
His feast day is 10 March.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESaint_Droctoveus_..._CatholicSaints_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESaint_Droctoveus_..._CatholicSaints-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Monks_of_Ramsgate_account">Monks of Ramsgate account</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Droctovaeus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Monks of Ramsgate account">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The monks of <a href="/wiki/St_Augustine%27s_Abbey,_Ramsgate" title="St Augustine&#39;s Abbey, Ramsgate">St Augustine's Abbey, Ramsgate</a>, wrote in their <i>Book of Saints</i> (1921),
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r996844942">.mw-parser-output .templatequote{overflow:hidden;margin:1em 0;padding:0 40px}.mw-parser-output .templatequote .templatequotecite{line-height:1.5em;text-align:left;padding-left:1.6em;margin-top:0}</style><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>DROCTOVEUS (DROCTONIUS) (St.)  Abbot. (Mar. 10)<br />(6th century) A disciple of Saint Germanus of Paris, who became Abbot of the monastery of <a href="/wiki/Symphorianus_of_Autun" class="mw-redirect" title="Symphorianus of Autun">Saint Symphorian</a> at <a href="/wiki/Autun" title="Autun">Autun</a>, a Religious House in which a Rule was followed modelled upon that of the Solitaries of Egypt. When Saint Germanus had become Bishop of Paris and King <a href="/wiki/Childebert_I" title="Childebert I">Childebert</a> had founded the Abbey of Saint Vincent (since called Saint Germain des Pres) Saint Germanus set Saint Droctoveus over it. He ruled the monastery till his death at the age of forty-five (about A.D. 580), “the embodiment (so the chroniclers describe him) of Christian and monastic perfection.” <a href="/wiki/Venantius_Fortunatus" title="Venantius Fortunatus">Venantius Fortunatus</a> has left us some lines of verse in praise of Saint Droctoveus.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTESt._Augustine&#39;s_Abbey,_Ramsgate192186_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTESt._Augustine&#39;s_Abbey,_Ramsgate192186-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> </p></blockquote>
<h2><span id="Butler.27s_account"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Butler's_account">Butler's account</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Droctovaeus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Butler&#039;s account">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The hagiographer <a href="/wiki/Alban_Butler" title="Alban Butler">Alban Butler</a> ( 1710–1773) wrote in his <i>Lives of the Fathers, Martyrs, and Other Principal Saints</i>, under March 10,
</p>
<link rel="mw-deduplicated-inline-style" href="mw-data:TemplateStyles:r996844942"/><blockquote class="templatequote"><p>St. Droctovæus, Abbot
<br />KING CHILDEBERT having built at Paris a famous abbey in honour of St. Vincent, this saint, who was a native of the diocess of Autun, had been educated under St. Germanus, abbot of St. Symphorian’s at Autun, and was a person eminent for his learning and extraordinary spirit of mortification and prayer, was appointed the second, according to Duplessis<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEDu_Plessis175360,_68_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEDu_Plessis175360,_68-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> according to others, the first Abbot of this house, since called St. Germain-des-Prez, in which he died about the year 580. His body is kept in that abbey, and he is honoured by the church on the 10th of March. His original life being lost, Gislemar, a Benedictin monk of this house, in the ninth age, collected from tradition and scattered memoirs that which we have in Bollandus, and more accurately in Mabillon.<sup id="cite_ref-FOOTNOTEButler1815112–113_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FOOTNOTEButler1815112–113-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> </p></blockquote>
