					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1033289096">.mw-parser-output .hatnote{font-style:italic}.mw-parser-output div.hatnote{padding-left:1.6em;margin-bottom:0.5em}.mw-parser-output .hatnote i{font-style:normal}.mw-parser-output .hatnote+link+.hatnote{margin-top:-0.5em}</style><div role="note" class="hatnote navigation-not-searchable">For Ida, daughter of Henry II of Louvain and wife of Baldwin II of Hainaut, see <a href="/wiki/Ida,_Countess_of_Hainaut" title="Ida, Countess of Hainaut">Ida, Countess of Hainaut</a>.</div>
<p><b>Ida of Louvain</b> (died around 1300) was a <a href="/wiki/Cistercian_nuns" title="Cistercian nuns">Cistercian nun</a> of <a href="/wiki/Roosendael_Abbey" title="Roosendael Abbey">Roosendael Abbey</a> in the 13th-century <a href="/wiki/Low_Countries" title="Low Countries">Low Countries</a> who is officially commemorated in the Catholic Church as blessed.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ida_of_Louvain&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ida was born into a well-to-do family in <a href="/wiki/Leuven" title="Leuven">Leuven</a>, <a href="/wiki/Duchy_of_Brabant" title="Duchy of Brabant">Duchy of Brabant</a> (now Belgium). At the age of 22 she felt a religious vocation but her father was a worldly man who would not accept this and subjected her to various forms of ill-treatment to discourage her.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> Despite parental disapproval, she first dedicated her life to God as an <a href="/wiki/Anchoress" class="mw-redirect" title="Anchoress">anchoress</a>, and later became a nun in the recently founded Cistercian Abbey of Roosendael (the Valley of the Roses) in what is now <a href="/wiki/Sint-Katelijne-Waver" title="Sint-Katelijne-Waver">Sint-Katelijne-Waver</a>. One historian has described her as adding "éclat" to the monastery.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> The only contemporary record of her life is in a series of letters by her confessor, a priest named Hugo.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legend_and_veneration">Legend and veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Ida_of_Louvain&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legend and veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Ida died with a reputation for sanctity and came to be considered a saint.<sup id="cite_ref-Herlihy1995_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herlihy1995-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-walsh275_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-walsh275-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> She was said to have experienced <a href="/wiki/Stigmata" title="Stigmata">stigmata</a> and mystical graces.<sup id="cite_ref-Collins_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Collins-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> These included miraculous visions and corporeal encounters with appearances of the infant <a href="/wiki/Jesus" title="Jesus">Jesus</a>, where she would hold him, bathe him, play with him and dress him.<sup id="cite_ref-Herlihy1995_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Herlihy1995-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-Classen2012p86_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Classen2012p86-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> She was <a href="/wiki/Beatified" class="mw-redirect" title="Beatified">beatified</a> for her piety and humility. Her official commemoration, granted by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Clement_XI" title="Pope Clement XI">Pope Clement XI</a> in 1719, is April 13.<sup id="cite_ref-walsh275_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-walsh275-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
