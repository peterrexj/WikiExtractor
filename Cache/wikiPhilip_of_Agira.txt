<p><b>Saint Philip of Agira</b> (also <b>Argirò</b>, <b>Aggira</b>, <b>Agirone</b>, <b>Agirya</b> or <b>Argira</b>) was an early Christian <a href="/wiki/Confessor" title="Confessor">confessor</a>. There are two parallel stories of this saint which give to possible dates in which this saint lived. Traditionally, through the writings of <a href="/wiki/St._Athanasius" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Athanasius">St. Athanasius</a>, it is maintained that Philip of Agira is a saint of the 1st century, born in the year 40 in <a href="/wiki/Cappadocia" title="Cappadocia">Cappadocia</a> (modern <a href="/wiki/Turkey" title="Turkey">Turkey</a>) and died on 12 May 103.
</p><p>Another recent study says to have been born of a Syrian father in <a href="/wiki/Thrace" title="Thrace">Thrace</a> on an unknown date in the 5th century whose elder brothers drowned whilst fishing. Philip was known as the "Apostle of the <a href="/wiki/Sicily" title="Sicily">Sicilians</a>", as he was the first Christian missionary to visit that island. Nothing else can be certainly stated about him.
</p><p>His feast day is 12 May,<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> and he is naturally, <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of the city of <a href="/wiki/Agira" title="Agira">Agira</a>, Sicily, and of the city of <a href="/wiki/%C4%A6a%C5%BC-%C5%BBebbu%C4%A1,_Malta" class="mw-redirect" title="Ħaż-Żebbuġ, Malta">Ħaż-Żebbuġ, Malta</a>, he is celebrated also in the town of <a href="/wiki/Limina" title="Limina">Limina</a>, Sicily, where he also lived. Putatively, his relics were discovered in the <a href="/wiki/San_Filippo,_Agira" title="San Filippo, Agira">church dedicated to him in Agira</a>. Philip is one of the patron saints of the United States Army Special Forces and also known for his power of accomplish <a href="/wiki/Exorcisms" class="mw-redirect" title="Exorcisms">exorcisms</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
