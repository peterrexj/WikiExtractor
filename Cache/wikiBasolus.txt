					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><p><b>Basolus</b> (Basle) (c.555&#8211;c.620) was a French <a href="/wiki/Benedictine" class="mw-redirect" title="Benedictine">Benedictine</a> and hermit.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> He was born near <a href="/wiki/Limoges" title="Limoges">Limoges</a>, and then became a monk near <a href="/wiki/Verzy" title="Verzy">Verzy</a>. He spent 40 years as a hermit on a hill near <a href="/wiki/Reims" title="Reims">Reims</a>.
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:202px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Saint_basle_vittel.jpg" class="image"><img alt="" src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Saint_basle_vittel.jpg/200px-Saint_basle_vittel.jpg" decoding="async" width="200" height="267" class="thumbimage" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Saint_basle_vittel.jpg/300px-Saint_basle_vittel.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f1/Saint_basle_vittel.jpg/400px-Saint_basle_vittel.jpg 2x" data-file-width="480" data-file-height="640" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Saint_basle_vittel.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Image of Saint Basle (Church at Vittel)</div></div></div>
<p><a href="/w/index.php?title=St-Basle_Abbey&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="St-Basle Abbey (page does not exist)">St-Basle Abbey</a> near Verzy was later named for him. He is a Catholic and Orthodox saint,<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> feast day November 26.<sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
