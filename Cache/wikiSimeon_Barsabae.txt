<p><b>Mar Shimun Bar Sabbae</b> (<a href="/wiki/Classical_Syriac_language" class="mw-redirect" title="Classical Syriac language">Classical Syriac</a>: <span lang="syc" dir="rtl">ܡܪܝ ܫܡܥܘܢ ܒܪܨܒܥܐ</span>, died <a href="/wiki/Good_Friday" title="Good Friday">Good Friday</a>, 345) was <a href="/wiki/List_of_Patriarchs_of_the_Church_of_the_East" class="mw-redirect" title="List of Patriarchs of the Church of the East">Bishop of Seleucia-Ctesiphon</a>, from Persia, the <i>de facto</i> head of the <a href="/wiki/Church_of_the_East" title="Church of the East">Church of the East</a>, until his death. He was bishop during the persecutions of King <a href="/wiki/Shapur_II" title="Shapur II">Shapur II</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Sasanian_Empire" title="Sasanian Empire">Sasanian Empire</a> of <a href="/wiki/Iran" title="Iran">Iran</a>, and was executed along with many of his followers. He is revered as a <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> in various Christian communions.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Biography">Biography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Shemon_Bar_Sabbae&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Biography">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Shimun Bar Sabbae was born the son of a <a href="/wiki/Fulling" title="Fulling">fuller</a>. In 316, he had been named <a href="/wiki/Coadjutor_bishop" title="Coadjutor bishop">coadjutor bishop</a> of his predecessor, <a href="/wiki/Papa_bar_Gaggai" class="mw-redirect" title="Papa bar Gaggai">Papa bar Gaggai</a>, in Seleucia-Ctesiphon (now <a href="/wiki/Al-Mada%27in" title="Al-Mada&#39;in">al-Mada'in</a>). He was later accused of being a friend of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_emperor" title="Roman emperor">Roman emperor</a> and of maintaining secret correspondence with him. On that basis, <a href="/wiki/Shapur_II" title="Shapur II">Shapur II</a> ordered the execution of all Christian priests. Because they would not convert to <a href="/wiki/Zoroastrianism" title="Zoroastrianism">Zoroastrianism</a>, Shimoun was beheaded with several thousands, including bishops, priests, and faithful. These include the priests Abdella (or Abdhaihla), Ananias (Hannanja), Chusdazat (Guhashtazad, Usthazan, or Gothazat), and <a href="/wiki/Pusai" title="Pusai">Pusai</a> (Fusik), Askitrea, the daughter of Pusai, the eunuch Azad (Asatus) and several companions, numbered either 1150 or 100.  <a href="/wiki/Sozomen" title="Sozomen">Sozomen</a>, a historian of the 5th century maintained that the numbers registered were 16,000 of the martyrs. Another historian, Al-Masoudy from the 10th century, held that there were killed around 200,000 Assyrians. They are commemorated on:
</p>
<dl><dd>April 21 in the <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>,</dd>
<dd>the Friday after <a href="/wiki/Easter" title="Easter">Easter</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Syriac_Orthodox_Church" title="Syriac Orthodox Church">Syriac Orthodox Church</a></dd>
<dd>April 17 in the <a href="/wiki/Greek_Orthodox_Church" title="Greek Orthodox Church">Greek Orthodox Church</a>,</dd>
<dd>April 30 in the <a href="/wiki/Melkite_Greek_Catholic_Church" title="Melkite Greek Catholic Church">Melkite Greek Catholic Church</a></dd>
<dd>August 17 in the <a href="/wiki/Assyrian_Church_of_the_East" title="Assyrian Church of the East">Assyrian Church of the East</a></dd></dl>
