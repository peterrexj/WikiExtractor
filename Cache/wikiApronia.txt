<p><b>Apronia of Toul</b>, also called <b>Evronie of Troyes</b>,<sup id="cite_ref-baringgold_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgold-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> was a <a href="/wiki/Nun" title="Nun">nun</a> and <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">saint</a> of the 6th century.<sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  She was born at Tranquille, a village in <a href="/wiki/Trier" title="Trier">Trier, Germany</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-baringgold_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgold-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  Her brother was <a href="/wiki/Aprus_of_Toul" title="Aprus of Toul">Saint Aprus of Toul</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Bishop" title="Bishop">bishop</a> in <a href="/wiki/Toul" title="Toul">Toul</a> in northeastern France, from whom she received the veil.<sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>  Hagiographer <a href="/wiki/Sabine_Baring-Gould" title="Sabine Baring-Gould">Sabine Baring-Gould</a> said of Apronia: "Drawn by her love of Christ to a religious life, she led on earth a virginal and angelic life, in imitation of her brother, a man of the highest sanctity.  During her life she loved innocence, purity, and holiness, which she preserved till her death".<sup id="cite_ref-baringgold_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgold-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>  She died in a convent at <a href="/wiki/Troyes" title="Troyes">Troyes</a>, about 140&#160;km (87&#160;mi) southeast of <a href="/wiki/Paris" title="Paris">Paris</a>, year unknown, on July 15, and was enshrined at Toul by the bishop <a href="/wiki/Gerard_of_Toul" title="Gerard of Toul">Saint Gerard</a> in 992.<sup id="cite_ref-baringgold_1-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgold-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-antiochian_2-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-antiochian-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>  She is the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patron saint</a> of women in <a href="/wiki/Childbirth" title="Childbirth">labor</a> and other dangers.<sup id="cite_ref-saintlywomen_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-saintlywomen-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
