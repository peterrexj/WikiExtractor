<p>Saint <b>Muiredach mac Echdach</b>, also known as <b>Murtagh</b>, was the founding <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Killala" title="Bishop of Killala">Bishop of Killala</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a> in the 6th century.
</p><p>Muiredach is described as an old man of Patrick's family, and was placed at the head of the Church of Killala by <a href="/wiki/Saint_Patrick" title="Saint Patrick">St. Patrick</a>, patron saint of <a href="/wiki/Ireland" title="Ireland">Ireland</a> as early as 442 or 443.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was connected to the royal family of King <a href="/wiki/L%C3%B3egaire_mac_N%C3%A9ill" title="Lóegaire mac Néill">Lóegaire mac Néill</a>. He allegedly met with <a href="/wiki/St._Columba" class="mw-redirect" title="St. Columba">St. Columba</a> in the year 575, in a town called <a href="/wiki/Ballysadare" title="Ballysadare">Ballysadare</a>. He probably resigned after a few years, and retired to an island off the <a href="/wiki/Sligo" title="Sligo">Sligo</a> coast in Donegal Bay.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2021)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p><p>The uninhabited island, <a href="/wiki/Inishmurray" title="Inishmurray">Inishmurray</a>, bears his name. He may also be the patron of the monastery that was on that island. He died there as a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>, and his <a href="/wiki/Feast_day" class="mw-redirect" title="Feast day">feast day</a> is 12 August.<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (November 2021)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup>
</p>
