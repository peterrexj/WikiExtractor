<p><b>Fulk</b> (1164 - 26 October<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 1229) was an <a href="/wiki/Italian_people" class="mw-redirect" title="Italian people">Italian</a> <a href="/wiki/Catholic_Church" title="Catholic Church">Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Prelate" title="Prelate">prelate</a> who served as the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Piacenza" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Piacenza">Bishop of Piacenza</a> from 1210 until 1217 and later as the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Pavia" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Pavia">Bishop of Pavia</a> from 1217 until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TC_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> He served in various capacities prior to his episcopal appointment such as a <a href="/wiki/Canon_(priest)" class="mw-redirect" title="Canon (priest)">canon</a> and <a href="/wiki/Provost_(religion)" title="Provost (religion)">provost</a>. He was known for making the effort of keeping out of political affairs since he wanted to dedicate himself more to diocesan affairs.<sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> He was not <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_consecration" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopal consecration">consecrated</a> as a bishop while in <a href="/wiki/Piacenza" title="Piacenza">Piacenza</a> until 1216 and some months after was transferred to <a href="/wiki/Pavia" title="Pavia">Pavia</a> where he would remain until his death.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-TC_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Fulk_of_Pavia&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Fulk was born in <a href="/wiki/Piacenza" title="Piacenza">Piacenza</a> in 1164 to Scottish parents who had Irish origins; he was also known as Folco Scotti with that surname being given during those times to Irish people who emigrated to the Italian mainland.<sup id="cite_ref-MR_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MR-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-SQPN_4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SQPN-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup> In 1184 he entered the <a href="/wiki/Canons_Regular" class="mw-redirect" title="Canons Regular">Canons Regular of Sant'Eufemia</a> before he did <a href="/wiki/Theology" title="Theology">theological studies</a> in <a href="/wiki/Paris" title="Paris">Paris</a> at <a href="/wiki/University_of_Paris" title="University of Paris">the college</a> there after having been sent there around 1185 (though he did first do his studies in Piacenza).<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> In or near 1194 he became the prior for Sant'Eufemia.<sup id="cite_ref-TC_3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Fulk for a brief period taught theological studies to students in Piacenza. He was appointed as a <a href="/wiki/Canon_(priest)" class="mw-redirect" title="Canon (priest)">canon</a> in Piacenza and after his studies in Paris became the <a href="/wiki/Archpriest" title="Archpriest">archpriest</a> for Piacenza.<sup id="cite_ref-SEB_5-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SEB-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup> He later was appointed as the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Piacenza" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Piacenza">Bishop of Piacenza</a> on 2 August 1210 but <a href="/wiki/Pope_Honorius_III" title="Pope Honorius III">Pope Honorius III</a> later transferred him to <a href="/wiki/See_of_Pavia" class="mw-redirect" title="See of Pavia">Pavia diocese</a> which he managed until his death. His selection for the Piacenza see received approval from the <a href="/wiki/Papal_legate" title="Papal legate">papal legate</a> and <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Novara" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Novara">Bishop of Novara</a> Gherardo da Sessia who ensured that <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a> confirmed the selection. The pope himself conferred <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_consecration" class="mw-redirect" title="Episcopal consecration">episcopal consecration</a> upon him in 1216 just before transferring him to <a href="/wiki/Pavia" title="Pavia">Pavia</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-TC_3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>It has been alleged in some sources that Fulk attended the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_Council_of_the_Lateran" title="Fourth Council of the Lateran">Fourth Council of the Lateran</a> in 1215.<sup id="cite_ref-TC_3-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> Fulk died on 26 October 1229 in Pavia and after his death <a href="/wiki/Pope_Gregory_IX" title="Pope Gregory IX">Pope Gregory IX</a> canonized him as a saint during his pontificate; his remains were transferred from the old to new cathedral in 1567.<sup id="cite_ref-TC_3-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TC-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
