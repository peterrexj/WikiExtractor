<p><b>Bogumilus</b>, in Polish <b>Bogumił Piotr</b>, (also known as <b>Bogimilus</b> and <b>Theophilus</b>)<sup id="cite_ref-Bunson_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bunson-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Gniezno" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Gniezno">Archbishop of Gniezno</a> and a <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
<meta property="mw:PageProp/toc" />
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bogumilus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Bogumilus and his twin brother, Boguphalus, were born into a noble family in about 1135 at <a href="/wiki/Dobr%C3%B3w,_Greater_Poland_Voivodeship" title="Dobrów, Greater Poland Voivodeship">Dobrów</a>, <a href="/wiki/Poland" title="Poland">Poland</a>. They studied in <a href="/wiki/Paris" title="Paris">Paris</a>, <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>. Having completed his studies Bogumilus was ordained a <a href="/wiki/Priest_(Catholic_Church)" class="mw-redirect" title="Priest (Catholic Church)">priest</a> near <a href="/wiki/Dobr%C3%B3w,_Greater_Poland_Voivodeship" title="Dobrów, Greater Poland Voivodeship">Dobrów</a>. His uncle, who was the <a href="/wiki/Archbishop_of_Gniezno" class="mw-redirect" title="Archbishop of Gniezno">Archbishop of Gniezno</a>, made him the <a href="/wiki/Chancellor" title="Chancellor">chancellor</a> of <a href="/wiki/Gniezno" title="Gniezno">Gniezno</a>. Bogumilus succeededed his uncle as Archbishop of Gniezno in 1167. Bogumilus founded a <a href="/wiki/Cistercian" class="mw-redirect" title="Cistercian">Cistercian</a> <a href="/wiki/Abbey" title="Abbey">abbey</a> at <a href="/wiki/Koronowo" title="Koronowo">Koronowo</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-Bunson_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bunson-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He resigned his <a href="/wiki/Episcopal_see" title="Episcopal see">see</a> in 1172, possibly due to opposition by his clergy to what they viewed as his excessive strictness. Bogumilus then joined the <a href="/wiki/Camaldolese" title="Camaldolese">Camaldolese</a> hermits at <a href="/wiki/Bielany_(Krak%C3%B3w)" class="mw-redirect" title="Bielany (Kraków)">Uniedow</a>, Poland,<sup id="cite_ref-Bunson_1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Bunson-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> where he remained until his death. While on his deathbed, it is believed that Bogumilus saw a <a href="/wiki/Vision_(spirituality)" title="Vision (spirituality)">vision</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Blessed_Virgin_Mary_(Roman_Catholic)" class="mw-redirect" title="Blessed Virgin Mary (Roman Catholic)">Virgin Mary</a> and <a href="/wiki/Child_Jesus" class="mw-redirect" title="Child Jesus">Child</a>, surrounded by a throng of <a href="/wiki/Angels" class="mw-redirect" title="Angels">angels</a>, who were inviting him to <a href="/wiki/Heaven" title="Heaven">heaven</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Legacy_and_Veneration">Legacy and Veneration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bogumilus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Legacy and Veneration">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Cult_(religious_practice)" title="Cult (religious practice)">cult</a> and <a href="/wiki/Veneration" title="Veneration">veneration</a> of Bogumilus began almost immediately after his death, especially in <a href="/wiki/Poland" title="Poland">Eastern Poland</a>. Many people prayed for his <a href="/wiki/Intercession_of_saints" title="Intercession of saints">intercession</a> for things such as <a href="/wiki/Health" title="Health">health</a>, good <a href="/wiki/Livestock" title="Livestock">livestock</a> and a good catch while <a href="/wiki/Fishing" title="Fishing">fishing</a>. When they were answered, many faithful visited his tomb and it became a place for local <a href="/wiki/Christian_pilgrimage" title="Christian pilgrimage">pilgrimage</a>. Yet it was not until 1625 that the formal process of <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> began under the <a href="/wiki/Primate_of_Poland" class="mw-redirect" title="Primate of Poland">Primate of Poland</a>, <a href="/wiki/Archbishop" title="Archbishop">Archbishop</a> <a href="/wiki/Maciej_%C5%81ubie%C5%84ski" title="Maciej Łubieński">Maciej Łubieński</a>.
</p>
<figure class="mw-default-size mw-halign-left" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/29/Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg/220px-Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg" decoding="async" width="220" height="163" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/29/Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg/330px-Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/29/Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg/440px-Dobr%C3%B3w_-_kaplica.jpg 2x" data-file-width="3131" data-file-height="2322" /></a><figcaption>Chapel of blessed Bogumił, <a href="/wiki/Dobr%C3%B3w,_Greater_Poland_Voivodeship" title="Dobrów, Greater Poland Voivodeship">Dobrów</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>The files were sent to <a href="/wiki/Rome" title="Rome">Rome</a> in 1651, however the process was never completed as the <i>Book of Miracles</i>, which was in the hands of a <a href="/wiki/Count" title="Count">Count</a>, by the name of Sebastian Głębocki, was burned at his <a href="/wiki/Court_(royal)" class="mw-redirect" title="Court (royal)">court</a> in <a href="/wiki/G%C5%82%C4%99bokie,_Kuyavian-Pomeranian_Voivodeship" title="Głębokie, Kuyavian-Pomeranian Voivodeship">Głębokie</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kruszwica" title="Kruszwica">Kruszwica</a>. In 1788 a small wooden chapel dedicated to Bogumilus was built, in the wetlands of the Rivers <a href="/wiki/Warta" title="Warta">Warta</a> and Teleszyny, near <a href="/wiki/Dobr%C3%B3w,_Greater_Poland_Voivodeship" title="Dobrów, Greater Poland Voivodeship">Dobrów</a>, and still stands until this day. The process of <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">beatification</a> would remain dormant until 1908, when Stanislaw Zdzitowiecki, the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Kujawy" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Kujawy">Bishop of Kujawy</a>, reactivated the process. On 27 May 1925 <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_XI" title="Pope Pius XI">Pope Pius XI</a> proclaimed Bogumilus, as <a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Blessed</a>. Later <a href="/wiki/Pope_Paul_VI" title="Pope Paul VI">Pope Paul VI</a> would bestow on Bogumilus the <a href="/wiki/Patron_saint" title="Patron saint">patronage</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Archdiocese_of_Gniezno" title="Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Gniezno">Archdiocese of Gniezno</a>.
</p>
<figure typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><a href="/wiki/File:Estatua_de_Bogumil,_Catedral_de_Gniezno,_Polonia,_2012-04-05,_DD_08.JPG" class="mw-file-description"><img src="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Estatua_de_Bogumil%2C_Catedral_de_Gniezno%2C_Polonia%2C_2012-04-05%2C_DD_08.JPG/150px-Estatua_de_Bogumil%2C_Catedral_de_Gniezno%2C_Polonia%2C_2012-04-05%2C_DD_08.JPG" decoding="async" width="150" height="244" class="mw-file-element" srcset="//upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Estatua_de_Bogumil%2C_Catedral_de_Gniezno%2C_Polonia%2C_2012-04-05%2C_DD_08.JPG/225px-Estatua_de_Bogumil%2C_Catedral_de_Gniezno%2C_Polonia%2C_2012-04-05%2C_DD_08.JPG 1.5x, //upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/f/f8/Estatua_de_Bogumil%2C_Catedral_de_Gniezno%2C_Polonia%2C_2012-04-05%2C_DD_08.JPG/300px-Estatua_de_Bogumil%2C_Catedral_de_Gniezno%2C_Polonia%2C_2012-04-05%2C_DD_08.JPG 2x" data-file-width="1883" data-file-height="3059" /></a><figcaption>Statue of Bogumilus located on the Gniezno Cathedral.</figcaption></figure>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Historical_Confusion">Historical Confusion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bogumilus&amp;action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Historical Confusion">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Some <a href="/wiki/Scholars" class="mw-redirect" title="Scholars">scholars</a> believe that Bogumilus is the amalgamation of two historical figures by the names of Bogumił and Piotr, one of whom was the archbishop and the other a monk or the <a href="/wiki/Bishop_of_Pozna%C5%84" class="mw-redirect" title="Bishop of Poznań">bishop of Poznań</a>.
</p><p>There is no definitive evidence, to support this, however, and most modern <a href="/wiki/Historians" class="mw-redirect" title="Historians">historians</a> regard Bogumił Piotr, as one historical person, who is <a href="/wiki/Venerated" class="mw-redirect" title="Venerated">venerated</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Catholic Church</a>.
</p><p>In support of this view, it is possible that the archbishop had been <a href="/wiki/Baptism" title="Baptism">christened</a> as Piotr and might have taken the name Bogumił as his monastic name. The support for this theory is the fact that this name already had a place of honor in the Camaldolese Order, as the original Bogumilus was a member<sup class="noprint Inline-Template Template-Fact" style="white-space:nowrap;">&#91;<i><a href="/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation_needed" title="Wikipedia:Citation needed"><span title="This claim needs references to reliable sources. (July 2014)">citation needed</span></a></i>&#93;</sup> of the first <a href="/wiki/Monastery" title="Monastery">monastery</a> of the Order outside of Italy. All the members of this 11th-century community were murdered and are considered martyrs within the Order. An account of their deaths was written by their colleague, <a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a> <a href="/wiki/Bruno_of_Querfurt" title="Bruno of Querfurt">Bruno of Querfurt</a>, and the memory of these martyred monks would have been still fresh to the Polish Camaldolese monks of his day.
</p><p>Archbishop Bogumił is mentioned as the probable founder of Gniezno door
</p>
