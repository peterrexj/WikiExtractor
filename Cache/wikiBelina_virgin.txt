					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">Roman Catholic martyr</div>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div class="honorific-prefix" style="display:inline;font-size: 77%; font-weight: normal;"><a href="/wiki/Saint" title="Saint">Saint</a></div><br /><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Belina</div></th></tr><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-header" style="background-color:gold;"><a href="/wiki/Virgin_(title)" title="Virgin (title)">Virgin</a> and <a href="/wiki/Christian_martyr" title="Christian martyr">martyr</a></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">unknown</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">1153<br /><a href="/wiki/Troyes" title="Troyes">Troyes</a>, <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Canonization" title="Canonization">Canonized</a></th><td class="infobox-data">1203 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">19 February or 8 September</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Saint_symbolism" title="Saint symbolism">Attributes</a></th><td class="infobox-data">palm branch, lilies, in rural attire with apron and a basket, sword</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Belina</b> (died 1153)<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> was a <a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic">Roman Catholic</a> <a href="/wiki/Virgin_(title)#virgin_martyrs" title="Virgin (title)">virgin martyr</a>. Her birth date is unknown, but she was born to pious parents who were <a href="/wiki/Serfdom" title="Serfdom">serfs</a> of John Paterne, Lord of <a href="/wiki/Pradines,_Corr%C3%A8ze" title="Pradines, Corrèze">Pradines</a> and of <a href="/wiki/Bois-d%27Arcy,_Yvelines" title="Bois-d&#39;Arcy, Yvelines">D'Arcy</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-baringgould-344_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgould-344-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> She was killed at <a href="/wiki/Landreville" title="Landreville">Landreville</a>, in <a href="/wiki/Champagne_(province)" title="Champagne (province)">Champagne</a> near <a href="/wiki/Troyes" title="Troyes">Troyes</a> in northeastern <a href="/wiki/France" title="France">France</a>,<sup id="cite_ref-holweck_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-holweck-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> this is why she is called both <i>Belina of Troyes</i> and <i>Belina of Landreville</i>.
</p><p>Hagiographer Sabine Baring-Gould described Belina as "a little peasantess".<sup id="cite_ref-baringgould-344_2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgould-344-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup> Belina was engaged to a young man of similar rank in her village, so her parents asked for Lord Paterne's permission, as was the custom. Paterne refused, expressing intention that he would marry her himself, but she rejected him. He surprised her one day while she was tending her father's sheep; she defended herself, so he killed her "for refusing to comply to his unchaste proposals"<sup id="cite_ref-holweck_3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-holweck-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup> with his sword, cutting off her head. Paterne's peasants were so outraged by his actions that they rose up against him and burned down his castle; they almost killed him, but he escaped in disguise. <a href="/wiki/Pope_Anastasius_IV" title="Pope Anastasius IV">Pope Anastasius IV</a> later <a href="/wiki/Excommunication" title="Excommunication">excommunicated</a> Paterne and took away his titles. The king confiscated his land, and the French parliament condemned him to permanent exile.<sup id="cite_ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4">&#91;4&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>Belina's relics were scattered and lost during the <a href="/wiki/French_Revolution" title="French Revolution">French Revolution</a>, but some bone particles and her head have been stored in a bust at the Mores Abbey in Landreville.<sup id="cite_ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5">&#91;5&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6">&#91;6&#93;</a></sup> She was <a href="/wiki/Canonized" class="mw-redirect" title="Canonized">canonized</a> by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Innocent_III" title="Pope Innocent III">Pope Innocent III</a> in 1203, and is venerated in Troyes.<sup id="cite_ref-baringgould-344_2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-baringgould-344-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7">&#91;7&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
