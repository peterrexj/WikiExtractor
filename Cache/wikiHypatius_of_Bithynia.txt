<p><b>Hypatius of Bithynia</b> (<a href="/wiki/Greek_language" title="Greek language">Greek</a>: Ύπάτιος; died ca. 450) was a <a href="/wiki/Monk" title="Monk">monk</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hermit" title="Hermit">hermit</a> of the fifth century.  A <a href="/wiki/Phrygia" title="Phrygia">Phrygian</a>, he became a hermit at the age of nineteen in <a href="/wiki/Thrace" title="Thrace">Thrace</a>.  He then traveled to <a href="/wiki/Constantinople" title="Constantinople">Constantinople</a> and then <a href="/wiki/Chalcedon" title="Chalcedon">Chalcedon</a> with another hermit named Jason.  He became <a href="/wiki/Abbot" title="Abbot">abbot</a> of a <a href="/wiki/Hermitage_(religious_retreat)" title="Hermitage (religious retreat)">hermitage</a> at Chalcedon.
</p><p>He was an opponent of <a href="/wiki/Nestorianism" title="Nestorianism">Nestorianism</a> and sheltered Alexander Akimetes and others whose safety was threatened by the Nestorians.
</p><p>He is credited with halting a revival of the <a href="/wiki/Olympic_games" class="mw-redirect" title="Olympic games">Olympic games</a> because of their <a href="/wiki/Pagan" class="mw-redirect" title="Pagan">pagan</a> origins.<sup id="cite_ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>His <a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">feast day</a> is June 17 in the <a href="/wiki/Eastern_Orthodox" class="mw-redirect" title="Eastern Orthodox">Eastern Orthodox</a> and <a href="/wiki/Byzantine_Catholic" class="mw-redirect" title="Byzantine Catholic">Byzantine Catholic</a> Churches.
</p>
