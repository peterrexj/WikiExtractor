					<div id="mw-content-text" class="mw-body-content mw-content-ltr" lang="en" dir="ltr"><div class="mw-parser-output"><div class="shortdescription nomobile noexcerpt noprint searchaux" style="display:none">16th-century Augustinian and martyr</div>
<p class="mw-empty-elt">
</p>
<style data-mw-deduplicate="TemplateStyles:r1066479718">.mw-parser-output .infobox-subbox{padding:0;border:none;margin:-3px;width:auto;min-width:100%;font-size:100%;clear:none;float:none;background-color:transparent}.mw-parser-output .infobox-3cols-child{margin:auto}.mw-parser-output .infobox .navbar{font-size:100%}body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-header,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-subheader,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-above,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-title,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-image,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-full-data,body.skin-minerva .mw-parser-output .infobox-below{text-align:center}</style><table class="infobox vcard"><tbody><tr><th colspan="2" class="infobox-above n" style="background-color:gold;"><div style="display:inline;" class="fn">Blessed Bartolomé Gutiérrez Rodríguez</div></th></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Born</th><td class="infobox-data">1580<sup id="cite_ref-Gaudium_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gaudium-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup><br /><a href="/wiki/Mexico_City" title="Mexico City">Mexico City</a>, Mexico</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Died</th><td class="infobox-data">3 September 1632<br />Nishizaka, <a href="/wiki/Nagasaki" title="Nagasaki">Nagasaki</a>, Japan</td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label">Venerated&#160;in</th><td class="infobox-data"><a href="/wiki/Roman_Catholic_Church" class="mw-redirect" title="Roman Catholic Church">Roman Catholic Church</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Beatification" title="Beatification">Beatified</a></th><td class="infobox-data">7 May 1867 by <a href="/wiki/Pope_Pius_IX" title="Pope Pius IX">Pope Pius IX</a></td></tr><tr><th scope="row" class="infobox-label"><a href="/wiki/Calendar_of_saints" title="Calendar of saints">Feast</a></th><td class="infobox-data">3 September</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p><b>Bartolomé Gutiérrez Rodríguez</b> (1580 Mexico City-1632 Nagasaki, Japan) was an Augustinian friar who was beatified in 1867.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Life">Life</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=Bartholomew_Gutierrez&amp;action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Life">edit</a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>He was born in Mexico City, the son of Alfonso Gutiérrez and his wife Ana Rodríguez. He joined the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_St_Augustine" class="mw-redirect" title="Order of St Augustine">Order of St Augustine</a> in 1596 at the age of sixteen. He was ordained a priest and sent to <a href="/wiki/Puebla_(city)" title="Puebla (city)">Puebla</a>, but wished to become a missionary. His colleagues teased him because of his corpulence, but he was undeterred.<sup id="cite_ref-Gaudium_1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gaudium-1">&#91;1&#93;</a></sup> 
</p><p>In 1605 he left from Acapulco for Manila, where he became master of novices. He left for Japan in 1612, where he served as prior to the Augustinians of <a href="/wiki/Osaka" title="Osaka">Osaka</a>. Two years later, he and other missionaries, were expelled from the country. He returned to Japan with Peter Zuniga and resumed his ministry while avoiding the priest hunters for twelve years.<sup id="cite_ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2">&#91;2&#93;</a></sup>
</p><p>He was arrested in 1629 and imprisoned in Omura. Bartholomew Gutierrez is mentioned as one of <a href="/wiki/205_Martyrs_of_Japan" title="205 Martyrs of Japan">205 martyrs of Japan</a>. He was scalded in boiling water then burnt alive in Nagasaki <sup id="cite_ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3">&#91;3&#93;</a></sup>
</p>
